# Rizz (英语: Rizz)
## English Explanation
*Rizz* is a playful and informal slang term that generally refers to a confident and stylish demeanor or attitude. This term can be used to describe someone who exudes coolness and has a distinctive style or attitude. In essence, it's about having that "it" factor that makes people notice and admire your presence.
## Chinese Explanation
*Rizz* 是一种充满趣味且非正式的俚语，用来形容一种自信且时尚的姿态或态度。这个词通常用来描述那些散发出酷感和独特风格或态度的人。简单来说，它指的是那种引人注目的气质，让你在人群中脱颖而出。

- English: She totally has rizz!
- Chinese: 她简直有型有款！
