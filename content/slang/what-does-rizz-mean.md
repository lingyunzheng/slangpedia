## Rizz (FYI: Formal and Yea-huh)
### English Explanation
**Rizz:** A term used to describe someone who carries themselves with a certain level of confidence, style, or flair. It's often used to praise someone's demeanor or attitude.
- _Example Sentences:_
- "She really had some **rizz** tonight with her new outfit!"
- "He brought the **rizz** to the party and got everyone hyped up."
### Chinese Explanation
**Rizz:** 表示某人以一种自信、风格或魅力的方式表现自己。这个词通常用来赞扬一个人的气场或态度。
