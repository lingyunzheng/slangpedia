### Rizz
**English:**  
Rizz is a slang term often used to describe someone's confidence, attitude, or style, especially when they are getting what they want or handling situations with ease. It often implies a cool, laid-back, or fashionable mannerism.
**Example Sentence:**  
She really knows how to handle a room with her rizz!
