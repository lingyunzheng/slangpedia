## Rizz (American English | Chinese)

### American English
**Rizz**: This term is used to describe someone's cool or slick demeanor. Originates from the verb "rize," which is a playful onomatopoeic variation of "rise" that adds a cool, laid-back vibe to the sentence. 

- Example: "He's always got that rizz, man. He's got those awesome skate tricks and that cool, chill vibe."

### Chinese
**Rizz**: 这个词用来形容一个人酷或帅气的样子。源自动词“rize”，这是一个玩味的拟声词，“rise”的变形，增添了一种酷、随的性格氛围。

- 示例：他总是酷得有rizz，不仅滑板技巧超帅，还很随性，让人羡慕。
