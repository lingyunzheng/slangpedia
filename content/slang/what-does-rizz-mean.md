## Rizz
### English Explanation
**Rizz** is a slang term, primarily used in urban and youth culture, that refers to a confident, stylish, and rebellious attitude. It often describes someone who exudes a cool aura, or who is dressed in a way that shows they don't care what others think. The term can also imply a sense of unapologetic self-expression and can be used to describe a person's demeanor or their ability to make a strong impression on others.
### Chinese Explanation
**Rizz** 是一种源自街头文化和年轻人之间的俚语，用来形容一个人自信、有型且略带叛逆的态度。这个词通常用来描述某人散发出的酷味，或是他们穿着打扮中展现出不care别人看法的态度。此外，"rizz" 还可以表示一个人的气场或是他们如何给别人留下深刻印象的能力。
