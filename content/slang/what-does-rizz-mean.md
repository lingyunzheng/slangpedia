## Rizz (English)
**Definition:** 
Rizz is a slang term that originated primarily within the fashion community and among young people. It describes a certain style, attitude, or energy that makes someone look sharply dressed and utterly cool.
**Example Usage:** 
"Check out the new guy in class, he’s got some real rizz!"
