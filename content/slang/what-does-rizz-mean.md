Example usage in a sentence:
- **English:** He really knows how to rizz in a suit.
- **Chinese:** 他穿起西装来真的会 rizz。
- **English:** That new haircut really put a rizz on her!
- **Chinese:** 那个新发型真的让她 rizz 了！
Note: The term "rizz" is often used in contexts where someone is being particularly cool or stylish. It can be used in both formal and informal settings, often in an enthusiastic or excited tone.
