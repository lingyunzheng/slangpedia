## Rizz (English)
### Definition
A term used to describe an aura of confidence and coolness; often associated with someone who carries themselves with a sense of bravado or self-assuredness.
### Example
"Man, that new guy came in and hit with some serious rizz. You could tell he was all about the swag!"
## Rizz (Chinese)
### 定义
一个用来形容充满自信和酷感的术语；通常与那些以一种自负或自信的态度行事的人相关联。
### 例子
“那个新来的家伙一进来就散发着强烈的 rizz，你马上就能感受到他就是一路 swag 来的！”
