## Rizz
**English:**
Rizz, short for **"class"**, is a slang term used to describe someone who exudes a lot of confidence, coolness, and style. It's commonly used to praise or emphasize the attitude or appearance of a person who is perceived as being very fashionable or having a commanding presence.
**中文:**
Rizz，源自于“class”，是一个用来形容某人充满自信、 cool 和时尚的网络流行语。它常用于赞扬或强调那些给人感觉非常有品味或气场强大的人的态度或外表。
