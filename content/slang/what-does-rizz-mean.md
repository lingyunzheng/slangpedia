```markdown
## Rizz (美式英语)
**Meaning:** A term used to describe someone or something that is very stylish, cool, or in vogue. It conveys a sense of attitude and hipness.
**Example:** "That new sneaker drop really has some rizz!"
## 里子 (英式英语)
**Meaning:** A term used to describe someone or something that is very stylish, cool, or in vogue. Similar to "rizz," it conveys a sense of attitude and hipness.
**Example:** "那个新鞋款真是里子十足！"
```
Note: While "rizz" is primarily an American English slang term, "里子" (lǐzi) is a corresponding term in Chinese that captures a similar meaning. Both terms are used to describe something or someone as very stylish or trendy.
