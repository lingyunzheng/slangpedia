# Rizz
## English
**Rizz**: A term often used in_online gaming_communities to describe a player's flair, style, or overall aesthetic when it comes to their character build, outfit, or playstyle. It's all about making a strong visual impact and looking cool. Think of it as putting a lot of effort into making your character the most visually appealing and stylish one in the game.
## 中文
**Rizz**: 经常用于_在线游戏_社群中，用来形容玩家独特的造型、风格或整体外观。这不仅包括角色的外观打扮，还包括玩法风格。它的核心在于让角色看起来既时尚又抢眼，就像是在游戏里打造最酷的人物形象。
