```markdown
# Rizz (俚语解释)
**中文释义：**
敲砖头 (qiāo zhuān tóu) —— 指的是动作性的炫耀，通常是男性在展示自己的体能或力量，比如大力拍打胸脯、随意踢碎易碎物品等行为来吸引注意或显摆自己。
**英文释义：**
flex —— Refers to the act of showing off, often to demonstrate one’s physical strength or abilities in a playful or aggressive manner. This can include actions like proudly thumping one’s chest,随意 breaking commonplace items, or generally trying to appear more impressive than others.
```
This translation and explanation bridge the gap between the English term "rizz" and its commonly understood slang meaning, translating it into Chinese using a culturally relevant term "敲砖头" (qiāo zhuān tóu), which is a close approximation in both connotation and usage.
