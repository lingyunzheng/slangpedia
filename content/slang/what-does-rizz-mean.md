# Rizz (English)
**Definition:** Rizz is a slang term that originated in the fashion and nightlife scenes, used to describe a person's confidence and attitude, particularly in how they carry themselves and pursue a style that commands attention.
**Example:** "That guy has mad rizz, he walks into the bar and all eyes are on him."
# 派气 (Pài qì, Chinese)
**释义：** 派气是源于时尚和夜生活场景的俚语，用来形容一个人的自信和姿态，特别是在如何表现自己以吸引目光方面。这个词特别强调一种充满活力、引人注目的气质和姿态。
**例句：** “那个小伙子派气十足，他走进酒吧，全场的目光都集中在他身上。”
