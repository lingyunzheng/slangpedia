## Rizz (英语: Rizz)
### English Explanation
Rizz is a playful and casual term, often used to describe someone who carries themselves with a certain amount of coolness, confidence, or charisma. It's like when someone just oozes style and swagger, making others take notice. It can also be used to describe a fashionable or stylish item, like clothing or accessories.
### Example Usage
- "That new hairstyle gives her a real rizz."
- "He's brought rizz to the party with his slicked-back hair and augmented reality glasses."
