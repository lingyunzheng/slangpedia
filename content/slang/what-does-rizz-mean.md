## Rizz (英式 / 美式)
### English
**Rizz**: This is a slang term often used in the UK and some parts of the US. It refers to a pose or stance that is meant to appear cool, confident, or attractive. Think of it as the "swag" of the UK.
### 中文
**Rizz**: 这是一个在英国和美国部分地区常用的俚语，指的是一个显得酷、自信或有吸引力的姿态。可以理解为英国版的“炫酷”或“酷炫”。
