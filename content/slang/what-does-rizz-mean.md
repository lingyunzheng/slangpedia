# Rizz (英语: Rizz, 汉语: 酷炫 / 利索)
## English Explanation
**Rizz**: Originating from the word "class," "rizz" is used to describe something or someone that is cool, stylish, or exuding an aura of confidence and elegance. It can be used to praise someone's appearance, behavior, or attitude. Think of it as a way to say that someone or something is really high-quality and makes an impressive impression.
- **Example**: "That new sneaker drop just came in, and it has the rizz that I can't resist!"
## Chinese Explanation
**酷炫 / 利索 (kùxiàn / lìsuǒ)**: "Rizz" 在中文中可以用来形容某人或某事物非常 cool、时尚，并且给人一种自信和高档的感觉。它常用来赞美某人的外貌、行为或态度。可以说它是表达某物非常出色、让人印象深刻的一种方式。
- **例句**: “那个新上市的运动鞋真的 rizz 很足，我都忍不住想入手了！”
