# Bilingual Slang Explanation: Rizz
## English
**Definition:** Extravagant coolness or style, often with a sense of flamboyance or confidence.
**Usage Example:** "That new outfit you're wearing has some serious rizz!"
## Spanish
**Definición:** Una extravagancia o estilo deslumbrante, a menudo con una sensación de confianza o exceso de confianza.
**Ejemplo de Uso:** "¡Ese nuevo look que estás usando tiene verdaderas riz!"
### Note
The term "rizz" is an playful and informal way to describe someone or something that exudes a sense of cool, style, or confidence in a highly enthusiastic manner.
