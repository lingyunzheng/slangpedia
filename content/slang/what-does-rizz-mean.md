## Rizz (形容词/动词)
**English:**  
Rizz is a slang term often used to describe something or someone with a cool, edgy, and confident vibe. It can be used to emphasize a particular attribute or demeanor that exudes style and attitude. Think of it as a trendy way to say "cool" or "awesome." 
**Chinese:**  
Rizz 是一个俚语，用来形容带有酷、酷拽且自信 vibe 的事物或人。它可以用来自由强调某种特质或态度，使其显得时尚且有态度。简单来说，就是一个时髦版本的“酷”或“ awesome”。
