## Rizz
**English:** 
A cooler, more confident demeanor or attitude. Essentially, it’s when someone is being super awesome and down-to-earth at the same time.
**Chinese:** 
一种更酷、更有自信的气质或态度。简单来说，就是某人表现出惊艳又接地气时的状态。
