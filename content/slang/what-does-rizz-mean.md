# Rizz
## English
### Explanation
"Rizz" is slang derived from the verb "to rizz," which means to exude confidence, style, or a particular cool attitude. It's often used to describe someone who carries themselves with confidence or who has a standout, fashionable appearance.
### Example Usage
- "That new band is full of rizz; they own the stage!"
- "She really has rizz on the dance floor; her moves are killer."
## Chinese
### 解释
“Rizz”是一个俚语，源自英语动词“to rizz”，意为散发出自信、风格或独特酷炫的态度。通常用来形容一个人表现自信或有特别时尚的外表。
### 用例
- “那支新乐队满是rizz，在舞台上完全掌控全场！”
- “她在舞池里真的很有rizz，她的舞步帅呆了。”
