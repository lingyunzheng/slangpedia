# Rizz (English)
**Rizz**: This slang term is a playful way to describe someone who is stylish or who has a cool, swaggy vibe. Think of it as a more casual, descriptive term for "style" or "cool factor." 
**Example**: "Man, that person has serious rizz, they look fresh and totally on point!" 
# Rizz (Chinese)
**Rizz**: 这个网络用语用来形容一个人很有style或者酷酷的气质。可以说成“有型”或者“酷”。 
**例句**：那个人真有rizz，穿得特别有品味！
