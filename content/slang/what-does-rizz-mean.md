# Rizz (Elevated Swagger)
## English
**Rizz** is a slang term used to describe an attitude or demeanor that's loud, confident, and often attention-seeking. It's all about exuding a sense of cool, flair, and style in a defiant or rebellious way.
## Chinese
**Rizz** 这个网络用语用来形容一种表现出张扬、自信且常常寻求关注的态度或气质。它体现了酷、风采和风格，通常带着一种叛逆或反抗的气息。
