## Rizz (俚语解释)
### 英文
Rizz: A term often used to describe a confident and cool demeanor or attitude. It can also refer to the stylish way someone carries themselves, exuding a sense of aloofness and self-assuredness.
### 中文
rizz：一个常用词汇，用来形容一个人自信而不羁的态度或气质。这个词也可以指某人的举止充满了时尚感和自信，给人一种高冷且自洽的感觉。
