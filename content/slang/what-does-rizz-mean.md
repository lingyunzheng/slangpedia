# Rizz (American English / Urban Slang)
**Definition:** A term used to describe confidence or an attitude that exudes coolness and style.
**Explanation:** Imagine someone walking into a room like the sun has risen just for them, their presence fills the space with a kind of glow. That's "rizz" in action. It's all about that *oomph* that makes you feel like you could rule the world.
- **Example Usage:** "Man, that new rapper's got serious rizz, I can't stop listening to his music!"
---
# 拿捏 (Pinyin: Náqiè, Used in Chinese)
**Definition:** 表示某人或某事做得非常好，展现出自信和风格。
**Explanation:** 拿捏这个词通常用来描述某人表现得非常自信和有风格，就像他们生来就是为了某个角色或场景。它传达了一种独特且引人注目的存在感，让人感觉他们完全掌控了全局。
- **Example Usage:** "那新来的说唱歌手真的很拿捏，他的音乐我听了一遍又一遍！"
