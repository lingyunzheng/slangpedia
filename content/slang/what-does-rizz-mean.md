### Rizz
**English:**  
Rizz is a playful and informal term that's often used to describe someone's confidence or style with a flair for the dramatic. It can also be used to express a sense of coolness or swag.
**Chinese:**  
Rizz 是一个充满活力的、非正式的术语，用来形容某人的自信或风格，带有夸张的意味。也可以用来表达一种酷或型的风格。
