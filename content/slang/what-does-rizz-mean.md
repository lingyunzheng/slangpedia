# Rizz Slang Explanation
## English
**Rizz**: A trendy slang term primarily used in the U.S. and UK to describe someone with an attractive or rebellious attitude, often exuding confidence, style, or attitude. It's commonly used to describe a person's aura or demeanor.
**Example**: "That new pop star has such rizz, I can't take my eyes off them!"
## 中文
**Rizz**: 这是一个在美英等地区流行的流行俚语，用来形容一个人有吸引力或反叛的态度，通常表现出自信、风格或个人魅力。这个词常用来描述一个人的整体气场或气质。
**例句**："那个新出道的流行歌手有股rizz，我根本无法移开目光！"
