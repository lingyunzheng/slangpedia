## Rizz (英国英语 / 英式俚语)
**Definition:**
- **定义:** There's no direct equivalent slang term for "rizz" in British English. "Rizz" is mainly an American term used to describe an aura of coolness or excitement, often in the context of fashion, music, or pop culture. It's used to express that something or someone is extremely cool, exciting, or attractive.
- **中文定义:** 在英国俚语中没有直接对应“rizz”的说法，这是一个美国常用的表达，用来形容一种酷、吸引人的氛围或状态，特别在时尚、音乐或流行文化中使用。它用来描述某人或某物极其酷炫、充满吸引力。
