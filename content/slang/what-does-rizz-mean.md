## Rizz (Americano)
**English**: "Rizz" is a slang term borrowed from the Italian language, meaning "attitude" or "swagger." It is often used to describe a confident and stylish demeanor, especially among young people. Think of it as the cool factor or the 'it' vibe someone exudes.
**意大利语**: "Rizz"是一个来源于意大利语的俚语，意为“态度”或“神态”。通常用来形容一个人充满自信和时尚的样子，尤其是对于年轻人来说。想象一下，这就是某人散发出来的酷劲或‘潮流’气息。
## Rizz (Britannico)
**English**: In British slang, "rizz" often carries a similar meaning to "riff," which is a musical term but can also refer to an informal, cool, or original style. It can be used to describe someone's personality traits or their way of expressing themselves that adds a touch of individuality and coolness.
**英国英语**: 在英国俚语中，“rizz”通常具有类似“riff”的含义，riff是一个音乐术语，但也可以指一种散漫、酷或原创的风格。它可以用来描述某人的个性特征或他们表达自己的方式，给增添了一些独特的酷感。
