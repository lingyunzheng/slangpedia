## Rizz (English)
### Explanation
Rizz is a trendy slang term used to describe someone who has an impressive or confident style, particularly when it comes to their fashion or overall demeanor. It's often used to praise someone for their swag, coolness, or overall presence. 
### Example
- "He absolutely killed it tonight with his rizz, totally stole the show with that leather jacket."
## Rizz (Chinese)
### 解释
“Rizz”是一个流行的网络 slang 词，用来形容某人的风格和气场非常棒，特别是在时尚或整体气场方面。这个词常用来赞美有人很有范儿，很酷或是非常有魅力。
