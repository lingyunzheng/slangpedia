## Rizz (英/美潮语)
### 中文释义
“rizz”在中文中的流行用法主要是用来形容某人的气场或者个性非常有魅力。当某人走路、说话或表现出自己时散发出一种难以抗拒的魅力时，你可以说他/她很有rizz。
### 英文释义
“Rizz” is a slang term used to describe someone's magnetic, appealing, or charismatic presence. It's often used when a person exudes an aura or style that is particularly attractive or powerful.
### 示例
- 中文：小明最近真的有一种rizz，大家都很羡慕他。
- English: Xiao Ming has really developed a strong rizz recently, and everyone is envious of him.
