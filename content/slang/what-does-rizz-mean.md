# Rizz (英 / 美)
**English Explanation:**
- **Rizz:** Originating from the word "ditz," this term is used humorously to describe someone who is silly or slightly offbeat in a charming way. It's often used to affectionately highlight someone's quirky or eccentric behavior or personality.
**Chinese Explanation:**
- **雷兹 (léi zǐ):** 这个词源自“ditz”，用作轻松幽默的方式来形容某人有点滑稽或以一种迷人的方式略显奇特。通常用来温柔地形容某人的怪癖或个性中的不拘小节。
