# What does "rizz" mean?（rizz 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Rizz" is a slang term that describes a cool, confident, or stylish demeanor. It is often used to describe someone or something that looks or feels trendy and edgy.

**中文解释：**  
“Rizz”是一个 slang 词，用来形容一种酷、自信或有型的态度。它经常用来形容看起来或感觉很潮流、前卫的人或事物。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: "They really brought the rizz to the dance tonight!"
   中文翻译: "他们今晚在舞会上真是展现出了非凡的潮流感！"

2) English sentence: "That new hoodie has so much rizz!"
   中文翻译: “这件新的连帽衫真是太有潮流感了！”

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "rizz" originated from the Italian verb "revizzare," which means to review or revise. In hip-hop culture, it began to be used to describe a confident and cool appearance, and has since spread into mainstream slang.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. Style
2. Coolness
3. Edge
4. Swagger
5. Presence
