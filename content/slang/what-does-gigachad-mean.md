## Gigachad
### English
**Definition:** Gigachad is a portmanteau of "giga" (meaning extremely large in size or intensity) and "Chad." The term is often used to describe a hyper-masculine, idealized, and often unattainable image of a male, sometimes exaggerated to the point of being humorous.
**Usage Example:** "John has a bit of gigachad swagger walking into the gym."
### 中文
**定义:** “Gigachad”是“giga”（表示非常大、强烈的大小或强度）和“Chad”（通常指的是一位典型美国郊区男性）的复合词。这个词汇常用来形容一种超 masculine、理想化且往往难以实现的男性形象，有时会被夸张到带有调侃的意味。
**用法示例:** "约翰走进健身房时带着些 gigachad 的气场。"
