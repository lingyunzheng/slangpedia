# Gigachad
**中文释义：** 吉嘉德，源自网络流行语，指那些在视觉上非常具有冲击力、满头肌肉的“完美”男性形象。与“肌霸”类似，但“吉嘉德”更强调外表的强大和魅力，是一种理想化和夸大的形象。
**English Explanation:** Gigachad is a term originating from online slang, referring to extremely muscular and visually imposing male figures, often embodying an idealized and exaggerated aesthetic. It's similar to terms like "musclemonger" or "beast mode", but "gigachad" particularly highlights the power and allure in their appearance.
