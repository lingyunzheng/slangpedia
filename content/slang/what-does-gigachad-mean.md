## Gigachad
### English Explanation
"Gigachad" is a playful way to describe a person, usually a man, who is perceived as having an exceptionally good-looking and charming physique. It originated from a makeup YouTuber and refers to a "giga" (meaning super) version of Chad, a name often used to describe muscular and attractive men. The term highlights a level of physical attractiveness that goes beyond typical standards.
### Chinese Explanation
"Gigachad" 超级卓多 (chuānguāng zhhuò duō) 是一种用来描述异常帅气和有魅力的男性的网络用语。它起源于一个化妆领域的YouTube博主，用来形容肌肉发达且有吸引力的男人，"giga" 意味着超级。这个词语强调的一种超乎寻常的外貌吸引力。
---
### Examples
- **English:** "He's a real gigachad, I envy his chiseled abs!"
- **Chinese:** "他真的是一个超级卓多，我好羡慕他的六块腹肌！"
