## Gigachad
**中文解释**：超级奶狗。形容一个非常帅气、具有极高魅力的男生，通常拥有完美的外貌和出众的条件。
**英文解释**：A Gigachad is an extremely handsome guy with perfect looks and incredible charm. The term is often used to describe someone who is exceptionally attractive and can cause a significant stir in their social circles.
