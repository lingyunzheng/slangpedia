# Gigachad
## English
**Gigachad**: A term used in online communities, especially on platforms like Reddit, to describe an extremely attractive (or to some, overpowered) man. The word was popularized by a meme showcasing a character from the video game Yakuza with incredibly buff and muscular appearance. The term implies that the individual is so incredibly good-looking or well-built that it exceeds normal human standards.
## Chinese
**Gigachad**: 在网络社区，尤其是在Reddit等平台，用来形容特别英俊（或者对某些人来说，过于强大）的男人。“Gigachad”这个词源自一个来自游戏《雅库扎》的角色的梗图，这个角色有着极其健硕、肌肉发达的外貌。这个词的用意在于表示该个体的帅气或肌肉发达程度超出了常人的标准，甚至可以说是“过载”的（giga意为巨大，超负荷）。
