## Gigachad: 巨卓
**English: Gigachad**
"Gigachad" is a term that originated from online communities, often associated with character artwork in the form of computer-generated images. The term describes a character design that is exceptionally attractive, often to an exaggerated or idealized extent, which reflects the user's concept of physical perfection. It can also be used descriptively for a person who is extremely good-looking in an equally exaggerated fashion. 
**中文：巨卓**
"巨卓"这个词来自于网络社区，通常与计算机生成的图像中的角色艺术相关。这个词用来形容一个非常吸引人的角色设计，常常达到夸张或理想化的程度，反映出用户心目中的完美身材。它也可以描述为一个极其英俊的人，同样夸张的程度。
