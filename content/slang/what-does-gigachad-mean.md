## Gigachad
**English Explanation:**  
"Gigachad" is a portmanteau combining "giga" (meaning extremely large) with the term "Chad," which originally referred to a stereotype of an attractive but bland, average-looking man. The term has gained popularity through internet memes and became particularly well-known in 2022 when it appeared in a viral TikTok dance. Essentially, "gigachad" refers to an extremely attractive, muscular, and conventionally handsome man, often used humorously.
**Chinese Explanation:**  
"Gigachad" 是将"giga"(表示极大的) 与 "Chad"（最初用来形容一种吸引人但又非常普通、乏味的男性形象）两词结合而成的新兴网络用语。“Gigachad”最初走红于2022年的TikTok舞蹈挑战，用来形容极其具有魅力、肌肉发达且符合传统意义上的英俊男性，通常是一种调侃和幽默的说法。
