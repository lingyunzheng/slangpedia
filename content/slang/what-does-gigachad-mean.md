# Gigachad
**English:**  
Gigachad is a term used in online communities, mostly on platforms like Twitter and Reddit, to describe a highly appealing, masculine version of Chad, which originally meant someone who was attractive and/or physically fit. Gigachad takes this concept to the extreme, often referring to a mythological or exaggeratedly handsome and strong character, capable of incredible feats. The term can be used sarcastically or admiringly depending on the context.
**Chinese:**
