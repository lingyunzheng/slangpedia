## Gigachad

**English:** A term used to describe a person, typically a man, who exudes an exaggerated level of attractiveness, confidence, and sexual appeal. The term "gigachad" often implies a level of chad (caveman, alluding to the type that's perceived as attractive to women) that has been magnified to an almost absurd extent. People who are described as gigachad usually have prominent features, like a perfectly chiseled jaw, muscular physique, and a suave demeanor.

**中文:** “gigachad”是一个用来形容一个具有强烈吸引力、自信和性感魅力的人，特别是男性。"gigachad"这个词通常指的是魅力程度被夸张到几乎是荒谬程度的型男。“chad”一词源自“caveman”，常被联想为对女性有吸引力的原始野蛮人形象，而"gigachad"则将其魅力提升到了极致。

### Example Usage

- **English:** "He's a gigachad, there's no doubt about it."
- **中文:** “他是一个几乎可以称得上是‘gigachad’的存在。”

---

This term might be exaggerated for comedic or emphatic effect, often used in internet and social media contexts.
