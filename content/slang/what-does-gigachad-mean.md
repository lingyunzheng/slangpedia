# Gigachad
## English
**Gigachad**: A term often used in online communities, especially on platforms like Reddit, to describe someone with an exceptionally attractive or charismatic presence. Typically, the person being labeled as "gigachad" is perceived as having a level of chad (a colloquial term primarily used to describe a socially attractive, good-looking person) that extends above the ordinary, usually to a level that is considered absurd or over-the-top. It's often used in a playful or sarcastic manner, particularly for someone who is seen as impressively good-looking without being stereotypically bulky or muscular. 
## Chinese
