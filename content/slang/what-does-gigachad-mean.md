# Gigachad
## English Explanation
**Gigachad** is a term used primarily on internet forums, social media, and within certain subcultures. It describes someone (usually a male) who is extremely attractive, confident, and swaggering. The word is a portmanteau of "giga" (meaning extremely large or vast) and "Chad," a slang term for an attractive young man. Gigachad is often associated with positive stereotypes, including having a prominent body, a good sense of style, and being successful.
