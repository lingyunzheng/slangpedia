## Gigachad
### English
**Gigachad**: A term commonly used in internet slang, particularly on social media and gaming communities, to describe a very attractive or handsome male character or individual. The prefix "giga-" intensifies the meaning to suggest an extreme level of attractiveness that goes far beyond average. It's often used to mock or compliment someone who is exceptionally good-looking.
### 中文
**Gigachad**：这是一个在互联网 slang 中常用的术语，特别是在社交媒体和游戏社区，用来形容非常有魅力或帅气的男性角色或个人。“giga-” 前缀增强了词汇的意义，表示比平均水平高出很多的极端魅力。这个词常用来嘲讽或赞美超有魅力的人。
