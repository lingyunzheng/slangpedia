# Gigachad
**English:** A term used to describe someone who is exceptionally attractive or has a great presence, often exaggeratedly so. It's a play on words, combining "giga" (meaning extremely large or of great magnitude) with "Chad," a slang term for a handsome man. The term is used both literally and humorously to emphasize one's attractiveness.
**Chinese:** "Gigachad”用来形容一个人极其英俊或有极强的气场，常常是夸大的意思。“giga”是表示非常大或极多，而“Chad”则是一个俚语，指的是帅气的男人。这个词既可以字面上使用，也可以开玩笑地用来强调某人的吸引力。
---
示例：
- In English: "He's a real gigachad!"
- 在中文：”他真是个真正的gigachad！“
