## Gigachad
### English Explanation
Gigachad is a term often used in online forums and social media to describe a male character who is extremely attractive and has unnaturally muscular and well-defined features. The term is a combination of "giga" (meaning extremely large or vast) and "Chad," an internet slang term for a good-looking guy, usually in a preppy or professional setting. It has become popular among video game communities, social media influencers, and anyone discussing fictional characters or real-life celebrities.
