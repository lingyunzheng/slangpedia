## Gigachad
**English:**  
"Gigachad" is a term derived from combining "giga," meaning extremely large or vast, and "Chad," which refers to a character who is seen as excessively attractive or muscular. This term is often used to describe someone who embodies an extreme ideal of masculinity, typically in the context of bodybuilding or fitness. The slang is highly influenced by internet culture and often used humorously or in memes.
