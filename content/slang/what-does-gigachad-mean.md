## Gigachad
### English Explanation
**Gigachad** is a term popularized on the internet, particularly within online gaming communities and social media platforms. It refers to someone who is perceived as extremely attractive or having an unrealistic level of physical fitness. The term is often used with a tone of sarcastic admiration or amusement, implying that the person's attributes are so extreme or perfect that they are almost fictional.
