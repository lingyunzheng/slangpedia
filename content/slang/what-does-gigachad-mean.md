# Gigachad
## English
### Explanation
"**Gigachad**" is a term used to describe a highly attractive or handsome individual, often exaggerated and in an hyperbolic sense. It combines "giga," referring to a large quantity (as in gigabytes), and "chad," slang for someone who is physically attractive, especially a man. The term is often used humorously or sarcastically.
### Example
*"Did you see that guy at the beach? He's a total gigachad, it's like he came straight from the pages of a fantasy novel!"*
