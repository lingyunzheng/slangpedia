## Gigachad (English/Chinese)
### English
**Gigachad:** This term is a playful reference to a super fit, muscular character, often depicted as having an exaggeratedly attractive and muscular physique. It's frequently used in internet culture to describe a character or person with an impossibly perfect body. The prefix "giga-" in this context is likely derived from "gigantic" or "gig" (referring to a gigabyte), meaning something immense or extremely large.
### Chinese
**Gigachad:** 这个词是对超级健美、肌肉发达人物的戏谑描述，经常用来形容拥有极度完美且肌肉发达身体的角色或人。在此语境中，“giga-”前缀可能来源于“巨型”或“gigabyte（吉字节，意即巨型或极大）”，意味着极其巨大或极其庞大的东西。
