# Gigachad: 巨卓
## English Explanation
**Gigachad** is a Sino-Anglicism slang term that became popular on the internet, particularly on platforms like Twitter and TikTok. It is used to describe a male character or individual who is extremely attractive, having a perfect body, handsome appearance, and sometimes an air of confidence and charisma. The term is often associated with fantasy or fiction contexts, but it can also be used humorously to express admiration for real-life influencers or celebrities. The suffix "chad" is a playful alteration, making it sound more like an internet meme or inside joke.
## Chinese Explanation
