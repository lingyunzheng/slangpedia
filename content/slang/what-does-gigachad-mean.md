## Gigachad: 超A型男
**English Explanation:**
"Gigachad" is a made-up term mostly used in internet communities, particularly on platforms like Reddit. The word blends "giga" (meaning extremely large or massive) and "Chad," a slang term for a good-looking, confident, and attractive man. Gigachad refers to a hyper-masculine, overly attractive or buff male figure, often exaggerated and pushed to the extreme, symbolizing the pinnacle of male attractiveness.
