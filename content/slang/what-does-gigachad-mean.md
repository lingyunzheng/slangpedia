## Gigachad
### English
**Gigachad**: This term is used to describe a fictional, highly idealized, and often handsome person, especially in the context of internet memes and advertisements. A "gigachad" is an exaggerated depiction of masculinity, complete with buff abs, defined jawline, and an air of confidence. It's often used humorously to mock or parody real-world advertising personalities.
### Chinese
