## Gigachad
### English
**Gigachad**: A term derived from "giga" (meaning huge) and " Chad," referencing a character from the anime *Garakand*. It describes a character who is exceptionally attractive and fit, often used humorously to denote an extremely hot or handsome person. It has become a meme文化中的一个梗，用来形容一个极其帅气或有魅力的人。
### Chinese
**Gigachad**: 这个词来自“giga”（意为巨大的）和“Chad”，这是来自动画《Garakand》中的一个角色。用它来形容一个异常帅气或健美的形象，常用来幽默地指某个极其有魅力的人。它已经成为了一种流行的梗，用来形容外貌或身材极佳的人。
