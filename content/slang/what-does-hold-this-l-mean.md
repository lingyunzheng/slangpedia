### "Hold this L" in English
**Definition:**  
An expression used to stress something significant or to express enthusiasm and support.
**Example Usage:**  
- **In a conversation:** "I can't believe we won the game! Hold this L, team!"
- **In a social media post:** "Just got a promotion! Hold this L until tomorrow!"
