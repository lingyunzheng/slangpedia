# "Hold this L" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English Explanation
"**Hold this L**" is a slang term that often refers to a gesture where someone holds their thumb and pinky finger together, forming the letter "L" with their hand. This gesture can be used in various contexts, but it is often associated with agreements, insisting, or as a form of encouragement. The phrase "hold this L" implies that someone is asking you to maintain a stance or belief that they are proposing.
### Usage Examples
- "Are you still with me, or should I hold this L for you?"
- "I'm serious, just hold this L with me."
## Chinese Explanation
"**Hold this L**" 这个手势通常是指用拇指和无名指捏在一起，形成字母 "L" 的动作。这个手势在各种情境中都有使用，但通常与同意、坚持或鼓励有关。“hold this L” 表示请求对方继续保持他们所提出的态度或信念。
### 使用示例
- “你还在和我站在一起吗？还是要我继续坚持这个 L？”
