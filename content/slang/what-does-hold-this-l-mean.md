## "Hold this L" 
### English
- **Meaning**: This phrase is used to quickly convey frustration or exasperation. It's a succinct way of getting someone's attention and saying, "Stop, listen to me, and be impressed by what I'm about to say."
- **Example**: "I finally got the promotion, and I'm telling you, hold this L! It's huge!"
### 中文
- **意思**：这是一个快速传达沮丧或厌烦的方式。它是一种简短的方法，用来吸引别人注意并对即将说的话表示印象深刻。
- **例句**："我终于升职了，我要告诉你，hold this L！这可是大事！"
