## "Hold this L"
- **English:** A casual way to make a request or comment that something is true or agreed upon. It often involves making an "L" shape with your index finger and thumb, as if spelling out the letter "L" on your forehead.
- **Chinese:** 一个非正式的表达，用于提出请求或确认某事的真实性。通常伴有将拇指和食指在前额形成字母"L"的动作。
