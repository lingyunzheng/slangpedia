# "Hold this L" — 保持这个L
**English Explanation:**
"Hold this L" is a more casual or slang way of saying, "Stay true to yourself" or "Just keep it real." It's often used when someone wants to encourage or remind someone to be genuine and consistent with their beliefs or actions.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"保持这个L"（保持这个L）是一种更随意或俚语的说法，意思是“保持本真”或“就保持这样”。当有人想要鼓励或提醒某人要真诚且言行一致时，经常会用到这句话。
**Usage Example:**
- English: "You're such a great friend, hold this L!"
- Chinese: 你是个很好的朋友，保持这个L！
