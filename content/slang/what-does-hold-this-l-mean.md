# "Hold This L" Slang Explanation
## English
**Meaning:** To consider or agree to something without being convinced. It's often used when someone isn't fully on board but still decides to go along with the plan.
**Example:**
- In a group discussion about skipping class, one member says, "Hold this L, I'm not 100% sure, but I'm still in."
## 生成的中文解释
**含义:** 在心里保留意见，在没有完全被说服的情况下同意某事。通常表示虽然不完全相信，但仍然决定跟随某个计划或提议。
**例句:**
- 在讨论逃课时，一个成员说: "Hold this L， 我不完全确定，但我还是同意了。"
