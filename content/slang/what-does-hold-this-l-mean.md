## "Hold this L" (Keep it locked)
**English:** A casual way to express agreement or approval, similar to saying "agree" or "keep it up."
**中文:** 表示认同或赞许的一种非正式说法，类似于说"同意"或"继续"。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I totally agree with what you said! Hold this L!"
- **中文:** "我完全同意你所说的！Keep it locked！"
