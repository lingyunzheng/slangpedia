# "Hold This L" Slang Explanation
## English
**Definition:** A phrase used to express that something impresses or excites the speaker, or to make a request for confirmation or agreement. It can also denote satisfaction or approval with a situation or action.
**Usage:** "I just saw the new car, hold this L!"
## 中文
**释义:** 这个词组用来表达某事物有多么惊艳或令人兴奋，也可以用来请求确认或同意。此外，它还可以表示对当前情况或动作的满意或认可。
**用法:** "我刚看到那辆新车，hold this L！"
