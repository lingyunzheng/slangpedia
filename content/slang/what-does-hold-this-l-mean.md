### "Hold this L"
**English:**  
- A casual, often playful way to express that someone is describing something in a way that seems either unbelievable or poorly executed. It's akin to saying "I don't believe you" or "That's a负荷不了，让我慢慢说好吗？"
**Simplified Chinese:**  
- 一种随意、通常带有戏谑意味的说法，用来表达对方所描述的事物显得不真实或执行不佳。类似于“我不相信你”或“这太离谱了”。
**Contextual Use:**
- Someone might say "Hold this L" if a friend is boasting about a situation that seems exaggerated or unbelievable. It's a way of saying, "I can barely take that seriously."
**Example:**
- "He says he ran a marathon and then swam the English Channel in one day? Hold this L."
- 他说他一天之内就跑了马拉松还游了英吉利海峡？hold this L。
