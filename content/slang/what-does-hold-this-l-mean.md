# "Hold this L" (保留这个L)
## English Explanation
**Hold this L**: This phrase is often used in online gaming communities, particularly in games where players are expected to perform quick and precise actions. It can be translated as "Hold this (the L key)" in games where the "L" key or button is crucial for a specific action. In some communities, it's also used as a playful way to tell someone to "hold on" or continue doing something that is working.
## Chinese Explanation
**hold this L**: 这个口令通常出现在游戏社区中，尤其是在玩家需要执行快速而精确的操作的游戏里。“L”通常是某个关键操作的按键。使用这个短语，往往是在告诉某人需要“继续按L”或做某种特定的操作。在部分社区中，它也被用来开玩笑地命令某人“继续”或“坚持”。
