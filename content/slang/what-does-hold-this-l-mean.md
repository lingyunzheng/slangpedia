## "Hold this L" (English)
- **Definition**: A casual phrase used to express that something is awesome, cool, or simply to let the speaker or listener know that they are doing or being something impressive.
- **Example Usage**: "Check out my new car! Hold this L!"
- **Origin**: The phrase gained popularity through social media and is often accompanied by the hand gesture where the thumb and pinky are held together, forming an "L" shape.
## "Hold this L" (Chinese)
- **释义**: 一个口头交际用语，用来表达某事物很酷炫或者很厉害，也可以用来告诉说话者或听众他们正在做的或保持的东西非常棒。
- **使用示例**: “看看我的新车！Hold this L！”
- **起源**: 这个短语在社交媒体上流行起来，经常伴随着手势：拇指和小指并拢，形成“L”形。
