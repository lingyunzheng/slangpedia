## "Hold This L" 
### English
"Decent" or "Not Bad"  
Explanation: Often used to give a slightly positive but casual agreement or approval. It's a way to say that something is okay or better than bad.
### 中文
“凑合”或“还不错”  
解释：通常用来表达一种轻微的肯定或认可，既不是完全满意，但也说不上差。意思是某件事情还可以或比预期要好一点。
