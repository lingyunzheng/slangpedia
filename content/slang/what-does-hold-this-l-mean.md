# "Hold this L" (英语: Hold this L)
## 中文解释
“Hold this L”是一种网上的流行语，通常在表达某种程度上的信心或者自豪感时使用。这里的“L”是一个弯曲的手指形状，类似于乐高积木英文 "Leg" 的首字母。在中文网络上，“L”这个手势经常被用来表示“吊炸天”或“超级棒”的意思。当人们打出这个手势或者说出这样的话时，往往是在夸赞某项成绩、某个创意，或者是强调自己的某种态度。
## 英文 Explanation
"Hold this L" is a popular internet slang used to express confidence or pride in a particular situation. The "L" here refers to a bent finger shape, similar to the "L" in the English word "Leg." In the Chinese internet, the "L" gesture is often used to mean "Slay" or "awesome." When someone makes this sign or says this, they are usually complimenting a result, a creative idea, or emphasizing a certain attitude.
