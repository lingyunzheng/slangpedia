## Copium (英语: Copium)
### English Explanation
"Copium" is a playful slang term derived from combining "cop" (short for "police officer") and "cocaine," which are both illegal substances. The term is often used to describe a police officer's interactions or behavior that remind one of a cocaine user, such as being overly aggressive, erratic, or unresponsive to common sense. It can also be used to describe police tactics or policies that seem nonsensical or overly harsh.
### 汉语解释
"Copium" 是从 "cop"（警察）和 "cocaine"（可卡因）两个词汇中融合而来的玩味俚语。这个词通常用来形容那些警察的行为或互动让人联想起可卡因用户，比如过于暴力、行为怪异或缺乏常理。此外，它还可以用来形容警察的策略或政策显得毫无道理或过于苛刻。
