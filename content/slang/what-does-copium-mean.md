## Copium (English/Slang) vs. Copium (Chinese/Slang)
### Copium (English/Slang)
**Definition:** A playful and casual term often used to refer to caffeine, as in a strong dose or hit that keeps someone alert and energized.
**Usage Example:** "Needed a copium to stay awake during the all-nighter study session."
### Copium (Chinese/Slang)
**Definition:** 拿来提神的药物或咖啡因的效果，通常指让你保持警觉和有活力的“猛量”或“一击”。这个词语在中文里通常用来形容类似提神饮料或药物的效果。
**Usage Example:** "需要喝杯拿铁来提神了，有点儿copium的效果。"
**Equivalent Slang in Chinese:** 
- 提神剂 (Dīshén jì) - Revitalizer
- 咖啡因猛击 (Kāfēyīn měng jī) - A strong hit of caffeine
- 神马新品 (Shén ma xīn w Tiên) - "What's new" in a playful reference to a new stimulant or strong caffeine effect 
Both terms are used lightheartedly and contextually to describe the energizing effects of caffeine or other stimulants.
