## Copium (English)
Copiaum (Noun): A playful, albeit informal, term used to refer to marijuana or cannabis. It's a creative way to keep conversation light and not too serious, especially among friends or in casual settings.
## Copium (中文)
Copium（名词）: 一个轻松、非正式的术语，用来指代大麻或 Cannabis。这是一种使对话保持轻松随意的方式，尤其是在朋友之间或在非正式场合使用时。
