## Copium
### English:
**Copium**: A humorous and often playful wordplay on the term "cocaine." It's not a real drug but rather a lighthearted way to refer to something you might need a little boost from to feel energized or inclined to stay up late. It's often used in casual settings, especially among younger people, to inject a bit of humor into jokes involving energy or focus.
### 中文:
**Copium**: 这个词是对可卡因的一种戏谑性的称呼，不是真正的药物，而是一种幽默的方式，用来形容让你感到备受激励或愿意熬夜的东西。它常用在轻松的场合，尤其是在年轻人中，用来增加一些关于能量或专注度的笑话中的幽默感。
