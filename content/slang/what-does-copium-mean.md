## Copium (English)
### Definition
"Copium" is a slang term that humorously alludes to "coffee," playing on the similar pronunciation with "cannabis" but replacing the "b" with a "p." It's often used in casual settings to denote strong coffee with high caffeine content.
### Usage Example
- "I need a copium to keep me awake during this late-night coding session!"
## Copium (Chinese)
### 释义
“Copium”是一个幽默的俚语，通过将“咖啡”发音与“大麻”接近的方式（用“p”代替“b”），来滑稽地指代“咖啡”。这个词通常在非正式场合用来形容高咖啡因含量的咖啡。
### 使用示例
- “在这次深夜编程会话中，我需要一杯copium来保持清醒！”
