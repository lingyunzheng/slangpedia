## Copium (俚语解释)
### 中文
**吃 copium**：形容一个人在进行某种快乐或满足的活动，尤其是在享受一些刺激或让人上瘾的事物时。这个词有个轻松幽默的味道，往往用于描述对某些事物的极度享受或沉迷。
### English
**Copiuming**：describes someone who is enjoying or indulging in something fun or exciting, especially when it involves some kind of stimulus or addiction-inducing activity. The term has a light and humorous tone, often used to express extreme enjoyment or addiction to certain things.
