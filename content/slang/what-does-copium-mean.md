# Copium
## English Explanation
Copium is a playful, informal term for caffeine. It's often used in contexts where caffeine's energizing effects are exaggerated or made into a joke. For example, someone might say they are going to "impress their boss with copium this morning" to mean they will drink coffee to stay very alert.
