## Copium
### English
"Copium" is a creative blend of the words "cop" (short for "police officer") and "dope" (an informal term for a drug, especially a narcotic). In casual slang, "copium" is used humorously to refer to a police officer or to describe a situation or behavior that is sly, sneakily helpful, or even slightly illegal. It's often used among friends or colleagues who enjoy a bit of playful banter, especially when discussing local law enforcement or police activity.
### 中文
"Copium" 是“cop”（警察的意思）和“dope”（毒品，通常指令人愉快的药物）两个词的混搭。在俚语中，“copium”通常用来幽默地指警察，或者形容那种狡猾、机智地帮助人，甚至有点灰色地带的行为。这个词经常被朋友或同事在轻松聊天时用到，尤其是在讨论当地警察或警局的活动时。
