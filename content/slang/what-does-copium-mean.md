## Copium (Slang Explanation)
**English:**
- **Definition:** "Copium" is a playful or humorous way of referring to caffeine, often due to its energizing and stimulating effects, reminiscent of how a police officer might need to stay alert.
- **Usage Example:** "I couldn't stay awake through the meeting, thanks to just one cup of copium!"
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** "Copium" 是一种幽默或轻松的说法，用来指代咖啡因。这是因为咖啡因能提供提神醒脑的效果，就像警察需要保持警惕一样。
- **使用示例:** "开会的时候我打不起精神，全靠一杯 copium 才撑过来了！"
