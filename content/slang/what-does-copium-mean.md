## Copium (English)
**Definition:** A playful or ironic term often used to refer to coffee. It's a fun way to describe the energizing and invigorating effect that coffee has on people, much like superpowers.
### Example Usage:
- "I need my copium to power through the midday slump."

## Copium (Chinese)
**定义:** 一个充满趣味或讽刺的术语，常用来指代咖啡。通过这种方式，可以形象地描述咖啡给人带来的充满活力和提神醒脑的效果，就像超能力一样。
### 例句:
- "我需要我的copium来对抗午后低迷时光。"
