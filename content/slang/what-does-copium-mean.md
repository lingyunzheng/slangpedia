## Copium (English)
### Definition:
Copium, a playful term often used among friends, is a slang term for a small but potent dose of a desired effect, whether it's from a caffeine boost, a social high, or any other form of stimulation.
### Usage Example:
- "I only needed a copium of coffee to wake up and focus for this big meeting."
### Tone:
Casual and lighthearted

## Copium (中文)
### 定义：
Copium 是一种朋友之间常用来形容的一种小剂量但效果显著的体验，不论是来自咖啡因的提振、社交的兴奋，还是其他任何形式的刺激。
### 使用示例：
- "我只喝了一点儿咖啡因的‘copium’就振作起来了，为这个重要的会议做准备。"
### 语调：
轻松随意
