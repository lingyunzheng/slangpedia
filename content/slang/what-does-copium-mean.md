## Copium
### English
"Copium" is a slang term derived from combining "cop" (a colloquial term for a police officer) and "cocaine." It's used to describe a cop or police officer who is behaving in a selfish or self-centered manner, reminiscent of how drug users might prioritize their drug use above all else. Essentially, anyone who acts as if they are truly addicted to their power or position in the police force can be referred to as a copium.
### 中文
"Copium" 是一个网络俚语，由“cop”（警察的一个俚语称呼）和“cocaine”（可卡因）组合而成。它用来描述一名行为自私或以自我为中心的警察，这种行为类似于瘾君子在一切事情上优先考虑自己使用药物。换句话说，任何表现得仿佛真正沉迷于自身权力和警察职位的人，都可以被称作 copium。
