# Copium (俚语解释)
## English
**Copium**: A creative and playful term often used to refer to a fictional or fictionalized version of a police department or law enforcement agency, often exaggerated or portrayed in a humorous or over-the-top manner. It's a vibrant and often satirical way to talk about or parody police work.
## 中文
** Copium (科皮恩)**：这是一个富有创意且带有幽默感的词，常用于指代一个虚构的或夸张化的警察部门或执法机构。它通常以调侃或戏谑的方式讨论或讽刺警察的工作，充满了生动和戏谑的色彩。
