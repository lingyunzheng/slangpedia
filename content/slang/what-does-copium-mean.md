```markdown
# Copium (US English / UK English)
## US English
**Copium** is a playful slang term used to describe a strong or potent form of something, often cocaine. It can also be used humorously to exaggerate the strength or effectiveness of a situation or experience.
*Example:*
- "Man, that party was a copium of fun!"
## UK English
The term "copium" in UK English is not commonly used. The slang for a very strong or potent substance similar to the US English usage would be more likely to be "mongo" or "zoomie," but not "copium."
*Example:*
- This is a mongo of a night!
- This juice is a zoomie, truly mind-blowing!
Note: "Copium" is primarily used in US English slang and might not have a direct equivalent in UK English slang.
```
