## Copium (英式英文)
### English Explanation:
"Copium" is a playful, non-standard term used humorously to refer to a “cop cola” or a soft drink that includes caffeine, sugar, and other substances to mimic the effects of a pick-me-up or energy boost, often consumed by police officers on duty. The term is mainly used in a light-hearted or fictional context.
### 中文解释:
"Copium" 是一个充满幽默感的非正式词汇，用来指代“警察可乐”或一种含咖啡因、糖分和其他成分的软饮料，旨在模拟提神或能量提升的效果，通常被值勤的警察饮用。这个词汇主要用于轻松或虚构的语境中。
