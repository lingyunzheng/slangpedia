## Copium (俚语解释)
### 中文
“ Copium ”是一个结合了“cope”（cope本意为对抗、应对，俚语中指嗑药）和“opium”（鸦片）的合成词。这个词语通常用来形容一种通过心理或物理手段来应对压力、困难或无聊的非药物方式。比如，当一个人通过玩电子游戏或追剧来逃避现实问题，这种行为就可以被谑称为“用copium来对抗生活中的纷扰”。
### 英文
“Copium” is a portmanteau of "cope" (meaning to deal with or handle) and "opium" (a drug). This term is used to describe non-drug methods people use to deal with stress, difficulties, or boredom. For example, when someone uses gaming or watching TV as a way to avoid real-life problems, they might have been "using copium to cope with the chaos of life."
## Example Usage
### 中文示例
小王最近工作压力大，除了加班他竟然还在白天打游戏，真是“正在用copium来对抗生活中的纷扰”。
### 英文示例
Lately, Xiao Wang has been under a lot of work pressure, and instead of resting, he's been playing video games during the day. He's definitely "using copium to cope with the chaos of life."
