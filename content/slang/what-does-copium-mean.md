## Copium (English)
**Definition:** A playful and informal term often used jokingly or figuratively to refer to caffeine. 
**Usage Example:** "I couldn't stay awake during the whole meeting; the copium must have worn off." 
---
## Copium (中文)
**定义:** 一个玩味且非正式的术语，通常用来戏谑地或比喻地指代咖啡因。
**使用示例:** “整个会议我都没法保持清醒，看来 Copium 的效果已经消退了。”
