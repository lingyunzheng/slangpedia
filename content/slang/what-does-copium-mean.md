# Copium (英汉双解俚语解释)
**English:**
A playful or nonsensical term that can refer to a variety of things, often used to describe something that is not strictly defined or does not make literal sense. It can be used to show creative or humorous thinking.
**汉语:**
一个轻松或无厘头的词汇，可以指代很多东西，常用来描述那些并不严格定义或不具实际意义的事物。这个词语可以用来展示创造性的或幽默的思维。
