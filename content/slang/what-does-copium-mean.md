# Copium (English-Spanish)
## English
**Copium**: A playful, informal slang term used to refer to caffeine or other stimulants. It's a improvised form of "coffee" combined with "drug," often used in humorous or casual settings. Example: "I need some copium to get through this meeting!"
## Spanish
**Copium**: Término informal y juguetón utilizado para referirse a la cafeína o otros estimulantes. Es una forma improvisada de combinar "café" con "droga," generalmente en contextos humorísticos o informales. Ejemplo: "Necesito some copium para pasar este reunión!"
