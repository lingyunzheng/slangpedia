# Copium (Slang)
## English
**Copium**: A slang term often used to refer to a strong or intensive form of caffeine, especially in the context of energy drinks or other stimulants. It can also indicate a stimulating or intense experience, similar to how the word might be used in other contexts to denote an extremely effective or potent substance.
### Example Usage
- "I'm crashing on this Copium, so I can't focus at all."
## 中文
**Copium**：一个俚语，通常用来指代一种强效或强烈的咖啡因形式，特别是在能量饮料或其他刺激剂的背景下。这个词也可能用来表示一种极其有效或强烈的体验，与在其他情况下用来形容极强的物质相似。
### 使用例句
- "我因为Copium的影响，现在完全无法集中注意力。"
