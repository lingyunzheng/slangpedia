```markdown
## Copium (Copic Slang)
**English:** A term used informally to describe a type of synthetic cannabis. It often refers to products like "Spice" or "K2," which are sold as legal alternatives to traditional marijuana but can have similar effects.
**汉语:** “Copium” 是一种非正式用语，指的是合成大麻素的一种类型。它常指像“Spice”或“K2”这样的产品，这些产品在传统大麻被法律禁止后作为合法替代品销售，但其效果可能与大麻类似。
```
