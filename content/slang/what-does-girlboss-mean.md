# Girlboss Slang Explanation
## English
**girlboss**  
A term popularized by the podcast and book series "GirlBoss Radio" and the associated book "Girlboss" by Sophia Amoruso. It refers to a strong, self-assured, and successful woman who is the founder or leader of a business, often characterized by her entrepreneurial spirit and determination. The term is a playful blend of "girl" and "boss," celebrating women who take charge and lead in their personal and professional lives.
## 中文
**girlboss**  
这个词源于播客和书籍系列“GirlBoss Radio”及其相关书籍《女老大》（Girlboss），由Sophia Amoruso撰写。它指的是一个强大、自信且成功的女性企业家或领导者，通常以其创业精神和决断力为特点。词语结合了“girl”和“boss”的含义，庆祝那些在个人和职业生涯中把控大局的女性。
---
### 示例用法
- **English:** She’s truly a girlboss, starting her own fashion empire from scratch.
- **中文:** 她真是个girlboss，从零开始建立了自己的时尚帝国。
