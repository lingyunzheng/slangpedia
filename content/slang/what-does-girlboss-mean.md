## Girlboss
**English:** 
A term often used to describe a woman who is assertive, successful, and in control of her business endeavors or career. This slang phrase gained popularity through the TV show "Girlboss," which centered around a fictional character portraying independence and female empowerment.
**Chinese:**
用来形容一个在自己的事业或职业生涯中表现出强势、成功并拥有掌控权的女性。这个流行语源自于电视连续剧《女老板》（Girlboss），剧中讲述了虚构的女主人公如何实现独立和女性赋权的故事。
