## Girlboss
**English:** A term used to describe a confident, independent, and successful woman who runs her own business or excels in a professional setting. It often carries a tone of empowerment and female entrepreneurship.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **女孩老板 (Girlboss):** 指的是一个自信、独立且事业有成的女性，她要么经营自己的公司，要么在职业领域表现出色。这个词常带有女性赋权和创业精神的意味。
