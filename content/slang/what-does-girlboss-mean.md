## Girlboss (English/Girlboss (Chinese))
**English Explanation:**
"Girlboss" is a slang term that emerged in the twenty-first century, popularized by the eponymous Netflix series and book series. It is used to describe a confident, independent, and successful female business owner who leads her business with strength and achievements. The term is often used to celebrate women who are succeeding in male-dominated fields.
**Chinese Explanation:**
“Girlboss” 是一个在二十一世纪流行的俚语，该词因 Netflix 系列剧作和同名书籍而广为人知。它用于描述一个自信、独立且富有成就的女性企业家，她以坚强的姿态领导着自己的事业。这个词常被用来赞扬那些在男性占主导地位的领域中取得成功的职业女性。
