# Girlboss (girl boss)
**English:**
- **Definition:** A slang term referring to a confident, empowered, and successful entrepreneurial woman.
- **Example Usage:** "Sarah is the girlboss of our startup, always making decisive and innovative decisions."
**中文:**
- **释义：** 一个用来形容充满自信、强大有力且成功的女性企业家的俚语。
- **用例示例：** "莎拉是我们 startup 的 girlboss，她总是做出果断且富有创新性的决定。"
