## Girlboss
**English:**  
*Term*: Girlboss  
*Definition*: A slang term referring to a confident, powerful, and successful woman who runs her own business or dominates her field. This term emphasizes the entrepreneurial spirit and leadership qualities of women in typically male-dominated industries.
**Chinese:**  
*俚语*: 女力boss  
*定义*: 通常指拥有强大自信和领导力，经营自己的公司或在传统上由男性主导的行业中占据主导地位的成功女性。这个词强调了女性在创业和领导方面的精神与素质。
