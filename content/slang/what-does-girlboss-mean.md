# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss**: A term used to describe a confident, self-assured, and successful woman who runs her own business. It often carries a tone of empowerment and may reference female entrepreneurs who have achieved a high level of success in their field.
### Example
"Jane is not just a regular employee; she's a true girlboss, running her own startup with a team of talented women."
## 中文
**女孩老板 (Girlobǎn)/**：用来形容那些充满自信、成功经营自己生意的女性。这个词通常带着一股力量感，可能指的是那些在自己领域内取得了很高成就的女企业家。
### 示例
“简不仅是个普通员工，她简直是真正的女孩老板，用一支才华横溢的女性团队经营着自己的创业公司。”
