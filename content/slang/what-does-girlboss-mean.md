# Girlboss
## English
A colloquial term that refers to a successful, independent, and empowered woman who excels in her career and often takes on entrepreneurial ventures. The term gained significant popularity after the release of the Netflix series _Girlboss_ in 2016, which centered around the life of a fictional woman starting her own business. 
**Example:**
She's a real girlboss in the tech industry, leading her startup and setting new standards in her field.
## Chinese
一个网络流行词，用来形容在职业生涯中成功、独立且具强大影响力的女性，她常常涉足创业领域并表现出色。这个词汇自2016年Netflix系列剧《Girlboss》播出后变得广为人知，该剧中讲述了一位虚构女性创立自己公司的经历。
**例句：**
她是科技行业的真正Girlboss，率领着自己的初创公司，在业界树立了新标杆。
