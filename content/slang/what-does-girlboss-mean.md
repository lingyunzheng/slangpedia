# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss**: An informal term used to describe a strong, successful, and independent woman who runs her own business or handles aspects of life with confidence and competence. Essentially, it's a modern, empowering way to describe a women leader or entrepreneur who does things her way.
## 中文
**Girlboss**: 一个非正式的术语，用来形容一位强大、成功且独立的女性，她能够经营自己的生意，或在生活的各个方面充满自信和效率。简而言之，这是一种现代的、有力的方式，用来描述一个女性领导者或企业家，按照自己的方式行事。
