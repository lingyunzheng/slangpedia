# Girlboss (English/ girl包 (Chinese))
## English Explanation
**Girlboss**: This term refers to a female business owner or entrepreneur who is independent, confident, and successful in her career. It emphasizes the female perspective in the business world, often highlighting the determination and resilience of women in male-dominated industries.
## Chinese Explanation
**girl包**: 这个词指的是独立、自信且在职业生涯中成功的女性企业家。它突出了女性在男性主导的商业世界中的视角，往往强调女性的坚韧和决心。
## Usage Examples
- **English**: "Jessica is a true girlboss, running her own tech company from home."
- **Chinese**: "Jessica真是个girl包，自己在家开了一家科技公司。"
