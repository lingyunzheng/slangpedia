# Girlboss
**English:**  
Girlboss is a term often used to describe a confident, ambitious, and successful woman who is in charge or runs her own business with gusto. The term gained popularity with the naming of the web-based magazine and book of the same name. It suggests not letting gender hold one back in leadership roles or entrepreneurial ventures.
**中文:**  
Girlboss 是用来形容一个自信、有野心并且成功地在自己的事业或企业中占据领导地位的女性。这个词因为一本同名杂志和书籍的流行而广为人知。它传达了一种不因性别而被限制于领导角色或创业追求的信息。
