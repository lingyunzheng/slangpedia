## Girlboss
**English:**  
"Girlboss" is a slang term used to describe empowered and successful women who run their own businesses or have significant control and influence in their careers. The term gained popularity after the release of the eponymous book and HBO series in 2016, emphasizing female leadership and entrepreneurship.
**中文：**  
“Girlboss” 是一个俚语，用来形容那些充满力量和成功的女性，她们自己经营业务或者在职业上有显著的控制权和影响力。这个词汇在2016年随着同名书籍和HBO电视剧的发布而变得流行，强调了女性的领导力和创业精神。
