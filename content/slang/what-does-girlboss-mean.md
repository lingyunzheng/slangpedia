# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss**: A term that describes a woman who runs her own successful business, often characterized by confidence, independence, and a strong entrepreneurial spirit. It embraces the idea of women taking charge of their professional and personal lives, challenging traditional gender norms and expectations.
## 中文
**Girlboss**：指一位女性创业者，她经营着自己的成功事业，通常具有自信、独立和强烈的创业精神。这个词倡导女性掌握自己的职业和生活，挑战传统的性别规范和期望。
