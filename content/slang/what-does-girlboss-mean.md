# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss:** A slang term coined in the early 2010s that refers to an empowered, confident, and independent woman who is the owner or leader of a business. The term gained popularity through the book and later the Netflix show *Girl Boss* by Jesse strtola, which features stories of successful women entrepreneurs. Essentially, a girlboss is someone who embodies strength, leadership, and economic self-sufficiency.
### Example Usage:
- "Sarah is the girlboss of her startup and has already turned a profit in less than a year."
## Chinese
