# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss:** A slang term for a confident, ambitious, and successful woman who is a leader in her profession or business. This term emphasizes a no-nonsense, self-reliant attitude and often implies someone who treats career and business as a form of empowerment and self-actualization.
### Example:
Kimberly is the CEO of her own tech startup and is considered a true girlboss in the industry.
## 中文
**Girlboss:** 这是一个用来形容自信、有雄心、在职业或业务方面非常成功且充满领导力的女性的俚语。这个词强调了直率、自力更生的态度，通常意味着将事业和业务视为一种赋权和自我实现的方式。
### 示例：
Kimberly 是自己科技创业公司的 CEO，被视为业界真正的 girlboss。
