## Girlboss
- **English:** A term used to describe a young, ambitious, and often energetic businesswoman who runs her own successful company or takes charge in entrepreneurial ventures. It highlights the confidence and determination of women in the business world, challenging traditional gender roles.
- **中文:** “ girlboss” 是指一位年轻、有抱负并且往往充满活力的企业家女性，她经营自己的成功公司或在创业活动中担任领导角色。这个词强调了女性在商界的自信和决心，挑战了传统的性别角色。
---
### Usage Examples
- **English:** She's a real girlboss running her own startup in tech.
- **中文:** 她是一位真正的 girlboss，自己经营一家科技类的初创公司。
