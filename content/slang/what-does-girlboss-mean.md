## Girlboss (女性精英)
### English:
**Girlboss**: This term is used to describe a confident, empowered, and independently successful woman. Often, a girlboss is the founder or leader of a business who operates under her own terms and sets her own boundaries. The term is both a celebration of female entrepreneurship and a nod to the idea that women can lead and succeed in traditionally male-dominated fields.
### Chinese:
**girlboss (女性精英)**: 这个词用来形容一个自信、独立且在事业上成功的新女性。通常，girlboss 是一家企业的创始人或领导，并按照自己的方式经营业务，设定自己的边界。这个词是对女性创业精神的庆祝，同时也暗示女性能够在男性主导的传统领域中领导和成功。
---
*Note: The term "girlboss" is often used in a playful and empowering context, emphasizing self-reliance and agency in the feminine experience.*
