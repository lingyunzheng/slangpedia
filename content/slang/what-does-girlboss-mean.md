# "Girlboss" Slang Explanations
## English
**Girlboss**: Refers to a confident and independent woman who operates her own business or command her own ventures, often with an attitude of self-assurance and a strong entrepreneurial spirit. The term gained popularity through an early 2010s TV show called *Girl Boss* which highlighted the journey of female entrepreneurs.
## 中文
**Girlboss**: 指的是自信且独立的女性，她们经营自己的生意或掌控自己的事业，通常表现出强烈的企业家精神和自我肯定的态度。“Girlboss”这个词汇因为2010年代初的一部同名电视剧《Girl Boss》而流行起来，这部剧讲述了女性创业者的旅程。
