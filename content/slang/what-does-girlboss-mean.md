# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss**: A slang term used to describe a confident, independent, and often entrepreneurial woman who is assertive and takes charge in her personal and professional life. It's also a play on the word "boss" with a nod to female empowerment.
*Example*: "She's a real girlboss, starting her own tech company at 25!"
## 中文
**Girlboss**: 这个俚语用来形容一位自信、独立并且通常是创业者型的女性，她在个人和职业生活中展现出很强的领导力。这个词也是一个对“老板”的戏仿，带有女性赋权的意味。
*例句*: “她真是个真·girlboss，在25岁的时候就开始了自己的科技公司！”
