# Girlboss (English/美式英语)
## English
This term refers to a confident, ambitious woman who runs her own business or excels in her career, often challenging traditional gender roles. It's a self-proclaimed label for women who are strong, successful, and in control of their lives and careers.
## Chinese (美式)
这个词用来形容一个自信、有抱负的女人，她自主创业或在事业上取得突出成就，经常挑战传统的性别角色。这个词通常是指那些坚强、成功并且掌控自己生活和事业的女性。

# 姐霸 (Chinese)
## English
While not a commonly used term, "girlboss" in Chinese can be translated as "姐霸" (ji ba), which means a strong, independent, and successful woman. It’s often used to describe a confident and successful female leader or entrepreneur.
## Chinese (中文)
虽然这不是一个常用词汇，“girlboss”在中文里可以翻译为“姐霸”（ji ba），意味着一个坚强、独立且成功的女人。这个词通常用来形容自信且成功的女领导或女企业家。
