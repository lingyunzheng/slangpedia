```markdown
# Girlboss
## English
**Girlboss**: A slang term often used to describe a confident, ambitious, and successful woman who runs her own business or takes significant control over her career and life. It became popular after the release of the 2016 movie "The Girl on the Train" and the subsequent book "-girlboss rules" by Emily Automated (pseudonym).
## Chinese
**Girlboss**: 一种常用的俚语，用来形容那些自信、有抱负并且在自己事业或生活中拥有重大控制权的女性，尤其是自营事业的女性。这个词在2016年电影《列车上的女孩》（The Girl on the Train）上映后流行起来，并受到了自主创业书籍《Girlboss规则》（-girlboss rules）的推动。
```
Note: "Emily Automated" is a fictional pseudonym used in this example to represent the author of the book.
