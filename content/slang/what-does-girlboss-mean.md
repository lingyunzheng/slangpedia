## Girlboss
**English:**  
- **Definition:** A woman who operates a successful business or works in a career that she has built and lead herself.
- **Explanation:** The term "girlboss" is used to describe a female who is leading her own venture, whether that is a startup, a corporate job, or any other career path she's chosen. It often connotes confidence, independence, and the ability to take charge and succeed in one's career.
**Chinese:**  
- **定义:** 一位经营成功业务或在自己创建和领导的职业中工作的女性。
- **解释:** "girlboss" 用来指代那些自己掌舵事业或职业的女人，无论是初创公司、企业职位还是任何她选择的道路。这个词通常意指自信、独立并能在职业中取得成功。
