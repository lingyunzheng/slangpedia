# Girlboss Slang Explanation
## English
### Girlboss
**Definition:** A term used to describe a highly successful, often young, woman entrepreneur or business leader who is confident, independent, and willing to take risks to succeed.
**Example Usage:** "Emily is a true girlboss, she started her online store from her college dorm and now it's worth millions!"
## Chinese
### 女汉子/女老板 (nǚ huángzi / nǚ lǎoshāng)
**释义:** 用来形容那些年轻、成功且充满自信的女性企业家或领导者，她们不畏惧风险，敢于创业并取得成就。
**例句:** "Emily真是个女汉子/女老板，她从大学宿舍开始做网店，现在她的公司市值已达数百万美元！"
