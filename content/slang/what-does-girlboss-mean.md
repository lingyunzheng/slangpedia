# Girlboss Slang Explanation
## English
**Girlboss**: A term originally popularized by the eponymous 2015 novel and 2016 HBO series. It refers to strong and successful female entrepreneurs or business leaders. Often used to celebrate women who run their own businesses, embody leadership qualities, and challenge traditional gender roles in the workplace.
## Chinese
**girlboss**: 是一个源自2015年的小说《Girlboss》和2016年HBO同名电视剧的词汇。用来形容那些强大且成功的女性创业者或企业领导者。经常用来赞美那些经营自己的事业、具备领导力并对职场传统性别角色提出挑战的女性。
