# Girlboss
## English
"Girlboss" is a slang term that originally comes from the title of a 2014 novel by Sophia Amoruso called "Girlboss: The Not-So-Secret Diary of the Girl Who Buyers Drugs for Her Startup and Gets Addicted to Diversity." The term has since evolved into a cultural phenomenon and is used to describe a multifaceted, self-made women who is successful in both her personal and professional life, often embodying traits like ambition, confidence, and independence. A girlboss is not just a business owner but someone who is a role model, daring, and unafraid to chase her dreams.
## Chinese
“Girlboss” 是一个用于形容自信、独立、成功的企业女性的俚语，这个词源于 2014 年索菲亚·阿莫鲁索 (Sophia Amoruso) 的小说《Girlboss：女孩最强者的不那么秘密创业日记》。这个词后来演变成文化现象，用来描述那些在个人和职业生活中都非常成功，体现出雄心、自信和独立等特质的女性。Girlboss 不仅仅是指一个企业家，而是一位能成为榜样、敢于追梦的女性。
---
**Note:** "Girlboss" 在中国使用时可能会引起文化差异，需根据具体语境适当调整。
