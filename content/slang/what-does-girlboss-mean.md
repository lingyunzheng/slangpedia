# Girlboss (English/Chinese)
## English Explanation
"Girlboss" is a slang term coined by American author and entrepreneur Sophia Amoruso, who used it to describe powerful, independent female business owners. The term has since evolved to be a cultural phenomenon, embodying the spirit of strong, confident, and successful women in business leadership roles. It's often associated with female empowerment and self-employment.
## Chinese Explanation
“Girlboss”（女孩boss）是一个由美国作家兼企业家Sophia Amoruso创造的俚语。这个词用来形容强大的独立女性企业家。随着时间的推移，这一词汇已成为一种文化现象，代表着那些在商业领导角色中表现出强大、自信和成功的女性的精神。它通常与女性赋权和个人创业联系在一起。
## Usage Example
In English: "She's really the girlboss of her industry."
在中文中: 她是她行业里的girlboss。
