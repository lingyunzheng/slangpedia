## Girlboss
**English Slang:** Girlboss  
**Chinese Slang:** 女王华尔街 (Nǚwáng Jiēkuàng)
**Explanation:**
### English:
"Girlboss" is a term used to describe women who are confident, independent, and successful in their careers or businesses. It carries a spirit of empowerment and self-assertion. The term gained popularity in the early 2010s, partly due to the successful fashion brand Warby Parker, which is known for featuring an independent, successful woman on the front of their glasses ads.
### Chinese:
"女王华尔街" (Nǚwáng Jiēkuàng) 直译为“华尔街女王”，通常用来形容那些在事业或商业上独立、成功并且充满自信的女性。这个词饱含对女性力量和独立精神的赞扬。在中国，随着女性地位的提高和职场独立的提倡，这个词也逐渐流行起来，表达了对女性创业者和职业女性的赞美和鼓励。
### Example Usage:
**English:**
"I think Sarah is the girlboss of our office."
**Chinese:**
"我觉得莎拉是我们办公室的华尔街女王。"
### Etymology:
The American slang term "girlboss" began to gain popularity with the rise of feminist movements and the celebration of women's independence and success. It reflects a broader cultural shift towards recognizing and celebrating women's leadership roles.
