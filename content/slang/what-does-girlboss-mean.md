# girlboss
**英语解释：** 
A woman who is the founder, owner, or core leader of a business. She is often seen as independent, ambitious, and focused on success. The term emphasizes the idea that women can be just as capable as, if not more so, than men in leading and succeeding in business.
**中文解释：** 
指经营公司的女性创始人、老板或核心领导者。她通常被视为独立、有抱负并且专注于成功的人。这个词强调女性在经商和成功领导企业方面与男性一样甚至更出色的观点。
