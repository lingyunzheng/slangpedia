# Girlboss
**English:**  
A term for a confident, self-assured woman who runs a successful business, embodying independence and feminist empowerment. It's often used to describe powerful and influential female entrepreneurs who lead with confidence and courage.
**Chinese (Translation and Explanation):**  
女强人 (nǚ qiáng rén)  
描述的是一个自信、自立的女性企业家，她成功地经营着自己的企业，体现了独立和女权主义的精神。这个词通常用来形容那些以信心和勇气领导的有影响力和实力强大的女性创业者。
