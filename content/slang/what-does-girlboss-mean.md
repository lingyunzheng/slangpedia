# Girlboss
## English
* **Definition:** A term used to describe a confident, successful, and self-made woman, often in a business or entrepreneurial setting.
* **Example Usage:** "Emma is the girlboss of our startup, she single-handedly turned our little idea into a thriving business."
## 中文
* **定义：** 用来描述一位自信、成功且自立的女性，特别是在商业或创业环境中。
* **例句：** "艾玛是我们初创公司的girlboss，她独自将我们的小小想法发展成了一个 thriving 业务。"
