#Girlboss
## English
*zōng cháng*  
Refers to an empowered and confident woman who runs her own successful business. She is self-assured, takes calculated risks, and is often seen as a role model for other women aiming to excel in their careers and business ventures. "Girlboss" captures the essence of female entrepreneurship and leadership.
## 中文
* girlboss  
指一位充满力量、自信的女性，自己经营着一家成功的公司。她自信满满，会做出明智的风险决策，并且往往被视为其他想要在事业上和商业上取得成功女性的榜样。“Girlboss”体现了女性创业和领导的精神。
