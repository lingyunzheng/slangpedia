## Girlboss
**English:** A term used to describe an empowered and successful businesswoman who practices leadership and entrepreneurship in traditionally male-dominated fields. This phrase originated from the name of a popular web series and has since entered mainstream usage.
**Chinese:** 女BOSS (Nǚ BŌSHĬ)
- **解释:** 用来形容拥有领导力和创业精神的成功女商人，特别是在传统上由男性主导的领域。这个词起源于一部受欢迎的网络剧集，现在已经被广泛使用。
- **例句:** 她是一名真正的女BOSS，不仅经营着自己的公司，还鼓励其他女性追求事业成功。
