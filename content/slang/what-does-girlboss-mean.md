## Girlboss
**English:**  
A term often used to describe a confident, self-made, independent woman who runs her own successful business or takes charge of her career and life with determination. The term gained popularity after entrepreneur Heidi Malek wrote a memoir and launched a popular apparel line using the term.
**Chinese:**  
一个用来描述充满自信、独当一面且成功经营自己事业或以决心掌控职业生涯和生活的独立女性的俚语。这个词汇在作家Heidi Malek撰写回忆录并推出以这个名字为品牌的服饰系列后广为流传。
### Example Usage:
**English:** She's a true girlboss – always setting new goals and achieving them, no matter the challenge.
**Chinese:** 她当之无愧是girlboss——总能设立新的目标并且不管任何挑战都一定能达成它们。
