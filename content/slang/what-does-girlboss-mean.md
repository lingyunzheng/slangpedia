# Girlboss Slang Explanation
## English: Girlboss
## Chinese: 女帝 / 女霸主 (妇人霸主)
**Definition:** 
- **English:** A female entrepreneur or businessperson who operates with confidence and independence, often disrupting traditional gender roles in business.
- **Chinese:** 指是一位自信且独立的企业家或商业人士，通常打破了传统的性别角色在商业中的限制。
**Examples:**
- **English:** "Samantha is a real girlboss, running her own tech company from the get-go."
- **Chinese:** "萨曼沙真是一位女帝，从一开始就自己经营一家科技公司。"
**Etymology:** 
- **English:** This term gained prominence through the book and subsequent TV show "Girlboss" by Sophia Amoruso. It emphasizes female autonomy and entrepreneurship.
- **Chinese:** 这个词通过斯奥菲亚·阿莫鲁索(Sophia Amoruso)的《女帝》一书及其改编的同名电视剧而广为人知，强调了女性自主创业的精神。
**Usage Notes:** 
- **English:** The term is often used with admiration or envy to describe a successful, assertive, and self-made woman in the business world.
- **Chinese:** 这个词常被用来赞美或嫉妒那些在商业界自立门户、充满自信和决断力的成功女性。
