```markdown
## Girlboss
- **English**: A modern term referring to a confident, self-assured, and often successful female entrepreneur or business owner. It implies ambition, independence, and leadership in a professional setting.
- **Chinese**: 女老板 (Nǚ lǎoshàng) - 指的是自信、独立且通常非常成功的女性企业家或商业所有者。这个词表达了职业场所中的雄心、独立领导和自力更生。
This term gained popularity with the release of "Girlboss" by Sophia Amoruso in 2016, and it emphasizes female empowerment and entrepreneurial success.
```
