## Girlboss 🌟
### English
**Girlboss**: A slang term popularized by the eponymous TV show, it refers to a confident, independent, and often successful woman who runs her own business or excels in her career. Think of her as a sassy, entrepreneurial powerhouse who doesn't need anyone else to validate her success.
### Chinese
**Girlboss**: 这是一个流行于拥有同名电视剧的口头禅，指的是自信、独立，常常拥有自己的事业或在职场上表现出色的女性。想象一下一个充满魅力的女企业家，她不需要任何人来证明她的成功。
