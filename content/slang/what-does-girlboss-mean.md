### Girlboss
**English:**  
A term used to describe a successful, confident, and independent woman who runs her own business or holds a leadership position. It often implies a strong, capable female figure who has taken charge of her own life and work. Popularized by the book and subsequent Netflix series "Girl Boss" by Sophia Amoruso.
