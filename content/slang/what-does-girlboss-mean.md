## Girlboss
### English
**Girlboss**: A term that originated on the Netflix show "Girlboss" and is used to describe a woman who is confident, enterprising, and runs her own business. It's not just about making money, but also about being in control of one's personal and professional life.
### Chinese
**Girlboss**: 这个词源自网飞的剧集《Girlboss》，用来形容一位自信、有闯劲的女性企业家。这个词不仅仅意味着赚钱，更多的是指掌控自己生活和事业的能力。
