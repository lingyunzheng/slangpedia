#Girlboss
## English
"Girlboss" is a term that originated from the name of a popular podcast and memoir by Sara Blakely, the founder of Spanx. It refers to a successful, independent, and confident woman who operates her own business or is in control of her career. The term gained popularity as a way to celebrate women who have achieved financial and professional success on their own terms.
## Chinese
“Girlboss” 这个词源自一位名叫萨拉·布莱克利（Sara Blakely）的成功女性创始人，她创办了Spanx品牌。这个术语用来描述独立、自信且成功经营自己的事业或掌控自己职业生涯的女性。这个词因为鼓励女性以自己的方式实现财务和职业上的成功而广受欢迎。
