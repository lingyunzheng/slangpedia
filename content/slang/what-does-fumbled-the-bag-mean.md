# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
## English
- **Meaning**: Made a mistake or messed things up, typically in a clumsy or awkward manner.
- **Example**: "He really fumbled the bag when he accidentally left his lunch at home."
## Chinese
- **含义**: 在做某事时出错或搞砸了，通常以笨拙或尴尬的方式进行。
- **例句**: "他真的把包给搞砸了，不小心把午餐留在家里了。"
