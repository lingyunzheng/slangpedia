## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
### English Explanation:
- **Phrase:** "Fumbled the bag"
- **Meaning:** To make a mistake or have a mishap, especially a minor one that results from being distracted or careless.
- **Example Usage:** "I completely fumbled the bag when I was packing my lunch, and now that sandwich is soggy!"
### Chinese Explanation:
- **短语:** "乱了套"
- **含义:** 指因为注意力不集中或粗心大意而犯了一个小错误或产生了混乱。多用于形容一些小失误或麻烦。
- **例句:** "我在打包午餐时完全乱了套，现在三明治都湿透了！"
