## Fumbled the Bag
**English Slang:** To "fumble the bag" means to make a clumsy or awkward mistake, often in a high-stakes or important situation. It can also imply dropping the ball or blowing a crucial moment.
**中文俚语:** "Fumble the bag" 指在关键时刻犯下笨拙或尴尬的错误，通常是在重要时刻掉了链子或搞砸了关键的时刻。它也可以意味着在关键时刻“手忙脚乱”或“搞砸了”。
**Example:**
- **English:** He was about to make the biggest sale of the year, but he tripped and spilled coffee all over the important documents. He definitely fumbled the bag.
- **中文:** 他本打算做出年度最大的一笔交易，但不小心绊了一跤，把重要的文件全都洒在地上了。他真是掉链子了。
