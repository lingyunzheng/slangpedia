```markdown
## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
### English
**Fumbled the Bag:** This is a colloquial expression often used to describe a situation where someone has failed to handle a task or responsibility properly, leaving a mess or a poor outcome. It can also be used to indicate that someone handled a sensitive or important situation inadequately, leading to a negative result.
### Chinese
**摸了袋子 (mō le dàzi):** 这是一个俚语表达，常用来形容某人在处理任务或责任时出现了失误，留下了糟糕的结果，或者是在敏感或重要的情况下处理不当，导致了负面的结果。
```
In both explanations, the phrase "fumbled the bag" is slightly adapted to fit the Chinese cultural context, where "mole dazi" is used to convey the essence of the phrase.
