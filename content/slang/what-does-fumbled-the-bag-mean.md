## Fumbled the Bag
### English
"Fumbled the bag" is a slang phrase often used to describe someone who made a mistake or failed to achieve their intended goal due to clumsiness or carelessness. For example, if you were supposed to sign a contract but accidentally dropped it and it blew away, you might say you "fumbled the bag."
### 中文
“Fumbled the bag” 是一种俚语，通常用来形容由于笨手笨脚或粗心大意而导致未能达成预期目标的人。例如，如果你本应签一份合同，但却不小心将其丢掉并吹走了，你可能会说你“ fumbled the bag”。
