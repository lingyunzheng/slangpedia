# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
## English
**Fumbled the Bag**: This phrase is often used in casual contexts to describe someone who has made a mistake or messed up something that they should have handled smoothly or well. The term "fumbled" implies that the person failed to handle a situation with care, resulting in an error or a less-than-ideal outcome. "Bag" here is a metaphorical term that can refer to any situation or task that the person was responsible for.
**Example**: "When the manager asked for the financial report, John fumbled the bag and couldn't provide the correct data on time."
## Chinese (简体字)
**摸了袋子 （Mō le Dāzi）**: 这个短语通常用于口语环境中，用来形容某人在应该处理得当或很好地完成某件事时犯了错误或者搞砸了。这里的“摸了袋子”中的“袋子”是一个比喻性用词，可以指任何这个人需要负责的情况或任务。
**例子**："经理询问财务报告时，约翰摸了袋子，未能按时提供正确的数据。"
## Bilingual Note
- The phrase "fumbled the bag" is not a direct translation and is used as a metaphor in this context.
- "摸了袋子"也是比喻用语，用来描述犯错或搞砸的情况。
