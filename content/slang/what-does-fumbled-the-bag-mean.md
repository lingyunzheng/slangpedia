## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
### English Explanation
"Got caught with your pants down" or "made a silly mistake."
### Chinese Explanation
"摸到了口袋"（mō dào le kǒu tuǐ）- 比喻做了很蠢的事情，或者是被别人看到了不该看到的东西。虽然这个表达直译有些不通，但意译为“做了个愚蠢的事”或者“被抓住了把柄”，比较贴近原意。
- **Mō dào le kǒu tuǐ** - 比喻做了很蠢的事情。这也是一种形象的说法，表示因为疏忽做了一件很傻的事情，或者在不恰当的情况下被发现了。
### Context Example
- **English:** He totally fumbled the bag during the interview, mentioning a competitor's secret strategy.
- **Chinese:** 他在面试中完全摸到了口袋，提到了竞争对手的秘密策略。
### Note
- This slang is often used informally and describes situations where someone makes a foolish or embarrassing mistake, or when they are caught off guard, leading to an awkward or compromising situation.
