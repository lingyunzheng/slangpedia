```markdown
## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:** Fumbled the bag
- **Meaning:** To make a clumsy or foolish mistake, often in a situation where you are trying to be stealthy or keep something hidden.
- **Example:** "He fumbled the bag and accidentally dropped his contraband, getting caught."
**中文:** 搅袋 (Jiǎo dài)
- **含义:** 在试图保持隐秘或藏匿某物时，做出笨拙或愚蠢的错误。
- **例句:** "他搅袋时不小心掉了藏品，被抓住了。"
```
