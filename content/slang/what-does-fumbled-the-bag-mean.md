## Fumbled the Bag
### English Interpretation
"Fumbled the bag" is an informal slang expression that means making a mistake or handling something poorly, often resulting in something being dropped or misplaced. It's used to describe a situation where someone wasn't careful, leading to an undesirable outcome.
### Chinese Explanation
“摸了袋”是一个非正式的俚语表达，意为犯错误或处理事情不当，常常导致东西被掉或遗失。通常用来形容某人不够谨慎，结果导致了不理想的后果。
### Example Usage
English: "I fumbled the bag, and the takeout fell on the floor."
Chinese: "我摸了袋，外卖掉地上了。"
English: "He fumbled the bag during the presentation and forgot his important points."
Chinese: "他在演讲的时候摸了袋，把重要的内容都忘了。"
