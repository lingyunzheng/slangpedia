### Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:**
- **Definition:** To mess something up or make a mistake.
- **Context Example:** During the big presentation, John fumbled the bag and forgot his key points, leading to a rough start.
**Chinese (简体中文):**
- **定义:** 搞砸了，犯错误。
- **使用示例:** 在重要的演示中，约翰把包包弄丢了，导致忘记了几个关键点，开始得很不顺利。
