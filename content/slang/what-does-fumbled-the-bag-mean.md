## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
### English:
- **Meaning**: Made a mistake, blundered, or dropped the ball (especially in a situation where you should have succeeded).
- **Example**: "He'd been practicing for weeks, but during the big game, he completely fumbled the bag with his pass."
### Chinese:
- **意思**: 犯了错误，搞砸了，或者掉链子（尤其是在本该成功的局面下出现失误）。
- **例句**: "尽管他练习了几个星期，但在关键比赛中，他完全掉了包传球的链子。"
