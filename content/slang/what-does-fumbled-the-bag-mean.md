## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
**English:**
"Fumbled the bag" is an informal phrase that typically means someone made a mistake or blundered. It often implies that the person was neglectful or careless, leading to an awkward or problematic situation.
**Chinese (Simplified):**
“摸到包”（fú mò dào bāo）是一个非正式的短语，通常意味着某人犯了一个错误或者搞砸了。这往往暗示这个人疏忽或者粗心，导致了尴尬或有麻烦的情况。
**Actual Usage Example:**
- **English:** He totally fumbled the bag when he was supposed to organize the presentation.
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 他明明应该整理好演讲材料，结果搞得一团糟。
- **Chinese (Traditional):** 他明明應該整理好演講資料，結果搞得一團糟。
Note: This phrase might not be commonly used in everyday English, and the translation is more idiomatic to show the equivalent feeling or caution.
