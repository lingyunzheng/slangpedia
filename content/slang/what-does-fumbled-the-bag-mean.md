### Fumbled the Bag (English)
**Explanation:** This phrase is used to describe someone who has made a mistake, usually one that was preventable or that led to a difficult or unfavorable situation. It might be used when someone dropped a key thing or made a silly error.
**Example Usage:**
- "I fumbled the bag while getting off the bus, and the driver closed the door on my important documents!"
### Fumbled the Bag (Chinese)
**解释:** 这个短语用来形容某人在某个可预见或可能被避免的情况下，犯了一个错误，进而导致了不好的状况。通常是指某人不小心漏掉了东西或者做了一个愚蠢的失误。
**使用示例:**
- "我下车时把包掉了，结果司机关门时夹了我的文件！"
