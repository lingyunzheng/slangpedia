## Fumbled the Bag (Both English & Chinese)
### English Explanation:
- **Meaning**: To make a mistake, often in a noticeable or embarrassing way.
- **Example**: "He fumbled the bag during the presentation, forgetting which file he was supposed to bring."
### Chinese Explanation:
- **意思**：犯下错误，通常是明显或难堪的。
- **例句**：他演示时把包弄乱了，忘记了该拿哪个文件。
