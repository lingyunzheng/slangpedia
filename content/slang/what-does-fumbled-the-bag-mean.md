## Fumbled the Bag
**English**: "Fumbled the bag" is a slang expression that means making a mistake or handling something poorly, usually in a context where it's crucial to do so correctly. It's often used to describe a situation where someone fails to accomplish a task or deal with an important object properly.
**中文**: "Fumbled the bag" 是一个俚语，用来形容做错事或处理某件东西的方式不恰当，特别是在需要正确完成任务或妥善处理重要物品的情况下。通常用来描述一个人未能成功完成某项任务或处理重要物品的方式。
### Example Usage
- **English**: He completely fumbled the bag during the crucial part of the presentation.
- **中文**: 他在Presentation的关键部分彻底搞砸了那个包。
