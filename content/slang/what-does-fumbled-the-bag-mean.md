# Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
## English
**Fumbled the Bag**: This phrase is a playful and informal way to describe someone who made a mistake or dropped the ball (in a situation). It's often used to represent someone who failed to perform a task correctly or lost something important.
## Chinese
**掉包了 (Diào Bāo Le)**: 这个短语是一种轻松而非正式的方式来描述某人犯了一个错误或掉了重要的东西。它经常用来表示某人在某个任务上的表现不尽如人意或失去了重要的东西。
### Example Usage
- **English**: Tom fumbled the bag when he was trying to hand in his project.
- **Chinese**: 汤姆在交项目时掉包了。
