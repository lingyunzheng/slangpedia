# "Fumbled the Bag" 
## English
**Meaning:** To make a clumsy, awkward mistake. It often refers to a situation where someone incorrectly handles a task or a perceiveable issue brings to light their lack of skill in a particular area.
## Simplified Chinese
**意思:** 糜烂地犯了错误，通常是指某人在处理某事时笨拙地弄砸了，或者某种明显的错误暴露了他们在某个领域的不熟练。
## Explanation
Imagine you're reaching into your bag to grab a notebook, but instead, you accidentally spill out your lunch! That's what "fumbled the bag" means — you tried to do something simple, but you did it in a clumsy or awkward way, leading to an embarrassing or unfortunate result.
