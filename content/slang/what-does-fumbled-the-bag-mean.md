## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
**English:** Fumbled the bag  
- **Definition:** Made a mistake or dropped the ball (usually in a casual or low-pressure situation).
- **Example:** "During the group meeting, Tom fumbled the bag when explaining the project timeline, which made everyone a bit nervous."
**中文:** 糊里糊涂把包拿错了  
- **Definition:** 糊里糊涂地拿错了包，通常用来形容在轻松或非正式的情境中犯了错误或搞砸了事情。
- **例句:** "在小组会议上，汤姆糊里糊涂把包拿错了，解释项目进度时出了些状况，让大家感到有些紧张。"
