## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
### English
**Definition:** To mess up or drop something awkwardly.
**Example Usage:** "He totally fumbled the bag when he tried to hand it to his friend, dropping half of the contents all over the floor."
### Chinese
**定义:** 拂乱或笨拙地弄丢或掉落某物。
**例句使用:** "当他试图把包递给朋友时，不小心把包弄乱了，结果把里面的东西都掉了一地。"
