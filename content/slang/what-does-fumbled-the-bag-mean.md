# "Fumbled the Bag"
## English
"Fumbled the bag" is an informal phrase, often used when someone tries to covertly take something that doesn't belong to them, but ends up tripping over, becoming obvious, or getting caught in the process. It's a humorous way to describe an attempt at theft or cheating that goes awry.
### Example:
- _"I tried to fumble the bag at the cash register, but the cashier was too sharp and got suspicious right away!"_
## 中文
“Fumbled the bag” 是一个非正式的表达，通常用来形容某人试图秘密地拿走不属于自己的东西，但最终由于失误或公开而失败的情况。这是一种幽默的方式来描述试图偷窃或作弊但过程失败的情形。
### 示例：
- _“我试图在收银台附近 fumble the bag，但收银员太灵了，立刻起了疑心！”_
