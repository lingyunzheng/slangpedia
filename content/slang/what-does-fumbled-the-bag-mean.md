# Fumbled the Bag
## English
**Meaning:** Made a mistake or blundered in a situation, often leading to an undesirable outcome.
**Example:** "He fumbled the bag when he was supposed to hand it to the teacher, and now he's in trouble."
## Chinese
**意思:** 在某种情境下犯了错误或搞砸了事情，通常会导致不理想的结果。
**例句:** "他在应该把书包递给老师的时候搞砸了，现在麻烦上身了。"
