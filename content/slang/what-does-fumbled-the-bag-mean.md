# "Fumbled the Bag"
## English
It means you clumsily dropped or mishandled a bag, whether it's dropping it, spilling its contents, or losing its grip on it. 
Example Usage:
- "I was rushing to catch the bus and totally fumbled the bag, dropping my phone too."
## Chinese
这个词组的意思是你笨拙地掉了手提包，无论是掉了、倒出了包里的东西，还是没拿稳包。 
使用示例：
- "我赶公交太急了，结果把包都掉了，手机也摔了。"
