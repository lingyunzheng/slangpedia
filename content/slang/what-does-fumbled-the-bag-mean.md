## "Fumbled the Bag"
### English
- **Definition**: Made a mistake or failed to handle a situation properly.
- **Example**: During the job interview, John fumbled the bag and spilled his resume, which made a bad impression.
### Chinese
- **解释**: 在处理某事时犯了错误或表现不佳。
- **例句**：在求职面试中，约翰把包弄丢了，简历都洒了，给人留下了不好印象。
