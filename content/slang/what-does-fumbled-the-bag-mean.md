# Fumbled the Bag
**English:**  
"Fumbled the bag" is an informal expression that usually means someone made a mistake or dropped the ball. It can be used in various contexts where someone mishandles a situation or task. The "bag" part is often just a placeholder, symbolizing whatever was involved in the failed attempt.
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
“Fumbled the bag” 是一个非正式的表达，通常意味着某人犯了错误或搞砸了。它在各种情况下都可以使用，指某人在处理某事或任务时出现失误或搞砸了。“bag”在这里通常只是一个占位符，象征着当事人在失败过程中涉及的具体情况或事情。
