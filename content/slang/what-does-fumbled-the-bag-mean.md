## Fumbled the Bag

### English
**Fumbled the Bag:** This expression is used when someone mishandled or accidentally dropped a bag, often resulting in its contents being spilled or scattered. It can also be used figuratively to describe a situation where something went wrong, especially in a clumsy or poorly managed way.

**Example:** "John fumbled the bag at the doorstep, and half of the groceries fell on the floor."

### Chinese
**扭了袋子 (niu le daizi):** 这个表达用来形容某人不小心拿袋子时弄掉了或者洒掉了袋子中的东西。也可以用来比喻某事进行得不成功，尤其是在处理得不精准或管理不当的情况下。

**例句:** "约翰把袋子拿错了，所有的杂货掉到了地上。"
