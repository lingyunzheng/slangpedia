## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
### English Slang: "Fumbled the Bag"
- **Meaning**: Made a big mistake or blunder in a situation, particularly one where it's important to be on top of your game.
- **Example Usage**: "John fumbled the bag during the crucial meeting, leading to a major misunderstanding with the clients."
### Chinese Slang: "摸了袋子" (mó le bā袋)
- **Literal Translation**: "摸了袋子" literally translates to "touched the bag," but in slang, it means making a significant mistake.
- **Usage**: “在重要的会议上， JACK 摸了袋子，导致跟客户之间产生了很大的误会。”
- **Translation**: "在重要的会议上，JACK 摸了袋子，导致跟客户之间产生了很大的误会。” translates to "In the crucial meeting, JACK made a big mistake, leading to a major misunderstanding with the clients."
### Context
Both phrases are used in situations where someone has made a significant error that could be critical to the outcome of a situation. They imply a lack of competence or attention to detail at a crucial moment.
