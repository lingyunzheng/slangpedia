## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
**English:** Fumbled the bag
- **Meaning:** To make a mistake or drop something in a clumsy or careless way. Often used to describe a situation where someone mishandles a package or a bag, causing it to be dropped or emptied.
- **Example:** "Oh no, I fumbled the bag and all the coins spilled out!"
**中文:** 糊弄袋子
- **含义:** 指在粗心或不谨慎的情况下犯错误或弄丢东西。常用来形容某人不小心弄丢或掉包里的东西。
- **例句:** "哎呀，我糊弄袋子，结果硬币都洒出来了！"
