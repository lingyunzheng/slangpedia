## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fumbled the Bag
- **English**: "Fumbled the Bag"
- **Chinese**: 糊弄包 (hù nòng bāo)
### Explanation
- **English**: This phrase generally refers to someone who has made a serious mistake or mishandled an important task, often when they themselves are trying to claim they handled everything perfectly. It's as if they accidentally dropped a bag they were telling everyone they were carefully carrying, symbolizing a fumble or miscalculation.
- **Chinese**: 这个短语通常用来描述某人在处理一个重要任务时出现严重失误的情况，而且他们自己还试图掩饰这个失误。就像他们不小心把一个正在声称自己小心携带的包给弄丢了，用来比喻搞砸或者搞错了。
### Example Sentences
- **English**: Tom fumbled the bag when explaining the project to the board, which led to significant revisions.
- **Chinese**: 汤姆在向董事会解释项目时糊弄包，导致进行了大量修改。
