#### English: Fumbled the Bag
- **Meaning**: Made a mistake or messed something up.
**Example**: During the big interview, I fumbled the bag and couldn't answer a crucial question, which really fumbled the bag for me.
#### 中文: 掉链子
- **Meaning**: 做事出现了失误或失败，表现不佳。
**例句**：在重要的面试中，我掉链子，没能回答上那个关键的问题，这真的让我在面试中表现不佳。
