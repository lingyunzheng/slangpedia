# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
## English
- **Definition**: To handle something awkwardly or make a mistake while trying to do so.
- **Example Use**: "He really fumbled the bag when he tried to grab it from the shelf."
## Chinese
- **定义**：在试图处理某物时笨拙或犯错。
- **例句**："他在试图从架子上拿袋子时真是手忙脚乱。"
