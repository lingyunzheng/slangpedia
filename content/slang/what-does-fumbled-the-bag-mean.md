## Fumbled the Bag 🏫💼
### English
- **Meaning**: Made a mistake or an error.
- **Usage**: If you "fumbled the bag," you probably dropped it or couldn't figure out how to open it, leading to a bit of a mess. This phrase can also be used more broadly to describe any kind of screw-up or botched situation.
### Pinyin and Chinese Example
- **拼音**: 搅和了包
- **例子**: 我在好朋友婚礼上致辞时紧张得手忙脚乱，确实把讲稿搅和了包，真尴尬！
- **Translation**: During my friend's wedding, I was so nervous that I stumbled with the speech notes, really embarrassing!
### Usage Context
- This phrase is often used in casual conversations, especially when people are talking about situations where they felt clumsy or made a mistake, but in an amusing or slightly exaggerated way.
