# Fumbled the Bag (Fumbled the Bag)
## English
**Fumbled the Bag**: This phrase is often used when someone has made a complete dud or blunder, similar to dropping the ball or letting it slip through their fingers. It's a humorous way to describe a moment where someone has failed at a task or missed an opportunity.
*Example*: "He really fumbled the bag in that job interview. He forgot all the important points and the interviewer was unimpressed."
## Chinese
**摸了一把空 (Mōle yí bǎ kōng)**: 这个短语通常用来形容某人完全搞砸或失败，类似于掉球或者让它从指缝中溜走。它是以一种幽默的方式来描述一个人在完成某项任务或错过机会时的失败。
*例句*: "他在那次面试中完全摸了一把空。他在面试中记不住所有重要的点，面试官对他也不满意。"
