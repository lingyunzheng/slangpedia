## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:** "Fumbled the bag" is a slang expression used to describe an awkward, clumsy, or messy situation.
**Chinese:** “摸糊涂了包”是一个俚语表达，用来描述尴尬、笨拙或一团糟的情况。
- **Literal Translation:**
- "Fumbled the bag" -> "摸糊涂了包"
- "Fumbled" -> "摸糊涂了" (literally "touched and was confused")
- "the bag" -> "包" (bāo)
- **Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I really fumbled the bag when I promised to organize the event, and now it's a total mess."
- **Chinese:** "我答应要组织这个活动时真是摸糊涂了包，现在一团糟了。"
- **Context:**
- This phrase is often used in casual conversation to comment on a situation where someone did not handle something properly, leading to negative outcomes.
