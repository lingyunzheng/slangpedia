## Fumbled the Bag
**English:** 
"Fumbled the bag" is an expression used to describe a situation where someone handles a bag or package in a clumsy or awkward manner, often leading to its contents becoming disorganized or spilled out. Similar to dropping or knocking over a bag, this phrase conveys a sense of mishandling or failing to manage the bag properly.
**中文:** 
“Fumbled the bag” 是一个用来形容某人笨拙地处理一个袋子或包裹，导致其内物排列混乱或散落一地的情况。这个表达包含了未能妥善管理袋子的感觉，类似于把袋子弄掉了或撞翻了。
