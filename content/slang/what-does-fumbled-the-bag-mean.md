# Fumbled the Bag (Chinese: 打了包不怕)
**English Meaning:** Made a mess of something, messed up a situation.
**Explanation:** Originally from American slang, "fumbled the bag" is used to describe a situation where someone made a mistake or mishandled a task, or when a plan didn't work out as intended. In Chinese, it is colloquially referred to as "打了包不怕" (dǎ le bāo bù pà), which literally translates to "after bagging, no fear," suggesting that no matter what happens afterward, the main mistake or issue has already occurred.
**Usage Example:**
- English: John fumbled the bag when he accidentally revealed the secret to the other team.
- Chinese: 约翰在不经意间把秘密泄露给了对方球队，这就叫“打了包不怕”。
---
In both languages, the concept emphasizes that the main problem has already occurred and cannot be undone, similar to the feeling of regret but also acknowledging that consequences have already been set in motion.
