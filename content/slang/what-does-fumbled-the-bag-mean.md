## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:** To botch or mess up something, often when you're trying to handle it.
**Chinese:** 在处理某件事情时搞砸了或搞乱了。
### Example Usage:
1. **English:** I totally fumbled the bag when I was trying to hand in my resume.
**Chinese:** 我在交简历的时候把袋子弄乱了。
2. **English:** Don’t fumble the bag on an important presentation!
**Chinese:** 在重要演示时可不要把事情搞砸了！
### Additional Context:
- Often used in contexts where someone is handling something delicate or sensitive and fails.
- Can also be used metaphorically to refer to any situation where one makes a mistake or overlooks something important.
