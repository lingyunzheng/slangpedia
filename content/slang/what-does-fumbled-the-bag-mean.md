## Fumbled the Bag (English)
### Explanation
"Fumbled the bag" is a slang term used to describe a situation where someone has made a embarrassing mistake, usually in a moment of misadventure or clumsiness. It's commonly used when someone fails to perform a task correctly, often leading to a humorous or awkward outcome.
### Example
- **Context**: A chef in the kitchen accidentally drops a tray of delicate dishes.
- **Slang**: "The chef totally fumbled the bag with that tray—now he's covered in broken glass!"
## Fumbled the Bag (Chinese)
### 解释
“Fumbled the bag”是一个俚语，用来描述一个人在某个时刻不小心弄糟了某个事情，通常是因为笨拙或失误而导致尴尬或滑稽的结局。这个词常用来形容某人在执行某项任务时犯了错误。
### 示例
- **情境**：一个厨师在厨房里不小心把一盘精致的餐具掉到了地上。
- **俚语**："这个厨师在那个盘子上完全 fumble 了（弄糟了），现在满地都是碎玻璃。"
