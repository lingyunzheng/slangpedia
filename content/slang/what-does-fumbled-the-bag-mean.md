## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:** 
"Fumbled the bag" is a casual phrase used to describe someone who handled a situation clumsily or made a mess of it. It often implies that the person was attempting to do something with a bag or purse, but didn't quite manage to do it properly, resulting in a fumble or spill.
**Simplified Chinese:**
“摸了袋子”用来形容某人处理事情时表现得很笨拙，或者把事情弄得一团糟。这个词通常用来描述一个人想用袋子或钱包做某事，但最终没有成功，导致了失误或物品撒落。
**Literal Translation:**
"摸了袋子" = "Fumbled the bag"
**Example Usage:**
- English: "Jack completely fumbled the bag and spilled his coffee all over the floor!" 
- Simplified Chinese: "杰克完全摸了袋子，把咖啡洒了一地！"
