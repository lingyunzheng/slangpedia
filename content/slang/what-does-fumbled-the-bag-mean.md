# Fumbled the Bag
## English
- **Meaning**: Made a mistake or botched a job.
- **Example**: "He totally fumbled the bag when he missed that critical pass in the last minute of the game."
## Chinese
- **意思**: 做错了事情或者搞砸了一件工作。
- **例句**： "他在比赛的最后一分钟漏过了一次关键传球，完全搞砸了机会。"
