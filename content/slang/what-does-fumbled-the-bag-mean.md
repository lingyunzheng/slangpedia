### Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:**  
- **Meaning:** To make a clumsy or awkward mistake, especially in a situation where you should have been more careful.
- **Example:** "He fumbled the bag, dropping the important documents on the floor."
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
- **释义：** 在本应更加小心的情况下，笨拙或尴尬地犯了错误。
- **例句：** "他在本应更加小心的情况下，笨拙地把包掉在了地上，把重要的文件弄丢了。"
