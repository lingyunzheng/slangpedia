```markdown
## Fumbled the Bag
**English:**  
"Fumbled the bag" is an informal phrase used to describe a situation where someone mishandles or drops something unexpectedly, usually in a clumsy or careless manner. 
**Chinese:**  
"fumbled the bag" 意指某人在处理或拿着某物时表现出极其笨拙或不小心，通常会导致东西被掉落或弄乱。
Example:
- English: He fumbled the bag, and the keys fell out on the floor.
- Chinese: 他把包弄丢了，结果钥匙掉在了地上。
Translation:
- The keys fell out on the floor.  
- 钥匙掉在了地上。
```
