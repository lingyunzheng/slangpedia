Here's the bilingual slang explanation in Markdown format:
```markdown
# Fumbled the Bag
## In English
- **Definition**: Made a mistake or mishandled something, often unintentionally.
- **Example**: "I fumbled the bag" means someone dropped or messily handled a bag.
## In Chinese
- **定义**：犯错误或不小心处理了某事，往往是无意的。
- **例句**："我 fumbled the bag" 意味着某人不慎掉了或不熟练地拿着一个包。
```
