```markdown
# "Fumbled the Bag" - Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:** To fumble the bag typically means to mess up or handle something poorly, often in a high-stakes or public situation. It refers to an embarrassing or clumsy mistake.
*Example:* During the job interview, John fumbled the bag when he tried to hand it over to the receptionist, nearly spilling its contents on her desk.
**Chinese:** “摸了一手好牌”（读作 “fú mă de hăo pái”）通常指的是在重要或公开的情况下处理事情不当，往往伴随着尴尬或笨拙的失误。
*例句:* 在求职面试中，约翰在递给接待处女士的时候，摸了一手好牌，差点把包里的东西都洒到她的桌子上。
```
Note: The Chinese translation provided is a bit of a play on "fumbled the bag" to make it sound like a phrase that people might actually use, as the literal meaning might not translate well. The intended meaning is more about handling things poorly.
