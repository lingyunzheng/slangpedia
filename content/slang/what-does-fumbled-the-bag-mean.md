## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fumbled the Bag"
**English:** 
- **Definition:** To handle something awkwardly, typically dropping or mishandling it. In this context, "fumbled the bag" can mean dropping or misplacing a bag, usually in a public or embarrassing manner.
- **Usage Example:** "Alex fumbled the bag on the crowded subway, causing his groceries to spill all over the floor."
**Chinese:**
- **释义:** 处理某件事情很笨拙，通常指的是不小心掉东西或者弄丢东西。在这个语境下，“fumbled the bag”可以指在公共场合不小心掉了包，或者是从包里什么东西掉了出来。
- **使用示例:** "艾莉克斯在拥挤的地铁上没把持住自己的包，结果把满满一袋子的杂货都洒在了地上。"
