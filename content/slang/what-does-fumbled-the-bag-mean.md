## Fumbled the Bag 🛍️💪
**English**: It means you dropped the ball, messed up, or failed at something you were supposed to do.
**Example**: "I was supposed to pick up the kids from school, but I fumbled the bag and forgot about it."
**Spanish**: Significa que fallaste, te equivocaste, o hiciste un落ち，失去了某样东西，未能完成应该完成的事情。
**Ejemplo**: "Tenía que llevar a los niños a la escuela, pero fui un desastre con la mochila y lo olvidé."
---
**注释**: "Fumbled the bag" 和"失落了某样东西" 都表示由于粗心或疏忽导致未能完成某事或丢掉了某样东西。
