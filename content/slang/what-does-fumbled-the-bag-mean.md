## Fumbled the Bag
### English
"Fumbled the bag" is a slang phrase that typically refers to accidentally dropping or mishandling something that looks like a bag, such as a wallet or purse, often implying a bit of clumsiness or distraction.
### Chinese
“Fumbled the bag” 是一个俚语，通常用来形容不小心掉了或者处理不当看起来像袋子的东西，比如钱包或手提包。这往往暗示了一点笨手笨脚或者分心。
---
Example Usage in a Sentence:
- **English:** He definitely fumbled the bag when he got out of the car; now I'm not sure where his wallet is.
- **Chinese:** 当他下车时肯定 fumbled the bag；现在我不确定他的钱包在哪里了。
