# Sigma (Sigma)
## 英文解释
**Sigma** is a popular slang term often used to refer to someone who engages in the specific type of insulting behavior on the internet known as "sigma trolling" or "sigma behavior." Sigma characters tend to play devil's advocate and escalate conflicts with passive-aggressive and awkward responses, often to the annoyance of others.
