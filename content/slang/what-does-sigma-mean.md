## Sigma (σ)
### English
**Meaning:** Sigma is often used in a colloquial sense to refer to a skilled or knowledgeable individual, usually in a specific field. It can also denote a person who is highly adaptable and competent.
**Example:** "That guy is a sigma; he can handle anything thrown his way."
### 中文
**含义:** 在俚语中，sigma常用来指代某个在特定领域非常有技能或知识渊博的人。也可以指一个人极具适应力和能胜任各种任务。
**例句:** "这家伙是个sigma，什么都能搞定。"
