# Sigma (Σ)
## English
**Definition:** Sigma typically refers to the Greek letter 'Σ', which is used to denote summation in mathematics. In some modern slang, it can also refer to an individual who is very methodical or detail-oriented, almost to a fault.
**Example Usage:**
- "He's so sigma about his work that even the smallest mistake can throw him off for days."
- "The project manager is super sigma, making sure every aspect of the project is perfect."
