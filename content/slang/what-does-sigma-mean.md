## Sigma (Σ) 🌐
### English
**Sigma**: In a broader slang context, particularly among gamers and tech enthusiasts, "Sigma" often refers to a player who is considered "sigma." A sigma player is one who consistently makes unpredictable or poorly-thought-out choices, leading to frustrating outcomes. Think of it as the opposite of "stable" or "predictable" players. Sigma behavior can refer to anything from excruciatingly slow play to sudden over-aggressive moves without clear strategy.
### 中文
**西格玛 (Sigma)**：在更广泛的俚语中，特别是在游戏玩家和技术爱好者之间，“西格玛”（sigma）通常用来形容一种“不稳定”或“不可预测”的玩家。西格玛玩家指的是那些经常做出难以预料或思考不周的行为的玩家，这会导致对方感到沮丧。西格玛行为可以是从极其缓慢的游戏方式到突然的过度激进而缺乏明确战略等任何行为。
