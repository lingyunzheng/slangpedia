## Sigma (Sigma)
- **English:** Sigma is a letter in the Greek alphabet often used in statistics and mathematics to represent standard deviation. In slang, it can also refer to someone or something that is extremely dominant or dominant in a particular field or context. For example, a player might be called "sigma" if they are dominating a video game match.
- **Chinese:** "sigma" 在俚语中通常指的是在某个领域或情境中极其优秀或主导的存在。例如，在电子竞技中，一个表现极其出色的玩家可能会被称为“sigma”。这个称号强调的是超凡脱俗的水平和显著的影响力。
