# Sigma (σ)
## English
**"Sigma-nirvana"** - A mythical state of perfect optimization or efficiency, often used in tech discussions. Imagine a world where everything runs smoothly and flawlessly, with no room for error. Sigma-nirvana is the promise land where systems are so well-oiled that they operate without any inefficiencies or bugs.
## 中文
**“赛默凡纳”** - 一种完美的优化或高效的理想状态，经常在技术讨论中提及。想象一个完美无瑕、运行无阻的世界，其中没有错误或bug。赛默凡纳是这样的境界，在这里系统运行得极其流畅，没有任何低效或缺陷。
