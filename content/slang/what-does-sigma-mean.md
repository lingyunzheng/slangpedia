## Sigma in English
### Definition
Sigma is a term used in financial markets and data analysis to represent standard deviation, a measure of variability or volatility.
### Slang Explanation
**Sigma (σ)**: In trader circles, Sigma is slang for "standard deviation". It's a quick way to describe the volatility of a financial asset. High Sigma means high volatility, while Low Sigma means steady performance.
## Sigma in Chinese
### 定义
Sigma 是金融市场上和数据分析中用来表示标准差的一个术语，它是衡量波动性和波动程度的一个量度。
### 成语解释
**Sigma (σ)**：在交易者圈子里，“Sigma”是“标准差”的俚语。这是快速描述金融资产波动性的方法。高位Sigma表示高波动性，低位Sigma表示稳定表现。
