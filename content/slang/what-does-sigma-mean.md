# Sigma (σ)
## English
### Meaning
- **Math Symbol**: Often used in statistics to denote standard deviation, which measures how spread out numbers are from the mean.
### Slang Explanations
- **In Data Science Circles**: Sigma can refer to someone who is extremely detail-oriented and pays close attention to statistics and data analysis.
- **In Investment**: Sigma might describe a professional who is a master of analyzing market trends and financial data to make informed investment decisions.
## Chinese
### 含义
- **数学符号**：通常用于统计学中表示标准差，衡量数值分布的离散程度。
### 脑残用法
- **在数据科学圈**：sigma 可能找到的义是那种极其注重细节且全神贯注于数据和分析的人。
- **在投资领域**：sigma 可能描述的是那位精通分析市场趋势和金融数据以做出明智投资决策的专业人士。
