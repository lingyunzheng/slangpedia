# Sigma (σ)
## English
**Meaning:** In statistics, sigma (σ) often represents standard deviation, which measures the amount of variation or dispersion in a set of values. In a more colloquial context, it might be used to denote something that is standard or expected—basically, "the norm."
## 中文
**含义:** 在统计学中，sigma (σ) 通常表示标准差，用来衡量一组值的变异或分散程度。在更俚语的用法中，它可能指的是标准的、常规的情况——就是“.orm”之意。
