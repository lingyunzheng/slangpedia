## Sigma (Σ)
### English
**Sigma**: In formal contexts, Sigma is a mathematical symbol representing the sum of a sequence of numbers. In slang, Sigma primarily refers to a specific character from the anime *Steins;Gate* known for his calculated and cold approach to problem-solving. The term is often used to describe someone who is calculated, methodical, and potentially uncaring.
### 中文
**Sigma (Σ)**：在正式语境中，Sigma 是一个数学符号，代表一系列数字的总和。在 slang 中，Sigma 主要指动漫《steins;gate》中的一个角色，为人处事极其理性冷静。这个称谓常用来形容那种行事缜密、冷酷、甚至是缺乏人情味的人。
