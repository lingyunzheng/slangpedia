# Sigma (σ)
## English Slang
In casual English, **sigma** is sometimes used to describe someone or something that is highly skilled or proficient in a specific area. It can be seen as a more direct or intense way to describe someone as a "pro" or "expert". For example:
- "He's a sigma player, definitely top-notch at strategy games."
## Chinese Slang
在中文俚语中，“sigma”有时用来形容某人或某事物在特定领域非常熟练或精通。它可能更直接或强烈地表示某人是一“专家”或“高手”。例如：
- “他可是 sigma 级别的玩家，策略游戏里绝对的高手。”
