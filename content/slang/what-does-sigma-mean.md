## Sigma (Σ)
### English
**Meaning:** In statistics, sigma (Σ) represents the summation of a series of numbers. But in a more colloquial sense, it is often used to describe someone or something that is "cool" or "awesome."
### Chinese
**意思:** 在统计学中，sigma (Σ) 代表一系列数字的总和。但在俚语中，它经常用来描述某个人或某件事是“酷”或“ awesome”。简单来说，当某人或某件事特别棒或者特别有型的时候，可以用“sigma”来形容。
