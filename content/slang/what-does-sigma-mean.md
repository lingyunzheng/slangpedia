# Sigma (σ)
## English Explanation
Sigma (σ) is not typically used as slang in English, but it is a term that has a specific meaning in statistics and mathematics. However, in more casual contexts, it can sometimes be used to describe something that is minimal or slightly present. The term is often borrowed from Greek or mathematical contexts in a non-technical way.
## Chinese Explanation
西格马 (Xiégémǎ, pronounced "sigma") 通常并不是英文俚语，它的数学和统计学意义是特定的。但在一些非正式的语境中，西格马可以用来形容某件事情的存在感很低，或者只是轻微的存在。这个术语经常被从希腊或者数学领域借用来以非技术的方式使用。
