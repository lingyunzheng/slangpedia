# Sigma (σ)
## English Explanation
In a more informal sense, "sigma" can refer to a personal or professional individual who is highly skilled in a specific field. This term is often used in tech or hacker communities to describe someone with advanced knowledge and capabilities, especially in programming, cybersecurity, or data analysis.
## Bilingual Slang Explanation
**Sigma (σ)**  
- **English:** A highly skilled and knowledgeable person, especially in tech fields like coding, cybersecurity, or data analysis.
- **中文:** 一个在技术领域（如编程、网络安全或数据分析）非常熟练和有知识的人。
