# Sigma (σ) — 塞 GAL (Sigma Gal)
## English
**Sigma (σ)**: 
- A programming concept that can refer to an iterator in a loop or a symbol used in mathematics to denote a sum. In slang, it is sometimes used as a team or group name, often in a tech or gaming context.
## Chinese (简体中文)
**塞 GAL (Sigma Gal)**:
- 在编程中，sigma (σ) 可以指一个循环中的迭代器，或者在数学中表示求和的符号。在SLANG中，它有时被用作团队或小组的名字，特别是在科技或游戏领域。
