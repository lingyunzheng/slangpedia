# Sigma
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: In formal contexts, sigma (σ or Σ) is the 18th letter of the Greek alphabet and is used in mathematics to denote summation or a large standard deviation in statistics.
- **Slang Usage**: In informal or tech-savvy communities, "sigma" is often used to describe someone or something that is exceptionally good, reliable, or stable. It can also refer to a high level of precision or consistency.
- **Example**: "He's a sigma player on the team, always reliable and consistent."
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义**：在正式的语境中，sigma (σ 或 Σ) 是希腊字母表的第 18 个字母，在数学中常用来表示求和或统计中的标准偏差。
- **俚语用法**：在非正式或技术性较强的圈子里，“sigma”通常用来描述某个人或某事物非常优秀、可靠或稳定，也可以指代极高精度或一致性。
- **例句**："他是队里的sigma选手，总是可靠且稳定。"
