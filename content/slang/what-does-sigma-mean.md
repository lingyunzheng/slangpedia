## Sigma (Σ)

### English
**Meaning and Usage:** In technical contexts, "sigma" is used in statistics to denote the standard deviation. However, in slang, it refers to a person or situation that is frustratingly complicated or annoyingly intricate. People often use it to describe a scenario where they're dealing with a lot of unnecessary details or dealing with someone who is overly complex.

**Examples:**
- "Ugh, this project is a sigma mess, we need to clean it up."

### 中文
**含义和用法：** 在技术上下文中，“sigma”表示标准差。然而，在网络用语中，它用来形容一个令人烦恼地复杂或者令人厌烦地繁琐的情况。人们常常用来形容自己处理大量不必要的细节，或者处理一个过于复杂的人或事物。

**例句：**
- “啊，这个项目一团sigma，我们需要整理一下。”
