## English - 中文
### **ate (吃了)**
- **Meaning**: Refers to consuming food or having a meal.
- **Usage**: Used to describe the act of eating something.
- **Example**: "I ate a big steak for dinner." - "我吃了顿大牛排的晚饭。"
- **Slang Usage**: Often used informally to discuss meals or food consumption.
- **Example**: "What did you eat for lunch?" - "你午饭吃了什么？"
### **ate (压垮)**
- **Meaning**: Slang term that means something is extremely burdensome or overwhelming.
- **Usage**: Used to express that something has left one feeling exhausted or defeated.
- **Example**: "They worked so hard, the project ate them." - "他们工作很努力，项目差点把他们压垮了。"
- **Slang Usage**: Often used in contexts where one feels overwhelmed.
- **Example**: "His debt is starting to eat him." - "他的债务正在压垮他。"
### **ate (玩够了, 哥们)**
- **Meaning**: Slang term that means someone has been having a really good time with friends.
- **Usage**: Used to express that someone has thoroughly enjoyed themselves with friends.
- **Example**: "After the party, they were so relaxed and ate." - "聚会结束后，他们玩得很高兴，感觉很放松。"
- **Slang Usage**: Often used in contexts of fun and enjoyment with friends.
- **Example**: "Let's eat and play until we're all ate." - "我们尽情玩直到都玩够了。"
### **ate (烂了补, 蛋疼的补)**
- **Meaning**: Slang term that means something is in such bad condition that it needs repairs or attention.
- **Usage**: Used descriptively to indicate something is broken or ineffective.
- **Example**: "My computer just ate, I need a new one." - "我的电脑用坏了，我得换一个了。"
- **Slang Usage**: Often used in technical or general context when something needs to be fixed.
- **Example**: "My phone ate and nothing works." - "我的手机脏了，什么功能都用不了。"
