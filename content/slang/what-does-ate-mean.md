# "Ate" (英文: Ate)
## 中文 slang: “吃了老本” (Eaten Old Savings)
- **Explanation**: 
- **中文**: 这个词组常用来形容那些依靠过去的积累或过去的资本而不是新的努力或技能的人。例如，一个演员可能会趁着年轻时积累下来的好评或名气“吃了老本”，不再积极接新戏，依赖过去的业绩维持生计。
- **English**: This phrase is commonly used to describe someone who relies on past积累 or past capital rather than new efforts or skills. For example, an actor might "eat their old savings" by relying on the good reviews or reputation they have built up in the past, without actively seeking out new roles.
## 中文 slang: “光吃不拉” (Eatch But Not Defecate)
- **Explanation**: 
- **中文**: 这个生动的表达方式通常形容那些只享受不付出，只花费不储蓄的人。用于讽刺那些挥霍无度，没有责任感，只图一己私利的人。
- **English**: This vivid expression is often used to describe someone who only enjoys without making any effort, only spends without saving. It is used to criticize people who are wasteful, undutiful, and only care about their own selfish interests.
## 中文 slang: “吃饱撑的” (Eaten till Filled and Fell Asleep)
- **Explanation**: 
- **中文**: 这句话用来形容那些无所事事的人，或者形容做一些毫无意义或不切实际的事情。常用于表示某人做了无聊或愚蠢的事情。
- **English**: This phrase is used to describe someone who has nothing to do, or to describe doing something meaningless or unrealistic. It is often used to ridicule someone who did a boring or foolish thing.
## 中文 slang: “喝西北风” (Drink North-West Wind)
- **Explanation**: 
- **中文**: 类比于英文的“starving”，形容极度饥饿或贫困的状态，有时也用来形容无业或无法维持生计的人。
- **English**: This phrase is a metaphor for extreme hunger or poverty and is sometimes used to describe those who are unemployed or cannot maintain a livelihood.
