# English Slang: Ate
**Meaning:**
- "Ate" is primarily used as a humorous slang abbreviation for "ate" (past tense of eat). However, it can also be used sarcastically or colloquially to describe having consumed something, often leading to a humorous or exaggerated reaction.
**Example Usage:**
- "I ate like a bear before the hibernation!" (used when someone has eaten an unusually large amount of food)
**Chinese Slang:**
- "吃光了" (Chī Guāng Liǎo): This phrase literally means "ate until there was nothing left," and it is often used to describe someone who has eaten a lot of food.
- "中奖了" (Zhōng Jiǎng Le): In a colloquial way, "中奖了" can also mean "ate like a champion," often said in a teasing or humorous manner when someone has consumed a huge amount of food.
**Example Usage in Chinese:**
- "我今天吃了好多，感觉自己中奖了。" (Wǒ jīntiān chīle hěnhao, gǎnjué de zěnme jǐng fù zhōng jiǎng le.) - "I ate so much today, feeling like I won a prize."
