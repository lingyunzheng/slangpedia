## English: Ate
### Slang Explanation
- **Meaning**: To eat a large or unusual amount of food.
- **Example**: "Man, I ate like a bird at the diner last night!" (I ate a very small amount of food.)
---
## Pinyin: Ate (Āi)
### 成语/俚语解释
- **意思**: 吃很多或者不寻常的食物。
- **例句**: "Man, I ate like a bird at the diner last night!" (昨晚我在饭店吃得像一只饥肠辘辘的鸟。)
