## Ate (中文 slang: 阿太 / 英文 slang: Grandma)
**中文 slang: 阿太**
- **定义**: 通常用来形容某个女性长辈，带有亲切和尊敬的意味。
- **使用场景**: 在家庭聚会或家族活动中，大家往往会提及“阿太”，用来表示对长辈的敬爱和关怀。
**英文 slang: Grandma**
- **Definition**: Refers to a female senior family member, usually used with a sense of affection and respect.
- **Usage Context**: In family gatherings or events, people often mention "Grandma" to express their love and respect for their elders.
