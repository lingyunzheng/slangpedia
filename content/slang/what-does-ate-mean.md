# "Ate" Slang Explanation
## English
**Ate**: This slang term can mean "ate shit" or "made a mess," suggesting that someone has done something clumsy, foolish, or has failed miserably in a way that would leave a sour taste in their mouth. It's often used as a humorous backhanded compliment or to point out someone's mistake.
## 中文
**Ate**: 这个俚语的意思通常是指“搞砸了”或“弄个一塌糊涂”，表示某人做了一件很笨拙、愚蠢的事情，或者失败得让人感到很不舒服。它常常被用作一种带有讽刺意味的恭维或者用来指出别人的错误。
