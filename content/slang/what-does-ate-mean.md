```markdown
### 肚子好饱啊 (肚子好饱啊 / dǔzi hǎo bǎo ā)
- **English:** "I'm totally stuffed, man!"
- **Explanation:** This phrase is used to express extreme fullness after eating a large meal. It's a colloquial way of saying you've eaten so much that you feel extremely satisfied or even uncomfortable from the amount of food.
---
### 吃了一肚子的苦力 (吃了一肚子的苦力 / chī le yī bù zhī de kǔ lì)
- **English:** "Got a belly full of hard work!"
- **Explanation:** This phrase means you have undertaken a lot of work or have been working really hard. It's a way to describe the physical and mental burden of a labor-intensive task.
```
