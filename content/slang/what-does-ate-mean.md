## Ate
**English:**  
A slang term used to refer to one's mother. It's commonly used in casual and informal settings, often as a way to show affection or familiarity. It can also be used as a form of address among female teens or friends.
**中文:**  
一个俚语，用来指代自己的妈妈。通常用于非正式和休闲的场合，可以表达亲密或亲近感。也经常用于少女之间或朋友之间作为称呼。
