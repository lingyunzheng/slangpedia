```markdown
## Ate (Chinese: 阿叔 / 姐姐)
- **Chinese**: 
- **Literal Meaning**: 爷爷 (Grandpa) / 奶奶 (Grandma)
- **Slang Use**: A term used to address elderly men as "Uncle Ate" (阿叔) or elderly women as "Aunt Ate" (阿姨 or 姐姐). It's a respectful yet casual way to refer to them, often implying a longer-term or familiar relationship rather than a blood relative.
- **English**:
- **Literal Meaning**: Uncle / Aunt
- **Slang Use**: Used similarly to refer to older male or female adults who are not one's direct relatives but are familiar or have a special relationship. It is often used in a friendly, respectful manner, especially in东亚文化 (East Asian cultures) where such relationships are highly valued.
```
