# "Ate" in Slang (English-Chinese)
## English
- **Ate**: Intuitively, "ate" might seem like an odd slang term, but it's commonly used to describe a situation where someone or something left a powerful, impressive, or impactful impression. It's often paired with an adjective to denote the quality of the impression.
**Example**: "That new rapper really ate last night, totally blew everyone away with his stage presence!"
## Chinese
- **吃了 (Chī le)**: 在俚语中，“吃了”常用来形容某人或某事留下了深刻、强烈或令人印象深刻的印象。它通常会和一个形容词搭配使用，以表示这种印象的质量。
**例句**: "那个新崛起的说唱歌手昨晚真吃了，他的舞台表现力完全征服了所有人！"
