# "Ate" in Slang
## English
**Meaning:** To eat or consume.
**Explanation:** Common slang term for eating or consuming, often used in casual or informal settings. It can also be used to refer to a large meal or overeating.
**Example:** "I ate a huge pizza last night!"
## 中文
**意思:** 吃或消耗。
**解释:** 常见俚语，用于表示吃或消耗，多用于非正式或随意的场合。也可以用来指吃大量的食物或者暴饮暴食。
**例句:** "我昨晚吃了一大块比萨！"
