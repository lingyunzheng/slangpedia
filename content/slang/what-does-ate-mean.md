# "Ate" in Chinese and English
## Chinese (简体中文)
**吃货 (Chīhuò)**: 
- **Explanation**: 通常用来形容喜欢吃、喜欢尝试新口味和食物的这个人或这类人。这个词在中文网络用语中非常流行，用来描述那些对美食有着强烈兴趣的人。
- **Example in Sentence**: 他真是个吃货，每次一吃完饭就跑到小门诊吃小吃。
## English
**Foodie**:
- **Explanation**: Used to describe someone who has a strong interest in food, especially trying different kinds and flavors. This term is widely used in the English-speaking online community and culture to refer to individuals who appreciate and enjoy food.
- **Example in Sentence**: She's really a foodie and can't wait to explore the new restaurant in town.
