### ate
**English:** 
1. **Eatin' -** This slang term is often used to describe someone who is extremely good at something, particularly in a way that suggests they could win every time. For example, "He's eatin' at basketball, man. He's untouchable!" Here, "eatin'" implies that this person is so proficient and skilled that they can "eat" the competition for breakfast.
2. **Clean Eatin' -** This phrase is used to describe a diet or food that is very nutritious and healthy. For example, "She's been focusing on clean eatin' to stay in shape for the next marathon." It's a more positive and health-conscious use of the term.
**Chinese:**
1. **吃老本 (Chī lǎo běn) -** 这个俚语用来形容某人非常擅长或擅长某事，通常意为空吃老本（用之前积累的才能或资本），表示一个人在某方面非常无敌，比如：“他在篮球上吃老本，无可比拟！”这里“吃老本”意味着这个人非常熟练和厉害，以至于能够轻易地胜过对手。
2. **吃素 (Chī sù) -** 这是描述一种健康饮食，主要指吃非常健康、营养的食物。例如：“为了接下来的比赛，她一直在吃素以保持体型。”这是一种更积极、更注重健康饮食的表达。
