## Ate (English: Bitch; Filipino: Ate)
- **Meaning**: Depending on the context, "Ate" can be a colloquial term with a double meaning. It can function as a term of endearment used to address female friends or relatives, similar to "sister." However, it can also be used derogatorily to insult or taunt.
- **Usage**:
- **Positive**: "Ate, you're looking beautiful today!" (in this case, it's a term of endearment)
- **Negative**: "You ate does not know how to do it!" (when it's used as an insult)
- **Examples**:
- Positive: "Ay, ate, let's go shopping together this weekend!"
- Negative: "You ate thinks she's so smart, but she's just arrogant."

## Bitch (English)
- **Meaning**: A derogatory term used to insult or taunt a person, typically a woman.
- **Usage**: Often used when someone is perceived as being difficult, aggressive, or toxic.
- **Examples**:
- "Why are you being such a bitch today?"
- "You really pissed me off, you bitch!"
