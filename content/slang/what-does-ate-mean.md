## Ate (English)
### Definition
- **Elder Sister**: A slang term often used in casual settings, particularly in East Asian cultures, to address an older female friend or relative.
### Explanation
Ate is a familiar and affectionate term, similar to "big sister" or "aunt" in English. It can be used with friends, family, or community members who are older than you. It conveys a sense of respect and close relationship.
## Ate (Chinese)
### Definition
- **姐姐 (jiějie)**: 一个用于称呼比自己年长一些的女性，特别是在中文网络用语中，用来表示亲近的女性朋友或亲戚。
### 解释
Ate 是一个亲昵且尊敬的称呼，类似于英语中的“大 sister”或“aunt”。它经常用在朋友、家人或社群中年龄比自己大的女性身上。这个词表达了尊敬和亲密的关系。
