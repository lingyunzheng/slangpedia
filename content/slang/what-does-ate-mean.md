## Ate (Chinese 艾特, English: Ate)
### Chinese
- **Definition**: 在中文网络语境中，"艾特"（拼音：ài tè）通常是指腾讯QQ社交软件中的“@提及”功能。当用户想要引用或提及某个QQ好友时，会在对方的用户名前面加上“@”符号，这种标注方式被称为“艾特”。
- **Usage**: 例如，小明在QQ群中评论时，想要让林晓知道这条消息，他会写下“@林晓，你好！” 来提及林晓。
- **Equivalent Slang in English**: In Chinese internet slang, "Ate" refers to the "mention" or "@tag" feature used in Tencent QQ. It is the act of tagging a friend's username in a QQ chat group or discussion to draw their attention.
### English
- **Definition**: In English internet slang, "Ate" is an informal and playful way to refer to the act of tagging or mentioning someone by their username, typically on social media platforms or messaging apps. It’s often used to get someone's attention within a conversation.
- **Usage Example**: John might write, "Hey @Sara, I need your help with this!" to get Sara's attention in a Facebook chat group.
- **Equivalent Slang in Chinese**: 在英语网络用语中，“Ate”是一种非正式且玩味的方式，指的是在社交媒体平台或消息应用中通过用户名指名某人。通常它用于在对话中吸引某人的注意。
