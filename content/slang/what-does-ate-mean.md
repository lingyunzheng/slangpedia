# "Ate" Slang Explanations
## English
- **Meaning:** To eat something, often in a casual or slang way.
- **Example Sentence:** I ate a whole pizza by myself!
## Chinese
- **含义:** 吃某东西，通常以一种随意或俚语的方式。
- **例句:** 我一个人吃了一整个比萨饼！
