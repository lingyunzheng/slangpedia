### ate
**English:** 
- *Slang:* Ate - This term is often used informally to indicate that someone has defeated, overwhelmed, or thoroughly outperformed another person in a contest, challenge, or competition. It can be used in various contexts, whether it's a sports game, an academic test, or any form of competition.
**Chinese (普通话):**
- *俚语:* 胜了 - "胜了" 用来非正式地表示某人在比赛、挑战或竞赛中彻底战胜了对手。它可以用于各种情境，无论是体育比赛、学术考试还是任何形式的竞赛。
- *例子:*
- English: "Our team ate their team last night's game." 
- Chinese: "昨晚的比赛，我们的队伍把他们的队伍彻底打败了。"
- English: "She ate the math test with a 98% score."
- Chinese: "她数学考试得了98分，把考试彻底碾压了。"
