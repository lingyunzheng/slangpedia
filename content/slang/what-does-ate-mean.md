# Ate
**English:** 
- "Ate" can be a casual term for "father" in some English-speaking regions, often used as a term of endearment or familiarity among family members.
**Chinese:**  
- "Ate"（爸爸）: 在某些英语地区，"ate"指代的是“爸爸”。它通常被家庭成员之间用作亲密或亲昵的称呼。
