## Ate (Chinese: 阿姐 / 阿姐儿; pinyin: ājiě / ājiěr)
### Chinese
**Meaning:** A term used to address an older sister, often with a hint of respect and family warmth.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:** "Ate" can be slang to refer to a close female friend who is older or considered a big sister figure. It's casual and friendly.
### English
**Meaning:** Slang term for a close female friend, often describing someone older or someone you consider as a "big sister" within your circle of friends. It conveys a sense of respect and closeness.
