# Dogwater
## Chinese: 狗皮膏药 (Gǒu pí gāo yào)
- **English Slang Explanation:** It's a tricky or deceptive remedy that doesn't work, much like something advertised on the internet that sounds too good to be true but doesn't deliver. Just as "dog water" would be ineffective for quenching a dog's thirst, these are methods or claims that are not beneficial or effective.
## English: Lemonade Stand (originally "water")
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:** 这就像开柠檬水摊一样 (Zhè xiàng 如 jiě kāi nǎ mxī shuǐ tāng yíyàng)，指那些看似有前途但实际上没什么用处的人或方法。就像卖柠檬水的摊子只能吸引有限的顾客，这样的东西或方法只能吸引很少的注意或者帮助。
- **English Slang Explanation:** It refers to something that initially sounds promising but doesn't live up to expectations, just like setting up a lemonade stand (which, in its literal form, doesn't make much money). Dogwater is a type of such a remedy or claim that is ineffective, much like a lemonade stand that doesn't attract many customers and thus isn't very profitable.
