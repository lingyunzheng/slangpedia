## Dogwater (狗水)
**English:** Dogwater  
**Chinese:** 狗水 (Gǒu shuǐ)
### Explanation:
**English:**  
In some English-speaking regions, "dogwater" is a slang term that means terribly bad, subpar, or something of extremely low quality. It's often used to describe food, drinks, or experiences that are so bad that they are barely worth mentioning.
**Chinese:**  
"狗水" is a playful or colloquial term in Chinese referring to water that is so bad that it's almost not fit for a dog to drink, implying it's incredibly bad, unsuitable, or of very low quality. This term is often used in jest or to emphasize that something is of such poor quality that it’s even worse than water. 
Usage Example in a Sentence:
- **English:** "This tourist trap doesn't serve dogwater, does it?"  
- **Chinese:** "这都不会卖狗水了吧？"
