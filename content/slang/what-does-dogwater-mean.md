# Dogwater: Slang Explanation
## English
### Definition
Dogwater is an informal term used to describe anything that is subpar, unimportant, or of poor quality.
### Usage Example
"Winning that competition was just dogwater, no real challenge for me."
## 中文
### 释义
“狗水”是一个非正式的用语，用来形容任何不 important，质量差或者毫无意义的事物。
### 使用示例
“赢得那场比赛简直就是狗水，对我来说毫无挑战性。”
