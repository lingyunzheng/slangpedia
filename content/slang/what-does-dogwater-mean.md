# Dogwater (狗水)
**English**: Dogwater can be used informally to describe something that is of poor quality or not very useful. It often implies that whatever is being referred to is essentially useless or not worth much.
**Chinese**: 狗水 (gǒu shuǐ) 通常用来形容质量很差或没有用处的东西。它常常暗指某物基本上是无用的，或者不值得太多考虑。
Example:
- English: "This computer is dogwater, it broke down again."
- Chinese: "这部电脑真是狗水，又坏了。"
