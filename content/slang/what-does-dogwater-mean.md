# Dogwater
**English:**  
"Dogwater" is a slang term often used to describe low-quality or worthless items or situations. It's derived from the idea that dirty, tainted water is not fit for human consumption and is akin to dog water, which is of such low quality that it is typically not even consumed by dogs.
**中文：**  
"狗水"（dogwater）是一个俚语，常用来形容低质量或毫无价值的东西或情况。这个说法来源于一个比喻，认为脏兮兮的、被污染的水不适宜人类饮用，而狗水更是低质量到连狗都不愿意喝。因此，这个词常常用来形容非常不好的东西。
