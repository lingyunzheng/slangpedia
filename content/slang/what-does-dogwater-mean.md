## Dogwater
### English
"Dogwater" is a slang term often used to describe water that is of low quality, dirty, or unsuitable for consumption. It can also be used figuratively to describe a situation or environment that is considered bad or undesirable.
### Chinese
“狗喝水” (Gǒu kànshuǐ) 是一个俚语，用来形容水质差、脏或者不适合饮用的水。这个词汇也可以比喻形容糟糕或不理想的状况或环境。
