### 中文 slang: 狗水
#### English slang: Dogwater
**狗水 (gǒu shuǐ) - Dogwater:**
- **Meaning:** In slang, "狗水" is a humorous and informal way to refer to tap water or plain water lacking in flavor or excitement. It's often used to describe anything that is considered ordinary, boring, or unremarkable.
- **Usage Example in Chinese:** "这个调料放了狗水，完全没有味道！" (Zhè gè tiáo liào fàng le gǒu shuǐ, wú féi yǒu wèi dào!)
- **Translation:** "I added dogwater to this seasoning, it has no flavor at all!"
- **Usage Example in English:** "Dogwater of the tap is nothing special to me, I prefer bottled water for its subtle flavors."
