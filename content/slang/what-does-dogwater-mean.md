## Dogwater
**English:** Dogwater
- *Definition:* A dismissive term used to refer to something of low quality or no value.
- *Example Usage:* "This week's special at the restaurant? Dogwater if you ask me."
**中文:** 狗水
- *定义:* 一种贬义词，用来形容质量极差或毫无价值的东西。
- *例句:* "餐厅这周的特别优惠？对我来说就是狗水。"
