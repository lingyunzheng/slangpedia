# Dogwater (英语: Dogwater)
## English Explanation
"Dogwater" is a slang term often used to describe something of very low quality, typically in reference to water that is unpotable or extremely dirty. It can also be used more generally to describe anything that is considered worthless, inferior, or not fit for use. 
Origin: The term is creatively derived from "dog" (implying it's of no value to even a dog) and "water," emphasizing the idea that the substance is not even fit to quench one's thirst. 
Example: "The juice he bought was just dogwater; not even worth the gallon container."
## 中文解释
“狗水”是一个俚语，用来形容质量极低的东西，通常指不适合饮用或极其脏的水。这个词也可以泛指任何被认为毫无价值、劣等或不适合使用的东西。
起源：“狗水”这个词巧妙地结合了“狗”（暗示价值低于狗的需求）和“水”，强调这种物质连解渴都不值得。
例句：“他买的果汁简直就是狗水；连买个加仑装都不值。”
