# Dogwater
## English
"Dogwater" is a slang term that is used to describe something of very poor quality or low value. The term is derived from the idea that if water is so dirty that even a dog would refuse to drink it, then it's extremely unpleasant.
## Chinese
“狗屎水（狗水）” 这个网络流行语用来形容非常糟糕或者没有任何价值的东西。这个词来源于如果水脏到连狗都不愿意喝的程度，那就说明水已经极其难喝了。
