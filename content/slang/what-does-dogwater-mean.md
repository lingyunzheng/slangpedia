# Dogwater (英语: Dogwater)
## English Explanation
- **Dogwater**: A slang term often used to describe something of very poor quality or value. It can also be used to refer to something that is dull, lame, or uninteresting. For example, "This movie is dogwater. I couldn't even finish it."
## 中文解释
- **狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ)**: 这是一个俚语，通常用来形容质量非常差或毫无价值的事物。它还可以用来指代单调乏味、无趣或不值得一看的东西。例如，“这部电影真是狗水，我都没法看完它。”
