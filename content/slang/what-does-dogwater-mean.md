### dogwater (Chinese: 狗水; pinyin: gǒu shuǐ)
- **English Slang:**throwing money down the drain
- **Explanation:** Refers to wasted money that is spent frivolously or on something of little or no value. It's like giving "dog water" (worthless water) when high-quality water is needed. This phrase is used when someone spends money on something that doesn't yield any significant or lasting benefit, as if they are flushing valuable resources down the toilet.
Example Usage in a Sentence:
- "He bought that fancy phone just to have a new app? It's like dogwater!"
```
### dogwater (Chinese: 狗水; pinyin: gǒu shuǐ)
- **English Slang:**useless effort
- **Explanation:** Describes an action or activity that is pointless, ineffective, or without any significant outcomes. It's akin to expending energy on something that doesn't produce any results, much akin to drinking "dog water," which offers no hydration or nourishment. This term is used to ridicule actions that waste time, effort, or resources without achieving desired goals.
Example Usage in a Sentence:
- "Going to that useless seminar was really dogwater; I could have stayed home and done something productive instead."
