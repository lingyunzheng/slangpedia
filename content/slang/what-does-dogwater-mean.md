# Dogwater
## English
**Dogwater**: A term used humorously or disparagingly to refer to something of poor quality or no value. It implies something is worthless or just barely meeting the bare minimum standard.
## Chinese
**狗水 (gǒu shuǐ)**: 一种用于幽默或贬义地指称没有价值或完全不值得的东西的俚语。这表明某物是无价值的，或者仅仅达到了最低标准。
Example Usage:
- English: "You just gave me dogwater when I was expecting something really good."
- Chinese: "你给我倒的狗水，我可是期待着好东西的。"
