## Dogwater (English/Slang)
### English
- **Definition**: A term used to describe water that has been contaminated or of poor quality. It can also refer to something that is not as valuable or desirable as what it was initially perceived to be.
- **Example**: "The juice from that expired bottle is dogwater. Don't drink it!"
### Bilingual Slang
- **中文释义**: 用来描述被污染或水质差的水，也可以指那些不如人预期值珍贵或有吸引力的东西。
- **例句**: “那瓶过期饮料里的东西根本就是狗水，别喝了！”
### Additional Notes
- The term "dogwater" often carries a negative connotation and is used to express disappointment or disgust with something of low quality.
