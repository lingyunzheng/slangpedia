### dogwater (English)
**Definition:** A term often used to describe something that is of low quality or not worth the price. It can also be slang for watered-down or diluted substances.
**Example Sentence:** This juice is just dogwater; it tastes like there's barely any fruit in it!
### 狗水 (Chinese)
**Definition:** 指质量差或者不值那个价钱的东西，也可以用于形容被稀释或淡化了的物质。
**例句:** 这果汁就是狗水，تداول竟然主要都还不是真正的果汁！
