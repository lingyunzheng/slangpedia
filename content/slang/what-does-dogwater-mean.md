### Dogwater (English)
**Meaning:**  
A term used to describe something that is stale, old, or no longer fresh. It can also be used to describe a situation that is old news or not relevant anymore.
**Example:**
- "I still get those emails about the company's launch in 2010, but for the rest of the team, it's dogwater now."
### 狗水 (Pinyin: gǒu shuǐ) (Mandarin Chinese) 
**Meaning:**  
用来形容某事物已经过时、不再新鲜或已经不再重要。也可以用于形容某消息已经是旧闻，不再具有相关性。
**Example:**
- “公司2010年发布的那些邮件对我们来说已经变成了狗水，其余团队成员对此已经不再关注。”
