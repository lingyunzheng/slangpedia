# Dogwater Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Dogwater**: Informal slang term often used to refer to **second-rate**, **subpar**, or **inferior** quality of something, usually in a disparaging manner. It can also be used to describe people or situations that are considered not good enough or of low quality.
- *Example Usage*: "The coffee at this place is dogwater. I can't believe they charge so much for such low-quality brew."
## 中文
**狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ)**: 常用的俚语，通常用于形容某样东西的质量非常差或者很差劲，多带有贬义。这个词也可以用来形容质量不高的事物、人或情况。
- *例句*: "这里的咖啡质量很差，就像是狗水。我简直不敢相信他们能以这样的价格出售这种低质量的饮品。"
