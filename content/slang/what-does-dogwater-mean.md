# Dogwater (English/Chinese)
**English:**  
"Dogwater" is a slang term that refers to an undrinkable, very dirty or polluted water. It might also be used to describe something as worthless or of very low quality. It's often used in informal or derogatory contexts.
**Chinese:**  
"狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ)"  
指的是非常脏或受污染到无法饮用的水。有时也用来形容某物毫无价值或质量极差。通常用于非正式或贬义的语境中。
