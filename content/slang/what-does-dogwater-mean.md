## Dogwater (English)
**Definition:**  
The term "dogwater" refers to something of extremely poor quality or tasting very bad. It's a colloquial expression used to describe things that are rather undesirable or lacking in value.
**Example Usage:**
- "This coffee is dogwater. I can't drink it."
## 狗水 (Gǒushuǐ) (Chinese)
**Definition:**  
"狗水"（Gǒushuǐ）是一个俚语，用来形容质量极差或非常难喝的东西。这是一种口语化的表达方式，用于描述那些相当不好或者没有价值的东西。
**例句使用:**
- "这杯咖啡是狗水，我喝不下。"
