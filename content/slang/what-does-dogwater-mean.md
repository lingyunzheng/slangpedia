# Dogwater
## 中文解释:
狗水
## 英文解释:
Dogwater
**中文含义:** 狗水通常是指水质不好，水里含有杂质或其他不干净的东西。它也可以用来形容某事物或情况恶劣到像不干净的水一样，通常包含贬义，表示不干净、不好或低劣。
**英文含义:** "Dogwater" is often used to describe water that is of poor quality, containing impurities or other unsanitary elements. It can also be used to describe something or a situation that is so bad it's compared to unclean water, typically carries a negative connotation of being dirty, bad, or of low quality.
