## dogwater
### English Slang Explanation
"Dogwater" is a slang term that refers to unremarkable or poor-quality water. It's often used to describe anything that is subpar, uninteresting, or of low quality. The term is playful and sometimes offensive, depending on the context.
### Chinese Slang 解释
"狗水 (gǒu shuǐ)" 是一种俚语，用来形容糟糕或质量低劣的水。它通常指任何不值得一提、无趣或质量差的事物。这个词在使用时比较随意，有时也可能带有一些侮辱性。
