## dogwater
**English Explanation:**
"Dogwater" is an informal slang term often used to describe something of low quality, questionable value, or something that is considered dirty or inferior. For example, it might be used to describe a poor quality drink or a place that is not well-maintained.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"狗水" 是一种非正式的俚语，常用来形容质量低劣、价值可疑或被认为肮脏或 inferior 的东西。例如，它可能被用来形容质量差的饮料或环境不佳的地方。
