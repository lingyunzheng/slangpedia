# Dogwater Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Dogwater**
- **Meaning:** Slang term often used to describe something of low quality or value, typically in a derogatory manner.
- **Example usage:** "This pizza is just dogwater; I wish I had ordered somewhere else."
## 中文
**狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ)**
- **意思:** 常用来形容质量低劣或价值不高的事物，通常带有贬义。
- **例句:** "这比萨简直是狗水，真希望能在网上订别的地方的。"
