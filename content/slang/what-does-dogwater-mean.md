# Dogwater
## English
### Explanation
"Dogwater" is a colloquial slang term used to refer to anything that is of very poor quality, ineffective, or completely worthless. It often is used to mock or criticize anything perceived as being of low standard or lacking in value.
### Example
- This new app is just dogwater; it doesn't have any useful features.
