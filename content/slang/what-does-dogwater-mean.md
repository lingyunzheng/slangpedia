# Dogwater Slang Explanation
## English
**Dogwater:** A slang term used to describe something that's not particularly valuable or useful. It can also refer to a contemptuous or derogatory term for someone or something that is perceived as having no real worth or as a mere waste.
- **Example Usage:** "This old car is just dogwater, it's going to cost more to fix than it's worth."
## 中文
**狗水 (Gǒu shuǐ):** 这是一个俚语，用来形容不特别有价值或有用的物品。也可以用来侮辱性地指某人或某事物被认为是没有真正价值或只是浪费的东西。
- **例句使用:** "这辆旧车简直是狗水，修理它的费用比它的价值还高。"
