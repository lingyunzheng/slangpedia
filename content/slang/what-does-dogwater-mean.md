### dogwater (英: dogwater)
#### Chinese
**狗喝的水 (Gǒu hē de shuǐ)**
- **Explanation:** 形容非常脏、不干净的水，通常是负面评价水质的情况。比如，某地的水源被污染了，人们形象地称之为“狗喝的水”，暗示这水连狗都不愿意喝。
- **Usage Example:** 居民们抱怨自来水变得越来越差，说那简直像“狗喝的水”。
#### English
**Dog Water**
- **Explanation:** Used to describe extremely dirty or clearly unfit-for-human-use water. It's usually a negative term used when referring to water that is too polluted or just plain unsafe to drink. For instance, when a water source gets so contaminated that it's deemed unacceptable for human consumption, it might be referred to as "dog water," implying that even dogs wouldn’t be willing to drink it.
- **Usage Example:** The residents complained that the tap water has become dirtier and dirtier, to the point where they jokingly call it "dog water."
