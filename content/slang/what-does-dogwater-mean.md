## Dogwater (狗水) - 中文/Chinese
**Dogwater** (狗水, Gǒu Shuǐ) is a slang term used in some regions in mainland China, especially in Guangdong. It refers to drinking water that is not clean or potable. In colloquial usage, the term often carries a negative connotation, indicating poor water quality.
**Bilingual Explanation**:
- **English**: Dogwater refers to the quality of water that is unclean or unsafe to drink, often used to describe the poor water conditions resulting from neglected or poorly maintained plumbing or water systems.
- **Chinese**: 狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ) 指的是一种不干净、不适合饮用的水质，通常用来形容由于管道维护不善或供水系统不完善导致的水质差。
## Dogwater (狗水) - 中文/Chinese
**Dogwater** in some Chinese-speaking regions, particularly in areas like Hong Kong and Guangdong, refers to water that is far from being drinkable; it symbolizes extremely poor water quality. The term is casual and often implies a high level of disgust or disdain for the water in question.
**Bilingual Explanation**:
- **English**: Dogwater refers to water that is of very poor quality and is not suited for drinking, often used to describe a situation where the water is highly contaminated or unclean.
- **Chinese**: 狗水 (Gǒu Shuǐ) 指的是非常不适合饮用的水，通常用来描述水质极其差的情况，含有非常高认为的负面意味。
