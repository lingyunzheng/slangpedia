## Dogwater (English)
**Meaning:** Dogwater is slang often used to refer to cheap or low-quality alcohol. It's also sometimes used to describe something that is considered worthless, subpar, or a ripoff.
**Example:** "I can't believe I spent $20 on that bottle of ‘dogwater’—it was so bad, I couldn't even finish it!"
## 狗水 (Dogwater - Chinese)
**Meaning:** 狗水 (gǒushuǐ) 是俚语，通常用来指质量低劣或廉价的酒精饮料。有时它也被用来形容某物被认为是无价值的、次品或欺诈的东西。
**例句:** "我真不敢相信，花了20块钱买那一瓶‘狗水’，那味道简直让我没法继续喝下去！"
