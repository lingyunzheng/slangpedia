# Dogwater (Chinese: 狗水; Pinyin: gǒu shuǐ)
## English Slang Explanation
"Dogwater" is a term used to describe a very unappetizing or low-quality liquid. It is often used when referring to water that is so bad-tasting or unclean that it's essentially useless.
### Example Usage
- "I can only hope the water at this place isn’t dogwater; otherwise, it’s a good thing we brought our own."
## Chinese Slang Explanation
“狗水” (gǒu shuǐ) 用来形容非常不好喝或者不干净的液体。这个词通常用于形容水质差到几乎无法饮用的情况。
### 例句
- “我希望这家店里的水不是狗水，不然我们还是自带的水比较好。”
