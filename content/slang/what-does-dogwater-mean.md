```markdown
## dogwater (English)
### Meaning
Dog water is a term used to refer to plain, unflavored, or boring water. It is often used in a derogatory manner to describe something that is considered tasteless or uninteresting.
### Example Usage
- "His presentation was dog water; nothing exciting or innovative."
## 狗 Water (Chinese)
### 意思
"狗水" 是个俚语，用来形容普通、无味或无聊的水。经常用于贬义，形容某事或某物缺乏趣味或吸引力。
### 举例使用
- "他的演讲就像白开水，没有任何令人兴奋或创新的元素。"
```
In both English and Chinese, "dog water" implies something bland or unremarkable.
