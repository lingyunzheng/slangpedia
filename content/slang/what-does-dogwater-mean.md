## dogwater
### English
*dogwater*: This term typically refers to water that has been used once or is of questionable cleanliness. It can also be used metaphorically to describe something that is not very valuable or useful, often discarded or considered low-quality.
### Chinese
*狗水 (gǒu shuǐ)*: 这个词通常指的是用过一次或者很有可能不干净的水。也可以用来比喻一些不值钱或没用的东西，通常会被丢弃或认为是质量低下的东西。
