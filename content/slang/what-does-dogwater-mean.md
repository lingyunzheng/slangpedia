```markdown
## Dogwater (俚语解释)
**中文：** 狗水
- **释义：** 用来形容非常糟糕或劣质的东西，类似的还有“狗屎”、“狗料”。经常被用于评价质量差的产品或环境恶劣的场所。
**英文：** Dogwater
- **Definition:** Used to describe something extremely poor quality or terrible, similar to "dog poop" or "dog chow". It is frequently used to evaluate products of low quality or environments that are恶劣的。
- **Example:** "This company's customer service is dogwater; I wouldn't recommend them to anyone."
```
注意：这里的“恶劣的”在英文中被翻译为“awful”，但更准确的翻译应为“poor quality”或“terrible”，以保持与“dogwater”和“狗水”的贬义相同。
