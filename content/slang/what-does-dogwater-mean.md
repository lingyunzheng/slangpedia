## Dogwater
**English:** Dogwater is a slang term used to refer to low-quality, flavorless, or undesirable water. It's often used to describe water that is not clean or pleasant to drink, or to compare it negatively against better or tastier water.
**中文:** "狗水" 是一个俚语，用来形容质量低劣、没有味道或不讨喜的水。常用来指水质不好或不适宜饮用的水，也可以用来将水质与更佳或更美味的水作负面比较。
**Example Usage:**
- English: I can't believe you wanted to drink that dogwater! There's plenty of clean water available.
- 中文：你居然想喝那狗水！明明有干净的水可以喝。
