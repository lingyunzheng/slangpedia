# dogwater (狗水)
## Simplified Chinese: 狗水
### English Explanation:
- **Context:** Primarily used in internet slang or among younger generations.
- **Meaning:** Refers to water of low quality or containing impurities, such as urine or other pollutants. It's often used metaphorically to describe something that is subpar, dirty, or of very poor quality.
- **Example Usage:** 
- "This river has become dogwater due to pollution." - Describing a body of water that is heavily contaminated.
- "He drank some dogwater after washing cups with dirty water." - Someone drinking very impure water by mistake.
## Simplified Chinese Usage Context:
- 通常在网络用语或年轻人中使用。
- 指低质量的水或包含污染物的水，如尿液或其他污染物。
- 常常比喻形容事物是劣质的、不洁净的或非常差的。
