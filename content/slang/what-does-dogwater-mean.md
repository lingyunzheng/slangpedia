## dogwater
### 在中文 (中文解释)
“狗水”是指那些看起来很有文化或很高的学历，但实际上在社交或专业场合中非常旱鸭子的人。简单来说，就是“虚有其表”的人。这种人在校园或社交圈子里可能非常受欢迎，但在更广泛的环境中，他们可能显得有点儿不自在或不适合。
### In English (English Explanation)
“Dogwater” is a term used to describe someone who seems highly cultured or educated, but in social or professional settings, they are often awkward or out of place. Essentially, it refers to a person who appears to have clout or knowledge but lacks the practical skills or understanding to back it up. In school or social circles, they might be very popular, but in a broader context, they might come off as slightly uncomfortable or unsuitable.
