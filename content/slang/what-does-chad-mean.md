```markdown
## Chad (noun or adjective)
**English:**  
A term used to describe a person who is seen as overly confident, often swaggering or showy, and can sometimes imply a level of incompetence or uncoolness despite displaying confidence. Originates from "Chad Sorzano," a character in an online video series that popularized the term.
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
用来形容一个人显得过度自信，常常 swagger 或炫耀，尽管表现得自信，可能会给人留下不酷或无能的印象。源自在线视频系列中的一个角色 Chad Sorzano，该角色使这个词汇流行起来。
```
```markdown
## Chad (noun or adjective)
**English:**  
Also used as an internet slang to refer to a style that is excessively masculine and/or very specific, often traditional, in appearance or behavior, focusing on practicality over fashion or appearance. This can be adapted from the term "savage" but with a more traditional, often rural take.
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
作为一种互联网用语，用来形容风格上过度男性化或非常特定、通常是传统，在外貌或行为上强调实用性，而不是时尚或外观。这个词可以从“savage”衍生出来，但带有更多传统的、往往是农村的视角。
```
