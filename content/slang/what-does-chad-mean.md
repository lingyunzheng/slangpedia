### Chad: 中文解释
**Chad**：源自英文，通常用来形容一个人在某些方面看起来很“硬核”或很“宅男”。在中文青少年网络用语中，这个词更多地被认为是一种贬义词，用来形容那些外表看起来肌肉发达、行为举止积极性高的男生，有时也带有讽刺意味，暗示这些人可能有点呆板或不适合交友。
**例句**：他平时穿着都很硬朗，看起来就是个“chad”。
### Chad: English Explanation
**Chad**：Originally from English, "chad" is used to describe someone who appears "hardcore" or "homebody" in certain aspects. In Chinese youth internet language, this term is usually considered as a derogatory term, used to describe guys who look muscular and act very energetic, sometimes with a hint of mockery, implying that they might be a bit dull or not very sociable.
**Example Sentence**: He always dresses very tough, so he looks like a "chad".
