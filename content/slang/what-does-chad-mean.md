## Chad (English)
**Chad** is a slang term used to describe someone who exudes characteristics of overly masculine or exaggeratedly traditional bromance dynamics. This person often displays traits like excessive physical fitness, ruggedness, and may engage in stereotypically "manly" activities. In essence, a chad is someone who is considered to be overly masculine and acts in a stereotypically stereotypical way.
