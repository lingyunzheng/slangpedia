# Chad
**English Slang:** "Chad" is primarily used in the United States to describe a kind of straight-laced, nerdish, or restrained person, often in a slightly mocking or dismissive way. It can also be used to describe a person who is overly patriotic or committed to a traditional lifestyle.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **English:** A "chad" is a description for someone who is rather conventional, conservative, or bookish. This term is often used in a context where the person's rigidity or lack of spontaneity is the subject of some ridicule or sarcasm.
- **Chinese:** "Chad" 描述的是一个比较传统、保守或书呆子的人。这个词通常用于嘲笑或戏谑那些过于僵化或缺乏 spontaneity 的人。
