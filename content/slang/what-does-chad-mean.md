## Chad
**English:** A term used to describe a traditionally masculine or stereotypically "manning up" behavior or appearance. It often implies someone who is overly confident, physically strong, or inappropriately sexually driven.
**Simplified Chinese:** 用于描述传统的男子气概或刻板印象中“硬汉”行为或外观的人。它通常暗示某人过度自信、身体强壮或在性方面过于冲动。
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "Chad" is often used to describe a type of behavior that is considered traditionally masculine, such as being overly confident and physically strong. This term can also imply someone who is not socially savvy and may use aggressive or dominating behavior to assert themselves. The term has evolved to become more stereotype-focused, emphasizing physical attributes and attitudes often associated with men in Western culture.
- **Simplified Chinese:** “Chad” 经常用来描述一种被认为具有传统男子气概的行为，比如过于自信和身体强壮。这个词还可能暗示某人缺乏社交能力，并可能通过使用侵略性或支配性行为来表达自己。随着词义的发展，它越来越集中在西方文化中与男性相关的物理特征和态度上。
