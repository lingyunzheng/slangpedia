## Chad (英译中版本)

### 中文解释
“查德”通常是指一个目标明确、充满斗志并且行为上显得非常“硬核”或“杠精”的人，这里的“杠精”指的是那种喜欢反驳或者争论的人。这个称呼带着一点调侃的语气，通常不完全是贬义，有时也可以表示夸赞。

### 英文解释
"Chad" typically refers to someone who is goal-oriented, aggressive, and quite 'tough' or 'argumentative.' This can sometimes be a bit of a joke, but not necessarily meant in a mean way. It can serve as both a playful insult and a form of praise.

## Chad (中译英版本)

### 中文解释
“查德”常用来形容一个目标明确、充满斗志，而且行为上显得非常“硬核”或“杠精”的人。这意味着他非常专注且好争论，有时这个词不仅仅带有贬义，也可以作为一种夸赞来使用。

### 英文解释
"Chad" often refers to someone who is goal-oriented, full of determination, and quite 'hardcore' or 'argumentative.' This means they are very focused and enjoy debating, with the term sometimes having more than just a negative connotation and serving as a form of praise as well.
