## Chad
### English
**Definition:** A slang term often used to describe a person, usually a man, who is perceived as overconfident, overly muscular, and generally socially awkward or 'dumb.' It can also be used as a compliment, but this is less common.
### Chinese
**定义：** 一个俚语词汇，通常用来形容一个人，特别是男性，显得过度自信、过分健壮，并且通常给人一种社交笨拙或“愚蠢”的感觉。这个词也可以作为一种赞美，但这种情况比较少见。
