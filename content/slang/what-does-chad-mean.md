## Chad
### English
**Chad**: A term used to describe a man who is extremely fit and athletic, often with a muscular build. It can also carry a connotation of being a bit of a show-off or trying too hard to be seen as impressive.
### Chinese
**查德 (Chádé)**: 这个词用来形容一个非常健壮、有肌肉的男性，通常很注重健身和体格。它还可以带有一种炫耀或试图显得很 impressive 的意味。
