## Chad
**English:**  
Chad is a term used to describe a person, often a man, who presents themselves as extremely fit, buff, and masculine. It can also refer to someone who is seen as overconfident or show-offy in their physical attributes or abilities. It is used in a critical or sarcastic manner. Originally a term that refers to a user of online dating apps who meets a lot of women, but doesn’t take any action or date them. 
**Chinese:**  
“查德”一词用来形容一个人，通常是男性，表现为极为健壮、阳刚。有时也用来指一个在体态或能力上表现得过于自以为是或炫耀的人。通常带有批评或讽刺的意味。最初用于指那些在交友软件中频繁遇见女性，但不采取任何行动或不约她们出去的用户。
