# Chad
**English**: A slang term used to describe someone who is socially awkward, often perceived as overly masculine and physically fit, but lacking in social skills or intellect. It can also be used to describe someone who is trying too hard to impress others and is seen as a bit dorky or clumsy.
**Chinese**: 用来形容那些社交能力较差、常被看作过于男性化且体型健壮，但在社交或智力方面不突出的人。这个词也可以用来形容那些试图卖力地给人留下好印象，却显得有点呆头鹅或笨拙的人。
