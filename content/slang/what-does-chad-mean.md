### Chad
**English**: A term often used to describe someone who exhibits certain stereotypical characteristics, such as being extremely dedicated to a particular activity or causing unnecessary drama. It can be used in a positive or negative context, depending on the situation.
**Chinese**: 该词常用来形容某人表现了一定的刻板特征，比如在某活动上极其投入，或者制造不必要的麻烦。使用时可以是正面也可以是负面的，具体视情况而定。
