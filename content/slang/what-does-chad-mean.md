## Chad
### English
A slang term often used to describe a man who possesses certain attributes that reflect traditional masculine ideals, typically including bravery, stoicism, and a certain level of confidence and effectiveness in various situations, often with an element of superiority. In some contexts, it can also have a negative connotation, implying that the person's success or ability is due to bravado rather than true skill or intellect.
### Chinese
一个常见的俚语，用来形容一个男人，通常体现出传统意义上的男性特征，如勇敢、坚韧以及在各种情况下表现出来的自信和有效性，有时还带有一种优越感。在某些情况下，这个词也可能带有负面含义，暗示这个人的成功或能力主要是由于蛮勇而不是真正的技巧或智力。
