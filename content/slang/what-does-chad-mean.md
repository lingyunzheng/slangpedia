## Chad
### English
A "chad" is a term often used to describe a person who is socially awkward, frequently resorts to sarcasm or irony, and may be seen as humorless or humor-impaired. This term can also imply a certain level of naivety or obliviousness to social norms.
### Pinyin
Chād (chad)
### Explanation
**Cháde rénrén** (chad rén rén): 社交上笨拙的人，常常通过讽刺或冷言冷语来解嘲，有时候看起来有点儿不开玩笑或者缺乏幽默感。这种人也可能显得天真或者对社会规范不太敏感。
