### Chad
#### English
**Meaning**: A slang term often used to describe someone who acts in a self-important, pretentious, and often clueless way. It can also refer to a rugged, bulky, or physically impressive man, especially one who is perceived as uncool.
**Example**: "That guy who keeps talking about his workout routine is such a chad."
#### 中文
**意思**: 这个俚语通常用来形容那些表现得自大、做作且通常显得很不明智的人。这个词也可以用来形容一个健壮、体型健硕或外表吸引人但被认为不太酷的男性。
**例句**: "那个总是谈论自己锻炼计划的家伙简直就是个chad。"
