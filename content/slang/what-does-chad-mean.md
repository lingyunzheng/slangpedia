## Chad
**English:** A term that evolved from a descriptor for someone who is extremely dedicated and over-the-top in a certain area, often to the point of being self-deprecating or humorous. It's taken on a life of its own, being used in both positive and negative connotations depending on the context.
**Simplified Chinese:** 一个原本用来描述极度投入且有些夸张的人的词汇，现在被广泛使用在正向和反向语境中，既有正面的褒义也有负面的贬义。
**Example Use:** 
- "He's such a chad when it comes to video games. He practices non-stop and can recall all the levels."
- "That chad guy in accounting is literally obsessed with numbers, but sometimes it's a bit much."
