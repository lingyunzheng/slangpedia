## Chad (English)
In internet slang, "chad" is a term often used to describe a person who is perceived as overly masculine, aggressive, or overly eager to show off their dominance, especially in a physical sense. It's generally used in a negative or mocking way.
### Example
- **English:** He's such a chad, always flexing his muscles in the gym.
---
## Chad (Chinese)
在网络slang中，“chad”用来形容一个人被感知为过于富有男子气概、好斗或过于乐于炫耀自己的优势，尤其是在生理上的优势。通常这种描述带有贬义或讽刺。
### 示例
- **中文:** 他简直就是个chad，健身房里总是炫耀自己的肌肉。
