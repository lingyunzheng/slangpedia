```markdown
## Chad
**English:** A term used to describe a person, usually a man, who is highly dedicated and skilled in a particular activity, often to the point of being seen as excessive or showy. It can also imply a level of fitness or muscular build.
**Chinese:** 用来形容一个人，特别是指男性，展现出对某项活动的高度专注和技能，有时会显得过度或炫耀。这个词也可能暗示一个人体型健硕或肌肉发达。
Chad 的用法示例：
- English: He's such a chad, always working out and posting his results online.
- Chinese: 他真是个“强壮邮件”，总是锻炼身体并在网上晒结果。
```
### Note
"强壮邮件" is an informal way to convey the same meaning that someone is too showing off their strength or fitness.
