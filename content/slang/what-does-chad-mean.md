```markdown
## Chad (英汉 slang 释义)
**English:** 
- **Chad** (ching-d): Refers to someone who attempts to act cool or sophisticated in a way that comes off as pretentious or corny. Often used to mock someone's exaggerated or forced attempts at being trendy or stylish.
**汉语:**
- **蔡爷 (Chài yé)**: 指某人尽力表现得酷或时尚，但这种方式显得做作或俗气。常用来嘲讽那些在试图显得时髦或潮流时表现出夸张或硬造的姿态的人。
```
