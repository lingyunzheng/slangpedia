# Bilingual Slang Explanation: Chad
**English:** Chad
- **Definition:** A term used to describe a confident, often silly or awkward, guy who is not particularly clever or witty but is very charming and persistent.
- **Example Usage:** "Tim is such a chad; he keeps asking me on dates even though I told him I'm not interested."
**中文:** 查德 (Chādé)
- **释义:** 用于形容一个自信但常常显得傻里傻气或尴尬的男生，虽然不特别聪明或机智，但非常迷糊且坚持不懈。
- **用法示例:** "Tim真像一个查德，尽管我明确拒绝了，他还是一直在跟我约会。"
