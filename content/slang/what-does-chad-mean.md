## Chad
**English:**  
Chad is a term used primarily in online and hip-hop culture to describe a person who is considered to be nerdy, square, or outdated in their style or behavior. It often carries a negative connotation, implying that the individual is not trendy or cool. 
**Simplified Chinese:**
查德（Chá dé）  
查德是网络和嘻哈文化中常用的一个术语，用来形容那些被认为是呆板、古板或过时的人，尤其是在风格或行为上不酷或不时髦。这个词通常带有贬义，暗示这个个体不够流行或时尚。
**Traditional Chinese:**
