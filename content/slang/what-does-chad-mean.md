# Chad
## English
**Chad**: A term commonly used to describe a person who is very attractive, particularly in terms of physical appearance. Often used in a complimenting sense, referring to someone who is considered "hot" or extremely good-looking. The word can sometimes be used with a slight teasing or mocking tone, often depending on the context.
### Example:
- "That new guy in the office is such a chad!"
## 中文
**Chad**: 一个用来形容非常有吸引力的人，尤其是在描述貌美的时候。这个词通常用作赞美，指一个极其好看的人。使用时可能会带有轻微的戏弄或讽刺意味，这取决于具体情境。
### 例句：
- "公司新来的那位男生真叫一个 chad！"
