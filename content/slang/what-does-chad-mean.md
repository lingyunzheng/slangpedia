```markdown
## Chad
### English: A person who is perceived as overly confident, possibly in a misguided or self-important way.
### Chinese: 禄chod (Lùchód) - 一个被认为过于自信的人，可能有些盲目或自以为是。
Example:
- In English: "That guy is such a chad, always showing off his achievements."
- In Chinese: "那个家伙真像一个禄chod，总是炫耀他的成就。"
```
Note: The Chinese word "禄chod" (Lùchód) is a transliteration and doesn't have a direct equivalent in Chinese slang. It's meant to convey the idea of someone overly confident and boastful.
