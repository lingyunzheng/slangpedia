## Chad
**English:** A term often used to describe a man who is considered to be overly muscular and buff, often to the point where it comes off as cringeworthy or out of place. It can also imply someone who is uncool or lacks style in their fashion or overall demeanor.
**汉语解释:** "Chad" 通常用来形容一个肌肉极为发达、外表过于健硕的男人，这种形容有时会显得尴尬或不合适。这个词也可能暗示一个人在时尚或整体形象方面显得不够cool。
* Usage Examples:
- In English: "That guy in the gym is such a chad, his muscles are bulging everywhere."
- In Chinese: "那个健身房里的家伙简直就是个chad，肌肉 everywhere 都鼓鼓的。"
