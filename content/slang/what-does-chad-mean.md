# Slang Term: "Chad"
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: Describes someone or something that is extremely dedicated, full of energy, and providing an entertaining spectacle, especially in a context of sports or any competition.
- **Example Usage**: "That guy who did 100 push-ups in a row is so chad!"
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义**: 用来形容某人或某事物极其投入、充满活力，并在任何竞赛（如体育赛事）中提供娱乐效果的场景。
- **例句**: "那个一口气做了100个俯卧撑的家伙真是太chad了！"
---
注释："Chad"一词通常带有正面或肯定的含义，但在某些使用情景下可能会带有讽刺色彩。
