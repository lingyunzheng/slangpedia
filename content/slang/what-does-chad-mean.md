```markdown
## Chad
**In English:**
A "chad" is an informal term often used to describe someone who is considered extremely macho, confident, or cool, especially in a retro or meme culture context. 
**In Chinese:**
"Ch德 (Chè)" 是一个非正式的俚语词，用来形容一个人被认为非常硬汉、自信或者酷，尤其是在怀旧或梗文化中。
```
Note: The Chinese characters for "德" (chē) are used here phonetically to represent "chad," as there is no exact equivalent in the Chinese language. In actual casual usage, people would understand it as a phonetic borrowing rather than a literal translation.
