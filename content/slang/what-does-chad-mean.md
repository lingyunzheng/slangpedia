# Chad
## English
**Chad**: A slang term often used to describe someone who is clumsy, lacking social skills, or dorky. The term can also be used to express respect for someone who is committed and works hard, similar to the term "slacker" but with a different connotation. It heavily depends on the context.
Example:
- "He's so chad with his awkward jokes and always missing his targets in the photo. 📸"
## 中文
**查德 (Chādé)**：一个俚语，常用来形容某人笨拙、社交能力差或书呆子。这个词在某些情况下也可以用来表达对一个有毅力、努力工作的人的尊重，但和“废柴”这种说法相比含义有所不同。这完全取决于语境。
示例：
- “他总是讲一些奇怪的笑话并且拍的照片都歪了，真是个查德。📸”
