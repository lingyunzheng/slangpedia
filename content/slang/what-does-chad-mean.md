## Chad
### English
**Meaning:**  
Chad is a slang term often used to describe someone who is considered boring, dull, or uncool. It is sometimes used to refer to someone who overcompensates for perceived inadequacies or who has a milquetoast personality.
**Example Usage:**  
"Wow, that new guy is such a chad. He's so boring; I can't even stand to look at him for more than five minutes."
### 中文
**含义：**  
“Chad” 是一个俚语，通常用来形容一些被认为无聊、乏味或不合潮流的人。有时这个词是用来形容那些试图弥补自身不足或人格软弱的人。
**用法示例：**  
“天哪，那个新来的家伙真是个无聊透顶的Chad，我连看他五分钟都受不了。”
