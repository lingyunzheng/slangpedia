# What does "snatched" mean?（snatched 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Snatched" is an informal term that describes quickly or unexpectedly obtaining something, often with a sense of excitement or anticipation. It can also be used to describe someone or something that is remarkably attractive, stylish, or desirable.

**中文解释：**  
“Snatched”是一个非正式的术语，用来形容快速或出乎意料地获得某物，常常带有激动或期待的感觉。它也可以用来形容某人或某物极为吸引人、时尚或有吸引力。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: She snatched the last piece of pie from the plate before anyone else could reach it.  
   中文翻译: 她在其他人能拿到之前，突然从盘子里抓走了最后一块派。

2) English sentence: He snatched a promotion at work and became a manager within a year.  
   中文翻译: 他在工作中突然获得了一个提升，一年内成为了经理。

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "snatched" originated from the noun "snatch," which means to quickly grab something. Over time, it evolved into a verb that conveys the idea of taking something by surprise or with urgency.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. Grabbed
2. Picked up
3. Secured
4. Obtained
5. Caught
