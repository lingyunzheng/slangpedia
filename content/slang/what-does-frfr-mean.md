## French Slang Explanations
### frfr
**Meaning:**  
- Informal phonetic shorthand: "fr fr" stands for "French French," which is a playful way to describe the French language in a gentle, friendly manner. This term is often used in chat or online communities where French speakers are interacting.
**Example Usage:**  
- "Hey, ça va? Tu es de la frfr?"  
- Translation: "Hey, how are you? Are you from French French?"
**Note:**  
- While "frfr" can be used in a lighthearted context, it's not typically used in formal settings.
