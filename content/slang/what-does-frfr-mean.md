### Bilingual Slang Explanation: "frfr"
**French ("frfr")**
- **Literal Meaning:** "frfr" is an abbreviation for "français-français" which means "French-French", referring to native French-speaking individuals.
- **Connotation:** This term is used to emphasize the purity or authenticity of French language and cultural practices, often used by francophiles or in niche online communities.
- **Example Usage:** "C'est vraiment frfr, I can tell it's made by real French people."
