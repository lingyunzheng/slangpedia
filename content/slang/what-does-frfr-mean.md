# French (frfr) Slang Explained
## 1. "Ben Laden"
- **Meaning in English:** "Well done"
- **Explanation:** Used to congratulate someone. For example, saying "Bravo, Ben Laden!" is equivalent to saying "Well done!" in English.
## 2. "Je te fous la merde"
- **Meaning in English:** "I’m screwing up" or "I’m making a mess"
- **Explanation:** A vulgar way to express that you are doing something incorrectly or that you are causing problems. It’s similar to saying "I’m blowing it" in English.
## 3. "C'est dumaxcdn"
- **Meaning in English:** "It's hardcore" or "It's for real"
- **Explanation:** Used to emphasize that something is intense, serious, or genuine. It’s akin to saying "It’s the real deal" in English.
## 4. "Faire la grasse matinée"
- **Meaning in English:** "To have a lie-in" or "To oversleep"
- **Explanation:** This phrase describes the act of lying in bed for an extended period after waking up, often on the weekend or when not in a rush. It’s similar to "snoozing the day away."
## 5. "Quoi de neuf ?"
- **Meaning in English:** "What’s new?" or "What’s happening?"
- **Explanation:** A casual greeting asking about the latest news or updates from the person’s life. It’s like saying "How’s it going?" or "What’s up?" in English.
