# French Slang Explanations
## 1. "Le top" 
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Awesome" or "Great"
- **Explanation:** Used to express admiration or approval. For example, "Ce restaurant est le top !" (This restaurant is awesome!)
## 2. "Les poulets"
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Chicken" (as in not brave or adventurous)
- **Explanation:** Used to refer to someone who is afraid or cowardly. For example, "Ce mec est un poulet" (This guy is a chicken)
## 3. "Faire la noce"
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Casino" or "Gambling" (literally, to do the wedding)
- **Explanation:** Used in the context of gambling. For example, "Il a perdu tout son argent en faisant la noce." (He lost all his money at the casino.)
## 4. "C'est quoi cette merde ?" 
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "What the hell is this?" or "What the f*ck?"
- **Explanation:** A very rude expression of frustration or anger. It translates literally to "What this shit?" For example, "C'est quoi cette merde de service dans ce restaurant ?" (What the hell is this service in this restaurant?)
## 5. "Faire des bisous"
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Kiss" (as in a quick, friendly kiss, often on the cheek)
- **Explanation:** Used when someone wants to give a quick kiss. For example, "Dis-moi bonjour à tes parents et fais des bisous." (Say hello to your parents and give them a kiss.)
## 6. "Faire des courses"
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Shopping" (literally, to do the shopping)
- **Explanation:** Used to refer to the act of grocery shopping or buying things. For example, "Je vais faire des courses pour la cérémonie." (I'm going to do some shopping for the celebration.)
## 7. "Faut pas kèter"
- **English Slang Equivalent:** "Don't get too excited" or "Don't get your hopes up"
- **Explanation:** Used to tell someone not to get too carried away with their expectations. For example, "Faut pas ke taper là-dessus, on ne sait pas encore." (Don't get too excited about that, we don't know yet.)
