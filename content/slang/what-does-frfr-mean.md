### French Slang Explained
#### frfr
**frfr** is a way to abbreviate "français-français" in French, which specifically refers to French people talking to other French people. It can also denote French language and culture, emphasizing a strong connection to French identity or a casual, in-group way of talking. 
Compare to the English term "Americans talking Americans" where someone might downplay their own regional or cultural identity for humor or emphasis.
Example Usage:
- "C'est une blague frfr, pas pour partir en guerre!" - "It's a French thing, don't take it seriously!"
