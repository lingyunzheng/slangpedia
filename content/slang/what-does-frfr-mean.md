## FrFR (French French)
### Franglais
A mix of French and English, often used among younger generations as a way to blend languages for casual or hip speech. Example: "J'veux une bière, mais pour moi, ce n'est pas le moment de beer-jeunisme." (I want a beer, but for me, it's not the time for beer-jeunisme - a play on words for "jeunesse," meaning youthfulness.)
### Boulot
A colloquial term for "travail" (work). Often used to denote work in a broad sense, including job-related conversations and experiences. Example: "J'suis à la sortie de l'open bar en fin de boulot, c'est la meilleure!" (I'm at the end of the open bar after work, it's the best!)
### Gourou
Literally "guru," but used more broadly to mean a wise or knowledgeable individual who provides guidance. Example: "Mon gourou me conseille de toujours préparer mes documents avant une réunion, c'est mon gourou en tout." (My guru advises me to always prepare my documents before a meeting, he's my guru in everything.)
