# French Slang Explained
## "Frfr"
**Meaning:** Short for "Franchement Frénétique Rapide," which translates to "Genuinely Fast and Impatient."
**Explanation:** This term is a playful way to describe someone who is incredibly quick and a bit impulsive. It’s often used when someone acts or responds very swiftly in a situation, sometimes maybe even a little too quickly for comfort. 
**Usage Example:**
- **French:** "Ben, c'est Frfr, il répond toujours en un claquement de doigt !"
- **English:** "Well, he's Frfr, he always answers in a flash!"
This slang is common in everyday French conversation, especially among younger speakers or in informal settings.
