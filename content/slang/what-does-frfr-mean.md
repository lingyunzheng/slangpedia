# French (frfr) Slang Explanations
## Petit pois
**English:** Peasant, someone from the countryside or lower class.  
**Explanation:** This term is often used in a derogatory manner to refer to someone who is considered less sophisticated or less educated than the user.
## Breizh
**English:** Brezhne, used among Breton people to emphasize something as being typically Breton or to express enthusiasm.  
**Explanation:** "Breizh" is a term used by people from Brittany (a region in the northwest of France) to refer to themselves and their culture, often with a sense of pride and enthusiasm.
## Merde
**English:** Shit, used to express frustration, displeasure, or to say that something is terrible.  
**Explanation:** "Merde" is a common expletive in French, used in a variety of situations, from mild annoyance to severe frustration, depending on the tone.
## Chelou
**English:** Cool, weird, or strange.  
**Explanation:** "Chelou" is a term used to describe something that is considered cooler or more unusual, and can sometimes carry a slight connotation of being strange or out-of-the-ordinary.
## Balai-cing
**English:** Mop-headed, used to describe someone with messy hair or to express mild frustration.  
**Explanation:** Literally meaning "sweeping brush", this term is used to describe someone's messy appearance or to express slight frustration, similar to "frizz" in English.
