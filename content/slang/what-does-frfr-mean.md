# French Slang Explanations (fr/fr)
## 1. **Trop cool**
- **French**: Trop cool
- **English**: Too cool, punk rock, swag

- **French**: piger
- **English**: to get it, to understand
- **Explanation**: Similar to "get it" in English, it's used to say that someone has understood a concept or idea. Can also be used in a more colloquial sense to express confusion or not understanding something.
## 3. **pouet**
- **French**: pouet
- **English**: Various uses, often as a filler word or to express surprise or exasperation
- **Explanation**: This is often used like a verbal filler, similar to "um" or "ah" in English, but can also be used to express something akin to "whatever" or "mind-blowing."
## 4. **singe**
- **French**: singe
- **English**: monkey, used informally to address someone
- **Explanation**: This word literally means "monkey" but is used colloquially as an endearment or a casual way to address someone, like saying "buddy" or "pal."
## 5. **la pédale**
- **French**: la pédale
- **English**: spam, to overload with messages
- **Explanation**: This term describes when someone is sending out too many messages, especially through text or social media, to the point of being overwhelming or annoying.
## 6. **se casser le cul**
- **French**: se casser le cul
- **English**: to go to hell, to get lost, informal way to tell someone to leave
- **Explanation**: This colorful expression is a mild way to tell someone to go away or to leave, often used in a casual or less offensive manner in French.

- **French**: y'a pas photo

- **French**: flippé(e)

- **Explanation**: This term describes someone who is extremely worried, anxious, or paranoid about something, often to the point of getting worked up about it.

- **English**: balls, testicles, used colloquially to express determination or assertiveness
- **Explanation**: This term can be used in a literal sense to refer to balls, or more colloquially to describe someone who is determined or assertive, akin to saying "someone who means business" in English.
