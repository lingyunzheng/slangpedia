# French Slang Explanations
## **Chelou** (Cheleu)
- **Meaning:** Strange, weird, or different.
- **Usage Example:** "Cette tenue est chelou." (This outfit is weird.)
## **Blagu** (Blagu')
- **Meaning:** Joke or silly thing.
- **Usage Example:** "Tu es un vrai blagu pour lui raconter cette histoire." (You are a real joke to tell him this story.)
## **Merde** (Mernes)
- **Meaning:** Shit or crud (used as a mild expletive).
- **Usage Example:** "Alors, tu veux que je te raconte les merdes de mes parents?" (So, you want to hear about my parents' problems?)
## **Zut** (Zet)
- **Meaning:** Damn or oh no.
- **Usage Example:** "Zut, je viens de renverser mon café." (Damn, I just spilled my coffee.)
## **Top** (Top)
- **Meaning:** Great, awesome, or excellent.
- **Usage Example:** "C'est un top film!" (That's a great film!)
## **Gros** (Grotes)
- **Meaning:** Big, but also can imply something is cool or impressive.
- **Usage Example:** "C'est un gros saut!" (That's a big jump! / That's a cool jump!)
## **C'est la cata** (S'la Katta)
- **Meaning:** It's the catastrophe, everything is going wrong.
- **Usage Example:** "Le train est en retard, il pleut, c'est la cata!" (The train is late, it's raining, it's a catastrophe!)
## **C'est naze** (S'la Naze)
- **Meaning:** It's lame or not good.
- **Usage Example:** "Cette chanson est naze." (This song is lame.)
## **Chelou** (Cheleu) - Repeated for emphasis
- **Meaning:** Strange, weird, or different.
- **Usage Example:** "Cette tenue est chelou, chelou, chelou." (This outfit is weird, weird, weird.)
