### French Slang Explanations (frfr)
#### 1. **Frappe**
- **English:** Rip/Shred
- **Definition:** When you're dominating in a conversation or game. It's like when someone takes the stage and every other person fades into the background.
- **Example:** "Hier, dans le tournoi de video game, Jean-François a complètement frappé les autres joueurs !"
#### 2. **Le Bistouri**
- **English:** The Scalpel
- **Definition:** A term used humorously to refer to someone who is very sharp or clever, often used as an insult or backhanded compliment.
- **Example:** "Marie est le bistouri de notre équipe, elle comprend tout de suite les sous-entendus dans les discussions."
#### 3. **L'Pinou**
- **English:** Loosy-goosy
- **Definition:** Describes someone who's not really committed or solid in their actions or decisions. It's like when someone is all over the place and not really sure about what they want.
- **Example:** "Depuis qu'on a eu le projet de voyager, Pierre est vraiment l'pinou, il change d'avis toutes les semaines !"
#### 4. **Le Crépin**
- **English:** The Napkin
- **Definition:** Used to describe a person who is considered marginal or peripheral, often not central to the main action or conversation.
- **Example:** "Je me disais que ce serait bien qu'on prenne une boisson, mais Charles, c'est clair le crépin, il préfère rester dans l'coin."
#### 5. **Pécho**
- **English:** Catch/Pick Up
- **Definition:** A slang term used for picking up dates or meeting potential romantic partners, especially in a casual or hook-up scenario.
- **Example:** "Jolie a prévu de pécho tout l'afterwork aujourd'hui, elle a duDefs de matches sur son application de rencontres."
These explanations provide a glimpse into the playful and often expressive nature of French slang.
