# French Slang Explained in Bilingual Markdown
## 1. T'as pas loupé !
- **French**: T'as pas loupé !
- **English**: You didn't miss it! / You aced it!
- **Explanation**: This expression is used to praise someone for doing something exceptionally well. It's like saying "You knocked it out of the park!" or "You aced it!" in English.
## 2. Ch'ti
- **French**: Ch'ti
- **English**: Mate / Mate from northern France
- **Explanation**: This is a casual way to address someone, particularly someone from the northern region of France (Lorraine, Nord-Pas-de-Calais, etc.). It's a friendly and familiar term.
## 3. Le chou à la nú
- **French**: Le chou à la nú
- **English**: The cabbage to the knife
- **Explanation**: This phrase is used to express agreement or confirmation. It's similar to saying "You're on to something" or "I got you" in English.
## 4. Ça pique !
- **French**: Ça pique !
- **English**: It stings! / It hurts!
- **Explanation**: This expression is used when something is unexpectedly painful or unpleasant. It can be physical (e.g., a bee sting) or metaphorical (e.g., feeling insulted).
## 5. C'est du pipeau !
- **French**: C'est du pipeau !
- **English**: It's a load of crap! / It's nonsense!
- **Explanation**: This phrase is used to dismiss something as false or absurd. It's similar to saying "That's a load of rubbish" or "That's complete nonsense" in English.
