## French Slang
### Balser
**French:** Balser  
**English:** BS'er or Blamer  
**Explanation:** To engage in placing blame or finding fault, often unwarrantedly. In a workplace or group setting, this can mean shifting responsibility onto others while trying to avoid accountability. Similar to saying "it was your fault" or "you messed it up."
### Râler
**French:** Râler  
**English:** Grumble or Complain  
**Explanation:** To express dissatisfaction or frustration about something, often in a less constructive manner. This can involve moaning or grumbling about minor inconveniences or perceived injustices.
