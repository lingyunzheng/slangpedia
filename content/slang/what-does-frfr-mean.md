# French Slang Explained
## "Tafesse" (also "tafus" or "tafasses")
- **Definition:**
- Unfolded or unfolded (clothes)
- **Usage:**
- "Fais le ménage, ta ta ta tafesse à la cuisine!" (Do some cleaning, your clothes in the kitchen are now all unfolded!)
## "Pote" (also "poulette" or "p'tite")
- **Definition:**
- Friend or buddy
- **Usage:**
- "Y a-toi un pote qui peut m'aider?" (Do you have a friend who can help me?)
## "Bras" (also "bras cassé")
- **Definition:**
- Arms, but often used as a playful or affectionate manner.
- **Usage:**
- "Mets ton bras sur mes épaules!" (Put your arms around my shoulders!)
## "Paquet" (also "petit paquet")
- **Definition:**
- Package, but also used to refer to a small, compact person or object.
- **Usage:**
- "C'est un super petit paquet de sourire!" (He's such a radiant little package!)
