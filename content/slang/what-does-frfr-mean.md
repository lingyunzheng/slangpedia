## French Slang Explanations (frfr)
### 1. **Chiotte (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Toilet
- **Meaning:** This term is a vulgar slang for toilet or washroom. It's often used informally or in casual conversations, with a slightly offensive connotation depending on the context.
### 2. **Pute (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Whore
- **Meaning:** This is a highly offensive term. It refers to a prostitute. While it can be used in a derogatory context, it’s important to use it with extreme caution and awareness of the other person’s sensitivity to such language.
### 3. **Merde (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Shit
- **Meaning:** An everyday slang term used to denote garbage or excrement. It’s commonly used to express frustration, anger, or to acknowledge something is incorrect or bad. The word can range from mildly negative to highly offensive depending on the intonation and context.
### 4. **Conard (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Dolt
- **Meaning:** A slang term meaning fool or idiot. It’s a colloquial way to express frustration or anger towards someone. Use with caution as it can be considered quite vulgar.
### 5. **Mec (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Dude
- **Meaning:** A casual term used to refer to a man, somewhat similar to "guy" in English. It’s often used among friends to address each other in a relaxed, informal setting.
### 6. **Fouiner (verb)**
- **Literal Translation:** To search, to inquire
- **Meaning:** This word commonly refers to snooping or prying into someone’s affairs without their permission. It can have both a neutral or negative connotation, depending on how it’s being used.
### 7. **Panthère (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Panther
- **Meaning:** In slang, this can be a metaphor for a woman who is aggressive, powerful, or intimidating. It’s often used in a positive or flirtatious manner to compliment or describe certain traits of a woman.
### 8. **Coñard (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Cousin
- **Meaning:** Informally, “coñard” is a term that translates to “cousin,” but it’s used to refer to a close friend. However, in other contexts, it can be a stronger, more familiar term of endearment, akin to how some English speakers might use terms like “dude” or “bro.” It can also be used in a derogatory way, similar to "conard."
### 9. **Boulet (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Pile of shit
- **Meaning:** A vulgar slang word used to describe something extremely bad, frustrating, or ridiculous. It can be used in conversations to express intense frustration or disappointment.
### 10. **Putain (noun)**
- **Literal Translation:** Whore
- **Meaning:** This is a more mild variant of "pute" and is used less formally. It can be used to express surprise, exasperation, or some frustration, but it’s still a vulgar term and should be used with caution.
