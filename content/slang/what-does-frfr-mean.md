Sure, here are some French slang terms explained in both French and English, formatted in Markdown:
```markdown
# French Slang Explained
## Salut
- **French:** Salut
- **English:** Hi/Buddy
- **Explanation:** A casual way to say hello or to greet a friend. It's very versatile and can be used in many situations, from a quick "hey" to a more formal "nice to see you."
## T'es qui ?
- **French:** T'es qui ?
- **English:** Who are you ?
- **Explanation:** A casual and friendly way of asking who someone is. It's similar to asking "Qui es-tu?" but more direct and informal.
## C'est la vie
- **French:** C'est la vie
- **English:** Such is life
- **Explanation:** This expression is used to accept or acknowledge unfortunate or unexpected situations in a positive manner. It's a way of dealing with reality and moving on.
## Les yeux de la mer
- **French:** Les yeux de la mer
- **English:** Sea-glitter or Mermaid's eyes
- **Explanation:** A playful and romantic way to describe something, often referring to someone's shimmery eyes. It adds a touch of fantasy and admiration.
## La panique à multiple multiplyeurs
- **French:** La panique à multiple multiplyeurs
- **English:** Panic at multiple multipliers
- **Explanation:** This term is used humorously to describe a situation that suddenly becomes much worse or overwhelming very quickly, often due to a series of bad events or misadventures. It's a way of exaggerating the chaos or fear.
## Te flinguer
- **French:** Te flinguer
- **English:** To shoot you
- **Explanation:** This expression is used for something extremely annoying or bothering you, often in a humorous way. It's an exaggeration to indicate that a situation or a person is very irritant.
## C'est pas ma tasse de thé
- **French:** C'est pas ma tasse de thé
- **English:** It's not my cup of tea
- **Explanation:** A way of saying that something is not to one's liking or that one doesn't enjoy or want to be involved with something.
## On a du plomb dans la cervelle
- **French:** On a du plomb dans la cervelle
- **English:** We have lead in our brains
- **Explanation:** An informal way of saying that someone is not very smart or is acting foolishly. It suggests that the person is as dense or slow as lead.
## Faire des pingouins
- **French:** Faire des pingouins
- **English:** To do penguins
- **Explanation:** This is a playful way of describing someone who is shivering, either from cold or for other reasons, often laughing about it.
## C’est la ponpon
- **French:** C'est la ponpon
- **English:** It’s your thing
- **Explanation:** This expression means that something is perfect for you or that you excel in it naturally. It's a way of offering support or encouragement.
```
These terms are meant to capture the fun and casual nature of French slang.
