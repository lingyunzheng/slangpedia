### Chez Mamie
- **Meaning:** Informal restaurant or pub.
- **Explanation:** When someone says they're going to "chez mamie", they're going to a cozy, low-key place to eat, often with a rural or hometown vibe. It's the equivalent of going to a local dive or pub in English slang.
### Chelou
- **Meaning:** Weird, different.
- **Explanation:** When something is "chelou" in French, it's not quite right, strange, or peculiar. It's used to describe anything that stands out, either negatively or positively.
### Chope
- **Meaning:** To get a drink or to pick up someone.
- **Explanation:** In French slang, "chope" can mean getting a drink in a bar or getting a romantic interest's attention. It's a playful, casual term for these actions.
