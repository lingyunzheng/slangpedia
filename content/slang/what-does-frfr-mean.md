# French Slang Explanations
## 1. "Frappe-leur" (pronounced as "Frap-leur")
**Literal Meaning:** Hit them  
**Bilingual Explanation:** Literally translates to "Hit them," often used to encourage someone to take action or to confront others. It's a colloquial way of saying you want someone to be assertive or aggressive in a particular situation.  
**Type of Slang:** Aggressive encouragement  
**Example Usage:** "Frappe-leur, ils sont trop arrogants!" (Hit them, they are too arrogant!)
## 2. "C'est du n'importe quoi"  
**Literal Meaning:** It's something random/stupid  
**Bilingual Explanation:** This phrase is used when something seems completely absurd, ridiculous, or nonsensical. It's a very common expression to show disagreement or disbelief.  
**Type of Slang:** Dismissive phrase  
**Example Usage:** "Their idea for the event? C'est du n'importe quoi." (Their idea for the event? It's something random/stupid.)
## 3. " tôlé" (pronounced as "to-lee")
**Literal Meaning:** Rough or untidy  
**Bilingual Explanation:** This slang term is used to describe someone or something that is messy or unneat, often with a playful or affectionate undertone.  
**Type of Slang:** Affectionate colloquialism  
**Example Usage:** "Elle est tellement tôlée, mais j'adore ça!" (She is so messy, but I love it!)
## 4. "Sauf que" (pronounced as "Swah kay")
**Literal Meaning:** But/Except  
**Bilingual Explanation:** This is a versatile expression used to introduce an exception or to convey disagreement, similar to "but" in English. It is used frequently in conversation to add nuance or to reinforce a point.  
**Type of Slang:** Transition word  
**Example Usage:** "Je ne veux pas manger de viande, sauf que j'ai faim maintenant." (I don't want to eat meat, but/except I'm hungry now.)
## 5. "F'in" (pronounced as "F-in")
**Literal Meaning:** F'ing (a mild expletive)  
**Bilingual Explanation:** This is a mild form of an expletive used to express frustration, surprise, or emphasis in a more controlled manner. It's a way of softening words without removing their intensity.  
**Type of Slang:** Emphatic        
**Example Usage:** "F'in late again!" (Frappé encore une fois en retard!)
---
Feel free to use these slang terms in context, keeping in mind the conversational and regional nuances!
