## Frfr (French for French)
### English Explanation
"Frfr" is a casual abbreviation used to describe something or someone as being "fr_shipped" or "Frenchified." It often refers to the moody or emotional quality that is stereotypically associated with French people, or anything that is very passionate or dramatic.
