# French Slang Explanations
## 1. **Balade**
### English: **Hangout**
It's a casual way to say that someone is out and about, maybe just hanging out. Often implies a relaxed and easy time without any specific purpose.
Example: "Ça va ? Ah, tu vas juste faire une balade ?" (Are you doing well? Oh, you're just going for a walk?)
## 2. **Chouette**
### English: **Cool (slightly more informal than 'cool')**
Used to express approval, excitement, or that something is awesome. A slightly more casual and enthusiastic way to describe something as cool.
Example: "Tu as gagné à la loterie ? Super chouette !" (You won the lottery? Super cool!)
## 3. **Pute**
### English: **Insult (not a slang but rather offensive)**  
This is a vulgar and offensive term used as an insult. It translates to "whore" in English and should be avoided unless talking in a slangy, derogatory context.
Example: (Not recommended) "Tu es vraiment une pute !" (You are really a whore!) - This is very offensive and not used in a casual or friendly manner.

## 5. **Troller**
### English: **To troll (slang)**  
This means to provoke or annoy someone online or in person. It can also refer to engaging in online behavior that is intended to bait or insult others.
Example: "Il a trollé tout le monde pendant la réunion." (He troll-ed everyone during the meeting.)
