## French Slang
### 1. **Mec**
- **French:** Mec
- **Meaning:** Buddy, guy, bloke
- **Example:** "C'est mon mec de toujours." (It's my guy from always.)
### 2. **Frein**
- **French:** Frein
- **Meaning:** Brake (in a figurative sense, it means 'to stop someone or something')
- **Example:** "C'est ta mère, frein !" (It's your mother, stop it!)
### 3. **Ben**
- **French:** Ben
- **Meaning:** Well, you see; often used to start a statement
- **Example:** "Ben, t'as pas idée !" (Well, you have no idea!)
### 4. **Flic (flics)**
- **French:** Flic (flics)
- **Meaning:** Cop, policeman (plural is "flics")
- **Example:** "Il n'a pas fait attention aux flics en escapade." (He didn't notice the policemen on his escape.)
### 5. **Pute**
- **French:** Pute
- **Meaning:** Prostitute (also used as a general insult)
- **Example:** "Ne me traites pas de pute !" (Don't call me a prostitute!)
### 6. **Baratiner**
- **French:** Baratiner
- **Meaning:** To talk someone into something; to blabber; to swindle
- **Example:** "Todd me baratina pour qu'il achète son manteau." (Todd talked me into buying his coat.)
### 7. **Merde**
- **French:** Merde
- **Meaning:** Shit (used as an expletive)
- **Example:** "Oh merde, j'ai oublié ma clé !" (Oh shit, I forgot my key!)
### 8. **Loulou**
- **French:** Loulou
- **Meaning:** Darling, sweetheart (endearment)
- **Example:** "C'est mon loulou ! " (It's my darling!)
### 9. **Petite**
- **French:** Petite
- **Meaning:** Petite, small (can also be used as a term of endearment, similar to "sweetie")
- **Example:** "C'est ma petite fille." (This is my little girl.)
### 10. **Con**
- **French:** Con
- **Meaning:** Idiot, moron (can be used as a respectful term depending on context)
- **Example:** "T'es un vrai con !" (You're a real idiot!)

### 11. **Perroquet**
- **French:** Perroquet
- **Meaning:** Parrot; often used to describe someone who repeats things without understanding them
- **Example:** "Il est un vrai perroquet, tout le monde lui parle et il répète tout !" (He's like a parrot, everyone talks to him and he just repeats everything!)
### 12. **Râteau**
- **French:** Râteau
- **Meaning:** Scalpel (used figuratively to mean someone who is very resourceful and acquires things easily, especially in a very materialistic sense)
- **Example:** "Il est un râteau, il ne passe pas devant une baguette sans la prendre." (He's a râteau; he doesn't walk past a loaf of bread without taking it.)
### 13. **Zut**
- **French:** Zut
- **Meaning:** Damn, darn (a mild expletive)
- **Example:** "Zut, mon téléphone !" (Damn, my phone!)
### 14. **Chuuuut (Chiiiiut)**
- **French:** Chuuuut (Chiiiiut)
- **Meaning:** Shhh (used to signal someone to be quiet; often drawn out for emphasis)
- **Example:** "Chiiiiut, ne gueule pas !" (Shhh, don't yell!)
### 15. **Y'a pas photo (Ya pas photo)**
- **French:** Y'a pas photo (Ya pas photo)
- **Meaning:** You can't get away with that; it's obvious (implies there are no arguments)
- **Example:** "Y'a pas photo, c'est toi qui as volé les CDs !" (There's no way you didn't steal the CDs!)
