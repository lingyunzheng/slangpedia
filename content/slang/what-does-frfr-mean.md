### Francais Français (French French)
**Meaning:** A slang term used to refer to someone who speaks French with a Parisian accent, often implying a cooler or more sophisticated connotation. It's a way to highlight pride in one's Parisian or French heritage.
**Exemple d'utilisation:** "Ça te va bien ton blouson de cuir, t'es clairement un vrai français français!" (You look great in your leather jacket, you're clearly a real French French!)
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- Français Français: (French French) It's all about the cool, stylish, and sophisticated accent and attitude of a true Parisian.
