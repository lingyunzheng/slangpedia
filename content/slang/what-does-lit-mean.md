```markdown
## Lit (Lì)
### English
**Definition:** "Lit" is a slang term often used to describe someone or something that is exciting, energetic, or impressive.
**Example:** "The party was lit last night with non-stop dancing and great music!"
### Chinese
**定义:** "Lit" 是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某事充满活力、刺激或令人印象深刻。
**示例:** "昨天的派对真是精彩不停，一直都在跳舞，音乐也很棒！"
```
Note: The English definition and example provided are the most common usages of "lit." The Chinese translation is a direct interpretation to convey the same idea.
