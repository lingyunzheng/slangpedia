## Lit (Sinóptico)
### English
**Lit**: Used to describe something or someone that is exciting, cool, or highly entertaining. Often used in social media or casual conversations to express enthusiasm or approval.
### 中文
**lit**: 用来形容某件事或某人很刺激、很酷或非常高娱乐性。常用于社交媒体或日常对话中，表达兴奋或赞赏。
