## Lit (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
**English:**
- **Definition:** Something or someone that is exciting, energetic, and cool.
- **Usage Example:** "The concert was lit last night; everyone was dancing and having a blast!"
**Chinese:**
- **释义:** 令人兴奋、充满活力且很酷的东西或人。
- **使用示例:** "昨晚的演唱会真 Lit，大家都尽情跳舞，玩得很开心！"
