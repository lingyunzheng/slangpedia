## Lit
### English
- **Definition**: Highly enthusiastic, energetic, or excited about something.
- **Example**: "She's lit up the party with her amazing dance moves!"
### Chinese
- **定义**: 非常热情、充满活力或对某事感到兴奋。
- **例句**: "她精彩的舞步让派对充满了活力！"
