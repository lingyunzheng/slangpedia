# Lit
## English
**Lit**: A slang term that originated in urban and college social scenes, used to describe someone who is extremely entertaining, fun, and enjoyable to be around. It can also describe a situation that's exciting or intense.
Example: "Wow, the party was lit last night! The DJ played great music, and everyone was having a blast!"
## Chinese
**lit**: (燃的, rán de) 一个源自都市和大学社交圈子的俚语，用来形容某人极其有趣、娱乐性强且让人愉快。它还可以用来描述一些令人兴奋或紧张的情况。
例句： "哇，昨晚的派对真 lit！音乐超棒，每个人都玩得非常开心！"
