## Lit (英语)
### English Explanation
- **Definition:** Describing something as 'lit' means it is exciting, fun, or trendy.
- **Example:** "The party last night was totally lit with great music and cool decorations."
### 中文解释
- **定义:** 当形容某样东西很“_lit_”时，意味着它非常有趣、刺激或者时髦。
- **例句:** "昨晚的聚会超lit，音乐和装饰都超级赞。"
