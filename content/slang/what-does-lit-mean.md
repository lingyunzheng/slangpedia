## Lit (俚语解释)
### 中文
抵得上酷、燃，用以形容某事物非常棒或者充满动感，让人兴奋。
### 英文
"Lit" means extremely cool, trendy, or exciting, often used to describe something or someone that is full of energy, impressive, or fashionable.
