## English to Chinese Slang: "lit"
- **lit** (形容词) - 狂野的；精彩的
### Explanation:
- **English**: "lit" is an adjective often used to describe something or someone that is extremely cool, exciting, or enjoyable. For example, a night out could be "lit" if it's full of energy and fun activities.
- **Chinese**: "lit" 可以形容某事或某人非常酷、刺激或令人愉快。比如，如果晚上外出充满了活力和有趣的活动，可以说那个晚上是“lit”的。
### Example Usage:
- **English**: This party was lit last night with tons of dancing and drinks.
- **Chinese**: 昨晚派对充满了跳舞和飲み物，真是“lit”！
