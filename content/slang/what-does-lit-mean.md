# Lit
## English
**Meaning:** Excited, fun, highly entertaining, or impressive.
**Usage Example:** "That party was totally lit! Everyone was dancing and having a great time."
## 中文
**意思:** 兴奋的，有趣的，非常娱乐的，或者令人印象深刻的。
**使用示例:** "那个派对超级lit！每个人都在尽情跳舞，玩得很开心。"
