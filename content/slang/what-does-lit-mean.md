## Lit (English)
**Meaning:** Exciting, energetic, or impressive. 
**Usage:** Often used to describe someone or something that is particularly energized, fun, or impressive.
## Lit (Chinese)
**含义:** 很酷，很兴奋或者很精彩。
**用法:** 经常用来形容某人或某事特别有活力、很有趣的，或者非常棒。
