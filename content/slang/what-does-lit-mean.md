## Lit
### English
**Meaning**: Exciting, energetic, or electric.
**Explanation**: When someone is described as "lit," it means they are full of energy or excitement, often in a way that's impressive or appealing. It can also refer to a situation or event that is particularly exciting or fun.
**Example**: "The party was lit last night; everyone was dancing and having a great time!"
### Chinese
**含义**: 兴奋的，充满活力的，或者充满电的。
**解释**：当有人被形容为“lit”时，意味着他们充满活力或兴奋，通常是在一种令人印象深刻或吸引人的方式。这个词也可以用来形容一个特别令人兴奋或有趣的场面或活动。
**例句**：昨晚的派对超级 lit，大家都在疯狂跳舞玩得很尽兴！
