## Lit (英 / 迈阿密)
### English
**"Lit"**: This slang term is often used to describe something or someone that is exciting, awesome, or extremely fun. It can be used to praise an event, a party, or someone who is having a great time. For example, you might say "That party was lit" if you had a very good time.
### Chinese (迈阿密)
**"Lit"**: 这个网络用语常用来形容某件事物或某个人非常酷炫，令人兴奋，或者超好玩。它可以用来赞美一场聚会、一次活动，或者某个正过得很嗨的人。例如，如果你觉得某个聚会非常精彩，你会说“那个聚会很 lit”。
