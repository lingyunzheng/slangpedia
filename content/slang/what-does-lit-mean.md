# Lit (Lì)
**English:**  
- **Definition:** overwhelmingly good, exciting, or intense. Often used in contexts involving parties, experiences, or expressions of happiness and enthusiasm.
- **Example Usage:** "This party is totally lit! The music and energy are out of this world!"
**Chinese (简体字):**  
- **定义:** 极其好、令人兴奋或强烈的。常用于描述派对、经历或表达高兴和热忱的情境。
- **例句:** "这场派对真是太'lit'了！音乐和氛围简直太棒了！"
