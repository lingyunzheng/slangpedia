## Lit
### English
**Meaning:** Excited, energetic, and impressive. 
Example: "The party was lit last night with all the cool DJs and new friends."
### 中文
**含义:** 兴奋的，充满活力的，令人印象深刻。
例句："昨晚的派对超燃，有好几个厉害的DJ和新朋友。"
