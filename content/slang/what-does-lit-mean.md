## Lit (Lí tī)
**English:**  
Lit is a slang term used to describe something or someone that is exciting, interesting, or extremely fun. It can also mean being very energetic or animated, often used in a party or social setting.
**Chinese (Lí tī):**  
"Lit" 是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某事物非常有趣、刺激或极其好玩。这个词语也可以用来形容人在派对或社交场合中充满活力和激动的样子。
