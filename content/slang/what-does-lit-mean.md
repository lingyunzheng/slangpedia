## Lit
**English:**  
"Lit" is a slang term that originated in the 1990s and has since evolved. Initially, it referred to something or someone that was extremely energetic, lively, or exciting. Today, it's often used to describe a fun, party-hard, and generally impressive or entertaining situation, person, or event. It can also be used to describe someone who is very attractive in a lively or entertaining way.
**Chinese:**  
"Lit"（火到不行）是一个起源于20世纪90年代的俚语，后来逐渐演变。最初，这个词用来形容非常有活力、充满活力或令人兴奋的人或事。今天，它经常用来形容非常有趣、疯狂派对、充满娱乐性和魅力的情境、人物或活动。此外，这个词也可以用来形容极具吸引力的人，特别是那种活波、有趣的方式。
