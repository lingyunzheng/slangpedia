# Lit (Noun and Adjective)
**English:**  
1. **Hyperexcited or Excited:** Describing someone who is incredibly excited or enthusiastic about something.  
- *Example:* "She's lit about her new job!"  
2. **Cool or Awesome:** Used to describe something as really cool or awesome.  
- *Example:* "His dance routine was so lit!"
**Chinese:**  
1. **极其兴奋或激动:** 表示某人对某事感到非常兴奋或热情。  
- *Example:* "她对她的新工作非常激动！" -> "She's lit about her new job!"
2. **很酷或很棒:** 用来形容某事非常酷或很棒。  
- *Example:* "他的舞蹈表演很燃！" -> "His dance routine was so lit!"
