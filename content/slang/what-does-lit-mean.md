## Lit
**English:**  
"Lit" is a slang term commonly used in the United States, often to describe someone or something as being exciting, energetic, or intense. It can also mean that something has been set on fire, though in this context, the use is more metaphorical.
**Chinese:**  
"Lit" 是美国俚语中常用的术语，通常用来形容某人或某事非常有趣、充满能量或极度吸引人。尽管这个词有时指代某物被点燃，但在这种语境中，通常是比喻性的使用。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "That party was so lit, everyone was dancing and having the time of their lives!"
- Chinese: "那个派对超级 lit，每个人都沉迷于舞蹈和尽情享受时光！"
