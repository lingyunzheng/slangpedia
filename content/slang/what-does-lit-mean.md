## Lit
### English
**Meaning:** Excited, awesome, or having a lot of energy.
**Usage:** "I went to the party last night and I was lit the whole time!"
### 中文
**意思:** 兴奋的、很棒的，或是充满活力的。
**用法:** "昨晚我去参加派对，全程都 Pretty Lit！"
