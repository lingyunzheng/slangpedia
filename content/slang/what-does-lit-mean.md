## "Lit" (形容词 - 精彩的 / 排面的)
### 中文解释
"lit" 在中文中通常用来形容一个活动、场景或者场合非常热闹和精彩。有时候也用来描述某人或某事非常出风头，显得特别抢眼。它可以用来形容派对、聚会等社交场合的气氛。
### 英文解释
"Lit" originally comes from the English language, generally used to describe an event, scene, or occasion as extremely lively and exciting. It also describes someone or something that stands out and appears particularly eye-catching. It can be applied to party scenes, gatherings, and other social events.
