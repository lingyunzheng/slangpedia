## Lit (Energized/Pumped up) 
### English
- **Definition**: Feeling extremely excited, energized, and enthusiastic about something.
- **Example**: "I'm feeling so lit about the concert tonight!"
### Chinese
- **释义**：感觉非常兴奋、充满活力和热情。
- **例句**：今晚的演唱会我超级兴奋！
