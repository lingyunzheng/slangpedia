## "Lit"
### English
- **Definition**: A high-energy, exciting, or wild activity or event.
- **Example Usage**: "We had a lit night out dancing and partying at the club last night."
### Chinese
- **释义**：形容某件事或活动充满活力、好玩或者非常精彩。
- **例句**：昨晚我们在俱乐部跳舞、聚会，玩得非常high，真是“lit”。
