## Lit (US English)
**English**: Walking the walk and talking the talk; being extremely cool and energetic.
**Chinese**: 炸裂 (zha leeè) - 表示某人或事物非常cool，充满能量，表现得特别出众。简单来说，就是极其酷炫或者兴奋到了极点。
**Example Usage**:
- English: She's always lit at parties, drinking, dancing, and laughing.
- Chinese: 她在派对上总是炸裂，喝着酒、跳舞、笑不停。
```
## Lit (Australian English)
**English**: Over-the-top or extremely enthusiastic; something extremely good or exciting.
**Chinese**: 炸串 (zha cuàn) - 形容某人或某事物非常精彩、兴奋或过度的表现，通常带有夸张的意味。
**Example Usage**:
- English: The concert was lit last night, and the crowd went wild.
- Chinese: 演唱会 yesterday晚上炸串，观众兴奋极了。
