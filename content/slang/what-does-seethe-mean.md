## Seethe (English)
**Definition**: To be extremely angry or annoyed, often to the point of being on the verge of erupting or losing self-control.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation**: "Seethe" in English can be translated to "冒血" (mào xuè) in Chinese, which literally means "blood boiling", implying extreme anger. When someone is seething, they are like a pot of water about to boil over; they're extremely upset and could potentially explode at any moment.
**Usage Example**: 
- English: "John was seething when he found out his roommate had used his toothpaste without asking."
- Chinese: 约翰发现室友未经允许用了他的牙膏，他简直快要冒血了。
## Seethe (Chinese)
**定义**: 非常生气或烦躁，到了快要爆发或失去控制的地步。
**双语俚语解释**: "Seethe" 在英语中可译作“冒血” (mào xuè) 在中文里，字面意思是“血沸”，意味着极度生气。当某人正在 seethe 时，就像水快要沸腾溢出一样；他们极度生气，很可能随时爆发。
**用例**:
- 英语: "John was seething when he found out his roommate had used his toothpaste without asking."
- 中文: 约翰发现室友未经允许用了他的牙膏，简直快要冒血了。
