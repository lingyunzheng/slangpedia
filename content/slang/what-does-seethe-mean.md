```markdown
## 英文 Slang Explanation: Seethe

**Seethe** - To seethe means to be extremely annoyed or irritated, often to the point where one feels like they are about to erupt or lose control. This phrase often implies a smoldering, barely contained anger.

### 中文俚语解释: 咸鱼翻身

**咸鱼翻身** - "咸鱼翻身" 在中文里常用来形容某人或某群体在长时间处于消极、绝望或低谷的状态后，突然有了转机或新的发展。这个词汇通常用来形容人们或群体情绪上从愤怒或不满中找到了转机，像快要翻转的咸鱼一样，有了新的活力或希望。这个成语描绘的是从一种压抑的状态中猛然扭转乾坤的情景。
```

Note: While "seethe" directly relates to intense anger or irritation, the Chinese "咸鱼翻身" is more about overcoming adversity or turning one's situation around after a period of difficulty. It can be adapted metaphorically to express similar emotions but comes with a different cultural connotation.
