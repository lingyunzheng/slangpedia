## Seethe (俚语解释)
### 英文
**Seethe:**
- **Meaning:** To experience a strong, usually painful emotion that is hard to express, such as anger, frustration, or anxiety.
- **Example Sentence:** "Ever since he broke up with his girlfriend, John has been seething with anger."
### 中文
** seethe:**
- **意思:** 指经历一种强烈的、通常难以表达的情绪，比如愤怒、挫败或焦虑。
- **例句:** “自从和女朋友分手后，约翰一直在忍受着巨大的愤怒。”
**拓展用法:**
- **英文:** "She was seething with jealousy when she found out her friend got a raise."
- **中文:** “得知朋友加薪后，她对朋友充满了嫉妒。”
