```markdown
## Seethe (In English)
**Definition:** To seethe means to be extremely angry or agitated and to express it in a simmering, barely suppressed way.
**Example Sentence:** When his boss criticized his presentation, John seethed with anger.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **英文意思:** \<seethe\> 指的是极度愤怒或烦躁，并且以一种几乎被抑制的沸腾状态来表达。
- **例句:** 当他的老板批评他的演示文稿时，约翰气得快爆炸了。
```

```markdown
## 嫩(Container Word) % (在中文中)
**定义:** "嫩"是一个容器词，通常用于形容某人或某事还比较年轻、弱小或不够成熟。在这里，"嫩"谐音"怒"，可以用来形容一个人生气的情绪十分高涨，就像是快要爆发的能量。
**例句:** 当他的老板批评他的演示文稿时，John 嫩 % 与愤怒。
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Definition:** "嫩" is a container word that typically describes someone or something as young, weak, or not yet mature. Here, "嫩" is a play on words, sounding like "怒" (anger), to describe someone's anger that is extremely high, like almost explosive energy.
- **Example:** 当他的老板批评他的演示文稿时，John 嫩 % 与愤怒。
```
