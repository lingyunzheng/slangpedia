## 英文 slang: Seethe
**Meaning:**
To seethe means to be extremely angry or annoyed to the point where you feel like you might explode or boil over with rage. It describes a person who is so agitated that they appear to be emitting a kind of heat from within, often under a surface of calmness.
**Example:**
- **English:** "Ever since his boss criticized his work, he's been seething all day."
- **Chinese:** "自从他的上司批评了他的工作后，他一整天都在暗自生闷气。"
## 中文 slang: 暗自发火（àn fā fā huǒ）
**Meaning:**
暗自发火（àn fā fā huǒ）意指人在表面保持平静的情况下，内心却充满了愤怒或不满。类似于『seethe』，表示一个人虽然看起来平静，但实际上充满了火气。
**Example:**
- **Chinese:** “自从他的上司批评了他的工作后，他一整天都在暗自发火。”
- **English:** "Ever since his boss criticized his work, he's been seething all day."
