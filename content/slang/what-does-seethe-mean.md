# Slang Explanation: "seethe"
**English:** Seethe
- **Definition:** To seethe means to be extremely angry or frustrated, with an underlying feeling of intense anger that can be expressed in various ways, such as through body language, gestures, or inarticulate sounds.
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文:** " seethe" 指的是极度生气或烦躁，心底有一种强烈的愤怒感，这种愤怒可以通过肢体语言、手势或无言的哼哼声等方式表达出来。
- **Example Usage:** "Ever since she found out about the prank, she's been seething at her colleagues." - "自从她发现那个恶作剧以来，她一直在同事们面前发怒。"
