```markdown
# Slang Explanation: Seethe
## English
**Seethe:** To be extremely angry or irritated, often in a way that is not openly displayed but can be sensed.
*Example:* "Ever since I found out about the mix-up, I've been seething at the office, even though I need to remain calm."
## Spanish
**Sibilar como una serpiente:** Estar extremadamente enojado o irritationado, a menudo de manera que no se expresa de forma abierta pero se puede sentir.
*Ejemplo:* "Después de enterarme del revés, he estado sibilando como una serpiente en el trabajo, aunque necesito mantenerme calmado."
```
Note: The Spanish translation provided here is a metaphorical equivalent rather than a direct translation. The metaphor "sibilar como una serpiente" conveys a similar intensity of hidden anger as "seethe" does in English.
