## Seethe
### English
**Definition:** To seethe means to be extremely angry or annoyed, often to the point of barely suppressing your emotions.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** To seethe means to be extremely angry or annoyed, often to the point of barely suppressing your emotions.
- **Slang:** 非常生气（用到极点，几乎要爆发出来），例如："He was seething with rage after the meeting." - 他在会议后气得快爆炸了。
### Example Sentences:
- **English:** She seethed with anger as she read the letter.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 她读信时气得快爆炸了。
- **Traditional Chinese:** 她讀信時氣得快爆炸了。
### Alternative Phrases:
- **English:** Fume, simmer, boil
- **Simplified Chinese:** 嫉妒（fume），冒烟（simmer），沸腾（boil）
- **Traditional Chinese:** 嫉妒（fume），冒煙（simmer），沸騰（boil）
### Usage Context:
- **English:** This phrase is commonly used in informal or literary contexts to describe intense negative emotions.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 在非正式或文學上下文中，常常用這個詞來形容強烈的負面情緒。
- **Traditional Chinese:** 在非正式或文學上下文中，常常用這個詞來形容強烈的負面情緒。
