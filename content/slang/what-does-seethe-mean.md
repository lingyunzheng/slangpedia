## Slang: Seethe
### English
- **Meaning**: To be extremely angry or annoyed, often showing this anger in a restrained or suppressed way.
- **Example**: After missing his bus, John seethed in his office, silently fuming about the traffic that had made him late.
### Chinese
- **含义**: 极度生气或烦躁，常常以一种受压制或不外露的方式表现出这种愤怒。
- **例句**: 由于公交车晚点，约翰在办公室里闷闷不乐，默默地对交通堵塞感到生气。
