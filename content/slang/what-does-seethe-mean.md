## 英文 Slang Explanation: Seethe
**英语释义：** Seethe means to be extremely angry or annoyed to the point where you seem to be boiling with anger. It’s often used when someone is internally upset but hasn't erupted into loud or direct anger yet.
**中文解释：** " seethe" 是指极度生气或烦躁到仿佛内心在剧烈沸腾的程度。这个词常用来形容一个人虽然内心很愤怒，但还没爆发出来，表现得不那么大声或直接。
### Example Usage:
**英语示例：** "Ever since that meeting, she's been seething, and it's only a matter of time before she blows up."
**中文示例：** "自从那次会议之后，她一直在 seethe，迟早会爆发出来的。"
