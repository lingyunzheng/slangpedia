## Seethe (Chinese: 气得发慌; pinyin: qì dé fā huáng)
**English Slang Explanation:**
When you're seething, you're extremely angry and almost ready to explode. It's like when you're so mad that you can practically feel the heat rising from your head. It's a deep level of frustration where you might feel like you're going to let out a boiling anger.
**Chinese Slang Equivalent and Explanation:**
气得发慌 (qì dé fā huáng) literally translates to "fretting because of anger." It describes a situation where you're so enraged that you're starting to worry about the impact of your anger or the consequences of the situation. This phrase is used when the frustration is so intense that it starts to affect other aspects of your life or mental state.
---
### Example Usage:
**English:**
"I seethed after he canceled our plans at the last minute."
**Chinese:**
"我气得发慌，他在最后一刻取消了计划。"
