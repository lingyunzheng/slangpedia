## 英文 slang explanation for "seethe"
### Definition:
To seethe means to feel intensely angry or irritated, often without showing it openly.
### Example:
- **English:** "Ever since she lost her phone, Mary has been seething at everyone around her."
- **中文解释：** 自从丢了手机，玛丽一直对周围的人感到非常生气，虽然她并没有明显表现出来。

## 中文 slang 解释 for " seethe"
### 定义：
“seethe” 表示内心充满强烈愤怒或烦躁，但没有明显地表现出这种情绪。
### 示例：
- **英文：** "Ever since she lost her phone, Mary has been seething at everyone around her."
- **中文解释：** 自从丢了手机，玛丽一直对周围的人感到非常生气，虽然她并没有明显表现出来。
