## 英文 Slang Explanation: Seethe
- **Meaning**: To seethe refers to being extremely annoyed or angry, often to the point where one is about to explode or have an outburst. It implies a strong, simmering anger.
- **例子**: "Ever since the team lost the game, the coach has been seething and yelling at everyone."
## 中文俚语解释: 一肚子火
- **意思**: 一肚子火（或者心里冒火）意味着某人极度生气或恼怒，通常到了要大发脾气或爆发的地步，表示内心强烈的愤怒情绪。
- **例子**: “从比赛输掉的那一刻起，教练心里冒火，一直在大声对队员喊话。”
