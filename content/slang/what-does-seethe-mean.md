# Slang Explanation: Seethe
## English
**Seethe:**
- To seethe means to be extremely angry or annoyed to the point that you appear to be about to erupt. It's like when you feel so mad that you feel like your insides are boiling.
Example: "After his presentation got canceled, John seethed in his chair, clearly upset about the whole situation."
## 中文
** seethe:**
- seethe 指的是极度愤怒或恼火到似乎要爆发的程度。就像是你感到非常生气，直到你觉得自己的内心在沸腾。
例句：在他演讲被取消后，约翰坐在椅子里愤愤不平，显然对整个情况很生气。
