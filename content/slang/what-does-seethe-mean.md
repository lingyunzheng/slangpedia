## S_langue (English) - 燽词 (Chinese)
### Seethe
**English**: To seethe means to be extremely angry and to let your anger show. It's like when you're so mad that steam is coming out of your ears!
**Chinese**: 燽词 指的是极度生气并明显表现出愤怒。就像是你气得像蒸汽从耳朵里冒出来一样！
---
Example Usage:
- **English**: After finding out the truth, John seethed with anger and demanded an apology.
- **Chinese**: 在得知真相后，约翰气得火冒三丈，并要求赔礼道歉。
