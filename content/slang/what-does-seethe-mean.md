## 英文 Slang Explanation: Seethe
**Seethe**: To be extremely angry or irritated, often to the point of boiling over. People who seethe usually display obvious signs of irritation, like clenching their fists or grinding their teeth.
**中文俚语解释: 煮沸**
**Seethe**: 表示极度生气或烦躁，常常到了快要爆发的程度。当一个人 seethe 的时候，他们通常会显露出明显的情绪不安，比如握紧拳头或咬紧牙关。
