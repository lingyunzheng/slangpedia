# Slang Explanation: Seethe
## English
**Seethe:**  
To seethe means to be extremely annoyed or angry to the point where you feel like you are about to boil over with anger. It often involves a low, growling sound coming from the throat that indicates intense frustration or irritation.
Example:  
"She seethed when she realized she was locked out of her own house."
## Chinese
** seethe (/nsaɪð/):**  
“seethe” 指极度生气或愤怒到快要爆发的情绪状态。通常伴随着喉咙里发出低沉的咕哝声，表示强烈的挫败感或烦躁。
例子：  
“她发现被锁在家里后气得直冒烟。”
备注：在中国的口语中，常用来形容一个人因为愤怒而气得脸色发青或浑身颤抖的样子。
