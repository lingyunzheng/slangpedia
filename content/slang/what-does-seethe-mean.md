# Slang Explanation: Seethe
**English:** Seethe
**Definition:** To seethe is to be extremely angry or annoyed, often to the point of becoming visibly displeased.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **English:** They were seething, stomping their feet and muttering curses under their breath.
- **Chinese:** 他们气得直冒烟，跺着脚低声骂骂咧咧。
(Translation: Tāmen qì de zhí mào yān, diàng zhe jiǎo dīnɡ zhe miànzhuō mà mà liè liè.)
**Usage Examples:**
- **English:** After missing the train, John seethed about the terrible service.
- **Chinese:** 因为误了火车，约翰气得一直在大骂糟糕的服务。
(Yīnwèi wù le huǒchē, Jiānghónɡ qì de zài yīzhāi zài dà mà zāoɡāo de fúwù.)
- **English:** Her boss's criticism made her seethe with anger.
- **Chinese:** 老板的批评让她愤怒到极点。
(Lǎobǎn de pínbié ràng tā fènghuì zhídào jídiǎn.)
**Additional Notes:**
- "Seethe" can be slang, and in more casual language, one might say "boil over" or "get worked up" as alternatives.
- It's often used in contexts where the emotion is not just felt but visibly apparent to others.
