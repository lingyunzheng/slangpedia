## 英文 slang: seethe
### Bilingual Explanation
**英文**: seethe
**中文**: 像是冒蒸气；愤怒难忍
**Explanation**: This slang is often used to describe a situation where someone is extremely upset or angry but hasn't yet exploded into anger. It can also metaphorically describe a rising tension or a brewing problem. 
Example Sentences:
- **英文**: He was seething with anger after the meeting.
- **中文**: 会议之后他气得几乎要喷火。
- **英文**: The city was seething with unrest due to the new policy.
- **中文**: 新政出台后，整个城市都处于极大的不安之中。
