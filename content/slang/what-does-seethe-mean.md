## Seethe (verb)
### English
**Meaning:** To be extremely annoyed or irritated, often to the point of explosive anger.
**Example Sentences:**
- "Ever since he lost his job, he's been seething at his boss." 
- "My roommate left the lights on all night, and I'm really seething about it."
### Slang Explanations**
- **中文：** 炸毛/冒火
- **Explanation:** “炸毛”和“冒火”都是用来形容一个人因为受到极大的刺激或者不顺心的事情而感到非常生气，像是毛被撩到一样炸开，或是火焰喷薄而出一样。这两个词形象地表现出一种愤怒的情绪高涨的状态，类似于英文中的“seethe”。
- **中文（广东话）：** 酱爆
- **Explanation:** “酱爆”是个粤语词汇，用来形容某人因为一件事情或者某种状态而非常生气，生气的程度达到爆发的程度。酱爆既可以形容愤怒，也可以形容情绪上的激动，用这种表达方式显得较为随意和亲近。
### Additional Contexts
- **In terms of imagery:** 
- You might visualize the person’s hair standing on end, or steam coming out of their ears, indicating intense anger or irritation.
- **In daily usage:** When someone is really seething, they might be clenching their fists, tapping their foot, or talking very fast and aggressively.
- **Synonyms:** boiling mad, furious, livid, beside oneself.
