## Slang Explanation: Seethe
### English
- **Definition**: To seethe means to feel anger or frustration so intensely that one's face or actions reflect it. It can also be used to describe something or someone that is full of intense, hidden anger or energy.
- **Example**: "She was seething when she heard about the delay, but there was no one around to tell that her face was red and her fists were clenched."
- **Synonyms**: Fume, simmer, boil, rage, incandesce
### Chinese
- **定义**: “ seethe”意指愤怒或沮丧到了极点，以至于面部或行为展现出这种情绪。也可以用来描述充满强烈但隐藏的怒气或能量的人或事物。
- **例句**：“她听说项目延期后气得脸都红了，拳头也紧握，周围没人时她的愤怒之情才得以显露。”
- **近义词**：火冒三丈（fume), 煞气冲天（simmer), 怒火中烧（boil), 愤怒得咬牙切齿（rage), 气得全身发热（incandesce)
