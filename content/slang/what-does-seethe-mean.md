## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Seethe
**English:** Seethe  
**中文:** 尾巴乱甩 (Translation: Wagging Tail)  
**Explanation:**  
- **English:** To seethe means to be intensely angry or upset in an almost uncontrollable way, often expressing this anger through obvious signs like clenching fists or grinding teeth.
- **中文:** "尾巴乱甩" 形容的是一个生气到极致的状态，通过不停地挥动尾巴来比喻这种状态，常用来形容人感到非常生气或不满，但又找不到合适的出口。
In informal contexts, "seethe" might be used to describe a person who is so angry that their facial expressions or body movements give away their inner turmoil. Similarly, "尾巴乱甩" is a playful way to describe someone who is extremely upset and might be visibly agitated.
Example usage:  
- English: She was seething with anger at the way they treated her.  
- 中文: 她被他们那样对待得气得尾巴乱甩。
