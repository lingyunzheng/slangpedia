## 英文 slang: Seethe
### 中文 slang: 抹鼻子
- **Meaning**: When someone is extremely angry and agitated, to the point where their anger is almost visible.
- **口语解释**: 当一个人非常生气, 气得恨不得要用鼻子去抹额头上的火气。
- **Example**: After hearing the bad news, he started seething, and his hands were clenched into fists.
- 听到这个噩耗后，他开始气得直抹鼻子，双手紧握成拳。
---
## 英文 slang: Seethe
### 中文 slang: 煞气冲天
- **Meaning**: Describing someone who is extremely angry, with a feeling of fury that seems to emanate from them.
- **口语解释**: 形容一个人生气得好像浑身散发出愤怒的气息。
- **Example**: He was so mad at the mistake he made that his anger seemed to seethe from every pore of his body.
- 他对自己的错误气得非常厉害，愤怒仿佛从身体的每个毛孔中散发出来。
---
## 英文 slang: Seethe
### 中文 slang: 暴跳如雷
- **Meaning**: Expressing extreme anger, often to the point where a person appears to be ready to lash out.
- **口语解释**: 形容一个人非常生气，好像快要跳起来发泄愤怒一样。
- **Example**: When I told him the news, he jumped up and down in seething rage.
- 我告诉他那个消息时，他暴跳如雷，跳起来生气。
