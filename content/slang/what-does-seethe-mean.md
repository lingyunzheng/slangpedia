# Slang Explanations: "Seethe"
## English
**To seethe**: To express great anger or frustration, often internally, rather than outwardly.
### Example Usage:
- She was seething over the injustice, but didn't say a word.
## Spanish
**Sisear o ronronear de rabia**: Exprese gran cólera o frustración, a menudo internamente, en lugar de externamente.
### Ejemplo de Uso:
- Ella siseaba de rabia por la injusticia, sin decir una palabra.
