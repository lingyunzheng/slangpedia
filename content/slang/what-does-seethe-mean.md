## English: Seethe
**Meaning:** To seethe means to be extremely angry or irritated, usually to the point where you feel as if you might explode with anger. It can also imply a feeling of intense frustration.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Seethe:** 暴跳如雷 (bào tiào rú léi) / 气得要命 (qì de yào mìng)  
- **Pinyin:** bào tiào rú léi, qì de yào mìng
- **Literal Translation:** 暴跳如雷 (bào tiào rú léi) means "to jump like a thunderbolt" and is used when someone is extremely angry, while 气得要命 (qì de yào mìng) means "so angry that one might die" and is used to express extreme anger and frustration.
## Example Usage:
- "Ever since she learned about the promotion going to someone else, she's been seething."
- **Bilingual Example:** "自从她知道晋升机会给了别人，她就气得要命。" (Zìcun tā zhīdào jīngshēn jīnhuò gěide rén, tā jiù qì de yào mìng.)
