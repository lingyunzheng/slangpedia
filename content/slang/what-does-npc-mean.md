## NPC (非玩家角色 / Fēi Yǎqiú Jiǎorén)
### English:
**NPC** stands for "Non-Player Character." This term is commonly used in video games and role-playing games (RPGs). NPCs are characters controlled by the game's software, not by individual players. They often provide quests, information, or items to the player.
### Chinese:
**NPC**（非玩家角色）指的是“非玩家控制的角色”。这个术语常用于电子游戏和角色扮演游戏（RPG）中。NPC是由游戏软件控制的角色，而不是由玩家控制。他们通常会给玩家提供任务、信息或物品。
Example Usage:
- **English**: The quest-giver in the castle is an NPC.
- **Chinese**: 城堡里的任务发布者是NPC。
