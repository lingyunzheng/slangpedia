```markdown
### NPC (Non-Player Character)
- **English**: A character in a video game, role-playing game, or other medium, who is controlled by the game itself, rather than by a human player.
- **Chinese**: 游戏中的非玩家角色，由游戏本身控制，而不是由玩家控制。通常用于角色扮演游戏、桌面游戏或在线游戏中。
```
In these explanations:
- NPC is the term in English.
- NPC (非玩家角色, fēi wán jiǎo zéi) is the term in Chinese.
- Short explanations are provided for both contexts to give a basic understanding.
