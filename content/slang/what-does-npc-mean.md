## NPC
### English
**NPC:** **Non-Player Character**  
These are characters in video games, role-playing games, or other interactive media who are controlled by the game itself or by another player. They often exist to interact with the player character, provide quests, or serve as shopkeepers or other support functions.
*Example:* When playing an MMORPG, you often encounter NPCs who offer you quests to complete.
### Chinese
**NPC:** **非玩家角色** (Fēi yùánhéjiǎoróu)  
这些是视频游戏、角色扮演游戏或其他互动媒体中的角色，由游戏本身或其他玩家控制。他们通常是为了与玩家角色互动、提供任务或充当商店老板或其他支持功能的。
*例句:* 在玩MMORPG时，你经常遇到提供任务的NPC。
