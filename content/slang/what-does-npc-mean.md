### NPC
**English:** Non-Player Character  
**Chinese:** 非玩家角色 (Fēi Yóuyán Juéализатор)
**Meaning:**
- **English:** An NPC is a character in a video game, role-playing game, or other digital medium that is controlled by the game itself, as opposed to a player-controlled character. NPCs often serve roles such as providing quests, selling items, or guiding the player through the story.
- **Chinese:** NPC是电子游戏中由游戏自身控制的角色，而不是由玩家控制的。这类角色通常负责提供任务、售卖物品或引导玩家推进故事情节。
**Explanation:**
- **English:** You'll often encounter NPCs in your adventure, they might ask for your help, offer you quests, or give advice. Some NPCs might even be enemies!
- **Chinese:** 在你的探险过程中，你经常会遇到NPC，他们可能会请求你的帮助、给你提供任务，或者提供一些建议。有些NPC甚至可能成为你的敌人！
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** Kill the NPC who blocks the path.
- **Chinese:** 杀掉挡住道路的NPC。
**Context:**
- **English:** Many games and stories rely heavily on the interaction between players and NPCs.
- **Chinese:** 很多游戏和故事都非常依赖玩家与NPC之间的互动。
