### NPC
**English:** 
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," which originally comes from the gaming world. It refers to any character within a game that is controlled by the computer, as opposed to a player-controlled character. Over time, the term has spread to other contexts, often used to describe someone who is not directly involved in the proceedings or isn't a key player (no pun intended). 
**Chinese:**
NPC意为"非玩家角色"（Non-Player Character），最初来自游戏领域，指的是游戏中由电脑控制的角色，而非玩家操控的角色。随着时间的推移，这个词的应用范围也不断扩大，在其他的语境中，NPC常用来形容那些未直接参与或不是关键角色的人。
