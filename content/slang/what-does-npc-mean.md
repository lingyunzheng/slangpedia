### NPC
**English:** NPC  
**Chinese:** NPC (玩家不可交互角色 / NPC, 玩家不可互动角色)  
**Explanation:**  
- **English:** NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In games, these are any characters that a player can't control directly. NPCs can include shopkeepers, dialogue participants, or quest givers, among others.
- **Chinese:** NPC 通常是指游戏中的“非玩家角色”。玩家无法直接控制这些角色。NPC 可以是商店老板、对话对象或任务发布者等。
