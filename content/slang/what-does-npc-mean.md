# NPC
## English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. In video games and role-playing games, an NPC is a character that is controlled by the game and not by a player. Slang can refer to someone who is not very engaging or interesting, often used in social contexts to describe someone who isn't very interactive or dynamic.
**Example**: "He's just an NPC in the office, never really joining in the team activities."
## 中文
**NPC**：非玩家角色。在电子游戏和角色扮演游戏中的 NPC 是由游戏本身控制，而不是由玩家控制的角色。俚语中常用来形容那些不那么引人注目或有趣的家伙，特别是在社交场合中指那些不太活跃、互动性不强的人。
**例句**：他在公司里就像NPC一样，从不参与团队活动。
