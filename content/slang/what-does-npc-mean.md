## NPC (Non-Player Character)
### 中文解释
NPC 是游戏术语，中文称为“非玩家角色”。在游戏中，NPC 是那些由游戏预先设定并操控的角色，玩家可以与其互动，但无法真正控制他们作为自己的游戏角色。常见的NPC有任务发布者、NPC商人、帮助解释游戏世界的NPC等。
### 英文解释
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character" in gaming terminology. In games, NPCs are characters that are pre-programmed and controlled by the game, which players can interact with but cannot control as their own in-game character. Common examples of NPCs include quest givers, NPC merchants, and NPCs that help explain the game world.
### Usage Example
- 中文例子：我刚接到一个NPC的任务，要去找某个特定的宝物。
- 英文例子：I just got a quest from an NPC that I need to find a specific treasure.
