# NPC
**English Slang Explanation:**
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In gaming and role-playing contexts, NPCs are the characters controlled by the game or other players, as opposed to the player character (PC) which is controlled by the player. NPCs often have predefined dialogues and actions, and they can be crucial for story progression or just providing comic relief.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
NPC（非玩家角色）是指在游戏或角色扮演环境中，由游戏本身或其他玩家控制的角色，与由玩家控制的主角（PC）相对。NPC通常有预设的对话和行为，它们对于故事情节的发展或者仅仅是提供轻松的对话都至关重要。
