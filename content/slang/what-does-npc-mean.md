### NPC
- **English:** Not a Player Character
- **Explanation:** In the realm of role-playing games (RPGs), an NPC stands for a Non-Player Character. These are characters within the game that are controlled by the game itself, not by individual players. NPCs often provide quests, sell items, or give information to the main player characters. They can range from helpful townsfolk to menacing enemies, but they are not the player-controlled entities (PCs) that the player actively controls and plays as.
---
### NPC
- **中文:** 非玩家角色
- **解释:** 在角色扮演游戏（RPG）中，NPC（Non-Player Character，非玩家角色）指的是游戏内置的、而非由玩家直接操控的虚拟人物。角色NPC在游戏中提供任务、出售物品或提供信息给主要玩家角色。他们可以是友好的村民，也可以是凶恶的敌人，但并不是玩家直接控制和操控的游戏主角（PC）。
