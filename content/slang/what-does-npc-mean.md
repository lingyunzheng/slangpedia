# NPC
## English
**NPC (Non-Player Character)**: Refers to a character in a video game, role-playing game, or other fictional setting that is controlled by the game's software rather than by a human player. These characters often fill roles such as quests, providing information, selling items, or serving as non-combat support.
## 中文
**NPC（非玩家角色）**：指在电子游戏中由程序控制而非玩家控制的人物。这类角色通常负责诸如任务、提供信息、售卖物品或担任非战斗支持等角色。
