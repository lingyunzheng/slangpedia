### NPC (Non-Player Character)
#### English Slang Explanation:
NPC is a term widely used in gaming and role-playing contexts. It refers to characters in a game or a story who are not controlled by a player but instead are controlled by the game AI or a game master. These characters often play minor roles such as vendors, quest givers, or minor antagonists. In casual conversation, "NPC" is often used to describe someone who is not contributing significantly to the conversation or action at hand, i.e., they are essentially a "non-player" in the situation.
#### Chinese Slang Explanation:
NPC是指游戏中由计算机程序或游戏主持人控制而非玩家操控的角色。这些角色通常在游戏或故事中扮演一些次要的角色，比如商人、任务接取人或小恶人。在日常对话中，“NPC”通常用来形容那些在对话或事件中不起重要作用的人，可以类比为“局外人”或“无关紧要的人”。
