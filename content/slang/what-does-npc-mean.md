## NPC (Non-Player Character)
- **中文解释**: NPC是指在游戏中扮演非玩家角色的虚拟人物。这些角色通常由游戏系统控制，玩家可以与其互动但不能直接控制它们。
- **英文解释**: NPC stands for Non-Player Character, which refers to the fictional characters in video games that are controlled by the game system rather than by players. These characters can interact with players but cannot be directly controlled by them.
### Examples:
- **中文例子**: 在《魔兽世界》中，你会发现许多NPC，如商人、任务提供者和守卫。
- **英文例子**: In World of Warcraft, you'll encounter many NPCs like merchants, quest givers, and guards.
