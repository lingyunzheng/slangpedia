# NPC
**中英文解释：**
- **NPC** (Non-Player Character)
- **中文解释：** NPC 是“非玩家角色”的缩写。在游戏中，NPC 包括了玩家之外的所有人物角色。这些角色通常不参与游戏进程的主要任务，但可以提供任务、信息或交易物品。
- **英文解释:** NPC stands for "Non-Player Character". In games, NPC refers to all the character roles aside from the player. These characters typically do not participate in the main game quests, but they can provide tasks, information, or trade items.
