# NPC (Non-Player Character)
## English
**NPC**: A term commonly used in role-playing games (RPGs), video games, or live-action role-playing (LARP) games. It refers to any character in the game who is controlled by the game itself (in digital games) or by another player (in live-action games), as opposed to Player Characters (PCs) which are controlled by human players.
### Example:
"In the game, whenever you need to buy a potion or get directions, you'll encounter NPCs. They do the grunt work of making the world feel vibrant and populated."
## 中文
**NPC**：一个常用于角色扮演游戏（RPG）、电子游戏或即兴扮演角色扮演游戏（LARP）中的术语。指游戏中由游戏本身（在电子游戏中）或由其他玩家（在即兴扮演游戏中）控制的角色，与由人类玩家控制的角色（PC）相对。
### 示例：
“在游戏中，当你需要购买药水或获取方向时，将会遇到NPC。他们使世界充满活力并且显得更为充实。”
