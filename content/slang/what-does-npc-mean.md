## NPC
**English:** Non-Player Character
An NPC is a character in a video game, role-playing game, or other interactive media that is controlled by the computer or the game's designers, rather than a player. Pirates, aliens, and townsfolk are all examples of NPCs. They often have pre-programmed dialogues or behaviors and can be interacted with within the game's environment.
**中文:** 非玩家角色
NPC 是视频游戏、角色扮演游戏或其他互动媒体中由电脑或游戏设计者控制而非由玩家操控的角色。海盗、外星人和城镇居民都是 NPC 的例子。它们通常有预设的对话或行为，并可在游戏环境中与玩家互动。
