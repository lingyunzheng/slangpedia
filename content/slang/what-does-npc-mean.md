## NPC (Non-Player Character)
### Chinese
**NPC**：非玩家角色，在游戏中指的是玩家之外的角色。他们通常由系统控制，执行特定的任务或剧情推动。在网咖聊天或游戏论坛中，玩家可能用“NPC”指代那些缺乏游戏技能或经验不足的玩家。
### English
**NPC** (Non-Player Character): In games, it refers to characters that the players are not controlling. These characters are typically managed by the system, tasked with specific objectives or advancing the story. In internet cafes or gaming forums, players might use "NPC" to describe players who lack game skills or have insufficient experience.
