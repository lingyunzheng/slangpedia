## NPC
### English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. In gaming or role-playing contexts, these are characters controlled by the game itself or a game master, as opposed to player-controlled characters. Slang usage can extend to refer to anyone with a stereotypically passive or uninteresting personality.
### 中文
**NPC**: 非玩家角色。在游戏中或角色扮演的背景下，这些是游戏本身或游戏主持人控制的角色，而不是玩家控制的角色。该术语的俚语用法可以扩展到指那些性格刻板或无趣的人。
