```markdown
### NPC
**English:** NPC  
**Chinese:** NPC  
**Explanation:**  
In gaming, NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." It refers to any character in a video game, story, or role-playing game that is controlled by the game itself, not by another player. NPCs often have dialogue, quests, or other interactions available for the player. They can range from friendly townsfolk to hostile enemies or even quest-givers.
In a broader sense, NPC can also refer to someone who is perceived as being non-essential, unhelpful, or less interesting in social or online contexts. They are often the people who are there but not actively contributing to the conversation or situation.
Example in a sentence:
- **English:** He was just an NPC in the story; no one really cared about what he said.
- **Chinese:** 他只是故事中的NPC，没人真的在意他说什么。
```

```markdown
### 逛吃
**English:** ChongChi (Browse and Eat)  
**Chinese:** 逛吃 (Gàng/chī)  
**Explanation:**  
ChongChi or 逛吃 (gàng/chī) is a popular term in China, referring to the activity of leisurely exploring and eating at various food stalls, restaurants, or food courts, often in a casual and relaxed manner. The term captures the essence of enjoying food while wandering around, possibly taking time to try multiple dishes or just wandering without a specific destination or purpose.
Example in a sentence:
- **English:** She loves to chongchi on her days off.
- **Chinese:** 她喜欢在休息日逛吃。
```

```markdown
### 打酱油
**English:** Da Soya Sauce (To Be Present but Inactive)  
**Chinese:** 打酱油 (Dǎ jiào yóu)  
**Explanation:**  
打酱油 (dǎ jiào yóu) is a humorous way to describe someone who is present but not actively participating in an activity, often in a situation where their presence might be perceived as unnecessary or unhelpful. The term comes from the idea of someone who is "just there" like a bottle of soy sauce in a kitchen, which serves a purpose but doesn't actively do anything.
Example in a sentence:
- **English:** He's just here to watch; he's not going to do anything, so he's just like a bottle of soy sauce.
- **Chinese:** 他只是来看热闹的，不去做任何事情，就像一瓶酱油一样。
```
