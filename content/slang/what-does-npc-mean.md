## NPC
**English:**
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In video games, these are the characters controlled by the game itself rather than by a player. NPCs often serve to provide quests, information, or other interactions within the game world.
**Chinese:**
NPC 是“非玩家角色”的缩写。在游戏中，这些角色由游戏本身控制，而不是由玩家控制。NPC 往往用来提供任务、信息或其他游戏世界中的互动。
### Example Usage:
- **English:** The NPC at the portal will guide you through the mystical realm.
- **Chinese:** 神秘领域的传送门 NPC 会引导你穿过那个传送门。
