## NPC
### English
**NPC:** An acronym for "Non-Player Character," which refers to characters in a video game, role-playing game, or other gaming or interactive contexts who are controlled by the game itself rather than by another player. NPCs often provide quests, information, or are part of the game's story.
### 中文
**NPC:** 网络用语，来自“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character）的缩写，主要用于指代电子游戏、角色扮演游戏或其他互动游戏中由游戏本身控制而非由玩家操控的角色。NPC 经常用来提供任务、信息或作为游戏剧情的一部分。
