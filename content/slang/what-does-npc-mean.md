## NPC
**Chinese:** NPC  
**English:** NPC  
### Explanation:
- **Chinese:** NPC 是游戏中常见的一种缩写，代表非玩家角色。这些角色通常由系统控制，在游戏情节中起辅助或推动作用。举例来说，你会遇到提供任务、售卖物品或进行对话的NPC。
- **English:** NPC is a common abbreviation in games that stands for Non-Player Character. NPCs are usually controlled by the game system and play auxiliary or plot-driving roles. You might encounter NPCs who provide quests, sell items, or engage in conversation.
### Example Usage:
- **中文例句：** 我在游戏里遇到了一个NPC，他告诉我今天晚上有神秘的怪兽会出现。
- **English Example:** In the game, I met an NPC who told me that a mysterious monster would appear tonight.
