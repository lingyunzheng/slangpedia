### NPC
- **English Slang Explanation:** NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In gaming and role-playing contexts, NPCs are characters you interact with but do not control directly. These characters can provide quests, shops, or just idle conversations. 
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:** NPC，即“非玩家角色”。在游戏和角色扮演中，NPC是你可以与其互动但不能直接控制的角色。他们可以提供任务、售卖物品或只是进行闲聊。
