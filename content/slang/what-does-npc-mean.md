## NPC
### English Explanation
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," a term used in video games, role-playing games, and other interactive entertainment contexts. NPCs are characters in the game that are controlled by the game itself rather than by players. NPCs can interact with the player, provide quests or information, or simply move around the game world. In some games, NPCs can be essential to the storyline and have their own personalities and behaviors.
### Chinese Explanation
NPC是“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character）的缩写，常用于视频游戏、角色扮演游戏和其他互动娱乐的上下文中。NPC是指游戏中的角色由游戏本身控制，而不是由玩家控制。NPC可以与玩家互动、提供任务或信息，或在游戏世界中移动。在某些游戏中，NPC对故事情节至关重要，他们有自己的个性和行为。
**举例：** 在《魔兽世界》中，你会遇到许多NPC，他们会指引你完成任务或售卖你需要的道具。
