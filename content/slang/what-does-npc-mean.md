# NPC
**NPC** - **Non-Player Character**  
- **English**: Refers to characters in a game, movie, or story that are not controlled by a player. These characters often serve as companions, enemies, or point to important plot elements.
- **Chinese**: 指游戏、电影或故事中不由玩家控制的角色。这类角色通常作为同伴、对手或重要剧情线索出现。
