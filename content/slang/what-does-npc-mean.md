```markdown
# NPC (Non-Player Character)
## English Explanation
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In video games, role-playing games (RPGs), and other game genres, NPCs are characters that are controlled by the game and not the players. These characters often have predefined roles, such as providing quests, selling items, or reacting to player actions in a predetermined way.
## Chinese Explanation
NPC代表“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character，简称NPC）。在游戏中，特别是在角色扮演游戏（RPG）和其他多种游戏中，NPC是指由游戏控制而不是由玩家操作的角色。这些角色通常具有预定义的角色和行为模式，例如提供任务、售卖物品或按照预设的方式对玩家的行为作出反应。
```
