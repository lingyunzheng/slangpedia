# NPC
## English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. In video games or role-playing games (RPGs), NPCs are characters that are controlled by the game and not by players. They can appear as allies, enemies, or simply as part of the scenery. Slang usage: Characters other than the main player character, often used to refer to side characters or extras.
Example: "Why did the NPC heal me? Could be a plot twist!"
## Chinese
**NPC**: 非玩家角色 (fēi yòuy Bürger). 在电脑游戏或角色扮演游戏中，NPC是被游戏控制而非玩家控制的角色。他们可以作为盟友、敌人或仅仅是背景的一部分出现。俚语用法：除主线玩家角色之外的角色，常用来指代配角或配景人物。
例句：“NPC 为什么帮我治疗？可能是剧情转折吧！”
