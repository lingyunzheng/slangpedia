# NPC
## 中文解释
NPC 是网络游戏中非玩家角色（Non-Player Character）的缩写。在游戏中，NPC 是由游戏开发者预先设定的角色，不同于玩家控制的角色。它们通常会在特定场景中为玩家提供任务指引、物品交易或其他类型的服务。
## 英文 Explanation
NPC stands for Non-Player Character, which is a term commonly used in video games, especially online ones. NPCs are characters created by game developers that do not belong to the player and are controlled by the game. They often provide players with quests, items, or other forms of assistance in specific locations within the game.
