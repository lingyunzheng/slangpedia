## NPC
**English Slang Explanation:**
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," which is a term used in video games, role-playing games (RPGs), and other narrative-driven games. NPCs are characters in the game that are not controlled by a player, but are controlled by the game itself. They often provide quests, serve as important in-game guides, or have dialogues that can affect the story. 
**Chinglish Slang Explanation:**
NPC就是“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character）。这个词常用于电子游戏、角色扮演游戏（RPG）以及其他有故事背景的游戏里面。NPC是游戏里由游戏自己控制而不是由玩家控制的角色。他们通常会提供任务、成为游戏中的重要指引，或者是有对话可以影响故事走向。
