## NPC
### English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. In video games, an NPC is any character that the player cannot control directly. These characters often interact with the player, provide quests, or offer information. Example: "I talked to the old guy at the inn, and he was an NPC who gave me a quest."
### 中文
**NPC**: 非玩家角色。在电子游戏中，NPC（Non-Player Character）指玩家不能直接控制的角色。这些角色通常会与玩家互动、提供任务或提供信息。例如：“我跟了客栈里的老头说话，结果发现他是个NPC，给了我一个任务。”
