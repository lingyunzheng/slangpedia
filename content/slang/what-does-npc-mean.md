## NPC (Non-Player Character)
### English Slang Explanation
NPC is a term commonly used in gaming, especially in online role-playing games (RPGs) or massively multiplayer online games (MMOs). It refers to characters that are not controlled by a human player but are instead controlled by the computer. NPCs can be quest-givers, shopkeepers, monsters, or any other character that exists within the game environment.
### Chinese Slang Explanation
NPC（非玩家角色，Non-Player Character）这个词在游戏尤其是在线角色扮演游戏或大型多人在线角色扮演游戏（MMO）中非常常见。它指的是由计算机控制而非由玩家操作的角色。NPC可以是任务提供者、商家、怪物或其他存在于游戏环境中的任何角色。
