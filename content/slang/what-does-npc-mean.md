# NPC
## Chinese
**NPC**: NPC 是玩家非玩家角色（Non-Player Character）的缩写。在游戏中，NPC 是与玩家互动的非玩家控制的角色。它们可能提供任务、出售物品或者提供信息。
## English
**NPC**: NPC stands for Non-Player Character, which is a shortened term commonly used in video games. NPCs are characters in the game that players can interact with but are not controlled by players. They might offer quests, sell items, or provide information.
