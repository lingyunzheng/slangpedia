## NPC (non-player character)
### 中文解释
NPC 是“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character）的缩写，通常用于角色扮演游戏（RPG）、电子游戏、在线论坛、电影和动画等领域。NPC 并不是玩家角色，而是由游戏设计者预先设定好并由系统控制的虚拟角色。NPC 可以提供任务、信息或情感支持等。在一些联机游戏中，NPC 也可能包括AI控制的其他玩家。
### 英文解释
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," a term commonly used in role-playing games (RPGs), video games, online forums, films, and animations, among other fields. NPCs are the characters pre-set by the game designers that are controlled by the system. Unlike player characters, NPCs do not control their own actions based on player input. They can offer quests, information, or emotional support. In some online games, NPCs can also include AI-controlled other players.
