## NPC
### English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. In games, especially in role-playing games (RPGs) and online games, NPCs are characters controlled by the computer rather than by other players. NPCs often provide quests, sell items, or offer information to the player.
### 中文
**NPC**: 非玩家角色。在游戏里，特别是角色扮演游戏（RPG）和在线游戏中，NPC指由电脑控制而不由其他玩家控制的角色。NPC通常提供任务、售卖物品或向玩家提供信息。
