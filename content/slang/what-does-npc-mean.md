### NPC
**English:** NPC  
**Chinese:** NPC (NPC: 非玩家角色)  
**Explanation:**
- **English:** In the context of video games and role-playing games (RPGs), an NPC (Non-Player Character) is a character controlled by the game, as opposed to a PC (Player Character) controlled by the player. NPCs often give quests, provide information, or sell items.
- **Chinese:** 在电子游戏和角色扮演游戏中，NPC（非玩家角色）是指由游戏控制的角色，与玩家控制的角色PC（玩家角色）相对。NPC通常会提供任务、提供信息或者出售物品。
