## NPC
**中文解释**: NPC 是"Non-Player Character"的缩写，在游戏中指不是由玩家控制的角色，通常是固定的剧情角色或守关boss。
**英文解释**: NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," which refers to characters in a game that are not controlled by players. Think of them as the static characters or permanent enemies/guardians that you encounter in the game.
**例句**: 
- 中文：你在游戏中遇到了一个提供任务的NPC。
- English: In the game, you encounter an NPC who gives you a quest.
