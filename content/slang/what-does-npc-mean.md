### NPC
**English Slang Explanation**: NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." It's commonly used in the context of video games, tabletop games, and role-playing scenarios to refer to characters that are not controlled by a player. NPCs can be friendly, neutral, or hostile and often provide quests, dialogue choices, or items.
**Chinese Slang Explanation**: NPC代表“非玩家角色”。这个词经常用于电子游戏、桌面游戏和角色扮演场景中，指的是玩家以外的其他角色。NPC可以是友好的、中立的或敌对的，往往提供任务、对话选项或物品。
