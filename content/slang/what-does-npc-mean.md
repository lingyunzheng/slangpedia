Sure, here is a bilingual explanation in Markdown format for the term "NPC":
```markdown
# NPC
## English
**NPC**: Non-Player Character. A character in a game, story, or simulation that is controlled by the game or story, as opposed to a player character. Commonly used in role-playing games, video games, and other interactive media.
## 中文
**NPC**：非玩家角色（Non-Player Character）。在游戏中、故事或模拟中由游戏或故事控制的角色，与玩家角色相对。常见于角色扮演游戏、视频游戏和其他交互式媒体中。
```
