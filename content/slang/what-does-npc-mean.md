# NPC (Non-Player Character)
## English
**Definition:** A character in a game, story, or online community that is controlled by the game's programming, script, or an AI, as opposed to being controlled by a player. NPCs often exist in video games and role-playing games to provide quests, information, or simply to populate the world.
**Example:** "I got helpful tips from a friendly NPC who was more insightful than many real players!"
## Chinese
**定义：** 游戏、故事或在线社区中的一个角色，由游戏的程序、脚本或AI控制，而不是被玩家控制。NPC通常存在于视频游戏中，用于提供任务、信息，或者仅仅是使世界充满人物。
**示例：** "我从一位友善的NPC那里得到了有用的建议，这位NPC比许多真正的玩家更有见识！"
