Sure, here's the explanation for "NPC" in both English and Chinese markdown format:
```markdown
## NPC
### English
**NPC:** A non-player character in video games, role-playing games, or other interactive media. These are characters controlled by the game's software rather than by other players.
### 中文
**NPC:** 角色扮演游戏或电子游戏中由游戏软件控制的非玩家角色。这些角色不是由其他玩家控制的。
```
