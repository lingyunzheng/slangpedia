## NPC (Non-Player Character)
### English
**NPC**: A character in a game or virtual world controlled by the game itself or software, as opposed to a Player-Character (character controlled by a player).
**Example**: In a role-playing game, you might encounter an NPC who offers you quests when you reach their village.
### Chinese
**NPC**: 游戏或虚拟世界中的非玩家角色，由游戏本身或软件控制，与玩家控制的角色（玩家角色）相对。
**示例**：在一款角色扮演游戏里，当你到达某个村庄时，可能会遇到一个NPC，他会给你提供任务。
