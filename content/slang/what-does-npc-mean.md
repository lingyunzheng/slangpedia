## NPC
### English
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In video games, movies, and role-playing scenarios, NPCs are characters that you don't control and are usually pre-programmed or spoken by pre-recorded voices. They can provide quests, information, or simply interact with the player for various purposes.
### 中文
NPC 是“非玩家角色”的缩写。在电子游戏、电影和角色扮演场景中，NPC 是你无法控制的角色，通常由预设程序控制或通过预录的声音呈现。他们可以提供任务、信息，或者只是为了与玩家互动而存在。
**Examples:**
- "Hey, why didn't the NPC give me the quest?!"
- "She's just an NPC, ignore her."
