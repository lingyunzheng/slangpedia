## NPC
### English
**Definition:** NPC stands for "Non-Player Character," commonly used in video games, role-playing games, and other game-based contexts. NPCs are characters that can be interacted with by the player but are not controlled by a player themselves.
**Slang Explanation:** Think of NPCs as the minor characters in a story or game. They can give quests, sell items, or provide information, but unlike the main player character, they are not a part of the larger, controllable narrative.
### 中文
**定义：**NPC 是“Non-Player Character”的缩写，常用于视频游戏、角色扮演游戏以及其他基于游戏的情景中。NPC 是玩家可以与其互动但不是由玩家控制的角色。
**俚语解释：** 你可以把 NPC 看作是故事或游戏中那些较小的角色。他们可以提供任务、出售物品或提供信息，但与主要的可控制角色不同，他们并不参与更大的、可控的剧情发展。
