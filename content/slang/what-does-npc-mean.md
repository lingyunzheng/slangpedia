### NPC
**English Slang Explanation:**
NPC stands for "Non-Player Character." In video games and role-playing games (RPGs), NPCs are characters that are controlled by the game itself, as opposed to player characters (PCs) which are controlled by human players. NPCs can provide quests, sell items, give hints, or just populate the world.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
NPC是指“非玩家角色”（Non-Player Character），在游戏中，NPCs由游戏自己控制，与玩家角色（PC）相对。玩家角色由人类玩家控制。NPC可以在游戏中提供任务、售卖物品、提供线索或者仅仅是增加游戏世界的丰富性。
---
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** The quest-giver in this game is an NPC.
- **Chinese:** 这个游戏中提供任务的人物是一个NPC。
