```markdown
## Stan (Standard)
- **English**: A fan who is extremely dedicated and overly supportive to a particular person, team, or content creator, often to the point of obsession.
- Example: "When the fan started brigading after the artist announced a concert, everyone knew it was time to prepare because she's a real stan."
- **Chinese**: 桩 (zhuāng)，指极度忠诚和热烈支持某个人、团队或创作者的粉丝，有时会达到痴迷的程度。
- 例句：当粉丝在艺术家宣布演唱会时开始疯狂宣传，大家就知道需要做好准备，因为她是个忠实的桩。
```
Note: The term "stan" can carry a variety of connotations depending on the context, so the translation and explanation are based on common usage.
