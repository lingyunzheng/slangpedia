# Slang Term: "Stan"
## English Explanation
"Stan" is a slang term typically used to describe an obsessive fan or admirer of someone or something, often to an unhealthy or irrational extent. The term has its roots in the hip-hop and internet culture, where it became popular through its use by rapper Drake in his song "Stan" (2009).
### Example Sentence:
- She's a massive stan for Taylor Swift, even buying all her merchandise and showing up to every concert she can.
## Chinese Explanation
"Stan" 是一个俚语词汇，通常用来形容对某人或某事物极其痴迷的支持者，这种痴迷有时会变得不健康或不理性。这个词源于说唱文化和互联网文化中，著名说唱歌手德雷克 (Drake) 在他的歌曲《Stan》(2009) 中首次使用这一词汇后变得非常流行。
### 示例句子：
- 她对泰勒·斯威夫特（Taylor Swift）是铁杆粉丝，连所有的周边产品都买了，并且尽可能多地参加她的演唱会。
