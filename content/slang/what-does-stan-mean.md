### stan (北美俚语)
**English Explanation:**
- **Definition:** A hyperdedicated fan who is obsessed with and displays extreme loyalty to a particular person, brand, or celebrity.
- **Example Sentence:** "She's such a stan for Taylor Swift, always waiting in line for her concerts."
**Chinese Explanation:**
- **定义:** 一种过度投入的粉丝，对特定的个人、品牌或名人表现出极度的热情和忠诚。
- **例句:** "她对泰勒·斯威夫特是个铁杆粉丝，总是排长队等着她的演唱会。"
