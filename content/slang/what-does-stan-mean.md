# Stan
**English:** A dedicated and often obsessive fan of a particular person, thing, or concept.
**Chinese:** �重伤粉 (zhòng shāng gěng fěn) - 深入骨髓的狂热粉丝 (shēn rù gǔ-suǐ de kuáng rè fěn sī)
- **Explanation:** This term combines the idea of being critically injured (重伤, zhòng shāng) and the concept of being a fan (粉丝, fěn sī), implying a fan who is so devoted that they are irreversibly affected by the target (often in a positive way). It's used to describe fans who are so loyal that their lives are deeply tied to their favorite person or thing, much like someone who has been critically injured and now lives with the experience permanently.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "She's a stan for her favorite singer."
- Chinese: "她对她最喜爱的歌手是超级粉丝（重伤粉）" (Tā dàidà de gēhennifer shì jiā qīng fěn (zhòng shāng gěng fěn).)
