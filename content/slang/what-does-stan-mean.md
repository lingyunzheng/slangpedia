## Stan
### English
A dedicated and often obsessive fan or supporter of a particular person, team, or thing. Stans are known for their extreme loyalty and sometimes for engaging in competitive fan behavior or engaging in online conflicts over their favorite subjects.
**Example:**
- "Tom was such a stan for the band that he memorized all their lyrics and attended every concert they played."
### 中文
"斯坦"指的是对某个人、团队或事物极其忠诚和热衷的粉丝或支持者。斯坦通常非常忠诚，有时会表现出极端的粉丝行为，或者在网络空间上就他们喜欢的事物与他人发生争端。
**示例:**
- "乔治对那个乐队如此忠实，他都能背下所有的歌词，并且会去参加每一次他们的演唱会。"
