# Stan
## English
A devoted and often obsessive fan. Originated from the term used for a victim fixated on a perpetrator, it has evolved into a term for anyone who is excessively attached to a person or thing. For example, "Johnny is a real stan for Taylor Swift—showing up at every concert and memorizing all her songs."
## Chinese
一个极度痴迷和投入的fans。来自用于描述受害者对犯罪者过分依恋的术语，现在泛指任何人对某人或某物过分执着的情况。例如，“Johnny 是 Taylor Swift 的狂粉——每场演唱会都到场，并且背诵所有的歌。”
