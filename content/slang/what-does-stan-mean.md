# Stan
## English
A stan is a die-hard fan who is extremely devoted and loyal to a particular person, group, or thing. Stans will often support their idol or favourite team/goer through thick and thin, and they can be incredibly forceful in their admiration.
## Chinese
斯坦是一家死忠粉丝，对某个人、团体或事物表现出极度的忠诚和热爱。斯坦们会在偶像或喜欢的人/事物遇到困难时给予坚定的支持，他们的崇拜可以非常热烈和强大。
