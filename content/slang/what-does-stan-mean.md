# Stan
## English
**Definition**: A devoted and sometimes overly passionate fan or supporter of a particular person, group, or online trend. Stans are known for their extreme loyalty and often fanatic behavior.
**Example**: "Ever since the YouTube star announced a new channel, her stans have been posting and sharing updates everywhere."
## 中文
**定义**: 某个人、团队或网络趋势的极度忠诚且有时过度热情的支持者。斯坦Fans以其极端忠诚和有时的疯狂行为著称。
**示例**: "自从这位YouTuber宣布开设新频道以来，她的斯坦Fans们已经在各处发布和分享更新信息。"
