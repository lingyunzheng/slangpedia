## Stan (noun) 🇺🇸 / 🇨🇳
### English:
**Stan:** Obsessive fan behavior, typically displayed by someone excessively devoted to someone or something. A fan who shows extreme loyalty and sometimes unhealthy levels of devotion.
*Example:* "She's such a stan for her favorite singer, she attended every single concert and bought all their merch."
### 简体中文:
**Stan:** 表示狂热粉丝的行为，通常指某人对某个人或某事物表现出极度忠诚，有时这种忠诚会变得不健康。乐迷过度痴迷，表现出极端的献身精神。
*例句:* "她是她最爱歌手的狂热粉丝，出席了每场演唱会并购买了所有唱片和周边商品。"
