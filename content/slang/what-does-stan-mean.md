**English:** A die-hard fan or supporter who is exceedingly loyal and often obsessively devoted to a specific person, group, or cause.
**Chinese (简体中文):** 任命神/死忠粉，指极其忠诚并常常表现出痴迷支持某个特定的人、团体或事业的粉丝。
