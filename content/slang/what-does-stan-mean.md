# Stan
## English
A dedicated, often obsessive fan or supporter of a person, place, thing, or idea. The usage of the term refers to someone who displays an intense loyalty and enthusiasm, sometimes to an extreme degree, often bordering on obsession.
Example: She's such a stan for Taylor Swift, she memorized all the songs and performs them at karaoke nights.
## 简体中文
一个极度忠诚且热衷支持某人、某地、某物或某个想法的狂热粉丝或支持者。这个词的使用指代那些表现出极度热情和喜爱的人，有时会变得极端，甚至接近痴迷。
例句：她是泰勒·斯威夫特的铁杆粉丝，把所有歌都背下来了，还在卡拉OK晚会上表演。

# Stan
## English
Stands for "Stanford" which was used in early Internet Relay Chat (IRC) protocols, and due to its shortened nature, it was taken as a slang term to refer to someone who is especially passionate or obsessed with a person or thing.
Example: He's such a stan for that new comic series, he spends every spare minute reading and discussing it.
## 简体中文
“斯坦”源于早期的互联网即时聊天（IRC）协议，由于它的缩写性质，后来被用作俚语，指代一个特别热衷或痴迷于某人或某物的人。
例句：他对那套新漫画系列如此痴迷，以至于每有空闲时间都在读和讨论。
