# Slang Term: "Stan"
## English
**Definition**: A dedicated, often obsessive, fan or supporter of an individual, group, or ideals. The term is derived from the phrase "stand in line," suggesting that a fan is so dedicated that they are literally or metaphorically at the back of the line for their idol.
**Example**: "She's a true stan for Taylor Swift; she has every album and concert t-shirt."
## Chinese
**定义**：一个忠实的、有时近乎痴迷的粉丝或支持者。这个术语来源于短语 "stand in line"（排队等候），暗示粉丝对他们的偶像如此忠诚，以至于他们愿意在队列中耐心等待，无论是字面意义还是比喻意义。
**例句**：她是个 true stan，对泰勒·斯威夫特狂热支持，每一张专辑和每一件演唱会T恤都有。
