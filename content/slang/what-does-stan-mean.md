```markdown
# Stan (noun)
## English
A devoted and often obsessive fan. Someone who shows extreme loyalty and excessive support for a particular person, group, or brand to the point of potentially undesirable behavior. 
## Chinese
极度忠诚且常常是痴迷的粉丝。对某个特定的人、团体或品牌表现出极大的忠诚和支持，有时可能会表现出不正常的行为。
**Example Usage in a Sentence:**
- English: He's such a stan for Taylor Swift; he follows every detail of her life.
- Chinese: 他简直就是泰勒·斯威夫特的斯坦；他关注着她生活的每一个细节。
```
```markdown
# Stan (verb)
## English
To exhibit behavior typically associated with a stan—usually demonstrating extreme loyalty or support for someone.
## Chinese
表现出斯坦行为，通常表现出对某人的极度忠诚或支持。
**Example Usage in a Sentence:**
- English: She stan for that band since she first heard their music.
- Chinese: 她自从第一次听到这支乐队的音乐就成为他们的斯坦。
```
```markdown
# Stan (slang)
## English
A typically internet-born slang term derived from the first line of the song "Stan" by Eminem, where the speaker talks about the obsessive behavior of a fan. It's now widely used to describe any fan who may be excessively supportive or overly dedicated.
## Chinese
起源于Eminem的歌曲"Stan"第一句中的俚语，描述粉丝的痴迷行为。现在广泛用于描述任何表现出过度支持或过度忠诚的粉丝。
**Example Usage in a Sentence:**
- English: Her stanning of the new movie is starting to get weird.
- Chinese: 她对新电影的斯坦行为开始变得有点奇怪。
```
