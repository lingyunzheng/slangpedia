# Stan ( noun | 名词 )
**English:**
- **Definition:** A term originally used to describe an obsessive fan or supporter, often to the point of unhealthy dedication.
- **Example Sentence:** "Johnny has become such a stan for Taylor Swift that he attends every concert she performs."
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** 一个原本用来形容极端粉丝或支持者，有时他们的狂热已经超出了正常范围。
- **例句:** "简尼对泰勒·斯威夫特的粉丝文化已经到了斯坦的地步，所以每场她的演唱会他都会到场。"
