```markdown
## Stan
### English
A stan is a die-hard fan who is overly devoted to a particular person, especially an celebrity, and will defend them vigorously against any criticism or negative opinions.
### Chinese
斯坦 (Stan) 指的是极度忠实的一风扇，尤其是对于某位名人，他们不仅会支持该名人，还会积极捍卫他们不受批评或负面评价的影响。
---
Example usage:
- In English: "She's a real stan for her favorite actor."
- In Chinese: "她对她最喜爱的演员是个真正的斯坦。"
```
