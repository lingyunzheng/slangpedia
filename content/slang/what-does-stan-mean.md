### stan (English)
**Definition**: A term originally from the British English slang, popularized by the internet, especially among年轻群体. It's used to describe someone who is overly devoted or infatuated with a person, a brand, a celebrity, or a character.
**Bilingual Explanation**: 
- ** Stan (名次 / 爱慕者) **: 用来形容某人对某人、某个品牌、名人或角色有过度的依恋或迷恋。
- ** Etymology**: Derived from the word "stand" (立坚定地站着), which metaphorically means sticking with something or someone strongly.
- **Usage Example**: "She's such a stan for Taylor Swift that she goes to every concert and buys all her merchandise."
### stan (Chinese)
- **含义**: 这个词最初来源于英式英语俚语，后被互联网广泛使用，尤其是流行于年轻人之间。用来形容某人对某人、某个品牌、名人或角色有过度的依恋或迷恋。
- **根源**: 从单词“stand”（坚定地站着）演变而来，比喻上指坚定支持某件事物或某个人。
- **用法示例**: “她简直就是泰勒·斯威夫特的粉丝，每次演唱会都去，还买她所有的周边商品。”
