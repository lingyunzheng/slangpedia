## Stan
**English:** A dedicated or obsessed fan, often to the point of unhealthy behavior. Often used in contexts where a person shows extremely strong support or loyalty towards a particular subject, often to an irrational degree.
**Chinese:** 横粉丝 (hèng shēn mí) - 一个极为狂热或痴迷的粉丝，通常表现出过度支持或忠诚，以至于达到了不健康的程度。这个词多用于描述一个人对某个人或事物表现出过度强烈的拥护或忠诚，有时显得有些不理智。
