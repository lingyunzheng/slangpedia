# Stan
## English
A stan is a very dedicated fan who extremely supports or idolizes a particular celebrity, team, or public figure. The term often carries a connotation of intense and sometimes obsessive devotion. 
Example:
- "She's such a stan for Taylor Swift that she attends every concert she tours with."
## 中文
Stan 是一个非常有激情的粉丝，他们会极其支持或崇拜某个明星、团队或公众人物。这个词通常带有狂热和有时近乎执念的崇拜意味。
例句：
- "她对泰勒·斯威夫特的粉丝身份如此狂热，以至于她会参加每次巡演。"
