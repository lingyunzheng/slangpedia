# Stan
## English
A super loyal fan or supporter of a person, place, or thing, often to the point of obsession.
Example: "She's such a stan for Taylor Swift, she knows every lyric to every song."
## 中文
极度忠诚的粉丝或支持者，往往是狂热到极致的程度。
示例：对她来说，泰勒·斯威夫特就是她的偶像，她能背出每一首歌的每一个歌词。
