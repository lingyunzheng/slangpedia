## Stan (英式 / 美式)
### English
**Definition:** A dedicated and often obsessively passionate fan, typically a supportive one.
**Example:** "Jim is a real stan for their team; he's been at every game and spent thousands on gear."
### Chinese
**释义：** 一个非常热情且常常表现得有点痴迷的粉丝，通常是支持性的。
**例句：** "吉姆是真爱粉球队的人；他参加了每一场比赛，并且在装备上花费了数千元。"
