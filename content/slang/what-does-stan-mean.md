## Stan (英文：Stan)
**中文解释：** 崇拜者，是指极度忠诚或超级喜欢某个明星、球队或者特定事物的粉丝。这种热情有时候甚至会变得极端，例如对偶像的支持可以凌驾于理智判断之上，表现出固执或冒失的行为。
**英文解释：** A stan refers to someone who is extremely loyal or fanatical about a celebrity, sports team, or a specific thing. This devotion can sometimes become so intense that it overrides rational thinking, leading to displays of stubbornness or reckless behavior in support of their idol.
```
## Stan (英文：Stan)
**中文解释：** 也指一种短暂或短暂而狂热的兴趣，可能是对某个人、事物或品牌的喜爱狂热阶段。有时也可以用作形容词，形容某人的行为或态度明显表现出对某事物的过度痴迷或热衷，如“Stan对于这款手机的喜爱简直出人意料。”
**英文解释：** It can also refer to a brief or intense interest in a person, thing, or brand. Sometimes, it is used as an adjective to describe behavior or attitude that clearly shows an exaggerated enthusiasm or devotion to something, like "Stan was really taken with this phone model."
```
