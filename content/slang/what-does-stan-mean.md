```markdown
## stan (Stan)
### English
Stan is a highly dedicated fan of a specific person, often a celebrity or public figure. Stans are known for their intense loyalty, and they often engage in online support, discussions, and sometimes cyberbullying of opponents. 
### Simplified Chinese
斯坦 (Stan) 指的是极度忠诚于特定人物（通常是名人或公众人物）的粉丝。斯坦们以其强烈的忠诚度著称，他们喜欢在网上支持、讨论相关话题，有时甚至会对反对者进行网络霸凌。
### Example Usage
- **English:** She has so many stans—her numbers are insane!
- **Simplified Chinese:** 她有很多斯坦——她的粉丝数简直疯狂！
### Example Sentence
- **English:** He rejected a proposal to start a fan club because he didn’t want to appear too stan-like.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 他拒绝了一个粉丝俱乐部的提议，因为他不想让自己看起来太像斯坦。
```
### Additional Slang Variants
#### Stan Nation
- **English:** A community of fans of a specific public figure, often sharing a common passion or interest.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 以特定名人粉丝为主的社区，通常有共同的热情或兴趣。
#### Ambient Stan
- **English:** A fan who secretly supports a cause or person without drawing attention to themselves.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 不愿被注意但私下支持某个事业或人物的粉丝。
#### Reaction Stan
- **English:** A fan known for their quick and strong reactions to public figures or content.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 快速且强烈反应的粉丝，对名人或内容反应迅速而激烈。
```markdown
## Additional Context
Stan culture is often characterized by extreme levels of support or criticism. While some communities are more positive and supportive, others can be quite toxic, with fans engaging in malicious online behavior.
### Example Context
- **English:** The debate was so heated that even stans of opposing sides started arguing.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 争论如此激烈，以至于反对派的斯坦们也开始争吵。
```
