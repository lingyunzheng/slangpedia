# Stan
## English
A dedicated or obsessive fan of a particular person, group, or thing. Often used to describe someone who is overly supportive but may also exhibit obsessive or unhealthy behavior towards their subject of admiration.

Example:
- "He turned into a stan the moment he saw her perform."

## Chinese
一个极度投入或狂热支持某个人、群体或事物的粉丝。经常用来描述对所崇拜的对象表现出过度支持，但可能还会表现出过度或不健康行为的人。

例句：
- "他一看到她的表演就变成了她的 stan。"
