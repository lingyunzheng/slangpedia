## Stan
**English:** A devoted and often obsessed fan, typically characterized by intense and sometimes obsessive support or enthusiasm for a particular person, group, or thing.
**中文:** 超级忠实的粉丝，通常是表现出对某个特定的人、团体或事物有非常热情和有时甚至是过分的支持或热衷的狂热爱好者。
