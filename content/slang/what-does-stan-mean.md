# stan (同人，拼音：tóng rén)
## English Explanation
A "stan" is a term used to describe an obsessed fan who has a strong and often one-sided infatuation with a particular person, usually a celebrity, artist, or public figure. This term has been popularized in Internet culture and social media.
### Example Usage
- "He's such a stan for Taylor Swift; he wears her merchandise everywhere and talks about her non-stop!"
## Chinese Explanation
"stan" 指的是一个极端执着的粉丝，他们对某个人（通常是一位名人、艺术家或公众人物）有深厚且往往是单方面的迷恋。这个词在互联网文化和社交媒体中广为流传。
### 例句
- "他简直对泰勒·斯威夫特有病，无论走到哪都会穿她的周边产品，不停地谈论她！"
