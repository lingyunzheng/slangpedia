## Stan (English-Used Informally in Specific Contexts)
### In English, in relation to sports and fandom:
**Definition:** A stan is a die-hard fan of a particular person, team, or entity, often to the point of being excessively devoted or protective. They actively support and defend their favorite with intense loyalty, sometimes even in the face of criticism.
**Example:** "Emily is such a Stan for the Los Angeles Lakers; she's there for every home game and can't stand it when they lose."
### In Chinese, 干饭人 (Gàn fàn rén, literally "Rice Eater"):
**Definition:** 在中国网络流行文化中，"干饭人"习惯用来形容一位极其喜欢某人的粉丝。这种粉丝不仅支持自己喜爱的对象，而且在任何情况下都会为他们辩护，有时甚至显得有些过度热情或固执。
**例句:** “李华是真·干饭人，他不仅支持王思聪，还到处为他开脱，连网友的批评都无法改变他的态度。”
---
用干饭人来描述斯坦的特点，主要是强调这种极端的忠诚和支持，类似于英文中对 "stan" 的使用。
