# Stan (English/Chinese)
## English
"Stan" is a term that originated from an internet meme and refers to a devoted or overly obsessive fan, particularly when it comes to a celebrity, actor, or fictional character. The term has become widely used to describe someone who is extremely dedicated and shows all-consuming interest or support, often to the point of becoming irrational or causing harm to themselves or others.
## Chinese
“斯坦”这个词起源于网络梗，是指一个极度忠心或过度痴迷的粉丝，特别是在谈论名人、演员或虚构角色时。这个词通常用来描述那些极度投入、表现出所有消靡兴趣或支持的人，有时这种痴迷会变得不理智，甚至可能导致自己或他人受到伤害。
用法示例:
- "I was a stan for the band for years, but I'm over it now." 我曾经很久都是那支乐队的斯坦，但现在我已经没有那么痴迷了。
- "She's such a stan for her favorite actor that she enters his hotel room to get his autograph." 她对她的最喜欢的演员如此痴迷，以至于她闯入他的酒店房间只为得到他的亲笔签名。
