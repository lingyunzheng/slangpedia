# Stan (English-Chinese Slang Explanation)
## English
**Stan:** A term typically used to describe a level of obsessive or deeply obsessive fandom. It often refers to someone who shows excessive support or devotion to a particular celebrity, athlete, or brand.
### Example:
- "Jerry has a huge case of stan on Taylor Swift. He's bought every single album and attends all her concerts."
## 中文
**Stan：** 这个词通常用来形容一种过度迷恋或者极度迷恋粉丝的态度。它通常指一个人对某位明星、运动员或品牌的过度支持和忠诚。
### 示例：
- "Jerry 对泰勒·斯威夫特的迷恋程度堪比斯坦。他买了她所有的专辑，还去参加她的每场演唱会。"
