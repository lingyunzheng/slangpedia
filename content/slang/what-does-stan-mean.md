## Stan (English/Chinese)
### English
A stan is a dedicated and often obsessive fan of someone or something, particularly in the context of celebrity or pop culture. If you're a die-hard fan who will defend your favorite person or group against all criticisms and attacks, you're a stan.
**Example:**
"I'm a loyal stan of Taylor Swift! Her music brings me so much joy."
### Chinese
斯坦 (pronounced: "Stān") 描述的是一个非常执着和狂热的粉丝，通常是指对某位名人或某个流行文化项目的极度喜欢。如果你是一个铁杆粉丝，无论他人怎么批评攻击，你都会为你的偶像或团体挺身而出，那么你就是一位斯坦。
**例句:**
“我是泰勒·Swift的忠粉斯坦！她的音乐给我带来了很多快乐。”
