## Stan (Standard/Bastard)
**Definition:**
- **English:** A devoted, often obsessive, fan or supporter, sometimes with a jealous or ruthless streak. It can also refer to someone who is a "follower" or lacks individuality, stuck in a rut.
- **Chinese:** （1）铁粉/粉丝狂热崇拜者，即极度忠诚或痴迷的粉丝，有时带有嫉妒或无情的特质。 （2）标准/黑白分明，特指对事物的看法非常僵化，缺乏灵活性。
### Examples:
- **English:** She's a real stan for her favorite band. They've been to every concert and can sing all the songs.
- **Chinese:** 她是她最喜欢的乐队的真· stan，参加过每一场演唱会，还能完美唱出所有歌。
- **English:** That movie was terrible, but some stan will still defend it.
- **Chinese:** 这部电影很烂，但一些 stan 仍然会为其辩护。
- **English:** He's such a stan for his own ideas that he won't listen to any criticism.
- **Chinese:** 他对他自己的想法如此执著以至于不听取任何批评。
