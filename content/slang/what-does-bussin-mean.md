## Bussin' ( Busy/Thriving )
### English
**Definition:** When something is really busy, moving swiftly, or incredibly successful. It can also be used to refer to a person who is extremely active or putting in a lot of effort.
**Example Use:** "That new restaurant is bussin'! They stay open late and keep a long line outside all night."
### 中文
**释义:** 当某事物非常忙碌、快速运行或极其成功时使用，也可以用来形容一个人非常活跃或投入大量努力。这里的 "bussin'" 体现了某个实体的繁忙或成功状态。
**例句:** “那家新开的餐厅可火了！他们营业到很晚，而且整晚门前都排着长长的队伍。”
