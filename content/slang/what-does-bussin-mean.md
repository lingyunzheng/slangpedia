## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Bussin'"
### English
**Meaning:** Engaging in or showcasing activities that are impressive, exciting, or prolific. It can also refer to being busy in a particularly effective or showy way.
**Example:** "Man, that guy is really bussin' with his new car and the way he’s living life!"
### 中文
**含义:** 指进行或展示令人印象深刻、激动人心或生产力极高的活动。也可以形容某人在特别有效或风光的方式下忙碌。
**例句:** "伙计，这家伙真是过着极其风光的生活，新买的车也是极具派头！"
