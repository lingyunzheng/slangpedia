## Bussin' (忙in')  
### English:  
"Bussin'" is a slang term commonly used to describe something or someone that is incredibly successful, active, or thriving. It can also be used to express enthusiasm or excitement about a particular situation or activity.  
*Example*: "Yo, his new business is really bussin' right now! He just landed a big contract."
### Chinese:  
“Bussin’” 是一种常用的俚语，用来形容某人或某事非常成功、活跃或兴盛。它也可以用来表达对某种情况或活动的兴奋之情。  
*例句*: "嘿，他的新生意现在真的非常火爆！他刚刚签了一个大合同。"
