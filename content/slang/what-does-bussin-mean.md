## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "bussin'"
### English
**Meaning:** 
- Performing an activity in a lively, energetic, or impressive manner.
- Running or operating a business, especially in a successful and profitable way.
**Example:** "He's really bussin' on that new entrepreneurial venture."
### Chinese
**含义:** 
- 形容某人在做事时充满活力、生动有趣。
- 指经营或运营业务，特别是在成功且盈利方面表现突出。
**例句:** "他在那项新的创业项目上真的干得风生水起。"
