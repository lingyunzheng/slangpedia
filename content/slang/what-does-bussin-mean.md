### Bussin' (Bussing in Chinese)
**English:**
- **Definition:** When someone is doing something extremely well, often in a business or entrepreneurial context. It can also refer to a cool or impressive situation or activity.
- **Example Usage:** "That new social media app is bussin' right now with all the viral marketing they're doing."
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** 当某人在某个领域（尤其是商业或创业领域）做得特别出色时，或者形容某个场景或活动非常酷或者令人印象深刻时，就会用到这个词。
- **例句:** "这家新的社交媒体应用现在正 bussin'，他们所有的病毒性营销都做得非常成功。"
