## Bussin' ( BUSINESS)
**English:**  
"Bussin'" is a slang term used to describe something or someone that's highly active, successful, or exciting. It can refer to a business that's thriving, a party that's lively, or an individual who's doing well in whatever they're undertaking. 
**Chinese:**  
"Bussin'" 是一个俚语，用来形容某事或某人非常活跃、成功或令人兴奋。它可以指一个正在蓬勃发展的企业、一个热闹的聚会，或者是一个在任何事情上都做得很好的人。
