# Bussin' (busy, booming)
**English:**  
"Bussin'" is a slang term often used to describe something that is very successful, busy, or lucrative. It's commonly used in hip-hop culture and urban areas to portray a high-energy, action-packed situation or enterprise.
**Example Usage:**  
- "That new restaurant is totally bussin' right now!"  
- "She’s really bussin' with her online business."
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
“Bussin’” 是一个俚语，常用来形容某件事情非常成功、繁忙或有利可图。这个词在嘻哈文化和城市地区较为常用，用来形容充满活力、场面火爆的情况或业务。
**例句：**  
- “那家新开的餐厅现在生意真是旺得很！”  
- “她的网络业务真是干得风生水起。”
