## Bussin (English)
### Explanation
**Bussin** is a slang term used to describe something that is very successful, profitable, or exciting. It often conveys the idea of a thriving or bustling activity or business.
### Example
- "The new coffee shop downtown is bussin' with all the hipsters and young professionals visiting every day."
## Bussin' (Chinese)
### 解释
**Bussin'** 是一个俚语，用来形容非常成功、有利可图或有趣的某件事。它通常传达一个兴旺或忙碌的活动或业务的印象。
### 示例
- 这家新开的咖啡馆每天都有很多年轻的上班族和潮流人士光顾，真是 bussin'。
