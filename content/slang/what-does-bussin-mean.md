# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "bussin'"
## English
**Meaning:** "Bussin'" is an idiomatic expression used to describe someone or something that is doing well, impressively, or in a stylish manner. It can also be used to indicate a situation that is exciting or intense.
**Usage Example:** "He's really bussin' with his new business model."
**Literal Translation:** There is no direct literal translation, as "bussin'" is a colloquialism.
---
## 中文
**含义:** "bussin'" 是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某事表现得很好、很酷或是振奋人心。它还可能表示一种令人兴奋或紧张的情况。
**使用示例:** "他的新商业模式真是一波三折，干得很不错。"（注意，这里“一波三折”是中文成语，原句是口语表达方式，用“bussin'”表示）
**直译:** 无法直译，因为“bussin'”是一个地道的俚语表达。
