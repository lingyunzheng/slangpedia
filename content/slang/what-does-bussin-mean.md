## Bussin (BusiNNess)

### English
**Meaning:** A slang term used to describe something that is highly profitable, successful, or lucrative. It can also be used to describe exciting, intense, or hectic activities or situations.

### Chinese
**含义:** 一个俚语词，用来描述非常有利可图、成功或有利润的事情。它还可以用来描述激动人心、紧张或忙碌的活动或情况。
