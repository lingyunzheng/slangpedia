### Bussin' (忙商, máng shāng)
#### English:
**Bussin'** is a slang term originated from the African American Vernacular English (AAVE). It is often used to describe someone who is extremely busy, typically in a positive sense, showing that the person has a lot of important commitments, responsibilities, or successful ventures.
**Example:**  
- "Oh, you've really got some bussin' going on there! Your schedule is packed; does that include work, family, and social life too?"
#### Chinese:
**忙商 (máng shāng)** 描述一个人非常忙碌，通常是在积极意义上使用，表示这个人有很多重要的承诺、责任或是成功的事业。
**例句：**
- "哇，你真忙啊！你的日程排得满满的，包括工作、家庭和社交生活，也都有涉及吗？"
