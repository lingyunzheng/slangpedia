### Bussin' (忙爆了, máng bào le)
In English: "Bussin'" is a slang term often used to describe a situation or activity that is going very well, excitingly, or with a lot of energy and success.
In Chinese: "忙爆了" (Máng bào le) is a casual expression meaning something is extremely busy, exciting, or successful. It conveys the idea that things are going exceedingly well.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "Man, this party is bussin' right now! There’s so much hype and energy!"
- Chinese: "这场派对真 bussin' 现在！气氛太热烈了！" (Zhēn bussin' zhème xiàn zài! Fùmìn tài rì liè le! )
```

### "That's bussin'" (那可真bussin'"，Nà kě zhēn bussin' )
In English: "That's bussin'" is a colloquial expression used to emphatically agree with the idea that something is going very well or is impressive.
In Chinese: "那可真bussin'" (Nà kě zhēn bussin' ) translates to "That's truly bussin'" and emphasizes that something is going exceptionally well or is impressive.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "That's bussin'! You really pulled that off!" 
- Chinese: "那可真 bussin'！你可真是有两下子！" (Nà kě zhēn bussin'！Nǐ kě rén shì yǒu liǎ xià zǐ！)
```

### "Gonna buss" (Gonna buss, 即将火爆，Jí jiāng huǒ bào)
In English: "Gonna buss" is a slang phrase indicating that something is about to become popular, exciting, or successful.
In Chinese: "即将火爆" (Jí jiāng huǒ bào) means "going to be very popular and exciting," describing something that is about to become a sensation.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "I’m sure that new song is gonna buss!"
- Chinese: "我敢肯定那首歌即将火爆！" (Wǒ gǎn kěn dìng nà shǒu gē jí jiāng huǒ bào！)
