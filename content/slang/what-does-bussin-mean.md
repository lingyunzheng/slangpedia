## Bussin' (Doing Business / Having Fun)
**English Explanation:**
"Bussin'" is a slang term commonly used in the United States, particularly in urban contexts. It can be used to describe someone who is very active, busy, or productive. It can also be used to indicate that someone is engaged in a successful or impressive business venture or social activity.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"忙疯了"或"玩得开心"（Doing Business / Having Fun）
- **忙疯了 (máng fēng le):** 当某人正在非常忙碌、高效地工作或进行一项成功的商业活动时，可以使用“忙疯了”来形容他们。这个表达体现了极大的投入和动力。
- **玩得开心 (wán de kāixīn):** 当某人的行动充满乐趣，或者是社交活动进行得很愉快时，可以说他们“玩得开心”。这种表达通常用于描述轻松玩乐或社交的状态。
"Bussin'" 可以根据上下文理解为 "忙疯了" 或 "玩得开心"。
