## Bussin' (英美俚语)
### English Explanation
- **Definition:** In casual English, "bussin'" can have multiple meanings. Most commonly, it's used to describe something or someone that is really good, cool, or trending.
- **Usage Example:** "That new restaurant downtown is bussin'!"
### Sino-Slang Explanation
- **定义:** 在口语中，“bussin’”有多种含义，最常见的用法是指某事或某人非常好、很酷或正流行。
- **使用例句:** “这家新开的餐厅真不错（bussin’）！”

## Bussin' (英美俚语)
### English Explanation
- **Definition:** Another interpretation of "bussin'" can involve being very busy, often in a positive sense.
- **Usage Example:** "I'm bussin' all day long with work!"
### Sino-Slang Explanation
- **定义:** 另一种用法，“bussin’”是指非常忙，通常是积极的意思。
- **使用例句:** “我整天都忙得不可开交（bussin'）！”

## Bussin' (英美俚语)
### English Explanation
- **Definition:** "Bussin'" can also have a more colloquial meaning, often used to describe a hashed-up or modified vehicle, especially a car.
- **Usage Example:** "That guy drives a Bussin' car – it’s modified beyond belief!"
### Sino-Slang Explanation
- **定义:** 在口语中，“bussin’”还特指改装或改变过的车辆，尤其是汽车。
- **使用例句:** “那家伙开的是一辆Bussin’车——它被改得彻底不像原样了！”
