## Bussin (BUSINESS)
### English:
**Bussin'**: This slang term implies that someone is extremely busy, often to the point of being hectic or overwhelmed. It can also describe someone who is very successful or putting on a high-energy, impressive performance.
- *Example*: "Wow, you've been bussin' all week! I didn't see you at the party."
### Chinese:
**Bussin'**: 这个俚语表示某人非常忙碌，通常到了紧张或深受困扰的地步。也可以形容一个人非常成功或表现得很精彩。
- *例句*: "哇，你这周一直在bussin'！都没见你参加聚会。"
