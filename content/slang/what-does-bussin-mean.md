## Bussin' (繁忙的/赚钱的)
### English Explanation
**Bussin'** is a slang term commonly used in American English, especially in hip-hop and street cultures. It describes something that is very lucrative, successful, or busy. When someone says they are "bussin'," it means they are running a profitable business or have a successful situation. For example, if a person is making a lot of money or has a lot of activity, they might be "bussin'." It often implies that the person is experiencing lots of activity, wealth, or success in their current venture.
### Chinese Explanation
**Bussin'** 是美式英语中的俚语，尤其在嘻哈和街头文化中广泛使用。它用来形容非常有利可图、成功或忙碌的事情。当有人说自己“bussin’”时，意味着他们正在经营一个利润丰厚的生意或者处于成功的情况。例如，如果一个人赚了很多钱或者有大量的活动，他们可能会说自己正在“bussin’”。这个词通常暗示这个人当前的 ventures 正在经历大量的活动、财富或成功。
