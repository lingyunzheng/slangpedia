## Bussin' (Business, pronounced with a 'b' sound like "business")
**English:**  
"Bussin'" is a slang term often used to describe something or someone that is very successful, active, and productive. It can also be used to describe a person or activity that is exciting, impressive, or making things happen.
**Chinese:**  
“Bussin'” 是一种俚语，通常用于形容某事或某人非常成功、活跃且富有成效。它也可以用于描述令人兴奋、印象深刻或正在创造行动的人或活动。
