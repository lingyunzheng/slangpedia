## Bussin (English) / 今晚生意好啊 (Chinese)
### English Explanation
**Bussin** is a slang term commonly used in informal settings, especially within certain urban and youth cultures. It refers to a profitable or successful business, activity, or situation. For example, if someone says, "That new sneaker drop is bussin!", it means the sneaker release is really popular and selling well.
### Chinese Explanation
**生意好啊 (bīngòu hǎo a)** 也是一个在非正式场合，尤其是城市青年文化中的俚语。它通常用来形容生意、活动或情况来看起来非常好或很有盈利。比如，如果你说，“那双新的运动鞋刚刚发售简直是生意好啊！”这表示这款运动鞋非常受欢迎且销量很好。
