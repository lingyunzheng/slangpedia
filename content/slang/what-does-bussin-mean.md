## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Bussin'"
**English:** 
- **Meaning:** To be busy or engaged in doing something, often in a flashy or showy manner.
- **Example Use:** "He's bussin' right now with his new business ventures."
**Chinese:** 
- **意思:** 表示忙于做某事，通常意味着在以炫耀或引人注目的方式做事。
- **例句:** "他正在忙着他的新商业项目呢。"
