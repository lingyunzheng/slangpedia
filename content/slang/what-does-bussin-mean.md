# Bussin' (Business)
**English:**  
"Bussin'" is a slang term that is primarily used in the United States, often in urban settings. It can have a few different meanings depending on the context:
1. **Active and Thriving:** To be "bussin'" can mean something or someone is very active, busy, or thriving. For example, "That new business is bussin' really well!" This suggests the business is doing a lot of good, possibly with a high level of activity.
2. **Cool or Stylish:** It can also imply that someone or something is cool or stylish. "That youngin' is bussin' with his new outfit!" Here, it means the person is looking very good or fashionable.
**Chinese:**  
“Bussin'” 是美国俚语，主要在城市环境中使用，其含义取决于上下文：
1. **很活跃，很成功：** “Bussin'” 可以表示某件事情或某个人非常活跃、忙碌或非常成功。例如，“这家新企业的生意简直太火爆了！” 表达的意思是这个企业经营状况很好，可能非常活跃。
2. **很酷，很酷炫：** 这个词也可以用来表示某个人或某件事物很酷、很时尚。例如，“这个小伙子穿着一套了不得的新衣服！” 在这里，“bussin'” 表达的是这个人看起来非常出色或帅气。
