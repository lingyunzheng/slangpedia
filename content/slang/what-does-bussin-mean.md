## Bussin' (Doing Business)
- **English**: Engaging in a lucrative or high-energy activity or lifestyle, often with a hint of excitement or intensity.
- **Chinese**: 赚钱做买卖 (zǎn qián zuò mǎi mài) - 指进行非常赚钱或活力满满的行为或生活方式，通常带有兴奋或紧张的感觉。
- **Example Usage**:
- English: "She's really bussin' on social media by selling handmade jewelry — she's getting tons of sales!"
- Chinese: 她在社交媒体上卖手工艺品真的很赚钱——销量很高！
