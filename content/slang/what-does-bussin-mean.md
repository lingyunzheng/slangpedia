# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "bussin'"
**English:**
- **Definition:** "Bussin'" is a slang term used to describe someone or something that is very active, successful, or impressive. It can also be used in a negative context to describe someone who is showy or flamboyant.
- **Example:** "That rapper is bussin' with all the new releases he has coming out this year."
- **Note:** The term can be used with a positive or a negative connotation depending on the context.
**Chinese:**
- **定义：** "bussin'" 是俚语，用来形容某人或某事物非常活跃、成功或令人印象深刻。它也可以用于描述那些夸张或炫耀的人。
- **例句：** “这位说唱歌手今年的新专辑发行真‘bussin’。”
- **备注：** 根据语境的不同，这个词可以用作正面或负面的形容词。
