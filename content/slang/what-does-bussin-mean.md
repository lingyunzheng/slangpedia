## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Bussin'
**English:** 
- **Definition:** A term commonly used to describe something that is very successful, lucrative, or exciting.
- **Example Usage:** "That new startup is really bussin' right now, everyone is talking about it."
**Chinese:** 
- **定义:** 一个常用来形容某事物非常成功、有利可图或令人兴奋的俚语。
- **例句:** "这家新创业公司现在真的非常 bussin'，大家都在谈论它。"
