### Bussin' (碌碡 / 碌碌)
**English Slang Definition:**  
"Doing business" or more colloquially, "having a good time" or "being successful and busy."
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**  
"碌碡" (lù zhóu) or "碌碌" (lù lù) is a term that can have a few interpretations depending on the context. "碌碡" typically refers to a large wheel used on farmlands to flatten the land or the wheel of a cart. When used as slang, it can mean someone who is consistently hardworking and busy. "碌碌" (lù lù) describes someone who is busy but may not be achieving much, or someone who lives a very ordinary and unremarkable life.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** He’s really into bussin' out, making sure he hits every deal at the conference.
- **Chinese:** 他特别忙，也努力在每个商业会议上争取合作机会。（Tā tèbié máng, yě nǔlì zài měi kěgè gōngyè huìshàng qǐnghǎo hézuò jīhuì.）
- **English:** She's always bussin', trying to balance work and social life.
- **Chinese:** 她总是很忙，努力在工作和社会生活中找到平衡。（Tā zǒngshì hěn máng, nǔlì zài gōngzuò héshè huózhōng qǔdào pángchéng.）
- **Note:** In a more colloquial context, "碌碡" might not be directly used, but the idea reflects being busy or successful, which can be conveyed through similar expressions in Chinese.
