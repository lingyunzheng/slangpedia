## Bussin' (Busin' - 聚焦的, 强劲的)
**English:** "Bussin'" is a slang term used to describe something that is very successful, profitable, or energetic. It often emphasizes the excitement and intensity of an activity or situation.
**Chinese:** "Bussin'"（聚焦的, 强劲的）表示某件事情非常成功、有利可图或充满活力。这个词常用来强调活动或情境的兴奋和强烈程度。
Example Usage:
- **English:** "This new business idea is really bussin'!" 
- **Chinese:** "这个新的商业创意真的很火！"
