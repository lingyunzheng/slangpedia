## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Bussin'"
### English:
**Definition:** "Bussin'" is an expression that describes someone, something, or an activity that is extremely successful, exciting, energized, or impressive. It can also be used to convey that someone is actively exhibiting cool, stylish, or attractive behavior or vibes.
**Example:** "She looked so good at the party last night – she was totally bussin'!"
### Chinese:
**释义："Bussin'" 这个表达用来描述某人、某事或某种活动极为成功、激动人心、充满活力或有影响力的状态。它还可以用来表示某人正在展现自己的酷、时尚或有魅力的行为或氛围。**
**例句：** “昨晚的派对上，她看起来好极了——完全就是bussin'！”
