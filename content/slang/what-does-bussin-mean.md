## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "bussin'"
**English:**
- **Meaning:** To be busy or very active with something. Can also imply being successful or doing well in a particular activity.
- **Usage Example:** "He's bussin' in the gym, trying to get bigger and stronger."
**汉语:**
- **含义:** 表示很忙或者在某个事情上很活跃。也可以说在某个领域做得很好或很成功。
- **使用示例:** "他在健身房很忙，努力让自己变得更大更强壮。"
