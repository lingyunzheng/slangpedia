- **English:** "bussin" is a slang term that originated from casual speech and urban youth culture. It can be used in several ways:
- **Meaning 1:** To indicate that something or someone is successful, busy, or going well. For example, "He’s bussin' this new business and it’s taking off!" Here, "bussin'" implies that the business is doing well.
- **Meaning 2:** To describe a person or situation as confident or stylish. For example, "She’s bussin' with her new outfit; it’s on fleek!" This usage emphasizes that the person looks or acts with confidence and style.
