## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Bussin'"
### English
**Definition:** Describing something as being very exciting, successful, cool, or busy.
**Example Use:** "I saw him posting all these photos from his new job. He's bussin' right now!"
### Chinese
**定义:** 用于形容某事物非常有趣、成功、酷，或者很忙。
**用例示例:** "我看见他发了许多新工作的照片。他现在‘bussin’！"
