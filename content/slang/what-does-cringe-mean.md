## Cringe (English)
"Cringe" is a slang term often used to describe a situation or behavior that is deeply uncomfortable, embarrassing, or foolish. It can also be used to express strong disapproval or disgust. For example, if someone makes a really awkward joke, people might say "That's cringe."
## 走不动地儿了 (Chinese)
“走不动地儿了”是一个网络流行语，用来形容某件事情特别夸张、过分，如同某人或某行为已经到了无法忍受的地步，极其尴尬或荒谬。类似于英文中的“cringe”。例如，如果有人讲一个极其尴尬或滑稽的笑话，人们可能会说“这真是走不动地儿了”。
