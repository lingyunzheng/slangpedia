## Cringe (Slang)
### English
**Cringe**: This term refers to something so awkward, embarrassing, or poorly executed that it makes your eyes or body cringe in discomfort.
- **Example**: "I can't believe she wore that outfit to the party! That's such cringe."
### Chinese
**翻车（fān qiē）**: 这个词表示某事如此尴尬、令人尴尬或执行糟糕，以至于让你在不舒服中皱眉或身体发僵。
- **例句**："我简直不敢相信她在聚会上穿了那身衣服！那真是翻车了。"
