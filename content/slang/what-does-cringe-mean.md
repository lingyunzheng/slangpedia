## Cringe
### English
**Cringe**: A term used to describe feeling or appearing extremely embarrassed, awkward, or uncool. It often refers to actions, comments, or situations that are so bad they make you feel physical discomfort or embarrassment.
**Example**: "That guy's dance moves were so bad, it totally cringed."
### Chinese
**崩剧 (bēng jù)**：一个用来形容感觉或看起来非常尴尬、傻气或不酷的词。这个词通常用来形容糟糕的行为、评论或情境，糟糕到让人感到物理上的不适或羞愧。
**例句**：他的舞跳得这么烂，真是崩剧。
