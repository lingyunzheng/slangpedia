```markdown
## Cringe (EN) / 尴尬 (CN)
**EN:** Anything that causes a feeling of mild embarrassment, awkwardness, or discomfort when someone sees or experiences it. Commonly used to describe situations, actions, or content that are perceived as highly embarrassing or socially unacceptable.
**CN:** 任何会引起轻微尴尬、局促或不适感的行为、姿势或情况。通常用来形容那些被认为是极其尴尬或社会上不被接受的行为、举动或内容。
Example Usage:
- EN: "Watching that dance video is cringe."
- CN: "看那个舞蹈视频真的挺尴尬的。"
```
```markdown
## Cringe (EN) / 尼玛 (CN)
**EN:** A slang term often used to express strong disagreement, disgust, or contempt. It is derived from saying "Oh my God" or "My God" in a rapid, emphatic manner. It's informal and has a strong emotional tone.
**CN:** 一个用来表达强烈不同意、厌恶或鄙视的俚语。它源自迅速说出“天啊”或“我的天”的方式。它是非正式的，带有强烈的情感色彩。
Example Usage:
- EN: "That's cringe! I can't believe you said that."
- CN: "尼玛！你居然说了那样的话，我简直无法相信。"
```
```markdown
## Cringe (EN) / 反人类 (CN)
**EN:** Used to describe something so bad, it goes against the morals, ethics, or common sense that society upholds. It conveys a sense of extreme dislike for the situation or action due to its negative impact.
**CN:** 用来形容某件事情如此糟糕，以至于违背了社会公认的道德、伦理或常识。它表达了对这种状况或行为的极端厌恶，因为其负面的影响。
Example Usage:
- EN: "The way they treated their employees is cringe and totally against human rights."
- CN: "他们对待员工的方式真是反人类，完全违反了人权。"
```
```markdown
## Cringe (EN) / 硬核 (CN)
**EN:** While "cringe" often conveys embarrassment or discomfort, "hardcore" in slang sometimes refers to something intense, committed, or extreme. In this context, it could mean something that's so ridiculous or uncomfortable as to be considered "hardcore" in the usual sense.
**CN:** 在俚语中，"硬核"有时指的是某种强烈、坚定或极端的情况。在这种情况下，"cringe"和"hardcore"的含义可能重合，指的是那种如此荒谬或不舒服以至于被认为是"硬核"的事物。
Example Usage:
- EN: "That out-of-tune guitar solo is cringe hardcore."
- CN: "那首跑调的吉他独奏真是硬核尴尬。"
```
