## Cringe (中英双解俚语解释)
**Cringe** is a term often used to describe an awkward or embarrassing situation or behavior. It conveys a sense of discomfort, often coupled with a feeling of wanting to back away or shield one's eyes from the scenario. 
### 中文解释
“Cringe”常用来形容尴尬或令人难堪的情境或行为，表达一种不舒服的感觉，有时伴随着想要回避或遮住眼睛的情绪。
### 英文解释
In everyday usage, "cringe" refers to a reaction to something perceived as markedly embarrassing or awkward. It can describe a person's behavior, an event, or even a poorly executed joke that makes people feel unease.
