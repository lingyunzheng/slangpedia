### Cringe
- **English:** Something or someone that is so bad, awkward, or embarrassing that it evokes feelings of revulsion or embarrassment. It often refers to an extremely uncool or embarrassing moment or behavior.
- *Example Usage:* "The way he dance to that song was just cringe."
- **Chinese:** 令人尴尬或不适的行为或事物，通常是如此糟糕、笨拙或令人尴尬，以至于会引起恶心或尴尬感。它经常指那些极其不酷或令人尴尬的时刻或行为。
- *Example Usage:* “他跳舞的样子真是令人尴尬。”
