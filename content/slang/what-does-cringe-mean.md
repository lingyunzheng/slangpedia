# Cringe (英 / 美)
**英语:** Cringe  
**中文:** 令人尴尬（令人不适）的行为或言论
**Explanation:**
- **英语:** "Cringe" is an informal term used to describe something that is so bad, awkward, embarrassing, or generally off-putting that it makes you want to cover your eyes and hide. It often describes behaviors or moments that are so poorly executed or off-keel that they elicit a cringing reaction from the observer.
- **中文:** "Cringe" 通常用来形容那些如此糟糕、尴尬、尴尬或不悦的行为或言论，让你想用手捂住眼睛，尽量避开。这经常是指那些执行得如此糟糕或偏离正轨的行为或时刻，会让观察者产生一种本能地想要避开的反应。
**Example Usage:**
- **英语:** "His dance move was so bad, it gave me cringe."
- **中文:** "他的跳舞动作实在太糟了，让我直冒冷汗。"
- **英语:** "I just can't believe she wore that outfit to the party; it's such cringe!"
- **中文:** "我简直不敢相信她竟然穿着那套衣服去 party，真是太尴尬了！"
**Contexts:**
- **英语:** Commonly used in social media posts and everyday conversations to express disbelief, frustration, or embarrassment.
- **中文:** 经常在社交媒体帖子或日常对话中用来表达难以置信、沮丧或尴尬。
