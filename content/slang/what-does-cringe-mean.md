# Cringe (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
**English:**
"Cringe" in modern slang is often used to describe an instinctive response to something considered embarrassing or awkward. It can also indicate disapproval or a strong sense of discomfort. People might use it to comment on someone's or something's behavior, appearance, or actions that they find uncomfortably bad.
Example: "When that celebrity did that silly dance, it made me cringe!"
**中文:**
"Cringe" 在当代网络用语中，通常用来形容一种本能反应，对于被认为尴尬或滑稽的事情。它也可以用来表示不赞成或者强烈的不适感。人们可能会用这个词来评论某人或某事的行为、外观或动作，觉得这些事情令人难以忍受地糟糕。
例如：“当那位名人做那个滑稽的舞蹈时，我简直羞愧难当（直呼cringe）！”
