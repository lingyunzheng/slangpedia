## Cringe (中文解释：尴尬/门槛)
**English:** Cringe
- **Definition:** Cringe describes a situation or action that is so bad, absurd, or embarrassing that it makes one feel physical discomfort, often accompanied by a desire to cover one’s eyes or get away from the situation.
- **Example:** If someone performs a dance move that is so poorly executed, it makes everyone watching cringe, the move is cringe.
**中文解释：**
- **定义:** Cringe指的是那种如此糟糕、荒谬或令人尴尬的情况或行为，以至于让人感到身体上的不适，往往伴随着想遮住眼睛或避开这种情景的欲望。
- **例句:** 如果有人跳了个舞，表演得非常糟糕，让所有人看得忍俊不禁，这个舞可以被描述为 cringe。
