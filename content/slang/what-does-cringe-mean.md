# Cringe
## English
**Cringe**: A feeling of squirming discomfort, often experienced when faced with something deeply embarrassing, awkward, or unfunny. It's used to describe situations or people who are so bad that it makes your knees cringe or your skin crawl. For example: "That dance move was so cringe, I had to cover my eyes."
## Chinese
**cringe**: 一种令人不适的感觉，通常是因为面对某种极其尴尬、尴尬或不好笑的情况。你可以用它来描述那些糟糕到让你膝盖发麻或皮肤起鸡皮疙瘩的时刻。例如：“那个舞蹈动作好cringe，我差点儿睁不开眼了。”
