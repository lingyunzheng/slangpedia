# Cringe
## English
**Cringe:** Used to describe a situation or action that is socially awkward or embarrassing. It can also be applied to someone who consistently performs in a way that others find uncomfortable or amusing in a negative way.
Example:
- "The way he proposed to his girlfriend was so cringe, it was actually funny in a bad way."
## Chinese
**崩 svo（崩svo）:** 用来形容一个情境或行为让人感到社交上的尴尬或尴尬到笑不出来的情况。也可以用来形容经常以令人不适或负面的方式表现的人。
例句：
- "他向女朋友求婚的方式实在是太崩了，竟然还有一点好笑。"
