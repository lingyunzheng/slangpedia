## Chinese (简体中文) - Cringe
**定义：** 肮脏的、令人尴尬或作呕的事情或行为。
**解释：**
- 这个词用来形容某人的某些行为、言论或情景非常令人不舒服，让人忍不住想要回避或感到恶心。
- 例如，如果你的朋友突然说出一些违反社会礼仪的话，你可能会说“这也太cringe了吧”。
**例句：**
- 这首歌的歌词太cringe了，听着真的让人觉得不舒服。
- 他的演讲太cringe，根本抓不住听众的注意力。
## English - Cringe
**Definition:** Something or someone that is disgusting, embarrassing, or extremely uncomfortable.
**Explanation:**
- This term is used to describe actions, statements, or scenarios that are so unpleasant that they elicit an involuntary feeling of discomfort or disgust.
- For example, if your friend suddenly says something that goes against acceptable social norms, you might say, "That's really cringy!"
**Example Sentences:**
- The lyrics of that song are so cringy, they make you want to turn it off.
- His presentation was so cringy that it couldn't hold the audience's interest.
