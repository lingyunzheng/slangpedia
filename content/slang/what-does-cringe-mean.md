# Cringe
**English:**  
Cringe is an internet slang term that describes a feeling of discomfort or embarrassment typically caused by something so stupid, embarrassing, or awkward that it merits cringing. It can also refer to someone who frequently acts in an awkward or socially inappropriate manner.
**Chinese:**  
“cringe” 是网络用语，用来形容因某事极为愚蠢、尴尬或滑稽可笑而产生的一种不适或尴尬的感觉。这个词也可以用来形容经常做出尴尬或社交不当行为的人。
