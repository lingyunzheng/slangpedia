### Cringe (English)
**Definition:** When something is so painfully embarrassing, awkward, or uncool that it makes you cringe, you have to physically or emotionally shrink or recoil.
**Example Usage:**  
- "The dance moves they performed were so out of tune and offbeat, it was cringe."
- "Cringing at my old fashion choices from high school."
### Cringeworthy (Chinese)
**定义：** 当某事是如此尴尬、羞耻或不酷，以至于让你感到紧张或不适时，你说它是 **cringeworthy**。
**使用示例：**
- “他们跳的舞完全跑调，让人直摇头，真是 **cringeworthy**。”
- “回忆起我高中时期的打扮，真是有些 **cringeworthy**。”
