### Cringeworthy in English & 羞愧寸心 in Chinese
**Cringe in English:**  
"Cringle" is an internet slang term used to describe situations or content that are so embarrassing or awkward that they make someone feel uncomfortable, often to the point of cringing (hence the name). It can be used to describe anything from a terrible performance or joke to a really bad fashion choice.
**羞愧寸心 (Xiū kuì cùn xīn) in Chinese:**  
羞愧寸心 (pronounced "xiū kuì cùn xīn") literally translates to "shameful heart block" and is used to express extreme embarrassment or embarrassment to the point of feeling shamed. It aptly captures the feeling one gets when witnessing or experiencing something cringe-worthy. This term emphasizes the depth of the feelings of shame and embarrassment.
In usage:
- **English Example:** "That guy on stage singing off-key is so cringe!"
- **Chinese Example:** "那个在台上跑调唱歌的人真是太羞愧寸心了！" (Nà ge zài shàngtái pǎodiào chànggē de rén zhēn shì tài xiū kuì cùn xīn le!)
