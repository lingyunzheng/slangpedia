```markdown
## Cringe

### English
**Definition:** The feeling of embarrassment or discomfort that arises when someone or something is considered awkward, uncomfortable, or embarrassing.

**Example Usage:** "I couldn't believe he wore that outfit to the party, it was so cringe."

### 中文
**定义：** 当某人或某事被认为是尴尬、不舒服或令人尴尬时，产生的一种尴尬或不舒服的感覺。

**例句：** “我根本没想到他会穿着那样的衣服去参加派对，真的太不给力了。”
```

Note: "不给力" is a colloquial expression in Chinese that roughly translates to "not impressive" or "not good" in the context of something being embarrassing or awkward.
