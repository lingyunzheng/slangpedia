# Cringe (英汉双解俚语解释)
**English:** Cringe is a term used to describe something or someone that is so embarrassing, awkward, or poorly executed that it elicits a feeling of cringing, or an instinctive desire to protect oneself from exposure to the described situation or content.
**Chinese:** “cringe” 是用来形容那些令人非常尴尬、不自在或执行得很糟糕的事物或人，让人在描述或遇到这些情况时会有本能地想躲避或保护自己的感觉。
Example Usage:
- English: "That dance move was so cringe, I almost fell out of my seat!"
- Chinese: “那个舞蹈动作太尴尬了，我都快从椅子上笑倒了！”
