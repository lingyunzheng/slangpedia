# Cringe (English)
**Definition:** Something or someone that is incredibly embarrassing, awkward, or socially unacceptable.
**Example Usage:** "His dance moves were so bad, it was cringe."
# 赌姐（拼音：cúi jǐe） (Chinese)
**释义:** 赌姐本意是指让人感到尴尬、奇怪或不合适的任何事物或行为。
**用法示例:** "他的舞蹈动作太烂了，真是赌姐。"
