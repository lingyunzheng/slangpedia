## Cringe (中文: 挣扎)
**English:** *Cringe*
- **Meaning:** This term generally refers to a situation or behavior that is so embarrassing or awkward that it causes someone to react with disgust or discomfort. People often display cringe behavior or reactions during social events or online interactions.
- **Example Sentences:**
- "The way he introduced himself was so cringe, I couldn't believe my ears."
- "Reading that old social media post is just cringe, it's so embarrassing."
**中文解释: 挣扎**
- **含义:** 这个词通常用来形容一种令人尴尬或难堪的情境或行为，这让人们在社交场合或网络互动中反应出厌恶或不适的情绪。人们可能在社交活动或者在线互动中表现出尴尬的行为或反应。
- **例句:**
- "他的自我介绍真让人心生厌弃，我简直无法相信自己耳朵里听到的内容。"
- "读那篇旧社交媒体帖子真让人感觉尴尬无比，因为它实在太丢脸了。"
