```markdown
## Cringe (英) vs. 腼腆 (中)
**Cringe (英):**
- **Definition:** A term used to describe a situation or behavior that is so awkward or embarrassing that it elicits a cringing reaction. It can also be used to express a feeling of uncomfortable amusement when someone does something really bad or silly.
- **Example Usage:** If someone posts a video of themselves accidentally saying something extremely out of context, their friends might say, "Wow, that's cringe!" or "You're being so cringey!"
**腼腆 (中):**
- **Literal Meaning:** The term "腼腆" literally translates to "modest" or "shy." However, in context to "cringe," it does not directly translate and is more of an analogy in terms of how one might react with a modest or shy demeanor when faced with embarrassing situations.
- **Usage Example:** If a person is asked a question they are not comfortable with, they might blush or show a modest demeanor. While they might not directly use "cringe" in this situation, the feeling of uneasiness or discomfort can be compared to having a cringe reaction.
```
