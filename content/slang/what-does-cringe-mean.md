## Cringe
**English:**  
"Cringe" is an internet slang term that describes an experience of feeling embarrassed, uncomfortable, or mildly disgusted by something. It often refers to behavior, content, or statements that are so bad or embarrassing that they illicit a physical cringing reaction.
**Bilingual Example:**
- English: "I can't believe John wore that outfit to the party. It was so cringe!"
- 中文: "我简直不敢相信约翰在聚会上穿了那样的衣服。真是太尴尬了！"
**Usage Context:**
- Commonly used on social media platforms, especially when someone expresses surprise, embarrassment, or disapproval about something seen online or in real life.
