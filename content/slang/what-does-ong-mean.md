## Ong (English: "On")
**Definition:**
Ong is a colloquial term used to affirm or agree with a statement in Singaporean and Malaysian Chinese dialects. It is equivalent to "yes" or "okay" in standard English.
**Example Usage:**
- Person A: "We can meet at 3 PM, right?"
- Person B: "Ong!"
---
## 快快 (English: "Ong")
**Definition:**
"快快" (Kuài kuài) is a Mandarin term that means "quickly" or "hurry up." It is often used in informal settings where someone is urging another person to do something quickly.
**Example Usage:**
- Person A: "We need to leave now."
- Person B: "快快! (Kuài kuài!) Let's go!"
