## Ong (汉语: 佬) 
**English:** Boss / Big Shot / Cool Guy
**Explanation:**
- **Boss / Big Shot:** Used when referring to someone who has a lot of influence or power, similar to how "big shot" is used in English. For example, "John is an ong in the tech industry."
- **Cool Guy:** Often used to describe someone who is considered trendy, fashionable, or popular. It can also denote someone who is seen as a role model or admired by others. For instance, "Lucy is the ong of our social circle – everyone wants to be like her."
**Usage Context:**
- While "ong" can be positive, it is also sometimes used in a somewhat casual or derogatory manner, depending on the situation and context.
