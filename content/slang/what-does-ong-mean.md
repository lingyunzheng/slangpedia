# Ong (Chinese:  Można себе представить)
## English Slang: "You've got to be kidding me"
- **Definition**: This phrase is used when something is so surprising or unbelievable that it's hard to believe it's true.
- **Usage Example**: "Ong, I got a promotion, but I have to work on weekends now." -> "You've got to be kidding me, I got a promotion but have to work on weekends now."
---
This Chinese slang "ong" (韦言韦语:|Mdíánmíng tígáng|) is loosely translated as English slang to capture a surprising or unbelievable situation. The exact context and tone can vary widely depending on the situation.
