## Ong (Chinese: 姓翁)
- **Chinese:** 姓翁 (Pronounced: xìng wēng)
- **English Slang Explanation:** "Old Man" or "Gramps" (colloquial)
Explanation: "Ong" in Chinese refers to "翁", which can be a surname but also carries a connotation of an elderly man, especially in a familial or respectful context. In a casual or slang usage, it is sometimes used to refer to a middle-aged or elderly man, much like "gramps" or "old man" in English. This term can be quite colloquial and is often used in a kind or familiar manner.
