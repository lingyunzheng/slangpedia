# Slang Term: "ong"
## Chinese
**定义**: “ong” 在网络用语中通常用来形容一个人做事效率极高，甚至有些许(circle)速成的意思，类似于“神速”或者“效率怪”。
**例子**:
- 真是个ong，几秒钟就搞定了！
## English
**Definition**: "ong" is a term commonly used in internet slang to describe someone who works extremely efficiently, possibly even with a slight implication of achieving impressive results quickly, similar to "super-speedy" or "high-efficiency monster".
**Example**:
- Truly an ong, completed it in just a few seconds!
