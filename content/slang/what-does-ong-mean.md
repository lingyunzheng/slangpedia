Here's the bilingual slang explanation for "ong" in Markdown format:
```markdown
# Ong (方言 slang in Chinese)
## Chinese
- 方言词汇，主要流行于福建地区
- 意味着“大哥”或“老大”，表示对某位有影响力或在群体中地位较高的男性的一种尊称
## English
- A dialect slang, mainly popular in Fujian region
- It means "big brother" or "boss" and is used as a term of respect for a male figure with significant influence or a higher status in a group
```
