## Ong
### Chinese: 哥 (Gē)
### English: Big Bro / OG
In Chinese internet slang, "ong" (哥) often refers to a male figure as a "big brother" or an "OG" (Old Guard). It can be used as a term of respect or admiration for someone who is older, wiser, or more experienced in a particular context or group.
