## Ong (English Slang)
**Meaning:** A term often used as a placeholder for words that you forget or are unable to think of at the moment, similar to "um" or "ah" in regular speech. It can also be used humorously to indicate hesitation or to fill silence.
**Example:**  
*Regular Speech:* "I need to go... oops, ong... to the store."  
*Humorous Example:* "Ong, what's the name of that movie I saw last night?"
---
## Ong (Chinese Slang)
**Meaning:** 在这里表示一个思维卡顿时的占位词，类似于英文中的“um”或“ah”。它也可以用于幽默地表示犹豫或填补沉默。
**例句:**  
*日常表达:* 我要去……哎呀，对不起，仅供参考去药店了。  
*幽默用途:* 哎呀，昨晚我看的那部电影叫什么名字来着？
