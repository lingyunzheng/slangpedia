# Slang Word: Ong
## English Explanation
**Ong**: A playful, informal term often used as a substitute for "flying fish" in certain contexts. It can also be used to express surprise or excitement in a humorous way, similar to how "whoa" or "wow" might be used.
Example:
- "Ong, that's a big fish!" (Referring to a very large fish, similar to saying "Wow, that's a big fish!")
## Chinese Explanation
**Ong**: 在某些俚语中，常用来代替“飞鱼”，带有一定的玩趣色彩。另外，它也可以用来表示惊讶或兴奋，类似于“哇”或“酷”的使用方式。
例子：
- "Ong，那条鱼好大啊！" (意思是“哇，那条鱼好大啊！”)
