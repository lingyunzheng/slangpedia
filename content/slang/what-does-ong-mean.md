# Slang Explanation for "ong"
## Chinese (普通话)
**ong** (翁)
1. **一家人** (yi ge jia ren): Refers to someone who is closely related to you, often used to address the husband of a family or a family member in a familiar or playful way.  
- *英文解释:* Refers to someone closely related to you, like a husband in a family or a family member in a casual context.
2. **有钱人** (yao qian ren): Informally used to describe someone who is wealthy or has a lot of money.
- *英文解释:* Informally used to describe someone who is wealthy or has a lot of money.
## English
**ong** (翁)
1. **Family Member**: Often used to refer to one's husband or a close family member in a familiar or casual manner.
- *中文解释:* 用于指代自己的丈夫或亲密的家庭成员，在熟悉或随意的情况下使用。
2. **Rich Person**: Informally used to describe someone who is wealthy.
- *中文解释:* 用于描述一个有钱的人，在非正式场合使用。
