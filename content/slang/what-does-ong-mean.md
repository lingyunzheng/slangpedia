## Ong (English-Simplified Chinese)
### English
- **Meaning**: An informal way to refer to an older sibling, typically an older brother.
- **Usage**: Often used among family members or close friends to show a relaxed or affectionate relationship.
- **Example**: "Hey Ong, pass me the salt please."
### 翻译 (Translation)
- **含义**: 一种非正式的方式称呼年长的兄弟，通常是大哥。
- **用法**: 常用于家庭成员或亲密朋友之间，表达一种轻松或亲密的关系。
- **例句**: "嘿，Ong，麻烦把盐递给我。"
```
## Ong (English-Traditional Chinese)
### English
- **Meaning**: An informal way to refer to an older sibling, typically an older brother.
- **Usage**: Often used among family members or close friends to show a relaxed or affectionate relationship.
- **Example**: "Hey Ong, pass me the salt please."
### 翻譯 (Translation)
- **意義**: 一種非正式的方式稱呼年長的兄弟，通常是大哥。
- **用法**: 常用于家庭成員或親密朋友之間，表達一種輕鬆或親切的關係。
- **例句**: "嘿，Ong，麻煩把鹽遞給我。"
