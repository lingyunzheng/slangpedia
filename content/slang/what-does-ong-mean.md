# Slang Term: "ong"
## English Explanation:
**On point**: 
- Meaning: Perfectly accurate or appropriate. This term is often used to describe someone or something that is highly relevant or effective.
- Example: "His analysis of the game was on point."
## Chinese Explanation:
**正着 (zhèng zhe)**:
- Meaning: 正着在中文俚语中类似于“正中红心”或者“点对了”，意味着精确、合适或有效。
- Example：他的分析完全正着（他分析得很到位）。
