```markdown
# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Touch Grass"
## English
**Meaning**: This phrase typically means to experience a situation or behavior that is considered excessively wrong, risky, or regrettable. It implies doing something that would make someone feel very guilty or ashamed.
**Example**: "Don't even think about going to that party; you might touch grass over there."
## Chinese
**含义**：这个短语通常用来描述一种体验或行为，被认为是错误的、冒险的或令人后悔的。它指的是做出让你感到非常内疚或羞愧的事情。
**例句**： "你千万别说去那个派对；你可能会在那里‘摸草’。"
```
In Chinese, "摸草" (mō cǎo) is a slang equivalent that carries the same connotation as "touch grass" in English.
