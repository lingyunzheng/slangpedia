## "Touch Grass" Slang
### English
**To touch grass:** This phrase is used humorously or ironically to describe a dramatic change in one's lifestyle or attitude, often involving quitting an addictive habit or substance. It's also occasionally used to express a desire to be somewhere quiet and peaceful, literally under some grass.
- *Usage Example:* "After realizing the dangers of smoking, he decided to touch grass and kick the habit."
### 中文
**触草（chù cǎo）:** 这个俚语通常用于幽默或讽刺地表达一个人生活方式或态度的剧烈变化，通常是指戒掉某种成瘾的行为或物质。有时，这句话也被用来表示希望到一个安静平和的地方去，真的躺在草地上。
- *使用示例:* "意识到吸烟的危害后，他决定触草，彻底戒烟。"
