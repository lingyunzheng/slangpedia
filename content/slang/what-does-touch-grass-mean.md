## "Touch Grass"
**English:** To touch the grass. This phrase is used to express regret for a bad decision or action, often in a situation where one wishes they hadn't done something.
**简体中文:** 触草。这个表达用来表示对自己做的某个糟糕的决定或行为感到后悔，通常意味着希望没做过这样的事。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I wish I could go back in time and touch grass for what I did to him."
- **简体中文:** "我希望我能回到过去，为我对他的所作所为而后悔。"
