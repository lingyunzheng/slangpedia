# "Touch Grass"
## English
- **Meaning**: To admit that you have done something very foolish or regrettable in the past.
- **Example**: "I need to touch grass because I should've never invested all my savings in that tech startup."
## Chinese
- **意思**: 用来表达自己曾经做了一件非常愚蠢或后悔的事情。
- **例句**: "我得承认我曾做过一件蠢事，所以我需要承认过去我全款买了那个科技创业公司的股票。"
