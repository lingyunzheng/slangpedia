## "Touch Grass" (English to Chinese)
**English:** Touch grass  
**Chinese:** 触草 (chù cǎo)  
**Explanation:** This phrase is often used to express extreme regret or to admit that one has done something wrong, usually in relation to leaving a relationship or a job. It is similar to saying "I am really sorry" or "I wish I could go back in time and change my actions" in English. The term "grass" in this slang is a substitute for a female romantic partner and is often used in the context of cheating or infidelity.
**Example:**  
When speaking to an ex-partner:  
"I'm so sorry I cheated on you. I would never do that again."  
In slang:  
"Touch grass, I shouldn't have touched the grass."
## "Touch Grass" (Chinese to English)
**Chinese:** 触草 (chù cǎo)  
**English:** Touch grass  
**Explanation:** This term is a slang variation in Chinese to express a similar sentiment of regret or remorse, particularly when one has acted in a way they deeply regret, such as leaving a significant relationship or job. It involves a metaphorical idea that one has touched something they shouldn't have, often referring to a female romantic partner.
**Example:**  
In a conversation with friends:  
"Really bad today, touched the grass."  
Loosely translates to:  
"I really messed up today, I cheated on my partner."
