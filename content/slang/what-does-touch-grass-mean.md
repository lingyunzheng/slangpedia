# "Touch Grass" (英文：Touch Grass)
## English Meaning
"Touch grass" is an informal expression used to indicate that someone is dead or not in a part of their body that requires functionality, often metaphorically referring to death or being unable to react or exist in a certain state. It is used humorously or informally to say someone is gone or no longer around.
## Chinese Explanation
“触草”是一个非正式的表达，用来表示某人去世了或者不再参与某事的状态，通常指的是死亡或失去行动能力。这个表达常常用于幽默地或非正式地说明某人不在了或消失不见。
## Usage Example
- **English**: John hasn't been seen for days, he must have touched grass.
- **Chinese**: 约翰几天都没人见过了，他肯定是去世了。
