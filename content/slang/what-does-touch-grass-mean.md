## "Touch Grass" (触草)
### English Slang:
- **Literal Meaning:** Literally, it means to touch grass, which is a surface that is typically green and made up of small plants. 
- **Figurative Meaning:** It's a slang expression used to encourage someone to take responsibility for their actions or to "fess up" about something one has done wrong. It often implies that the person should stop making excuses and face the consequences. The origin of the term is unclear, but it's popularly believed to have emerged from the idea that facing the truth (the green grass) is a necessary step to fixing a wrong.
### Chinese Slang:
- **直面现实 (Zhí Miàn Shí Xiàn):** 站在草地上，直面自己所做的事情，承认错误，承担后果。这句话鼓励人们面对自己的不当行为，承认错误，而不是找借口或逃避责任。
- **字面意义:** 字面意思是触摸草地，指的是一种表面覆盖着细小植物的绿色地面。
- **俚语意义:** 这是一个俚语表达，用于鼓励某人承认他们的行为，并承担相应的后果。这通常意味着那个人应该停止找借口，面对问题。
