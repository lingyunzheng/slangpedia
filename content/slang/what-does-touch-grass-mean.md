## "Touch Grass" (英语: Touch Grass)
**中文解释**: 这个短语源自俚语，在中文中常用的含义是表达极度不满、推销某种新产品或者寻找新鲜感。当某人说"我要去碰碰草了"时，往往意味着他们想要尝试一些全新的、令人惊讶的事情，或者彻底改变现状。
**英文解释**: "To touch grass" is a slang expression in English, often used to indicate extreme dissatisfaction, the promotion of a new product, or the desire to seek excitement. When someone says "I'm going to touch the grass," it usually means they are ready to try something completely new and surprising, or make a significant change in their life.
