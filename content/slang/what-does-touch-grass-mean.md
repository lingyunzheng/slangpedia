## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Touch Grass
### English
**Touch Grass**: This phrase is a way of expressing regret, shame, or some form of foolish behavior. It's often used as a hypothetical expression of doing something extremely silly or unbelievable.
**Usage Example**: If someone claims to have done something outrageous, another person might say, "You must have touched some grass (done something really silly)."
### 中文
**触草**：这个说法用来表达后悔、羞愧或者是某种愚蠢的行为。它通常作为一种假设，意味着做了些非常愚蠢或难以置信的事情。
**用法示例**：如果有人声称做了一件匪夷所思的事情，另一个人可能会说，“你一定是触了草（做了些非常愚蠢的事情）。”
