# Touch Grass
**English Slang:**
To "touch grass" is an informal expression used to admit that you are about to die, that you are in a very bad state (mentally or physically), or that you are about to experience something terrible, usually the end of life. The phrase suggests a drastic decline in one's situation, leading to an imminent end.
**Chinese Slang:**
“拍拍草坪”是用来表示某人即将死去，或者处境非常糟糕（无论是精神上还是身体上），或者即将经历某种非常糟糕的情况，通常是生命的终结。这个短语暗示着人的状态急剧恶化，即将结束。
例句：
- "English: I'm really not feeling well, might have to touch grass tonight."
- "中文: 我今天真的不舒服，可能要‘拍拍草坪’了。"
