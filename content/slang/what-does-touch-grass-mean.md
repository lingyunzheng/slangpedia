```markdown
## English: Touch Grass
### Chinese: 触草
- **Explanation**: This phrase is an informal and often humorous way to express regret or admitting that you did something you wish you hadn't—typically the consumption of illegal substances. It suggests that the person has deeply felt remorse and the experience is so undesirable that they would prefer to "touch grass," a term associated with the feeling of grass on one's skin after getting off drugs.
- **Example Usage**:
- English: "I really regret smoking that weed; I need to touch grass."
- 中文: "我真后悔抽了那支大麻，我需要接触草本植物。”
```
This explanation provides a clear translation and usage context for both audiences.
