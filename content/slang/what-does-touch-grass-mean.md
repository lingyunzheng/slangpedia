# "Touch Grass" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
- **Definition**: A phrase used to express extreme regret for a past action or decision.
- **Usage**: "I really touched the grass when I agreed to that contract without reading the terms. Now I'm losing money left and right."
- **Explanation**: This slang emerged from the internet, originally meaning to do something that is seen as deeply foolish, like touching grass, which is illegal and can be harmful. It's now used to indicate severe regret or remorse.
## 中文
- **定义**: 一个表达对过去行为或决定的极度后悔的俚语。
- **用法**: "我真是太蠢了，没有仔细阅读合同就答应了，现在损失惨重，我真的碰到了草地。"
- **解释**: 这个俚语来源于网络，最初意思是指做了一件非常愚蠢的行为，如接触草类，这是非法的且可能有害的。现在它被用来表示严重的悔恨或遗憾。
