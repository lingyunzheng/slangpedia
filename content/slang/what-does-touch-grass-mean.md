## English: Touch Grass
**Definition and Usage:**
"Touch grass" is a phrase often used to indicate an intention to commit suicide. It's a very informal and emotive expression used in informal settings, especially in online discussions or forums. The literal meaning of "touch the grass" doesn't apply; it's more of a hyperbolic way to express a desire to die.
**Example:**
- "I’m seriously thinking of just touching the grass if this continues." 
This phrase conveys extreme frustration and a strong desire to end one's life due to a situation they're experiencing.
## 中文: 触草
**定义和用法:**
“触草”这个词语通常表示有结束生命的意图，是一种非常非正式且情绪化的表达，常用于非正式场合，特别是在线讨论或论坛中。这里的“触草”并不是字面意思，而是一种夸张的说法，表达强烈的自杀念头。
**例句:**
- “如果这样继续下去，我真的会考虑触草了。”
这句话传达出极大的挫败感和强烈的想结束自己生命的情绪，因为某种情况而感到困扰严重。
