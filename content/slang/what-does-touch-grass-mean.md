## English: "touch grass"
### Chinese: "碰草"
- **Explanation**: It's a slang expression used when someone is feeling extremely down or depressed. When a person is "touching grass," they are so desperate that they might resort to doing things they wouldn't normally do due to their low mood. Suggesting someone "don’t touch grass" is like advising them to stay positive and not dwell on negative feelings.
Example usage:
- English: "John has been touch grass lately because of his job loss."
- Chinese: "因为失业，约翰最近状态非常低落，简直到了‘碰草’的程度。"
