## "Touch Grass"
### English
"Touch grass" is an informal slang often used to express an intense feeling of shame, embarrassment, or regret. It can also be used to describe a very embarrassing situation. The phrase is generally used humorously or in a serious context of self-deprecation.
Example: 
- "I'm going to touch grass after losing the game to my sister even though I practiced for weeks."
### 中文
"Touch grass" 是一种俚语，多用于表达极度的羞愧、尴尬或后悔感。它也可以用来形容一种非常尴尬的情况。这句话通常用于幽默或自我调侃的严肃语境中。
例句：
- “输给我妹还我练了那么久，我现在真的要自愧不如了。”
