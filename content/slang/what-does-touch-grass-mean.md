## "Touch Grass" in English and Chinese
### English
- **Slang Explanation:** "Touch grass" is a slang term used to express deep distress, shame, or an apology. It's often used in a self-deprecating or joke manner to describe feeling so bad about something that one wishes they could just disappear or wish away their problems.
- **Example Usage:** "I completely messed up the project! I need to touch grass for a while."
- **In Chinese:**
- **俚语解释:** “触草”（拼音：“chù cǎo”）是一个俚语表达，用来形容极度痛苦、羞愧或道歉的心情。它通常用于自嘲或开玩笑的方式，描述因为某件事感觉很糟糕，以至于希望自己能消失或解决掉问题。
- **使用示例:** “我完全搞砸了这个项目！我需要触草一会儿。”
### Pronunciation in Chinese (Pinyin):
- **触草 (chù cǎo)**
### Note:
- This expression is not commonly used in formal or professional contexts.
- The term is primarily used among friends or in casual settings for comedic or self-deprecating purposes.
