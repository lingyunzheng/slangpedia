# "Touch Grass" Slang Explanation
## English
**Touch Grass**: This is a playful and informal phrase used to express relief, especially after experiencing or dealing with a difficult, frustrating, or draining situation. It can mean that you feel like you are moving away from the problem or negative feelings and are getting some reprieve.
- **Example**: "Finally got that big project done, I can touch grass for a while now!"
## 中文
**触草**：这个口头禅是一种轻松且非正式的表达方式，通常用来表示在经历或处理了麻烦、沮丧或耗神的情况后得到了解脱。这句话意味着你感觉自己已经从问题或负面情绪中解脱出来，得到了某种程度的喘息机会。
- **例句**：“终于把这个大项目搞定了，现在我可以暂时喘口气了！”
