# "Touch Grass"
## English
- **Meaning:** Referring to someone who has died or passed away. It's a more euphemistic way of saying "has died."
- **Example Usage:** "I saw Bob yesterday, but he's touching grass now. He was really sick."
- **Origin:** The term comes from the idea that when a person dies, their grass (grave) is "touched" or "touched by" them.
- **Indicative Age Group:** Often used in gaming communities, among young adults, and in informal conversations.
## 中文
- **含义:** 指某人已经去世。这是一个比较委婉的说法，用来代替“已经死了”。
- **例句使用:** "昨天我还见到Bob，但他现在‘触草’了，他病得很重。"
- **来源:** 该词来源于当一个人去世时，他们的墓地（即“草”）会“触”到他们。
- **适用年龄段:** 经常在游戏社区、年轻人以及非正式对话中使用。
