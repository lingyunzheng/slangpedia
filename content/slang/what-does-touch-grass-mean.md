## English: Touch Grass
### Definition:
To retract a harsh statement, admit defeat, or publicly apologize. It's a way of saying "I was wrong" or "I'm sorry," often without directly owning up to the mistake.
### Example:
- "I used to say that quantum computing was overhyped, but after talking to experts, I've totally touched grass."
## 中文: 触草
### 释义：
收回尖酸刻薄的话、承认失败或者公开道歉。这是一种委婉地说“我错了”或“我向你道歉”的方式，通常不直接承认错误。
### 例子：
- “之前我一直都说量子计算被高估了，但和专家交流之后，我彻底触草了。”
