# "Touch Grass" Slang Explanations
## English
**"Touch Grass"**
- **Meaning:** The expression "touch grass" is an informal and somewhat vulgar way of saying that someone is dead or will soon die.
- **Usage Example:** "Look at how he's complaining about his ex. He's touched grass if he thinks he can get back together with her."
- **Origin:** The phrase is a figurative expression that indicates someone is in a state of complete despair, potentially leading to the worst possible outcome, including death.
## 中文
**“触草”**
- **含义:** “触草”是一个非正式的、带有一些粗俗意味的表达方式，用来形容某人已经死了或者很快就会死。
- **用法示例:** "看看他怎么还想着和前女友复合。如果他以为能复合，那他‘触草’了。"
- **来源:** 这个短语是一个修辞表达，用来形容某人处于彻底绝望的状态，可能导致最糟糕的结果，包括死亡。
