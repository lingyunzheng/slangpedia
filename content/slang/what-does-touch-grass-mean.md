# Slang Explanation: "Touch Grass"
## English
**Touch Grass**: A phrase that is used to describe a person who is acting like a child, being overly sensitive, or making immature decisions. It can also be used as a challenge to someone, similar to "touch the grass with your nose" to see if they are really as arrogant as they think.
**Example**:  
- "Stop being so touch grass and grow up already!" – This indicates that the person is being too sensitive or immature.
## Chinese
**触草 (Chù Cǎo)**: 一个用来形容一个人行为像孩子，过于敏感，或者做出幼稚决定的俚语。也可以用来向某人发起挑战，类似于“用鼻子触碰草地”来考验他们是否真的像他们认为的那样傲慢。
**例句**:  
- “别那么多触草了，快点成熟起来！” – 这表示那个人过于敏感或者太幼稚。
