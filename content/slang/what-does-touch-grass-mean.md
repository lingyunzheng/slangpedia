# "Touch Grass" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## **English**
**Definition:**  
- To retreat or abandon a plan; to give up on something. Commonly used when someone concedes or backs off from a scenario or situation.
**Example Sentence:**  
- After realizing he couldn't win the argument, he had to touch grass and admit he was wrong.
## **Chinese**
**释义：**  
- 放弃某个计划或方案；通常是当某人不得不承认失败或放弃时使用的表达。
**例句：**  
- 当他意识到很难赢得这场辩论时，他不得不停下来承认自己错了。
