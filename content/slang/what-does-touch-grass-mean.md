## "Touch Grass"
### English
- **Meaning**: An expression used to denote doing something incredibly stupid or admitting defeat in a sarcastic manner.
- **Example**: "I thought I could learn a new programming language in a week, but it was too hard. Touch grass."
### 中文
- **含义**：表示做了什么极其愚蠢的事情，或是在 sarcastic（刁钻刻薄的）意义上承认失败。
- **例句**："我以为我能在一个星期内学会一门新的编程语言，但实际上太难了。Touch grass。
