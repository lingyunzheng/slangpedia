## "Touch Grass" (英汉双解俚语)
**English:** Touch grass
**Literal Meaning:** 草
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "Touch grass" is an informal phrase used to describe a person who is extremely pregnant, to the point where the added weight and physical changes are so significant that they could be "touching" their grass (the ground or floor).
- **Chinese:** "Touch grass" 通常用于形容一个孕妇怀孕到了非常严重的状态，以至于腹部重量增加或身体变化如此显著，以至于腿部或身体其它部位可能会碰到地面或草地。
Example:
- *English:* "She is so pregnant, she’s started to touch grass."
- *Chinese:* “她怀孕已经严重到开始‘碰草’了。”
Note: This phrase is primarily used in casual or informal settings and is not appropriate in more formal contexts.
