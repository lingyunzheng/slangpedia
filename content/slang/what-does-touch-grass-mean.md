# "Touch Grass" in English and Chinese
## English
- **Definition**: To say something that is usually only said privately or to oneself, especially when reacting angrily or embarrassedly. It can also be used to express extreme disappointment or regret. 
- **Example Usage**: "I didn't get the job I applied for. I should've studied harder. Touch grass!"
## 中文
- **定义**: 通常只对自己或别人私下说的一些话，尤其是在感到生气或尴尬时。它也可以用来表达极大的失望或后悔。
- **例句**: "我没有得到我申请的工作，我应该更努力地学习。Touch grass!"
