## English Slang: "Touch Grass"
### Explanation
- **Meaning**: To regret an action or a decision one made in the past, often implying a change in perspective or a desire to undo a choice. It can also be used to express a change in attitude or behavior.
- **Example**: "I really touched grass when I accepted that job offer. I should have gone for the one in San Francisco."
## 中文俗语: "触草"
### 解释
- **含义**: 表达对过去所做决定的后悔，往往意味着改变态度或行为观点，或者渴望撤销之前的决定。也可以用来表达态度或行为的转变。
- **例句**: "我接受那份工作真是触草了。应该去旧金山的那份才对。"
