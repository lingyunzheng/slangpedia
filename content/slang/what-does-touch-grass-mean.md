## "Touch grass" (俚语)
### 中文解释
"触草"，通常用来形容想要放弃或承认无法达成某个目标或愿望时的泄气行为。
### 英文解释
"Touch grass," a slang term used to express a sense of extreme frustration or resignation, implying that one is so discouraged that they want to give up on their goal or wish. It's often used when someone feels they've reached an insurmountable obstacle.
