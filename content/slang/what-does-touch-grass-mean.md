## "Touch Grass"
### English Explanation:
- **Meaning**: To reconsider one's actions, thoughts, or decisions, often implying that someone has come to regret or reconsider a choice they previously made.
- **Example Usage**: "He thought about quitting his job but decided to touch grass and stayed, reconsidering his previous decision."
### Chinese Explanation:
- **意思**: 重新考虑自己的行为、想法或决定，通常意味着某人已经后悔或重新考虑了之前所做的选择。
- **例句**: "他考虑过辞职，但决定重新考虑自己的决定，继续留在原公司。"
