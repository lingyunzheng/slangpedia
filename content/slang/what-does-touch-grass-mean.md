# "Touch Grass"
## English
**Meaning:**
- To regret or admit past foolish behavior.
- Often used after making a mistake or after something negative has happened.
**Example:**
- "I shouldn't have loaned him the money. I really need to touch grass."
## 中文
**含义:**
- 承认过去的愚蠢行为，后悔。
- 经常在犯错或发生了负面事件后使用。
**例句:**
- "我不该把钱借给他，我真的应该好好反省自己。"
