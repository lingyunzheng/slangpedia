```markdown
## "Touch Grass"
**English:** "Touch grass" is an informal phrase used to express deeply regretting a decision or action. It's often used humorously but can also convey genuine remorse.
**Chinese:** “触草”是一个非正式的说法，用来表达对某个决定或行为的深深后悔。它可以幽默地使用，也可以表示真诚的懊悔。
- Usage Examples:
- English: "I should never have quit my job! I touch grass for that one."
- Chinese: “我本不该辞掉那份工作！我为此深感后悔，几乎要自杀。/ 我本不该辞掉那份工作！我要是碰一下草我就后悔了。”
- English: "If I had known what would happen, I would touch grass for suggesting that idea."
- Chinese: “要是我知道会发生什么，我建议那个点子我就悔得肠子都青了。”
- Variations:
- "Touch my grass" or "Touch her/his grass"—This is a stronger version indicating even more severe regret.
- Chinese: “碰我的草”或“碰她的草”或“碰他的草” —— 这是一个更强烈的版本，表示更加严重的后悔。
```
Note: The phrase "touch grass" is quite informal, and the usage might vary depending on the context and the speaker's tone.
