# "Touch Grass" Slang Explanation (Bilingual)
## English:
- **Expression:** Touch grass
- **Meaning:** Apologize or admit that one was wrong about something. This phrase is often used in British English but can be understood in American English as well.
- **Usage Example:** "I was wrong to say that, I should touch grass."
## 中文:
- **表达方式:** 触草
- **含义:** 承认自己在某件事上是错的，向别人道歉。这个词组通常用于英国英语，但美式英语中也应该能理解。
- **使用示例:** "我说那件事是错的，我应该触草道歉。"
