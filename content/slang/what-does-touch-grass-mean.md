# "Touch Grass"
## English
### Meaning
The phrase "touch grass" is a slang expression used to indicate that someone is about to die or is very close to dying. It's a metaphorical way of saying that someone is going to meet their end soon. 
### Example
"I've missed too many flights today; I really should have the surgery now. Touch grass, dude."
## 中文
### 含义
短语 "touch grass" 是一种俚语表达，用来表示某人即将死去或非常接近死亡。这是一种形象的说法，意思是某人将要赴死。
### 示例
“今天我都錯過太多班飛機了；我真的應該現在做手術了。快死了，伙计。”
