## "Touch Grass"
### English
**Meaning:** To express regret or remorse for a past action, often implying self-harm as a metaphor for an extreme penalty.
**Example Sentence:** "Man, I really touched grass when I skipped out on work last week."
### 中文
**含义:** 表示对过去行为的悔恨或懊恼，通常用自我伤害作为极端惩罚的隐喻。
**例句:** "伙计，我上周逃班真是太自作孽了。"
