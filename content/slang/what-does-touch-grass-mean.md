# "Touch Grass" (UK English / American English)
## English
**Touch Grass**: This phrase is an informal, vivid expression of giving up or quitting something, typically when referring to quitting smoking or other addictions. It's often used humorously or to convey a sense of liberation from an unwanted habit or situation.
**Example**: 
- "When I finally decided to quit smoking, I really felt like I was touching grass."
- "He's been touch grass for a year now, and he's feeling much better!"
## 中文
**触草 (Chù Cǎo)**: 这个短语是一个非正式、生动的表达方式，用来表示放弃或停止做某事，通常指的是戒烟或其他不良习惯。它经常被用作一种幽默的方式，或者表示从不 desired 的习惯或状况中解脱出来的感觉。
**例句**：
- "当我终于决定戒烟时，我真的感觉像是触了草。"
- "他现在已经戒烟一年了，感觉好多了！"
