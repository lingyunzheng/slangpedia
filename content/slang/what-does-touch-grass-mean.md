## "Touch grass" (Englisch) / 摸草 (Mō Cǎo, Chinese)
### Englischer Erklärung:
**"Touch grass"** is an American slang expression that is used to signify a dramatically negative circumstance or situation. When someone says "I might have to touch grass," it means they might have to commit suicide, often due to extreme hopelessness or despair. It's a very direct and concerning way of expressing that things are at their absolute worst.
### Chinesischer Erklärung:
**摸草 (Mō Cǎo)** 是一种中文俚语，用来形容一种极其悲观或绝望的情况。当有人说 "我可能不得不摸草" 时，这意味着他们可能不得不自杀，通常是由于极度的绝望或无望感。这是一个非常直接且令人担忧的表达方式，表明事情已经到了最糟糕的地步。
### Usage Examples:
- **Englisch:** "If she finds out about my failed business, I might have to touch grass."  
**Chinesisch:** "如果她发现我的企业失败了，我可能不得不摸草。"
- **Englisch:** "He's been unemployed for months, and just lost his house. He might have to touch grass."  
**Chinesisch:** "他已经失业了几个月，还失去了房子。他可能不得不摸草。"
### Note:
Using or hearing "touch grass" in this context is serious and should be taken very seriously. If you or someone you know is thinking about suicide, please seek help immediately from a professional or a crisis hotline.
