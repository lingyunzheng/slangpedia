## Touch Grass
**English:**  
- *Slang Meaning:* To do something that one considers cowardly or dishonest, often to avoid confrontation.
- *Usage Example:* "He tried to shirk responsibility by blaming the whole team; I told him it's time to touch grass."
**Chinese:**  
- *俚语解释:* 用来描述某人做一件被认为是胆小或不诚实的事情，通常是为了避免冲突。
- *使用示例:* "他试图通过指责整个团队来逃避责任；我告诉他是时候去触草了。"
