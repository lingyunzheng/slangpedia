# Slang: "Touch Grass"
## English
**Meaning:** To be dead; to be deceased.
**Example:** "He's touch grass now." (He's passed away.)
## 中文解释
**含义:** 表示某人已经去世。
**例句:** "他_touch grass_ 现在." (他已经去世了。)
