# "Touch Grass" - Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Touch Grass:** This is a colorful expletive used in internet slang and casual conversation. It's commonly used as a more mild form of a stronger curse word. When someone says, "Touch grass," they are expressing frustration, dissatisfaction, or anger, but in a less vulgar way compared to more harsh curse words. For example, if someone is upset about a misunderstanding, they might say, "Touch grass, I can't believe this is happening."
## 中文
**触草（Touch Grass）:** 这是一个在互联网俚语和日常对话中常用的粗话替代词。当有人使用"触草"这个表达时，他们通常是在表达沮丧、不满或愤怒的情绪，但它比更重污秽的脏话要温和得多。例如，如果某人因为误解而感到不满，他们可能会说，“触草，我简直不敢相信这会发生。”
用例：
- English: "Why did you change your mind so suddenly? Touch grass, I can't deal with this."
- 中文：你为什么突然改变主意了？触草，我真的受不了。
注意：这种表达方式带有较强的口语性和情绪化的色彩，使用时请根据语境和个人关系加以考虑。
