## "Touch grass" (英汉双解俗语)
### English Explanation
"Touch grass" is a slang phrase used to express a strong desire to commit suicide or to die. It is often said jokingly or in a vein of tough toughness, but the actual meaning can be quite serious.
### Chinese Explanation
"触草"（“触草”是网络用语，用于表达一个强烈的自杀欲望或想要死去的意思。这个词组通常被用来开玩笑或是装出一副不把生死当回事的样子，但实际含意可能非常严重。）
