## English: touch grass
### Chinese: 刷草（shuā cǎo）
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "Touch grass" is an informal way to describe someone as a disappointment or a failure, as if they're so left behind that they have to spend time "touching" the grass on the ground instead of progressing or succeeding in life.
- **Chinese:** "刷草（shuā cǎo）" is a term used in the context of online gaming, especially in mobile games, and it refers to a player who is not improving their game despite possibly having more time to practice or a better skill level, often causing frustration to other players. It can also be used metaphorically to describe someone who does not make progress despite having opportunities or time to do so.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "He's been playing for months, but he's still a touch grass when it comes to advanced levels."
- **Chinese:** "他玩了好几个月，但在高级关卡上还是个‘刷草’，让人大失所望。"
