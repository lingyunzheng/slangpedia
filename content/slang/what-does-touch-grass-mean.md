# English Slang: "Touch Grass"
**Definition:** To regret a past decision or action, often due to the experience of someone else or a change in one's own circumstances.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** "To regret a past decision or action, often due to the experience of someone else or a change in one's own circumstances."
- **Chinese (Simplified):** "悔不当初，指因他人的经历或自己情况的变化而后悔过去做出的决定或行动。"
- **Chinese (Traditional):** "悔不當初，指因他人的經歷或自己情況的變化而悔恨過去作出的決定或行動。"
