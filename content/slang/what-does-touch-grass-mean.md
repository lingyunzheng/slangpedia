## "Touch Grass" (俚语解释)
### 英语: "Touch Grass"
- **Explanation**: This expression, "touch grass," is an informal phrase used to express a strong desire or determination to take drastic action, typically involving a risky or extreme move, such as cutting one's own hair instead of going to a professional. It can also be used humorously to express a willingness to take bold actions, often metaphoric in nature.
### 中文: "触草"
- **解释**: "触草" 这个俚语用来表达强烈的欲望或决心采取极端行动，通常涉及冒险或非正规的行为，比如自己剪头发而不是去找专业人士。它也可以用于幽默地表达愿意采取大胆行动的意愿，往往是比喻性的。
