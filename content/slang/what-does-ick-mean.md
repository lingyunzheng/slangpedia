# Ick (English/Chinese)
## English
**Ick**:  
This word is often used to describe something that is unappealing, unpleasant, or gross. It's a type of verbal disgust, commonly used to express a strong aversion to something that might be slimy, smelly, or just plain yucky.
- *Example*: "That slimy oyster appetizer? Ick, it's too gross for me!"
## 中文
**ick**:  
这个词常用来形容令人感到不愉快、恶心或讨厌的事物。它是表达对某种可能滑腻、有异味或只是特别难吃的东西的强烈厌恶。
- *例句*: "那天味噌章鱼沙拉？呃，ick，太难吃了！"
