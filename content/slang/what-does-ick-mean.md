## Ick (ick)
### English
ICK - A word used to express disgust or revulsion. Typically accompanies something that is considered unpleasant, yucky, or gross. 
*Example:* "The slimy texture of the snail was totally ick!"
### 中文
ICK - 一个用来表达恶心或厌恶的俚语。通常伴随着一些令人不适、恶心或不卫生的事情。
*例句:* “蜗牛的黏腻质感真是让人心生厌恶！”
