# "Ick" in Bilingual Slang
**English:**
- **Definition:** "Ick" is a casual term used to express disgust, often paired with an ick sound made while making a face or wrinkling the nose.
- **Example Use:** "The moldy cheese is ick!" or "Ick, that's a gross habit."
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** "Ick" 是一个非正式的词汇，用来表达厌恶和嫌恶，常伴随着皱鼻子发出轻蔑的声音。
- **例句:** "发霉的奶酪真 ick！" 或者 "Ick，那是个糟糕的习惯。"
