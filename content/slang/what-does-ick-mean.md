## English
- **Definition**: Describes something that is disgusting, unpleasant, or gross. Often used to express disgust or revulsion.
- **Example**: "The slimy texture of the swamp was just ick."

- **例句**: "沼泽地里黏糊糊的感觉真是让人觉得 ick。"
