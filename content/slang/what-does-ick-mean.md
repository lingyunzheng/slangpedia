```markdown
### Ick (英语: Ick)
- **English:** Ick is an exclamation used to express disgust or revulsion towards something, usually something slimy, yucky, or otherwise unpleasant.
- **Chinese:** "我呸" (Wǒ pēi) or "恶心 (xīwen)" is often used to express disgust similarly to "ick." "我呸" can be more aggressively contemptuous, while "恶心" implies a stronger sense of revulsion or disgust.
```
