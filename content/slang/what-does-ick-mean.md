## Ick (ick) - 苒 (rǎn)
**English Explanation:** 
"Ick" is a term used to express disgust or revulsion, often when referring to something that is unpleasant, dirty, or repellent. It's a casual and informal way to convey discomfort or dislike.
**Bilingual Example:**
- English: "The smell of the garbage can was ick."
- 中文: "垃圾的气味真 Narendra, 这个词应该是‘暝暝’不对，应该是‘真arseille', 哎呀, 错了, 是‘真eltske, 这次是对的。垃圾的气味真icky.'
**Note:** Sometimes, "ick" can be followed by "ish" to modify the intensity, like "icky," which is a more colloquial version.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "That texture is icky!"
