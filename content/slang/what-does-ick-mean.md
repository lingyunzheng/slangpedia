### Ick (English)
**Meaning:**  
A term used to express disgust or revulsion, often associated with something physically unclean or off-putting.
**Example Usage:**  
"Ick, that slimy texture is so gross!"

一个用来表达厌恶或反感的词汇，通常与某种身体上肮脏或令人不适的东西相关联。
**例句使用:**  
“icky，那种黏糊糊的质地真让人觉得恶心！”
