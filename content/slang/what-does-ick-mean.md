## Ick (ICK)
**English:**  
Ick is a term used to express disgust or distaste. It's often used to describe something visually unappealing or unpleasant in smell, taste, or texture.  
**Example Usage:**  
- "This slime is just ick!"  
- "Yuck, that slimy thing is ick!"  
**Chinese (Pinyin):**  
Ick 的意思是表达厌恶或反感。通常用于描述视觉上不悦、难闻、难吃或粘腻的东西。  
**Example Usage:**  
- “这种胶水真恶心！”（This glue is just ick!)  
- “噁心，那个黏糊糊的东西真恶心！”（Yuck, that slimy thing is ick!）
