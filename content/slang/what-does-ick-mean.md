### ick (英语)
#### Explanation:
- **Meaning:** A slang term used to describe something unpleasant, disgusting, or off-putting.
- **Usage Example in English:** "The smell of the garbage can is ick."
### 厌恶心 (中文)
#### Explanation:
- **Meaning:** 一个俚语词汇，用来描述令人不悦、恶心或令人反感的事物。
- **Usage Example in Chinese:** "垃圾桶的味道真让人厌恶心。"
