## Ick (英: Ick)
### English
- **Definition:** A slang term used to express disgust, displeasure, or revulsion. It can be used for physical or metaphorical things that are unpleasant.
- **Example:** "The texture of slimy mud is just ick!"
### 中文
- **定义:** 俚语，用来表达厌恶、不悦或反感。可以用于描述令人不悦的物理或隐喻的东西。
- **例句:** "泥巴的黏腻质地真是让人觉得icky(icky是ick的发音形式)!"
