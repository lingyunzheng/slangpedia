## Ick (ick)
- **English**: An informal exclamation used to express disgust or aversion, often when confronted with something unclean, unpleasant, or unappetizing.
- **Chinese**: icky（icky）：一个非正式的短语，用来表达厌恶或反感，通常在面对不干净、不愉快或不美味的事物时使用。
