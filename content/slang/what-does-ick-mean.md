# Ick (English)
**Meaning:** A word used to express disgust or distaste, often in reference to something that seems unpleasant, slimy, or smelly.
**Usage Example:** "The slimy texture of that gunk was just icky!"
# 呕（ǐ）（Chinese）
**Meaning:** 用来表达厌恶或不耐烦，通常指的是令人不舒服、黏滑或难闻的事物。
**用法示例：** "那个黏糊糊的东西真是让人感到呕！"
