## Ick (ick)
### Chinese (简体中文)
**含义**: 这个词用来形容某样事物让人感觉不舒服或恶心，通常带有强烈的负面情感。
**例句**: 这个垃圾桶里全是吃剩的食物，闻起来好“ick”。
### English
**Meaning**: This term is used to describe something that feels unpleasant or gross, often accompanied by strong negative feelings.
**Example**: The trash can is full of leftover food, it smells really "ick".
