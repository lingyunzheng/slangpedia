## Ick (ICK)
### English
>Ick: A term used to express disgust, particularly about something repulsive or unpleasant, such as a slimy texture, an unpleasant smell, or a revolting sight.
Example: 
> "The slimy feeling of that kind of gel made my skin crawl. Ick!"
### 中文
>Ick: 一个用来表达厌恶或嫌恶的词汇，通常用于描述令人不悦的事物，比如黏腻的感觉、难闻的气味或令人反感的景象。
例句：
> “那种胶状物摸起来黏糊糊的，真的让我很不舒服。Ick！”
