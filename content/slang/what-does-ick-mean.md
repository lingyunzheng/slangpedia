# Ick (英式英语: Yuck)
## English Explanation
**Ick**: This word is used to express disgust or revulsion, typically in response to something yucky or gross. It's often an onomatopoeic term that mimics the feeling of something unpleasant. For example, "This slimy snail is ick!" 
## Chinese Explanation
**Ick (英式英语: Yuck)**：这个单词用来表达厌恶或反感，通常是针对那些又脏又恶心的东西时使用的。它常被用来模仿一种不舒服的感觉。例如，“这只黏糊糊的蜗牛真icky！”
