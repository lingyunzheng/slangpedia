### Ick (英) / 厌恶 (中)
**English Explanation:**
Ick is a short, somewhat informal term used to express disgust or aversion. It can be used to describe something that is unpleasant, unclean, or just plain yucky. For example, when someone says "That smells ick," they are indicating that they find the smell distasteful or repulsive.
**中文解释:**
"ick" 是一种简短且稍微非正式的词语，用来表达厌恶或反感。它可以用来描述令人不快、不干净或者就是难吃的任何事物。例如，当有人说到 "That smells ick," 他们表示对这种气味感到不喜欢或反感。
