## ick (ICK)
### English
An expression of disgust or revulsion. Often used when something is unappetizing, unpleasant, or just plain yucky.
**Example:** "This vegetables are so stale, they're ick!"
### 中文
表示厌恶或反感的表达。通常用于描述不诱人、不舒服或干脆难吃的某物。
**例句**："这些蔬菜太难吃了，简直就是‘ick’！"
