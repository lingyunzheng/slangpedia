# Ick (US English)
**Meaning:** An informal term used to express disgust or distaste for something, often something visually or bodily.
**Example:** I had to clean up all the spilled milk, and it was ick!
# 吐槽 (Tù cáo, 中文)
**Meaning:** 指的是表达对某人或某事的不满、批评或嘲笑，类似于英语中的“complain”或“rant”。
**Example:** 吐槽一下这个问题的设计真的很烂！ (Complain about how this design is really terrible!)
**Note:** "吐槽" also can be used more casually, like complaining or criticizing something in a joking manner.
