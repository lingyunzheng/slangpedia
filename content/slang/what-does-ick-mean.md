# Ick (俚语)
## 中文解释
"icky"（发音类似“ick”）是一句常用的俚俗表达，用来形容某些感觉或看上去令人不舒服或厌恶的事物。例如，湿湿黏黏的感觉、不干净的东西或者不愉快的气味。
## 英文解释
"Ick" is a slang term used to describe something that is unpleasant, unclean, or makes one feel uncomfortable. It's often used to express disgust or revulsion at the sight, smell, or feeling of something unwelcome or unpleasant.
