## Ick (ick)
**English:**
A term used to express disgust or revulsion, often when referring to something gross or unclean. It's like saying "yuck" or "ewww," but more casual and often used to express a strong dislike.
**Chinese:**
一个表示厌恶或反感的词汇，当你觉得某事恶心或不干净时使用。这就像说“噁心”或“好噁心”，但更加随意，并且通常用来表达强烈的反感。
