# Ick (英 / 伊克)
## English
### Explanation
"Ick" is a term used to express disgust, contempt, or dislike for something that is considered unpleasant, such as a certain smell, appearance, or texture. It's a short and informal way to convey a visceral negative reaction.
### Example
- "The sliminess of that food is ick!"
- "Ick, that stain really looks permanent!"
## 中文
### 解释
"伊克"（Ick）是一个用来表达对某种令人不快、厌恶的东西（如气味、外观或质地）的厌恶、轻蔑或反感的术语。这是一种简洁且非正式的方式来表达一种直觉上的负面反应。
### 示例
- “那食物的黏糊糊真的很伊克！”
- “伊克！那个污渍真的很难洗掉！”
