# Ick
## English
**Ick**: Used to express disgust or revulsion, often about something that looks or feels slimy or yucky. 
*Example*: "The texture of that slimy plant is ick!"
## Chinese
**icky (ick)**: 表达对某物感到厌恶或反感，尤其是那些看起来或摸起来黏糊糊或恶心的东西。 
*例句*: “那种黏糊糊植物的质感真icky！”
