**English:**  
Ick is a versatile slang term used to express disgust or revulsion, often in response to something that is unpleasant, odd, or simply not enjoyable. It can be used to describe both tangible and intangible things, from the sight of slimy creatures to the sound of a nauseating noise. 
Example usage:
- "The texture of that slimy cucumber dish was ick!" (那个滑腻的黄瓜菜尝起来太恶心了！)
- "Their arguing is ick." (他们的争吵真让人恶心。)
