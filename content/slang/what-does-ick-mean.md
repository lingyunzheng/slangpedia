# Ick (俚语解释)
## 英文
**Ick**: A simple yet versatile term used to express disgust or aversion to something, often anatomical or bodily. It can be used to describe the feeling of feeling physically or mentally unwell, or when something just feels "off" or wrong.
*Example*: "The slimy texture of the octopus made my stomach feel icky."
## 中文
**ick**: 一个简洁且多功能的词汇，用来表达对某物的厌恶或反感，尤其是涉及解剖学或身体的部分。它可以用来描述身体或精神上的不适感，或者某种东西感觉“不对劲”或“有问题”。
*例句*: "八爪鱼黏黏的质地让我觉得很反胃。"
