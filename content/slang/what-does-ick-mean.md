## ick (也作 yucky)

- **English:**  
  - **Meaning:** Something unpleasant or disgusting. Used to describe food, feelings, or situations.
  - **Example Usage:** "The smell of last night's leftovers is ick!"

## ick (也作 yucky)

- **English:**  
  - **Meaning:** Something unpleasant or disgusting. Used to describe food, feelings, or situations.
  - **Synonyms:** gross, revolting, nauseating, distasteful
  - **Example Usage:** "The oozy texture of that oozing cheese is ick!"
  
- **Chinese (简体中文):**  
  - **含义:** 用来描述令人厌恶或恶心的事物，可以指食物、感觉或情境。
  - **同义词:** 令人作呕的，恶心的，令人反胃的，不好吃的
  - **例句:** "那种流质奶酪的黏糊糊质感真 yucky！"
