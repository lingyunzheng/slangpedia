# Ick (英 / 伊克 / i克)
## English:
**Ick:** A word used to convey disgust or aversion, often when describing someone or something that is unappealing or unpleasant. It can be used as a noun or a verb, and sometimes it's used to emphasize the negative feelings toward something.
**Example Sentences:**
- *Ick, that's a really old sweater.* (Expressing disgust at the appearance of an old sweater.)
- *She feels icked by the idea of eating insects.* (She feels disgust towards the idea of eating insects.)
## Chinese:
**伊克 (i克):** 一个用来表达厌恶或反感的词，当描述某人或某物让人觉得不悦时常用。它可以作为名词或动词使用，有时用来强调对某事物的负面感觉。
**例句:**
- *伊克，这件毛衣太旧了.* (表达对一件旧毛衣外观的厌恶。)
- *她听到吃虫子这个想法就觉得伊克.* (她对吃虫子这个想法感到厌恶。)
