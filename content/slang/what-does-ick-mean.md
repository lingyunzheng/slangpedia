## ick (ICK)
### English
**Meaning:** Something that is disgusting or unpleasant, often used to describe food.
- **Example:** "The moldy cheese is so ick; I can't even look at it!"
### Chinese
**含义:** 用来描述觉得恶心或不高兴的东西，尤其是食物。
- **例句:** "那发霉的奶酪真是太 ick 了，我都看不下去！"
