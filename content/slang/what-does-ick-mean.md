## Ick (ick)
### English
**Definition:** A term used to express disgust or revulsion, often in a casual or dismissive manner.
**Example:** 
- "The smell of that rotting food is **ick**!"
- "Eating that slimy frog leg is **ick**, man!"
### Chinese
**释义:** 一个用来表达厌恶或反感的俚语，通常在非正式语境中使用。
**例句：**
- "那腐烂食物的气味真 **呕**！"
- "吃那黏糊糊的蛙腿真 **呕**，哥们！"
