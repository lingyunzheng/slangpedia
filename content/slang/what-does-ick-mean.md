# Ick (英式英语)
**Definition:** A general term used to express strong disgust, often when something is unpleasant, especially in a physical way.
**Example in a sentence:** "Ick, that slimy snail on the sidewalk gave me the creeps!"
# Ick (美式英语)
**Definition:** A casual term used to express strong disgust or displeasure, often when something is gross or unsavory.
**Example in a sentence:** "Ick, that old sandwich smells awful!"
