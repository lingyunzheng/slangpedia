## Ick (英式英语: Ick)
### English
**Definition:** Ick is a disgust term used to express revulsion or dislike, often towards something that is considered unpleasant or gross.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义:** Ick 是一个表示厌恶或不喜欢的词，通常用来形容不愉快或肮脏的东西。
- **用法:** 
- *Example 1:* "This can of soup has sat in the cupboard for days. Ugh, that's ick!"
- *中文例句:* "这罐汤已经放在柜子里好几天了。哎呀，真恶心！"
- *Example 2:* "Ick! My phone is full of sticky sweets."
- *中文例句:* "哦恶心！我的手机满是粘人的糖果！"
### English
**Definition:** In informal English, ick can also refer to a sense of annoyance or irritation.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义:** 在非正式的英语中，ick 还可以表示一种不耐烦或烦恼的感觉。
- **用法:** 
- *Example:* "That guy keeps talking about himself. ick!"
- *中文例句:* "那个人老是说自己。真烦人！"
