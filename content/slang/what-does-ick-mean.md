## Ick (英: Ick)
**English:** A slang term used to express disgust or disapproval, often when something is perceived as gross or unappealing. It's like saying "ick!" when you see something yucky.
**Simplified Chinese:** (形容)一种表示厌恶或不悦的俚语，通常表示某物令人作呕或不吸引人。就像看到恶心的东西时说“呸！”
**Pinyin:** Tán (form of disapproval) yì zhǒnɡ biǎo.submitì ɡǎo (表示) yǒnɡ bù yuè (不悦) de  lí yǔ ， thiÁo ɡéné (般) yònɡ biǎo shén me yōu qín (什么龌龊) de clue (东西).
**Example:**
- **English:** That food smells ick!
- **Chinese:** 这个食物气味好难过！ (Zhège shíwù wèiwèi hǎo hào náiguò! )
