## Ick (英/ick/ 汉/伊克/)
**English:**  
Ick is a slang term used to express disgust or revulsion. It's often used in a context where someone finds something unpleasant, especially something slimy, smelly, or just plain yucky. For example, you might say "This slimy texture is ick" if you find a food or substance squishy and unappealing.
**Chinese:**  
伊克 (Ik) 是用来表达厌恶或反感的俚语。通常在描述某物不令人愉快时使用，特别是那些让人觉得恶心、黏腻、臭，或者仅仅觉得不舒服的东西。例如，如果你觉得某种食物或物质摸起来黏糊糊的，不堪入口，你会说“这种黏糊的感觉真 伊克”。
---
### Example Sentences:
**English:**
- The slimy texture of that jelly is ick!
- Ick, that smell makes me want to throw up!
**Chinese:**
- 那果冻黏糊糊的触感真是伊克！
- 天啊，这个味道简直让人想吐！伊克！
