# Ick (ICK)
## English
**Definition:** Describes something that is unpleasant or disgusting.
**Example:** "Ick, that slimy snail on the sidewalk! Gross!"
## Chinese (简体中文)
**释义:** 描述令人不适或令人恶心的事物。
**例句:** "ick, 路上面那只黏糊糊的蜗牛！真恶心！"
