## Ick (English)
**Definition:** A slang term used to express disgust or revulsion, often towards something that is visually or physically unpleasant.
**Example:** "The sliminess of the jelly was ick!"
## 厌恶 (Ick in Chinese)
**释义：** 一个俚语，用来表达对令人厌恶或恶心的事物的反感，通常是对视觉上或物理上不愉快的事物。
**例句：** “这果冻的黏滑感真是**厌恶**。”
---
Both terms are used to convey feelings of disgust or revulsion, but "ick" is used in English, while "厌恶" (wù è) is its Chinese equivalent.
