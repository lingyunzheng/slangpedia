## Ick (ICK)
### English
**Definition:** Ick is an informal term used to express disgust or distaste, usually in relation to something that is unclean, unpleasant, or just plain off-putting.
**Examples:**
- The smell in the kitchen made me feel ick.
- The texture of that slimy worm was ick!
### Chinese
**定义:** "ick" 是一个非正式的术语，用来表达对某些不清洁、令人不悦或仅仅令人不适的东西的厌恶感。
**例句:**
- 厨房里的气味让我感觉很“ick”。
- 那条滑腻腻的虫子的质地让我感觉很“ick”。
