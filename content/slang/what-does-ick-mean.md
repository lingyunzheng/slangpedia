### 超级无敌辣鸡 (Chuānɡjí Wúbǐ Dàjī)
- **English**: Ick
- **Explanation**: Used to describe something extremely unpleasant, disgusting, or bad. It can refer to food, smells, or situations that are simply so bad that they elicit a visceral negative reaction. For example, if someone cooks a meal that tastes absolutely awful, you might say, "This is so ick! It smells like it just rotted!" In spoken language, this term often conveys a sense of disgust or revulsion.
