# Ick
**English:**
In English, "ick" is a noun or interjection used to express disgust or revulsion, often about something that is unclean, unpleasant, or simply unsettling. It can also be used as an adjective to describe such things.
Example:
- "The slimy texture of that oyster made me feel icky."
- "Ick! Don't touch that, it's covered in dirt."
**中文:**
在英语中，“ick”是一个名词或感叹词，用来表示厌恶或反感，通常是对不干净、不愉快或仅仅是令人不安的事物。它也可以用作形容词，来描述这样的事物。
例句：
- "那牡蛎黏黏的质感让我感觉很icky。"
- "哎呀！别碰那个，上面全是泥土。"
