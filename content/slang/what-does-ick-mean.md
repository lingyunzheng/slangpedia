## Ick (English)
- **Definition**: A colloquial term typically used to express disgust or revulsion, often in reference to something that is physically unpleasant or off-putting.
- **Example**: "That slimy texture really ickes me out."
## 厌恶心 (Yànxīwěi, Chinese)
- **Definition**: 表达对某物的厌恶或反感，通常指一些让人感到不适或反感的事物。
- **Example**: "那种黏糊糊的感觉真的让我感到厌恶心。"
