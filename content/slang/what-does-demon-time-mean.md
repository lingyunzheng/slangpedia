## Demon Time (Demon Tiempo)
### English
**Demonic**: Referring to something extremely powerful, overwhelming, or intense, often in a negative context. When someone is in "demon time," they're in a supremely focused or determined state, possibly to the point of being aggressive or irritable.
Example: "She's been in demon time all day, don't mess with her."
### 中文
**恶魔时间**: 形容某人处于非常强大、压倒性或者强烈的状态，通常是负面的含义。当某人在“恶魔时间”时，他们处于一种极度专注或决心的状态，可能表现出侵略性或者易怒。
例子: “她整天都在恶魔时间，最好不要跟她胡来。”
