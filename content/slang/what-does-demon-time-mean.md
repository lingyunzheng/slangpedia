### Demon Time (恶魔时间 - Mòdiān Shijiān)
**English Explanation:**
"Demon time" is a colloquial term used to describe a particularly challenging or frustrating period when everything seems to go wrong. It's often associated with situations where a person is going through a series of unfortunate events or deals with multiple stressful situations simultaneously. It's as if the universe is aligned to make life difficult for the individual.
**Chinese Explanation:**
“恶魔时间”是用来形容一个特别艰难或令人沮丧的时期，这时候几乎所有的事情都出问题。这个词常常用来描述一个人经历一系列不幸事件或同时面临多个压力较大的情况。就像是在特定的时刻，命运会安排一系列困难局面来考验这个人的能力。
