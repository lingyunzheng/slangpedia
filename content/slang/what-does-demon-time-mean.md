```markdown
## Demon Time (恶魔时间)
**中文**：恶魔时间
**英文**：Demon Time
**解释**：
- **中文**：这是一个流行于网络和社交平台的梗，通常用来形容一个人在参加朋友聚会、毕业聚会或其他社交活动时，过度放纵自我，尽情享受、游戏直至深夜甚至通宵达旦的状态。在这个时间里，人仿佛释放了所有的天性，追求快乐到极限。
- **英文**：This term refers to a slang expression commonly used on the internet and social media. It describes a situation where a person lets loose and indulges completely, staying up very late or all night during social gatherings, parties, or other social events. During demon time, one seems to unleash all their natural instincts, pursuing happiness to its extreme.
```
