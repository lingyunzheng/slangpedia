# Demon Time
## English Explanation:
"Demon Time" is often used humorously to describe an extended period of time when tasks seem to take forever to complete. It can also be used more metaphorically to describe any situation that feels particularly challenging or time-consuming, especially in the gaming or tech world.
## Chinese Explanation:
“Demon Time”是一种俚语，用来幽默地描述一个任务需要花费比预期长时间来完成的时期。它也可以在更隐喻的意义上使用，特别是指那些感觉特别困难或耗时的情况，特别是在 Gaming 或科技领域。
