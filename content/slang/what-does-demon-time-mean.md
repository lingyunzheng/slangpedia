## Demon Time (恶魔时间, Démo Shíjiān)
- **English**: "Demon Time" is a playful, informal term often used to describe a situation where time seems to either stretch or speed up, making it feel like time moves in a distorted manner. This phrase usually arises in the context of being occupied with an extremely engaging or stressful activity, like completing a challenging task or playing a highly immersive video game.
- **Chinese**: "恶魔时间"是一个轻松且非正式的术语，通常用来形容一种时间似乎以扭曲的方式流逝的情况。这种情况往往发生在人们全神贯注于一项极具挑战性或充满压力的活动时，比如完成艰巨的工作任务，或沉浸在一款扣人心弦的电子游戏中。
