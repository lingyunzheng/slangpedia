# Demon Time (Demon Time)
## English
**Demon Time**: A phrase used to describe an extreme state of focus, determination, or intensity, often associated with the amount of effort or time spent working or studying. It can also imply a period where all efforts are poured into completing a challenging task, similar to when someone goes into a trance-like state due to their intense focus.
## Chinese
**恶魔时间 (恶魔时间)**：用来形容一种极度专注、决心或强度的状态，通常与投入大量精力工作或学习的时间相关。它也可以指一个所有努力都投入完成艰巨任务的时期，就像某人因极度集中注意力而进入一种类似入魔的状态一样。
