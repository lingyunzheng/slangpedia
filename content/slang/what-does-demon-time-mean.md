# Demon Time (恶魔时间 - Diémò Shíjiān)
**English Slang Explanation:**
"Demo time" is often used in online gaming communities to describe a period or moment when a player or a team suddenly performs exceptionally well, seeming to use supernatural abilities. It can also refer to the advantage a gamer gets when their rhythm and strategy align perfectly.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
"恶魔时间"通常用来形容游戏过程中某玩家或队伍突然表现出色，仿佛拥有了某种超自然的能力。也可以指玩家在手感和策略完全契合时所获得的优势。
Example in use:
- **English:** "He got demon time in that round, absolutely flawless play!"
- **Chinese:** "他那一局就是恶魔时间，简直无敌了！"
