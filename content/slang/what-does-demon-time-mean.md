## Demon Time
### 中文解释：
“Demon Time” 是一种网络用语，常用来形容一种高效的、超人的工作或学习状态。在这种状态下，人的效率直线上升，仿佛时间被放慢了，能够短时间内完成大量的工作或学习任务，就像“魔鬼时间”一样。
### 英文解释：
“Demon Time” is a slang term used in internet culture to describe a period of extremely efficient and superhuman work or study. During this state, a person's efficiency skyrockets, as if time is being slowed down, and they can complete a large amount of work or study tasks in a short period, akin to working or studying in "demon time."
