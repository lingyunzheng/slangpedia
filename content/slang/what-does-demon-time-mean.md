## Demon Time (also known as "鬼马时间" in Chinese)
### English:
**Demon Time:**  
This slang term is used in English-speaking regions, typically among teenagers and young adults. "Demon Time" refers to a period when a person is extremely energetic, hyperactive, and prone to mischievous or comedic behavior. It's like when you feel supercharged with energy and can't keep still, often leading to spontaneous pranks or antics. Think of it as your "inner demon" coming out to play!
### Chinese:
**鬼马时间 (Guǐmǎ Shíjiān):**  
这个俚语在中国的青少年和年轻成人中流行，通常是在描述一个人异常活跃、充满活力且容易搞怪或调皮的状态。"鬼马时间"就像是你的“恶魔”内核爆发出来，让你变成一个无法静止且总是充满创意搞怪的人。你可以想象成在你的血液里流淌着一股不可抑制的活力和创意，让你的各种机智或偶尔的调皮瞬间释放。
---
**Example Usage:**
- English: "Man, after midnight it's demon time in the dormitory! Everyone is up, playing pranks, and the room is a mess."
- Chinese: "哎呀，半夜过后宿舍里就是鬼马时间了，大家都在搞怪，房间一片混乱。"
