## Demon Time (恶魔时间 - Démon Shíjiān)
**English Explanation**:
"Demo Time" is a slang term often used in video games, particularly in the popular genre of fighting games. It refers to the critical moment right before a character's super move or final attack can be executed. In this timeframe, a skilled player can use specific inputs or combos to achieve more powerful effects or to chain together a series of attacks, often making the game more challenging and rewarding.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation**:
- **恶魔时间 (Démon Shíjiān)**: 在格斗游戏中，特别是在某些角色扮演或对战类型游戏中，“Demo Time”指的是角色在使用超级技能或最终攻击之前的那个关键时刻。在这段时间里，高超的玩家可以利用特定的输入或连招来实现更强大的效果，或者将一系列攻击连锁起来，使得游戏更加充满挑战和成就感。
