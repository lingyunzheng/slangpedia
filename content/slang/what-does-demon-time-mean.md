## Demon Time
### English
**Demon Time**: This phrase is often used to describe an extended period of time when someone works, studies, or focuses on a task so intently that they completely forget about food, sleep, and other basic needs. It's akin to being in a trance or a flow state where everything seems to slow down and time flies by.
### Chinese
**恶魔时间**：这个短语通常用来形容一个人为了完成工作、学习或专注在某项任务上，而完全忘记了吃饭、睡觉及其他基本需求的漫长时光。这时候，人们仿佛进入了一种迷醉或全神贯注的状态，感觉时间变得缓慢甚至飞逝。
