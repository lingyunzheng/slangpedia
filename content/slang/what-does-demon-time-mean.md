## Demon Time (鬼畜时间)
### Chinese:
**鬼畜时间 (Guichu Shijian)**
- **Explanation:**
"鬼畜时间" 是一种网络流行语，用来形容一种令人抓狂、让人感到烦躁的时间段，或者是事情变得让人难以忍受的情况。这个词组中的“鬼畜”原本指的是那些令人抓狂、沉迷其中难以自拔的视频内容，如“鬼畜视频”。现在则被广泛用于描述现实生活中的各种让人感到焦虑或厌烦的时段或情况。
- **Example:**
“自从公司的那个项目开始，这几天都是鬼畜时间，压力山大。”
### English:
**Demon Time**
- **Explanation:**
"Demon Time" is a slang term commonly used on the internet to describe a period that is extremely frustrating or annoying. Originally derived from the term "ghostly entertainment" which refers to videos that are extremely addictive or irritating, it is now widely used to describe situations in real life that cause anxiety or frustration. It captures moments when time seems to drag on endlessly and everything feels chaotic.
- **Example:**
"Ever since the project started at work, these few days have been demon time, really putting a lot of pressure on me."
