## Demon Time (恶魔时间)
**English:** Demon Time:  
This phrase is often used to describe a specific moment, typically a brief period of time, when someone is extremely busy or overwhelmed. It's as though the person is living through a period where the normal rules of time don't apply, and they are struggling just to keep their head above water.
**中文:** 恶魔时间：  
这个词组通常用来形容某一特定时刻，通常是短时间内极其忙碌或处在崩溃边缘的状态。就好像这个人正经历一段时期，时间的常规规则都不适用，他们正努力让自己不至于被时间拖垮。
---
### Example Usage:
**English:** "I've been in demon time all week, but at least I completed the project!"
**中文:** "过去一周我都是在恶魔时间里度过的，但至少项目搞定了！"
**English:** "Don't call me right now, I'm in demon time and can't deal with distractions."
**中文:** "现在别打电话了，我正处于恶魔时间，没法分心。"
