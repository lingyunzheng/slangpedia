# Demon Time
## 中文解释
**妖孽时刻**：形容一个人或一个场景特别吸引人注意力的瞬间。通常指的是异常吸引人、具有强烈吸引力和魅力的时刻。
## 英文解释
**Demon Time**: refers to a moment when someone or something is incredibly captivating and draws a lot of attention. It describes instances of extraordinary allure and strong charm that stand out attractively.
