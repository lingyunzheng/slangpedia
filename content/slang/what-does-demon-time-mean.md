## Demon Time
- **English**: "Demon Time" is a term often used to describe a situation where someone, usually under extreme time pressure, feels like they have lost control over how time passes, almost as if time is moving in a way that is unfavorable to them. It's like being stuck in a perfect storm of delayed tasks and tight deadlines.
- **Chinese**: “恶魔时间”通常用来形容一个人在极度时间紧迫的情况下，觉得自己失去了对时间的掌控，好像时间在以一种不利的方式流动，就像是被延期的任务和紧张的截止日期困住了。就像是掉进了时间风暴里，完全没法按计划行事。
---
Here, "demon time" conveys the feeling of being overwhelmed by time-related stress, almost as if an evil entity (the demon) is manipulating time to make things difficult for you.
