## Demon Time (恶魔时间, Maidemo Shijian)
**English:** "Demon Time" might be a playful or figurative way to describe a period when someone is exceptionally busy or has an abundance of energy, almost as if they're a demon. It can also be used to describe a situation where someone is feeling particularly stressed or frustrated.
**中文:** "恶魔时间"这个词通常用来形容一个人特别忙碌或是精力旺盛，仿佛变成恶魔般活跃。这个词也可以用来描述一个人感到特别紧张或沮丧的情况。
### Example sentences:
**English:** During her demon time, she managed to finish both projects on time.
- 中文：在她的恶魔时间里，她设法同时完成了两个项目。
**English:** I've been dealing with so much work these days, it's like demon time.
- 中文：这些天的工作量真的很大，感觉像处在恶魔时间一样。
