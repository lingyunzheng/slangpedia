## Demon Time (Demon Time)
### English Explanation
"Waking up crazily in the middle of the night. Spawning a surge of energy and getting into action, whatever that may be. Often characterized by doing tasks, playing games, or just generally being super productive."
### Chinese Explanation
“夜里突然精神抖擞， Fostered by 异次元空间 (Divergent Dimensional Space), 开启了疯狂加班/游戏/创作的时间。这期间可能会做很多事，即使在半夜也能觉得精力充沛。”
### Usage Example
- **English:** Suddenly, it's Demon Time!
- **Chinese:** 突然间，这就是Demon Time！
---
### Explanation Breakdown
- **Demon Time** is a fun way to describe sudden nighttime productivity or enthusiasm that turns nighttime into a mini-day with lots of action.
- **异次元空间 (Divergent Dimensional Space)**: This term, often used in Chinese internet slang, gives a humorous reference to the concept of "another time dimension" where anything can happen.
- **疯狂加班/游戏/创作的时间 (Crazy Working/Game/Creative Time)**: This directly translates to the idea of hyper-productivity or immersion in activities, regardless of the time.
