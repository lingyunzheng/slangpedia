## Demon Time (恶魔时间: Mò ér shí jì)
### English Explanation
"Demon Time" is a phrase often used in casual conversation to describe a difficult, stressful, or chaotic period of time. It can also refer to a brief but intense burst of activity or problems. The term might be used to lament or describe situations where things seem to go wrong or get complicated unexpectedly.
### Simplified Chinese Explanation
“恶魔时间”这个说法通常用于日常对话中，用来描述一段难以、高压或混乱的时间。这个词也可以指一段短暂但异常忙碌或问题众多的时段。这句话可能用来感叹或描述那些事情突然变糟或变得复杂不顺的情况。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "I'm really struggling with my project right now, it feels like demon time."
- **Simplified Chinese:** “我现在真的很难搞定我的项目，感觉像进入了恶魔时间。”
### Example Usage in a Sentence
- **English:** "It has been one demon time after another this month. I can't even catch a break."
- **Simplified Chinese:** “这个月简直是一段恶魔时间接着一段，我都喘不上气来休息一下了。”
