## Demon Time
**中文含义：**
- **含义1：** 指一个非常短暂但极度激烈的时刻，常用来形容运动比赛的关键时刻、紧张刺激的情节转折等。
- **含义2：** 有时也用于形容在一段时间内效率极高，尽全力去完成任务或达到目标的时刻。
**英文解释：**
- **Meaning 1:** Refers to a very brief but intense moment, often used to describe key moments in sports games or dramatic plot twists.
- **Meaning 2:** Also refers to a period when you achieve maximum efficiency and work hard to accomplish a task or goal with all your effort.
