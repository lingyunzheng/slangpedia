## Demon Time (中文：鬼時間)
**English Explanation:**
- **Demon Time:** Also known as "Devil Time" or "鬼时间" in Chinese, it refers to periods when things seem to go wrong, or when someone's luck or mood takes a turn for the worse. It's often used to describe a time when you're consistently running into obstacles, making mistakes, or experiencing bad luck.
**Slang Usage Example:**
- If someone is having a series of unfortunate events like losing keys, spilling coffee, or missing train connections, they might joke, "I must be in demon time today."
**Chinese Explanation:**
- **鬼时间 (guǐ shí jiān):** 通常指的是运势不佳或心情糟糕的时段。当人们经历一系列不顺利的事情，比如遗失钥匙、洒咖啡或者错过火车，他们可能会开玩笑地说自己“今天鬼时间很准”。
**Usage Example:**
- 如果有人连续发生一系列倒霉的事，比如掉钥匙、咖啡洒了或是错失了火车，他可能会自嘲道：“我今天真是鬼时间！”
