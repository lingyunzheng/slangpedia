## Demon Time (Demon Time)
**English Explanation:**  
"Demon Time" is a slang term often used to describe a period when someone is exceptionally productive, energized, or simply in a high-performing state. It's as if they are channeling a mysterious and supernatural power to accomplish a lot in a short period.
**Chinese Explanation:**  
"Demon Time" （妖魔鬼怪时间）是指一个人在特定时间段内特别有干劲、充满能量或表现非常出色的状态。仿佛他们被某种神秘而超自然的力量加持，能在短时间内完成大量工作。
Example Usage:
- She seems to be in demon time today, finishing all her projects in just a few hours!
- 今天她似乎进入了妖魔鬼怪时间，几个小时内就把所有项目搞定啦！
