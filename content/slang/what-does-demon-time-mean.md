# Demon Time (Demon time)
## English
**Demon Time**: A phase or moment when someone is exceptionally productive, usually referring to a burst of creativity or periods of intense focus and output. It's as if a supernatural being is lending them strength.
Example:
- "When she finally gets into Demon Time, there's no stopping her from completing that massive project."
## 中文
**Demon 时间 (Demon time)**：一个人在极短时间内非常高效、充满创造力或极度专注并产出大量成果的阶段，就好像有某种超自然的力量在助力他们。
例句：
- “当她进入 Demon 时间，就没人能阻止她完成那个大项目了。”
