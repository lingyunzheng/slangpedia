## Demon Time
**中文解释：** 
“Demon Time” 或者叫“佛系时间”，是一种表示时间管理松懈、做事不着急的生活态度。这种说法来源于一种轻松、不赶时间的生活方式，就像轻功をしている妖怪（妖怪，即日本对“妖怪”或“鬼怪”的称呼，也比喻一种逍遥自在的状态）一样，可以随心所欲，不拘泥于现实的紧时间线。
**English Explanation:**
"Demon Time" or "Buddhist Mode Time" is a slang term used to describe a laid-back, no-haste attitude towards time management. It originates from a relaxed, un-rushed lifestyle, much like how a free spirit or a "demon" (a playful reference to a carefree and unhurried state) moves through time. This term embodies a casual, unpressured approach to time, akin to being untethered by the constraints of strict schedules.
---
**Example Usage:**
- 中文：项目本来应该赶在晚上之前完成，但他决定在“Demon Time”享受最后的自由时光。
- English: The project was supposed to be completed by evening, but he decided to enjoy the last bit of free time in "Demon Time."
- 中文：今天的任务都安排得比较宽松，我们就可以给自己留出一些“Demon Time”。
- English: Today’s tasks are rather flexible, so we can set aside some time in "Demon Time" for ourselves.
