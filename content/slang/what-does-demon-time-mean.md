## Demon Time
### 中文解释
“Demon Time”是指一种时间利用上的比拼，通常是双方约定在一个特定的时间段内完成某项任务或挑战。这种说法原意偏向竞赛或游戏，会在一些竞技活动中出现，或者在朋友间比胜负时使用。
### 英文 Explanation
"Demon Time" refers to a period of time where individuals or groups compete to see who can accomplish a task or challenge within a specified timeframe. This phrase is often used in competitive or gaming contexts, or when friends are challenging each other and want to see who performs better.
