## Demon Time
**中文解释：恶魔时间**
**英文解释:** Demon Time
"Demon Time" is a slang phrase often used to describe a period or situation when someone is at their absolute worst. It can be used to refer to a phase in life when a person behaves poorly, makes bad decisions, or is generally acting in a way that reflects their "darker side." 
Similar to the concept of "hell week" or "dark days," this phrase is often used with a tone of humor or sarcasm, suggesting that a person is experiencing a challenging or unproductive period. For example, "I'm really struggling with project deadlines, it's demon time!" or "After a long night out, I woke up feeling like it was demon time."
