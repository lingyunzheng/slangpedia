## Demon Time (汉语： demon time)
**中文解释：** 
「Demon Time」这个词汇源自网络用语，常用来形容一个人突然精神状态爆棚，效率或能力大幅提升的时间段。就像恶魔会给人们戴上魔法帽子，让人变得特别有干劲或特别能干。常常用于自嘲或幽默场合，表示某段时期内自己表现得特别好，但这种状态通常不会持续太久。
**英文解释：**
"Demon Time" is a term from internet slang used to describe a period when someone's productivity or capability suddenly surges. It's like a demon has put a magical hat on them, making them extremely motivated or exceptionally capable. It's often used in a self-deprecating or joking manner, indicating a period when you perform exceptionally well, but this peak state usually won't last long.
