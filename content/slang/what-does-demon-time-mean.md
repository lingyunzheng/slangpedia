## Demon Time (恶魔时间)
### English Slang Explanation:
"Demotion time" is a humorous or informal term used to refer to a period when someone feels their career or social standing has taken a downturn. It can also be used to describe a situation where someone is having a tough time or facing multiple challenges all at once.
Example:
- After the company downsized, she had a real demotion time trying to find a new job.
### Chinese Slang Explanation:
"恶魔时间" (démò shíjiān) 通常用来形容一个人的职业或社会地位出现下滑的情况，也可以用来指一个人处于艰难时期，面对诸多挑战。
例句：
- 自从公司裁员后，她经历了一段恶魔时间，努力找新工作。
