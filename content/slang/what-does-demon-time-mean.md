# Demon Time (恶魔时间 - Mò'érrén shíjiān)
## English Explanation
"Demon time" in English slang often refers to a time where someone is in a particularly bad or annoying mood, or when things seem to go wrong constantly. It can also describe a period when someone is overly critical or overly sensitive to minor issues. 
**Example:**
- "It's demon time today, I keep messing up even simple tasks."
## Chinese Explanation
“恶魔时间”在中文网络用语中，指的是一个人情绪特别糟糕或烦人的时段，或是事情总是出乎意料地变得一团糟的时候。这个词也可以用来形容一个人对小事过于挑剔或者敏感的时期。
**例句：**
- "今天是恶魔时间，我连简单的任务都搞砸了。"
