## Demon Time (恶魔时间)
### English
**Demon Time**: Refers to a thrilling or intense period or situation. It can also denote the feeling of having very little time to complete a task or achieve something. It's often used to describe moments where you're closely following a tight deadline or in the middle of an exciting but stressful event.
### 中文
**恶魔时间**：常用来形容一段充满刺激或紧张的时期或情况。它也可以指你感到在完成某项任务或达成某个目标方面时间非常紧迫的时候。这个词经常被用来描述你紧跟截止日期或是处于一场既令人兴奋又充满压力的活动中时的时刻。
