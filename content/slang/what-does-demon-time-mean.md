## Demon Time
- **中文解释**: 恶魔时间是指在某个时间段内，事情总是出错，效率极低，进展缓慢，感觉像是有无形的恶魔在捣乱，时间仿佛停滞。
- **英文解释**: "Demon Time" refers to a time period where things go wrong constantly, productivity is extremely low, and progress is slow. It feels like an unseen demon is messing with you, and time seems to stand still.
---
例句:
- 中文：自从昨天晚上开始，项目进展就进入了恶魔时间。
- English: Ever since last night, the project progress has been in demon time.
