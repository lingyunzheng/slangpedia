## Demon Time
**English:**  
Demon Time (恶魔时间) is a slang term referring to a period when one feels extra energized, almost supernatural, and seems to perform better than usual. It often describes a moment when stress, adrenaline, or other factors lead to an exceptionally productive or thrilling state.
**Chinese:**  
“恶魔时间”是一个网络流行语，用来形容一个人在某个时间段感觉精力特别旺盛，仿佛超出了常人的范围，通常会表现出比平时更高效或更充满激情的状态。这样的时间段可能会受到压力、肾上腺素等多方面因素的影响而产生。
