## English Explanation
**Demon Time**: Also referred to as "Devil Mode" in some contexts, this term is used when someone is extremely focused and determined to accomplish a task. It's like the character from the anime *Black Clover* going into "Demon Soul" mode, where they vastly improve their performance and abilities. It's often associated with pushing through difficulties or working extra hard to meet a deadline.
## Chinese Explanation
**恶魔时间（Demon Time）**: 这个词通常用来形容一个人完全沉浸在一个任务或目标中，表现出极高的专注力和坚定的决心，就像动漫《黒の魔导士》中主角进入“恶魔灵魂”模式那样，能力大幅度提升。这个词往往用于描述人们在遇到困难时坚持到最后，或者为了完成任务而加倍努力的情况。
