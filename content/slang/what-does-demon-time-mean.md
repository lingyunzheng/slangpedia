```markdown
## Demon Time (妖精时间 / 恶魔时间)
### English Explanation
"DemTime" or "demon time" is a term often used to describe situations where time seems to move much faster or slower than usual, depending on the context. It's the feeling that time has flown by so quickly you feel like you haven't done anything, or that time has stood still and you're repeatedly doing the same actions over and over. Commonly used in gaming communities and casual conversations.
### Chinese Explanation
"妖精时间"或"恶魔时间"是用来形容时间感知变得非常快或非常慢的一种说法，具体是时间感觉过得飞快，以至于觉得自己什么都没干，或者时间仿佛停滞不前并一直在重复某个动作。这个短语常见于游戏社区和日常对话中，用来形容那些陷入了某种状态或循环的场景。
```
### Additional Notes:
- In certain contexts, "妖精时间" (yao jing time) might be used more widely, but "恶魔时间" (m琰 time) is more commonly used for more intense feelings of time distortion.
- This term might also be used humorously or sarcastically to emphasize a situation that feels particularly frustrating or mundane.
