```markdown
# Demon Time (恶魔时间 - Mòdiàn Shíjiān)
**Chinese:** Mòdiàn Shíjiān  
**English:** Demon Time
## 中文解释:
"恶魔时间"是指一种让人无法集中注意力、时间过得飞快的状态，经常会让人感觉时间过得很快，但却什么都没做。这种状态常常发生在玩手机游戏、刷社交媒体或者陷入无意义的在线冲浪时。这种时间仿佛被一种无形的恶魔所掌控，使人迷失在虚拟世界中。
## 英文解释:
"Demon Time" refers to a state where one loses focus and time flies by without accomplishing much. It often describes the feeling of time passing quickly yet achieving nothing, especially when engaging in activities like endless scrolling through social media or gaming. It feels as though a无形恶魔 has controlled the passage of time, luring you into a virtual world.
```
Note: "无形恶魔" was translated as "invisible demon" to maintain the implication, but in the actual phrase, "无形" isn't visible in English rendering. The concept of an "invisible demon" is more elaborate in Chinese, and it should be noted that in the original context, it's more abstract and less literal than the English translation suggests.
