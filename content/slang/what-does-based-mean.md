# Based
**English:**  
*An informal adjective or adverb used to express approval, support, or credibility, or to describe something as being on point or effective.*  
Example: "This new fitness routine is totally based!"
**中文:**  
*一个非正式的形容词或副词，用于表示赞同、支持或可信度，或描述某事物为精准有效或到位。*  
例句：“这个新的健身计划真给力！”
Note: "Based" often carries a colloquial tone and is used in casual or informal settings.
