## based (英式英语 / 美式英语)
### English Explanation:
#### *British English:*
1. **Literal Meaning:** "基于的" or "根据的"
2. **Slang Usage:** Primarily used to describe something or someone who is cool, on point, or as good as it gets. It can be used as a compliment to express approval or admiration for something.
#### *American English:*
1. **Literal Meaning:** "基数的" or "基于的" (though this is not typical slang usage)
2. **Slang Usage:** Often used to indicate that something is impressive, stapled (copied or duplicated), or that it is something to aspire to. It's a way of saying "yes" to indicating that something is on point.
### 中文解释：
1. **字面意义:** "基于的" 或 "根据的"
2. **俚语使用:** 主要用来形容某人或某事物很酷，很帅，或者是最棒的，是一种表示赞赏或钦佩的赞美语。
### Example Sentences:
- *British English:* "The new album is totally based, I love it!"
- *American English:* "That street dance is based, you should see it live!"
