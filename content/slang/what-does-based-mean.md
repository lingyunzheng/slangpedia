# Based
**English:**  
Based is a term often used to describe someone or something that is confident, cool, and has a good vibe or attitude. It's used to give a positive or approving acknowledgment.
**Example Usage:**
- He totally killed the party, super based!
- That move was based, I had to give it up.
**Literal Meaning:**
- Nothing; used essentially as slang to express approval or to affirm something is on point.
---
**中文:**  
基于这个词常用来形容某人或某事物很酷、自信并且具有良好的气场或态度。它用来给予肯定或批准的认可。
**使用示例:**
- 他完全搞定了派对，真基于！
- 他那招真的很基于，我得给他个赞。
**字面意思:**
- 没有；这个短语主要用于表达认可或肯定某事很棒。
