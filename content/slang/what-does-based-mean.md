# "Based" Slang Explanation
## English
### **Based:**
- **Definition:** Originally used to describe someone or something that is strong, confident, or cool. It has been adopted in various contexts, often to express approval or admiration.
- **Example:** "That new hip-hop song is totally based!"
## Chinese
### **基于:**
- **释义:** 原意形容某人或某物很强大、自信或酷。它在各种语境中被采用，通常用来表达赞赏或认同。
- **例句:** “那个新的嘻哈歌曲真是太基于了！”
