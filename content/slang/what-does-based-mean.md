## Based
### English Explanation
"Based" is a slang term that originated in online and social media communities. It is used to describe someone or something that is real, authentic, or going through the motions in a calculated way. It can be used positively when someone is showing real or genuine effort, or it can be used negatively when someone is seen as exaggerated or overly dramatic.
- **Positive Usage**: "That athlete is based! They've been training hard and are really improving."
- **Negative Usage**: "He's based in his claims that he's a top expert without any proof."
### 中文解释
"Based" 是一个起源于在线和社交媒体社区的俚语。它用来描述某人或某事是真实的、真诚的，或者是以一种经过计算的方式展现。它可以用来正面地形容某人展示出真实的或真诚的努力，也可以用来负面地形容某人显得过度夸张或情绪化。
- **正面使用**："那个运动员真基于！他们一直在刻苦训练，并且有了真正的进步。"
- **负面使用**："他夸大其词说自己是顶级专家却没有任何证据，真叫人觉得他很基于。"
