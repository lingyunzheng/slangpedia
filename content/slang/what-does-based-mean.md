# Based 
## English
**Based**: Originating from hip-hop and urban culture, "based" is typically used to describe someone or something that is extremely confident, selfishly entitled, or cutting-edge. It can also mean that someone is behaving in a manner that is unusually "on point" or authentic.
Example: "Man, his outfit was so based today!"
## 中文
**基于**：起源于嘻哈和都市文化，“based”通常用来形容某人或某事物极其自信、自私地认为自己理所应当，或者非常前卫。也指某人的行为非常“到位”或真实。
例句：“他的穿着今天真是太基于了！”
