# Based
**English:**  
Based is an informal adjective used to describe someone or something that is thoroughly cool, impressive, or on-point. It's often used to express approval or admiration. For example, "That dance move was totally based."
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
Based 是一个非正式的形容词，用来形容某人或某事非常酷、令人印象深刻或到位。常用来表达赞赏或钦佩。例如，“那个舞蹈动作真是太based了。”
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** That new album is totally based!
