# Based
## English
**Based**: This slang term, derived from "basic," is used to describe something or someone who is cool, trendy, or just good at what they do. It implies that a person or thing stands out from the ordinary and is impressive or authentic.
*Example*: "Wearing a face mask and whole-body hoodie, he's based on the dance floor."
## 中文
**Based**: 这个俚语来源于“basic”，用来描述某人或某物很酷、时髦或是做事情很出色。它意味着一个人或事物比普通事物更突出，给人留下深刻印象或显得很真实。
*例句*: "戴着口罩和全套连帽衫，他在舞池上简直based得不得了。"
