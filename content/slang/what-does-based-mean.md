# Based
## English
"Based" is a slang term that originated in hip-hop culture, and is often used to describe something or someone as being genuine, authentic, or having a strong belief in their own abilities or achievements. It can also be used to express admiration for another person's confidence and success.
*Example:* "Joan aced the final exam and she's totally based this semester."
## Chinese
"基于"（based）这个词来源于hip-hop文化，通常用来描述某事或某人是真实的、诚实的，或者是有着强烈自我信念的能力或成就。它也可以用来表达对另一个人的自信和成功的钦佩。
*例句:* “琼这次期末考试拿了高分，这个学期她真是牛逼（注释：这里的“牛逼”是对“based”的中文表达）。”
