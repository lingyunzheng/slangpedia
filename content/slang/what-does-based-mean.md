# Based
## English
**Based:** A term originating in urban youth culture, used to describe something or someone that is impressive, on-point, or genuinely cool. Often used to affirm an action, attitude, or performance as being top-notch or well-deserved.
Example: "He absolutely shredded the guitar solo; that was based!"
## 汉语
**Based:** 这个词源自城市青年文化，用来形容某件事物或某个人非常出色、非常到位或真正酷炫。通常用于肯定某个动作、态度或表现是顶级的或理所应当的。
例句：他彻底碾压了吉他独奏，那真是太有料了！
