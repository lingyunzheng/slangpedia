# Slang Explanations: "based"
**English:**
- **Definition:** When something or someone is deemed to be "on point," "lit," or "phenomenal."
- **Example Usage:** "That rap battle was totally based!" 
**Chinese:**
- **释义:** 当某人或某事被形容为“手段高明”、“酷炫”或“出色”的时候。
- **例句:** "那场说唱对决真是基于！"
