### Based
- **English**: A slang term that has gained popularity in recent years, mostly associated with someone or something being cool, skilled, or on-point. It can also be used to express agreement or excitement, similar to "on point" or "yes."
Example: "This new rapper is based—his rhymes are top-notch!"
- **中文**: “基于”一词在近年来流行起来，通常用来形容某人或某事物很酷、很厉害或很到位。也可以用来表示认同或兴奋，类似于“on point”或“yes”。
例子：“这位新说唱歌手很based——他的韵脚劲爆得很！”
