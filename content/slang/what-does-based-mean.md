# Based
## English
In current internet slang, **based** is used to describe someone or something that is serious, committed, or doing something in a way that shows dedication and authenticity. It can also imply that a person is acting "on point" or "slam."
### Examples
- "He's really based with his study habits."
- "That's a based move in the game."
