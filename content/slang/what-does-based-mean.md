# Based
**English:**  
Based is a slang term used to describe something or someone who is cool, confident, or impressive. It generally means that something is trustworthy or reliable, and aligns with what is considered "tight" or "tight-fisted" (meaning generous) in a more positive way.
Example: "His entrepreneurship skills are totally based!"
**Chinese:**  
Based是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某事很酷、自信或令人印象深刻。通常意味着某物是可靠和可信的，并且与“tight”或“tight-fisted”（大方）在更积极的意义上相一致。
例句：“他的创业技能简直是太based了！”
