# Based
**English:**  
A slang term often used in the context of authenticity, coolness, or dedication to a certain behavior or belief. It can imply that someone is on point, committed, or genuinely into something.
**Chinese:**  
俚语表达，通常用于描述某人的真实、酷或对某事的投入。它可以表示某人很靠谱、很投入或真正对某事感兴趣。
- 释义：真实（形容某人真诚或可靠）；酷（形容某人或某事很潮、很酷）；投入（形容某人非常专注或努力）。
**Examples:**
- **English:** He's really based on his workout routine.
- **Chinese:** 他在锻炼计划上真的很有成就感。
- **English:** That party was totally based!
- **Chinese:** 那个派对真是太酷了！
