```markdown
## Based
**English:**  
Derives from "basically," but often used to denote something is very cool, relevant, or on point. It can be used to describe a trend, a behavior, or an attitude. When someone says something is "based," they mean it's fantastic or impressive in a certain context.
**Example:**
- My new phone is so based with all its cool features!
**Pinyin & Translation:**  
- 基本上 (jī běn shàng)  
- 指某事物非常酷、相关或很到位。可用于描述趋势、行为或态度。
- 例句：  
- 我的新手机功能超全，超级 based！
```

```markdown
**Example:**
- Her approach to solving problems is really based and effective.
**Pinyin & Translation:**  
- 基础的 (jī chǔ de)  
- 用来形容人或事物是有现实根基的，实用或直截了当的。这种用法可以是正面也可以是负面的，意味着某事物不复杂或不具幻想性。
- 例句：  
- 她解决问题的方法很实际且有效，真的很 based。
```
