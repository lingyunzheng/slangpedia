# Based
## English
"Based" is a slang term that originated in the hip-hop and internet culture, often used to describe someone or something as being cool, on-point, or authentic. It's a way of saying that someone or something is doing well, aligning with their own values, or generally behaving in a way that is considered admirable or impressive. For example, "He's really based on the dance floor" would imply that someone is impressively skilled at dancing.
## Chinese
"基于"（based）这个词源于嘻哈文化和互联网语言，用于形容某人或某事很cool、很到位或很真实。它用来表示某人或某事做得很好，符合自己的价值观，或者总体上以一种被认为令人钦佩或印象深刻的方式行事。例如，“他在舞池里真的based”意味着某人的舞蹈技巧非常出色。
