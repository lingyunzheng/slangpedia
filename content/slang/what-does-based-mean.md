## Based (Based)
基于（Based）
### English Meaning:
- Something that is based is perceived as being genuine, authentic, or cool. It often refers to behaviors or attitudes that are considered to be true or real. Formerly, it was often used to denote behavior or actions that were seen as excessive, outlandish, or off-the-charts. Now, it has largely shifted to a more positive connotation.
### Usage Example:
- "Man, his fashion sense is totally based!"
- "She's based in every way, and that's what makes her so lovable."
### 汉语解释：
- 基于这个词现在用来形容某人或某事是真实可信的，充满了正能量。在过去，这个词有时用来形容行为或态度过于夸张或离谱，但现在更多用于表示某人或某事非常真实或有趣。
### 使用例句：
- "他的时尚感真是基于啊！"
- "她各个方面都非常基于，这一点使她格外可爱。"
