### Based
#### English
**Meaning:** 
- *Describing something, or someone, as cool, impressive, or on-point.* 
**Example:**
- "Man, that freestyle rapping was really based!"
#### 中文
**意思：**
- *用来形容某样东西或某个人很酷、很有气势或者很到位。*
**例句：**
- “哇塞，那freestyle真的超级based！”
