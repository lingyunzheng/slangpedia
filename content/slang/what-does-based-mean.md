# Based
## English
**Based**: Originating from the phrase "on base," it means something or someone is solid, cool, or just hanging tight. It's a way to describe things that are good, righteous, or straightforward in a casual or informal context. For example, if someone is being totally committed to a goal or doing something that feels real and legitimate, you might say they are "based."
*Example:* "She's totally based for putting in hours of practice every day!"
## 中文
**基于**: “基于”一词源于“on base”，它的含义是指某事或某人是稳固的、酷的或者只是稳稳地进行着。这个词用于描述一些被认为是好的、正直的或直接的行为，在一种轻松或非正式的语境中使用。比如，当某人非常坚定地致力于某个目标，或是他们所做的都显得真实且正当的时候，“基于”可以用来形容他们。
*例句:* “她每天都投入大量的练习时间，真是个基于的人！”
