## Based: 基数
**English:** Based
*In English slang, "based" is used to describe something or someone who is intense, impressive, or inspiring. It can also be used to describe a situation that is ridiculous or not in the right way.*
*Example:*
- *English:* That party was based!
- *Bilingual:* 那场派对真是太有料了! (Nà chǎng pàitè(EVENT WAS OVER) tài yǒu liào le!)
*English:* He did so well in the competition, it was so based!
