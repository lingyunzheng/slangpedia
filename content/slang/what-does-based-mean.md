## Based (Based)
### English
**Definition:** This term is commonly used to indicate that someone or something is "on point" or really good at something.
**Example:** "Man, that rapper is really based, every single word hits the mark!"
**Usage:** Primarily used in informal contexts, especially among younger generations or in social media conversations.
### 中文
**释义:** 这个词常用来表示某人或某事是“很牛”或真的很擅长某事。
**例句:** "这家伙的说唱真的基于，每一句话都说到点子上了！"
**使用场合:** 主要用于非正式的情境中，特别是在年轻人群中或在社交媒体上的对话中。
