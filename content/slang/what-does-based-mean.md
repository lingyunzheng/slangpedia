## Based
**English:** 
"Based" is a slang term that originated in the hip-hop and internet culture. It is used to describe someone or something that is cool, talented, or readily accepted. Essentially, it's a compliment that indicates someone or something is in the know and doing things in a way that aligns with style and substance. For example, if someone is consistently performing stunts that others find impressively daring and cool, they might be called "based."
**Chinese:**
“Based” 是源自嘻哈文化和互联网的一种俚语。它用来形容某人或某事很酷、有才华或者被广泛接受。简而言之，这是一种赞誉，表明某人或某事做事有风格且有实力。例如，如果某人经常做出令人印象深刻的、令人感到大胆又酷炫的行为时，可能会被称为“based”。
### 示例用法：
- English: "Man, that guy is totally based with all the tricks he's doing in the skatepark."
- Chinese: "那个滑板小子在滑板公园里做出了这么多令人惊叹的技巧，完全称得上是‘based’。"
