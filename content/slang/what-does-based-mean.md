# Based
## English
"Based" is an internet slang term that originated in the early to mid-2010s. It is used to describe someone or something that is cool, on-point, or simply doing something impressive or worthy of admiration. Essentially, if something is "based," it means it's good or awesome in an authentic, genuine way.
example: That new dance move he did was so based!
## Chinese
"基于"（Based）这个网络流行语起源于2010年代中前期。它用来形容某人或某事物很酷、正点，或者正在做一些令人印象深刻或值得钦佩的事情。换句话说，如果某件事被认为是"基于"（based），那就意味着它是好或很棒，是一种真实而纯净的好。
例如：他新学的那个舞蹈动作好基于！
