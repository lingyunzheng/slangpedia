## Based
### English
"Based" is a slang term often used to describe someone or something that is cool, on-point, or valid. It can also be used to express approval or when something feels right and makes sense. 
Example: "Man, that latest release by this artist is totally based!"
### Chinese (简体)
“Based”是一个俚语，常用来形容某人或某事很酷、很在点上或很合理。也可以用来表示赞同，或者当某件事感觉正确且合乎逻辑时。
例句：这个艺术家的最新发行简直是基于现实！（Man, that latest release by this artist is totally based!）
