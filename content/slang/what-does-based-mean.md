# Based
## English
**Based**: Originating from hip-hop culture, "based" is used to describe someone or something that is extremely effective, confident, or cool. It can also imply that something is so good that it is impossible to argue with its quality.
*Example*: "Her dancing skills are totally based!"
## 中文
**基于** (Based): 来自嘻哈文化的词汇，“based”用来形容某人或某事非常有效、自信或者酷。这个词也可以表示某事如此优秀，以至于其质量无法被质疑。
*例句*: "她的舞蹈技巧真是基于的！"
