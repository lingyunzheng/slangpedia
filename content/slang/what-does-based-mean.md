## Based
**English**: Based is a term used to describe something or someone as cool, great, or having a lot of style. It often implies that something is easy to believe or expected, given the scenario.
**Chinese**: "Based" 用来形容某人或某事很酷、很棒或者很有风格，常常表示某事在特定情况下显得非常合情合理，不稀奇。
Example Usage:
- English: "He aced the exam, so he's totally based."
- Chinese: "他考试考得很好，所以完全没问题。"
