## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Meaning:** 
- Extremely muscular, often to the point of appearing overly so.
**Usage Example:** 
- "After months of intense workouts, he's really swole now!"
### Chinese
**意思:** 
- 极度发达的肌肉， often 指肌肉看起来过于夸张。
**使用示例:** 
- "经过几个月的高强度锻炼，他现在肌肉真得很夸张！"
- "自从开始疯狂 gym 后，她的肌肉特别 swole。"
