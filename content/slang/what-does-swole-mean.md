# Slang Term: Swole
## English
**Meaning:** 
- **Muscular or heavily toned:** Refers to a muscular physique, especially in the arms, legs, or chest. Often used to describe an exaggeratedly toned appearance that some people might associate with bodybuilding.
**Example:** 
- "The gym coach was so swole, it was impressive!"
## Chinese
**含义:** 
- **肌肉丰满或高度紧实**: 通常用于形容肌肉发达、特别在手臂、腿部或胸部的强壮体形。有时人们会将这种极度健美的外观与健美操或者健身联系起来。
**例句:** 
- "健身房教练肌肉丰满得令人印象深刻！"
