## Swole (英) / 咧 Herald (中)
- **Swole (英)**: A slang term commonly used to describe someone who has developed significant muscle mass, often referring to lifting weights and achieving a body that is predominantly muscular. It can also be used to describe other forms of swelling or enlargement.
- **妾 Herald (中)**: 一个流行俚语，用来形容肌肉非常发达、体型壮硕的人，通常是指通过举重和其他锻炼增加肌肉量。这个词也可用于描述其他形式的肿胀或增大。
