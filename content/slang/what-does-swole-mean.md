## Swole (English)
### Meaning
"Swole" is a slang term used to describe someone who has developed significant muscle mass, often to the point of looking very bulky and well-defined, especially in the context of bodybuilding or intense weight training. It conveys a sense of extreme muscularity and size.
### Example Usage
- "After months of heavy lifting, John has really gotten swole!"
## Swole (Chinese)
### 含义
“Swole”是一个俚语，用来形容肌肉极度发达、体型非常健壮和明显的状态，尤其是在健美或者高强度力量训练的背景下。它传达了一种极端的肌肉质量和体型的感觉。
### 使用示例
- “经过几个月的大力训练，约翰真的变得非常壮实了！”
