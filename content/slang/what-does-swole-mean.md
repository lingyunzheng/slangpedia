## Slang Term: Swole
### English Explanation
**Swole** is a slang term typically used to describe a muscular and extremely toned physique, often emphasizing the exaggerated and pumped-up appearance of muscles. It's often associated with bodybuilding or intense gym workouts, and people might use it to describe their own or others' muscle development.
### Chinese Explanation
**Swole** 是一个俚语，用来形容肌肉发达且极度紧实的体型，经常强调肌肉的膨胀和紧致外观。这个词通常与健美或者高强度的健身锻炼相关，人们可以用它来描述自己的或者别人的肌肉发展。
