## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Definition:** "Swole" is a slang term used to describe a highly muscular and beefy physical appearance, often achieved through weightlifting or bodybuilding. It implies that a person has developed a significant amount of muscle mass and definition.
**Example:** "I started hitting the gym hard, and now I feel really swole!"
### Chinese
**释义："Swole" 是一个俚语词，用来描述肌肉非常发达、体型健硕的模样，通常通过举重或健身达到这种效果。它表示一个人在肌肉量和线条上有了显著的增长。**
**例句：** "自从我开始拼命去 gym 锻炼后，感觉自己真是太 swole 了！"
