## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Meaning:** The term "swole" describes a highly muscular and bulky appearance, often associated with intense weightlifting or bodybuilding. It's used to express admiration for someone's developed physique or to describe how a particular body has grown in muscle mass.
### Chinese
**Meaning:** "Swole" 描述的是肌肉非常发达和健壮的身体外观，通常与剧烈的力量训练或健美训练相关。这个词可以用来表达对某人发达的身形的钦佩，或者描述某个身体在肌肉量上有显著增加。
