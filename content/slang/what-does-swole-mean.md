## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Definition**: "Swole" is a slang term commonly used to describe a body that has become noticeably bulky and muscular, often from weightlifting or weight training. It emphasizes the exaggerated and impressive look of muscle mass.
**Usage**: "After months of consistent workouts, Jake finally hit his muscle growth goals and really swoled up."
### 中文
**定义**："Swole" 是一个俚语，用来描述因举重或力量训练而导致肌肉明显增大、看起来非常强壮的身体。它强调了肌肉量的显著增加所带来的视觉效果。
**用法**："经过几个月的规律锻炼，杰克终于实现了肌肉增长的目标，肌肉真的增大了不少。"
