# Slang Explanation: Swole
## English
**Swole:** A slang term typically used to describe a person who has significantly increased muscle mass, often to the point where their muscles appear extremely large and well-defined, giving an exaggerated look of strength and bulk.
**Example Usage:** "After months of intense lifting, he's truly swole now!"
## Chinese
**Swole:** 一个俚语，通常用来形容一个肌肉显著增长的人，肌肉非常大且清晰可见，给人一种强烈的力量感和体积感。
**用例：** "经过几个月的高强度锻炼，他现在真的好‘壮硕’！"
