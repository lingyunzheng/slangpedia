## Swole (英语: Swole)
### English Explanation
"Swole" is a term commonly used in fitness and bodybuilding communities to describe a certain type of muscular appearance. It refers to being exceptionally well-developed, often to the point of being visibly larger and more defined than average. The term is often used in a positive manner to describe someone who has achieved impressive muscle mass and definition through consistent training and a balanced nutrition plan.
### Chinese Explanation
"swole" 是健身和举重圈子常用的一个术语，用来描述一种特殊的肌肉形态。它指的是肌肉异常发达，通常比一般人的肌肉更大、更分明。这个词语通常用于称赞那些通过持续训练和均衡饮食达到令人印象深刻的肌肉质量和定义的人。
### Usage Examples
- English: "He's really swole after that intense workout last week."
- Chinese: "经过上周那场剧烈的训练，他肌肉真的很发达。"
- English: "That guy is a real swole, I swear his arms are the size of watermelons!"
- Chinese: "那个家伙真厉害，他的手臂粗得像西瓜一样！"
