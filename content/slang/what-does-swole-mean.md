## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Swole**: A term that describes the state of having extremely firm and well-defined muscles, often seen as a result of intense weight training or bodybuilding. It can also be used sarcastically to describe muscle gain that is viewed as excessive or unattractive.
### Chinese
**Swole**: (形容词) 描述一种肌肉异常结实、线条分明的状态，通常与高强度的力量训练或健身有关。这个词语也可以用来带贬义地形容肌肉增长过多或不吸引人。
