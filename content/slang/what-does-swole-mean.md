# Slang Term: Swole
## English
**Meaning:** To describe a person or thing that has grown or become more developed, specifically in a way that suggests strength or muscle mass, often to an exaggerated or desirable level. It's often used in the context of bodybuilding or fitness to describe a noticeable increase in muscle size.
**Example:** "After months of intense training, John is really swole now."
## Chinese
**意思:** 用来描述一个人或事物变得更为发达或增长，特别是在形容肌肉量增长时，带有程度上至一定的夸张或魅力感。通常用于健身或健身领域，形容肌肉显著增长的状态。
**例句:** "经过几个月的高强度训练，约翰的肌肉真的很发达了。"
