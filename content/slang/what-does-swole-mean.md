## Slang Word: Swole
### English Explanation:
"Swole" is a slang term often used to describe someone or something that has significantly increased in size or bulk, typically in a way that gives a muscular or swollen appearance. This term is particularly popular among bodybuilders or those involved in strength training who are trying to build muscle mass. It can also be used more broadly to describe something that has grown or expanded in an impressive or exaggerated way, not just in size but also in intensity or complexity.
### Chinese Explanation:
"Swole" 是一种俚语，常用于描述某人或某物在大小或体积上显著增加，通常给出肌肉或肿胀的外观。这个词特别受健美运动员或参与力量训练的人欢迎，他们试图增加肌肉质量。此外，“swole”也可以泛指事物在大小、强度或复杂性方面以令人印象深刻或夸张的方式增长。
