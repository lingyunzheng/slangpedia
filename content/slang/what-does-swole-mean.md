# Slang Term: Swole
## English
**Swole** refers to an extremely muscular and bulky state, often associated with bodybuilders or serious fitness enthusiasts aiming to achieve significant muscle mass. It's a fun way to describe looking highly muscular or well-toned.
## Chinese
**swole**（练得很壮）用来形容肌肉非常发达，块头很大的状态，通常与健美运动员或致力于增肌的健身爱好者有关。用这个词可以轻松地形容一个人肌肉非常健硕或体态匀称。
