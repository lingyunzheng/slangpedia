## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Meaning**: Extremely muscular and bulky, often exaggerated or to the point of looking very excessive or impressive. This term is commonly used in fitness and gym contexts.
**Example**: "After months of intense weight training, he's really swole now!"
### Chinese
**意义**: 极度肌肉发达，体积庞大，有时夸张到看起来非常惊人或印象深刻。这个词常用于健身和健身房的语境中。
**例句**: "经过几个月的高强度力量训练，他现在肌肉真的好发达！"
