## Slang Term: Swole
### English Explanation
**Swole** is a term that describes a highly defined and muscular physique, often used in fitness contexts. It emphasizes the appearance of having well-developed muscles, suggesting a look of muscularity that is noticeable and impressive.
### Chinese Explanation
**swole**（肿胀的）形容肌肉非常发达，通常用于健身场景中。这个词强调肌肉发展的显著效果，让人看起来肌肉饱满、引人注目。
