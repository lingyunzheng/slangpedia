## Swole (English / 吃 Gain (Chinese) )
**English:**  
"Swole" is a slang term used to describe someone or something that has become extremely muscular or strong, often as a result of intense weight lifting or fitness training. It can also be used as a boastful or self-deprecating way to refer to one's physique or strength.
**Chinese:**  
“吃 Gain”是一种俚语，用来形容一个人或某物变得极为肌肉发达或强壮，通常是由于高强度的举重或健身训练的结果。它也可以作为一种自夸或自嘲的方式来指代自己的体格或力量。
Example Usage:
- English: "Look at that guy, he's so swole!"
- Chinese: 看那 guy, 他吃得真猛!
- English: "I just got swole from all the weights I've been lifting."
- Chinese: 我从recently doing lots of举重activity变得吃起来了。
Note: The "吃 Gain" translation is not a direct one-for-one word conversion but conveys the same meaning and usage context in a culturally representative way.
