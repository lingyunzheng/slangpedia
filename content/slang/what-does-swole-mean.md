# Swole (英 / 翻译)
## English
**Swole** is a slang term used to describe a person who has built a significant amount of muscle mass, often with visible definition. This term is commonly used in fitness and bodybuilding communities to describe a physique that looks "ripped" or exceptionally muscular. 
### Example Sentence
"I've been hitting the gym hard and I think I'm starting to swole."
## 翻译
**Swole** 是一个俚语词汇，用来形容肌肉量显著增加且肌肉线条清晰的人。这个词汇通常在健身和健美社区中使用，用来描述一种“精壮”或非常发达的身材。
### 例句
“我最近在健身房刻苦锻炼，我觉得自己开始拥有强劲的肌肉了。”
