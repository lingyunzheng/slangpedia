## Slang Term: Swole

### English
- **Definition**: Typically used in the context of weightlifting and bodybuilding, "swole" describes a state of having extremely developed muscles, giving an appearance of being swelled or puffed up. It's often used to describe someone who has achieved a significant increase in muscle mass and strength.
- **Example**: "Man, look at John in the gym, he's totally swole now! He's been lifting weights non-stop."

### Chinese
- **释义**：通常用于重量训练和健美运动的语境中，“swole”指的是肌肉极度发达的状态，给人以膨胀或鼓起的形象。通常用来形容肌肉量和力量有显著增加的人。
- **例句**：“哇，再看一眼健身房里的约翰，他现在好肌肉啊！他最近一直在拼命健身。”
