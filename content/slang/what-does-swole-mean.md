## Swole
**English:** *Swole* is a slang term used to describe muscles that have grown to be very large and well-defined, usually associated with weightlifting or bodybuilding.
**Chinese:** *Swole* 这个俚语用来描述肌肉变得非常发达和明显，通常与举重或健美相关。健身爱好者常用这个词来形容自己训练后的肌肉状态。
例子：
- English: Showing off his swole biceps, John couldn't stop flexing in front of the mirror.
- Chinese: 约翰展示着他发达的肱二头肌，对着镜子不停地展示肌肉。
- English: After months of intense training, her body has really swoled up.
- Chinese: 经过几个月的高强度训练，她的身体真的变得肌肉发达起来了。
