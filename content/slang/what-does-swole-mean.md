# Swole 🌟
## English
**Swole** is a slang term used to describe someone who has become impressively muscular, often from weightlifting or bodybuilding. It can also imply that someone has a noticeable build, making them appear strong and fit.
## Chinese
**swole** 是一个俚语，用来形容肌肉发达、健壯的人，通常是因为举重或健身体练而变得如此。这个词也可以用来描述某人明显健壮的体型，让人看起来非常强壮和健康。
