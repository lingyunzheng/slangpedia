## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Definition:** The state of being very well-built or muscular. Often used in a positive context to describe someone who has impressive muscle mass and strength, particularly after weightlifting or other forms of strength training.
**Example Usage:** "After months of intense training, he's really swole!"
### 中文
**释义:** 一种非常健壮或肌肉发达的状态。通常在正面的语境中使用，用来形容经过重量训练或其他形式的力量训练后，肌肉体积庞大且强壮的人。
**使用示例:** "经过几个月的高强度训练，他真的肌肉发达了！"
