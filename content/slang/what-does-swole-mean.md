## Swole (English)
### Definition:
The term "swole" is slang, primarily used in fitness and bodybuilding contexts. It describes the appearance of muscles that are significantly enlarged, often with a lot of visible size and definition. 
### Bilingual Explanation:
**English:** A term used to describe muscles that are abnormally big and impressive-looking.
**Chinese:** 主要用于健身和健美语境中的俚语，用来形容肌肉异常发达和显眼的外观。
