## Slang Term: Swole
### English
**Definition:** The term "swole" refers to a highly defined and muscular physique, often achieved through intense strength training and enjoying a diet high in protein. 
**Usage Example:** "After months of intensive weightlifting, he became swole."
### Chinese
**释义：** "Swole" 是指经过高强度力量训练后肌肉发达、体型清晰的体型，通常伴随着高蛋白的饮食。 
**用法示例：** "经过几个月的重量训练后，他的肌肉变得非常发达。"
