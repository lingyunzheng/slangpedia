## Swole
### English
**Swole**: A slang term used to describe a person's muscles that have become very large and defined, often used when referring to someone who has been working out intensively and successfully building muscle mass.
### 中文
**Swole**: 一个用来形容肌肉变得非常大且明显的俚语，通常指那些通过高强度锻炼成功增肌的人。
