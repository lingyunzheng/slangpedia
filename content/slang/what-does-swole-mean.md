## Slang Term: Swole
### English:
**Swole** is a slang term derived from "swollen" and is most commonly used to describe a muscular physique, often to an exaggerated or extreme extent. This term is often used in the context of bodybuilding or健身界的口头禅，用来描述肌肉发达，通常指肌肉非常紧实、发达的身材。这种表达常用于健身爱好者、增肌人群之间的交流中。
### Example Usage:
- In English: "After weeks of intense weightlifting, he's really swole now."
- 在中文：经过几周的高强度力量训练，他现在肌肉真是非常扎实了。
### Related Terms:
- **Beefy:** Another term for someone who is very muscular and strong. 形容某人非常强壮和肌肉发达。
- **Tonally Defined:** When muscles are clearly defined and well-developed. 肌肉线条明显，肌肉发达。
