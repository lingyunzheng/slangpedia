# Grindset
**English Definition:**
A grindset refers to the lifestyle and mindset focused on consistent, diligent effort towards a goal or series of goals, often associated with hard work, persistence, and dedication. It describes someone who is driven and willing to put in long hours and significant effort to achieve success.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
Grinder (磨砺者) 的生活方式和心态，专注于目标的持续努力，通常与辛勤工作、坚持不懈和强烈意愿联系在一起。它描述的是那些愿意投入大量时间与努力以达到成功的人。
