# Grindset (英语: Grindset)
## English Explanation
**Grindset** is a term often used in the context of work ethic and lifestyle. It generally describes a mindset or attitude where an individual is driven to consistently put in hard work and effort, often with a goal-oriented focus. This term is associated with people who are committed to their tasks and are willing to maintain a high level of productivity, often at the expense of personal leisure time.
### Examples
- "Sara has a real grindset; she's always here, working long hours."
- "John's grindset keeps him ahead in the competition, even when things get tough."
## 中文解释
**Grindset** 通常用来描述一种工作态度或生活方式，指的是一个人具有持续投入辛勤工作和努力的精神，并且目标导向性强。这个词常用来形容那些对工作充满热情、致力于投入大量时间和精力的人，即使有时会牺牲个人休闲时间也无妨。
### 示例
- "莎拉确实有坚毅的磨砺精神，她总是很早就来加班到很晚。"
- "约翰凭借自己的磨砺精神，在竞争中始终处于领先地位，即使遇到困难也没有后退。"
