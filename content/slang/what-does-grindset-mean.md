# Grindset (Lingo Breakdown)
## English
**Grindset**: A term often used in context of both work and lifestyle, describing someone's approach to their work or a specific activity with dedication and perseverance. It conveys the mindset or attitude of working diligently and consistently towards a goal.
**Example**: "John has a real grindset when it comes to his programming job. He works late hours and always strives for perfection in his projects."
## 中文
**Grindset**: 这个词通常用于描述一个人对待工作或特定活动的投入和坚持的态度。它传达的是朝着目标努力工作的勤奋和持续性。
**示例**：“约翰对待编程工作有着真正的磨练心态。他经常加班，并且总是力求在项目上做到完美。”
