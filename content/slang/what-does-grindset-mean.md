## Grindset
**English:**  
A grindset refers to the mindset or attitude of someone who is committed to a high level of productivity and dedication, particularly in areas like work, fitness, or personal projects. People with a strong grindset are always in pursuit of improvement and are willing to put in the necessary effort to achieve their goals.
**中文:**  
grindset 指的是那些对自己所从事的工作、健身或个人项目保持高度投入和生产力的心态或态度的人。拥有强大 grindset 的人总是致力于提升自己，并愿意付出必要的努力去达成目标。
