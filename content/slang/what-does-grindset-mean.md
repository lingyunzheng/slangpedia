# Grindset
## English
**Grindset:** A mindset or lifestyle focused on persistent effort and dedication towards a particular goal, often in a work or career context. It refers to the relentless pursuit of success through continuous hard work and discipline. People who identify with this term often enjoy the satisfaction and fulfillment that comes from consistent effort.
## Chinese
**Grindset:** 一种专注于持续努力和奉献特定目标的心态或生活方式，尤其是在职业生涯或工作中。它指的是通过持续的努力和纪律追求成功的执着追求。认同这一术语的人们通常享受持续努力所带来的满足感和成就感。
