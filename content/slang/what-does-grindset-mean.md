## Grindset (noun)
### English
- **Definition**: A mindset that prioritizes hard work and dedication to a particular goal or lifestyle.
- **Usage**: "She's got a real grindset going on; she works every day after class to stay on top of her projects."
- **Slang Explanation**: Think of it as having "grittiness" or being "dedicated" to getting the job done, often regardless of the time or effort required.
### Chinese
- **定义**: 一种将努力和专注放在特定目标或生活方式上的思维模式。
- **用法**: “她真的很有冲劲；每天下了课还在为项目努力。”
- **俚语解释**: 想象一下，这就像拥有“韧性”或“专注”以完成任务，通常无论付出多大努力和时间都无妨。
