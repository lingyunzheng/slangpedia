# Grindset (English-Chinese)
## English
A grindset is a lifestyle and mindset centered around working hard, putting in consistent effort, and going after one's goals with persistence and dedication. It often involves sacrifice, especially in terms of leisure time and personal pleasures, to achieve a desired outcome or climb the social or professional ladder.
### Example Usage:
- "He’s got a real grindset, always at the gym and studying until late at night."
- "Earning a master's degree is a grindset that requires a lot of hard work and patience."
## 粤语
“磨內心態”，是指一種努力工作、付出持續努力、並堅持不懈地為達成目標而不懈努力的生活方式和心態。這通常包括犧牲，尤其是在閒暇時間和個人樂趣方面的犧牲，以實現預期的結果或在社會或職業道路上升迁。
### 使用例句：
- “他磨內心態真強，每天都會去健身房，晚上還會一直溫書到深夜。”
- “讀碩士文憑是一種磨內心態，需要很多努力和耐心。”
