# Grindset (English)
*Grindset* is a slang term used primarily in social media and urban contexts. It refers to the attitude or mindset that someone has towards their daily activities, especially when it comes to consistently working hard towards a goal, often involving a repetitive or monotonous routine. It's about being fully committed to the process and not giving up, no matter how challenging or time-consuming it might be. 
For example: "She's got a strong grindset, always pushing herself to achieve her goals no matter what obstacles come her way."
