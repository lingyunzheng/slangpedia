## Grindset (磨砺心态)
**中文解释：** 磨砺心态，指个人持续不断地努力工作或追求目标的态度和状态。这是一种坚韧不拔的精神，强调即使遇到困难也不放弃，坚持到底的精神面貌。
**英文解释：** A grindset refers to a mindset or approach characterized by persistent and unremitting hard work or pursuit of a goal. It conveys a resilience and determination to keep going, even in the face of challenges and setbacks.
