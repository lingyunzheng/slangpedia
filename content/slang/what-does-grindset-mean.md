## English Explanation
- **Definition:** Grindset refers to the mindset and lifestyle where someone spends a significant amount of time and effort, often doing repetitive and not particularly meaningful tasks or activities, in pursuit of their goals or to build up experience. This mindset is commonly found among individuals who are striving for professional achievement or personal goals.
- **Examples:**
- A programmer who stays up late at the office, constantly writing code to improve their technical skills demonstrates a grindset.
- Fitness enthusiasts who strive to shape their bodies or lose weight by running miles in the gym also show a grindset.
- **Usage in Sentences:**
- He had been a grinder since his student days, spending nights in the library reading and studying, which led to his becoming a leader.
- His friends say he has a grindset, so he works late every time to make sure the project goes perfectly.
