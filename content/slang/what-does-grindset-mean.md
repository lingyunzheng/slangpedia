## Grindset (Grinding心态)
**English**: *Grindset* refers to the mentality or mindset of putting in sustained, hard work and effort over a long period, often in a context of striving for personal or professional goals. It embodies a lifestyle or approach to work that emphasizes persistence, dedication, and often a bit of grit.
**Chinese**: *Grindset* 表示一种持续付出努力和辛勤工作的心理状态或心态，通常是在追求个人或职业目标的过程中。它代表了一种工作方式或生活方式，强调坚持、敬业和一丝不苟的态度。
---
Example usage in a sentence:
* **English**: "He's always been a part of the grindset, always pushing himself to reach the next level."
* **Chinese**: "他始终秉持着磨砺心态，不断自我激励去达到下一个层次。"
