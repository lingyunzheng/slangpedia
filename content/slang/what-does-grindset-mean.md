# Grindset (英语: Grindset)
## English Explanation
**Grindset** is a slang term that refers to a lifestyle or mindset focused on relentless hard work, dedication, and productivity, often in an intense or competitive environment. People who embody a "grindset" are typically committed to pushing themselves and others to achieve their goals through tireless effort, often at the expense of leisure and personal relaxation. This term is commonly used in contexts such as work, athletic training, and entrepreneurial pursuits.
### Example Usage
- "Since he joined the startup, John has adopted a hardcore grindset, working 12-hour days to ensure the project's success."
## 中文解释
**Grindset** 是一个俚语，指的是那种以不懈努力、全力以赴和高效率为目标的生活方式或心态，通常在充满竞争的环境中表现出来。具有“grindset”的人通常会致力于推动自己和他人不辞辛劳地追求目标，常常牺牲休闲和个人放松。这一词汇常用于工作、体育训练和创业等情境中。
### 示例用法
- "自从加入初创公司以来，约翰就采取了一种彻底的grindset，每天工作12个小时以确保项目的成功。"
