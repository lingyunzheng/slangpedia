## Grindset
### English
**Definition**: Grindset refers to a lifestyle or mindset focused on working hard, often overtime or beyond regular hours, in order to achieve particular goals, whether they are personal, professional, or related to lifestyle improvements.
**Example Usage**: "Since starting his new job, Mark has really adopted a grindset, putting in long hours and sacrificing weekends to excel in his career."
### 中文
**定义**：Grindset 指的是那种专注于勤奋工作的生活方式或心态，这种工作时间往往超出了正常的工作时间，无论是为实现个人目标、职业目标或是生活方式的提升。
**示例用法**：自从开始新工作后，马克就真的采用了一种 grindset，投入了大量的加班时间，并牺牲了周末时间来在职业上出人头地。
