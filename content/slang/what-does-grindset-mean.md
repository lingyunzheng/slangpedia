# Grindset (英语: Grindset)
## 英语解释
**Grindset:** A term often used in the millennial and Gen Z culture, it refers to the mindset or lifestyle of persistently working hard, usually in an intense or even unorthodox manner, to achieve one's goals. This term is common in contexts involving professional development, personal growth, and entrepreneurship, emphasizing dedication and determination.
## 中文解释
**Grindset:** 一个常用于千禧一代和Z世代的文化术语，指的是坚持不懈地努力工作、通常在高强度或非传统的方式下追求目标的生活态度或生活方式。这个词常用于职业发展、个人成长和创业等情境中，强调专注和决心。
