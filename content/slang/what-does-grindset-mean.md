# Grindset (俚语解释)
## 英文解释
A grindset refers to a person's attitude or mindset focused on working hard and consistently to achieve their goals, often in a particular industry or field. It's about being dedicated, relentless, and willing to put in long hours of effort towards a particular passion or career.
**Example Sentence:**
She's got a real grindset when it comes to her passion for baking.
## 中文解释
grindset 指的是一个人致力于持之以恒地努力工作，以达成目标的心态或态度，特别是在某个特定的行业或领域中。这是一种专注、不懈的精神，愿意投入大量时间和精力去追求特定的兴趣或职业。
**例句:**
当谈到烘焙时，她真的有那种不断努力的心态。
