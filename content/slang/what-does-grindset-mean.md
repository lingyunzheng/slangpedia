## Grindset (英语-中文)
- **Grindset (英语)**: A lifestyle or mentality characterized by relentless effort, often in pursuit of success or personal goals, emphasizing hard work and dedication over leisure or easy living.
- **中文释义**: "扛研心态" 或 "挣扎心态"，指一种持续努力、不懈追求成功的理念或生活方式，强调的是勤奋和专注，而非轻松悠闲的生活态度。
- **Example Usage**:
- English: She has a strong grindset and dedicates most of her time to her career.
- 中文: 她有很强的扛研心态，把大部分时间都投入到事业上。
