```markdown
## Grindset (.Company Phrase: 咚咚声 / 磨砺)
**English Slang Explanation:**
"Grindset" originates from the term "grind," which refers to working or exerting oneself vigorously to achieve a goal, often in the context of a hustle culture. The "set" part adds a sense of being dedicated to this way of life. A strong "grindset" is about having the mindset and motivation to keep pushing through obstacles and consistently working hard.
**Bilingual Example Usage:**
*English:* 
- I couldn't stay in the grindset; I needed a break.
- Her grindset is clear — she always shows up on time and puts in the work.
*Chinese:*
- 我没法保持那种研磨心态；我需要休息一下。
- 她的研磨心态很明显——她总是准时出现，而且很努力。
```
This translation and explanation provide a bilingual context for the term "grindset," including how it is used in both English and Chinese.
