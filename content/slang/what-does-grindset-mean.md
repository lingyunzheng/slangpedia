## Grindset (English)
- **Definition**: A mindset or lifestyle of relentless hard work and dedication, often driven by a strong sense of ambition and the desire to achieve a specific goal or improve oneself.
- **Usage**: This term is commonly used in contexts related to entrepreneurship, fitness, and personal development. It can also imply a disciplined and tireless approach to any effort aimed at achieving success.
- **Example**: "Since joining the startup, he has been living a grindset, working long hours and pushing himself to the limit."

## 磨炼圈 (Móliàn Quān, Chinese)
- **定义**: 一种持续且不懈努力工作的思维模式或生活方式，通常由强烈的进取心和达到特定目标或提升自我的愿望驱动。
- **用法**: 这个词常用于创业、健身和个人发展等领域的语境中。它也可以表示对任何旨在取得成功的努力的严格和不懈的态度。
- **例句**: "自加入创业公司以来，他一直过着磨炼圈的生活，工作时间很长，不断自我挑战。"
