## Grindset (英汉双解俚语)
### English
**Grindset**: This term is commonly used among younger generations to describe a particular lifestyle or mindset that emphasizes hard work, dedication, and persistence, often in the context of pursuing artistic, academic, or professional goals. It implies a relentless drive and a strong sense of purpose.
### 汉语
**Grindset**：这个词通常被年轻一代用来形容一种特定的生活方式或思维方式，强调勤奋、专注和坚持不懈，往往是在追求艺术、学术或职业目标的背景下。它意味着一种不懈的努力和强烈的使命感。
