# Grindset (Grindset)
## English
**Grindset**: This term originates from "grind," which refers to consistently putting in hard work, often without regard for leisure. In the context of a grindset, it represents a mindset or lifestyle where someone is dedicated to their career or passion with relentless effort and determination. It can be seen in various fields, from fitness to entrepreneurship.
### Example: 
"Loving my grindset as a startup founder – it's intense, but I'm all about that hustle."
## Chinese
**Grindset**: 这个词来自 "grind"，指的是付出艰苦努力工作，通常不考虑休闲时间。在grindset的语境下，它表示一种专注于事业或激情的坚定心态，带着不懈的努力和决心。它可以适用于各个领域，从健身到创业。
### 示例：
"作为初创公司的创始人，我深爱我的 grindset ——虽然很紧张，但我是那种喜欢奋斗的人。"
