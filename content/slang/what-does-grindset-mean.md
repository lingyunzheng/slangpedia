# Grindset (Grinding 钻营)
## English Explanation
**Grindset** refers to the mentality or lifestyle of consistently working hard and pushing oneself to achieve goals, often in a context of competition or struggling to succeed in a particular field. It's about being dedicated, persistent, and possibly even sacrificing other aspects of life (like social activities or rest) to excel.
## Chinese Explanation
**Grindset**（钻营）是指一种坚持不懈地努力和推动自己达成目标的心态或生活方式，通常是在竞争或努力在某个领域成功的情境下。它涉及到专注、坚持，有时甚至可能要牺牲其他方面的生活（如社交活动或休息）来达到卓越。
## Usage Examples
- *He really has a grindset; he's in the gym five days a week.*
- *要想在这个行业脱颖而出，你需要有一种磨钻的心态，坚持不懈地努力。*
