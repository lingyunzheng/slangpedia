## Grindset
### English
**Definition:** A mindset or lifestyle focused on working hard and putting in consistent effort, often in a competitive or challenging environment.
**Example:** "To get that promotion, you've really got to have a strong grindset and show up every day, even when it's hard."
### 中文
**释义：** 一种致力于努力工作并持续投入精力的生活方式或心态，通常在竞争性或具有挑战性的环境中表现得尤为突出。
**例句：** "为了晋升，你真的需要拥有一种强大的磨砺心态，在面对困难时也要坚持每天到场。"
