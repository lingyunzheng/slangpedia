## Grindset (Literal - 努力状态; Slang - 努力模式)
**English:**
- *Grindset:* Describes a mindset or lifestyle focused on consistent hard work, particularly in a professional or personal context. It often implies working tirelessly towards long-term goals, often with little immediate reward or recognition.
**Chinese:**
- *grindset (努力状态 / 努力模式):* 用来形容一种专注于持续努力的心态或生活方式，特别是在职业和个人层面。它常常意味着为长期目标不懈努力，即使短期内可能得不到认可或回报。
