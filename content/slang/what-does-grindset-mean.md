# Grindset (English-Simplified Chinese)
## English Explanation
**Grindset** is a slang term often used in contexts related to hard work, dedication, and persistence, often in a competitive or demanding environment. It describes someone who is deeply involved in a specific activity, often sacrificing relaxation or leisure time to achieve their goals.
## 中文解释
**Grindset** 是一个俚语，通常用于描述在竞争或需求高的环境中，投入大量努力、专心致志和坚持不懈的状态或态度。这个词用来形容那些在特定活动上投入大量时间和精力的人，有时会牺牲休闲或休息时间来实现目标。
