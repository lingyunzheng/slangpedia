# Grindset (英语: Grindset)
## English Explanation
"Grindset" is a term often used in the context of social media and street culture. It refers to a mindset or lifestyle centered around the idea of putting in consistent, hard work or effort to achieve a goal. It can also evoke an attitude of determination and resilience in the face of challenges.
### Example Usage:
- "She has a real grindset on, always working out and focusing on her career goals."
