# Slang: Grindset (English & Chinese)
## Grindset (English)
**Definition:** A mindset or lifestyle centered around continuous hard work, often to achieve personal or professional goals, even if it means long hours or sacrifice.
**Example Sentence:** "Even though the job is demanding, she keeps a strong grindset to achieve her career ambitions."
## Grindset (Chinese)
**定义:** 一种以持续努力工作为中心的心态或生活方式，常常为了达到个人或职业目标而付出长时间的努力或牺牲。
**例句:** "尽管工作要求很高，但她依然保持积极的磨砺心态以实现自己的职业抱负。"
