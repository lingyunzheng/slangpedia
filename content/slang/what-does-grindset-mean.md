# Grindset (英语)
## Explanation:
- **Definition**: A grindset refers to a lifestyle and mindset focused on hard work, persistence, and dedication towards achieving long-term goals, often associated with careers or personal projects.
- **Example**: "John has a strong grindset when it comes to his coding projects; he works late nights consistently to improve his skills."

# 磨砺圈 (汉语)
## 解释:
- **定义**: 磨砺圈指的是以勤奋、坚持和专注实现长期目标的生活方式和心态，通常与职业生涯或个人项目相关联。
- **示例**: "约翰在编程项目上有着强烈的磨砺圈；他经常加班到深夜，致力于提高自己的技能。"
