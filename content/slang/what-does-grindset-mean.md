# Grindset
**中文解释：**
Grindset 是一个流行的网络用语，用来形容像机器一样坚持不懈地努力工作或完成某项任务，即使过程单调或挑战巨大。这个词强调的是一种付出大量时间和精力、不怕吃苦、持续努力的积极态度。
**英文解释：**
The term "grindset" is a popular internet slang used to describe the relentless and persistent effort one puts into a task or job, even when the process is monotonous or challenging. It emphasizes a positive attitude of putting in a lot of time and energy, not minding hardships, and continuing to work hard.
