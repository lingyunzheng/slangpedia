# Grindset (English)
**Definition:** A mindset or lifestyle centered around productivity, often associated with relentless effort and dedication to a particular goal, often in a professional or personal context.
**Example Usage:** "Ever since John joined the startup, he has adopted a real grindset, working long hours and pushing himself to meet his targets."
# Grindset (Chinese)
**定义:** 一种以高产、不懈努力和致力于特定目标为标志的心态或生活方式。
**用例示例:** "自从约翰加入初创公司以来，他就养成了真正的‘grindset’，工作时间很长，努力完成自己的目标。"
