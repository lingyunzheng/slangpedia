# Grindset (English/Grindset 中文/钻营心态)
**English:** "Grindset" is a term often used in the context of hip-hop culture and everyday life to describe a person who is highly motivated and consistently puts effort into achieving their goals, often working hard and sacrificing time with friends and family for their aspirations.
**中文:** "钻营心态" (Zuoying Shi tai) 一词通常用于描述一种心态，这种人极具动力，持续不断地努力去实现自己的目标，往往会为了追梦而辛勤工作，甚至牺牲与家人朋友相聚的时间。这种心态也常与不懈努力、不屈不挠的精神联系在一起。
In the context of urban slang, having a strong grindset means someone is dedicated and persistent, always pushing themselves to the limit in pursuit of success.
