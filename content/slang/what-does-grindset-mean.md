# Grindset (English)
**Definition:** A grindset refers to a mindset or lifestyle centered around hard work and continuous effort, often in a specific context like fitness, business, or creative endeavors. It's about putting in the consistent, often strenuous effort to achieve goals.
**Example:** "Alex lives for his grindset, pushing himself hard in the gym every day and working late to make his startup successful."
# Grindset (Chinese)
**定义：** 磨砺圈或生活态度，围绕着努力工作和持续的付出而展开，通常在健身、商业或创造领域中体现。这是指在特定目标上持续努力，即使付出艰辛的努力以达成目标。
**示例：** "艾丽克斯热衷于他的磨砺圈，在健身房里每天都很努力，在创业公司加班到深夜。"
