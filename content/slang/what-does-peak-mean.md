# Peak (形容词)
**中文 slang:** 爆款 / 高潮
**English slang:** 
1. **Peak** - When something is at its highest point of popularity or excellence.
- *Example:* This new movie is a total **peak** – it's getting everyone talking!
- *翻译:* 这部新电影是个爆款——它让大家纷纷热议！
2. **At its peak** - Describing the moment when something is most successful or popular.
- *Example:* His popularity was at its peak last year before the controversy.
- *翻译:* 他在去年争议之前处于最高峰。
**Usage:**
- When something is the best or most exciting, you might call it a **peak**.
- It can also refer to a person or thing being at the top of their game.
