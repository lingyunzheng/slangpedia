# Peak
## English
**Meaning**: The best, most awesome, or most intense state or quality of something.
- **Example**: "Tonight's party is the peak of fun!"
## Chinese
**含义**: 最好、最棒或最刺激的状态或品质。
- **例句**："今晚的聚会是最大Fun的时刻！"
