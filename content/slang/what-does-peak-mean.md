# Peak (高峰) 🏔️
## English
### Meaning
- **Literally**: Refers to the highest point of a mountain or the highest degree of something.
- **Slang**: Often used to describe something that is at its best or most impressive.
- "He was in peak form" — He was in the best shape of his life.
- "Her acting skills are in peak performance" — Her acting skills are at their best.
### Usage Example
- "She's in her peak career right now and has won every award this year."
## Chinese
### 含义
- **字面意思**：指的是山峰的最高峰或某事物的最高程度。
- **俚语**：常用来形容某事物处于最佳或最辉煌的状态。
- "他在巅峰状态" — 他正处于最佳的状态。
- "她的表演技巧正处于巅峰表现" — 她的表演技巧处于最佳状态。
### 使用示例
- "她正处于职业巅峰期，在这一年中拿到了所有的奖项。"
