## Peak ( Peak )
**English:**  
Peak: Refers to someone or something that is at the absolute best or highest level possible. It's often used in the context of describing the pinnacle of quality, performance, or popularity.
*Example:* "When it comes to streetwear, Off-White is currently the peak."
**中文:**  
peak: 指某人或某物处于最佳或最高水平。它常用于描述质量、表现或流行度的巅峰。
*例句:* “从街头服饰来看，Off-White 目前就是巅峰的存在。”
