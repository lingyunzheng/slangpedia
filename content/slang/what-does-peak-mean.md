# Peak (形容词/名词)
**中文解释：**
- 炫酷的，极好的
- 顶点，高峰
**英文解释：**
- Super cool, awesome
- The highest point or peak level
**例句：**
- 中文：这份工作对她来说就是 peak experience。
- English: This job was a peak experience for her.
**例句：**
- 中文：这件外套超级 peak，我一定要买。
- English: This coat is super peak; I have to buy it.
