# Peak
## English
**Peak**: This term is used to describe something that is at the highest point of quality, success, or performance. It often refers to the best time or moment something can be.
*Example*: "They reached their peak during the last quarter of the year."
## Chinese
** peak**: 这个词用来描述某事物处于最优质的时刻、最成功或表现最佳的时候。它通常指的是事物的最佳时期或瞬间。
*例句*: "他们在去年第四季度达到了顶峰。"
