```markdown
## peak
### English
"Peak" in slang often refers to something being at its best or most intense point. It can be used to describe the top of a hill, the highest point of a project or career, or a moment of maximum excitement or achievement.
#### Example:
- "This concert was the peak of the summer festival season."
### 汉语
“peak”在俚语中通常指的是某事处于最佳或最强烈的状态。它可以用来形容山顶、项目或职业的最高点，或者是最大兴奋或成就的时刻。
#### 例子：
- “这场演唱会是整个月度音乐节的巅峰。”
```

```markdown
## peak
### English
Moving on, "peak" can also denote the point of maximum demand or highest usage, often in relation to a product's sales or service's user base.
#### Example:
- "The app experienced its peak during the holiday season."
### 汉语
“peak”还可以表示需求最大或使用量最高的点，通常与产品的销售量或服务的用户基数相关。
#### 例子：
- “这款应用在假日季期间达到了用户使用量的最高峰。”
```
