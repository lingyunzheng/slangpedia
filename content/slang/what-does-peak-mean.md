## Peak
- **English:**  
*Literal Meaning:* Top of a mountain or general pinnacle.  
*Slang Meaning:* In modern times, "peak" can refer to someone or something being at the absolute height of their power, quality, or popularity. For example, "That album was a peak moment in his career."
- **汉语:**  
*字面意义:* 山顶或者一般意义上的最高点。  
*俚语意义:* 现代语境中，“peak”可以指某人或某事物处于顶峰状态，即拥有最高的权势、质量或流行度。例如，“那张专辑是他事业中的巅峰时刻。”
