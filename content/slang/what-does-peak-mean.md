## peak (巅峰 / 顶峰)
**English:**
- **Definition:** "Peak" can mean the highest point of something, like the top of a mountain, but it is also used informally to describe someone or something that is at the best point in its development or popularity.
- **Usage Example:** "Her singing career is at its peak right now."
- **Slang Explanation:** In modern slang, "peak" is used to describe the top or best part of something. It can highlight someone or something that is in prime condition or the most successful stage.
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** "巅峰" 简直就是“顶峰”的意思，通常用来形容最高的点，比如山的顶峰。但在非正式场合，“peak” 用来描述某事物处于最好发展或最流行阶段的时候。
- **用法示例:** “她的音乐事业正处于巅峰时期。”
- **俚语解释:** 在现代俚语中，“peak” 表示最佳状态或最成功阶段，用来形容某个时刻或者某个状态处在最佳或是最成功的点。
