# Peak Slang Explanations
## English
**Peak**: 
- Meaning: Something that is at or near the best, most impressive, or perfect state.
- Example: "That's a peak moment in our relationship!"
## 中文
**peak**:
- 含义：表示某事物处于或接近最佳、最令人印象深刻的或完美的状态。
- 例句："那是我们关系中的一个巅峰时刻！"
