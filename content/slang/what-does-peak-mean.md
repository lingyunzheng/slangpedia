# Peak
## English
### Definition
- The "peak" in slang often refers to the highest point of something, especially when describing excitement, excitement level, or performance.
### Example Usage
- "The party was at its peak when the DJ started playing those old school tracks."
- "He was at the peak of his career before he retired."
## Chinese
### 解释
- 在俚语中，“peak”通常指的是某事物的最高峰，特别是在描述兴奋程度、兴奋水平或表现时。
### 示例用法
- "派对在DJ开始播放那些老学校曲目时达到了顶点。"
- "在他退休前，他职业生涯达到了巅峰。"
