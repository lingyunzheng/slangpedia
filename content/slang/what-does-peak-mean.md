# Peak (顶流)
## English
**Peak**: Refers to someone or something that is at the peak of popularity or success. It can also be used to describe the pinnacle or highest point of something.
Example:
- "She's the peak of social media influencers."
## Simplified Chinese
**顶流 (dǐng liú)**: 指的是某人或某事物处于极度流行或成功的顶点。也可以用来形容某事物的最高点。
例句：
- “她就是社交媒体上的顶流。”
```

# Peak (顶峰)
## English
**Peak**: Often used to describe the highest point of a mountain or the highest point of something non-geographical, such as popularity or achievement.
Example:
- "The mountain reaches a peak at 4,000 meters."
## Simplified Chinese
**顶峰 (dǐng fēng)**: 经常用来形容山峰的最高点或非地理意义上的最高点，如流行度或成就的顶点。
例句：
- “那座山的海拔达到了4000米的顶峰。”
```
```markdown
# Peak (顶包)
## English
**Peak**: Can also be a slang term in some contexts, like "peak" in fashion, where it focuses on the latest, most current style.
Example:
- "The new collection from the designer is the peak of winter fashion."
## Simplified Chinese
**顶包 (dǐng bāo)**: 在某些语境下也可以作为俚语使用，类似于时尚中的“peak”，强调最新的、最流行的趋势。
例句：
- “设计师的新系列是冬季时尚的顶包。”
```
Note: The term "顶包 (dǐng bāo)" in Chinese slang does not directly translate to "peak" in the same sense as the English slang term. It's more of a cultural note on how the word is used in Chinese.
