## Peak
### Chinese (中文)
**解释**: 指某人的表现、情绪或者某件事情达到了一个非常高的状态，通常带有佩服和羡慕的意味。
**例句**: 她在比赛中拿到了金牌，真的是一项peak的表现！
### English
**Explanation**: Refers to when someone's performance, mood, or a situation reaches an incredibly high level, often with a tone of admiration and envy.
**Example**: She won a gold medal in the competition, which was a peak performance!
