# Peak (Eng <-> Chinese)
#### English to Chinese:
***Peak*** - "顶流" (dǐng liú)  
**Definition**: Used to describe something or someone who is extremely popular or trending at a moment in time. It conveys the idea of being at the highest level of success or popularity, similar to being a "front runner" or "trendsetter" in a specific field.
**Example**: "The new song by Jack is peak right now on social media!" - "杰克的新歌现在顶流了！"
#### Chinese to English:
***顶流 (dǐng liú)*** - "peak"  
**Definition**: Refers to something or someone who is extremely popular or at the highest level of success at a specific moment, often used in online or social media contexts.
**Example**: "The celebrity's new appearance became the peak moment of the evening." - "名人的这次亮相成为了晚上的顶流时刻。"
