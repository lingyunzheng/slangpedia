## Peak (高峰)
### English
- **Meaning**: Top tier, the best, or something that is at its highest level of achievement or popularity.
- **Example**: "She's the peak of her career right now."
### Chinese
- **释义**: 顶尖的、最好的，或是达到最高峰的状态或成就。
- **例句**: "她正处在事业的巅峰。"
