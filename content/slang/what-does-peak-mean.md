```markdown
# Peak
**English:** 
- **Meaning:** Something at the highest point or most extreme level; often used to describe a person or thing that is at the absolute best.
- **Example Sentence:** "She's the peak of her career right now."
**Chinese:**
- **释义:** 表示最高点或最极端的水平，常用来形容某人或某物处于最佳状态。
- **例句:** "她现在正处于事业的巅峰。"
```
```markdown
# Peak
**English:** 
- **Meaning:** Informal slang term often used to describe something or someone that is at the top or best possible level, emphasizing quality or effectiveness.
- **Example Sentence:** "This new tech device is the peak of innovation."
**Chinese:**
- **释义:** 常用来形容某种事物或某人处于顶级或最佳状态，强调质量和效果。
- **例句:** "这款新型科技产品是创新的巅峰。"
```
