# Peak (Slang)
## English
**Peak** (peak): This term is commonly used to describe something or someone at the highest level of quality, performance, or popularity. It often refers to a peak performance, being on top of your game, or being extremely successful.
Example: "She's really in her peak as a singer; her latest album is amazing!"
## 中文
**peak** (最 peak): 这个词通常用来形容某事或某人在质量、表现或流行程度上达到最高水平。它经常指的是巅峰表现、处于最佳状态或极其成功。
例如：“她的最新专辑太棒了，真的处于歌唱生涯的巅峰。”
