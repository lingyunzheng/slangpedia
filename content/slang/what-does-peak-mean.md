## Peak (英语/英语)
### English
- **Meaning**: An extremely high level; the best.
- **Usage**: Often used to describe something at its highest or most impressive point.
### Chinese (英语)
- **意思**: 极其高水平；最好的。
- **用法**: 常用来形容某事物达到最佳或最高点的时候。
### Example Sentences
- **English**: She's at the peak of her career.
- **Chinese (英语)**: 她的职业生涯正处于巅峰时期。
### Related Phrases
- **English**: Hit the peak, Peak performance, At the peak of success
- **Chinese (英语)**: 达到顶峰, 最佳表现, 成功的巅峰
