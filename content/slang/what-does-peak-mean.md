## Peak
### English
- **Meaning:** At its best; the highest point; something excellent.
- **Example:** "This burger is a peak experience in fast food!"
### Chinese
- **含义:** 最好的状态；巅峰；极好的东西。
- **例句:** "这家汉堡店提供的汉堡是快餐中的巅峰体验！"
