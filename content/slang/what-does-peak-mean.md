# Peak (English-Chinese Slang)
## English
**Peak** often refers to something at its highest or best point. It can be used in different contexts such as "peak flow" in asthma management or metaphorically to describe something that is at its best.
## Chinese
** peak** （顶峰）: 常用来形容某事物在最顶尖或最佳状态。它可以用于不同的语境，比如哮喘管理中的“峰值流量”，或者是比喻某事物处于最佳状态。
Example:
- "The athletes are running at their peak performance." 
- "运动员们正处于最佳竞技状态。"
- "This is a peak moment in my career."
- "这是我在事业中的巅峰时刻。"
