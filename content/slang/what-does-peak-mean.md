# Peak
## English
**Meaning:** Something that is at the highest level of quality, performance, or success.
**Example Sentence:** "That was a peak moment in his career."
## 中文
**含义:** 表示最顶尖的质量、表现或者成功时刻。
**例句:** "那是他职业生涯中的一个巅峰时刻。"
