## Peak (汉语：顶峰 / 底峰)
- **English Slang Explanation:** "Peak" can be used as a slang term to describe something that is at the highest point of quality, popularity, or performance. It often implies the culmination or the best moment of something.
> Example: "Her singing career really hit its peak after the Grammy win."
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:** "顶峰" (dǐng fēng) or "底峰" (dǐ fēng) is commonly used to indicate the highest point or the peak of something. It can describe a period of highest achievement or a top point in terms of quality or popularity. "底峰" is less common and is used to refer to the lowest point.
> 例句: "她在格莱美获奖后，事业真正达到了顶峰。" (After winning the Grammy, her career truly reached its peak.)
> 反义句: "他们的爱情跌入了底峰，再也无法回升。" (Their love fell to the bottom of the valley and could no longer rise back up.)
