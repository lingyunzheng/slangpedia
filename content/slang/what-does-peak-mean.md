## Peak (高峰)

### Chinese (中文)
- **含义**: 原本意为山峰的最高峰，现在用来形容某事物的顶点或最佳状态。
- **例句**: 他在这场演出中表现得非常peak。
- **解释**: "他在这次演出中的表现非常出色，达到了顶峰水准。"

### English
- **Meaning**: Originally meaning the highest point of a mountain, it now describes the peak or best state of something.
- **Example**: He performed very peak in this show.
- **Explanation**: "He gave an excellent performance in the show and reached the peak level of quality."
