```markdown
# Peak (English-Chinese Slang)
## English
**Peak** [piːk]  
- As a term, it is commonly used to describe something that is at its highest level, most impressive, or the best.
## Chinese (English Slang)
**顶峰 (dǐng fēng)**  
- 顶峰 is a direct translation of "peak," which refers to the highest point or greatest achievement, whether in terms of quality, popularity, or another measure. It is often used in a similar context to describing something that is at its absolute best.
### Examples
- **English:** She's at the peak of her career.
- **Chinese (English Slang):** 她正处于职业的顶峰。
```
### Note:
In casual language, some English slang uses "peak" in different contexts or with different connotations. The provided example is a common usage in both languages.
