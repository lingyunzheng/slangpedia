# Peak (Both Chinese and English)
## English Explanation
"Peak" in slang terms can refer to someone or something that is at the top of their game or the best version of themselves. It can also be used to describe a period where something is at its most intense, impressive, or noteworthy. 
Example: "She had a real peak moment at the award show last night."
## Chinese Explanation
“peak”在俚语中可以表示某人或某物正处于最佳状态或生涯的顶峰时期，也可以用来描述某个时刻是最为强烈、印象深刻或最精彩的时候。
例句：“她昨晚在颁奖典礼上的表现简直是职业生涯的巅峰。”
