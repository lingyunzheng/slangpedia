## Peak
### English:
- **Meaning:** Top, best, or peak performance or condition. Often used to describe something at its absolute best or most intense point.
- **Example Usage:** "That was a peak moment in the game when he scored three goals in a minute."
### Chinese:
- **意思:** 最好、巅峰或最佳状态。常用来形容某事物达到最顶峰或最强烈的状态。
- **例句:** "那是在比赛中他一分钟内进了三个球，是比赛中的巅峰时刻。"
