# Peak Slang Explanation
## English
**Peak**: Refers to something or someone that is at the highest point of quality, enjoyment, or coolness. It is often used to describe things that are exactly on trend, stylish, or in their best form. For example, "This outfit is peak style."
## Chinese
**peak**: 用来形容某个事物或某个人正处于最顶级、最棒的状态，或者是最符合潮流、最酷的状态。比如，“这件衣服真是peak了！”
