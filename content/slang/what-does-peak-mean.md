## Peak
### English
**Meaning:** 
- *Noun:* Something that is at the highest level of quality, performance, or popularity.
**Example:**  
- "Last night was the peak of our night out, the party was incredible!"
### Chinese
**含义：**
- *名词:* 表示某事物处于最高水平的质量、表现或流行阶段。
**例句：**
- "昨晚是我们夜生活最高峰的夜晚，派对超级精彩！"
