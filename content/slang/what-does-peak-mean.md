# Peak (English-Chinese Slang Explanation)
## English
### Definition
- **Literal Meaning**: Pinnacle, Apex
- **Slang Usage**: Refers to the highest level, best moment, or most intense phase.
### Example Sentence
- "That was her peak performance, and everyone was impressed."
## Chinese
### 解释
- **直译**: 顶点，巅峰
- **俚语用法**: 表示最高水平、最佳时刻或最激烈阶段。
### 示例句子
- "那是她的巅峰表现，所有人都非常印象深刻。"
