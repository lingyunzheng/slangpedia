```markdown
# English: Real
# 中文: 真实的/搞得夸张
- **Definition:** Genuine, without pretense or exaggeration. Can also refer to something or someone that is actual, factual, or true.
- **Example Use in a Sentence:**
- English: That's real cool.
- 中文: 这是真的酷。
- **Definition in Slang:** Can also be used to exaggerate something, similar to "super" or "very" in English, but often with a tone of mock seriousness or emphasis.
- **Example Use in a Sentence:**
- English: This party is real lit.
- 中文: 这个派对真的很燃。
```
This markdown format helps clearly distinguish between the literal and slang usage of the word "real," providing both definitions and example sentences for each interpretation.
