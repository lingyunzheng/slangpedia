```markdown
# Real (English/Chinese)
## English
**Real**: This slang is often used to mean something is genuine, authentic, or true. It can also express that something is happening in a straightforward and honest manner. Example: "He's real when it comes to his love for music."
## Chinese
**真]): 这个俚语常用来表示某事是真正的、真诚的或真实的。它也可以表达某事是诚恳直接的。例如：“他对音乐的热爱是真紳的。”
```

```markdown
# Real (English/中文)
## English
**Real**: Often used to describe something that is genuine or honest, or when something is done in a straightforward, unpretentious way. Example: "She's real about her career goals."
## Chinese
**真]: 用于描述某物是真正或诚实的，或者某事是以一种直接、不招虚张声势的方式进行。例如：“她对自己的职业目标是真紳的。”
```

```markdown
# Real (English/中文)
## English
**Real**: This slang expresses something that is genuine, down-to-earth, and not fake or artificial. Example: "This new technology is real and has brought a lot to the industry."
## Chinese
**真]: 这个俚语表示某物是真正、本真的，没有虚假或做作。例如：“这项新技术是真紳的，并在行业中带来了许多变革。”
```
```markdown
# Real (English/中文)
## English
**Real**: Often used to emphasize the authenticity or reality of something. Example: "The news was shocking but it was all real."
## Chinese
**真]: 通常用来强调某事物的真实性或真实性。例如：“消息令人震惊，但这一切都是真实的。”
```
These explanations use different contexts and examples to showcase various usages of the slang term "real" in both English and Chinese.
