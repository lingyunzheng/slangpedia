## English Slang: Real
### Explanation:
**Meaning**: Something or someone that is genuine, authentic, or true, not fake or artificial.
### Example Usage:
- "His love for her is **real**."
- "The deal is **real**, no need to worry about scams."
## Chinese 滨海俚语: 真实的
### 解释:
**意思**: 指的是真实、真诚或者真实的，不是假的或者人造的。
### 使用示例:
- "他对她的爱是**真实的**。"
- "这生意是**真实的**，不需要担心骗局。"
