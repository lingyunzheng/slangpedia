## Real (英语: Real)

### English Explanation
**Real** - When used positively, it means something or someone is genuine or true. It can also suggest something is happening in real life rather than in the internet or media. It's often used to emphasize something is true, serious, or actually happening.

Example: "That's real cool, I mean, it's actually happening!"

### Chinese Explanation
**real** - 当这个词用于积极意义时，意味着某样东西或某人是真正、真实的。它还可能用来表明某事是在现实生活中发生，而不是在互联网或媒体中。这个词常用来强调某事是真的、严肃的，或者实际上正在发生。

例句："那是真的酷，我指的是，这实际上正在发生！"
