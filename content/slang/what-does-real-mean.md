## English: Real
### Definition
authentic, genuine, or genuine-feeling; can also be used to describe something that is not fake or is true to its essence.
### Example
- "That new brand of sneakers is real; they're exactly like the expensive ones."
## 中文: 真实（real）
### 释义
真的，真正的，或者说是正宗的；也可以用来描述某事物不是假的或保持了其本质的真实性。
### 示例
- “那款新的运动鞋真的很真实；它们和贵的完全一样。”
