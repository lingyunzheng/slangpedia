## English Slang: Real
### Definition
When something is "real," it means it's genuine, truthful, or true to form. It can describe something or someone that is authentic, not fake, or genuinely felt.
### Example Usage
- **Example 1:** "This concert experience is real. The venue was packed, and the crowd was so energized."
- **Example 2:** "He ain't playin', this is real. He's mad at me 'bout something."
### Chinese Slang: 实（shí）
### Definition
"实"（shí）通常用来形容某事或某物是真实的、真诚的或原始未修改的。它用于表示没有夸大或虚假的、真正存在的状态。
### Example Usage
- **例句 1:** "这场演唱会真的很真实，现场非常热闹，粉丝们都非常激动。"
- **例句 2:** "他不是在装，这是真的。他真的对我生气了。"
