## English
**"Real"** - This slang is often used to denote something genuine, authentic, or genuine, implying that something is not fake or an imitation. It's commonly used to express that something is true or serious, especially when dealing with matters of trust or credibility.
## 中文
**"Real"** - 这个俚语常用来表示某物是真正、真实的或原汁原味的，意味着某物不是虚假或仿制品。通常用来表达某物是真的或认真的，尤其是在涉及信任或可信度的问题上。
