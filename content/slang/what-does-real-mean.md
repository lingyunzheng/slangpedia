```markdown
### English: Real
**Definition:** Something that is honest, genuine, or existing in fact; not fake or imagined.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Real (真的):** In informal contexts, "real" can be used to describe something that is real and not pretend. It can also convey authenticity or genuineness. For example:
- "That's real" (这真的): This means the thing being discussed is genuine or not an imitation.
- "He's real" (他真的): This can also be used to describe a person as someone who is honest and true to their word.
```
```markdown
### English: Real
**Definition:** High-quality or of great importance or interest.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Real (很有料 / 真正的):** This slang term is used to describe something as of high quality or importance, especially in digital or social media contexts. It emphasizes that the information or content is valuable or noteworthy. For example:
- "This news is real" (这条新闻很有料): This means the news is genuinely interesting or important.
- "That's really impressive" (这真是很有料): This is used to express that something is genuinely impressive or noteworthy.
```
```markdown
### English: Real
**Definition:** Relevant and important in the context of a particular group or situation.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Real (.street-real):** This term is often used in streetwear or youth culture to denote something that is relevant or significant within a specific subculture. It implies authenticity and relatability. For example:
- "This trend is real" (这个潮流真是street-real): This means the trend is genuinely relevant or significant in the context of current streetwear culture.
```
```markdown
### English: Real
**Definition:** When something or someone is genuinely interesting or exciting.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Real (真刺激 / 太真实):** Used to express that something is genuinely thrilling or real in a good way. It can also emphasize that something is highly relatable or impactful. For example:
- "That concert was real" (那个演唱会真的太真实): This means the concert was genuinely exciting and impactful.
- "This movie is super real" (这部电影真的超刺激): This indicates the movie is thrilling and exciting.
```
