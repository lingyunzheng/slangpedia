## English Slang: Real
- **Meaning**: Authentic, genuine, or true; often implying something is not fake or is of high quality.
- **Example**: "The concert experience was real, with excellent acoustics and energized crowd."
## Chinese Slang: 真实 (zhēn shí)
- **Meaning**: 真实指的是真实可信或真实的；常用来强调某物不是假的或质量很高。
- **例句**: "演出体验很真实，音质优秀且观众热情高涨。"
