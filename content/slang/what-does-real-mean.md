# Real
**English:**  
- *Meaning:* Something that is genuine, true, or genuine and authentic; a term often used to affirm or emphasize the authenticity of something or someone.
- *Example Sentences:*  
- "This is real skincare, not those fancy but ineffective products."  
- "He's real about his plans, no joking around."
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
- *意思:* 指的是真实的、真正的、货真价实的，常用来确认或强调某事物或某人的真实性。
- *例句:*  
- "这都是真正的护肤产品，不是那些花哨却无效的产品。"  
- "他说话算话，没有开玩笑的一面。"
