## English: Real
### Chinese: 真实的/正经的
**Meaning and Usage:** "Real" can be used in several contexts, but it broadly means genuine, authentic, or serious. It is often used to emphasize truthfulness or sincerity in a conversation. This term can also be used as a shortened form of "real talk" or "real deal," indicating honest or genuine information/drama.
**Example:**
- *English: "That's the real deal!"*
- *Chinese: "这才是真的！" (Zhè jiù shì zhēn de!)*
### Example Usage in a Sentence:
- *English: "He's a real friend, always there for you."*
- *Chinese: 他是个真正的朋友，总是在你需要的时候出现。 (Tā shì gè zhèng zhēn de péng you, zǒngshì zài nǐ xūyào de shíhou chūxiàn.)*
```

## English: Real
### Chinese: 老实说
**Meaning and Usage:** "Real" can also be used to convey a more casual, colloquial form of "honestly" or "to be honest." It's a way of starting a statement to give a frank or genuine expression or opinion.
**Example:**
- *English: "Real quick, what are you doing today?"*
- *Chinese: 老实说，你今天忙什么？ (Lǎoshí shuō, nǐ jīntiān máng shénme?)*
### Example Usage in a Sentence:
- *English: "Real quick, what are you doing today?"*
- *Chinese: 老实说，你今天忙什么？ (Lǎoshí shuō, nǐ jīntiān máng shénme?)*
```

## English: Real
### Chinese: 确实
**Meaning and Usage:** "Real" can also be used to convey the sense of being a fact or truly existing. It adds a degree of certainty to a statement.
**Example:**
- *English: "It's real."*
- *Chinese: 确实是。 (Quèshí shì)*
### Example Usage in a Sentence:
- *English: "It's real, they are going to have a meeting tomorrow."*
- *Chinese: 确实是，他们明天要开会。 (Quèshí shì, tāmen míngtiān yào kāihuì.)*
