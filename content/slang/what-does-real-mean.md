## English: Real
### Definition
Something that is authentic, genuine, or truly happening.
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
- **English:** Real
- **Chinese:** 靠谱 (kào pǔ)
- **Explanation:** 靠谱 means "reliable" or "trustworthy," but it also carries the connotation of being genuine or true. It's often used to describe someone or something that's real in a positive sense.
- **English:** Real
- **Chinese:** 正宗 (zhèng zōng)
- **Explanation:** 正宗 literally means "authentic" or "genuine," which is very close to the concept of "real." It is often used in the context of food or traditions to describe something that is in its purest or most original form.
- **English:** Real
- **Chinese:** 硬碰硬 (yìng pèng yìng)
- **Explanation:** 硬碰硬 is an idiom that literally means "hard against hard," but it's used colloquially to mean "real" or "genuine" and is often used to emphasize toughness or authenticity.
