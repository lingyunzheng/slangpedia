# Real
## English
- **Meaning**: Genuine, authentic; something that is true or genuine, not fake or artificial.
- **Usage**: "This watch is real, not cheap knockoff."
- **Example Sentence**: "His story about meeting the celebrity is real, he has pictures to prove it."
## 中文
- **含义**：真正的，真实的；指的是真实或真诚的事物，而不是假的或做作的。
- **用法**："这块手表是真货，不是廉价的假冒伪劣产品。"
- **例句**："他遇见那位名人的故事是真实发生的，他有照片可以证明。"
