# Real
## English
**Meaning:** Genuine or authentic; something that is not fake or put on.
**Example Usage:**  
- "This is real gold, not fake like those cheap imitations."
- "His smile is real, not forced."
## 汉语
**含义:** 真实的或正经的；不是假的或装出来的。
**用例示例**：  
- "这是真金，不是那些廉价仿制品。"
- "他的笑容是真笑，不是假的。"
