```markdown
# Real (真实)
**English:** Real  
- Meaning: Authentic, true, or genuine. It can also denote something that is not fake or staged.  
- Example: "This is real nice, but it's not real expensive." (This is genuinely nice, but it's not expensive.)
**中文:** 实在  
- 解释: 真正的、真实的或真正的。通常用来形容不假、没有假装的东西。  
- 例句: "这真是好的，但并不实惠。" (This is really good, but it's not really expensive.)
```
**Note:** The slang "real" can also be used in various more colloquial or context-specific ways, such as emphasizing intensity ("real good"), or when expressing agreement and affirmation ("That's real!"). The Chinese equivalent can vary based on context, but common translations might include "真的" (truly) or "实际" (actually).
