## English
**Real**: Something or someone that is genuine, authentic, or not fake. It can also be used to describe experiencing emotions strongly or someone who is serious and not just joking around.
Example: "This is real talk," means what is being said is sincere and not just casual chit-chat.
## 中文
**Real**: 真实、 genuine，或指某人或某物是真实的，不是假的。这个词也可以用来形容情感体验强烈，或是指某人是认真的，而不是在开玩笑。
示例：“这是real talk”，意思是说这些话是真诚的，不是随便说说的。
