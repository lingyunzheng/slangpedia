# Real
## English
**Real**: This slang term is often used to emphasize that something is genuine, true, or authentic. It can also be used to express that something is happening now or is genuinely felt and not pretend.
Example: "This is real, we just got the promotion!" 
In this context, "real" underlines the authenticity of the situation.
## Chinese
**real**: 这个俚语常用来强调某事是真实的，真正的，或原汁原味的。它也可以表示某事正在发生，或真正的感觉，而不是假装的。
例句："我们刚升职了，这是真的！" 
在这句话中，“real”强调了升职这件事情的真实性。
