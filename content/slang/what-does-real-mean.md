## Real (English)
**Meaning:** Something or someone that is authentic, genuine, or true.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **English:** "Real" can mean something or someone that is genuine and not fake.
- **Simplified Chinese:** "真的" (Zéde) 指的是真正没有水分，真实的，没有伪装的东西或人。
## Real (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 真正的，真实的，不假的。
