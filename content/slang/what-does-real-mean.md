```markdown
## Real
**English:** 
- **Definition:** Authentic, genuine, or true; can also refer to something happening in the present.
- **Example:** "That's real deal music." (That's genuine or true music.)
**Simplified Chinese:**
- **定义：** 真实的、真诚的或是真正的；也可以指发生在当前的事情。
- **例句：** "那才是真的东西。" (That's the real thing.)
**Traditional Chinese:**
- ** định義：** 真實的、真摯的或是真正的；也可以指發生在當前的事情。
- **例句：** "那才是真地東西。" (That's the real thing.)
```
This markdown format provides both the English and simplified/traditional Chinese explanations and examples, making it accessible and easy to read.
