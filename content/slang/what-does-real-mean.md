# Real
## English
**Real**: This slang term is used to emphasize something or someone as genuine, serious, or true. It often conveys authenticity or genuineness of an experience, emotion, or trait. For example, "That's real funny" means the joke or situation is genuinely funny.
## Chinese
**real**: 这个网络用语用来强调某事或某人是真实、认真的或真实的。它通常传达出某种体验、情感或特质的真实性和真诚。例如，“那真的很滑稽”意味着这个笑话或情境是真正好笑的。
