```markdown
## "Real" (Both English and Chinese Slang Explanations)
### English
- **Definition:** Genuine, authentic, or true to the claimed standard.
- **Usage Example:** "This is a real classic car, not a replica."
- **Slang Context:** Often used to emphasize the genuineness or authenticity of something.
### Chinese
- **释义：** 真实的，正宗的，或者符合所声称的标准。
- **使用示例：** "这是一辆真正的经典车，不是复制品。"
- **俚语语境：** 经常用来强调某事物的真实性或正宗性。
```
This format clearly distinguishes the definitions, examples, and context in both languages, providing an easy-to-understand reference.
