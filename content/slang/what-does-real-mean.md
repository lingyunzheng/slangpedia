# Real
**English:** 
- **Definition:** Genuine or authentic, not fake or pretended.
- **Usage:** "That's real skrilla, I didn't expect you to show up."
- **Example Sentence:** "He's real about his work ethic."
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** 真实的或原创的，不是假的或假装的。
- **用法:** "这是真的老兄，我没有料到你会来。"
- **例句:** "他的工作态度很real。"
