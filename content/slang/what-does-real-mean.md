# Real
## English
**Real**: Something that is genuine, true, or genuine and not fake. Used to describe someone or something that is genuine, not an act, or something that is happening in reality rather than in a virtual or pretend environment.
Example: "This is real life, not a video game."
## Chinese
**real** (真实的): 真正的，真实的，不虚假的。用来描述某人或某事是真实的、真诚的，不是假装的，或者是指正在发生的真实生活，而不是虚拟或假装的环境。
例句："这很真实的生活，不是游戏。"
