## Real (英/Reəl/ 中/真的/实际的)
### English
- **Meaning**: Genuine, actual, or true.
- **Example**: "That's real quality stuff."
- **Usage**: Often used to emphasize authenticity or truth.
### Chinese
- **解释**: 真正的，真实的。
- **例句**: “这真是高质量的东西。”
- **用法**：通常用来强调真实性或真实性。

## Real (英/Reəl/ 中/真棒/真的)
### English
- **Meaning**: A colloquial way to say "good" or "awesome."
- **Example**: "That was a real game."
- **Usage**: Used to express that something was genuinely impressive or enjoyable.
### Chinese
- **解释**: 一种口语说法，意为“很好”或“真的很棒”。
- **例句**: “那场比赛真的很精彩。”
- **用法**：用来表达某事物确实给人留下了深刻印象或感觉很棒。
```
