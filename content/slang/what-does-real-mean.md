## English
### Real
**Meaning:** Genuine, authentic, or true. It can also refer to something or someone who is not fake or artificial.
**Example:** "Her joy was real when she received the news."
## Chinese
### 真的 (Zhēnde)
**意义:** 真实的，真诚的，或者真实的。这个词也可以用来表示某人或某物不是假的或不真实的。
**例句:** "她收到消息时的喜悦是真的。"
