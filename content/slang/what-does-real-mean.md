# Slang Explanation: Real
## English
**Real** - This slang term is heavily used in various contexts to describe something as authentic, genuine, or true.
- *Example*: "That's real nice of you to help out!"
- *Meaning*: "That's genuinely nice of you to help out!"
- *Example*: "This is real sauce!"
- *Meaning*: "This is genuine and intense!"
## Chinese
**真（zhēn）** - 这个俚语在各种情况下被广泛使用，用来形容某物是真实的、真诚的或真实存在的。
- *例句*：你帮我们做事真是太真（zhēn）棒了！
- *意思*：你帮我们做事真是太真诚棒了！
- *例句*：这真是真（zhēn）香！
- *意思*：这真是很香！
