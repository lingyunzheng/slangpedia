# Real (真实/真心)
**English Slang Explanation:**
- **"Real"** is often used to describe something that is authentic, genuine, or true. It can also be used to express that something is happening in the present time or is true in comparison to something less true or fake.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
- **“真实”/“真心”** 这两个词用来表达某事是真实的或真诚的。有时候，人们也用“真家伙”或“真·”来表示某物或某人的真实性或真实性。例如，在提到朋友时，如果你认为某朋友是真正关心你的，你可能会说“这真是个真心的朋友”。
## Examples
- **English:** "She’s a real friend."  
**Chinese:** 她真是个真心的朋友。
- **English:** "That's the real deal."  
**Chinese:** 这真是个真家伙。
