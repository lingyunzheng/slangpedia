# "Real" (English and Chinese Slang)
## English
- **Meaning**: Genuine, authentic, or not fake.
- **Usage**: "That shirt is real, it's from the original brand, not a knockoff."
- **Bilingual Example**: "He wears real diamonds — they're not fake." — 他戴着 real 钻石——它们不是仿制品。
## Chinese
- **Meaning**: 真正的，真实的，不是假的。
- **Usage**: "这件衬衫是 real 的，是从原品牌买的，不是仿制品。"
- **Bilingual Example**: "他戴着 real 钻石——它们不是仿制品。" —— He wears real diamonds — they're not fake.
