```markdown
# Real
**English:**  
"Real" can mean something that is authentic, genuine, or true. It can also denote something that is not fake or artificial, often used to describe a person, situation, or product that is perceived to be of high quality or genuine.
**Simplified Chinese:**  
"真"（zhēn）可以指真实、真实的、真诚的。它也可以用于形容某人、情况或产品是真实的，高质量的或真诚的，而非虚假或不真实的。
**Examples:**
- English: This album is real good, says the fan.
- 中文: 这张专辑真的很不错，粉丝说。
- English: He's real talented, I tell you.
- 中文: 他真的很有 talent，我相信。
```
```markdown
# Real
**English:**  
"Real" can also be slang meaning that something is true, actual, or happening in the present moment. It can be used to emphatically agree with or confirm something.
**Simplified Chinese:**  
"真"（zhēn）还可以指真实、实际的或正在发生的事情。它可以用来更加坚定地同意或确认某事。
**Examples:**
- English: You guys are really the best!
- 中文: 你们真的是最棒的！
- English: It's real crazy out here!
- 中文: 这里真的很疯狂！
```
