```markdown
## English: Real
### Chinese: 真实的 / 纯真的 (zhēn shí de / chún zhēn de)
**Explanation:**
- **English:** In informal or colloquial English, "real" can be used to describe something that is authentic, genuine, or true, as opposed to fake or artificial. For example, "That's real good!" (That's really good!) or "It's real important to me." (It's really important to me.)
- **Chinese:** 真实的 (zhēn shí de) and 纯真的 (chún zhēn de) are similar terms but can be used in slightly different contexts. 真实的 (zhēn shí de) is often used to describe things that are genuine or accurate, like in "That's real important to me" (那真是重要的 nà zhēn shí jiàn de). 纯真的 (chún zhēn de) implies something that is innocent, pure, or unspoiled, like "It's a real (pure) experience" (那是一次非常纯洁的经历 nà shì yī cì fēi cháng chún jié de jīng lì).
**Examples:**
- **English:** "That's real good!" (That's really good!)
- **Chinese:** "那真是好的！" (Nà zhēn shì hǎo de!)
- **English:** "It's real important to me."
- **Chinese:** "这对我真是重要的。" (Zhè duì wǒ zhēn shì zhèng jiào de.)
- **Chinese:** "那是一次非常纯洁的经历。" (Nà shì yī cì fēi cháng chún jié de jīng lì.) - "It's a real (pure) experience."
```
