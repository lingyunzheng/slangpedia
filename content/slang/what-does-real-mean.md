## English: Real
### Chinese: 真的 (Zhēnde)
**Explanation:**
- **English**: Used to emphasize something is true or genuine. For example, saying "It's real!" when something unexpected and amazing happens.
- **Chinese**: 用来强调某事是真实的或真挚的。例如，当发生一些令人惊喜的事情时，可以说“真的！”以表达这确实是真实的。
```
## English: Real
### Chinese: 实际的 (Shíjì de)
**Explanation:**
- **English**: Often used to indicate something is serious or sincere, as opposed to fictional or imaginary. For example, "Saying it as real, I didn't expect this to happen."
- **Chinese**: 用来表示某事是严肃的或诚恳的，与虚构或想象的相反。例如，“说实话，我没想到会发生这样的事。”
```
