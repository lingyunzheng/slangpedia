```markdown
# Real
**中文释义**: 真实的，符合实际情况的。
**英文解释**: Authentic, genuine.
**例子**:
- 中文: 这个包是真的LV吗？
- 英文: Is this bag a real Louis Vuitton?
- 中文: 他终于说出了实话，我们才知道他是真的生气了。
- 英文: He finally told the truth, and we found out that he was really angry.
```
This bilingual explanation provides a straightforward translation and usage of the term "real" in both Chinese and English, along with examples that demonstrate the concept in context.
