# Slang Explanations: "Real"
## English
- **Definition**: Genuine, not fake or fake.
- **Example Usage**: "She's real when it comes to her goals, always working hard."
## Chinese
- **释义**: 真正的，不是假的或假装的。
- **例句**: "她做事很real，对目标坚持不懈。"
