# Slang Explanation: Real
## English
**Real** is often used to describe something that is genuine, authentic, or true. It can also imply that something is effective, lacking pretense, or directly relevant.
- **Example**: "That's real music" means the music is genuine or of high quality.
## Chinese
**real** 通常用来描述一些真正的东西，表示真诚、真实或有效。也可以用来表示缺乏做作或直接相关。
- **例句**：“那首歌是 real” 意味着这首歌是真诚的或质量很高。
