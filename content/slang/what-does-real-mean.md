# Real
## English Slang
**Real**: Authentic or genuine, often used to describe something that is true or honest, as opposed to fake or artificial.
**Example**: "The new art exhibit is real, with authentic Viking artifacts on display!"
## Chinese Slang
**真·real**：真实的或真诚的，常用来描述是真的或真实的，而不是虚假或做作的。
**例句**："新展览很真，展出的维京文物是真的存在！"
