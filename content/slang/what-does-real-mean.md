## Real
### English
**Meaning**: Genuine, authentic, or sincere. Can also refer to situations or experiences that are not fake or staged.
**Example**: "This concert ticket is real, I bought it from the official website."
### Chinese
**含义**：真实的，真诚的，或者指不是假的或装出来的。也可以用于描述情况或经历是真实的。
**例子**："这张演唱会门票是真的，我在官网上买的。"
