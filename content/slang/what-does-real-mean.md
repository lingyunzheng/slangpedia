# Real (EN/CH)
**EN**: Authentic, genuine. Used to describe something or someone that is sincere and true, not fake or artificial.
**CH**: 真实的，真心的。用来形容某物或某人是真诚和真实的，而不是虚伪或做作的。
> Example in a sentence:
> 
> **EN**: That new smartphone is real; it has all the latest features.
> 
> **CH**: 这款新智能手机很real，它包含了所有最新的功能。
**EN**: Cool, awesome. Emphasizes something that is extremely cool or impressive.
**CH**: 真酷，真牛逼。强调某事物极其酷炫或令人印象深刻。
> Example in a sentence:
> 
> **EN**: This party was real lit last night!
> 
> **CH**: 这场派对昨晚真燃！
**EN**: Honestly, truly. Used to stress the truthfulness of something.
**CH**: 真实地说，真心的意思。用来强调某事的真实性。
> Example in a sentence:
> 
> **EN**: Real talk, this job isn't for everyone.
> 
> **CH**: 真实地说，这份工作不是适合每个人。
