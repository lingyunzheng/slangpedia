## deadass
**English:** Extremely serious or tense; sometimes used to emphasize the intensity of a situation or someone's focus.
**Chinese:** 极其认真或紧张；有时用于强调情况或某人的专注程度。例如，当某人非常投入或严肃对待某事时，可以使用这个词来形容。
**Example Sentence:**
- **English:** He's grinding that goal so deadass!
- **Chinese:** 他在追求那个目标时超级认真！
