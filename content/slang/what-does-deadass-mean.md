# "Deadass" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Deadass**: This term is predominantly used in casual speech, especially among younger generations. It can be defined as:
- **1. Honestly**: When someone uses "deadass," they are emphasizing their sincerity or honesty.
- **2. Really**: It can be used to stress the intensity or the truth of a statement.
**Example**: 
- *Deadass honest, I am not lying.*
## Chinese
**死猪**: "死猪" 是一种常用的非正式用语，尤其是在年轻人之间。它可以被解释为：
- **1. 真诚地**: 当有人使用“死猪”时，他们正在强调他们的真诚或诚实。
- **2. 真正地**: 它也可以用来强调陈述的强度或真实性。
**示例**:
- *死猪说实话，我绝对没有撒谎。*
