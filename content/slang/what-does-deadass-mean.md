## Deadass
**English:**  
"Deadass" is an informal term often used to describe something as completely or utterly sincere, intense, or to the fullest extent. It can also be used to emphasize the truthfulness of a statement, adding authenticity or conviction. This term is commonly used in casual conversations and informal contexts.
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
"Deadass" 是一个非正式用语，用来形容某事是完全的、彻底的真诚、强烈或到极致的程度。它也可以用来强调陈述的真实性，增加真实性和坚定性。这个词语通常用于 casual 交流和非正式的语境中。
