## English Slang: "deadass"
- **Definition**: Describes someone who is extremely serious, focused, or committed to a task or situation, sometimes to the point of being intense or overzealous. It can also imply that a person is nonchalant or bored, depending on the context.
- **Example**: "She's been working deadass all day, you can tell she's really into this project."
- **Bilingual Explanation**: 纯粹认真的，全神贯注的，有时候带有过度投入或显得无趣的意思。例如：“她今天一直工作得超级投入，看得出她真的很看重这个项目。”
