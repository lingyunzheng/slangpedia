# deadass (英汉双解俚语)
**English Explanation:**
- "Deadass" is an informal slang term often used to describe something or someone in a straightforward, direct, and unambiguous way. It can also be used to emphasize the seriousness or stubbornness of a situation or person.
**Chinese Explanation:**
- "死板正" 是一种非正式的俚语表达，通常用来形容某事或某人以一种直接、明确且不带委婉的方式进行。此外，这个词也可以用来强调一种情况或人的严肃性或顽固性。
