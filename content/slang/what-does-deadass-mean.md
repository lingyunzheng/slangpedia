## English
**Deadass** - This term is used as a colloquial intensifier meaning "dead serious" or "100% genuine." It's often used when someone wants to emphasize that they are being completely sincere or serious about something.
### Example:
"Deadass I didn't do it! I have an alibi."
## 中文
**Deadass** - 这个词是一个俚语，用作强调词，意为“非常认真”或“百分之百真诚”。当有人想要强调自己非常认真或真诚时，就会用到这个词。
### 例句：
“deadass 我没有做这件事！我有不在场证明。”
