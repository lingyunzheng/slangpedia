# deadass
**English:** A slang term used to describe something or someone in a very serious or intense manner. It can also be used to express disbelief or sarcasm.
**Chinese:** “deadass” 是一个俚语，在表示某事或某人以非常严肃或强烈的方式时使用。它也可以用来表达惊讶或讽刺。
Example Usage:
- English: "You actually finished the entire assignment in one day? Deadass!"
- Chinese: "你居然一天就把整个作业写完了？deadass！"
