# deadass (英汉双解俚语)
## English Explanation
"Deadass" is an informal term used in American English, often used to emphasize the truthful or serious nature of something. It can also be used to describe someone who is extremely serious, rigid, or takes things too seriously. The term is somewhat similar to "dead serious" but tends to be more colloquial.
**Example in a sentence:**
- "Deadass, I really need you to focus on this project!"
## Chinese Explanation
"Deadass" 是美式英语中的非正式用语，常用来强调某事的真实性或认真程度。它还可以用来形容某人极其认真、僵硬或太认真。这个词有点类似于“dead serious”，但更口语化。
**例句：**
- "Deadass，我真的需要你把这份项目当真对待！"
