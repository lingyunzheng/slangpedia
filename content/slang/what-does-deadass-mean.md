## deadass (英汉双解俚语)
### English Explanation
- **死FileSync**: Literally meaning "dead ass," this term is used to describe something that is extremely bad, terrible, or awful. It is similar to saying something is the worst.
### Chinese Explanation
- **极烂的**: 这个词组用来形容某事物极其糟糕、极差，就像是在说这是最糟糕的情况一样。它和“简直是最烂的”意思相近。
