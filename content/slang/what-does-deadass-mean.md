# Deadass
**English:**  
Deadass is a slang term used to describe something or someone as extremely sincere, serious, or intense. It can also indicate that someone is entirely dedicated to something without any intention of joking around. The term adds emphasis to the seriousness or dedication.
**Chinese (Pinyin and Translation):**  
Deadass (tèlèsè) 表示极度认真、诚恳或专注，也可以表示某人完全投入某事，没有任何开玩笑的想法。使用这个词语可以强调认真或专注的程度。
例句： 
1. **English:** She's being deadass serious about that new business venture.  
**Chinese:** 她对那个新的创业项目是非常认真（tèlèsè）的。
2. **English:** He’s acting deadass cool, but I know he’s really scared inside.  
**Chinese:** 他表现得很酷（dékù），但我知道他里面其实很害怕。
