# Deadass
## English
**Deadass**: 
1. **Definition**: A very straightforward or direct way of describing something or someone without being too polite or diplomatic.
2. **Usage Example**: "He gave me a deadass answer to my question, which means it was a very clear and to-the-point response, not beating around the bush."
## Chinese
**死认真** (sǐ zhēn rèn):
1. **定义**: 表示某人或某事物非常直接或坦率，不拐弯抹角，不含蓄。
2. **用法示例**: "他给我一个死认真回答我的问题，这意味着他的回答非常直接了当，没有含糊其辞。"
