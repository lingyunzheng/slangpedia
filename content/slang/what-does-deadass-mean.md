### 死板 (sǐ bǎn) - Deadass
**English Explanation:**
- **Deadass:** Informal term used to describe someone who is very serious or overly concerned with rules and structure. It can also imply someone who is inflexible or not possessing a sense of humor.
**Chinese Explanation:**
- **死板 (sǐ bǎn):** 形容一个人非常严肃或者过于注重规则和结构的人。它也可以用来形容一个人缺乏幽默感或者非常死板不让步。
