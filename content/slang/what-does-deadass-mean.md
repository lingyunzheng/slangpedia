```markdown
## "Deadass" (in English) vs. "死认真" (in Chinese)
### English: "Deadass"
- **Meaning**: This slang term is often used to emphasize that someone is completely serious, genuine, or intense about something. It can also imply that someone is being extraordinarily direct or straightforward in a conversation or an action.
- **Example**: "When he said he was studying for the whole weekend, he was deadass about it."
### Chinese: "死认真" (sǐ zhǐrén)
- **Meaning**: This phrase emphasizes that someone is being extremely serious or sincere about something. It conveys the same idea of intense commitment or focus without any joking or insincerity.
- **Example**: "他说他整个周末都要学习，绝对是死认真！" (Tā shuō tā zhěnggè zhoumò dōu yào xuéxué, jídìng shì sǐ zhǐ rén!) — He said he would study the whole weekend, and he was absolutely deadass about it!
```
