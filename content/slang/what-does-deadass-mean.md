# deadass
**English:** Swear Word / Strong Intensifier  
- Used to emphasize a negative quality or situation.  
Example: She was deadass tired after working late again.  
- Can also be used as an intensifier for other descriptors.  
Example: Her humor was deadass hilarious.  
**中文:** 粗口 / 强烈强调词  
- 用以强调某种负面的特质或状况。  
例句：因为她又加班到很晚，所以她累得要死。  
- 也可以用于强化其他形容词。  
例句：她的幽默感极其有趣。
