```markdown
### deadass [🇺🇸 English / 🇨🇳 烂开场白]
- **English**: A casual, informal term used to describe someone who is extremely sincere, blunt, or sometimes overly serious. It can also be used to describe a situation or topic that is taken very seriously.
- **Chinese**: 一个用来形容人极度真诚、直白或有时过于认真的口语词汇。也可以用来形容被非常认真对待的情况或话题。直译可以为“烂开场白”，意思是对事物的描述过于直白或认真，没有灵活性。
```
This definition gives a clear idea of the term's usage in both English and Chinese, providing a useful reference for individuals looking to understand or use the term "deadass" in different contexts.
