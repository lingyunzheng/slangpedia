```markdown
# deadass
## English
- **Meaning**: Extremely or thoroughly
- **Example**: "I studied deadass for that exam, and it paid off!" This means the person studied very hard and thoroughly for the exam.
## 中文
- **含义**: 非常或彻底
- **例句**："我为了那场考试，复习得死ace，功夫没白费！" 这里的意思是我为了考试复习得很认真、很彻底，结果果然不错。
```
(Note: In the Chinese example provided, "死ace" is a playful translation of "deadass". In Chinese slang, there isn't a direct one-to-one equivalent, so creative or playfully adapted terms are used to convey the intended meaning.)
