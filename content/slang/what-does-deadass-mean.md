# Deadass (英汉便携解释)
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: This term is commonly used to describe someone who is extremely serious, intense, or dead serious about a situation, to the point of potentially being boring or overly solemn.
- **Example**: "John is being utterly serious, dead ass."
## 中文解释
- **定义**：这个词语常用来形容某人非常认真、严肃，以至于可能会显得枯燥或过于庄重。
- **例句**："约翰说得极其认真，死serious。"
