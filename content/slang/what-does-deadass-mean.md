# "Deadass" ( slang term )
## English
### Meaning
- Extremely serious; not joking in the slightest
- Used to emphasize the seriousness or importance of a situation
### Example
- "Deadass honest, I did not touch that cookie jar!"
## 中文
### 含义
- 极其认真；一点也不开玩笑
- 用于强调情况的严肃或重要性
### 示例
- "死认真，我真的没有碰那个饼干罐！"
