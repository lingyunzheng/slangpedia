```markdown
## English: deadass
### Chinese: 死板正经/板正正经 (sǐ duǎn zhèng jīng / bǎn zhèng zhèng jīng)
- **Explanation**: "Deadass" is a informal, colloquial term often used to describe someone who is very serious, unamusing, or overly formal. It can also imply someone who is being extremely sincere in a way that might be seen as too extreme. 
- **Example**: "He's such a deadass that he takes every joke seriously!" - 他太死板正经了，连个笑话都当真！
- **Context**: Commonly used in text messages, social media posts, and casual conversations among friends.
```
This translation and explanation captures the essence of the term "deadass" in both English and Chinese, providing a clear understanding and context for usage.
