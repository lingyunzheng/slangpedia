# Deadass (英式英语: Deadass)
## English Explanation
"Deadass" is a colloquial term used to describe something or someone that is extremely serious or without any humor, often in a blunt or direct manner. It can also be used to describe a person who is very focused and intense.
### Example Sentences
- "This meeting is deadass important, so make sure you're fully prepared."
- "He’s been really deadass into his work lately, barely taking a break."
## Chinese Explanation
