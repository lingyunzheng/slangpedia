## "Deadass" 
### English
"Deadass" is an informal and colloquial term that directly means the same as "ass," which can also refer to a person or their rear end. However, in slang, it’s often used to describe someone who is stubborn, unyielding, or obstinate. It's especially used to emphasize that the person in question is extremely steadfast in their beliefs or actions.
**Example:**
- "He's such a deadass when it comes to his political views; he won't budge."
### 中文
“Deadass” 是一种非正式且俚语化的词汇，相当于“ass”，可以指代人或臀部。但在俚语中，它通常用来形容一个人非常固执、顽固或坚持己见。这个词汇经常用来强调某人在某些观点上极其坚定不肯妥协。
**例句：**
- “他在这方面如此固执己见，简直像块顽石；他不会动摇的。”
