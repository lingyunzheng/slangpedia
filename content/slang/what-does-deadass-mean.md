## English: deadass
### Chinese: 死板（“死板”的“死”读作“死板板”的“板”声音）
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "Deadass" is an informal term that can be used to describe someone or something as very serious, uninteresting, boring, or rigid. It often implies a lack of flexibility or creativity.
- **Chinese:** 这个词“死板”通常用来形容人或事物非常严肃、无趣、无聊或僵硬。它常常暗示缺乏灵活性或创造性。“死板”的“死”在这里可以读作“死板板”的“板”的发音，以增加表达的幽默感或强调。
**Usage Examples:**
- English: "He's being so deadass about this project, it's not fun to collaborate with him."
- Chinese: 他对这个项目太死板了，和他合作真是没意思。
