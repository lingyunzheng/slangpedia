# Deadass
## English
"Deadass" is a colloquial term used to describe something or someone as serious, intense, or deadly serious. It can also imply that someone is being surprisingly straightforward or direct, but in a forceful manner. It's commonly used in text messages, social media, or when speaking informally.
Example:
- "He came out with that deadass serious expression, I had no idea what he was thinking!"
## 中文
"死认真"（死认真地）是一个俚语，用来描述某事或某人极为严肃、认真或不留余地。它也可以表示某人出乎意料地直接或坦白，但方式较为强硬。这个词汇通常在短信、社交媒体或非正式交谈中使用。
例句：
- “他一脸死认真地说出这句话，我完全不知道他在想什么！”
