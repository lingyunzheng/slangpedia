## deadass
### English
- **Definition:** A term used to emphasize that someone is extremely serious or sincere about a situation or feeling.
- **Usage Example:** "Man, I am deadass tired after that long hike!"
### 中文
- **定义:** 用于强调某人对某种情况或感觉是非常认真或诚恳的。
- **使用示例:** "我真的是累死个算了，徒步走了一天真的好累！"
