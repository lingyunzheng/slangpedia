## 死板 (Shí bǎn) - Deadass
**English Explanation:**
"Deadass" is often used to describe someone who is extremely认真 (jǐnzhèng) or conscientious to the point of being overbearing or lacking creativity. It can also denote a person who is very traditional and doesn't accept change easily, similar to "死板" in Chinese.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"死板" (shí bǎn) 指的是一个人非常认真、一丝不苟，有时候甚至显得过于刻板和缺乏创意。这个词也可以用来形容守旧、不愿意接受新事物的人。
