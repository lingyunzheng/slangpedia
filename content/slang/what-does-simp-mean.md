## Slang Word: Simp
- **English Explanation:** A "simp" is someone who is excessively infatuated with the physical appearance or behavior of others, especially women. They often express their admiration in overly affectionate or attentive ways, which can be seen as obsequious or even creepy by others.
- **Chinese Explanation:** "Simp" 暧维 (ǎi wéi) 是指过度地迷恋或崇拜他人的外貌或行为，特别是女性。他们经常以过于温情或殷勤的方式表达自己的仰慕，这可能会被认为过于阿谀奉承或甚至有骚扰的嫌疑。
