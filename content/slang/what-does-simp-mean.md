```markdown
## Slang: Simp (简体中文: 花瓶)
**English Explanation:**
"Simp" is a slang term commonly used to describe someone who is overly eager to impress or be close to, and to cater to, women. Simps are often seen as being overly sensitive and overly invested in romantic relationships. The term is often used with a hint of amusement or derision.
**中文解释:**
"花瓶"（Simp）是描述那些过度渴望讨好或亲近女性的人的常用俚语。这些人通常对恋爱关系非常敏感和投入。这个词汇往往带有幽默或嘲讽的意味。
```
