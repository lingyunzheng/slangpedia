# Slang Term: Simp
## English Explanation
A "simp" is a term commonly used in internet culture, social media, and dating apps to describe a man who displays overly affectionate, admiring, and often one-sided behavior towards another person, usually a woman. Simps often exhibit traits such as excessive flattery, giving gifts, and expressing intense romantic interest without receiving much in return.
## Chinese Explanation
"simp" 在网络文化、社交媒体和交友应用中，用来形容一个表现出过度的、单方面的、几乎是崇拜式的对另一个人（通常是对女性）的溺爱和仰慕的男人。SiMP 们常常会展现出过度的恭维、频繁送礼和表达浓厚的浪漫兴趣，但往往得不到相应的回馈。
## Example Usage
- **English**: He’s such a simp for her, always buying her gifts and never expecting anything in return.
- **Chinese**: 他简直就是受她掌控的 simp，总是给她买各种礼物，从不期待任何回报。
## Note
The term is often used humorously or derisively. It is a form of toxicity in online and real-life interactions and should be used with awareness of its connotations.
