# Simplified Chinese (Simp) Slang: 简体中文（Simp）网络用语：坎（坎）
## English Explanation
**Simp**: Originally derived from the term "simple," it refers to someone who is superficial, or focused on appearances rather than deep substance or qualities. They are known for admiring, supporting, or prioritizing attractiveness, charisma, or other surface-level characteristics, often to the detriment of intellectual or moral depth. A "simp" might be overly infatuated with an individual, often in a romantic or sexual context, solely because of their physical appearance or perceived charisma. 
## Simp Chinese Explanation
**坎 (Kǎn)**: 来源于“简单”的词语，用来形容表里不一、只关注表面而不注重深层次特性和素养的人。他们常常因为外表、魅力或其他表面特征而迷恋、支持或重视某个人，往往忽视了智力或道德深度。一个“坎”可能会因为某个人的外貌或被感知的魅力而极度迷恋对方，通常这种情况发生在浪漫或性向的互动中。
In both versions, the term is used to describe someone who is more focused on the superficial aspects of a person or a situation, disregarding deeper or more substantive qualities.
