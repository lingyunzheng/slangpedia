# Slang Term: Simp
## English Explanation
A **simp** is a term commonly used to describe a man who is overly focused on impressing and pleasing women, often to the point of being seen as overly eager or insecure. This term is often used in a derogatory manner to highlight a lack of confidence or genuine attraction. 
Example: "He's such a simp, always doing silly things just to get her attention."
