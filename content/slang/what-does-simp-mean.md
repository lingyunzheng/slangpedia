# Slang Term: Simp
## English
A "simp" is a guy who is excessively romantic and loves to show his love for a female, often in an extremely clichéd or outdated way. They are perceived as very devoted and often are seen trying to woo their romantic interest by doing overly sentimental or trivial things.
## 中文
" simp" 指的是一个极其浪漫的家伙，总是极力展示他对目标女性的爱，往往以陈词滥调或过时的方式进行。他们被认为非常忠诚，经常通过做一些过于感性或琐碎的事情来追求自己的意中人。
