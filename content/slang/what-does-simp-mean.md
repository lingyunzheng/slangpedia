## Slang: SImp
### English Explanation
A "Simp" is a term often used in online and social contexts, primarily by young people, to describe someone who is overly infatuated or affectionate towards someone, often to an extent where their behavior may seem inappropriate or excessive. A simp is usually someone who is willing to do a lot to make someone else happy or to win their affection.
