## Slang Term: Simp
- **English Explanation:** A "simp" is an informal term for someone who exhibits characteristics commonly associated with romantic admiration or infatuation, especially directed towards women. This term often implies a behavior that is perceived as overly idealistic, enthusiastic, or even annoyingly devoted.
- **Chinese 解释:** "Simp" 是指一个人表现出对某人（尤其是女性）极度的浪漫迷恋或者仰慕的行为。使用这个词时，通常会暗示这种行为让人觉得过于理想化、热情过度，甚至是令人感到厌烦的忠贞。simp 的行为可能包括过于殷勤、送礼物、过度关注对方的需求和喜好，以及在公众场合表现得过于献媚等。
