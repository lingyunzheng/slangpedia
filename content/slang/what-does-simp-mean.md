# Slang: "Simp"
## English Explanation
"A simp" is a lighthearted term used to describe someone who is overly infatuated with or overly obsessed with a particular person, often to the point of doing things that seem foolish or unrequited in pursuit of that person's affection. It is typically used in a humorous or teasing manner.
## Chinese Explanation
"Simp" 在英语中指那些对某个人极度倾心、过度迷恋的人，有时会为了追慕对象而不顾一切，以至于做出一些看起来愚蠢或单相思的行为。这个词通常用于轻松幽默或开玩笑的场合。
