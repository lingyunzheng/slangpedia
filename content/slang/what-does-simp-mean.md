## Slang Term: Simp
**English:**  
A simper is a term often used to describe a man who is overly obsessed with women, is constantly trying to impress them, and displays a level of puppy-dog adoration that might be seen as sappy or mocking. Simps are often known for their flattering, supportive nature towards women, but this can sometimes be at the expense of rational thought or personal growth. The term is both derogatory and widely used in pop culture to highlight this type of behavior.
**Chinese:**  
“simper” 通常用来形容一个男生过度沉迷于女性，不断试图取悦她们，并表现出一种让人觉得有些矫情或是模仿小狗般讨好的行为。Simper 们往往以其夸赞和支持的态度对待女性，但这有时可能会牺牲理智或是个人成长。这个词在流行文化中既带有贬义，也被广泛用来揭示这种行为特征。
