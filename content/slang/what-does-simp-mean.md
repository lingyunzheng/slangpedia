## Slang Term: Simp
### English Explanation
A "simp" is a slang term often used to describe a man who is overly infatuated with women, often to the point of being seen as weak or foolish. They tend to romanticize their interests and sometimes come off as overly attentive, submissive, or even naive in their relationships.
### Chinese Explanation
“ simp”是指一种常常用来形容太过迷恋女性、甚至可能显得软弱或愚蠢的男性。他们通常在关系中浪漫化自己的兴趣，有时显得过分体贴、顺从，甚至在感情上有些幼稚。
