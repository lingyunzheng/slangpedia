## Slang Term: Simp
### English Explanation
A simp is someone who is overly infatuated with someone of the opposite sex, often not reciprocating the same level of infatuation or often lacking in other social or personal attributes. They tend to chase after romantic or sexual relationships despite clear signs of disinterest or if they themselves are not in a similar mindset.
### Chinese Explanation
“庸俗追求者” (yōng sú qiú zhuī zhě) 指的是对异性有过度迷恋的一个人，通常不会得到对方同样的感觉，或者自身在其他社交或个人方面存在不足。他们往往追求浪漫或性关系，尽管对方明显表现出了不感兴趣，或者他们自己也没有相似的心态。
### Usage Examples
1. **English**: He's such a simp for Sarah that he's willing to do anything she asks!
**Chinese**: 他对莎拉简直是个庸俗追求者，愿意做她要的任何事情！
2. **English**: The simp won't stop asking Luisa out even though she's clearly into someone else.
**Chinese**: 这个庸俗追求者依旧不停地约丽萨出去，尽管她明显喜欢着别人。
3. **English**: Don't be a simp, sis. Just be yourself.
**Chinese**: 妹妹，别当庸俗追求者了。做你自己就好。
