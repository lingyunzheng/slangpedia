## Slang: Simp
### English
**Definition:** A simp is someone who displays a clear and obvious affection or desire for someone, often to a point that it could be seen as uncool or self-righteous. This term is often used humorously or in a derogatory manner to describe someone who is overly impressed by another person's physical appearance or status.
### Chinese
**定义:** simp 是指那些对某人表现出明显而明显的喜欢或渴望的人，有时这种表现可能会被视为不太酷或自作多情。这个词语通常用来幽默地或者贬义地描述那些过分被另一个人的外貌或地位吸引的人。
