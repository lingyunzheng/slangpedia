# Slang Term: "Simp"
## English
- **Definition**: A "simp" refers to a man, often in casual or online contexts, who excessively idolizes or tries to impress a woman with whom he is infatuated. This often involves treating her as if she is superior to himself and goes to great lengths to make her happy, sometimes to the point of being seen as ridiculous or putting himself at a disadvantage.
## Pinyin: tāng rén (蹚人)
- **Explanation**: "Tāng rén" literally translates to "step on people," which conveys the idea that a "simp" will knowingly put others, especially other men, at a disadvantage to cater to his infatuation with a woman. This term highlights the potentially harmful or self-defeating behavior of the "simp." The term emphasizes a more negative connotation, suggesting someone who is overly eager, and willing to sacrifice others or his own well-being for the sake of his infatuation.
