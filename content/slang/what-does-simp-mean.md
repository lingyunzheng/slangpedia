## Slang Term: SKE (Simp)
### English
**SKE (Simp):**  
A person who is openly displaying and maintaining an exaggeratedly attractive or desirable image, often in pursuit of romantic or sexual attention. This term often refers to someone who is perceived as showy in their displays of affection or admiration for others.
### 中文
**SKE (斯珂，俗称：自来熟):**  
指那些公开展示并维持着一种过度吸引或讨人喜欢的形象，通常是为了赢得浪漫或性吸引力。这个词通常指的是那些在表现对他人的好感或欣赏时显得过于夸大、外露的人。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "He's such a ske these days, always boasting about how important he is to his girlfriend."
- **中文:** "他现在真是斯珂了，动不动就吹嘘自己在他女朋友心目中的重要性。"
