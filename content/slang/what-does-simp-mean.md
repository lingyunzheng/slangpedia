```markdown
# Slang Term: "Simp"
## English
### Definition
A "simp" is a non-aggressive, overly admiring and devoted man who is often seen pursuing or obsessed with a woman, often to the point of being seen as silly or foolish. The term is used to describe a man who goes out of his way to impress or win over a woman, sometimes at the expense of his own self-respect or happiness.
### Example
- "It's no surprise he's such a simp for her; she's got the whole town swooning."
## Chinese
### 定义
"抠脚大汉" 是一种对女性过于宠爱和仰慕的男人，经常表现出一种追求或痴迷的态度，有时会被认为是愚蠢或傻气的行为。这个术语用来形容那些为了取悦或赢得一个女人而过度付出的男人，有时甚至可能损害自己的尊严或幸福。
### 示例
- "他这么一个‘抠脚大汉’对她也是理所当然，连整个镇子都被她迷住了。"
```
Note: The Chinese translation provided uses the phrase "抠脚大汉" (kōu jiǎo dà hàn) which is a derogatory term and not officially recognized. "Simp" is more of an online term and has different connotations in Chinese, so a direct translation can be nuanced.
