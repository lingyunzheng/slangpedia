# Simplified Chinese Slang: "菜鸡" (cài jī)
**Definition:** 直译为“菜鸡”，指的是技术或能力较差的人或事物。类似于英文中的"simp"。
**Explanation:** 当某人在某些领域表现出明显的低水平、缺乏技巧或是做事情水平贼差的时候，可以用“菜鸡”来形容他，说起这种人常常连带使用这个词。在英文中，“simp”通常用来形容对某个特定人有强烈的单相思或不切实际的幻想，类似于中文中的“追星族”或“脑残粉”的意思。
**Example Usage:**
- 他写代码真的很菜鸡，简直是bug的制造机！
- 在追星的路上，他变成了菜鸡粉，为了偶像什么都愿意做。

# American English Slang: "simp"
**Definition:** A person who excessively and unreasonably admires or is in love with someone, often to the point of not seeing that person's flaws or even idealizing them.
**Explanation:** A "simp" is typically someone who has a strong, one-sided admiration or romantic interest in someone, often resulting in behavior that is perceived as weak, immature, or overly idealistic. "Simp" can also be used more broadly to describe someone who excessively admores someone and might go to extremes to gain their approval or attention.
**Example Usage:**
- He’s really a simp; he’s made a total fool of himself in front of her.
- The way he talks about her, you'd think she's a goddess—he’s such a classic simp.
