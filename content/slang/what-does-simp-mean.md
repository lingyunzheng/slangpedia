```markdown
## 平直 (Simp)
- **English:** Simp
- **Explanation:** A simp is a person who is overly or uncritically infatuated with someone, usually of the opposite gender, often to the point of acting in an aesthetically or behaviorally unappealing way because of their infatuation. They may have a strong desire to be seen as attractive or as a romantic interest by the object of their admiration.
**Example Usage:**  
- "He's a total simp for her, always trying to groom himself and make a good impression."  
- "Just because she's beautiful, doesn’t mean he has to be a simp about it."
```
