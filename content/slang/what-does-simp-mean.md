# Slang Term: Simp
## English Explanation
### Meaning
- A person who excessively and unwisely admires or tries to impress someone, especially a woman. Often associated with being overly affectionate or fawning.
### Example Usage
- "He's such a simp for her. Always sending her sweet notes and flowers."
## Chinese Explanation
### 含义
- 通常指一个对某人， especially 女人，表现出过度幻想或试图讨好、过分迷恋的人。经常与过于殷勤或逢迎有关。
### 例句
- "他简直就是对她的痴情种子，总是给她送甜言蜜语和花束。"
