## Slang Term: Simp
### English Explanation
A "simp" is a term commonly used in internet culture and social media to describe a guy who is extremely infatuated or overly attentive towards a woman. Often, the term is used in a derogatory or mocking sense, implying that the person might be幼稚 (yōuchì - childlike, immature) or overly devoted.
### Chinese Explanation
"simp" 通常用来描述在网络文化和社会媒体中对某位女性极度迷恋或过分关注的男性。这个词往往带有贬义或嘲笑的意味，暗示这个人的行为可能显得非常 童稚 (给孩子的感觉) 或者过于投入。
Example Usage:
- **English**: He’s such a simp for her social media posts.
- **Chinese**: 他对她的社交媒体帖子简直是 朝思暮想 (cáo sī mù xiǎng - dream about, pine for).
