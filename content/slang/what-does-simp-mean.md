# What does "simp" mean?（simp 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
A "simp" is a slang term that refers to a man who is overly or obsessively infatuated with and/or admires women, often to an extent that is considered excessive or foolish. He may be seen as flattering or ministering to women, implying a lack of self-confidence or independence.

**中文解释：**  
“simp”是一个网络用语，指那些对女性过度或近乎痴迷的仰慕和羡慕的男人，这种程度往往被认为是过度的或愚蠢的。他可能会被看作是对女性过于逢迎或迁就，暗示他缺乏自信或独立性。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: "Tom is such a simp for Sarah, he always chases her and writes her love letters."
   中文翻译: “汤姆简直就是一个背诵情书追女生的傻逼，总是追着莎拉跑。”

2) English sentence: "I can't believe he still acts like a simp after all the rejections."
   中文翻译: “我真不敢相信他被拒绝这么多回了，还是一副情场白痴的模样。”

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "simp" likely originated from internet culture, maybe from the verb "to simpleton," meaning to act like a fool or a sap. Over time, it evolved into a specific term to describe a man's excessive behavior in romantic pursuit.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. 心动浅薄者 (shànbó de xīndòng) - 浅薄，here referring to someone who is easily infatuated
2. 情场老宅男 (qíngchǎng lǎozánán) - 传统的直男，这里指在情感中表现得过于直白、不自信的男性
3. 追星仔 (zhuīxīng zǎi) - 追星的男性，“仔”是口语化的称呼，带有昵称意味
4. 情场墙头 (qíngchǎng qiángtóu) - 这个表达指的是在情感上表现得非常痴情，容易为情感所困的人
5. 哥们儿 (gēménr) - 这里指在情感上对女性过于迷恋的男性，带有一定的调侃意味
