```markdown
## Slang Term: Simp
**English:** A person who is overly eager to be perceived as romantic or attractive, often acting in an obsequious or childish manner towards women or individuals they find attractive. This behavior is seen as superficial and insincere.
**Traditional Chinese:** 稼軼 (jià yì)
- **解釋:** 一個/Area的人特別渴望被視為浪漫或吸引人，往往對女性或他們覺得有吸引力的人表現出奴顏戚態或孩子氣。這種行為被認為是膚浅而不真誠。
**Simplified Chinese:** 傀儡 (kuǐ lěi)
- **解釋:** 一个人特别渴望被看作是浪漫或吸引人的，往往对女性或他们觉得有吸引力的人表现得奴颜婢膝或孩子气。这种行为被认为是非常表面化的且不真诚的。
```
Note: The terms "稼軼" (jià yì) and "傀儡" (kuǐ lěi) are not direct translations of the term "simp" but are used here to provide a sense of the typical mannerisms associated with a "simp" in Chinese.
