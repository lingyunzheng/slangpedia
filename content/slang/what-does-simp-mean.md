# Slang Term: Simp
## English
**Meaning:** A term often used to describe a man who is overly eager to prove his romantic or sexual worth to a woman, often in a silly or excessive manner. This person might do things like buying expensive gifts or showing off in hopes of impressing a specific woman.
**Example:** "Why is he always saying he loves sushi? He's such a simp for his ex-girlfriend."
## Chinese
**意思:** 一个用来描述那种过于急切地想要证明自己在感情或性方面的价值的男人的词语，通常表现得有些傻气或过于夸张。这类人可能会通过送昂贵的礼物或在各方面炫耀自己，以期打动某个特定的女人。
**例句:** "他为什么总是说自己爱寿司？他就是被前女朋友迷住了。"
