# Slang Term: Simp
## English
**Definition:** A "simp" is a man or, in some cases, a woman who is overly affectionate or attentive to one person, often with the aim of impressing them romantically. They can be seen as bland and unoriginal in their attempts to show love or interest.
**Usage Example:** "Tom is such a simp when it comes to Sarah; he always tries to buy her expensive gifts just to win her over."
## 中文
**定义：** “simper”通常指的是一个男人，有时也指一个女人，对他喜欢的人表现出过度的体贴和关注，往往是为了在恋爱方面吸引对方。他们在示爱或展示兴趣时可能会显得单调乏味，没有新意。
**用法示例：** "汤姆对莎拉就是个simper，总是想送她贵重的礼物来博得她的青睐。"
