# Built Different
- **English:** This phrase is often used in casual conversation to describe someone or something that is unique or stands out in a distinctive way. It implies that the person or thing has a special quality that makes them different from the norm.
- **Chinese (简体中文):** 这个短语常用于非正式的对话中，用来描述一个人或某样东西以一种独特或突出的方式与众不同。它暗示这个人或东西具有某些特殊品质，使他们与众不同，不拘于常规。
- **Example in English:** "Man, he really is built different—must work out like six days a week!"
- **Example in Chinese (简体中文):** "天呐，他真的有点与众不同——每周必须锻炼六天吧！"
