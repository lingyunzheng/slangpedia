## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Built Different"
### English
**Meaning:** This phrase is used to describe someone who appears or is constructed uniquely or in a way that sets them apart from others in a physical, personality, or lifestyle sense.
**Example:** "Ever since I met him, I knew he was built different. His confidence is something else."
### Chinese
**意思:** 这个词组用来描述某人在物理上、个性上或者生活方式上以一种独特的方式存在或者与众不同。
**例子:** “自从认识他后，我就知道他与众不同。他的自信简直非同寻常。”
