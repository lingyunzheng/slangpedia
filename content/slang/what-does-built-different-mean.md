## Built Different
**English:**  
"Built different" is a slang phrase that indicates someone has a unique or unconventional appearance or physical attributes. It's often used to describe someone who stands out from the crowd due to their specific characteristics, such as height, build, or overall look, in a positive or neutral way. This phrase can be used to highlight individuality without implying any negative judgment.
**中文:**  
"Built different" 是俚语，用来描述某人的外貌或身体特征独特或不寻常。通常用来形容某人因为其特定的特征（如身高、体型或整体外观）而与众不同，是一种积极或中性的表达方式，目的是突出个性而非带有任何负面评判。
**Example:**
- **English:** That guy is built different; I can’t take my eyes off him!
- **中文:** 那个家伙长得完全不同，我根本无法移开目光！
