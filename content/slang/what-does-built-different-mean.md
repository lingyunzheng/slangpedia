## "built different" (English to Chinese)
### English Explanation
- **Meaning**: When someone is described as "built different," it means they have a different body type, usually implying a more muscular or athletic build compared to the general population. This term can also be used more generally to describe anyone who stands out physically.
### Chinese Explanation
- **Meaning**: 当某人被称为“built different”时，这意着他有不同于普通人的体型，通常指的是更健壮或更具运动感的身体类型。这个词也可以更广泛地用来形容任何在外貌上与众不同的个人。
- **例句**: John is built different—he's got those huge biceps and triceps that make him stand out in the gym.
- **Translation**: 约翰的体型与众不同——他那大得惊人的二头肌和三头肌让他在健身房里脱颖而出。
