```markdown
## Built Different
**English:**  
"You've got a unique physical build or attributes that set you apart from the norm. It's like saying you're 'built differently' compared to other people."
**中文解释：**  
你具有独特的身体构造或外貌特征，与常人有所不同。就像是说你“与众不同”或“特色鲜明”，和别人不一样。
```
