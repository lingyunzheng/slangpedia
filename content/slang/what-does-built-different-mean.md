# Built Different
## English
"Built different" is a slang phrase used to describe someone or something that is unique, unconventional, or seemingly out of the norm in terms of appearance or character. It often carries a positive connotation, emphasizing individuality and uniqueness.
### Example
- She's built different, with a vibrant personality that lights up the room.
## 中文
"built different" 是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某物在外观或性格方面与众不同、独一无二或非典型。这个说法通常带有积极的意义，强调个性和独特性。
### 例句
- 她与众不同，独特的个性让她仿佛能点亮整个房间。
