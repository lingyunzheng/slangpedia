# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "built different"
## English
**Meaning**: Describes someone or something that is perceived as strong, unique, or extraordinary in a physical or metaphorical sense. Often used to highlight remarkable physical features or abilities.
**Example**: "He's built different, that's why he's such a great athlete."
## 中文
**含义**：形容某人或某物在外界看来是强壮、独特的或异常出色的，通常用来强调显著的体格特征或能力。
**例句**："他长得不同寻常，所以成了一个很棒的运动员。"
