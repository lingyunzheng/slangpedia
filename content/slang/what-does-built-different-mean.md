# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Built Different"
## English
**Built Different**: This slang is used to describe someone or something that stands out from the norm, often in a positive way. It can mean that a person is unique in their physical attributes (like height, body type, or style) or that they have a unique set of skills or qualities that make them special. Essentially, it's a way to emphasize that someone is special in a distinctive manner.
**Example Usage**: "She's built different, with a body that just commands attention."
## 中文
**built different**：这个俚语用来形容一个人或事物与众不同，通常带有积极的意味。它可以指一个人在物理特征上（如身高、体型或风格）与众不同，也可以指他们拥有独特的技能或品质使之特别。总的来说，这是一种强调某人以独特方式特别的方式。
**示例用法**：“她被‘build different’了，身材格外引人注目。”
