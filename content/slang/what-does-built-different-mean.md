# Built Different Slang Explanation
## English
**Built Different:**  
This phrase, often used in casual and street slang, means someone or something is unique, unusual, or distinctive in their build, appearance, or character. It suggests that the person or thing stands out from the norm due to their physique, abilities, or overall presence.
**Example Usage:**
- "He's built different, muscled up and tall, for sure someone's boyfriend material!"
- "Her dance moves are built different, almost like she’s performing gymnastics!"
## 中文
**built different:**  
这个词组常用于休闲和街坊俚语中，意指某人或某物在体型、外观或性格上是独特、不寻常或与众不同的。它暗示某人或某事因体型、能力或整体存在方式而与众不同。
**例句：**
- "他的体格与众不同，肌肉发达又高大，绝对是某人的男友候选人！"
- "她的舞步与众不同，几乎就像是在做体操！"
