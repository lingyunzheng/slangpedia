# Built Different (Built Liètóng in Chinese)
**English Explanation:**  
"Built different" is a slang phrase commonly used to describe someone who is unique, has a distinctive appearance, or has a unique personality that sets them apart from others. It can also imply that someone is one-of-a-kind, non-conformist, or has a unique physical build. The term often carries a positive connotation, suggesting that the person is special or unique in some way, without necessarily being in a literal sense of having a different muscular structure.
**Chinese Explanation:**  
"Built different" 可以用 "Built liètóng" 来翻译，这个词通常用来描述那些独特、有鲜明个性或外貌独特的人。它还可以暗示某人与众不同、非主流或有着独特的体型。这个词通常带有正面的含义，表明某人是特别的或独特的，不一定是指肌肉结构上的不同。
