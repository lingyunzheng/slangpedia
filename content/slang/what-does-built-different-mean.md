```markdown
## Built Different
**English:** 
"Built different" is a slang term that describes someone as physically unique or unusual in their physique. It often implies that the person has a body type that is not considered standard or typical. 
**Chinese:** 
“Built different” 是一个俚语，用来描述某人具有独特或不寻常的体型。它通常意味着这个人的身材特征不符合标准或常见的标准。
```
