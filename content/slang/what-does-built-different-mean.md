# Built Different: 
**English:**  
"Built Different" is a slang term often used to describe someone who has an unconventional or unique way of presenting themselves, often through their appearance, demeanor, or overall style. It can also refer to someone who has a distinctive or unusual set of skills or attributes that set them apart from others.
**Chinese (翻译):**  
"Built Different" 是一个俚语，常用来形容某人以与众不同的方式展现自己，这可能体现在外貌、气质或整体风格上。此外，这个词也可以用来形容某人拥有独特或非同寻常的技能或特质，使他们与众不同。
