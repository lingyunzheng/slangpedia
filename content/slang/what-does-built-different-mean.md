## English Slang: Built Different
**Meaning:** Refers to someone who stands out physically or bio-physically due to distinct and noticeable characteristics. It can also imply uniqueness or a one-of-a-kind appearance that is different from the norm.
**Example Usage:** "That guy is built different, he's not your typical gym rat with bulging muscles, but he's got a unique, towering physique."
## 中文俚语: 建设得不一样
**意思:** 指某人在外貌或生理特征上与众不同，具有显著的、与众不同的特点。也可以暗示一种独一无二的外貌，不同于常规。
**例句:** “那个 guy 建设得不一样，他不是典型的健身房肌肉发达的那种，但他有着一种独特的、高大的体型。”
