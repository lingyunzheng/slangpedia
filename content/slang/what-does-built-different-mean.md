# Built Different
**English:**  
"Built Different" is a slang phrase used to describe someone or something that has a unique or unusual physical appearance. It often implies that the person or thing stands out in a positive way, resembling a more exotic or stylish build. For example, if someone has a distinctive body composition or a specific style of dressing, they could be described as "built different."
**中文:**  
"built different" 是一个俚语，用来形容那些身体结构或外貌与众不同的人或事物。通常，这个说法用于指某人的外貌或者体型特别吸引人或者有独特之处，给人一种时髦或独特的视觉效果。例如，如果某人的体型非常独特，或是有自己独特的穿衣风格，就可以说他们 "built different"。
