# "built different" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English Explanation
- **Meaning**: This phrase often refers to someone who is significantly different from others, usually in a way that sets them apart in a noticeable or unique manner. It can imply someone is unusual, unconventional, or has qualities that distinguish them from the norm.
- **Usage Example**: "Ever since he got those piercings, he's been built different from the rest of us."
## Chinese Explanation
- **意思**: 这个短语常用来形容一个人非常与众不同，通常是区别于其他人的一种明显或独特的方式。它可以暗示某人是不寻常的、非传统的，或者有使其与众不同的人格特质。
- **使用示例**: “自从他做了那些穿孔之后，他就显得和我们都不一样了。”
