## English Slang: "Built Different"
- **Meaning**: Refers to someone who looks or is built in a unique, unconventional, or unusual way. They might have an atypical physique, physical appearance, or even an unconventional personality that sets them apart from the crowd.
- **Example**: "Wow, that guy is built different! He's taller than everyone else and has a muscular physique, but his height adds to his unique charm."
## 中文俚语: "built different"
- **含义**: 描述某人的外貌或体型非常独特、不寻常或与众不同。他们可能有非典型的身材、外观，甚至个性独特，给人留下深刻印象。
- **例句**: "wow，那个小伙子长得真特别！他比其他人高很多，肌肉发达，但这种高度反而让他更有魅力。"
