# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Built Different"
## English
"Built Different" is a term often used to describe someone who has a unique or unusual appearance, physique, or personality. It suggests that the person stands out from the norm in a way that is distinctive and set apart. This phrase can be used positively to celebrate individuality, or negatively to mock someone's uniqueness.
**Example Usage:**
- "Sarah is truly built different, with her bright purple hair and wearable piercings, but that's what makes her so interesting!"
## Chinese
"建得不同" 这个词语通常用来形容一个人在外貌、体型或个性上有独特或不寻常的特点。它表明这个人以一种独特且与众不同的方式脱颖而出。此词可以用来积极地赞美独特的个性，也可以用来消极地嘲笑某人的独特性。
**例句：**
- “莎拉真是建得不同，她的鲜艳亮紫的头发和可穿戴的穿孔，但这正是她如此引人注目的原因！”
