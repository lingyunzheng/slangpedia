```markdown
## English: "Built Different"
### Explanation:
"Built Different" is an internet slang phrase often used to describe someone or something that is unique, special, or stands out from the crowd. It's used to emphasize that an individual or entity has a distinctive appearance, personality, or abilities that set them apart from others.
### Example Usage:
- "He's built different, his style is totally unique!"
- "Jenny is built different, not just physically but also in her mindset and approach to life."
## Chinese: "建造不同" (Zàojiàng Bútóng)
### 解释:
"建造不同"（zàojiàng bóutóng）是一个网络流行语，用来形容某人或某物独特、特别或与常人不同。它强调某个人或实体在外观、个性或能力方面与众不同。
### 例句:
- "他建造不同，他的风格完全独特！"
- "珍妮建造不同，不仅在身体上，也在她的思维方式和生活方法上。"
```
In the Chinese context, "建造不同" is used creatively to capture the essence of the English slang phrase "built different". However, it's worth noting that "建造" (zàojiàng) literally means "construction" and in this case, it's metaphorically used to convey the meaning of being unique or distinctive.
