## Built Different
**English Explanation:** "Built different" is a slang term commonly used to describe someone who stands out from the crowd or is unique in appearance or character. It implies that this person has a distinctive set of attributes that make them different from the average or typical person. This slang can be used positively to highlight uniqueness or negatively to point out someone's distinctive, possibly odd characteristics.
**Chinese Explanation:** “built different” 是一种常用的俚语，用来描述那些与众不同的、具有独特外观或个性的人。这个词组暗示这个人具有一系列独特的特征，使他与众不同。它既可以用来正面强调独特性，也可以用于负面地指出某人的独特、可能古怪的特点。
