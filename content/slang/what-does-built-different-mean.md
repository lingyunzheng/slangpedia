## Built Different
**English Slang: Built Different**
- **Explanation:** This phrase is often used to describe someone who has a unique and distinctive physical appearance or build. It implies that the person is not typical or conforming to the usual standards or stereotypes.
- **Example Usage:** "He's built different, no doubt about it. He's got muscles that look like he could lift the world!"
**中文俚语: 身材与众不同**
- **解释:** 这个短语用来形容某人的体型或外貌与众不同或非同寻常。它意味着这个人不同于常规标准或刻板印象。
- **使用示例:** “他身材与众不同，这一点毫无疑问。他的肌肉看起来像是能举起世界一样强大。”
