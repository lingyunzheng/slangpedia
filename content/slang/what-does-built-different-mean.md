## Built Different
**English:**  
"Built different" is a slang phrase commonly used to describe someone or something that stands out from the norm, either physically or in terms of personality, capabilities, or attributes. It suggests that the individual or entity is unique or extraordinary in some way.
**中文:**  
"Built different" 是一个俚语，用来形容某人或某物与常人或常态有所不同，无论是从身体上还是个性、能力或特征方面。它暗示这个人或事物在某些方面是独特或格外出色的。
