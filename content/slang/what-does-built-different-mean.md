# Built Different
**English:**  
"Built Different" is a phrase often used to describe someone who is unique in their physical appearance, personality, or lifestyle. It implies that this person stands out from the norm and does not fit into typical categories. 
**Chinese:**  
"built different" 指的是某人在外貌、性格或生活方式上与其他人大不相同，与众不同，不按常理出牌。这句话用来形容一个人的独特之处，暗示此人与大多数人截然不同。
