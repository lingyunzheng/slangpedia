```markdown
# "Built Different" (简体中文: “固若金汤” / 英文: "Built Different")
**英文解释:**
- **"Built Different"** means someone or something has a unique or unconventional structure, appearance, or functionality compared to typical examples. It often implies a sense of resilience, uniqueness, or innovation.
**中文解释:**
- **“固若金汤”** 指某人或某物具有独特的或非传统的结构、外观或功能，与其他典型的例子不同。这个词常常用来形容具有极强抵抗力、独特性或创新精神的人或事物。
```
```markdown
# "Built Different" (简体中文: “与众不同” / 英文: "Built Different")
**英文解释:**
- **"Built Different"** is often used to describe someone who is not like others in a positive way—their unique qualities or appearance make them stand out and set them apart from the norm. This term can be applied to almost anything from fashion to personality traits.
**中文解释:**
- **“与众不同”** 用来形容某人或某物有独特的品质或外观，使他们不同于常人或主流。这个词可以应用于几乎所有方面，从时尚到个性特征。
```
```markdown
# "Built Different" (简体中文: “特立独行” / 英文: "Built Different")
**英文解释:**
- **"Built Different"** can also refer to someone who goes against the norm, isn't conformist, and has a distinct style or approach that sets them apart. This can be about their attitude, lifestyle, or even the way they approach work.
**中文解释:**
- **“特立独行”** 用来描述那些不遵循常规、不随大流、有自己独特风格和方法的人。这可以体现在他们的态度、生活方式或工作方式上。
```
