# Built Different
## English
**Built Different**: This slang term is often used to describe someone who is unique or has a distinct appearance or personality. It can also imply that the person has a different physical build, often in a way that's impressive or striking.
*Example:* "He's built different, with broad shoulders and chiseled abs!"
