# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Built Different"
## English
**Meaning:** To be physically built or shaped in a unique or unusual way, often emphasizing an unconventional or striking appearance. This can be used to describe someone's body type, or how they present themselves.
**Example Sentence:** "That guy over there is built different—so muscular and tall!"
## Chinese
**意思:** 特别强调体格或外貌上独一无二或不寻常的特征。这个词可以用来形容某人的体型或他们如何展示自己。
**例句:** “那边那个人长得特别不一样——又高又壮！”
**英文-中文对照：**
- Built different - 特别不一样
- So muscular and tall - 又高又壮
