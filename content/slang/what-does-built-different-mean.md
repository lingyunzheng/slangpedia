## Built Different 🌍
**English:**  
"Built different" is a slang phrase used to describe someone who is unique, not fitting into the norm, or standing out in a specific way. This could be in terms of appearance, attitude, skills, or any characteristic that makes a person stand out from others. The phrase suggests that the person has something special or remarkable about them.
**Chinese:**  
"built different" 用来形容某人是独特的、与众不同的，或以某种特定的方式突出。这可能是从外貌、态度、技能或任何让他们与其他人大不相同的特点方面来描述的。这句话表明这个人在某些方面有特别或杰出之处。
