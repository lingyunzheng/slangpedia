## "Built Different" (Built Different)
### English Explanation:
- **Meaning:** Describes someone or something that is unique, unconventional, or outside the norm.
- **Usage:** Often used to highlight someone's distinctive or individual qualities. For example, "He's built different, with a style all his own."
### Chinese Explanation:
- **意思:** 形容某人或某物是独特的、非典型的或与众不同的。
- **用法:** 常用来强调某人的独特或个人特质。例如，“他天生与众不同，风格独具一格。”
---
### Example Sentences:
#### English:
- They have a unique sense of style that makes them stand out. They're built different.
- Being built different means you get to express yourself in a way others can't.
#### Chinese:
- 他们有一种独特的风格感，使他们与众不同。他们天生与众不同。
- 生来与众不同意味着你可以用自己的方式去表达自己，而别人做不到这一点。
