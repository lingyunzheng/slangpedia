## Built Different
### English Explanation
"Built different" is a slang expression used to describe someone who is unique or exceptional in a way that sets them apart from others, often in a positive sense. It can also be used to highlight how someone is physically distinctive or has a special ability.
### Example Usage
- "John is built different, which is probably why he's so good at weightlifting."
- "Every artist is built different, with their own unique style and vision."
### Chinese Explanation
“built different”是一个俚语表达，用来形容某人独特或杰出，这种独特之处让其与其他人大不相同，通常是正面意义上的描述。它也可以用来强调某人在身体上与众不同或拥有特殊的能力。
### 示例用法
- “约翰长得与众不同，可能这就是他为什么在举重方面特别擅长的原因。”
- “每个艺术家都与众不同，他们都有自己独特的风格和视角。”
