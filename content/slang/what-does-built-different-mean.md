# Built Different
**English:**  
"Built different" is an idiomatic expression used to describe a person or thing that is unique, distinctive, or unconventional in nature. It typically implies that there's something special or noteworthy about the individual or object that sets them apart from others in a significant way. This phrase can be used to emphasize a person's unique appearance, personality, or abilities.
**Chinese:**  
"built different"（built different）用来形容人或事物具有独特、特别或非传统的特征。通常这个词组用来强调某个人或物体在重要方面与众不同，使其显得特别或值得注意。这句话可以用来形容一个人的独特外貌、性格或能力。
