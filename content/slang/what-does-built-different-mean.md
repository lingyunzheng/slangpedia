# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Built Different"
## English
**"Built different"** is an informal slang term often used to describe someone who has a unique appearance, body type, or personality that's distinct from what's considered typical or average. It's often used positively to highlight individuality and uniqueness.
Example:
- "He's built different, but that's what makes him special."
## Mandarin
**“built different”** 是一个非正式的俚语，用来描述那些外观、体型或个性与常人截然不同的 person。这个词常被用来强调个人的独特性和独特之处。
例子：
- 他 **built different**，但这就是他特别的地方。
