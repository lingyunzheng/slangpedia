## English: Built Different
### Chinese: 建设不同 (jiàn shè xié tóng)
**Explanation:**
"Built Different" is a term often used to describe someone or something that stands out, is unique, or has a non-conformist approach. It implies an individual has a distinctive style or way of doing things that sets them apart from others. The term is often used in a positive sense, highlighting uniqueness and individuality.
Example Usage:
- "She's built different, with her unconventional fashion sense and creative spirit."
- "His business model is built different, focusing on sustainable practices and community engagement."
```
## English: Built to Last
### Chinese: 建立持久 (jiàn lì cháng jǐ)
**Explanation:**
"Built to Last" means something is designed or constructed to have a long and enduring life. It often refers to quality construction or a well-crafted item that can withstand the test of time and continue to function effectively. It can also be used metaphorically to describe people or ideas that are robust and enduring.
Example Usage:
- "This furniture is built to last, with strong construction and high-quality materials."
- "Her reputation for integrity is built to last; she is known for staying true to her principles."
