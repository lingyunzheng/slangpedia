## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Built Different
- **English:** "Built Different" is a phrase often used to describe someone or something that stands out in terms of appearance, quality, or personality. It emphasizes uniqueness or being distinctive.
- **Chinese:** "built different" (与众不同) 通常用来形容某人或者某事物在外观、质量或个性上与众不同，强调独特性或与众不同之处。
