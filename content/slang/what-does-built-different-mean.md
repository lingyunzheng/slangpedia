## Built Different
**English:**  
"Built different" is a slang phrase often used to describe someone who is uniquely or unusually attractive or strong. It can also imply that someone's physical appearance, skills, or personality stand out from the norm in a noticeable way.
**中文:**  
"Built different" 是一种俚语，用来形容某人具有独特的或异常的吸引力或强壮。也可以表示某人的外貌、技能或个性从常人中脱颖而出，显得与众不同。
