# Built Different
**English:**  
Refers to someone or something that is quite unique or distinctive, often in a way that sets them apart from the norm. It's a way of expressing that something or someone has a unique structure, ability, or quality that might not be immediately apparent but makes them special in their own right.
**Chinese:**  
指某人或某事物非常独特或与众不同，往往以一种方式将其与常态区别开来。这是表达某人或某事物具有独特的结构、能力和品质，这种特点可能并不显而易见，但却使他们在其独特的方式上变得特别。
