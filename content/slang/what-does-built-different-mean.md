## English Slang: "Built Different"
"Built different" is a phrase used in modern slang, particularly in urban and internet culture. It can mean a few things:

1. **Physically Different**: The person is literally built in a way that sets them apart. They might be exceptionally tall, short, muscular, or have a unique body aesthetic.

2. **Casually Descriptive**: It's a more casual and positive way to say someone stands out or has a distinctive look or feature that makes them noticeable.

Example:
- "Man, he’s built different, that’s why he always stands out in the crowd."

## 中文俚语: "built different"
"built different" 是现代俚语中，特别是在城市文化和互联网文化中常用的一种表达。它的意思可以有几种：

1. **身体构造不同**：这个词组用来形容一个人在生理上与众不同，比如特别高、特别矮、肌肉发达或有独特的体形特点。

2. **非正式描述**：这是一种更随意且积极的说法，用来形容某人与众不同或有独特的外貌特征，使其容易被注意到。

例句：
- "他长得很特别，所以他总是能在人群中脱颖而出。"
