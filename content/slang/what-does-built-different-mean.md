# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "built different"
## English Explanation
- **Meaning**: Refers to someone or something that is unique in a way that sets them apart from others. It can describe physical appearance, personality, abilities, or characteristics that are distinctly different and noticeable.
- **Usage**: Often used to highlight unusual or distinctive qualities in a positive or neutral context.
- **Example**: "That guy is built different – he's taller and has a deliberately rugged look."
## Chinese Explanation
- **意思**: 指某人或某事物在某种方面与众不同，从中可以突出出人意料、独特或显著的不同之处。它可以描述物理外貌、性格、能力或特性，这些特性明显不同且值得注意。
- **用法**: 经常用于突出不同之处时的正面或中立语境。
- **例子**: “那个人的模样很特别，又高大、还有种故意的粗犷感。”
