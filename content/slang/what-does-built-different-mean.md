# "Built Different" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
- **Meaning**: This phrase is typically used to describe someone who is unique, stands out, or has a distinctive style or personality.
- **Usage**: It's commonly used in casual conversation to praise someone for having a unique or different, cool vibe.
Example: "He totally built different! That tattoo is sick!"
## Chinese
- **意思**: 这个词组常用来形容某人是独一无二的、有鲜明特色或个性非常鲜明的人。
- **用法**: 通常在非正式场合用来赞美某人有独特的或不同的个性或风格。
例句: "他真是built different! 那个文身可真酷！"
