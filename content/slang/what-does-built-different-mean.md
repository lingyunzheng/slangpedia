# Built Different
## English Explanation
"Built different" is a slang term often used to describe someone who is unique, unconventional, or doesn't fit in with the norm. It suggests that the individual has characteristics, abilities, or traits that make them stand out. This term can be used to express admiration for someone's uniqueness or to acknowledge that someone is just not like everyone else.
**Example Usage:**
- "Jaden is built different, always coming up with creative solutions that no one else thinks of."
## Chinese Explanation
"built different" 这个俚语常用于描述那些独特、特立独行或不符合常人的个体。这句话表明这个人的特征、能力或特质使他们与众不同。这句话既可以用来表达对一个人的独特性的赞赏，也可以用来承认某人就是与众不同。
**例句：**
- "贾登就是不一样，总是想出其他人想不到的创新解决方案。"
