## Built Different
**English:** 
"Built Different" is a slang phrase used to describe someone or something that stands out from the norm, often because of their unique personality, appearance, or abilities. It can also be used to emphasize that someone is special or has qualities that make them exceptional.
**中文：**
"Built Different" 是一个俚语，用来描述那些与常人不同的个体或事物，通常是由于其独特的性格、外表或能力。这个词还可以用来强调某人是特别的，或者具有使其与众不同的特殊品质。
