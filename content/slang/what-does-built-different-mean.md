# "Built Different" - Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:**
- **Meaning:** This phrase is often used to describe someone who is unique or stands out from others in a distinctive way, often in a positive sense.
- **Example Usage:** "Man, he's really built different. His style is so out there!"
**汉语:**
- **含义:** 这个短语用来描述某人以独特或与众不同的方式突出，通常是积极意义上的。
- **例句:** "哇，他真是个性独特。他的风格太特立独行了！"
