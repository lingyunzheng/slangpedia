# "Built Different" - Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English Explanation
"Built different" is an American slang phrase often used to describe someone who is unique, has a distinct appearance, or carries themselves in a way that makes them stand out from the crowd. It can also refer to someone who has a different build, such as being taller, shorter, or having a more muscular physique compared to the general population. This phrase emphasizes individuality and unique characteristics.
Example: "He's built different, with his towering height and chiseled muscles, he really stands out in the crowd."
## Chinese Explanation
"Built different" 的美式俚语解释通常用来描述某人是独一无二的，有着与众不同的外貌，或者举止方面与其他人大不相同。这个词也可以用来指某人的体型与众不同，比如更高、更矮或肌肉更加发达。这个短语强调的是个性和独特的特征。
例句：他是个与众不同的人，高大的身材和雕刻般的肌肉让他在人群中显得格外突出。
