# "Built Different" (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
## English
**Built Different**: This phrase is often used to describe someone or something that stands out from the crowd. It implies that the individual or thing has unique qualities, features, or abilities that make them special or unusual. For example, "She's really built different; she's not like the other athletes I've seen."
## Chinese
**built different**（与众不同）: 这个短语常用来形容某人或某物与其他的截然不同。它意味着这个个体或事物具有独特的品质、特征或能力，使它们与众不同或特殊。例如，“她真的很与众不同；她和其他我见过的运动员不一样。”
---
**Note**: The term "built different" often carries a positive connotation, implying uniqueness and special qualities rather than literal physical differences. The context is crucial for fully understanding the meaning and intent.
