```markdown
### Built Different
**English:**  
To be physically strong or distinctive in appearance, often in a way that sets someone apart from others. This phrase is often used to describe someone who is physically fit, perhaps with a defined physique or striking features.
**Chinese:**  
身体强壮或者在外貌上与众不同，通常以一种使人与众不同的方式。这句话经常用来形容某人身体健壮，或许有棱角分明的体型或引人注目的特征。
```
Note: While "built different" can have slightly different connotations in slang usage, the core idea often revolves around physical strength or distinctive appearance. The provided explanations try to capture this essence.
