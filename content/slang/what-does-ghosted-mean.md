## Ghosted (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
**English Definition:**
To unilaterally end a relationship or communication without any explanation or further contact.
**Chinese Explanation:**
被断绝联系，没有给出任何解释或后续联系。简单来说，就是突然没音信了，仿佛消失了一样。
**Example Usage:**
- In English: She ghosted me after we had a fight.
- 在中文：我们吵架后，她直接ghost了我。
