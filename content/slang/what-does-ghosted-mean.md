```markdown
# Ghosted
## English
**Definition:** To abruptly and unexpectedly stop communicating with someone without explanation or any further contact.
## Chinese
**定义：** 突然且没有解释地停止与某人沟通，不留任何联系方式。
**例子：**
- English: She just ghosted me after our first date. No calls, no texts, nothing.
- Chinese: 她在第一次约会之后突然不理我了，没有任何电话或信息。
```
