## Ghosted (English)
**Explanation:**
"Ghosted" is a modern slang term often used in the context of online and offline relationships. When someone is "ghosted," it means they suddenly stop communicating with another person without any explanation or warning. This can be seen as a passive-aggressive or deceptive way of ending a relationship or disengaging from a conversation or interaction.
**Example Usage:**
- "After six months of back-and-forth, he just stopped responding to my texts. He must have been ghosted."
- "I thought we were good friends, but then one day she just stopped answering my calls. Classic ghosting!"

## 被幽灵化 (中文)
**解释：**
“被幽灵化”（ghosted）是现代流行的一种俚语，通常用于在线和线下关系中。当有人被“幽灵化”时，这意味着他们突然无任何解释或警告地停止与另一人沟通。这可能是一种消极被动或有欺骗性的结束关系或停止交流或互动的方式。
**例句：**
- “交往了六个个月后，他突然就不再回复我的短信了。他肯定是被幽灵化了。”
- “我还以为我们是好朋友，但她有一天突然不再接我的电话。典型的被幽灵化。”
