## Ghosted
**English Slang Explanation:** 
To ghost someone means to suddenly and secretly stop communicating with them without any explanation. It's like disappearing without a trace, leaving the other person in the dark about what went wrong in the relationship or friendship.
**中文俚語解説：**
“ghosted” 指的是一种突然且秘密地停止与他人沟通的行为，而完全不提供任何解释。这就好比突然人间蒸发，让对方完全不知道发生了什么，也让对方陷入一种迷茫的状态。
---
例句用法：
* "I thought we were still on, but she just ghosted me after the last call."
* 原文翻译：我以为我们还挺在状态的，但她就是在最后一次通话后突然就不再联系我了。
* "He ghosted them so quickly that they didn't even notice he was gone."
* 原文翻译：他消失得如此迅速，以至于他们甚至都没有意识到他离开。
