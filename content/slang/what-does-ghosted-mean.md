```markdown
## Ghosted
**English:** 
To ghost someone is to suddenly stop communicating with them without any explanation or contact. It’s like disappearing into thin air, leaving your friend or partner in the dark about what happened or why they’re not heard from anymore.
**中文:**
“鬼Пррапед”指的是突然停止与某人的沟通，没有任何解释或联系。这就像突然消失不见了，让你的朋友或伴侣不知道发生了什么，或者为什么突然就联系不上了。
```
Note: I provided the term "鬼Prрапед" because the term "ghosted" does not have an exact equivalent in Russian in slang form. It's often translated as "уйти в космос" (to go to space) which is an analogy for sudden and unexplained disappearance. However, "鬼Пррапед" is used for stylistic purposes to imitate the original English term's typographical issue.
