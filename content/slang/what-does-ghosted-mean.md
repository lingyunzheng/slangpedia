# Ghosted
## English
"Ghosted" refers to the situation where someone suddenly stops communicating with another person for no apparent reason, often leading to feelings of rejection or abandonment. It's a way of cutting ties abruptly, and is commonly seen in digital contexts like dating apps, social media, or online chats.
**Example:**  
"Sarah ghosted me after we had a great conversation, never responding to my messages again. I really hoped it wasn't because of something I said."
## 中文
"被Ghost"是指某人突然不回复另一方的消息，而且没有给出任何原因，通常会导致被对方抛弃或遗忘的感觉。这是一种单方面断绝联系的方式，常见于数字交流中，比如交友软件、社交媒体或在线聊天。
**示例：**  
"萨拉和我曾经有过很棒的交谈，但她从不再回应我的消息，最后干脆把我拉黑了。我真的希望不是因为我说了什么才这样的。"
