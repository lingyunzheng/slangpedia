# Ghosted
**English:**  
"Ghosted" is a term used when someone suddenly stops communicating with you without a good explanation, essentially disappearing or ignoring you completely. It's like they've "disappeared like a ghost," leaving the other person hanging.
**中文:**  
"Ghosted" 是一个用来形容某人突然停止与你沟通，没有给出合理解释，就像是“消失得无影无踪”或“置之不理”的表达方式。就像被鬼魂带走了一样，留你一个人原地等待。
Example Usage:
- English: After we started dating, he just stopped texting me and ignoring my calls and messages. He ghosted me.
- 中文：当我们正式开始交往后，他突然停止了所有的信息往来，无视我的电话和信息。他被我 ghosted 了。
