```markdown
### Ghosted
- **English:** "Ghosted" refers to the act of suddenly and without explanation cutting off all forms of communication with someone, often in a romantic or social context. It is seen as a rude and abrupt way to end a relationship.
- **Chinese (简体中文):** "被 ghost 了" 指的是一种突然且没有解释地中断一切形式的联系，通常是在恋爱或社交关系中的一种行为。这种行为被视为一种粗鲁且不礼貌的结束关系的方式。
- **Explanation:** 比如，某人在没有给出任何理由的情况下，不再回复你的信息、电话或任何社交互动，这就意味着他们“ghosted”了你。
```
```markdown
### 被 ghost 了
- **English Explanation:** "Ghosts you" means abruptly ending all forms of communication with someone, often in the context of terminating a relationship quickly and without providing any explanation. It is considered a rude and abrupt way to end things.
- **Example:** If you reach out to someone and they stop all forms of communication without any explanation, you could say you have "been ghosted."
```
```markdown
### 例句
- **Ghosted example (English):**
- "I guessed John ghosted me after he didn't reply to any of my texts for the past week."
- "She was so confused when she realized she had been ghosted; one minute he was all over her, and the next, nothing."
- **被 ghost 了的例子 (中文):**
- "我猜 John 在过去一周都没有回复我的任何信息后把我 ghost 了。"
- "她完全不明白自己为什么会被 ghost，前一秒他还对她很热情，下一秒就没有任何消息了。"
```
