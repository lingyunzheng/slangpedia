## Ghosted (英语)
### Explanation
"Ghosted" is a slang term that emerged in the digital age, particularly referring to a situation where someone suddenly stops communicating or interacting with another person without any explanation. It can imply abandonment or a cold, abrupt end to a relationship, whether it be personal or professional. The term "ghost" metaphorically describes how a person disappears without a trace, akin to a ghost looming in the background of a memory.
### Example Usage
- "After months of chatting, she just stopped responding to my messages last week—clearly a ghosting situation."

## 幻影 (中文)
### 解释
“幻影”是在数字时代流行起来的一种网络用语，特指某人在突然间停止与另一个人的交流或互动，而没有任何解释的情况下消失不见。这种情况往往暗示着被冷落或被无情地抛弃，无论是个人关系还是职业关系。用“鬼”这个词来形容某人突然无影无踪，就像一个飘忽不定的幽灵，在回忆中留下挥之不去的影子。
### 示例用法
- “我们经常通过消息聊天，但她在上个星期突然不再回应我的消息，明显是被‘幻影’了。”
