## Ghosted
### English
**Meaning**: When someone suddenly stops responding to you, loses interest in you, or disappears without any explanation.
**Example**: "After dating for a month, I completely got ghosted when I didn't hear from them for a week."
### 中文
**含义**: 当某人突然停止回你的消息、失去对你的兴趣，或者没有给出任何解释就消失不见时。
**例句**："当我们有一个月没有消息之后，我被彻底晾在一旁了，这属于被Ghosted。"
