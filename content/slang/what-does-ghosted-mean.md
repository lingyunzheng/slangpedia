## Ghosted
**English Explanation:**
To ghost someone means to unexpectedly end a relationship or communication with them without any notice or explanation. It's like vanishing without a trace, leaving the other person in the dark completely.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
"Ghosted" 指突然和某人结束关系或中断沟通，没有任何通知或解释。就像突然消失，不留任何消息，让对方完全摸不着头脑。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** I thought we were on good terms, but she ghosted me after the meeting.
- **Chinese:** 我本来以为我们关系不错，但在会议后她就ghosted我了。
