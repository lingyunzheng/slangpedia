## Ghosted (Ghosted)
**English:** To suddenly stop all communication with someone without any explanation.
**Simplified Chinese:** 突然不联系某人并且不给出任何解释。
**Explanation:**
- **English:** When someone you've been talking to suddenly stops responding to your messages, calls, or posts and does not give you a reason, it means they have "ghosted" you. This can leave the other person feeling confused, hurt, or angry.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 如果你一直在和某人聊天，但他们突然停止回应你的信息、电话或帖子，并且没有给出理由，这就说明他们对你“失联”了。这会让对方感到困惑、受伤或是生气。
**Example Sentences:**
- **English:** After a month of text messages, John completely ghosted Mary.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 在一个月的短信交流过后，约翰完全失联了 Mary。

## Ghosted (Ghosts)
**English:** To suddenly stop all communication with someone without any explanation.
**Simplified Chinese:** 突然不联系某人并且不给出任何解释。
**Explanation:**
- **English:** When someone you've been talking to suddenly stops responding to your messages, calls, or posts and does not give you a reason, it means they have "ghosted" you. This can leave the other person feeling confused, hurt, or angry.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 如果你一直在和某人聊天，但他们突然停止回应你的信息、电话或帖子，并且没有给出理由，这就说明他们对你“失联”了。这会让对方感到困惑、受伤或是生气。
**Example Sentences:**
- **English:** She had no idea why he ghosted her until he decided to message her back a week later.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 她不知道他为什么会失联，直到一周后他给她发了消息。
