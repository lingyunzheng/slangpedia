```markdown
# Ghosted
**English:**  
To ghost someone means to abruptly end a relationship or interaction without providing any explanation or communication. It's like disappearing into thin air. Usually, it's perceived as a very rude and hurtful way to end things.
**Chinese:**  
被玩失踪了 (pī bèi wán móxiāngxǐ le)  
意思是指突然中断关系或互动，而不进行任何解释或沟通。就好像消失了一样。通常被视为一种非常粗鲁且伤人的方式结束关系。
Example Usage in a Sentence:
- **English:** They ghosted me the day after I bought them flowers and wrote them a love letter.
- **Chinese:** 他们在我给他们买花并写情书的第二天就玩失踪了。
```
