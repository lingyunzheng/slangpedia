## Ghosted
**English:**  
To ghost someone means to suddenly and un Expectedly end a relationship (often a romantic one) and to stop communicating with that person entirely, leaving them in the dark about what happened. It's like disappearing into thin air, leaving no explanation or communication behind.
**Simplified Chinese:**  
被幽灵化（ghosted）意味着突然并出乎意料地终止一段关系（通常是浪漫关系），并完全停止与对方的沟通，不给对方任何解释或反馈。就像突然消失在空气中，没有任何说明。
**Phrases and Examples:**
- "She just ghosted me after our date. I don't know why."
- 她在我们约会后突然就消失不见了，我不知道她为什么这么做。
- "I was hurt when my ex ghosted me without any explanation."
- 当我的前男友在没有任何解释的情况下消失时，我很受伤。
- "Just don't ghost people, it's not nice."
- 不要突然消失不管人，这样做不太友善。
