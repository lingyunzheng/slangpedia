## English: Ghosted
### Definition
- To suddenly and inexplicably stop communicating with someone without any response to their messages, calls, or attempts to contact.
### Explanation
- Imagine you're playing a video game, and your friend just vanishes without logging off or leaving a message. That's kind of what it feels like when someone gets "ghosted."
### Example
- After a few weeks of weekly dates, John never texted or called back, effectively *ghosting* the relationship.
## 中文: 被玩消失了 (Ghosted)
### 定义
- 急剧且解释不清地停止与某人的交流，对他们的信息、电话或联系方式没有任何回应。
### 解释
- 想象一下你正在玩一个游戏，你的朋友突然间就不见人影了，既没有下线，也没有留下任何消息。这就是“被玩消失了”给人的感觉。
### 例句
- 经过几周的周约会，约翰再也没有回复或联系过她，于是她感觉自己被“玩消失了”了。
