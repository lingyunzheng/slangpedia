## Ghosted (英语)
### 英文解释
"Ghosted" is a term that originally referred to the practice of suddenly cutting off all contact with someone without any explanation. In modern usage, it’s often applied to digital contexts like social media, texting, or dating apps where someone abruptly stops responding or communicating, leaving the other person hanging.
### 中文解释
"鬼混"是一个源于英语的网络用词，最初指的是突然与某人完全没有联系，不作任何解释的行为。在现代用法中，它常常用来描述在社交媒体、短信或约会应用中，某人突然停止回复或沟通，让对方不知所措的情况。
