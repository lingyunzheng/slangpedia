## Ghosted (中文释义: 被玩忽)
- **Definition**: To suddenly stop communicating with someone without any explanation, leading them to become unaware of the friendship or relationship.
- **Bilingual Explanation**:
- **中文解释**: 突然中断与某人的所有沟通且没有任何解释的行为，导致对方对该友谊或关系一无所知。
- **Usage Example**: "I tried reaching out to her last week, but she ghosted me after our conversation ended."
- **Context**: Commonly used in casual conversations about social interactions or relationships, often when someone feels lied to or betrayed by another person's quick disengagement.
- **Derived Terms**: 
- **Ghosting someone**: 害某人不知所踪
