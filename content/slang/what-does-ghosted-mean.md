## Ghosted
### English 
To ghost someone is when you suddenly stop all communication with them without any explanation or notice, essentially leaving them in the dark. It's like you've disappeared into thin air, rendering them clueless about what went wrong in your relationship.
### 中文 
"ghosted" 指的是你突然中断与某人的所有交流，不给予任何解释或通知，让他们一头雾水。这就像是你突然间消失不见，让他们无法知道你们之间的关系出了什么问题。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "I know we had a fight, but I didn't expect her to ghost me like that."
- **中文:** "我知道我们当时有冲突，但她居然就这样ghost了我，我也感到很意外。"
### Variation
- English: "Ghosting" can also be used in a broader sense, to describe discreetly ending any form of contact, like ghosting a business deal or a social engagement.
- 中文: "Ghosting" 这个词也可以用来描述有策略地终止任何形式的联系，比如 ghost 一个商业交易或社交邀约。
