## Ghosted
### English
**Definition:** When someone suddenly stops communicating with you without any explanation or reason, essentially disappearing or "ghosting" you.
**Example:** "After a week of back-and-forth text messages, John never replied and ghosted me. I thought we had a good connection, but now I'm just left wondering what I did wrong."
### Chinese
**定义:** 当有人在没有任何解释或理由的情况下突然停止与你沟通，把你“拉黑”或“消失”掉的行为。
**例句:** "我和John短信往来了一个星期，他后来就再也没有回复，彻底失联了。原本以为我们聊得挺投机的，现在只能自己猜他在生我的气了。"
