```markdown
## Ghosted
**中文释义：** 被鬼魂附体；被抛弃或忽视。
**英文解释:** To be ghosted means that someone suddenly and unexpectedly cuts off all contact, leaving the other person feeling left in the dark. It's a form of emotional and psychological abandonment, often used in the context of casual dating or romantic relationships.
**Example Usage:**
- 中文例句：自从那次愉快的晚餐后，她就再也没跟我联系了，这让我感到被鬼魂附体。
- 英文例句：After that nice dinner, she simply vanished and hasn't been in touch,ghosting me completely.
```
