## Ghosted
- **English Explanation**: To ghost someone is to suddenly stop communicating with them without any explanation, leaving them in the dark about what happened. It's like disappearing into thin air, leaving them wondering what they did wrong.
- **Chinese Explanation**: "被幽灵附身" (bèi yōulíng fùshēn) 指突然与某人断绝联系，没有任何解释地消失不见了，留给他们许多疑问。就像被幽灵附身一样神秘莫测，让人找不到踪迹。
---
使用示例：
- 英文：She totally ghosted me after our date!
- 中文：她在我约会后完全失联了！
- 英文：He got ghosted by his ex-girlfriend after a week of ignoring all his calls and messages.
- 中文：他被前女友幽灵附身后，一个星期都不接他的电话和信息。
- 英文：It feels like getting ghosted every time you try messaging them, and they never respond.
- 中文：每次尝试给他们发信息，他们却没有任何回应，感觉就像是被幽灵附身了一样。
