## Ghosted
- **English Explanation**: When someone abruptly cuts off all communication with you without any explanation or warning, essentially disappearing into thin air. It's like they've been playing the longest, quietest game of hide and seek and have chosen to stay hidden forever.
- **Chinese Explanation**：当某个人突然切断与你的所有联系，没有任何解释或前兆，就像是隐身了一样消失不见。这就像他们玩着最漫长、最安静的捉迷藏游戏，选择了永远保持隐身状态。
- **Example of Usage**：
- English: "I can't believe they ghosted me after hanging out for months. What a jerk!"
- Chinese：真不敢相信他们在我们一起度过了几个月之后就ghost了我。真卑鄙！
- **Related Terms**：
- "Ghosting" can be seen as a playful or sneakier cousin of the old-fashioned "dumping," which often involves a more direct conversation or explanation.
- "Ghosted" is sometimes used in the form of "to ghost," indicating an action of cutting all ties and contact with another person.
## Related Slang Phrases
- "Why'd you ghost me?" （ Why did you ghost me?） - "你为什么突然不联系我了？"
- "Ghosting is so in these times." （ Ghosting is so in these times.） - "现在的潮流就是ghost一下别人。"
