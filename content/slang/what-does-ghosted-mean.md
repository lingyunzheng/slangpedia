## Ghosted (English)
**Meaning:** To suddenly stop contacting someone without any kind of explanation or closure, often used in the context of romantic relationships or friendships.
**Example:** "After two weeks of ghosting, Tom is still MIA (missing in action)."
## 鬼混（汉语）
**Meaning:** 突然停止与某人联系，不给出任何解释或收尾，常用于恋爱关系或友谊中的情境。
**Example:** "经过两周的鬼混，汤姆仍然处于失联状态。"
