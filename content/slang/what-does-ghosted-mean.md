```markdown
## Ghosted (英语) vs. 幻影弃 (中文)
### Ghosted (英语)
- **Definition**: When someone suddenly stops communicating with you and disappears without explanation.
- **Usage**: Commonly used in social media and online dating contexts.
- **Example**: "When Alex stopped responding to my messages, I realized he had been ghosted."
### 幻影弃 (中文)
- **Definition**: 指突然中断与某人的沟通，没有任何解释就消失不见。
- **使用场景**: 常见于社交媒体和在线约会的情境中。
- **示例**: "当Alex停止回复我的信息时，我才意识到他被 幻影弃 了。"
```
