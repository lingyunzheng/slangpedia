## Ghosted
**English:**
To ghost someone is to suddenly and without warning end all communication with them, leaving them in the dark about the state of your relationship.
**Chinese (Pinyin):**
Gǔshì rènzhòng jié ér bùjìng fǎnghuì biǎodìng, jiùzhe ràng tā zài kěnéng de guānxi zhuàngtài zěnme ràng tā kuài pǎo.
**Explanation:**
Original Scenario: A friend suddenly stops responding to your texts and calls without any prior notice.  
Simplified Scenario: Imagine you're playing a game with someone, and they just abruptly stop showing up for your next 'playtime' with no explanation. 
**Example:**
"上次约我吃饭他答应了，结果今天根本不来，还把我微信也删了，真是典型的ghosting行径。"
(The other person agreed to meet for dinner last time, but didn't show up today and even deleted my WeChat. Truly a textbook case of ghosting.)
This term has been borrowed from the original English slang and has been used similarly in both cultures to describe this distinct and often unnecessary way of ending a relationship.
