## Ghosted (Both English and Chinese)
### English
To ghost someone is an informal term, typically used in the context of a romantic relationship or friendship, meaning to suddenly stop all communication with a person without explanation or any warning. It's like disappearing into thin air. 
#### Example:
"I wanted to end the relationship but didn't want to hurt her feelings, so I ghosted her."
### Chinese
“ghosted” 一词通常用于描述在一段感情或友谊中，突然无任何解释和警告地停止与某人所有接触或沟通的行为，就像突然消失不见了。
#### 例句：
“我本不想伤害她的心情，所以就ghost了她。”
---
Ghosting can be seen as a way of avoiding confrontation or dealing with the emotional fallout of a separation, though it often leaves the person being ghosted feeling confused and hurt.
Ghosting 可以被视为一种避免直接冲突或处理分手带来的情感后果的方式，但通常会让被 ghost 的一方感到困惑和受伤。
