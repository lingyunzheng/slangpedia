# Ghosted (Ghosted)
## English
**Ghosted:**
To suddenly stop communicating or interacting with someone without explanation, leaving them confused and feeling rejected.
*Example:* "After dating for six months, John just stopped responding to messages one day. I feel like he got ghosted."
## Chinese
**被玩消失（被 ghosted）:**
突然停止与某人交流或互动，没有任何解释，让人感到困惑和被背叛。
*例句:* "在交往六个月之后，约翰有一天突然停止回复消息。我觉得他被玩消失了。"
