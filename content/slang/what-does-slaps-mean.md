## English
**Slaps**
- *Meaning:* To hit someone lightly with the open hand, often in a playful or teasing manner.
- *Example:* "She slapped him lightly on the back for fun."
## 汉语
**Slaps**
- *含义:* 用手掌轻拍某人，通常带有一些轻松或戏谑的意味。
- *例句:* “她轻拍了他的后背，作为开玩笑的方式。”
