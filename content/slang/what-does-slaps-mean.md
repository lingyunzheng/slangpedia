# Slang Explanation: Slaps
## English
**Slaps** - This term can be used in a few different contexts. Generally, it refers to the act of hitting someone lightly on the back or buttocks as a form of friendly teasing or playful acknowledgment. It can also be used more broadly to mean delivering a strong critique or reprimanding someone in a forceful manner.
## 中文
**Slaps** - 这个词在不同的语境中有几种不同的含义。通常，它指的是用轻拍背部或臀部的方式来开玩笑或表示友好认可的一种行为。它也可以更广泛地用来形容强烈地批评或严厉地指责某人。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "She gave him slaps on the back to congratulate him." - 她给了他轻拍来祝贺他。
- Chinese: "他被朋友轻轻地拍了拍背以示赞赏。" - He was lightly slapped on the back by his friend as an expression of appreciation.
