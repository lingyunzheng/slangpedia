## Slaps (noun)
### English Explanation
**Slaps**: A term used to describe a situation or action where someone is applying a forceful hit or strike, typically with the hand. It can also be slang for giving someone a compliment in a playful or teasing manner.
### Chinese Explanation
**slaps**: 这个词通常用来形容一个人用手用力拍打或击打他人的行为。它也可以被用作一种戏谑或开玩笑的赞美方式。
### Example Usage
- **English**: She gave him a friendly slap on the back.
- **Chinese**: 她友好地拍了拍他的背。
- **English**: He just gave her a huge slap on the cheek.
- **Chinese**: 他狠狠地亲了她一记耳光。
