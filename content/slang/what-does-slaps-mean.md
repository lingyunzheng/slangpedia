## Slaps (noun)
### English
**Meaning:** A gesture or act of hitting someone with an open hand, often used in a friendly or playful context.
**Example:** "He gave her a light slap on the back as a joke."
**Slang Usage:**  
- *In a friendly context:* "Yo, give me a slap on the arm, we're chillin'!"
- *戏谑或开玩笑*: "She got a good slap on the face from her friend, it was all in good fun."
### Chinese
**意思:** 用开放的手掌拍打某人，通常用于友好的或玩笑的场合。
**示例:** "他给了她一个友好的拍背，开玩笑似的。"
**俚语用法:**  
- *在友好场合:* "嘿，来一个胳膊拍，咱们聊聊！"
- *玩笑:* "她从朋友那里得到了一个友好的拍打，虽然只是开玩笑。"
