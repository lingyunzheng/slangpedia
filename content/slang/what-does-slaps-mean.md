# Slaps (Slapping)
**English Explanation:**
A "slap" in slang terms can refer to a physical gesture of hitting someone, which can be used as a form of punishment, greeting, or even playful teasing. It can also mean giving someone a compliment or agreement in certain contexts.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"Slaps" 在俚语中可以指一种肢体动作，即拍打某人，可以用作惩罚、打招呼或是开玩笑的方式。在某些情境下，它也可能意味着给某人赞美或者赞同。
