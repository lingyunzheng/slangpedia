# Slang Term: "Slaps"
## English
- **Definition**: A direct, forceful hit, often used in a playful or teasing manner.
- **Usage**: "He gave a good slaps on my back when I told him the joke."
## Chinese
- **定义**: 一种直接而有力的打击，通常用于开玩笑或戏弄的场合。
- **用法**: "当他听说我讲的笑话时，给我来了个好狠狠的 slaps。"
