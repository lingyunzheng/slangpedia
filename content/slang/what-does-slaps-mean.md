# Slaps
## English
**Slaps**: A playful or sarcastic way to refer to a smack. Often used in a light-hearted or joking manner.
Example: 
- "You just got a slap for saying that!" 
## Chinese
**slaps**: 一巴掌 (yī bāzhǎng) - 指轻轻地打或拍打某人，常用于开玩笑或友好交流中。
例句:
- "你这句话让我挨了一巴掌！"
