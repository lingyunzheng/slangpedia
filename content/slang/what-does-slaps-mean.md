## Slaps
### English
**Meaning**: A quick, firm strike with the hand, often used to express frustration, annoyance, or to indicate disapproval. In some contexts, it can also mean a physical altercation or a metaphorical form of criticism.
**Example**: "I can't believe you did that, you just got slapped in the face with reality."
### 中文
**含义**：用手迅速而有力地拍打，常用来表达烦躁、不满或表示不赞成。在某些情况下，也可以比喻为身体上的冲突或一种隐喻性的批评。
**例句**：我简直不敢相信你竟然这样，真是太给你一记耳光了。
