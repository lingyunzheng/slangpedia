## Slaps
**English:**
- **Definition:** A quick, striking blow with the open hand.
- **Context Example:** "He gave her a hard slap on the back for a job well done."
**Simplified Chinese:**
- **定义:** 用手掌迅速打击的行为。
- **语境示例:** "他拍拍她的背表示赞扬，说她工作干得不错。"
**Internet Slang:**
- **Definition:** In internet slang, "slaps" often refers to a teasing or playful interaction, especially in a text or online chat context.
- **Context Example:** "Don't slap me with more homework, I'm so tired!" (别再给我布置作业了，我已经很累了！)
**Simplified Chinese:**
- **定义:** 在网络用语中，“slaps”往往指一种戏谑或轻松的互动，特别是在文字或在线聊天上下文中。
- **语境示例:** “别再给我布置作业了，我已经很累了！”(Don't slap me with more homework, I'm so tired!)
**Regional Variation:**
- **Definition:** Depending on the region, "slaps" can carry different connotations. In some regions, it might also refer to a more metaphorical or sarcastic form of teasing.
- **Context Example:** "You're really trying to slap me with accusations, huh?" (你是在用指控来打我的脸吗？)
**Simplified Chinese:**
- **定义:** 而在不同地区，“slaps”可能会带有不同的涵义。在某些地区，它也可能指一种更隐晦或讽刺的方式的调侃。
- **语境示例:** “你真的在拿指控来教训我吗？”(You're really trying to slap me with accusations, huh?)
