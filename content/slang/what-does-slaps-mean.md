# Slaps in Slang
## English
**Slap**:   
In casual English, "slap" often refers to a physical action of striking someone with an open hand, typically with force. It can also mean a sharp, unexpected response or rejection. 
### Example
"I was just slapped with those bad grades!" - Here, "slapped" is used metaphorically to indicate the student was unexpectedly given poor grades.
## Chinese
**一巴掌 (yī bāzhǎng)**:
在中文里，“一巴掌”通常指的是用手掌打一个人，带有力量的拍击动作。它也可以比喻一种突然的、令人震惊的拒绝或反应。
### 示例
“成绩一出来，就被老师一巴掌拍下来了。” - 在这个例子中，“一巴掌”被用来比喻老师突然给出不理想的分数，就像被一记打击一样。
