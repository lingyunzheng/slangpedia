## Slaps
### English
**Phat Slap**
- *Explanation*: A powerful and satisfying smack on the back. Often used to show appreciation or encouragement. Equivalent to "well done" or "you're10/10!" in a more physical form.
### 中文
**听板儿**
- *解释*: 用力拍一下对方的后背，通常用来表达赞赏或鼓励。类似于说"干得好"或"你太棒了!"，但更具体地体现在肢体动作上。
