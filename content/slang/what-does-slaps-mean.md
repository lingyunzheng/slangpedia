# Slang: Slaps
## English
**Meaning:** A playful or humorous way to insult someone or to express disapproval or annoyance.
**Example:** "You’re slapping me with your bad attitude again."
## Chinese
**含义:** 一种轻松或幽默的方式来侮辱某人或表达不满或烦躁。
**例子:** “你又被你那糟糕的态度给‘扇了一巴掌’了。”
