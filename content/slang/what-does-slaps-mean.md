# Slang Explanation for "slaps"
## English
**Slaps**: To slap someone on the back as a sign of encouragement or as a friendly gesture. It's similar to giving a high-five but with an extra push.
## 中文
**slaps**: 拍背，表示鼓励或者友好的一个动作，类似于击掌，但力度更大一些。
