# Slang Explanation: Slaps
## English
**Slaps** - This term is often used in gaming contexts, especially in competitive games like Counter-Strike or Fortnite, to refer to scenarios where one player or team eliminates another with a single, decisive move. It implies a swift and effective game-winning action or play.
## Chinese (简体字)
**Slaps** - 这个词通常用于游戏语境中，特别是在像《反恐精英》或《堡垒之夜》这样的竞技游戏中，用来指代一个玩家或团队以单一、决定性的方式击败了另一方。它意味着一次迅速而有效的游戏胜利操作或玩法。
## English
Synonyms: One-hit KO, kill, snipe
