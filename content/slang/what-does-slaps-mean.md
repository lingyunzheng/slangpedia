## Slaps
### English
**Slaps**: To slap someone can often mean to hit them, usually on the face, with an open hand. In slang, it can sometimes be used more figuratively to mean give someone a friendly tap or a metaphorical "bro love" to show approve, or to playfully tease someone.
### Chinese
**Slaps**: 打耳光。字面上是指用手掌拍打别人的脸，但通常比这含义更广泛。在俚语中，它也可以用来表示给某人一个友好的轻拍或一种比喻的“哥们儿的爱”，以示赞同或开玩笑地戏弄某人。
