## Slaps

- **English**: A slap is a quick, sharp strike with the open hand that is often used as a form of mild punishment or in playful fights. It can also be a way to express annoyance or frustration, often done with the back of the hand.
- **Chinese**: 打巴掌，通常是一种轻微的惩罚或戏谑的动作，用开放的手掌快速挥击。它也可以用来表达一些不满或烦躁，通常用手指背进行。

### Examples in Use

- **English**: "Slaps the kid on the back to get his attention." - 他在后面轻轻拍了一下那个孩子，以引起他的注意。
- **Chinese**: "他用手指背轻轻拍了一下那个孩子，以引起他的注意。" - He slaps the kid on the back to get his attention.
