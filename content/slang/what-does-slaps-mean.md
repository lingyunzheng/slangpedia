# Slaps
## English
**Slaps**: A gesture where one strikes another person's face with their hand to express anger, disappointment, or for emphasis in a conversation. However, in casual slang, it could also refer to a more humorous or playful "slap" without literal physical contact.
Example: 
- "He gave me a real slap on the back for laughing at his joke!"
## Chinese
**Slaps**：指的是一个人用手在另一个人的脸上打一下，主要用于表达愤怒、失望，或者在对话中强调某个点。但在非正式的俚语中，它也可能指一种更幽默或轻松的“拍打”动作，不涉及实际的身体接触。
例子：
- “他因为我笑了他的笑话还给我来了个实实在在的拍背！”
