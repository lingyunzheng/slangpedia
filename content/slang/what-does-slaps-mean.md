# Slang Explanation for "Slaps"
## English
**Slaps**: This term is used primarily in online gaming contexts, especially in video games where players engage in battles or challenge each other. It refers to the act of striking an opponent's character with a melee weapon or attack, often tauntingly or to taunt as a show of superiority. It can also be used in a non-game context to describe a sarcastic or mocking gesture.
Example:
- "I got slaps from the others after I won the battle!"
## 中文
**Slaps**: 这个词主要在网络游戏或视频游戏的语境中使用，特别是在玩家彼此交战或相互挑战时。它指的是用近战武器或攻击动作击打对方角色的行为，通常带有挑衅或炫耀胜利的意味。它也可以在非游戏的情境中形容一种讽刺或嘲弄的手势。
例句：
- “我赢了战斗后，其他人给了我slaps。”
