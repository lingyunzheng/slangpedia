# Slang Explanation: Slaps
## English
**Slaps**: This term is often used to describe a scenario where one person delivers a physical hit or tap to another person's buttocks, especially as a form of playful teasing or affection. It can also be used more metaphorically to indicate harsh criticism or a harsh response.
Example: "He got a good slap on the butt from his sister for his naughty behavior."
## 中文
**Slaps**: 这个词通常用来形容一个人给另一个人的屁股来一下，作为一种 playfully teasing 或者 affection 的方式。它也可以用来比喻性地表示尖锐的批评或严厉的回应。
例如：“因为他的调皮行为，他受到了姐姐的一记屁股拍打。”
---
注意：在实际使用时，"slaps" 和 "拍屁股" 这种行为有可能涉及隐私和身体接触，需要在合适的场合和关系之间使用。
