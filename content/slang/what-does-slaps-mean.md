# Slang Term: Slaps
## English:
**Meaning:** A playful or affectionate hit or tap on the back or bottom.
**Explanation:** When friends or romantic partners tease each other in a friendly way, a slap can be an expression of humor or affection. It's a physical action that can range from gentle to playful, not meant to cause actual harm but rather to show closeness or friendliness.
**Example:** "After they made a joke about each other's physique, they both playfully slapped each other on the back, laughing and bonding over their inside joke."
## Pinyin/Chinese:
**意义:** 一种友好的轻拍或抚摸在背部或臀部上的行为。
**解释:** 当朋友或伴侣在友好的情况下相互戏谑时，拍打可以是一种幽默或亲密的方式。这是一种物理动作，可以是温柔的也可以是戏谑的，不算真正的伤害，而是表示亲近或友好。
**例句:** "在他们互相开玩笑提到对方体型后，他们互相拍背嬉笑，在这种内行话的共享中加强了友谊和亲密感。"
