# Goated
**English:**  
Goated is a slang term that has gained popularity in recent years, particularly in hip-hop and street culture. It is used to describe someone or something that is considered the best, most impressive, or the number one in a certain category or context. Essentially, it is a more colorful way to say "number one" or "the best."
**Chinese:**  
Goated 是一个在近年来流行的俚语，尤其是在 hip-hop 和街头文化中。它用来形容某人或某物被认为是最佳的、最令人印象深刻，或者在某一类别或情境中排名第一。简而言之，它是一种更生动的说法，相当于“最好的”、“排名第一的”。
