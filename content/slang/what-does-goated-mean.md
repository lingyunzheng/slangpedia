## Goated
### English Explanation:
"Goated" is a slang term often used to express admiration or approval of someone or something. It refers to a person or thing being the "greatest" of its kind. 
### Chinese Explanation:
"goated"（最頂）是一种常用的俚语，用来表示对某人或某事物的钦佩或赞赏。它用来形容某人或某事物是其领域的“顶峰”或“最佳”。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "That basketball player is goated."
- Chinese: "那个篮球运动员真是goated。"
