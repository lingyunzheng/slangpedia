# Goated 🐐
## English
"Goated" is an internet slang term that is often used to describe something or someone that is outstanding, exceptional, or at the top of their game. It can be used to praise or congratulate someone who has performed exceptionally well, or to highlight an impressive feat or achievement.
### Example
- "Wow, that Nike commercial was goated!" meaning it was exceptionally well-made.
## 中文
"goated" 是网络用语，通常用来形容某件事或某人是卓越的、出色的，或处于最顶尖的水平。它可以用来赞美或祝贺表现出色的人，或是突出令人印象深刻的成就或表现。
### 例子
- “哇，那个 Nike 广告简直牛逼（goated）！”意指广告做得很出色。
