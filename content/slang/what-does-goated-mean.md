# Goated
## English
**Goated**: An informal slang term used to describe something or someone that is extremely impressive, cool, or on point. It emphasizes the highest level of quality or skill.
Example:
- "He took first place in the competition, so he's really goated today!"
## 中文
**Goated**：一个非正式的俚语，用来形容某样东西或某人非常棒、很酷或者状态极佳。它强调了最高级别的质量和技巧。
例句：
- “他在比赛中获得了第一名，真是太强了，今天的他真是Goated！”
