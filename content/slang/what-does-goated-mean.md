# Goated
**English Slang:**
- **Definition:** Used to describe someone or something that is considered the best or number one in a particular category or field.
- **Example:** "When it comes to street dancing, this guy is totally goated!"
**中文俚语：**
- **定义：** 用于形容某人或某事物在其特定领域或类别中被认为是最棒的或排行榜第一的。
- **例句：** "在街舞领域，这个人可真是goated！"
