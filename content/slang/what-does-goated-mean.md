# "Goated" Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:**  
"Goated" is a slang term that originated from the slang term "goat" which refers to someone who is cool, awesome, or particularly skilled. The addition of the -ed ending adds a sense of affirmation or emphasis, meaning they are extremely effective or popular.  
**Chinese:**  
"goated" 是一个流行语，起源于“goat”这个词。"Goat" 原本是指酷炫的、厉害的或者特别擅长的人。在“goated”这个表达中，词尾的 -ed 结构增加了肯定或强调的意味，意味着某人极其有效或受欢迎。
