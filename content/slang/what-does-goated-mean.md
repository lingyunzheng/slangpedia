## Goated
**English:**  
"Goated" is a slang term used to describe someone who is extremely talented, popular, or admired in a certain area. It originated from the word "goat," which was initially used as an insult in hip-hop culture, but it has evolved into a complimentary term.
Example:  
- "Lil Wayne is the GOAT (Greatest Of All Time) rapper, and he just dropped a goated performance at the awards show."
