## Goated
**English:** 
"Goated" is a slang term that originated in African-American communities, often used to describe someone or something that is particularly impressive or outstanding in a way that is trendy and fashionable. It can be used to express admiration or to indicate that something is "lit" (extremely cool or energetic).
**汉语解释：**
"goated" 这个俚语起源于非裔美国人的社区，用来形容某人或某事物非常出色、引人注目，且这种趋势和时尚气息浓厚。它可以用来表达赞赏，或者表示某物极其酷炫或充满活力。
