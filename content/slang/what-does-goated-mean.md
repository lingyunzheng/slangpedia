## Goated
**English:** A term used to describe someone or something that is exceptionally cool, amazing, or the best at what they do.
**释义 (Simplified Chinese):** 这个词用来形容某个人或某件事极其酷炫、超级棒或者在某个领域最优秀。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** Lil Wayne is goated at rapping.
- **中文示例:** 莱尔·韦恩在说唱方面绝对是goated。
