# Goated
## English
**Meaning:** To be the best at something, often to such an extent that one becomes a standout or a figurehead.
**Example:** Sarah won the racing championship last year, and now she's goated in the community of racers.
## 中文
**含义:** 在某件事情上表现得极其出色，以至于成为佼佼者或标志性人物。
**示例:** 赛季末，莎拉赢得了赛车冠军，现在她是车圈公认的标志性人物。
