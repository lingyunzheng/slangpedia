# Goated
## English
"Goated" is a slang term that originates from the term "gKing" or "king," which is a way to describe someone as being extremely gifted, dominant, or exceptional in a particular field or aspect. The term "goated" became popular in the early 2020s, largely due to social media trends. It's often accompanied by the hashtag #Goated. People are "goated" when they perform exceptionally well, whether it's in sports, music, fashion, or any other domain where skill and talent are highly valued.
## 中文
"goated" 是一种俚语，源自“gKing”或“king”，用来形容某人在特定领域或方面极其出色、占优势或与众不同。这个术语在2020年初开始流行，主要是由于社交媒体的趋势。当某人表现得非常出色时，通常会被称为“goated”，并常常伴随使用 #Goated 热门标签。人们被称为“goated”时，意味着他们在体育、音乐、时尚或其他任何领域中展现了极高的技能和才能。
