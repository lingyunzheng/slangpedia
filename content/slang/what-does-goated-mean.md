## Goated
### English
"Goated" is a slang term derived from the word "cheetah," a fast and agile big cat. It is used to describe someone or something that is extremely skilled or impressive in a particular area, often with a connotation of being the best. For example, if someone outperforms others in a game or competition, they might be described as "goated."
### Chinese
"goated" 是一个网络流行语，来源于“猎豹”这个词。这个词用来描述某人在某一方面极其优秀或令人印象深刻，常常带有最佳的状态。例如，如果有人在比赛中表现得比其他人更出色，就可以说他是“goated”。
