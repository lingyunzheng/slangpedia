## Goated
### English
**Definition:** Being "goated" is a slang term that means you are the best at something, or you have achieved something impressive. It often implies that you have outperformed others and are in the lead or at the top of the pack.
**Example:** "Serena is the best player on the team; she's definitely goated at tennis."
### Chinese
**定义：** "Goated" 是一种俚语，表示你某方面是最棒的，或者你已经取得了一些令人印象深刻的成就。通常意味着你超越了他人，处于领先地位或在某个领域处于顶端。
**例句：** "塞雷娜是团队中最好的选手，她在网球方面绝对可以说是 goated。"
