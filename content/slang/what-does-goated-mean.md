# "Goated" ( slang term )
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: Being number one or the best at something. Derived from being "horned" like a *goat*.
- **Usage Example**: "This new rapper is goated on the internet, everyone is talking about his latest song."
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义**：在某件事上是顶级的或者是最好的。来源于像山羊一样有角的意思。
- **使用示例**：“这位新说唱歌手在线上是超 goated 的，大家都在谈论他的最新歌曲。”
