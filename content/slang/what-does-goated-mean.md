## Goated
**English**: 
- Top-notch; extremely skilled or successful.
- Often used to describe someone who is leading in a competition or field, or simply the champion or best at something.
**Chinese (简体中文)**:
- 极好的；非常出色或成功。
- 经常用于描述在比赛中处于领先地位或在某个领域是最优秀的、最好的人。
**Example Usage:**
- English: She's goated at dancing, everyone agrees.
- Chinese（例句）：她在跳舞方面非常出色，大家都这么认为。
