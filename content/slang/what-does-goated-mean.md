# Goated
## English
**Meaning:** Referring to someone or something that is the "king" or "champion" in a particular field or situation. Often accompanied by strong admiration or envy.
**Example:**
- "Obviously, _______ is goated in rap music!"
## 中文
**含义:** 指某人在某个领域或情境中是最厉害的“领头羊”或“冠军”。通常伴随着强烈的钦佩或羡慕。
**例句:**
- “______在说唱界可谓是碾压级别的存在！”
