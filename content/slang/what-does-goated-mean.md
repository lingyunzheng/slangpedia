## Goated
### English
"Goated" is a slang term that originated in the rap and hip-hop communities. It generally means "the best" or is used to describe someone or something that is exceptional or superior to others. 
**Example:** "He just won the slam dunk contest, he is **so goated** on those moves!"
### 中文
"goated" 是一个源自说唱和 Hip-hop 社区的 slang 词汇。它通常表示 "最棒的" 或者用来描述某人或某物有多么出色或超越一般水平。
**例句:** "他刚刚赢得了扣篮大赛，他的动作真是 **goated**!"
