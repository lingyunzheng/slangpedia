## Goated
### English
"Goated" is a slang term often used as an adjective to describe someone or something that is "lit" or extremely impressive. It originally derives from the phrase "gpga goat" in the context of competitive gaming, referring to a player who is clearly better than others, but has since expanded into general use to denote anything that stands out as amazing or cool.
### Chinese
"goated" 是一种俚语，通常用作形容词，用来形容某人或某事物非常“酷”或极其令人印象深刻。它最初源自电子竞技领域的“gpga goat”一词，用来形容远胜其他人的玩家，但现在已经广泛用于表示任何令人赞叹或酷炫的东西。
