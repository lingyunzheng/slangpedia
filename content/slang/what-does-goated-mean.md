## Goated
### English
"Goated" is a slang term often used to describe someone or something that is extremely skilled, talented, or impressive. The origin of the term involves the word "legend" or "legendary," where "goat" is used as a metaphor to express exceptional abilities or achievements. People might say "They're totally goated" when praising someone's performance or skills.
### Chinese
"goated" 是一种俚语，用来形容某人或某事物非常出色、有才能或令人印象深刻。这个词的起源与“传奇”或“传奇性的”有关，其中使用“山羊”这个词作为隐喻，来表达非凡的能力或成就。当人们要赞美某人的表现或技能时，可能会说“他们完全是goated”。
