# Goated
## English
"goated" - This term is a slang variation of "goal" with the "-ted" suffix added to create a playful or cheeky reference. It typically refers to something or someone being the best at what they do, achieving an impressive feat, or being the top choice among peers. It's often used in the phrase "being goated" when someone is admired for their skills or achievements.
## Chinese
"goated" - 这个词是 "goal" 的俏皮变体，通过添加 "-ted" 后缀来创造一种轻松或调皮的引用。它通常意味着某人或某事在某个领域是最优秀的，完成了一些令人印象深刻的表现，或是被伙伴们视为最佳选择。通常在"被goated"（意为"被当做榜样/佼佼者"）这一短语中使用，当某人的技能或成就是在称赞别人时。
