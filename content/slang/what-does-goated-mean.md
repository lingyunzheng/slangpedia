## Goated 🐂
**English:**  
"Goated" is a slang term that originated from the phrase "being goat," which means being dominant, on point, or in the best form. It often refers to someone who is exceptionally good at something or has made a standout performance.
**Chinese:**  
"goated" 是一个起源于“being goat”的网络用语，意为表现最佳、表现卓越或非常擅长某事。它通常用来形容某人在某个领域表现出色或做出突出表现。
