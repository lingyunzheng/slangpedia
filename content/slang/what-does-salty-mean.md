## Salted (咸的, Xián de)
**English Slang:** Salty
- **Definition:** Refers to someone who is feeling upset, irritated, or jealous, often in an over-the-top or dramatic way. This can be due to a perceived slight or a situation that they find upsetting.
- **Example Usage:** "When Alex heard his friend got a promotion, he totally went salty and started complaining about the unfairness of the process."
**Chinese Slang:** 咸的 (Xián de)
- **Definition:** 形容一个人因受到某种误解或不公对待而感到不满或嫉妒，情绪上的反应非常强烈。人们开玩笑时会用“咸的”来表达这种情绪。
- **Example Usage:** "听到朋友升职，亚历克斯整个人 feelings 都咸了（非常咸），开始抱怨过程的不公。"
