### Slang:咸鱼 (xián yú) - Salty
**English Explanation:**
- **Salty**: This term is used to describe someone who is feeling bitter, disappointed, or resentful. It often implies that the person is not taking life well or responding to situations in a neutral or negative manner.
**Chinese Explanation:**
- **咸鱼 (xián yú)**: "咸鱼" literally translates to "salty fish." This term is used colloquially to describe someone who is feeling bitter or disappointed, just like a fish that is not expected to improve its condition (since fish aren't typically considered to be replenishing, they are seen as "咸" or salty, and "鱼" or fish). It can also be used to describe someone who is消极 (jiāo qī) or 待宰 (dài zǎi) (waiting to be slaughtered), meaning they are just going through the motions without much effort or enthusiasm.
