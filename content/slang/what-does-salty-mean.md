# Salty
## English Explanation:
"Salty" is an internet slang term often used to describe someone who is feeling or showing feelings of bitterness, frustration, or resentment. It often implies that the person is being overly critical or negative, possibly in response to a perceived slight or unfair situation.
## Chinese Explanation:
“咸鱼”是一种网络流行语，用来形容一个人处于负面情绪中，比如苦涩、烦躁或怨恨。这个词通常用来指那些对任何事情都表现出过度批判或消极态度的人，尤其是当他们觉得自己受到了不公平对待时。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "He's really salty about losing the game."
- Chinese: "他对输掉比赛感到非常咸鱼。"
