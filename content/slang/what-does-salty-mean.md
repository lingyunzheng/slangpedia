**English:** Salty
- **Definition:** Feeling resentful or jealously spiteful, often because someone else is perceived as having an unfair advantage over you.
- **Example:** If your friend wins a contest that you entered, you might feel salty about the outcome.
**中文解释：** 腌臜 (māzhā)
- **定义：** 因为别人似乎在某件事上有不公平的优势而感到嫉妒和怨恨。
- **例句：** 如果你的朋友赢得了你参加的比赛，你可能会因为比赛结果而感到腌臜。
