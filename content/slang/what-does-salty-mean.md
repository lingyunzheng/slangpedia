```markdown
## Salty (咸鱼)
### English Explanation
"Salty" is a slang term often used online to describe someone who is feeling bitter, resentful, or complain excessively. It usually refers to a person who has a cynical attitude and is not in a good mood, often responding to situations with anger or frustration.
### Chinese (Simplified) Explanation
“咸鱼”是一个网络用语，用来形容一个人感到苦涩、怨恨或者过度抱怨的状态。通常指的是那些态度消极、心情不好的人，他们往往会对情况产生愤怒或沮丧的反应。
### Example Usage
- English: "Gosh, why does he get so salty when he loses?"
- Chinese: "天哪，他输掉比赛后为什么这么抱怨？"
```
### Related Slangs
- **翻车（翻船）**: Like "salty," it describes someone who feels frustrated, usually after a failed attempt or a loss.
- **emo**: An abbreviation of "emotional," it's used similarly to “salty.” Both terms describe a person who shows strong emotions, often negative ones.
This will help you understand and use the term "salty" in both English and Chinese contexts.
