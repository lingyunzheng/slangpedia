## Salty (Salty)
### English
*salty*: A term used to describe someone who is feelin' bitter, annoyed, or upset, often due to feeling that they've been wronged or unfairly treated in a situation.
### Chinese
*salty*: 用来形容一个人感到怨恨、烦躁或愤怒，通常是因为觉得自己受到了不公正的对待或不公平的对待。这个词常用于描述对不满情绪的一种表达。
Example:
- **English**: "I can't believe she got the promotion instead of me, after all I've done for the company. She's been so salty about it."
- **Chinese**: “我简直不敢相信她比我更有资格而得到了晋升，我对公司可是出了不少力的。她这人真是太‘咸’了。”
