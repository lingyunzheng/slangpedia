```markdown
## Salty (咸鱼)
**English**: *Salty*
- **Definition**: Used to describe someone who is feeling resentful, envious, or bitter, often as a result of something happening that made them feel wronged or unhappy. It can also be used to describe content or comments that are excessively negative or bitter.
- **Example Usage**: If a friend posts a photo from a vacation and you comment, "Wow, what a nice trip! Happy for you, I guess," if they feel slighted somehow, they might say, "Why are you being so salty about my trip?" 
**Chinese**: *咸鱼 (xián yú)*
- **Definition**: 盐味的鱼（实际并不吃这种咸鱼，而是形容一种情绪状态），常用来形容那些感到沮丧、羡慕或苦涩的人，也可以用来形容过于负面或模仿咸鱼低落心态的内容或评论。
- **Example Usage**: 如果朋友晒了一张度假的照片，你说：“哇，真不错啊！真为你高兴呢，”如果对方觉得受到了某种伤害，可能会说：“你怎么这么咸鱼？”
```
This table provides the bilingual slang explanation, giving both the English and Chinese context.
