## Salty (咸鱼)
### English
**Salty**: 
This term often describes a person who is feeling frustrated, jealous, or bitter, often reacting negatively to situations they perceive as unfair or negative. It's a colloquial way to describe someone who is being a bit envious or overly critical of others. For example, if someone sees their friend getting a promotion and feels they should have it instead, which leads to them being irritable, they might be described as "salty."
### Chinese
**咸鱼 (xián yú)**:
"咸鱼" 是用来形容一个人感到沮丧、嫉妒或心烦意乱，往往对负面情况表现出负面的反应。这通常是指一个人表现出了一点点羡慕或对他人过于苛刻的批评。例如，如果某人看到朋友得到了晋升，并且觉得自己本应得到这个职位而感到不满和恼火，他们可能会被称为“咸鱼”。
