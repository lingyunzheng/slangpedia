## English: Salty
### Definition
A person who expresses frustration, usually in an unpleasant or critical way, typically after feeling they have been wronged or treated unfairly. 
### Example Usage
- "Wow, that guy's being really salty about losing the game."
## 中文: 盐系 (Yán Xì)
### 解释
指那些因为感觉自己受到了不公平的对待或者输掉某件事而表现出怨气或不满的人，通常以一种不愉快或批评的方式表达出来。
### 使用示例
- "天呀，这家伙因为输掉比赛而怨气好重啊。"
