### Chinese: 羡慕嫉妒恨 (Xiàn mù jí dù hèn)
**Definition:** "Salty" is a slang term used to describe someone who is feeling bitter, resentful, or irritated, especially in response to something another person has achieved or received. It often conveys a sense of unhealthy or maladaptive feelings of envy, jealousy, and hatred.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "She got promoted, and John was feeling pretty salty about it."
- Chinese: "她升職了， JOHN特羨慕嫉妒恨。"
*Note: In the Chinese sentence, "JOHN特羾慕嫉妒恨" is a colloquial way to express that John feels very salty.*
**Etymology:** The term "salty" probably originated from maritime slang where sea salt would get into the eyes, causing a stinging or burning sensation, akin to the feeling of being irritated or bitter.
