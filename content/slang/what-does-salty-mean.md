## English: Salty
### Description
When someone is feeling "salty," it means they are expressing strong emotions, typically negative ones, often in response to a perceived slight or unfair situation. The term comes from the metaphor of someone feeling "salt in their wounds," meaning that a comment or action has made them feel hurt or upset, intensifying any existing negative feelings.
### Example Usage
- "After losing the game, he was really salty about not playing his usual position."
- "She got really salty when she found out the publishing credits weren't her name."
## 中文: 俗气 (咸鱼)
### 描述
当有人感到“俗气”时，这表示他们因为受到某种暗示或不公平对待而表现出了强烈的情绪，通常带有负面情绪。这个词汇源自于一个隐喻，让人感觉就像是盐渗入了伤口，使得轻微的伤害变得更为痛苦和负面。
### 示例用法
- “在比赛失利后，他对不能按照自己熟悉的位置上场感到很生气。”
- “当发现自己没有在出版物的版权中被正确署名时，她非常生气。”
