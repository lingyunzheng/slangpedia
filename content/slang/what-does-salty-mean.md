## Salty (English)
**Definition:** Feeling upset, jealous, or offended, especially in response to something perceived as an unfair or undeserved situation.
**Example Usage:** "I saw that my friend got the promotion while I didn't, and now I'm feeling pretty salty about it."
---
## 盐值高 (Yánzhí gāo, Chinese)
**Definition:** 感觉被亏待了，或者是对某件不公平或不公正的事情感到愤慨和嫉妒。这种情绪通常源于感觉到自己受到了不公平的对待，或者是对某件事情的不满。
**Example Usage:** "看到同事晋升而自己没有，现在感觉盐值好高。" (Felt salty after seeing a colleague get promoted while I didn't.)
