### Slang: Salty

#### English Explanation:
**Salty**: This term is used to describe someone who is upset, annoyed, or frustrated, especially when their criticisms or complaints are perceived as unwarranted or petty. The term can be applied to both emotional and situational contexts, where the person feels a strong negative reaction disproportionate to the event or situation.

#### Chinese Explanation:
**咸鱼 (jiǎn yú)**: 这个词用来形容某人感到沮丧、不满或烦躁，特别是当他们的意见或抱怨被认为是过分或小题大做时。这个词既可以用在情感上，也可以用在情境上，表示一个人对事件或情况的负面反应超出了应有的程度。
