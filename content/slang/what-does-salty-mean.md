# Slang Explanation: "Salty"
## English
**Salty**: This term is often used to describe someone who is upset, irritated, or bitter, usually in response to something they feel is unfair or to which they think they have a right. People can be described as "feeling salty" if they are reacting with more emotion than warranted in a given situation.
## Chinese
**咸鱼 (xián yú)**：这个词用来形容一个人感到沮丧、烦躁或怀有怨恨的情绪，通常是由于感觉受到了不公平的待遇或者认为自己有某种权利。如果有人的行为比实际情境更情绪化，就可以说他们“好咸鱼”。
