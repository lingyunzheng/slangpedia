## Salty (咸鱼)
**English Explanation:**
The term "salty" is typically used to describe someone who is upset, angry, or bitter about a situation, often without any justified reason, or feeling jealous of others. It can also imply that someone is feeling down or not well, generally in a passive-aggressive or unenthusiastic manner.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
“咸鱼”用来形容那些因为一些不值一提的事情而感到生气、怨气冲天或者嫉妒他人的人。这个词也可以用来表达一个人感觉很消沉或状态不佳，通常以一种冷淡或没精打采的方式表现出来。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** She got rejected from the job and is now feeling really salty about it.
- **Chinese Simplified:** 她被拒了工作，现在很咸鱼地对这件事感到生气。

## Salty (咸鱼)
**English Explanation:**
In the context of online interactions, "salty" describes a behavior where someone gets irritable or angry about something, often publicly. It can be a term for a person who is consistently negative, jealous, or upset, and often oversharing their emotions on social media or forums.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
在网络互动中，“咸鱼”用来形容一个人在某些事情上变得脾气暴躁或生气且公开表现出这种情绪的人。这个词也可以用于描述一个总是消极、嫉妒或难过的人，并且经常在社交媒体或论坛上过度分享自己的情绪。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** He posted a lot of salty comments on the discussion forum after not getting the promotion he wanted.
- **Chinese Simplified:** 他在没有得到晋升后，就在讨论论坛上发了很多咸鱼评论。
