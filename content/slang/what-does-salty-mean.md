## Salty (咸鱼)
**English:**  
"Salty" is a slang term often used to describe someone who is feeling resentful, bitter, or upset, especially over something that happened in the past. The term can also imply someone is feeling inadequate or jealous.
**Chinese (咸鱼):**  
"咸鱼"（xián yú）这个词常用来形容一个人感到怨恨、苦涩或沮丧，特别是对过去的某些事情感到不满。这个词还可以暗示一个人感到不足够或嫉妒。
