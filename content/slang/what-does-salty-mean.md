## Salty (English)
Salty is a slang term that describes a person who is feeling irritable, resentful, or overly sensitive, often due to a perceived or actual slight. It's used to describe someone who is taking things personally and is negatively reacting to a specific situation.
### Example Usage:
- "When Jackson didn't get the promotion he wanted, he became super salty about it."

## 盐值高 (Chinese)
"盐值高" 是一个网络用语，用来形容一个人因为感到被冒犯或者敏感而变得烦躁、怨恨或过度敏感。这个词用来描述当有人对特定情况做出负面反应时，是因为他们个人认为受到了伤害。
### 例句：
- "当杰克逊没有得到他想要的晋升时，他的盐值非常高。"
