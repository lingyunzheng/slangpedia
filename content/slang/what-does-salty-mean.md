## English: Salty
### Definition
Salty is a slang term often used to describe someone who is feeling or showing signs of frustration, disappointment, or envy, usually in response to a situation or a person they feel has unfairly benefited in some way. It can also be used to describe a situation that is unsatisfactory, leading to a negative emotional response.
### Example
- **English**: Her reactions on social media are super salty after hearing about a promotion that went to someone else.
- **Chinese**: 她在社交媒体上的反应非常咸Fish那样，在听说别人得到了晋升之后。
## 中文: 咸鱼
### 定义
"咸鱼"是一个网络用语，用来形容那些因为感到沮丧、失望或嫉妒而表现出负面情绪的人，通常是由于他们认为某些人以不公正的方式获得了某种优势。它也可以用来形容一种令人不满意的局面，引发负面情绪。
### 示例
- **中文**: 她在社交媒体上的反应非常咸鱼，在听说别人得到了晋升之后。
