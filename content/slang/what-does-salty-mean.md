```markdown
## Salty: 苦手 (kǔ shǒu)
### English:
**Salty**: If you've ever seen a person spike with emotions after something unpleasant or unfair, you might label them as "salty." Salty often describes a person feeling bitter or resentful about a situation, especially when they feel they've been wronged.
### Chinese:
**苦手 (kǔ shǒu)**: 当一个人在经历不愉快或不公平的事情后，情绪变得苦涩或怨恨时，你可能会形容他们为“苦手”。苦手通常用来描述人们感到悲伤或怨恨的一种状态，尤其是在他们觉得自己被不公平对待时。
```
