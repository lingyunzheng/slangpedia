### 中文解释
**咸鱼** - 在网络上，"咸鱼"通常用来形容那些感觉生活没有动力、对周围事情漠不关心的人。比喻就像一条晾干后变得毫无价值的咸鱼一样，毫无生气。
### 英文解释
**Salty** - In internet slang, "salty" refers to someone who expresses frustration, disappointment, or anger often in response to something they perceive as unfair or to a situation that displeases them. It's akin to feeling like a stale fish with no motivation or energy.
