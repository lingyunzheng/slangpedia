```markdown
## Salty (English)
**Definition:** Feeling bitter, resentful, or jealous, often due to a perceived unfairness or a loss in a competition or interaction.
**Example Sentence:** "After losing the game, he's feeling pretty salty about the referee's calls."
## 盐值高 (Simplified Chinese) / 盐值高 (Traditional Chinese)
**Definition:** 心情沮丧或怨恨，通常是因为觉得自己受到了不公正对待或在竞争中失利。这种情绪经常伴随着嫉妒感。
**例句:** "输掉比赛后，他对裁判的判罚感到非常心累（salty）。"
```
In both English and Chinese, "salty" is used to describe someone who is feeling bitter or resentful, often in the context of a perceived unfairness or defeat.
