## Highkey
### English
**Highkey**: A slang intensifier used to emphasize a statement. It's often used to stress that something is happening to a significant or noticeable degree. 
**Example**: "I'm highkey excited for the concert! → I am very, very excited for the concert!"
### 中文
**高开key (gāo kāi key)**：一种强调说话内容的口头禅，常用来强调某件事情发生的程度较大或非常明显。它通常用来表示强而有力的肯定或强调。
**示例**：我高开key期待这场演唱会！→ 我非常、非常期待这场演唱会！
