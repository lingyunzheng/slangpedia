# Highkey
**English:** (Adverb) Used to emphasize that something is true to a high degree or extent. It's essentially a colloquial way to say "really" or "very."
**中文:** (副词) 用于强调某事的程度非常高或非常到位。本质上来说，是口语中的一种表达“非常”或“真的”的方式。
Example sentences:
- "I go to the gym highkey every day."
- "I love this place highkey."
