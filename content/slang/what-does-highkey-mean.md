# highkey
**English:**  
A slang term used to emphasize that something is true or important. It's often used in social media or casual conversation to indicate that the speaker believes something strongly. For example, "I highkey need to study tonight because the test is tomorrow."
**Chinese:**  
一个用来强调某事是真的或重要的俚语。它经常用于社交媒体或 casual 交谈中，以表明说话者非常坚信某事。例如，“我 highkey 需要在今晚学习，因为明天就要考试了。”
**Example Usage:**  
- English: I highkey didn't expect that to happen.
- Chinese: 我 highkey 没有预料到那件事会发生。
