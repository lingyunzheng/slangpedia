# Highkey (High Meaning Key)
## English
**Highkey**: A slang term used to emphasize the importance or significance of something. It implies that something is very true or noticeable. For example, saying "I highkey need that" means you strongly and genuinely need that thing.
## 中文
**Highkey**: 一个俚语，用来强调某事的重要性或显著性。它表示某事非常真实或明显。例如，说“I highkey need that”意味着你需要那件东西非常强烈和真诚。
