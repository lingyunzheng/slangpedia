# Highkey
**English Slang:**
- **Definition:** Highkey is a slang term used to emphasize something in a very strong way. It is often used to indicate that something is extremely true or notable.
- **Usage Example:** "I highkey love this song!"
- **Explanation:** When someone says something is "highkey," they are saying it with a lot of emphasis, as in "high key" or "highly key." It's a fun, informal way to express strong feelings or importance.
