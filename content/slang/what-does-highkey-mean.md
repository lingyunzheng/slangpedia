## highkey
### English
"Highkey" is an informal intensifier often used in text or speech to emphasize the significance or frequency of something. It's akin to saying "really" or "definitely."
### Chinese
"Highkey" 是一种非正式的强调词，常用于文本或口语中，用来加强某个事物的重要性或发生的频率。它类似于说 "真的" 或 "绝对地"。
