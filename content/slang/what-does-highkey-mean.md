# Highkey
**English:** 
- Super, definitely! Used to emphasize something with high certainty or importance. Example: "I'm highkey excited for the concert tonight!"
**中文:**
- 超级，非常！用来强调某件事情的高度确定性或重要性。例如：“今晚的演唱会我超级激动！”
