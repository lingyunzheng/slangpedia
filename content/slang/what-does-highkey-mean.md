## Highkey
### 英文解释
"Highkey" is an intensifier used to emphasize the level of something. It's derived from "highly key" or "highly keyly," but it has evolved into its current form. When someone uses "highkey," they are strongly emphasizing or highlighting a particular aspect of what they're saying.
Example: "I'm highkey into that new album; I really, really love it!"
### 中文解释
"Highkey" 是一种强调表达的方式，用来强调某件事情的程度。这个词最初来源于 "highly key" 或 "highly keyly"，但现在已经演变成目前的形式。当某人使用 "highkey" 时，他们强烈地强调或突出了他们所说的内容的某个方面。
例句：我对那张新专辑highkey喜欢；我真的很喜欢它！
