# Highkey
## English
**Highkey:** Used to emphasize that something is definitely true or very significant. It's an intensified way of saying "highly" or "definitely." Essentially, it's a slang way to put extra emphasis on your statement.
**Example:**
- "I'm highkey excited for the concert this weekend!" 
- "The new restaurant is highkey amazing!"
## 中文
**Highkey:** 用于强调某件事绝对是真的或非常重要。它是“highly”或“definitely”的加强版表达方式。简单说来，这是一种用以加强陈述语气的俚语。
**例句:**
- “我超级超级兴奋，这个周末有演唱会！”
- “新餐厅超级棒！”
