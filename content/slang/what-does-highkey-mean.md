# Highkey
**English:**  
"Highkey" is an intensifier that emphasizes the described action or state. It is derived from "highly key," where "key" is emphasized and borrowed from "within the key," an audio term. This slang is commonly used to stress that something is definitely or very much true. For example, saying "I'm highkey excited for the party" means you are extremely excited about it.
**中文:**  
"Highkey" 是一个用于强调描述的动作或状态的加强词。这个词语源于 "highly key"，其中 "key" 被突出强调并借用自音频术语 "within the key"。这个俚语常用来强调某事是绝对或非常真实的。例如，说 "I'm highkey excited for the party" 就意味着你非常兴奋地期待着这个聚会。
