# Highkey
**English:**  
Highkey is a slang term used to emphasize that something is definitely true or very likely to happen. It is often used to indicate that the speaker is certain about something.
**中文:**  
Highkey是俚语词汇，用来强调某件事情是确实的或非常有可能发生的。这个词常被用来表明说话人对某事很有把握。
