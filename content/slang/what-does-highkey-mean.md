## English Slang: highkey
### Bilingual Explanation
- **English:** You're emphasizing that something is very much the case or very significant.
- **Chinese:** 你非常强调某件事情是事实，或者某件事情是很重要的。比如说 "I am highkey hungry." (我非常饿。) 意思是你真的非常饿，不仅仅是有一点饿。
