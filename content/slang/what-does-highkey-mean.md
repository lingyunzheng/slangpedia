# Highkey
**English:**  
USED AS: A slang intensifier  
MEANING: Really, definitely, very  
EXAMPLE: "I'm highkey excited for this party!"  
MEANING: "I'm really, definitely, very excited for this party!"
**Chinese:**  
用于：俚语加强词  
意思：真，绝对，非常  
例子：我高key期待这个聚会！  
翻译：我真地，绝对地，非常期待这个聚会！
