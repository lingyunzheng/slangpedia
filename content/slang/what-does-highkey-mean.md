```markdown
# Highkey
**English Slang**: Highkey
**Definition**: When you want to make something very clear and emphasize that you are serious about what you are saying.
**Example Usage**:
- "I'm highkey excited for the concert tomorrow!" 
- "Highkey, she's the best dancer in the class."
**Chinese Slang**: 高高低低 (gāo gāo dī dī)
**Definition**: 高高低低 (Gāo Gāo Dī Dī) is a colloquial term in Chinese, sometimes used with a similarly emphatic tone to highlight that you mean something specifically or strongly. 
**Example Usage**:
- “我明天去音乐节，真的高高低低的兴奋！” (Wǒ míngtiān qù yīuè jié, zhēn de gāo gāo dī dī de xìngfèn!) - I'm really, really excited for the music festival tomorrow!
- “她高高低低是最好的舞者。” (Tā gāo gāo dī dī de shì zuì hǎo de wǔzhě.) - She's truly the best dancer, without a doubt.
```
