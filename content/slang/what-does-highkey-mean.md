# Highkey (High Key)
**English:**  
A slang intensifier meaning "highly" or "very." It's often used to emphasize the significance or intensity of something.
**Example:**
- "I'm highkey excited for the concert tonight!"
- "The weather is highkey awesome with clear skies and warm temperatures."
**中文：**
一个俚语强化词，意为“非常”或“很”。它常用于强调某事的重要性或强度。
**例句：**
- "我今晚去演唱会超级激动！"
- "天气高键很好，晴朗又温暖。"
