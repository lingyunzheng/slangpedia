# Highkey
**中文解释**: 非常、极其。
**例子**: 
- 原文: She's highkey a genius!
- 中文: 她真的是个天才！
**英文解释**: A very strong emphasis on something; used to indicate that something is true or is being done to a significant degree. 
**例子**:
- Original: He's highkey excited about the new job.
- Translation: He is genuinely very excited about the new job.
