# highkey
## English
- **Definition:** A way of emphasizing that something is true, important, or frequently mentioned.
- **Example Usage:** "I highkey need to check my emails right now." This means "I really, really need to check my emails right now."
## 中文
- **释义：** 用来强调某件事情是真的、重要的或者是经常被提及的。
- **用例示例：** "I highkey need to check my emails right now." 这意味着 "我真的很需要现在就检查下我的电子邮件。"
