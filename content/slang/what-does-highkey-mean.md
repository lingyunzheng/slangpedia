## Highkey (强 high-key)
**English Explanation:**  
"Highkey" is an informal intensifier that emphasizes the truth or significance of a statement. It is derived from "highly key" and is used to express that something is very true or important.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**  
- **中文解释:** "Highkey" 是一个非正式的强调词，用来加强陈述的真实性或重要性。这个词来源于 "highly key"，用来表示某事是非常真实的或重要的。
