# "Highkey"
## English
- **Definition**: Emphasizing the importance or frequency of something.
- **Example Usage**: "I'm highkey excited about the concert tomorrow!" translates to "I'm really, really excited about the concert tomorrow!"
## Chinese
- **定义**: 强调某事的重要性或频率。
- **例句**: 我高键期待明天的演唱会！（相当于“我really really期待明天的演唱会！”）
