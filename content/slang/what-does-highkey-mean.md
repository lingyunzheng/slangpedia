# Highkey
## English
**Definition:** Used to emphasize that something is very true or important.
**Explanation:** When someone says "I am highkey excited about the party," they are saying they are **really** or **very** excited about it.
**Example:** "Highkey, I need to study for that exam tomorrow. It's highkey important."
## 中文
**定义:** 用于强调某事是真实的或重要的。
**解释:** 当有人说到"I am highkey excited about the party"时，他们是在说他们**真的**或**非常**兴奋。
**示例:** "高key，我明天需要复习那个考试。考试非常关键！"
