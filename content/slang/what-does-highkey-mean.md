# Highkey
## English Explanation
"Highkey" is a slang term that emphasizes something strongly or noticeably. It's often used to describe a situation, feeling, or action that is very notable or significant. 
Example: 
- "I highkey need to finish this project by tomorrow."
- "She highkey lit up the room when she walked in."
## Chinese Explanation
"Highkey" 是一个俚语词汇，用来强调某件事情非常明显或显著。通常用来描述非常显著或重要的情况、感觉或行为。
例句：
- "我 highkey 需要在明天之前完成这个项目。"
- "她在走进房间时 highkey 让房间亮了起来。"
