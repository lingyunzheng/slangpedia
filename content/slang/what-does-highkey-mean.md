# Highkey
## English
**Definition**: A slang term emphasizing that something is definitely true or very likely to happen. 
**Usage**: Used to stress the importance or the certainty of a statement. It's often used in informal conversations or texts.
**Example**: "Highkey raining today, so I suggest you bring an umbrella."
## 中文
**定义**: 一个俚语，用来强调某事一定是真实的或很可能发生。
**用法**: 常用来强调陈述的重要性或确定性。它通常用于非正式对话或短信中。
**例句**: "高键今天要下雨，所以建议你带把伞。"
