## Highkey (高.key)
### English
- **Definition**: Used to emphasize that something is very likely, important, or true. It's a more pronounced way of saying "highly" or "pretty much."
- **Example**: "I highkey need to get this project done by tomorrow. It's super important."
### Chinese
- **释义**: 用于强调某事非常有可能、重要或真实。相当于“非常”、“几乎”或“事实上”。
- **例句**: “我高key需要明天前完成这个项目。这非常重要。”
