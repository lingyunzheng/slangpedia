# Highkey
## English
* **Definition**: Very much so, extremely.
* **Usage**: Used to emphasize the importance or frequency of something. For example, "I'm highkey going to the party tonight." means "I'm extremely going to the party tonight."
## 中文
* **定义**：非常，极其。
* **用法**：用于强调某事的重要性或频率。比如，“I'm highkey going to the party tonight.” 可以翻译为“我今晚是非常会去参加派对的。”
