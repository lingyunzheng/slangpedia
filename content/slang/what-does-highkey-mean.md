# What does "highkey" mean?（highkey 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Highkey" is an intensifier often used to clearly and emphatically state that something is true, significant, or obvious.

**中文解释：**  
"highkey" 是一个强调词，通常用来明确而强烈地表示某事的真实性、重要性或显而易见。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: I'm highkey excited for the concert!
   中文翻译：我非常期待这场音乐会！

2) English sentence: She's highkey the best cook around here.
   中文翻译：她真是这儿最好的厨师。

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "highkey" stems from social media and online platforms, particularly among younger generations, starting in the early to mid-2010s. It originated from the phonetic pronunciation and text shorthand of "highly key," where "key" can represent value or importance.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. super
2. extremely
3. really
4. totally
5. definitively
