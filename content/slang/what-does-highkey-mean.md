## Highkey (High Key)
**English:**  
Used to emphasize that something is happening to a very significant or noticeable degree. It's a hyperbolic way of saying "totally" or "very."
**Example Usage:**
- "I highkey missed you!" 
- "This party is highkey awesome!"
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
用来强调某事发生得非常显著或明显。这是一种夸张的说法，类似于“完全”或“非常”。
**中文例句：**
- “我高键没你啊！”
- “这个聚会超级high键！”
