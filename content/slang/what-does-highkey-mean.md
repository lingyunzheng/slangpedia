## Highkey

### English
- **Meaning:** A more intense or emphatic version of 'highly' or 'very'. Often used to convey a stronger emphasis than just 'highly'.
- **Example Usage:** "I'm highkey into this new album—it's spectacular!"

### 中文
- **含义:** 一种比“很”或“非常”更强烈强调的表达方式。“highkey”类似于"highly"或"very"，但使用上更加俏皮或强调。
- **示例用法:** “我highkey喜欢这张新专辑——它太棒了！”
