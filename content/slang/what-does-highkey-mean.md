# Highkey (英 /aɪˈhaɪkɪː/; 简 /aiˈhaɪkiː/)
## Explanation in English
"Highkey" is a slang term derived from "highly" and "key" that emphasizes the statement being made. It's often used to indicate that something is true to a significant extent or is the case all the time. For example, saying "I'm highkey tired" means you are extremely or very tired.
## Explanation in Chinese
"Highkey" 是一个来自 "highly" 和 "key" 的俚语，用来强调所陈述的内容。通常表示某事的程度非常高或总是如此。比如，“I'm highkey tired” 的意思是你非常非常累，或者用中文可以表达为“我实在是累死累活的”。
---
Example Usage:
- English: "I'm highkey excited for the concert!"
- Chinese: "我对这个演唱会实在是激动得不得了！"
