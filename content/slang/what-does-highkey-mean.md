## highkey (英）/ 高キー (日）
**English Explanation:**  
"Highkey" is an intensifier that emphasizes the degree to which something is true. It's often used in casual speech and social media to stress that something is very, extremely, or indeed happening. For example, if you say "I've been highkey missing you," you're saying you've been really, really missing someone.
**Japanese Explanation:**  
"高キー (Kō ki)" もとは英語の "highkey" から来ており、何かが本当に、非常に、または実際に起こっていることを強調します。例えば、"I've been highkey missing you" は "本当にあなたが欠けていたよ" という意味になり、非常に強く何かが起こっていることを伝えます。
---
例文:
- English: "I've been highkey feeling this way."
- Japanese: "highkey 高キー の状態でいたの。"
- English: "It's been highkey been amazing!"
- Japanese: "それはたぶん高キー첩시적으로素晴らしい!!"
