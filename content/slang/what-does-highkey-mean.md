## Highkey
### English
**Highkey**: This slang term is used to emphasize that something is true or significant. It's often equivalent to saying "definitely" or "very much so." For example, if someone asks if you enjoyed the party, you might say, "Yeah, I highkey had a blast!" to mean you really, really enjoyed it.
### 中文
**高.key (Highkey)**：这个俚语用来强调某件事情的真实性或重要性，相当于说“非常”或“真的很……”。比如，别人问你是否喜欢派对，你可以回答说，“Yeah, I highkey had a blast!” 意思是你真的很喜欢这个派对。
