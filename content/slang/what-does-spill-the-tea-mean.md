## Spill the Tea
### English
To "spill the tea" means to share secret information or gossip, especially when it was intended to remain private. It's like telling a closely-held secret or revealing confidential details to others, often in social media contexts or casual conversations.
### Chinese (简体中文)
" spill the tea" 的意思是在不应该公开的时候透露秘密信息或八卦，比如分享了本应保密的内部消息或者详细细节，通常在社交平台或闲聊中使用。
### Example
- **English**: She finally spilled the tea about who won the contest.
- **Chinese**: 她终于透露了谁在比赛中获胜的消息。
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
- **English**: I can't believe she spilled the tea about our private meeting!
- **Chinese**: 我都不相信她竟然把我们私下会面的事说了出去！
