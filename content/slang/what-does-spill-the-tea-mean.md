## Spill the Tea
**English:** To share confidential or juicy information, often in a gossiping or dramatic manner.
**中文:** 指泄露秘密或令人兴奋的消息，通常是以八卦或戏剧性的方式传播。
**Example:** "I swear, Sarah was going to spill the tea about the surprise party, but I managed to convince her to keep it a secret."
- **示例:** “我发誓，萨拉本来要八卦 surprise party 的细节，但我成功说服她保密了。”
**Origin:** The phrase is believed to have originated from the ritual of serving tea in a communal cup, where one could "spill the tea" by accidentally letting someone know the contents of the cup or the gossip within it.
- **来源:** 该短语源于共饮一杯茶的仪式，一个人不小心会让别人知道杯中的内容或八卦，因此“spill the tea”一词由此而来。
