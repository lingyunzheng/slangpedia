## Slang Explanation: "Spill the Tea"
### English
- **Meaning**: To share confidential or insider information, often gossip or unverified details.
- **Example**: "She decided to spill the tea about the company's new project before anyone else knew."
### Chinese
- **含义**：泄露内部消息或未经证实的八卦，通常是指分享秘密信息。
- **例句**：她决定在别人之前先透露关于公司新项目的内部消息。
