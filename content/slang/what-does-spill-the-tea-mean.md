## Spill the Tea (English)
**Definition:** To share confidential or juicy details about someone or something, often gossip.
**Example Usage:**
- "She can't keep her mouth shut! She's going to spill the tea about the new project's failure."
## 泄露茶秘 (Chinese)
**释义:** 分享有关某人或某事的机密或八卦细节。
**例句使用:**
- "她嘴巴不把门！她要把新项目的失败悄悄话泄露出去了。"
