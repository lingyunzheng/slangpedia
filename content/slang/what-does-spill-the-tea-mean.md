## Spill the Tea
### English
**Definition:** To share secret or gossip about someone or something.
In casual conversations, when someone says they are going to "spill the tea," it means they are about to reveal insider information or gossip. It's often used in a friendly manner, but can also denote a more serious breach of confidence, depending on the context.
### Chinese
**定义:** 分享关于某人或某事的秘密或八卦。
在非正式的对话中，当有人说他们要"spill the tea"时，意味着他们准备揭露内部信息或八卦。通常用在友好的语境中，但在某些情况下也可能表示严重泄露秘密，具体取决于语境。
