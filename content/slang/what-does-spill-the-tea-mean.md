## Spill the Tea (English)
**Meaning:** To share confidential or behind-the-scenes information, often in a gossiping manner.
**Example:** "She finally decided to spill the tea about the company's internal drama."
## 泄露茶秘 (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 透露八卦或背后的信息，通常是出于八卦的目的分享秘密消息。
**Example:** "她终于决定泄露茶秘，分享公司的内部八卦。"
