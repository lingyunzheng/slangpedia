```markdown
# Spill the Tea
## English Explanation
**Meaning:** To share confidential or juicy information, especially about someone else. It implies divulging details that are not supposed to be public, often in a gossiping context.
## Slang Usage Example:
If someone asks what happened with your friend's new relationship, you might reply, "I don't want to spill the tea, but... let's just say it's not exactly what it seemed."
## Chinese Explanation
**意思是:** 透露机密或精彩的信息，特别是关于某人的。它意味着泄露不应该公开的信息，往往是在八卦的语境中使用。
## 俚语用例:
如果有人问你的朋友的新恋情发生了什么，你可以回答，“我不想‘抖机灵’，但...说白了，一切都不是表面上的样子。”
```
Note: "抖机灵" is a slang term in Chinese that somewhat corresponds to "spill the tea" in English, implying to divulge gossip or confidential information.
