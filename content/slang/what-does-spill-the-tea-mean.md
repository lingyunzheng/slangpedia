# Spill the Tea 🌿📝
## English
**Spill the Tea**: This slang phrase means to share confidential or juicy information, often gossip about someone. It’s like when someone can't keep a secret and lets everyone know something private that was meant to be kept under wraps.
Example:
- "She finally spilled the tea about her new boyfriend, and it turns out he's been seeing multiple people!"
## 中文
**Spill the Tea**: 这个网络用语指的是分享机密或八卦信息，通常指的是泄露他人的秘密。就像有人忍不住把不该说的秘密告诉了大家。
例句：
- "她终于把关于她新男友的秘密说出来了，结果发现他一直在见好几个女朋友！"
