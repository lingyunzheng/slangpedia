## Spill the Tea
### English
To "spill the tea" means to tell confidential information or gossip that was previously kept secret. It often refers to sharing juicy details or insider information that others weren't aware of.
### Chinese
"Spill the tea" 溢美之词地泄漏秘密或八卦，通常指的是分享一些之前被保密的有趣或内部的信息，是将保密的事情公开。
例句：
- **English:** She finally spilled the tea about who's dating her.
- **Chinese:** 她终于把关于谁是她男友的事 publicized 了。
