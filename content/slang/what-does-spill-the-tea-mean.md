## Spill the Tea
**English:** To "spill the tea" means to share confidential or secret information, often in a gossiping or insider manner. It's when you let out information that was intended to be kept private.
**中文:** "Spill the tea" 指的是泄露保密或秘密的信息，通常是八卦或内部交流的方式。当你透露本该保密的信息时，就相当于“倒茶”了。
