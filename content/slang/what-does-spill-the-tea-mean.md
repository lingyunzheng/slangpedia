## Spill the Tea (English)
### Explanation
To "spill the tea" means to share confidential or juicy information with others, often in a gossiping manner. It’s like letting out a secret that was supposed to be kept under wraps.
### Example
- "She finally spilled the tea about who the new CEO is going to be."
## 泄露茶货 (Chinese)
### 解释
"泄露出货" 指的是分享一些本该保密的或让人感兴趣的内幕消息，通常是以八卦的方式传播。就像透露了一个本应保密的秘密。
### 示例
- "她终于泄露出货了，关于新的CEO是谁的真正消息。"
