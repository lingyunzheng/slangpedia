## Spill the Tea
### English
- **Definition**: To share gossip or secret information, especially in an informal or dramatic manner.
- **Example**: "She promised not to spill the tea, but she couldn't resist telling everyone about the surprise party."
### Chinese
- **释义**：分享八卦或秘密信息，尤其是在非正式或戏剧化的状态下分享。
- **例句**：她答应不透露消息，但她还是忍不住把惊喜聚会的事告诉了所有人。
