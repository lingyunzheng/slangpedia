# Spill the Tea
## 中文解释
“Spill the tea” 等同于“放茶”或“倒茶”，形象地比喻泄露秘密或八卦，通常是在朋友圈或社交媒体上分享未经证实的信息。
## 英文 Explanation
“Spill the tea” means to泄露八卦或秘密，often referring to sharing unverified information or juicy details on social media or among friends.
