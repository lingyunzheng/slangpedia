## Spill the Tea (English)
### 晒八卦 (Chinese)
"Spill the tea" is a phrase used when someone is about to share confidential or juicy information with others, often gossip. It implies that someone is about to reveal something that hasn't been made public yet. In Chinese, a similar expression is "晒八卦" (shài gàn'à), where "八卦" (gàn'à) means "gossip" and "晒" (shài) means "to show off" or "to air out." Both convey the idea of sharing confidential or sensational information with others, often for the purpose of entertainment or shock value.
