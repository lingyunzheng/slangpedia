## Spill the Tea (English)
**Meaning:** To share gossip or secrets about someone or something, often in a dramatic or indiscreet manner.
**Example Usage:**  
- "I can't believe she just spilled the tea about her breakup with her boyfriend."
## 泄露秘密 (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 分享某人或某事的秘密或花边新闻，通常是故意泄露或以戏剧性的方式分享。
**Example Usage:**  
- "我简直不敢相信她刚刚泄露了关于她与男朋友分手的秘密。"
