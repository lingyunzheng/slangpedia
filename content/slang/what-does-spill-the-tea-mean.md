```markdown
## Spill the Tea (英语)
- **含义**：透露八卦或秘密信息。
- **例子**：She can't keep her mouth shut! She's always spilling the tea about her colleagues behind their backs.
## 泄露茶闻 (中文)
- **含义**：八卦或泄露秘密消息。
- **例子**：她真是嘴不严啊！总是背后泄露茶闻，说同事的闲话。
```
This explains the slang "spill the tea" and its equivalent in Chinese, "泄露茶闻," which is a playful way to describe gossip or prying into and revealing other people's private information.
