## Spill the tea (EN) / 泄露茶密 (CN)
**Definition:** To share confidential or private information with others, typically gossiping or revealing secrets that weren't meant to be made public.
**Usage:** When someone tells you a juicy secret or exposes something that shouldn't be known, they have "spilled the tea."
**Example in sentence (EN):** "She spilled the tea about her ex-boyfriend's new girlfriend when they met at the coffee shop."
**Example in sentence (CN):** "她在咖啡店里把关于前男友新女友的秘密全说了出来。"
