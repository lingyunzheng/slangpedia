## Spill the Tea
### English
**Definition**: To share confidential or juicy information, often gossip or things that should remain secret.
**Example**: "She finally spilled the tea about the company's latest project—no one was expecting it to be so secretive!"
### 中文
**定义**: 分享机密或有趣的内幕消息，通常是流言蜚语或者本应保密的信息。
**例句**: "她终于把关于公司最新项目的内幕消息透露出去了——没人料到它会如此保密!"
