### Spill the Tea
**English:**  
To share confidential or juicy information, often in a gossip-like manner. Essentially, it means to let out secret details that were previously kept hidden.
**Chinese (翻译):**  
分享 confidential 或者 juicy 的信息，通常以八卦的方式进行。简而言之，就是透露之前保持秘密的细节。
**Example Usage:**  
- English: She spilled the tea about her relationship status.
- Chinese: 她八卦了关于她的关系状态。
