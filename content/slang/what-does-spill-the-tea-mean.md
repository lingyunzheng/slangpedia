# Spill the Tea (English-Chinese Slang)
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: To share gossip or personal information, especially in a confidential or exclusive manner.
- **Usage Example**: "I promise I won't spill the tea, but I heard she got fired because of the accounting mistake."
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义**: 分享八卦或个人信息，尤其是以保密或独家的方式。
- **使用示例**: "我保证不泄露消息，但我听说她因为会计错误被解雇了。"
### Related Terms
- **喝下午茶 (hē xià wǔ chá)**: Although literally "have afternoon tea," it can metaphorically be used to imply gossip or the act of sharing secrets, similar to "spill the tea."
