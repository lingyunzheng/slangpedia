## English: Spill the Tea
### Simplified Chinese: 茶叶蛋解放（t行李解放, 茶叶蛋解放）
**Explanation:**
- **English:** To give away confidential or insider information, often gossip or rumors.
- **Chinese:** "茶茶叶蛋解放" literally translates to "let the tea egg go free," but it's a playful way to say "to spill the beans" or "let the cat out of the bag." Here, "茶叶蛋" (tea egg) is used metaphorically to represent the hidden or secret information that is now being disclosed.
- **Usage Example:** "She finally spilled the tea about the company's new project plans after being convinced by her friend."
This slang expression is often used in social media contexts or among friends to describe the act of sharing previously secret or confidential information.
