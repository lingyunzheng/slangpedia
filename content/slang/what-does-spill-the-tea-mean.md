## Spill the Tea
**中文解释：** 溜出八卦
### Meaning:
- **English**: To share confidential or juicy information, often gossip or details that were meant to be kept secret.
- **Chinese**: 指泄露八卦或秘密信息，通常是一些小道消息或私密细节。
### Usage Example:
- **English**: "She really spilled the tea about the company's financial losses."
- **Chinese**: "她真的把公司亏损的消息给泄露了出去。"
### Etymology:
- **English**: Originally from the Turkish phrase "çay dökmek" meaning "to pour tea," possibly due to its ceremonial importance in revealing important news. It's also associated with the practice of pour-over coffee, where the water is poured slowly to reveal the nuanced flavor profile.
- **Chinese**: 来源于土耳其语“çay dökmek”（倒茶），可能是因为传递重要消息时的仪式感。也和渐进式冲泡咖啡有关，缓缓注入水以揭示其细微风味。
