## Spill the Tea
### English
**To spill the tea:** This phrase is a colloquialism used to describe the act of sharing confidential or secret information with others, often to discuss personal or gossip-related matters.
### Chinese
**吐槽（tù tǒu）:** 这个短语用来形容泄露秘密或敏感信息的行为，通常用于讨论八卦或私人话题。相当于“把茶倒在别人面前”，意味着把隐藏的事公开说给别人听。
### Example
- **English:** She promised not to tell anyone, but she ended up spilling the tea about the surprise party.
- **Chinese:** 她答应不告诉任何人，但最后还是把那场惊喜派对的计划给吐槽了。
