# Spill the Tea
## English
**Spill the tea**: This phrase is used to describe the act of sharing confidential or juicy information, often in a dramatic or gossipy manner. It's like having a secret and just blabbing it out to everyone.
## Chinese
** spill the tea**：这个短语用来形容分享秘密或很有料的信息，常常是以一种戏剧性或八卦的方式。就像你有一个秘密，然后突然就全部告诉别人了。
