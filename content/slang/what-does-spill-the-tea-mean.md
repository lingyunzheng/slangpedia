## Spill the Tea
**English:**  
To reveal or share confidential or juicy information, often on a gossip or scandalous topic.
**Chinese (繁體字):**  
洩漏茶 começar  
inglês: 爆料或泄露机密或有趣的（通常是八卦或丑闻性质的）信息。
**Example Usage:**
- English: She finally spilled the tea about the office romance.
- Chinese: 她終於 blasts the tea 關於辦公室情 affair。
**Note:** "茶" here is a playful reference to the idea of sharing news around a cup of tea, which is a common social activity that adds to the informal and intimate feel of the phrase.
