## Spill the Tea (English)
### Explanation
To spill the tea means to share information or gossip that hasn't been made public yet. It's like letting out a secret that was supposed to stay hidden.
### Bilingual Example
- **English:** "She finally spilled the tea about the surprise party."
- **Chinese:** 她终于把惊喜派对的事说出来了。（Tā zhōngyú bǎ jīngxǐ pàΙΙtà shuō chū le.）
