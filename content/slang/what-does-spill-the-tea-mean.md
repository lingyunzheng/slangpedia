# Spill the Tea 🍵
## English
**Spill the tea** is a slang term meaning to share confidential or private information with others, often in a gossipy or scandalous way. 
*Example:*  
"Did you hear Sarah spilled the tea about the new project? She was supposed to keep it a secret!"
## 中文
**Spill the tea** 是俚语，指分享机密或私人的信息，常常以八卦或揭露负面消息的方式来传播。
*例句:*  
“你听说了吗？萨拉把新项目的秘密都透露出去了。她本来是要保密的！”
