```markdown
## Spill the Tea
**English:** To tell secrets or gossip about someone; to share undisclosed information.
**Chinese:** 透消息；说八卦；分享小道消息
Example Usage:
- English: She promised not to spill the tea, but she told everyone about the surprise party anyway.
- Chinese: 她答应不会透消息，但还是把惊喜派对的事告诉了所有人。
```
This markdown format clearly showcases the translation and provides a concise example of how to use the expression in both languages.
