## Spill the Tea (英语：Spill the Tea)
### English Explanation:
To disclose or reveal confidential or juicy information, often in a gossiping or scandalous manner.
### Slang Usage Example:
At the office party, someone finally spilled the tea about who got the promotion.
### Chinese Explanation:
_atom语境_：透露或泄露秘密或八卦性质的信息，通常以议论纷纷或揭秘的方式进行。
### 例句：
在公司的聚会上，有人终于透露了关于谁得到晋升的秘密。
