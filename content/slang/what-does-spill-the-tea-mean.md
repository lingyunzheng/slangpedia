## Spill the Tea (English)
- **Meaning**: To reveal confidential or juicy information, often gossip or details that were previously unknown.
- **Example**: "She can't keep a secret! She always spills the tea whenever she has new information."
## 泄露茶壶里的秘密 (中文)
- **Meaning**: 泄露茶壶里的秘密 也指泄露机密或八卦信息，通常是人们想知道但还未公开的内容。
- **Example**: "她简直就是个爆料狂人，只要有的新消息，她就会毫不保留地 泄露茶壶里的秘密。"
