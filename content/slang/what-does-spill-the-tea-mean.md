## Spill the Tea 🍵 Vintage 🌺
**English:** To spill the tea refers to sharing confidential or secret information that was supposed to be kept private. It can also mean to tell unnecessary details, often gossip.
**Chinese:** "Spill the tea" 溢美之词 (yì měi zhī cí) 意思是指泄露原本需要保密的信息，也可以比喻分享过多或不必要的细节，常带有八卦的意味。
### Usage Examples:
- **English:** "I can't believe she spilled the tea about our date! It was supposed to be a surprise!"
- **Chinese:** “我真没想到她在我们的约会时就打听了好多消息，本来是惊喜的！” 
- **English:** "He spilled the tea about his new job to his friends, and now everyone knows!"
- **Chinese:** “他把新工作的消息给朋友们都说了，现在全都知道了！”
### Related Expressions:
- **英语相关表达:** “Let the cat out of the bag” (泄露秘密)
- **中文相关表达:** “把壶里没冒出那股热气说出去了” (把锅里没冒出来的那股热气说出去了)
