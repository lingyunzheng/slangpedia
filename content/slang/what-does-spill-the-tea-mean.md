### Spill the Tea (English)
#### Definition
To share confidential or often juicy information, usually in a gossiping context. It's used when someone is revealing secrets, tips, or private details that they have learned or overheard.
#### Example
"Hey, I heard that Mary and John are planning to get married! She spilled the tea at the last company party!"
### 倒茶 （Chinese）
#### Definition
分享秘密或往往是关于小道消息的信息，通常在八卦的背景下使用。它指的是有人泄露了他们得知或听到的机密或私人细节。
#### 例句
“嘿，我听说玛丽和约翰计划要结婚了！她在上次公司聚会上无意中透露了这个消息！”
