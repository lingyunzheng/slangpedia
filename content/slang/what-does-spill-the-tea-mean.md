**English:** spill the tea  
**Chinese:** 泄露秘密 (xiè luò mì mì)  
**Explanation:** This slang is used to describe the act of sharing confidential or exclusive information that was previously kept secret. It's often used when someone is gossiping or revealing something private or undisclosed. For example, if your friend tells you all the juicy details about a celebrity's behind-the-scenes drama, they are "spill[ing] the tea."
