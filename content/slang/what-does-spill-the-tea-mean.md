```markdown
## Spill the Tea
### English
**Meaning:** To share gossip or confidential information that was previously kept secret, often with a sense of excitement or intent to involve others.
**Example:**  
- *English:* "_I've got some tea to spill._" means "_I have some gossip to share._"
### 中文
**含义:** 分享秘密消息或私人信息，通常伴随着一种兴奋感或想将他人卷入其中的意图。
**例句:**  
- *中文:* "_我有一些茶要倒出来._" 意味着 "_我有一些八卦要分享._"
```
In both languages, the phrase communicates the act of sharing secrets or gossip, emphasizing the informal and often unverified nature of the information being shared.
