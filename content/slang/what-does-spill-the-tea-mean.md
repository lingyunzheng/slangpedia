# Slang Explanation: Spill the Tea
## English
To **spill the tea** means to share confidential or private information with others, often without the consent of the individuals involved. It can also imply gossipy or revealing discussions.
## Chinese
** spill the tea** 指的是泄露保密或私密的信息，通常是在未经涉及到的人的同意下公开这些信息。也可以用来形容八卦或揭露性的谈话。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "I can't believe she spilled the tea about our vacation plans to the whole office!"
- **Chinese:** "真没想到她竟然把我们的假期计划都告诉了全办公室的人！"
- **English:** "He spilled the tea on who the new CEO would be, and it's causing a lot of rumors."
- **Chinese:** "他泄露了新CEO的人选，这引起了很多谣言。"
