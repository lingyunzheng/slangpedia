## Spill the Tea (English)
**Explanation:**
To "spill the tea" means to share confidential or juicy information with others, often revealing something surprising or scandalous that was previously unknown. It's like letting the cat out of the bag, but it's more casual and often involves gossip or prying into others' private lives.
**Example Usage:**
- "Did you hear that Taylor and Jordan are breaking up? Someone finally spilled the tea on the whole situation!"
## 泄露茶壶里的秘密 (Chinese)
**解释：**
“泄露出茶壶里的秘密”意味着分享机密或令人兴奋的信息，通常涉及八卦或窥探他人的私生活，透露一些之前未知的令人惊讶或有争议的事情。
**例句使用：**
- “听说泰勒和乔丹要分手了，有人终于泄露出他们关系破裂的全部内情！”
