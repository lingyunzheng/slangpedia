### Spill the tea
**English Explanation:**  
To "spill the tea" means to tell others confidential or secret information that should not be shared. It's like when you accidentally let out a secret that was supposed to be kept between you and someone else.
**Simplified Explanation:**  
泄漏小道消息
- **中文解释：** "泄漏小道消息" 指的是泄露不应该透露的秘密或私人信息。就像不小心将本应保密的信息告诉了其他人。
- **例句：** 别把我的新手机是谁给我买的这件事说出去，否则你会“泄漏小道消息”。
