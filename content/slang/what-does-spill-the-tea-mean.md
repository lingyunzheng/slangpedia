## Spill the Tea (英汉解释)
**Spill the Tea**:
- **中文解释**: 爆料，透露秘密或八卦。
- **英文解释**: To spill the tea means to unintentionally reveal or share confidential or juicy information. It usually refers to gossip or details that were meant to be kept private.
**Example**:
- **中文例句**: 她无意中在朋友面前说了那个秘密，简直就是在“spill the tea”。
- **英文例句**: She accidentally let the cat out of the bag and spilled the tea in front of her friends.
