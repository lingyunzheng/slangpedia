## Spill the Tea
**English:** 
To reveal or share confidential or juicy information, typically gossip about someone or something. 
**Chinese:** 
泄露独家猛料或八卦消息，通常是指分享关于某人或某事的秘密或有趣的信息。
Example Usage:
- **English:** "She's been spilling the tea about her co-worker's relationship troubles."
- **中文:** "她一直在泄露她同事的感情问题上的独家猛料。"
