## Spill the tea
**English Definition:**
To share gossip or information, often confidential or revealing, with others.
**Simplified Explanation:**
When someone "spills the tea," they're telling others about secrets or juicy details that they shouldn't be sharing. It's like giving away all the behind-the-scenes information.
**Example Usage:**
- "After the meeting, Sarah went to the coffee machine and spilled the tea about the new layoffs."
- "Don't be mad if I spill the tea; it's just that I couldn't keep it a secret anymore."
**Chinese Translation:**
分享八卦或信息，通常是保密或揭露性的，与他人共享。
**简化解释:**
当有人说“倒茶”时，他们是在告诉别人一些秘密或有趣的细节，本来不应该告诉别人的。这就像是泄露了所有的幕后消息。
**使用示例:**
- "在会议之后，莎拉去了咖啡机旁，开始倒茶说有关新裁员的消息。"
- "别生气我倒茶了，只是我实在憋不住不想再保密了。"
