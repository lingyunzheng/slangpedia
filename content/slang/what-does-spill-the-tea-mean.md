## Spill the Tea
**English:** To reveal or share confidential or juicy information, often in a gossip-worthy manner.
**中文:** 泄露或分享机密或有趣的内幕消息，通常是八卦性质的。
**Example Usage:**
- *English:* "She spilled the tea about the new product launch date, and now everyone knows it's happening next month."
- *中文:* "她把新的产品上市日期给泄漏了，现在大家都明白下个月就要发布了。"
---
This phrase is often used in social and media contexts where sharing secrets or recent developments is common.
