## Spill the Tea
### English
**Spill the tea**: To share confidential or private information, often in a gossiping or informal manner. It's like telling all the juicy details that were meant to be kept hidden.
### Chinese (简体中文)
**Spill the tea**: 释出茶 (shì chū chá) - 泄露小道消息或隐私，通常是八卦和非正式的方式。就像泄露了所有的秘辛，本来是打算保密的。
### Usage Examples
- **English**: She spilled the tea about the new product launch schedule.
- **Chinese**: 她把新产品发布计划的细节全部泄露出去了。
