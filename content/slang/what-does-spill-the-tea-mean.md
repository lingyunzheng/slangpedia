## Spill the Tea
**English:** To share confidential or insider information; to gossip.
**Literal Translation (English to Chinese):** 泄露茶秘 (xiè lòu chá mì)
**Chinese Explanation:** 指分享秘密或内部信息，通常带有八卦和小道消息的意思。类似于八卦或闲言碎语，但更侧重分享那些只有少数人才知道的秘密或不为人知的信息。
**Usage Example:**
- English: "She knows all the ins and outs of the company because she has been working here for a long time. You should ask her to spill the tea."
- Chinese: "她对公司的事情了如指掌，因为她在这里工作很长时间了。你应该请她泄露茶秘。”
**Note:** The expression "spill the tea" is often used in contexts where someone wants to share a surprise or reveal a secret that is not yet public knowledge. In Chinese culture, there is a similar expression, but it might not carry the exact same connotation of gossip or sharing secrets.
