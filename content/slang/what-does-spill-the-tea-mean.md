## Spill the Tea
### English
To "spill the tea" means to share confidential or exclusive information, often uninvited and usually in a gossiping manner. It's about letting out secrets that were meant to be kept private.
**Example:**
- She finally spilled the tea about the company's budget cuts.
### 中文
“ spill the tea” 指的是泄露机密或独家信息，通常是未经邀请地八卦分享秘密。这个词用来形容那些本该保密却被泄露出去的秘密。
**例句:**
- 她终于把公司裁减预算的秘密都说了。
