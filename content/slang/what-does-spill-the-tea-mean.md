## Spill the Tea
**English:** 
To share confidential or behind-the-scenes information, often in a dramatic or gossipy way.
**Chinese (电商平台常用):**
透底消息，分享一些内部动态或者有意无意之间传播某种小道消息，通常带有八卦或者引起某些话题的效果。
**Explanation:** 
"Spill the tea" is a common slang term used to describe the act of sharing private information or revealing secrets. In an e-commerce context, it can refer to leaking insider information about products, sales, or business practices in a way that stirs up interest or debate among consumers or competitors.
