## English Slang: "No Thoughts - Head Empty"
- **Meaning**: This phrase describes a state of not having any ideas, thoughts, or opinions about something. It can also indicate being completely clueless or confused about a topic.
- **Example**: "He just looked at me with a blank stare, like he had no thoughts in his head about the project we were discussing."
## 中文俚语： "没想法 - 头空"
- **意思**：这个短语用来形容没有任何想法、意见或观点的状态，也可以表示对某个话题完全不清楚或困惑。
- **示例**：当他只是用空洞的眼神看着我，像是对正在讨论的项目没有任何想法时。
