## 中文解释
**no thoughts head empty**: 指大脑完全没有思考或主意，脑子空空的。可以形容一个人思维迟钝或做决定时毫无头绪。
## 英文解释
**no thoughts head empty**: means having a completely empty head with no thoughts or ideas. It describes someone who is mentally sluggish or struggling to make decisions without any clear thoughts.
