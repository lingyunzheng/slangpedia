## No Thoughts Head Empty
**English:**  
* Empty mind, not thinking about anything  
* Could also mean feeling particularly confused or lost
**Chinese:**  
* 头空空，没想法  
* 也可能表示特别迷茫或不知所措
Example Usage:  
- English: "I just had a meeting with my boss and my head is completely empty right now."  
- Chinese: "我刚刚和老板开完会，现在脑袋空空的完全没想法。"
