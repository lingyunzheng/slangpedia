## English Slang: "No Thoughts Head Empty"
- **Meaning:** When someone says they have "no thoughts head empty," it means they don't have any ideas or thoughts in their head at the moment. It implies that their mind is completely blank or empty.
- **Usage Example:** "I’ve been sitting here for 15 minutes trying to think of what to write, but my head is completely empty! How do you deal with writer’s block like this?"
## 中文俚语: “脑袋里空空如也，思无一物”
- **意思:** 当有人说他们的“脑袋里空空如也，思无一物”时，意味着他们脑海中没有任何想法或思绪。这表示他们的头脑是完全空白或者空空如也。
- **使用示例:** "我在这里坐了15分钟，试图想出要写什么，但我的脑袋却是空空如也！你如何应对这种写作障碍？"
