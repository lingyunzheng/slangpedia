### 中文解释
**no thoughts head empty** - 指头脑中空空如也，没有想法或主意。通常用来形容一个人状态懒散，毫无创新或积极性。
### 英文解释
**no thoughts head empty** - means having a completely empty mind with no ideas or agendas. It often describes someone who is lazy, uncreative, or lacks enthusiasm.
