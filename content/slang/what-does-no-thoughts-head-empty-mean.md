### 中文解释
"没有想法，脑袋空空"
#### 英文解释
"No Thoughts, Head Empty"
This phrase is used to describe a state of having no ideas or thoughts, usually in a relaxed or dreamy manner. It can also imply a lack of concentration or focus.
**Example Usage:**
*中文：工作了一下午，我还是没有想法，脑袋空空的。*
*英文：I worked on the project all afternoon, but I still had no thoughts and felt my head was empty.*
*中文：他最近总是说没有想法，脑袋空空的，似乎对工作失去了兴趣。*
*英文：He's been saying he has no thoughts and feels his head is empty lately, as if he's lost interest in his work.*
