## 中文 slang: 头空 (tóukōng) - head empty
**Explanation:** 当你觉得自己脑子一片空白、毫无思绪的时候，可以使用“头空”来形容这种状态。一般用于形容心情烦闷、感到迷茫或者突然失去思路时的状态。
**Example sentence:** 这个问题我想了很久，但还是头空，不知道该怎么回答。（我这个问题想了很久，但还是没有任何头绪，不知道该如何回答。）
## English slang: Brainfart
**Explanation:** "Brainfart" is a colloquial term used to describe a moment when you suddenly feel like you can't think clearly—your mind goes blank and you're unable to remember or think of something you normally would have known or could do. It's often used humorously to explain moments of forgetfulness or confusion.
**Example sentence:** I was trying to recall the name of the book but had a brainfart and couldn't remember it at all. (I was trying to recall the name of the book but suddenly couldn't think of it, like my brain went blank.)
