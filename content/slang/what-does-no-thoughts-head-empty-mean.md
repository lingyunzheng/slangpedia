## 中文 slang: 头秃了 (tóu tū le)
**Explanation:** 
意思是指脑袋里空空的，没有想法或主意。通常用来形容一个人思路不清晰或者比较愚钝的状态。
**英文 slang:** head bald
**Explanation:**
This slang means you have an empty head with no thoughts or ideas. It's often used to describe someone who seems to lack mental clarity or is not particularly clever.
