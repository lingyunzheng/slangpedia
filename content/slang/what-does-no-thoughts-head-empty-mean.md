### "No Thoughts, Head Empty" (English-Spanish)
- **English:** "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
- **Spanish:** "Sin Pensamientos, Cabeza Vacia"
**Explanation:**
This phrase, commonly used in casual conversation, is a playful way to describe someone who is completely uninterested, bored, or lackadaisical about a topic or situation. It suggests that the person’s mind is completely blank or not engaged, often due to a lack of motivation, interest, or excitement.
For example:
- *English:* "He just sat there, no thoughts head empty, during the whole presentation."
- *Spanish:* "Él solo se sentó allí, sin pensamientos, cabeza vacía, durante todo el presentación."
**When to use:**
- To describe someone who is disinterested or uninvolved.
- In contexts where you want to express that someone isn’t paying attention or doesn’t have any ideas about something.
