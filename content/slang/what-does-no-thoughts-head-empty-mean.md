## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
**English**: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
- **Definition**: This phrase is used to describe someone who is confused, lacking in ideas, or seems out of it.
- **Example Usage**: "He's been up all night partying, no thoughts, head empty when he walked in."
**中文**: “no thoughts, head empty”
- **释义**：这个短语用来形容某人困惑、缺乏想法或是好像神志不清的状态。
- **例句**：她昨晚熬夜狂欢，进来的时候头空空的，完全没有头绪。
