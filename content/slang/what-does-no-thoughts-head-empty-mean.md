## 英语解释：no thoughts, head empty
- **Meaning**: When someone has "no thoughts, head empty," it means they are deeply absorbed in thought, daydreaming, or temporarily without any worries or plans. It can also suggest they are completely exhausted or minded to ignore something.
## 中文解释：脑袋空空，没有思绪
- **含义**：当某人说自己"脑袋空空，没有思绪"时，意味着他们要么正沉浸在深思、白日梦中，要么没有任何忧虑或计划。也可以表示他们疲惫无比或选择忽视某些事情。
