### No Thoughts / Head Empty (英汉双解俚语)
- **中文释义**：形容一个人完全没有思考能力，大脑一片空白的状态。
- **英文释义**：Refers to someone who has no thought or thinking ability, basically having an empty head.
Example Usage:
- **中文示例**：他被问题弄得头空空的。
- **英文示例**：He's been stumped by the question and his head is completely empty.
