## English: No Thoughts, Head Empty
- **Definition:** When someone is completely clueless, confused, or without any idea about a situation or question.
- **Example:** "I asked him what his plans are for the weekend, and he just gave me a blank look. Clearly, no thoughts, head empty!"
## 中文: 空空如也，脑中一片空白
- **定义:** 当某人对某种情况或问题完全没头绪，感到困惑或者没有任何想法时。
- **例句:** "我问他这周末有什么计划，他只是给了我一个空洞的眼神。显然，脑子里空空如也，一片空白！"
