### English
**No thoughts, head empty**
- Meaning: This phrase is used when someone has no ideas, thoughts, or plans. It describes a state of blankness in the mind.
- Example: If you ask someone for suggestions, and they appear confused or unable to come up with any ideas, you might say, "Looks like their head is empty right now."
### 中文
**没思路，脑袋空**
- 含义：这句话用来形容某个人没有想法、思路或计划，形容大脑一片空白的状态。
- 例句：如果你向某人寻求建议，而他们显得困惑，或开不了口，说不出任何想法，你可以说：“他们的脑袋现在是空的。”
