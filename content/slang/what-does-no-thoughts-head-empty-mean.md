### Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
- **English:** "No Thoughts, Head Empty" is an informal expression used to describe someone who is in a state of mind where they are not thinking clearly or deeply. It often indicates a lack of mental focus or concentration.
- **Chinese:** "没有思考，脑袋空空"（méiyǒu sīkǎo, èdɑn kōngkōng）描述一个人思维不清晰或没有专注的情况。它常用来形容注意力不集中或精神空虚的状态。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I couldn't focus on my work, my mind was just blank – no thoughts, head empty."
- **Chinese:** "我工作时总是注意力不集中，头脑一片空白——没有思考，脑袋空空。"
