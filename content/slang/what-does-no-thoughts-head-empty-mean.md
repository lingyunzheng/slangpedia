## 中文解释
**no thoughts head empty**: 表示一个人脑子一片空白，没有什么想法或者主意。相当于“脑袋空空如也”。
## 英文解释
**no thoughts head empty**: means someone's mind is completely empty with no ideas or thoughts. It's equivalent to "brain is empty" or "head is blank".
