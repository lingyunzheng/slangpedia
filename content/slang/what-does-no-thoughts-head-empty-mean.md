# No Thoughts Head Empty
**English:**  
_No Thoughts Head Empty_ is a slang term used to describe a state of being very confused or having absolutely no ideas. It can also indicate lethargy or a complete lack of motivation. The phrase implies that one's mind is essentially empty or devoid of any clear thoughts.
**中文：**  
"_No Thoughts Head Empty_" 是一个俚语表达，用来形容非常困惑或者完全没有想法的状态。这句话也可以表示精神上的疲惫或完全没有动力。简单来说，就是形容头脑空空如也，缺少清晰的思路。
