## 中文解释
头铁丝 - 表达脑袋空空如也，没有什么想法的状态。就像一根铁丝一样，没有任何弯曲和力量。
## English Explanation
No Brainer - Used to describe a situation where someone has no thoughts or ideas, just like a "no-brainer," which implies that the situation is so obvious or simple that it requires no thought or consideration.
