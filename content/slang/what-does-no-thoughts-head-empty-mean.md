## 中文解释：脑袋空空
- **含义**: 表示某人在某个时刻没有主意或没什么想法。
- **例子**: "他都晚上下班了还在那儿发呆，脑袋空空的，明显是没想好今晚吃什么。"
## English Explanation: No Thoughts, Head Empty
- **Meaning**: It indicates that someone doesn't have any ideas or thoughts at a certain moment.
- **Example**: "He's still zoning out after work time, which shows his head is empty; clearly, he hasn't figured out what he's going to have for dinner."
