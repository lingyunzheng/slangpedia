## English Slang: "no thoughts head empty"
- **Meaning**: This phrase is used to describe a state of being completely uninterested, bored, or having no ideas or opinions about something. It suggests that the person's mind is completely blank or idle regarding a topic.
## 中文俚语: “no thoughts head empty”
- **意思**: 这句话用来形容完全没兴趣、无聊或对某件事没有任何想法或意见的状态。它表示一个人在某个话题上完全没有思路或大脑一片空白。
