# "No Thoughts, Head Empty" - English to Chinese Slang Explanations
## English
- **No Thoughts, Head Empty**: This phrase is used to describe someone who is completely unaware or lackadaisical about something. It means the person is not paying attention or simply doesn't care about the situation at hand.
## Chinese
- **没思路，脑袋空**: 这个短语用来形容某人对某事完全无意识或漫不经心。表示这个人根本不在意或对当前的情况完全没有关注。
### Examples
- **English**: "He's been so busy with work, he hasn't even noticed his girlfriend is unhappy."
- **Chinese**: "他工作太忙了，根本没思路，脑袋空，都没注意到女朋友不开心了。"
- **English**: "I tried to suggest a new project, but she just had no thoughts, head empty."
- **Chinese**: "我提议一个新的项目，但她完全没思路，脑袋空，根本就没有反应。"
