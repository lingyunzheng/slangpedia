## English: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
### Chinese: "脑袋空空" (nào dai kōng kōng)
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "No Thoughts, Head Empty" is a colloquial expression used to describe a state of mind where someone is not thinking about anything, or they are feeling mentally empty or disconnected. It can be used to describe moments of boredom, absent-mindedness, or when an individual is daydreaming.
- **Chinese:** "脑袋空空" (nào dai kōng kōng) literally translates to "empty head" and is often used in a similar context to describe someone who is not thinking about anything, feeling bored, or simply mindlessly going through the motions.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I was just sitting there with no thoughts, head empty, watching the raindrops fall."
- **Chinese:** "我坐在那里，脑袋空空的，看着雨滴落下来。"
