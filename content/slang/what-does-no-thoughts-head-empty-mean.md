## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
**English:**  
- **Meaning:**  
Describes someone who is completely blank, lacking any ideas or thoughts. It can also be used to indicate disinterest or apathy.
- **Example:**  
"After a night of binge-watching TV, he woke up with no thoughts in his head, totally empty."
**Chinese:**  
- **意思:**  
形容某人完全没有思绪或想法，也可以用来表示无兴趣或者漠不关心。
- **例句:**  
"经过一夜的连续追剧，他醒来的时候脑袋空空的，完全没有思绪。"
