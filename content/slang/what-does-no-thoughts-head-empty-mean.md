### 中文 slang: 头_pwd_
#### Explanation:
- **英文**: head_pwd
- **含义**: 头_pwd_是一个非常随意和非正式的说法，用来形容一个人脑子一片空白，思维停滞的状态。类似于"空了"或者"没主意"。当一个人在面临决策困难、无法快速找到解决方案或者陷入某种困境时，可能会说"我的头_pwd_"来表达自己的困惑或者无力感。
### English slang: no thoughts, head empty
#### Explanation:
- **中文**: 头空
- **Meaning**: "no thoughts, head empty" is a casual and informal way to describe someone whose mind is blank and devoid of ideas. It's similar to saying "mind blank" or "no ideas." When someone is having difficulty making a decision, failing to come up with a quick solution, or is stuck in a situation without a clear thought, they might say "I have no thoughts, head is empty" to express their confusion or helplessness.
