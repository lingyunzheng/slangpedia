## 中文 slang: 头皮痒 (tóupí yǎng)
- **Explanation:** 这个表达通常用来形容一个人完全没有主意，思维空白。就像头皮痒，让人感到一种难以集中注意力的轻微不适感。
## English slang: Braindead/Blank as a wall
- **Explanation:** This phrase is used to describe someone who is completely mindless or has no mental acuity, as if their mind is as empty as a wall. It can also suggest someone is not paying attention or is unresponsive.
