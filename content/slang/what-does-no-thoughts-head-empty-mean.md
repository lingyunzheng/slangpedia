### 中文解释
**头铁**: 形容脑袋空空如也，没什么想法或者思路。相当于“一片空白”。
### 英文解释
**Head Empty**: Used to describe a situation where one has no thoughts or ideas, essentially an empty head. It's like being blank or without any concept.
