## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
**English:**  
"Thoughts" in this slang context means ideas or mental processes. "No Thoughts, Head Empty" implies someone is not thinking or is not conscious, often used to describe someone who seems zombie-like or in a daze.
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
"Thoughts" 在这种俚语的用法中指的是思维或心理过程。"没想法，脑袋空" 表示某人没有思考或意识不清，常用来形容看起来像行尸走肉或处于恍惚状态的人。
**English Example Sentence:**  
"She's been staring at the wall for hours; she must have no thoughts, head empty."
**Chinese Example Sentence:**  
她盯着墙好几小时了，肯定是没想法，脑袋空。

## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Thoughts, Head Empty"
**English:**  
"Thoughts" here means mental processes or ideas. "No Thoughts, Head Empty" means someone is mentally vacant or not engaged in thinking, often describing a state of mindlessness or boredom.
**Chinese (Traditional):**  
"Thoughts" 在這種俚語用途中指的是思考或心理過程。"沒想法，頭空" 表示某人沒有思考或精神懈怠，常用来形容一種沒有思緒或厌倦的狀態。
**English Example Sentence:**  
"He was so bored he could hardly focus; he was in a state of no thoughts, head empty."
**Chinese Example Sentence:**  
他太無趣了，幾乎無法集中精神；他處於沒有想法，頭空的狀態。
