# "No Thoughts Head Empty"
## English
- **Meaning**: Having nothing to think about or no ideas in your mind. It can also imply confusion or a lack of clarity.
- **Example**: "I feel like my head is empty today; no thoughts at all."
## Chinese (简体中文)
- **意思**: 没有思考的东西，头脑空空。也可以用来形容迷茫或缺乏清晰的思维。
- **例句**: "今天我的脑子里空空的，完全没有想法。"
