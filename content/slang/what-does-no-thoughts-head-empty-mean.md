## 中文解释：头都不晕
- **含义**：形容头脑一片空白、完全没有想法的状态。
- **使用场景**：通常在讨论思维停滞不前或者突然间不知道该做什么的时候使用。
## English Explanation: Head Doesn't Even Spin
- **Meaning**: Describes a state of complete blankness in the mind, with no thoughts whatsoever.
- **Usage**: Commonly used when discussing a lack of ideas or being unable to think clearly, such as when you're completely stuck and don't know what to do next.
