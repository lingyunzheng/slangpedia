### 中文解释 ("Chinese Explanation")
**no thoughts head empty**  
脑空空：形容一个人思想空白、毫无想法的状态。就像脑袋里被清空了一样。
### 英文解释 ("English Explanation")
**no thoughts head empty**  
Head Empty: Describes a state where someone has no thoughts or ideas. It's as if their mind has been completely emptied.
---
Here are a few examples of how these phrases might be used:
#### 中文例句 ("Chinese Examples")
1. 她刚才还滔滔不绝地说话，现在却脑空空的，不知道该说些什么。
(She was just talking non-stop just now, but now her head is empty, and she doesn't know what to say.)
2. 做了一天的论文，我已经头空空了，想喝茶放松一下。
(After writing a whole day of papers, I'm brain dead, I want to have some tea to relax.)
#### 英文例句 ("English Examples")
1. She was just talking endlessly just now, but now her head is empty and she doesn't know what to say.
2. After a day of writing papers, I’m brain dead; I just want to have some tea to relax.
