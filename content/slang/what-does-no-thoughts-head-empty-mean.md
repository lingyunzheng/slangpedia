## 中文解释：no thoughts head empty
- **含义：** 表示大脑一片空白，没有思路。
- **例子：**
- 问：这个题目怎么写啊？
- 答：努力想了一下，但我头依然空空的，no thoughts。
## English Explanation: no thoughts head empty
- **Meaning:** Indicates that one's mind is blank and they have no ideas.
- **Example:**
- Q: How do I approach this problem?
- A: I thought about it for a while, but my head is empty, no thoughts.
