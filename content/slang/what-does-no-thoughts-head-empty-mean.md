## no thoughts head empty
**English:** 
- *Head blank, no thoughts*: Describes someone who is having a hard time coming up with ideas, thoughts, or solutions. Also used to indicate a state of being confused or not knowing what to do.
**Chinese:** 
- *头脑一片空白,没有任何想法*: 形容一个人想不出主意、没有思路或解决方案的状态。也用来形容困惑或者不知道该怎么办的状态。
