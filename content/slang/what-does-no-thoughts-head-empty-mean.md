## No Thoughts, Head Empty (English)
**Meaning:** 
Literally translated, it suggests having no ideas or thoughts, a mind that is completely blank or empty. This phrase is often used to describe a state of complete confusion or a lack of motivation.
**Example Use:**
"I woke up this morning with no thoughts in my head, couldn't even think of what to make for breakfast!"

## 脑袋空空如也 (脑袋 dǎn dài, head; 空空 kōng kōng, empty; 如也 rú yě, just so; like that) (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 
"Just so empty," literally meaning the head is completely empty. It is used to describe a situation where someone is distracted, confused, or has no idea what to do next.
**Example Use:**
"我今天早上醒过来，脑袋空空如也，根本不知道吃什么早餐。"
(I woke up this morning and my head was completely empty; I had no idea what to eat for breakfast.)
