## English Slang: No Thoughts, Head Empty
- **Meaning**: When someone is so confused, overwhelmed, or in a state of shock that they don't have any clear ideas or thoughts.
- **Example Usage**: "After the unexpected news, he was just standing there with no thoughts, head empty."
## 中文俚语: 没思路，一片空白
- **意思**: 当某人感到困惑、不知所措或震惊到没有清晰想法或思路时的状态。
- **例句**: "在听到这个突如其来的消息后，他只是站在那里，一片空白，没有思路。"
