# "No Thoughts, Head Empty" (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
## English
- **Meaning**: This phrase is used to describe someone who is completely idle, unprepossessing, or mentally blank. It can also mean someone who lacks ideas or creativity.
- **Example**: "I haven't had any new ideas for my project, I feel like I have no thoughts and my head is completely empty."
## 中文
- **意思**：这个短语用来形容某人完全 idle 或缺乏创造力，思想空洞。也可以指某人没有主意或创造性。
- **例句**："我对这个项目没有什么新想法，感觉自己完全是空的，脑袋里没有思绪。"
