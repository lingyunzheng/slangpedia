# Skibidi
## English Explanation
"Skibidi" is a fun, catchphrase that originated in the UK around 2013, when it became a meme and part of internet slang. It is often used to express excitement or as a humorous way to say something isn't a big deal. For example, if a friend says they just got a new phone, you might respond, "Skibidi, that’s cool!"
## Chinese Explanation
"Skibidi" 是起源于英国的一句网络俚语，大约在2013年流行起来，常被用作表达兴奋或一种幽默的方式来表示某事不算什么。例如，当一个朋友告诉你说他们刚刚得到了一部新手机，你可以用“Skibidi，挺不错！”来回应。
