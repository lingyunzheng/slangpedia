# Slang Term: "Skibidi"
## English
**Meaning**: 
- Skibidi is an informal term with a playful and nonsensical quality. It can be used to express excitement, to interrupt someone, or simply to add some humor to a conversation. It's often used in meme formats or as part of internet culture.
**Example Usage**: 
- "Hey, did you hear about the new restaurant opened downtown? Skibidi, I'm super excited to try their new menu!"
## Chinese
