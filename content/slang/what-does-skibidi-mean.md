## Skibidi
**English:**  
"Skibidi" is a viral dance challenge that gained popularity on TikTok. It features a rapid, repetitive arm and leg movement that participants perform to the tune of the song "Skibidi" by the Spanish band Kinness. The dance has multiple variations and is often used as a meme or as a playful way for friends to coordinate and entertain each other.
**Chinese:**  
"Skibidi" 是一个在 TikTok 上流行的舞蹈挑战。它包含了快速重复的手臂和腿部动作，参与者在Kinness乐队的“Skibidi”歌曲伴奏下跳舞。这个舞蹈有多样的变体，常被用作网红梗或朋友间协调娱乐的一种方式。
