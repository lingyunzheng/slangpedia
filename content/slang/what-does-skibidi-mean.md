## Skibidi (English)
**Meaning:** 
- ***Bro/Brother***: Often used as a casual greeting or term of endearment among friends, especially in casual settings. It's a playful and informal way to address a male friend.
**Usage Example:**
- *"Skibidi, man, how's it going?"*
## Skibidi (Chinese)
**意思:**
- ***兄弟***: 通常用于朋友之间的 casual 问候或亲昵称呼。在休闲场合，它是一种轻松而非正式的方式称呼男性朋友。
**用例示例:**
- *"Skibidi, 伙计，你好吗？"*
