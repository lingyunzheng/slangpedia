## Skibidi
**English:**
"Skibidi" is a term that originated as part of a viral dance challenge and meme. It usually describes a playful or exaggerated motion, often associated with a specific hand movement resembling a duck's head bobbing. The term has been used in various contexts ranging from dance to references in online content.
**Chinese (简体字):**
"Skibidi" 是一个源自病毒舞蹈挑战和 meme 的术语。通常它描述一种轻松或夸张的运动，常与特定的手部动作相关，类似于鸭子的头部摇摆。这个术语被用于各种上下文，从舞蹈到在线内容的引用。
**English Example:**
"Let’s do the skibidi dance at the party this weekend!"

“我们这个周末在聚会上跳 skibidi 舞吧！”
