# Skibidi
## English
**Skibidi:** This term is primarily associated with the catchphrase from the song "Skibidi" by UK dance-pop producer Sens & Kasi. It's widely used as a cheeky or playful exclamation, often in a silly or teasing context. People might use it when they're playing around or trying to be amusing.
### Example Usage
"Are you ready to dance? Skibidi!"
## 中文
**Skibidi:** 这个词源于 UK 流行舞曲制作人 Sens & Kasi 的歌曲《Skibidi》。它通常作为一种调皮或有趣的感叹词被广泛使用，常常用于装可爱或搞轻松幽默的情境中。当人们想要嬉戏打闹或试图逗人开心时，会用到这个词。
### 例句
“准备好跳舞了吗？Skibidi！”
