# Skibidi
## English
**Skibidi**: A term that originated from the chorus of the song "Carambar" by French duo Dmatchik & Dinos. It's often used as an exclamation of excitement or surprise, similar to "Wohoo" or "Yay!". It can also be used in syncing dances or challenges where participants yell "Skibidi" in unison for fun and excitement.
## Chinese
**Skibidi**: 这个词起源自法语组合 Dmatchik & Dinos 的歌曲 "Carambar" 中的副歌部分。它通常用作表示兴奋或惊奇的感叹词，类似于“Wohoo”或“Yay!”。在参与同步跳舞或挑战时，参与者会齐声喊出“Skibidi”以增加乐趣和兴奋感。
