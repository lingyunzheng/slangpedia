# Skibidi
## English
**Definition:** 
"Skibidi" is a playful and often nonsensical term that's used in casual conversation to express surprise, excitement, or to punctuate a joke. It can be used almost interchangeably with "skidaddley," another slang term that serves the same purpose.
**Example Usage:**
- "Did you see that goal? Skibidi!"
- "We're going on a road trip tomorrow. Skibidi!"
## Chinese

**使用示例：**
