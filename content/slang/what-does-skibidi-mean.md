## Skibidi (中文: 肴炮)
### English Explanation
"Skibidi" is a term mostly used in internet slang, particularly in gaming communities and memes. It often refers to an overpowered or extremely useful in-game item, power-up, or strategy that gives a player a significant advantage. Think of it as that one weapon or power-up that changes the game for a player.
### Chinese Explanation
"肴炮" (yáo pào) is a slang term used in Chinese internet language, especially in gaming contexts and memes. It describes an extremely powerful or heavily boosted item, skill, or tactic in a game that gives a player a huge advantage, often changing the game dynamics.类似于游戏中的神器或超级能力，能使玩家在游戏过程中获得明显的优势。
