# Skibidi (A phrase often used in internet challenges and meme culture)
**English:** 
"Skibidi" is a phrase that originated from internet culture, particularly within online challenges and memes. It often accompanies a dance move or challenge where the phrase is chanted or shouted out before or during the action. The term is often used to build excitement or setup a challenge.
**Chinese:**
