## Skibidi
### English:
**Skibidi:** A playful, improvisational phrase often used in dance challenge videos and internet memes, originating from the song "Skibidi" by Littlemix. It's typically used to create a viral dance move or style, where participants are encouraged to invent their own moves while shouting the phrase in rhythm. 
### Chinese:
**Skibidi:** 一个充满趣味和即兴发挥的短语，通常用于舞蹈挑战视频和网络病毒式传播中，源自 Littlemix 的歌曲《Skibidi》。人们在跳这种舞蹈时，通常会按照节奏喊出这个短语，以此创作出独特的舞蹈动作或风格。它是创造个人特色和互动的一种方式。
