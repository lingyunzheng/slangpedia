## Skibidi (English)
### Definition
"Skibidi" is a slang term that comes from the song "Skibidi," a catchy tune that was very popular among children, especially in the late 2000s. The term itself doesn't have a specific definition, but it's often used as an exclamation or to communicate a sense of excitement or enthusiasm.
### Usage Example
- When someone does something fun or impressive, a friend might exclaim, "That was skibidi cool!" 
### Etymology
The term gained popularity due to a viral song by the Romanian singer Liviu Clain, released in 2007. The song featured repetitive lyrics and a catchy hook that kids found easy to sing and dance to.

“Skibidi”是来自2007年罗马尼亚歌手Liviu Clain发行的热门歌曲《Skibidi》的一句流行俚语。这个术语本身没有具体的定义，但它通常被用作一种兴奋或热诚的表达方式。
### 使用示例
- 当有人做了一些有趣或令人印象深刻的事情时，朋友可能会惊叹：“那真是skibidi酷！” 
### 起源
该术语因2000年代末儿童对《Skibidi》歌曲的高度共鸣而流行起来。这首歌曲以其重复的歌词和容易歌唱和舞蹈的旋律受到了小朋友们的喜爱。
