# Skibidi
## English
**Definition:** An emphatic affirmation or expression of excitement, often used in a playful or colloquial manner. It is similar to saying "Yes!" or "Absolutely!" with a sense of enthusiasm.
**Example:**  
- "Can I go to the party tonight?"  
- "Skibidi! Of course you can!"
## 中文
**定义：** 一种强调肯定或表达兴奋的方式，通常用在一种轻松或非正式的场合下，类似于用一种兴奋的语气说“是！”或“当然！”。
**例句：**  
- "今晚我可以去派对吗？"  
- "Skibidi！当然可以！"
