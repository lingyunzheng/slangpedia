# What does "skibidi" mean?（skibidi 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Skibidi" is a word that was popularized in the early 2010s as part of a dance craze and was used in various songs and videos. It doesn't have a specific meaning, but it is associated with fun, excitement, and participation in a dance or trend.

“Skibidi”是一个在2010年初流行起来的词语，它与舞蹈热潮和多首歌曲、视频相关。这个词本身没有特定的意思，但它与乐趣、兴奋和参与舞蹈或潮流有关联。

The word "skibidi" originated from a track titled "Skibidi" by the Dutch hip hop group VNV Nation. The track became popular as part of a dance trend, especially among teenagers and young adults.
