## Skibidi
**English:** 
*a playful, often silly or exasperated expression used to mimic surprise or disbelief.*
**中文释义:**  
*一个充满趣味、通常带有戏谑或厌烦意味的表达，用来模仿惊讶或不信的表情。*
---
Example usage:
- English: "Skibidi, you can't be serious!"  
中文: "天啊，你不可能是在认真的吧！"
