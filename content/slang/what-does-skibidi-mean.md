## Skibidi: A Bilingual Slang Explanation
### English Explanation
**Skibidi** is a humorous and nonsensical phrase often used to imitate the sound of a rapid, mechanical or movement-related noise. It started as a meme popularized by the internet video game community and later became a catchphrase in various online contexts. Its exact origin is hard to trace, but it has been used in gaming streams, cartoons, and other online media.
### Chinese Explanation
**Skibidi** 是一个富有幽默感且毫无意义的词语，常用来模仿快速机械或动作的声音。这个词汇起源于网络游戏社区，并迅速在各种在线交流中流行开来。它的确切起源难以追溯，但如今它被广泛用于游戏直播、动画和其他网络媒体中。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "He did a skibidi hop to get across the room in record time."
