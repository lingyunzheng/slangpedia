## Skibidi
### English Explanation
"Skibidi" is an internet slang term that was popularized by the kid-friendly song "Skibidi" by Toby Keith's son, Tyler Kenney. The song's repetitive and catchy phrase "skibidi flibidy bobo" has become a meme, often used in humorous or nonsensical contexts. People use it to express confusion, excitement, or simply to add a playful, fun element to a conversation.
### Chinese Explanation
"Skibidi" 是一种网络俚语，起源于泰勒·肯尼 (Tyler Kenney) 的热门儿童歌曲《Skibidi》。这首歌中重复且朗朗上口的短语 "skibidi flibidy bobo" 已经成为了一个网络梗，通常用于幽默或无厘头的情境中。人们使用它来表达困惑、兴奋，或者只是为了让对话增添一些有趣和轻松的元素。
