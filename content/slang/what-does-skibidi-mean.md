# Skibidi
## English
**Meaning**: Skibidi is a playful and catchy term often used to describe something unexpectedly good or hilarious. It’s a slang term that adds a whimsical and positive tone to the conversation.
**Example**: "Just heard that you aced the exam! Skibidi, that’s awesome!"
### Origin: 
The term "skibidi" is believed to have originated from a song titled "Skibidi" by the British group Newsmonds, which became popular in the early 2000s. However, it has since been adopted into broader slang use for its energetic and positive connotation.
