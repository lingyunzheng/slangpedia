### L rizz
**English:** This is a playful or friendly way of referring to someone who is attractive or has a certain charm or style.
**Chinese:** 这是一个轻松或友好的方式来称呼某人很有吸引力或具有某种魅力或风格。
This term often implies a sense of cool or sassiness, and is commonly used in informal settings among friends or in social media.
