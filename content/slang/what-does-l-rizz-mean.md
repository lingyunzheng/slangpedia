# L rizz
## English:
"L rizz" is an internet slang used commonly in platforms like TikTok and memes. It's a playful and informal way to describe someone or something that exudes style, coolness, or a particular flair that is unique and often catches the eye. The term "rizz" itself is derived from the possessive form of "chill" and is used to emphasize a natural and effortless coolness. 
Example: "That guy at the party has some L rizz, man — he's totally breezing through all the conversation topics!"
## Chinese:

例句：“那个派对上的小伙子 L rizz 飘了，他真是自如地说着各种话题！”

Note: The term "L" in "L rizz" is often used as part of a phrase or hashtag, similar to "lit," indicating something that is very cool or exciting.
