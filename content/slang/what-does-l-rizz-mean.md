## L rizz
**English:**  
"L rizz" is a slang term often used in hip-hop culture and among young people to describe confidence, skill, or having a certain attitude or vibe that is revered within certain groups. It's like saying someone is "lit" or "on point."
**中文:**  
"L rizz" 是嘻哈文化及年轻人中间常用的一个俚语，用来形容一个人拥有自信、技能或某种受特定群体尊敬的态度或气场。可以说，它相当于“火”或“表现很棒”。
