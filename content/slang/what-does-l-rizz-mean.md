## L rizz
### English
"L rizz" is a playful and casual way to describe something as cool, stylish, or having a strong sense of style. It's commonly used in social media and amongst younger generations. For example, if someone has a unique and fashionable outfit, you might say, "Wow, L rizz!"
### Chinese
"L rizz" 用来形容某事物很酷、很有风格或者很有品味。这个俚语在社交媒体和年轻人中非常流行。例如，如果有人穿着非常独特且时尚的服装，你可以评价说：“哇，L rizz！”
