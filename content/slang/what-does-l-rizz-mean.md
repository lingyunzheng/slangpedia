## L rizz
**English:** A colloquial term often used to describe someone who is skilled, cool, or attractive, especially in a street or urban context. It can also imply a confident or laid-back attitude.
**Chino (中文):** 一个常用的俚语，用来形容某人很酷、很有吸引力或很擅长某事，特别是用于街头或城市文化语境中。它还可以暗示一种自信或随性的心态。
Example Usage:
- English: "That guy over there has some real L rizz!"
- Chino: "那边那个人有很地道的 L rizz!"
