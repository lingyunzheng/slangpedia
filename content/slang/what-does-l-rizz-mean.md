# L rizz
## English
"LRizz" is a playful way of saying "love," often accompanied by a wink or a nudge. It's a casual and affectionate term used between friends or in a romantic context. The "Rizz" part is a playful twist, with "izz" sometimes being a casual shortening of "it's" or "is."
## 中文
“L rizz” 是一种俏皮的方式来说“love”，通常会伴有眨眼或轻碰的动作。这个词在朋友之间或浪漫情境中使用，是一种轻松而亲昵的称呼。“Rizz”部分是一个俏皮的变化，有时“izz”会被当作“it's”或“is”的非正式缩写。
