## L rizz
**English:**  
"L rizz" is a slang term often used in the hip-hop and internet culture. It refers to someone who has a sharp, clever, or witty sense of humor, particularly in the way they use internet shorthand or slang to make clever comments or comebacks. It's a way of implying that someone is quick-witted and can adeptly use language in a humorous and effective manner.
**Chinese:**  
"L rizz" 是嘻哈和互联网文化中常用的俚语。它通常用来形容某个人具有敏锐、幽默或机智的幽默感，尤其是他们使用互联网短语或俚语来进行机智的评论或反击时。简而言之，它表示某人反应快且善于用语言来制造幽默和高效的回应。
