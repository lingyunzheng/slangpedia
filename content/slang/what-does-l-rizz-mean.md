### L rizz
**English:**  
"L rizz" is an internet slang term that suggests someone has a strong visual sense or is good at presenting themselves in a stylish or attractive way. The phrase often implies that the person has a certain "littlerizz" (市场规模较小)，类似于“小巧精致”之意) appearance or style that is appealing to others. 
**中文:**  
“L rizz” 是一种网络俚语，用来表示某人具有强烈的视觉感或善于以时尚或吸引人的方式展示自己。这个词通常暗示这个人在外表或风格上有某种“精美好看”的特质，能够吸引他人。
