# "L rizz"
## English
An Instagram and TikTok meme that combines the letter "L" with the concept of "rizz" (a colloquial term for attitude or coolness). The phrase "L rizz" is often used to sarcastically acknowledge someone's perceived lack of style or coolness. 
Example: "Man, did you see that new outfit? L rizz."
## 中文
一个源自Instagram和TikTok的表情包和梗，结合了字母"L"与“rizz”（一种非正式用语，指态度或酷感）。"L rizz"这个短语通常被用来讽刺性地指出某人的风格或酷感被认为不足。
示例：“哇，你的新装扮看了好吗？L rizz。”
