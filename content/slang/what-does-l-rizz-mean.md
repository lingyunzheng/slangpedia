### "L rizz" - 拉里·里兹
**English Explanation:** "L rizz" is an internet slang used to describe someone who is sassy, snarky, or sarcastic. It often implies a sense of self-aware humor or mockery, particularly directed at perceived foolishness or pretentiousness. The term can also be used to describe a style of communication that is clever, witty, and cleverly undermining, often at the expense of someone or something else.
**中文解释:** "L rizz" 是一种网络俚语，用来形容一个人具有俏皮、酸唧唧或讽刺的特点。这通常暗示了一种自知式的幽默感或自嘲，特别是针对那些被认为是愚蠢或装腔作势的人或事。这个词还可以用来描述一种风格化的表达方式，即充满智慧、俏皮且巧妙地拆解他人的表达，常常是以别人的开涮为代价的。
