## L rizz
### English
"L rizz" is a slang term that originated in some subcultures, particularly among young people. It generally describes someone or something that lacks the ability to read between the lines or understand subtle social cues. Essentially, it means that someone or something is straightforward or naive, often in a way that comes off as uncool or inappropriate in certain social situations. It can also imply someone is overly dramatic or overly emotional in a way that’s perceived as fake.
### 中文
"L rizz" 是一种起源于某些亚文化群体，尤其是年轻人使用的俚语。这个词汇通常用来形容某人或某事物缺乏理解和解读微妙社会暗示的能力。简单来说，它表示某人或某事物过于直白或天真，往往在某些社交场合给人一种不酷或不适当的印象。它也可以表示有人在情感表达上过于戏剧化或过于情绪化，给人一种作秀的感觉。
