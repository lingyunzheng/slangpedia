## L rizz
**English:**  
L rizz is a term used to describe someone who is relaxed, cool, and laid-back. It can also refer to a person's natural charisma or a certain cool vibe they possess. The term often implies a confident and self-assured demeanor.
**中文:**  
"L rizz" 用来形容一个人有点散漫但很酷，很放松自如的样子。这个词也可以指某人与生俱来的魅力或者他们身上散发的那种酷劲。通常用来形容自信而从容不迫的人。
