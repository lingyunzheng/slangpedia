# L Rizz
## English
**L Rizz**: This is a playful and informal term often used in rap music and street slang to denote a particularly clever, smart, or impressive lyric or performance. It's slang for "L" (which stands for "legend" or "lit") followed by "rizz," which is a reference to style, attitude, or charisma. It's used to describe someone or something that exudes coolness, wit, and high quality.
## 中文
**L Rizz**: 这个词语常用来描述特别聪明、有才或令人印象深刻的一段说唱歌词或表演。它通常是指“L”（表示“传奇”或“酷炫”）后面跟着“rizz”，这里的“rizz”指的是风格、态度或魅力。用来形容某人或某物散发出的酷感、机智和高品质。
