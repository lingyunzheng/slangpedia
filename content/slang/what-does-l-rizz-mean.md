# L rizz
## English
**"L rizz"**: This is a blended term used in internet slang or_OPERATOR_ language. "L" is often used to represent "lit," meaning something is awesome or cool, and "rizz" is slang for attitude or flair. So, "L rizz" generally means an attitude or style that is awesome or cool. It's often used to describe someone's confident or stylish behavior.
## 中文
**"L rizz"**: 这是一个在网络用语或杂糅语言中使用的混合词。“L”通常用来代表“lit”，意指某样东西很酷炫或者很棒，“rizz”则是态度或风采的 slang 词。所以，“L rizz”一般意味着一种很酷炫或很棒的态度或风格。它经常用来描述某人的自信或时尚的行为。
