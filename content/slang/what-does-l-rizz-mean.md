# L rizz
## English
"L rizz" is a playful way of saying someone has a unique, groovy, or especially stylish sense of humor. It originated from the term "rizz," meaning style, cool factor, or charisma. When someone has "L rizz," they add a dash of levity and wit to any situation, often in a humorous or lighthearted manner.
## Chinese
"L rizz" 是一种充满趣味的说法，用来形容某人有一种独特、潮流或特别有魅力的幽默感。这个词汇来源于“rizz”，意为风格、酷感或魅力。当某人有“L rizz”时，他们会以轻松幽默的方式为任何场面增添一份趣味和魅力。
