## Rizz God 🏴‍☠️
### English:
**Definition**: A slang term used to describe someone who exudes a cool, laid-back, and effortlessly stylish aura. This person is often admired for their unique fashion sense, attitude, and overall vibe that commands attention.
### 汉语:
**定义**: 这是一个用来形容那些散发着酷、随意且风格独特的迷人氛围的人的网络用语。这个词语常用来赞赏那些以独特的穿衣风格、态度和整体气场吸引目光的人。
