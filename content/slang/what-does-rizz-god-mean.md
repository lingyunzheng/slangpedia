## Rizz God (英语: Rizz God)
**中文解释**: 一个在派对或社交场合中表现得极其潇洒、自信和吸引人的家伙。这个词通常用来形容一个人在各种情境下都能展现出迷人的魅力和魄力，能轻松吸引周围人的注意，让人们羡慕且尊敬。
**英语解释**: A person who is exceptionally cool, confident, and attractive in social or party settings. The term is often used to describe someone who exudes charm and charisma effortlessly, able to command attention and leave a lasting impression on others, inspiring both admiration and envy.
