## Rizz God (英文: Rizz God)
**中文释义:** ˈrizz ɡəd
Rizz God 是一种对身材健硕、气场强大、充满魅力的男性的一种夸赞，跟“肌肉男”、“型男”类似，但更侧重于这种人给人带来的那种独特的吸引力和自信感。
**英文解释:** A term used to describe a muscular, charismatic, and highly attractive male individual. It often refers to someone who projects a strong and powerful presence, making them stand out with an unparalleled level of confidence and charm.
