## Rizz God (英语: Rizz God)
### Chinese Explanation
**Rizz God** 是网络用语，常用于形容一个非常自信， street style（街头风格）穿搭很棒，行为举止也非常吸引人的人。这个词界的使用情况广泛，尤其在年轻人之间的社交网络上更为流行。这个词可以理解为**“型神”**或**“穿搭天王”**。
### English Explanation
**Rizz God** is a slang term often used to describe someone who is extremely confident, has great street style fashion sense, and generally exudes an attractive presence. It’s widely used in social media and among young people. Essentially, it can be translated as **"Style God"** or **"Fashion King"**.
