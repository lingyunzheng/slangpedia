## Rizz God: 撸神
**English Explanation:**
"Rizz God" is a slang term often used in online gaming communities, particularly among players of first-person shooters (FPS) and other action games. It is used to describe a highly skilled player who showcases exceptional abilities in the game, like masterful aiming, quick reflexes, and strategic thinking. The term can be used as a compliment to praise someone's gaming prowess or to refer to a professional gamer or streamer known for their exceptional performance.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"撸神"（Lü Shén）一词常见于在线游戏社区，特别在FPS（第一人称射击）和其他动作游戏中。它用来形容一位展示出卓越能力的玩家，例如出色的目标瞄准、快速的反应速度和战略思考能力。这个词汇既可以用作赞美某人的游戏技巧，也可以用来指那些以卓越表现著称的职业玩家或主播。
