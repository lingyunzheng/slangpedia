# Rizz God (中文：里兹教主)
**英语解释**: "Rizz God" is a slang term used in online gaming communities, particularly in MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) games like Dota 2 and League of Legends. It refers to a player who consistently demonstrates outstanding skill, making clever and effective plays, often in the role of a melee or carry hero/joueur. These players are often respected and looked up to by their teammates for their game knowledge and playstyle. The term is a play on words combining "rizz," which can mean style or attitude, and "god," indicating near-legendary status.
**中文解释**: "里兹教主" 是一个流行于网络游戏社区（特别是 MOBA 游戏如 Dota 2 和 League of Legends）中的俚语。用于形容那些始终保持卓越技术水平，做出聪明且有效的游戏决策的玩家，特别是在肉搏或核心英雄/角色中表现出色的玩家。这些玩家往往会受到队友们的尊重和仰慕，因为他们具备丰富的游戏知识和风格。这个词结合了 “rizz”（表示风格或态度）和 “神”（表示传奇般的地位）的意思。
