# Rizz God ( slang )
## English Explanation:
- **Definition**: A person who is widely recognized for their exceptional style, attitude, and flair, particularly in fashion or overall presence. Essentially, a "cool vibe" magnet.
- **Example Usage**: "Wow, that new dress he bought is sheer rizz. He's such a rizz god!"
## Chinese Explanation:
- **定义**: 一个以极佳的风格、态度和风采而闻名的人，尤其是在时尚或整体气场方面。简而言之，就是自带“酷感”的人。
- **例句**: "哇，他新买的这条裙子简直爆炸 rizz 啦，真是 rizz 福星！"
