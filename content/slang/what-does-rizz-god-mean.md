## Rizz God (英译：Rizz God)
### English
**Rizz God**: This term is a playful and colloquial way to describe someone who exudes a confident and cool demeanor. It's often used to praise someone who looks and acts in a way that makes them stand out as exceptionally stylish or charismatic. This can apply to fashion sense, attitude, or overall presence.
### Chinese
