## Rizz God
### English
"Rizz God" is a slang term often used in the context of urban and hip-hop culture to describe someone who is extremely confident, stylish, and stylishly competitive in terms of fashion, attitude, and overall presence. This person is seen as a role model in terms of "rizz," which is a combination of style, swagger, and attitude.
### Chinese
"Rizz God" 是一个俚语，在城市和嘻哈文化中常用来形容一个极度自信、时尚且在时尚、姿态和整体存在感上竞争性很强的人。这个人在“rizz”（一种结合了风格、傲慢和态度的特质）方面被视为一个榜样。
