# Rizz God (俚语解释)
## 中文解释
“Rizz God”是一个网络流行语，来源于英文“Rizzle”，原意是英国歌手Dappy的歌曲《Rizzle》中的一个词汇，后来被中国特色地赋予新含义，常用来自称或称别人为“有型有款”的人。这个词用来形容一个人特别有型或者特别有魅力，甚至可以理解为一个酷毙了的人物。
## 英文 Explanation
“Rizz God” is a slang term that originated from the English word “Rizzle,” which originally comes from the song "Rizzle" by UK singer Dappy. This term has been localized and commonly used to describe someone who is exceptionally stylish or attractive. It essentially means someone who is incredibly cool or simply put, a "chill" character.
