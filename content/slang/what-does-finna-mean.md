## Finna (slang)
### English Explanation:
"Finna" is a colloquial term that serves as a shortened form of "fixin' to," which is an archaic Southern American English expression meaning "about to" or "getting ready to." It's commonly used in informal settings and street language.
**Example:**
- English: "I'm finna go to the store."
- Meaning: "I'm getting ready to go to the store."
### 中文解释:
"Finna" 是一个俚语，它是 "fixin' to" 的缩写版本，在南方美国英语中，“fixin' to” 通常用来表示“正要”或“准备”。这个词汇常用于非正式场合和街头语言。
**例句:**
- 中文: "我 finna 去超市了。"
- 意思: "我正要去超市。"
