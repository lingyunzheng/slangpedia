## Finna
### English Explanation
"Finna" is an informal, colloquial term that originated in the southern United States. It is a contraction of "fixin' to," which is itself a contraction of "fixing to." People use "finna" to indicate that they are about to do something. For example, "I'm finna go to the store."
### Chinese Explanation
“Finna” 是美国南部地区常用的非正式俚语，来源于“fixin' to”（原意为“准备要”），后者又是“fixing to”的缩写。“Finna” 用来表示即将做某事。例如，“我 finna 走去超市”（I'm finna go to the store）。
