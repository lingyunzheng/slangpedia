## Finna
**English:** 
"Finna" is a colloquial slang term often used to indicate that someone is about to do something or is on their way to do something soon. It's a fun and casual way to convey current plans or immediate intentions.
**例子用法:**
- I'm **finna** head to the gym after work.
- 我下班后 **准** 去健身房。
**Pinyin:** 
- **finna** 可以读作 fēnnà
## 解释:
“Finna” 是一种口语俚语，用来表示某人即将去做某事，或者很快就要去做。这是一种轻松愉快的方式来表达当前的计划或即将采取的行动。
