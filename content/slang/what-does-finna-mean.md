## finna
### English
**finna** - Slang for "fixin' to" or "getting ready to." It's commonly used in Southern American English.
**Example:**
- "I finna head to the store after work." means I am getting ready to go to the store after work.
### Chinese
**finna** - “finna” 是“fixin' to”或“getting ready to”的口语表达，常用于美国南部地区的英语中。
**例句:**
- "下班后我 finna 去商店。" 意思是下班后我正要/打算去商店。
