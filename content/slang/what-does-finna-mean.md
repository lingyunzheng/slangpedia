# Slang Term: "Finna"
## English
**Definition:** 
"Finna" is an informal contraction of "fixin' to" or "gonna," often used to indicate future intent or plans. It's commonly used in Southern American English and is particularly popular in casual speech.
**Example Usage:**
"I finna go to the store after work." 
This would mean "I am fixing to go to the store after work."
## Chinese (Simplified)
**释义：**
"Finna" 是英语中南方英语地区常用的非正式缩写词，主要是用来表示未来的打算或计划。它通常是 "fixin' to" 或 "gonna" 的一种缩写形式，常见于非正式对话中。
**例句：**
“下班后，我 finna 去买些牛奶。”
这意为 “下班后，我打算去买些牛奶。”
