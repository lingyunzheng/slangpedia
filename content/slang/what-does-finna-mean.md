## Finna: 等会儿要去
**English Explanation:**
"Finna" is a colloquial term used to indicate that someone is in the process of going to do something or is about to do something. It's commonly used in informal or casual conversations. For example, if you're waiting for a friend and they say, "I finna get the tickets," it means they are on their way to get the tickets.
**中文解释:**
"Finna" 是一个在口语中表示某人正在准备去做某事或即将去做某事的俚语。通常用于非正式或闲聊的场合。例如，当你在等待朋友时，如果他们说，“I finna get the tickets”（我 finna 去买票），实际上就是在说他们会很快就去买票。
