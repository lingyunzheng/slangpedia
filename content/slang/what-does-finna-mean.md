# What is "finna"? (Part 1: English Explanation)
"Finna" is a colloquial term often used as a contraction of "fixin' to," which comes from "fixing to," a phrase used in Southern American English. It's commonly employed to indicate an intention or plan to do something in the near future. 
**Example:**
- "I'm finna head to the store after work." 
- Meaning: "I am planning to go to the store after work."
# 什么的是“finna”?（部分2：中文解释）
"Finna" 是一种俚语，常被用作 "fixin' to" 的缩写，“fixin' to” 是美式英语中南部地区常用的一种表达方式。它通常用来表示近期内打算做某事的意图或计划。
**例句：**
- 我下班后想 finna 去超市。
- 意思：下班后打算去超市。
