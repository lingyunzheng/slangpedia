## finna
### English
**Definition**: "Finna" is a slang term used to mean "about to" or "getting ready to." It is a combination of "fixin' to" (a colloquial expression from the American South) and "gonna" (short for "going to").
**Example**: 
- "I'm finna head to the park after work." (I'm about to go to the park after work.)
### Chinese
**解释**: "finna" 是一个俚语，用来表示“正要”或“即将”。它是由南部美国口音中的“fixin' to”（意为“正打算”）和“gonna”（意为“going to”）结合而成的。
**例句**:
- “下班后我finna去公园。”（下班后我正要去公园。）
