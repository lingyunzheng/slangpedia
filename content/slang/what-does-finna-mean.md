## Finna
### English Explanation
"Finna" is a slang term primarily used in Southern and African American English. It is a contraction of "fixin' to," which means "preparing to" or "about to." For example, "I finna go to the store" means "I am about to go to the store."
### Chinese Explanation
"Finna" 是一种俚语，在美国南部和非裔美国英语中较为常见。它是“fixin' to”的缩写，意思是“即将要”或“正要”。例如，“I finna go to the store” 可以理解为“我要去商店”。
