### "Finna"
#### English Explanation:
"Finna" is a slang term used primarily in the Southern United States. It is a contraction of "fixin' to" and is used to indicate that someone is about to do something. 
**Example:**
- *They're finna leave in a few minutes.* (They are about to leave in a few minutes.)
#### Chinese Explanation:
"Finna" 是美国南部地区常用的俚语，它是一个"fixin' to" 的缩写形式，用来表示某人即将要做某事。
**例句:**
- *他们 finna 几分钟后离开.*（他们过几分钟就要离开了。）
