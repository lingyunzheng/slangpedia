# "Finna" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Meaning**: 
- "Finna" is a colloquial term used in American English, particularly in Southern and urban areas.
- It's a contraction of "fixin' to" or "gonna" (going to), which indicates that someone is about to do something.
**Usage Example**:
- "I finna head to the store after work." — "I am going to go to the store after work."
## 中文
**含义**：
- “Finna” 是美式英语中的俚语，特别是在南部和城市地区广泛使用。
- 它是“fixin' to” 或“gonna”（going to）的缩写，用来表示某人即将要去做某事。
**使用示例**：
- "I finna head to the store after work." — "下班后我去商店。"
