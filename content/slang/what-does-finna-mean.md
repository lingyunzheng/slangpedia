## Finna
### English
**Meaning**: 
- **In Progress**: Used to indicate that something is about to happen or is in the process of happening.
- **Upcoming**: Signifies that an action is imminent.
**Example**:
- "I finna go to the store. Do you need anything?"
- "We finna meet up after work."
### Chinese
**意思**：
- **即将进行**：用于表示某件事情即将发生或正在发生。
- **即将到来**：表明一个动作即将来临。
**示例**：
- “我 finna 去购物。你需要什么吗？”
- “下班后我们 finna 相见。”
