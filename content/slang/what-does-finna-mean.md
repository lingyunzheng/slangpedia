## Finna
**English**: 
- **Meaning**: 
- "Finna" is a colloquial term used in American English, typically by African American communities. It is a contraction of "fixin' to," which means "getting ready to" or "about to."
- **Example Sentences**:
- "I'm finna go get some lunch." (I'm about to go get some lunch.)
- "Finna head on over now." (About to go over now.)
**中文解释**：
- **含义**：
- “Finna”是美国英语中的一个俚语，通常由非洲裔美国人使用。它是“fixin’ to”的缩写，意思是“正要”或“即将”。
- **例句**：
- “I'm finna go get some lunch.”（我正要去吃午饭。）
- “Finna head on over now.”（我正要出发过去了。）
