## "Finna" Slang Explanation
### English
**Meaning**: In the process of doing something; about to do something.
**Example**: "I'm finna go to the store, want anything?"
### Chinese
**意思**: 即将做某事；正要去做某事。
**示例**: "我 finna 去超市了，你需要我捎什么？"
