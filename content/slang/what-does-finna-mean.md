# "Finna" Slang Explanation
## English
**Definition:** "Finna" is an informal slang term, short for "fixin' to," which means someone is about to do something or is in the process of doing something.
**Example Sentence:** "I'm finna head to the store after work."  
This means the person intends to go to the store shortly.
## 中文
**定义:** "Finna" 是一种非正式的俚语，来自于 "fixin' to"，意指某人正要去做某事或正在做某事。
**例句:** "I'm finna head to the store after work." 翻译为中文是 "下班后我打算去超市。" 表示说话的人打算下班后去超市。
