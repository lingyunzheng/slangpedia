# "Finna" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**Definition:** "Finna" is a colloquial term used to denote "about to" or "getting ready to" do something.
**Example Usage:**
- "I'm finna go to the store."
- "She's finna leave for work soon."
## Chinese
**定义:** "Finna" 是一个俚语，用于表示“正要”或“准备去做某事”。
**例句使用:**
- "我要去商店 finna。"
- "她马上就 finna 去上班。"
