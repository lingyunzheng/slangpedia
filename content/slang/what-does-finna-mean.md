## finna
**English:**  
- *Slang*: Short for "fixin' to," which means to be about to or going to do something.  
- Example: "I finna head to the store after work."  
- Colloquial usage: Common in informal settings, particularly in Southern American English.
**中文解释:**  
- *俚语*: "fixin' to" 的缩写，意思是正要做某事或者计划要做某事。  
- 例句: "下班后我 finna 去商店。"  
- 口语使用: 常用于非正式场合，特别是在美国南部的英语中。
