### finna
#### English:
**Definition**: An informal abbreviation of "fixin' to," which means "about to" or "going to do something."
**Example**: "I finna head to the gym after work." (I am about to go to the gym after work.)
#### Chinese:
**定义**: 一个非正式的缩写，来自 "fixin' to"，意为“即将做某事”或“打算做某事”。
**例句**: "下班后我 finna 去健身房。" (下班后我打算去健身房。)
