## Finna
### English Explanatory Slang
"Finna" is a colloquial way of saying "about to" or "going to." It's commonly used in informal conversations to indicate that something is about to happen. For example, if someone is about to leave the house, they might say, "Finna go now."
### Chinese Explanatory Slang
"Finna" 将近 (jiàng jìn) 是一种非正式的说法，表示“即将要”或“正要去”。它通常用于非正式对话中，表示某事即将发生。例如，如果有人即将离开家，他们可能会说，“Finna”去，“将近去”的意思。
- **英语例子**： "I'm finna go to the store."
- **中文例子**： “我要去商店，将近去的意思。”
### Example Usage
- English: "I'm finna meet you at the park later."
- Chinese:  “我将近在公园里找你。”
