## "Finna" in English and Chinese
### English
**Definition:** 
"Finna" is a colloquial term commonly used in American English to mean "about to" or "going to" do something. It is typically used to refer to an action that is imminent or impending.
**Example:**
- "Finna head to the store, you want anything?" means "About to go to the store, do you want anything?"
### 中文
**定义：**
“finna”是一个美式英语中的俚语，意思是可以替代“going to”或“about to”表示即将要做某事。通常用来描述即将要发生的动作。
**例句：**
- "Finna head to the store, you want anything?" 可以理解为“要出门去超市了，需要帮忙买点什么吗？”
