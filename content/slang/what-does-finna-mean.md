## Finna
### English
"Finna" is a colloquial term commonly used in African American Vernacular English (AAVE) to mean "about to" or "going to." It's often used to express immediate plans or actions that are about to happen.
**Example:**
"I finna head to the store."
### 中文
"Finna" 是美国非裔美国人英语（AAVE）中的一种俚语，意思是“正要”或“即将去”。它常用于表达即将发生的计划或行动。
**例句:**
“我 finna 去那时候。”
