## finna
### English
**Meaning**: A colloquial way to say "going to" or "about to" do something.
**Example**:
- "I'm finna head to the gym after work." 
### Chinese
**含义**: 民间常用的表达“要去做某事”或“即将去做某事”的方式。
**例句**:
- "下班后我准备去健身房，我要finna去。"
