## "Finna" - A Concise Guide
### English Slang Explanation:
"Finna" is a casual, informal way to say "gonna" (going to), used to express future intentions or actions. It's often used in spoken English, particularly in Southern American English. 
> Example: "I finna go to the store after work."
### Chinese Slang Explanation:
"Finna" 要去 表示一个即将发生的动作或意图，类似于“gonna”。它通常用于口语，尤其是在美式英语中流传广泛。
> 例句：下班后我 finna 去买点东西。
