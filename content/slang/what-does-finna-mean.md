## Finna
### English
**Definition**: A colloquial term used to indicate an intention to do something in the near future, often implying immediate or soon-to-follow action.
**Example**: "I finna go to the store after this meeting." 
### Chinese
**定义**: 一个 colloquial 词汇，用来表示即将进行某项活动，常暗示即将发生或不久之后的行为。
**例句**: “等这场会议结束后，我 finna 去商店。”
