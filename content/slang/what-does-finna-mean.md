## finna
**English:** 
"Finna" is an informal slang primarily used in Southern United States, especially among younger generations. When someone says "I'm finna do something," it means they are about to do or are getting ready to do it, often in the near future. It's a combination of "I'm gonna" (I'm going to) and "finishing up" or "fixin' to," a shortened form of "fixin' to do," which originates from Southern American English.
**中文:**
"Finna" 是一种非正式的俚语，主要在美国南部地区流行，特别受到年轻一代的欢迎。当有人说“Finna do something”时，意思是他们即将去做或正在准备去做某事，通常是在近期内。这是一种“finna”（即将）与“I'm gonna”（我要）或“finishing up”（完成）、“fixin' to do”（准备做）的简略形式的组合，后者源自南部美国英语。
