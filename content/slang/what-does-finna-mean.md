## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "finna"
**English:** 
- **Meaning:** "finna" is a colloquial term used in informal speech, deriving from "finna be" or "finna going to." It's often used to express an intention or plan shortly to happen.
- **Example:** "I finna hit the gym after work."
- **Usage Context:** Commonly used in southern and urban American English.
**Chinese:**
- **含义:** "finna" 是一种非正式用语，来自“finna be”或“finna going to”。它常用来表示即将发生的意图或计划。
- **例句:** “下班后我要去 gym 出出汗。”
- **使用场景:** 常见于美国南方和城市英语中。
