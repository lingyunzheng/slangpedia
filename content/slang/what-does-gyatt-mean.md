# Gyatt (UK Slang)
## 英语
### Definition
"gyatt" is a humorous and informal British slang term that often suggests something is very bad, very poor, or not up to standard. It's similar to saying something is "crap" or "terrible" in more casual contexts.
### Usage Example
"I tried the new restaurant downtown, but the food was completely gyatt."
## 中文
### 释义
“gyatt”是一个幽默且非正式的英国俚语，通常表示某事物非常糟糕、非常差或者达不到标准。它相当于在更随意的语境中说某事是"垃圾"（crap）或"糟糕透了"（terrible）。
### 使用例句
“我去了一家城里的新餐厅，但食物完全就是‘gyatt’的。”
