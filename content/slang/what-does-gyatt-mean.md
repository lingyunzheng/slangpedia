# Gyatt (粤语 slang)
## English Explanation
"**Gyatt**" is a playful and casual term used in Cantonese (粤语) to express mild confusion, surprise, or amazement. It's often used when something unexpected or bizarre happens. It's similar to saying "what?" or "huh?" but with a more laid-back and fun tone.
### Example:
- **English:** "Hey, you got a new haircut! Gyatt, it's so cool!"
- **Cantonese:** 喂，你俾到新頭髮！ Gyatt，真系好型！
## Pronunciation
The pronunciation is similar to the English sound: "gyat".
## Usage
It's commonly used among friends and in informal settings in regions where Cantonese is spoken. It can be used to express a range of reactions, from mild surprise to genuine amazement.
