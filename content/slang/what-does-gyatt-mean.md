## Gyatt
### Chinese (简体中文)
**释义**: "贼6" 或 "贼炫"，意为非常 cool 或炫酷。
**用法**: 常用于形容某人的行为、衣服或者所做的事非常棒、很时尚。
**例子**: 
- 他的新手机真的 g抗体t，大家都很羡慕。
- 他这波操作真是 g抗体t，让我想起了当年的自己。

### English
**Definition**: "really cool" or "really rad", meaning something that is extremely awesome or stylish.
**Usage**: It's commonly used to describe someone's actions, clothes, or something they've done, which is very impressive and fashionable.
**Example**:
- His new phone is really gyatt, everyone is jealous.
- His manuever was really gyatt, reminding me of myself back then.
