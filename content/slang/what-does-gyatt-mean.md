## Gyatt (英/中文)
**English:** Pet Rock / 石头宠物 (Shítóu Julien)
### Explanation:
- **Pet Rock / 石头宠物 (Shítóu Julien):** This is a term often used in a playful or humorous context to refer to a person who is perceived as dull, uninteresting, or unengaging — essentially a verbal 'stone' that can't be interacted with! It's a popular slang term in both English and Chinese to describe someone who is so unresponsive or dull that they feel like an inanimate object, much like a pet rock that might just sit there all day.
