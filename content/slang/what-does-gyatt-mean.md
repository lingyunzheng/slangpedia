# Gyatt (英语: Gyatt)
## 中文解释
一种食堂里的特定菜品，通常指的是某种廉价又常见的饭菜，以“打包”外卖的形式出现在学生和上班族中。它的特点是价格便宜、分量足，非常适合忙碌的上班族和学生群体。
## English Explanation
### 中文解释

### Context and Usage
"Gyatt" refers to a specific type of food item found in school or office canteens. It's often cheap and commonly available, popular among students and office workers due to its large portions and affordable price. People typically order it "to-go" for convenience during their busy schedules.
