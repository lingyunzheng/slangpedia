## Gyatt
- **English Slang Explanation:**
A term often used to describe a situation or person that is considered to be overly complex, confusing, or unnecessarily difficult. It can also be used to express frustration or bewilderment.
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:**
通常用来形容一种复杂、混乱或者无必要的麻烦的情况或人。它也可以用来表达一种沮丧或困惑的情绪。
