# Gyatt (粤语/普通话)
## 英语解释
"Gyatt" is a Cantonese slang term. It is often used to denote something that is weird, strange, or out of the ordinary.
## 普通话解释
" Gyatt "是一个粤语俚语，通常用来形容一些 weird（奇怪的）、strange（奇异的）或 out of the ordinary（不寻常的）的事物。
## 示例
- 英语示例：That new video game is gyatt, no one seems to like it.
- 普通话示例：这个新电子游戏真是怪异，好像没有人喜欢它。
