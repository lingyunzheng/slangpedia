## Gyatt (English)
### Definition
A playful and casual term often used to express mild frustration or annoyance about a situation or person. It can also be used to express a feeling of slight disgust or distaste.
### Example Usage
"Man, I can't believe I have to work with Jane again this week. She's such a gyatt sometimes!"
