## Gyatt (假特)
### Chinese
**含义**: "假特" 在网络用语中是指打标签、装模作样的行为，通常用于批评那些表面上看起来很优越或者很特别，但实质上却没有什么真才实学的人。"假" 指假装，"特" 的意思是比较特别或者特殊。这个词的使用往往是带有一定的讽刺意味。
**示例**: "你天天说自己是文化人，但实际上什么都不懂，真是个假特！"
### English
**Meaning**: "Gyatt" is a slang term used in Chinese internet language to describe putting on a facade or pretense, often to criticize individuals who claim to be exceptional or superior in some way without actually having any real talent or ability. "Gyatt" implies pretending to be special or cultured. The usage of this term often comes with a hint of irony.
**Example**: "You claim to be a cultured person every day, but you actually know nothing. You are really just a gyatt!"
