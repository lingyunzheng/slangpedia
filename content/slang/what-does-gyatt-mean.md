## Gyatt (Early English SLANG) 
### English
- **Meaning:** Usually refers to a person who is overly fanatical or enthusiastic about something, often in a silly or nonsensical way.
- **Example Usage:** "That guy is a gyatt about trampolines; he jumps on one every chance he gets, like it's the coolest thing ever!"
### Chinese
- **意思:** 通常指对某事过于狂热或痴迷，常常以一种滑稽或无意义的方式。
- **使用示例:** “那个家伙对蹦床上瘾了；只要有机会，他就跳上去，好像那才是最酷的事情一样！”
