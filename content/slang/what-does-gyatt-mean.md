# Gyatt (Singaporean/Brunei English)
## In English
A term used to describe something that is extremely cool or awesome. It can also be used to express surprise or admiration.
**Example:**
I saw those street performers just now and they were **gyatt**! 
## In Mandarin
一个用来描述某物非常酷或 Awesome 的词，也可以用来表达惊讶或钦佩。
**例句:**
我刚才看到那些街头表演者了，他们真的 **很 gyatt**！
---
Gyatt 这个词源自新加坡和文莱英语，是一种表达惊喜或赞叹的俚语，类似于“so cool”或“awesome”。
