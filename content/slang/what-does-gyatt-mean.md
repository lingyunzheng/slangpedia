# Gyatt (粤语 slang)
## 汉语解释
“gyatt”是粤语中的一种常见俚语，它的含义会根据上下文有所不同。最常见的意思是“喜欢到不行”或“超爱”。
## 英语解释
"Gyatt" is a common Cantonese slang term, and its meaning can vary depending on the context. Most commonly, it means "super duper like" or "absolutely love."
