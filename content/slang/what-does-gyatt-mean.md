## Gyatt (就喊)
**中文解释：**
“就喊”是一个网络用语，来源于闽南语，意为“直接喊出来”或“直接讲”。在网络交流中，常用于表达直接、坦率的意思，有时带有一定程度的调侃或玩笑性质。
**英文解释：**
“Gyatt” is a net slang term derived from the Hokkien language, meaning “to directly say it out” or “to directly tell.” In online communication, it is often used to imply a direct and straightforward approach, sometimes with a hint of jest or teasing.
