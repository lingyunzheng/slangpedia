```markdown
# Gyatt (中文/英文)
## 中文
** meaning: 一个在网络和社交媒体上非常流行的表示惊讶或赞叹的弹幕，类似于"卧艹"，常用于直播、弹幕视频网站等场景。  
**发音:** 该词读作“gyatt”，类似于英文中“g渴望的”加上“ya感叹词”的发音  
**例句:**  
- 他一显现，屏幕上就满是 "卧艹, 这个人是谁?" 的弹幕，其中就包括一个大名鼎鼎的“gyatt”。
## English
** meaning: A widely used term on the internet and social media platforms, similar to "wow" or "oh my god," often expressed as a comment or 'bullet-screen' in live streams or video sites.  
**pronunciation:** The word is pronounced as "gyatt," which sounds somewhat like the English "g" + "ya" string put together.  
**example:**  
- When he appeared, the screen was filled with comments like "oh my god, who is this person?" including a famous "gyatt."
```
Note: Please remember that "卧艹" is a very crude, vulgar term, and "gyatt" is an attempt to mimic its usage and effect in a more socially acceptable manner.
