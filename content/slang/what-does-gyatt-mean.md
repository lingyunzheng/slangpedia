# Gyatt
## English
**Gyatt**: A playful, mildly derogatory term, often used to refer to someone as a "nerd" or "geek" with a hint of admiration mixed in. It's somewhat similar to the term "geek" but with a more casual and friendly twist. Think of it as a cousin to the term "dork" but with a lighter touch.
## Chinese
**Gyatt**: 一个带有轻微戏谑和欣赏意味的词汇，常用来形容某人是“极客”或“书呆子”，带有别样的友好感。可以理解为类似于"极客"这个称呼，但语气更加轻松和随意。
