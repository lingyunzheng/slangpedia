## Gyatt (粤语口语)
### 中文解释
“gyatt” 在粤语中是一种口语化表达，通常用来形容某件事情特别easy或者简单，是一种口语化的感叹词。
### 英文解释
"gyatt" is a colloquial term in Cantonese used to express that something is very easy or simple. It can be seen as a colloquial exclamation similar to saying "easy peasy" or "a piece of cake" in English.
