# Gyatt (English slang)
**Definition:** A playful, often humorous way to suggest a situation or action that involves sudden, unexpected action or movement. It can be used similarly to a shortened version of "gyo-at," which means "get a move on" or "hurry up" in some contexts.
**Example:** "The party's about to start, but everyone's still dragging their feet. Gyatt, guys! We don't want to miss the music!"
# 加特 (Chinese slang)
**定义:** 用来表示突然或意想不到的动作或行为的一种轻松、幽默的方式。它可以用作催促或加快速度的一种简短表达，类似于“快点行动”或“赶紧”。
**例句:** “派对马上就要开始了，但大家还一直磨磨蹭蹭的。快点，各位！我们可不想错过音乐。”
