## "gyatt" (港台 slang)
**Definition:**
"gyatt" is a playful and informal term used in Cantonese and Taiwanese slang. It generally means "cute" or "adorable," but can also be used to imply a strong affection or to mock someone in a friendly manner.
**Example Usage:**
- **Cantonese:**
- 嗰個女好 gyatt！
- "That girl is so cute!"
- **Taiwanese:**
- 這隻貓真 gyatt！
- "This cat is really cute!"
**Explanation:**
In informal contexts, this term is often used among friends to complement someone or something, adding a touch of friendliness or affection. It can also be used humorously to tease someone, in a lighthearted way.
