## Gyatt (粤语)
### 中文解释
“ Gyatt” 是粤语里的一个网络流行语，常用来形容一个人富有个性、非常酷或非常独特的样子。有时也用来形容某些事物具有新奇或特别的特质。
### 英文解释
"Gyatt" is a popular linguistic term in Cantonese, used to describe someone who is very cool, unique, or distinctive. It can also be applied to certain objects or situations with new and unusual characteristics.
