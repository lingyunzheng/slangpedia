## Gyatt (Gamers)
**English:** :
A term used to describe a situation where a player gives up or makes a poor decision that significantly impacts the game's outcome in a negative way, often by accident or due to poor skill. It's a blend of "gay" and "rookie," implying that the player is inexperienced or subpar, much like a rookie who might also be considered gay in certain contexts within gaming culture.
**中文：**
“gyatt”是一个用来形容在游戏中因疏忽或技巧不足而做出严重负面影响行为的术语。这个词结合了“gay”和“rookie”的概念，隐含的意思是玩家缺乏经验，就像新手可能会因为缺乏经验而显得有点“gay”一样，在游戏中常常表现为毫无意义的失败或是错误决策。
