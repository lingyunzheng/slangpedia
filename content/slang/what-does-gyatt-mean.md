# Gyatt (粤语 slang)
## English Explanation
"Gyatt" is a playful and informal term often used in Cantonese-speaking regions. It can be used to describe someone as being cool, laid-back, or hip. It can also mean something is of high quality or well-executed.
## Cantonese Explanation
" Gyatt" (粤语: 谐士) 是一种广东话中的俚语，用来形容某人很酷、随和或时尚。它也可以表示某事物的质量很高或执行得非常好。
