# Gyatt
## English
"Cheater" or "Scammer" - Someone who tries to gain an unfair advantage or deceive others in a game or online setting.
## Chinese
"骗子"或"作弊者" - 在游戏或在线环境中试图不公平地获得优势或欺骗他人的人。
