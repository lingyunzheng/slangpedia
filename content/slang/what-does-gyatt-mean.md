# Gyatt (粤语 slang)
## 中文解释
“gyatt” 在粤语中常用来形容某人或某件事非常出色、厉害，具有非常高的评价。类似于“大神”、“高手”等词。
## 英文解释
"Gyatt" is a Cantonese slang term used to describe someone or something that is exceptionally good or impressive. It's akin to saying someone is a "master" or a "genius" in a particular field.
