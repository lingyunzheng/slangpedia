## 英文 slang: Gyatt
### 中文 slang: 葛 PM (Gé Píngrén)
**Explanation:**
Gyatt is a humorous term commonly used in online forums or casual chats, derived from the English word "fry" which can also refer to "grill" or "cooking" by applying heat. In the context of social interactions or gaming, it refers to someone being made to do something they would rather avoid or being teased and pushed into situations. The term is often used in a lighthearted or playful manner, similar to how "fry" can imply indirectly forcing someone into a situation, as if they are being cooked.
Example usage in a sentence:
- English: "Man, did you see how Sarah gyatted Tom into doing the dishes? He didn't even want to!"
- 中文: "你知道莎拉是怎么Gyatt托姆做 dishes 的吗？他根本不想呢！"
