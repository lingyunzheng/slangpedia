## Gyatt
### English Explanation
"GYATT" is a internet slang that stands for "Get Ya Ass To Time." It's a playful and casual way of telling someone to hurry up or to do something at a specific time without delay. It's often used in text messages or online conversations when people want to stress the urgency of a situation.
### 中文解释
"GYATT"是互联网上的 slang 词，意思是“Get Ya Ass To Time”。这个词是一种轻松随意的方式，用来告诉别人快点或在特定的时间内行动，不要拖延。它经常被用在短信或在线对话中，当人们想要强调某件事情的急迫性时。
**Usage Examples:**
- "The movie starts in 10 minutes, GYATT!"
- "我们要10分钟后见，GYATT！"
