# What does "gyatt" mean?（gyatt 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"gyatt" is a slang term that is often used to refer to someone or something as being annoying or irritating. It can also be used to express a mild form of disapproval or frustration.

**中文解释：**  
"gyatt" 是一个俚语，通常用来形容某人或某事物是烦人的或令人懊恼的。它也可以用来表达轻微的不满或烦躁。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: He just keeps on talking and talking, it's a real gyatt!
   中文翻译: 他一直在 talking，真是烦死了！

2) English sentence: That song is such a gyatt after you've heard it a hundred times.
   中文翻译: 听了上百遍后，这首歌真的很烦人。

### Origin / 来源背景
The origin of "gyatt" is not clearly documented, and it seems to have originated in British English as a slang term. Its exact roots and how it evolved into this meaning are not well-known.
