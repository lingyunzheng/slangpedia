## "Mother is mothering"
**English:**  
"Mother is mothering" is an informal way of saying that someone's mother is being very caring and nurturing towards them. It implies that the mother is taking a protective and supportive role, perhaps by checking up on them frequently, offering advice, or just being around to provide comfort.
**中文:**  
"'Mother is mothering' 是一种非正式的说法，用来表示某个人的母亲在非常关心和照顾他们。这暗示母亲在扮演保护和支持的角色，也许会频繁地关心他们，提供忠告，或者只是在旁提供安慰。"
