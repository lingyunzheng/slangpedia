## "Mother is mothering"
**English:** "Mother is mothering" is a playful way to say that a woman is being very nurturing, caring, and attentive, typically to a child but can also be used in other contexts to describe the act of being protective and supportive.
**中文:** "Mother is mothering" 是一种轻松的说法，用来形容一位女性表现得非常有养育照顾的天性，通常是对待孩子，但在其他情境下，也可以用来描述一个人表现得充满保护和支持的态度。
Example Usage:
- "Since her partner was away, she really had to mother her younger sister all day."
- "Her presence in class makes everyone feel like a kid when she starts mothering them to finish their homework on time."
