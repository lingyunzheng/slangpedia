## "Mother is Mothering"
### English Explanation:
- **Meaning:** When someone is being overly caring, nurturing, or overprotective towards someone else, especially in a way that is seen as patronizing or excessive.
- **Example Usage:** "She was mothering him about every little thing, I couldn't believe how overbearing she was being."
### Chinese Explanation:
- **意思:** 当某人过度关怀、养育或过度保护他人，尤其是在被看作是说教或过分的情况下。
- **例句:** “她在每件小事上都表现得无微不至，我简直无法相信她有多过分。”
