# "Mother is mothering"
## English Explanation
When someone is "mothering," they are acting in a very caring and nurturing manner, often going out of their way to look after and take care of others. It's like being a mother to someone, showing them lots of love and attention.
**Example:** After her friend broke up with her boyfriend, her mother came over to "mother" her, bringing a delicious meal and listening to her vent about the situation.
## Chinese Explanation
“母亲化”意味着一个人表现得非常体贴和包容，通常会超出正常范围来照顾和关照他人。这就像是对某人扮演母亲的角色，给予他们很多的爱和关注。
**例子：** 她朋友和男友分手后，她的妈妈特意过来“母亲化”她，带了一顿美味的饭菜，并耐心倾听她吐槽那个情况。
