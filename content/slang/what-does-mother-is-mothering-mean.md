## "Mother is Mothering"
**English Slang Explanation:**
"Mother is mothering" is an American slang phrase used to describe someone who is taking care of or nurturing others, especially in a caregiving or parental role for non-blood-related individuals. It can also be used to describe a strong, supportive, and nurturing behavior or attitude. For example, a friend might "mother" a group of other friends, offering advice, emotional support, or practical help.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
“妈妈在带孩子”（māma zài dài háizidan）用来形容某个人在照顾或培育其他人，尤其是在非血缘关系的人身上扮演类似父母的角色。也可以形容一个人表现得很坚强、充满关怀和养育的行为或态度。比如，一个朋友可能会“带”一群其他朋友，提供建议、情感支持或实际帮助。
