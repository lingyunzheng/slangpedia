# "Mother is Mothering"
## English
"Mother is mothering" is a term used humorously to describe someone, often an older woman, who is overly attentive or protective in a way that might be seen as patronizing or excessive. It can also be used to describe a situation where someone is trying to care for another in a very nurturing, but perhaps overly involved, manner. It can carry a slightly negative connotation, implying that the person or behavior is a bit much.
## Chinese (简体字)
“mother is mothering” 这个说法用来幽默地形容某人，通常是上了年纪的女人，对别人过度关心或保护，这种行为可能会被看作是有点自作多情或者过于干涉。此外，这个词也可以用来描述一个人试图以一种极其呵护的方式去照顾他人，但或许有点过多。这个词带有一定的负面含义，暗示这种行为有点过分。
## Bilingual Slang Explanation
A humorous way to describe someone who is being overly attentive or protective, often patronizing or excessive, much like an overbearing mother.
