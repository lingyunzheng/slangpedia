## "Mother is mothering" — 中文 slang explanation
### English Translation:
- **"Mother is mothering"** means a mother is taking good care of or nurturing someone or something. It can be used to describe a mother's protective and caring behavior towards her child, or even towards another person or animal.
### Chinese Explanation:
- **“母亲在‘妈咪’（当妈咪）”**: 这个俚语用来形容一个人，尤其是女性，像母亲一样照顾和养育他人或事物。这里“妈咪”不是一个实词，而是用来形象化地表达一种“母亲般照顾”的行为。它既可以用来形容母亲对自己孩子的照顾，也可以形容一个人对其他人或动物的照顾和爱护行为。
### Example Usage:
- **English**: "Since my sister moved in with her boyfriend, her mother has been pretty much mothering her non-stop."
- **Chinese**: 自从我姐姐搬进她男朋友的家里，她妈妈就一直像照顾自己孩子一样无微不至地照顾着她。
