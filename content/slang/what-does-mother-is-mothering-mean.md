## Mother is Mothering

**English:**  
"Mother is mothering" is an idiom meaning that someone (often a woman) is being nurturing, caring, and looking after others. It describes a maternal or protective behavior, usually shown towards children, but can also extend to pets or less vulnerable individuals. Example: "When her friends were feeling down, she was really mothering them with her kind words and gentle advice."

**汉语：**  
“Mother is mothering” 是一个俚语，指某人（通常是女性）表现出一种养育、关怀及照顾他人的行为。它描述了一种母性的或保护性的行为，通常是对孩子展现出来，但也可能扩展到宠物或不太脆弱的个体。例句：“当朋友们情绪低落时，她给了他们很多温暖和温柔的建议，真是典型的母亲的行为。”
