## "Mother is mothering"
**English:**  
"Mother is mothering" is a colloquial expression suggesting that a mother is being overprotective or overly involved in a situation, often to the point of being intrusive or unnecessarily controlling. For example, if a mother is constantly checking in on her adult child to make sure they are eating properly, this is an example of mothering too much.
**中文：**  
"Mother is mothering" 是一种俚语表达，意味着母亲表现出过度保护或者过度介入某事的行为，有时甚至显得烦扰或过于干预。例如，如果一位母亲频繁地打电话询问她的成年孩子是否按时吃饭，这就属于过度母亲化的例子。
