## 英文解释
**Mother is mothering** in English can mean that someone is showing nurturing or caring behavior, typically in a maternal way. It often refers to someone who is taking on the role of a mother in caring for others or showing a caring and protective attitude.
### 示例用法
1. "Really? You think he's just mothering a bit too much? He's always trying to solve everyone's problems."
2. "I don't mind her mothering me, but sometimes I just need to be left alone to handle things on my own."
## 汉语解释
**母亲在母性地照顾** 指的是某人在表现出一种母性的、充满关爱和照顾的行为。通常是指某人承担起了母亲的角色，关怀和保护他人。也可以指某人过多地表现出一种照顾他人的态度。
### 示例用法
1. “真的？你觉得他在过度照顾吗？他总是在帮大家解决问题。”
2. “我不介意她母性地照顾我，但有时候我需要独自处理事情。”
