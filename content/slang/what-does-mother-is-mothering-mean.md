## "Mother is Mothering"
### English Explanation
When someone is "mothering" someone else, they are being very caring, protective, and nurturing, often in a way that might remind you of a mother's behavior. It typically involves providing comfort, affection, or a sense of security.
### Chinese Explanation
“mother is mothering” 是指某人表现得非常关心、保护和呵护另一个人，通常这种行为让人联想到母亲的行为。它通常包括提供安慰、关爱或安全感。
### Example Usage
English: "After his friend got dumped, you could see his mother was mothering him by giving him a shoulder to cry on and cooking extra meals."
Chinese: "在他被甩之后，你可以看到他的母亲在‘mothering’他，给他一个可以靠着哭泣的肩膀，还准备了额外的饭菜。"
