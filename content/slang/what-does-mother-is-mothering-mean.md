# "Mother is mothering"
## English Explanation
- **Meaning**: To be overprotective or overly concerned, especially towards a person or in a particular situation.
- **Example**: "She keeps mothering him about his inheritance, as if he can't manage it on his own."
## Chinese Explanation
- **意思**: 用过度保护或过分关心的态度对待某人或某个情况。
- **示例**: "她总是对他怎么怎么的，好像他自己不能处理那份遗产一样。"
This phrase is often used in a humorous or slightly mocking way to describe someone who behaves as though they are the parent, often in a way that is excessive or unnecessary.
