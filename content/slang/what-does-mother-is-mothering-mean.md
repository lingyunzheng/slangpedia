```markdown
## "Mother is mothering"
**English:** 
"Mother is mothering" is an informal way of saying that a woman, typically a mother or a caretaker, is taking care of someone or something with great patience and affection. It often implies that she is doing a lot of nurturing and looking after.
**Chinese:**
"妈妈在‘妈妈’着" 是一种非正式的说法，指某个女性，通常是妈妈或看护者，以极大的耐心和关爱照顾着某人或某事物。通常意味着她正在做很多养育和照顾的事情。
```
