## "Mother is mothering"
### English Explanation
The phrase "mother is mothering" is an informal way of saying that a mother is caring for her child or children in a nurturing and supportive manner. It implies that the mother is taking on the natural roles and responsibilities associated with motherhood, such as feeding, comforting, and protecting her offspring.
### Chinese Explanation
“母亲在母ing”是一个非正式的说法，意味着母亲正在以呵护和支持的方式照顾她的孩子。它暗示母亲正在承担与母职相关的自然角色和责任，比如喂养、安抚和保护她的后代。
### Example Usage
- **English**: "Since her daughter got sick last night, her mother has been mothering her non-stop."
- **Chinese**: "自从她女儿昨晚生病以来，她妈妈一直在持续不断地母ing她女儿。"
