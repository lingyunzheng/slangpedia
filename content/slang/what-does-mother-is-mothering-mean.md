## "Mother is mothering"
### English Explanation
- **Meaning**: This phrase is not a standard slang, but it can be understood as a humorous or derogatory way to suggest that someone is overprotective or excessively involved in the life of another person, usually a child or a spouse. It refers to the behavior of a mother who is being too nurturing, intrusive, or overly concerned.
- **Example**: "Since Grandma moved in, she's been constantly mothering me, telling me what to eat and how to dress."
### Chinese Explanation
- **意思**: 这个短语不是标准的俚语，不过可以幽默地或讽刺地理解为表示某人过度呵护或过分干涉另一个人（通常是孩子或配偶）的生活。这里描绘的是一个“母性过强”的行为，表现为过度呵护、侵入或过于关心。
- **例句**: "自从奶奶搬来和我们一起住后，她一直在不断地关怀我的生活，告诉我该吃什么、该穿什么。"
