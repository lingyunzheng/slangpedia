## Mothering
**English**: When someone is "mothering," they are showing nurturing, caring, and protective behavior, similar to how a mother would take care of their children.
**中文**：当有人“母性发作”或“母亲化”时，他们会展现出关怀、照料和保护的行为，就像母亲照料自己的孩子一样。
