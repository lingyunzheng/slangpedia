## "mother is mothering"
### 在中文中的解释
**“妈妈在妈妈做着”**  
意指妈妈在“管着”、“照顾着”或者“操心着”某人或某事。比如说，如果一个年轻人总是在家乱放东西，他的妈妈可能就会说“她这妈妈又在妈妈做着了”，意思是妈妈又开始收拾整理了。
### 在英文中的解释
**"mom is momballing"**  
This means that mom is taking care of, worrying about, or managing something or someone. For example, if someone is always leaving things in messy or disorganized states, their mom might say “She’s mom-balling again,” implying that the mom is once again cleaning up after them.
