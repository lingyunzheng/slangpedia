### Mother is mothering
**English:**  
"Mother is mothering" is an idiomatic phrase that means someone is taking on the role of a mother, typically by being very nurturing, protective, and supportive, even if they are not biologically related to the person in need. It implies that someone is caring and showing a maternal instinct towards another person who is struggling or vulnerable.
**中文：**  
"母亲在‘母性’着" 这个短语是一个习语，意味着某人正在承担一种母亲的角色，通常表现为非常关怀、保护和支持，即使这个人与求助者没有血缘关系。它暗示某人在照顾和表现出母性的本能，尤其是在另一个人遇到困难或显得脆弱时。
