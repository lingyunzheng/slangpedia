## Bilingual Slang Explanations
### Chinese: 母亲在“母爱”（mǔ qìngzài “mǔ ài”）
- **Simplified Chinese:** 母亲在“母爱”
- **Pinyin:** Mǔ qìngzài “mǔ ài”
- **Meaning:** "The mother is being a mothering, showing maternal love and care." 
- **Bilingual Explanation:** When someone says a mother is "mothering", they mean she is acting very nurturing and caring, often in a way that is warm and protective, much like what a mother would do. This could involve any form of support, care, or attention.
### English: Momma's Little Helper (or just "Mothering")
- **Simplified English:** Momma's Little Helper (or just "Mothering")
- **Meaning:** Describes someone (often a child or spouse) who is very supportive and caring, doing a lot of the "mothering" work by taking care of others or managing household duties.
- **Bilingual Explanation:** When someone is acting as a "mothering" figure, they are showing a lot of care and concern, much like a mother would for her children. This could include cooking, cleaning, providing emotional support, or just being very attentive and nurturing.
---
Note: These expressions are informal and commonly used in everyday conversation.
