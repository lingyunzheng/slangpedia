## "mother is mothering"
### English Explanation
This phrase "mother is mothering" is a colloquial expression used to describe a mother's nurturing and caring behavior toward her children or other people. It implies that the mother is actively taking care of someone or something, providing support, and showing a maternal instinct.
### Chinese Explanation
