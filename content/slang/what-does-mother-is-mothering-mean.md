## Mother is Mothering
**English:**  
People often use the phrase "mother is mothering" to describe someone who is being very nurturing, caring, or supportive. It's usually used in a light-hearted or affectionate way. For example, if a friend is taking a lot of care of you or doing something extra nice for you, you might say "oh, she's really mothering over me today!"
**中文:**  
当有人说“mother is mothering”时，他们通常是指某人表现得非常有母爱、关怀或者支持性。这个词组通常用于一种轻松或亲昵的语境中。比如，当一个朋友特别照顾你或者为你做了一些额外的好事时，你可以这样说：“她今天真的很像在‘母爱’我。”
**Example:**
- **English:** It's exam season, and my friend's mom has really been mothering over me, making sure I have everything I need for study.
- **中文:** 到了考试季，朋友的妈妈特别“母爱”我，确保我所有学习必备的东西都有。
