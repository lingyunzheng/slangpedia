# "Mother is mothering" in Bilingual Slang
## English
**Translation:** "Mother is mothering" can be used to describe a situation where a mother is taking care of her child or other children, showing a nurturing and protective attitude.
**Examples:**
- "Look at how she is with the kids! Mother is mothering really well today!"
- "It's Mother's Day, and my sister is really mothering my niece, even though she's not her biological parent."
## Chinese
**翻译:** "mother is mothering" 用于描述一个妈妈在照顾她的孩子或其它孩子时展现出了养育和保护的态度。
**示例:**
- "看看她对孩子们的样子！今天妈妈真的照顾得很好！"
- "今天是母亲节，我妹妹对我的侄女照顾得很周到，虽然是收养而非血亲。"
