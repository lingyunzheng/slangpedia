```markdown
## "Mother is Mothering"
**English:** 
"I think my mom is checking up on me a bit too much."
**Explanation:**
When someone say "mother is mothering," it typically means the mother is being overly attentive or watchful. This can be seen as a subtle criticism or complaint about the mother's level of engagement, often implying that the person feels they are not getting enough personal space or are being overprotected.
**Chinese (简体中文):**
“我觉得妈妈老是时不时地给我来点‘母爱攻势’。”
**解释：**
当有人说“妈妈在母ing”，通常是指妈妈表现得过于关心或照顾。这往往是一种含蓄的批评或抱怨，表明这个人觉得他们没有得到足够的个人空间或者感觉被过度保护了。
```
This explanation provides both a straightforward translation and a contextual understanding of the slang phrase.
