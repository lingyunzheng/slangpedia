## "Mother is Mothering" Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:**
- **Meaning:** When someone is being overly protective, nurturing, or caring towards another person, especially in a way that involves a lot of effort and attention.
- **Example:** "Since her best friend broke her leg, her mother is totally mothering her, bringing her meals, making sure she takes her medication, and even doing her homework."
**Mandarin:**
- **意思:** 形容某人对另一人表现出过度保护、关怀或照料，尤其是以付出大量努力和关注的方式。
- **例句:** "自从她最好的朋友摔断了腿，她的母亲完全在照顾她，给她做饭，确保她按时吃药，甚至帮她做作业。"
