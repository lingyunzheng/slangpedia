## "Mother is Mothering"
**English Slang Explanation:**
"Mother is mothering" is a playful way to describe someone who is showing a lot of care, attention, or nurturing towards others, just like a mother would do. It can also be used to describe the act of someone being overly protective or over-attentive.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
“妈妈在带孩子”通常用来形容某个人非常关心、照料他人，就像母亲对待孩子一样。这个表达也可以用来形容某人表现得很招人疼爱，或者过于保护人。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "When Emily was babysitting, she was really mothering the kids, making sure they had all their snacks and comforting whiny ones."
- Chinese: "当艾米丽临时照看孩子们时，她就像个好妈妈一样负责，确保他们都有了零食，还安慰那些哭闹的孩子。"
