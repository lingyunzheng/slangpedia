## "Mother is Mothering"
### English Explanation
"Mother is mothering" is an idiomatic expression that implies someone (often a woman) is showing a nurturing or caring behavior, similar to how a mother takes care of her children. It often describes someone who is naturally kind, attentive, and supportive towards others.
### Chinese Explanation
“妈妈在母ing”是一个意象表达，意味着某人（通常是女性）正在展示一种养育或关怀的行为，类似于母亲如何照顾孩子。它常用来描述某人天生充满善意、体贴和支持他人。
---
例子：She really knows how to mother—she’s always there with a comforting word or a home-cooked meal.  
她真会母ing——她总是有安慰的话语或一顿家常饭在身边。
