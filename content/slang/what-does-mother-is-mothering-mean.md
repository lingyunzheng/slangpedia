# "Mother is mothering"
## English Explanation
"Mother is mothering" is an idiom that describes someone (often a woman) who is providentially taking care of or attending to others with a nurturing and maternal attitude. It can also imply that someone is being overbearing or overly protective, similar to a mother's behavior.
## Chinese Explanation
"母亲在娘家人" (mǔqīn zài niáng rén) 是一个比喻，用来形容某人（通常是女性）在照顾或关照他人时表现出一种慈爱和母性的态度。它也可以意味着某人过度保护或过于关爱，就像母亲的行为一样。
这个短语中的“娘家人”在现代语境中已经不太常用，但保留了传统的母性关爱的意思。
