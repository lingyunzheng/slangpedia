# "Mother is Mothering"
## English
"Mother is mothering" is a casual expression that means someone is taking care of, nurturing, or being very attentive and loving toward someone or something. It often describes a nurturing behavior or attitude, similar to how a mother would care for her children.
Example: "She's been real busy, but when she's home, she's really mothering her new kitten and cooking big dinners for the family."
## 中文
“mother is mothering” 是一种非正式的表达，意味着某人正在照顾、哺育或对某人或某物非常关爱和呵护。它通常用来描述一种哺育的行为或态度，就像母亲对待她的孩子一样。
例句：“她最近很忙，但一有空就忙着照顾她的新宠物猫和为全家人准备丰盛的晚餐。”
