### Mother Is Mothering
**English**: "Mother is mothering" literally means that a mother is taking care of someone or something, typically showing a nurturing or protective attitude.
**Chinese (Simplified)**: "妈妈在娘胎里" (Māma zài niángtāi lǐ) 直接翻译为“母亲在娘胎里”可能不太符合语境，这里可以解释为“妈妈在扮演母亲的角色”或更直接一点，“妈妈在照顾/关心”某个孩子或家庭成员。更常见的表达是“妈妈在给孩子/家庭‘带’（mǔdòu）”，就意味着妈妈在负责照顾和教育。
**Explanation**:
- **English**: This phrase doesn't have a common unique slang translation but can be explained in a straightforward way. It's used to describe the act of a mother being attentive and caring towards someone, which could be a child, a family member, or even a home.
- **Chinese (Simplified)**: “母亲在娘胎里”这种直译实际上不太符合意译，常见说法可能是“妈妈正在给孩子‘带’（mǔdòu）”，指的是妈妈在进行照顾和教育。它可以简单理解为：“妈妈正在负责任地照顾或关心某人或某事”。
**Example Usage**:
- **English**: "Since her sister is going on vacation, her mother is mothering her niece to make sure everything is okay back home."
- **Chinese (Simplified)**: “由于她的妹妹要出去度假，所以她的妈妈在照顾/带她的侄女，确保家里一切安好。”
