# Glowed Up 🌟
## English Explanation
"Glowed up" is a slang term used to describe a person who has dramatically improved or changed in a positive way, often in terms of appearance, attitude, or behavior. It suggests a transformation that makes the person more attractive or impressive.
### Example Usage
- "She totally glowed up after the holiday! Her hair color, makeup, and new wardrobe gave her that extra spark."
## Chinese Explanation
“Glowed up” 是一个俚语，用来形容一个人在外观、态度或行为等方面发生了显著的提升或改变，使其变得更加有吸引力或令人印象深刻。它暗示了一场能让人焕然一新的转变。
### 示例用法
- “她休假后简直焕然一新！换了个发型，化了妆，还添置了新衣，整个人都亮了起来。”
