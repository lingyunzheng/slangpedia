# Glowed Up
## 中文解释
“glowed up”直译为“升级了光彩”，是一种表示某人在某个方面（比如外貌、气质、精神状态等）变得更加吸引人的口语表达。通常用于描述某人经过努力或变化后，整体形象更加出色。
**例子：**
- 她这次聚会上真的 glow up 了，整个人都散发着自信的光芒。
## 英文解释
“glowed up” is a slang term originating from digital culture, meaning that someone has "upgraded" in terms of appearance, aura, or overall vibe. It is commonly used to describe someone who has undergone changes or improvements, making them more attractive and impressive.
**Example:**
- She really glowed up at the party, radiating confidence from every pore.
