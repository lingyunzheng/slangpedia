## Glowed Up (Literal meaning: 皮肤发亮提升)
**English Slang: Glowed Up**
- **Explanation:** This term is used to describe someone who has significantly improved their appearance, look, or overall vibe. It often implies that someone has made noticeable changes, such as improving their skincare, gaining confidence, or making fashion statements that make them seem more attractive and radiant.
- **Example:** "After joining the fitness class, she totally glowed up! Now she always wears her favorite colorful blouse and earrings—she looks amazing!"
**Chinese Slang: 皮肤发亮提升**
- **Explanation:** 这个词用来描述某个人的外貌、气质或整体感觉有了显著提升。通常意味着某人做出了明显的改变，比如改善了皮肤状况、变得更加自信或者通过时尚装扮让自己看起来更加迷人和有光彩。
- **Example:** “参加健身房后，她完全皮肤发亮提升！现在她总是穿她最喜欢的彩色上衣和耳环—她看起来真好！”
