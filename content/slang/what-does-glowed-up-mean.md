## Glowed Up
**中文释义：**
“glowed up” 的中文流行表达是“暴富”或者“焕然一新”。这个说法通常用来形容某人或某物在某个时刻变得非常美好或者拥有了大量财富、物质上的巨大改变。例如，一个贫困家庭突然中了大奖，生活条件发生了翻天覆地的变化，可以说他们“暴富”了。
**例句：**
1. 去年他还住在出租屋里，现在住进了大别墅，简直就是“暴富”！
2. 她换了新手机新衣服后，整个人看起来“焕然一新”，仿佛“glowed up”。
**英文释义：**
"Glowed up" is a slang term that means someone or something has suddenly become much better or more desirable. It often implies a significant positive transformation, such as dramatic improvement in appearance, wealth, or overall condition. For example, if someone drastically improves their appearance after a period of being quite ordinary, they can be said to have "glowed up."
**Example Sentences:**
1. Last year, he was living in a rented apartment, but now he’s moved into a luxurious villa. He’s really "glowed up"!
2. After she changed her phone and clothes, she looked "brand new," as if she had "glowed up."
