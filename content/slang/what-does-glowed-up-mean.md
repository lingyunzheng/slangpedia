# "Glowed Up" (English-Chinese)
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a popular slang term used to describe a significant improvement in someone's appearance, behavior, or overall vibe. It's often used in a positive way to highlight that someone has "bee-lined" their game, basically meaning they've enhanced themselves in a noticeable way.
**Chinese:**  
"Glowed up"（焕然一新）是指某人在外貌、行为或整体气质上有明显的提升。这个词通常用于正面地描述某人已经“升级”，也就是说他们在某个方面进行了改进，变得更好了。
---
例句:
- In English: She totally glowed up after the makeover!
- In Chinese: 她在整容之后焕然一新！
- In English: He really glowed up when he started working out!
- In Chinese: 他开始健身之后整个人都焕然一新！
