# Glowed Up
## English
**Glowed Up**: This slang term, originating from social media platforms, typically refers to someone who has significantly improved or enhanced themselves in appearance, mood, or general demeanor. It can be used to describe a drastic positive transformation, for instance, after getting a haircut, getting into better shape, or just putting extra effort into how they present themselves. For example:
* "She totally glowed up after her holiday: new hair, new clothes, she even smile more!"
## Chinese
**挂了个满** (glowed up): 这个起源于社交媒体平台的俚语，通常用来形容一个人在外观、情绪或整体 demeanor上有了显著的改善或提升。它可以用来描述一种突然令人印象深刻的变化，比如做了新发型、健身塑形，或者只是更用心地打扮自己。例如：
* “她假期后彻底挂了个满：新的发型，新的服饰，她笑得也更多了！”
**例句/Usage Examples:**
- English: "He totally glowed up after his haircut!"
- Chinese: “他在剪发后彻底挂了个满！”
- English: "She glowed up after breaking up with her ex."
- Chinese: “分手后她彻底挂了个满。”
