## Glowed Up
### English
"Glowed up" means someone looks much better or more attractive than they did before, often because of a change in appearance, behavior, or mood. It can also refer to someone who has become more successful, popular, or confident.
**Example:**
She got a new haircut and did a lot of makeup, and now she's totally glowed up!
### Chinese
"Glowed up" 指某个人比之前看起来更好看或更吸引人，通常是因为外表、行为或情绪发生了变化。这个词也可以用来形容一个人变得更有成功、更受欢迎或更有自信。
**例句:**
她剪了一个新的发型，做了很多化妆，现在完全是个new look了！（直译）
她变得完全非常亮眼了！（意译）
