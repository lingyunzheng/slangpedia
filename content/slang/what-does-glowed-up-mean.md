# Glowed Up (both Chinese and English)
## 拉满 (Lǎ mǎn)
### Chinese Explanation
意指某人在某些方面（比如外貌、状态、形象等）有了显著的提升和改善。这就像看了一部电视剧后角色的状态全面提升了，让人刮目相看。例如，一个朋友换了新发型或新衣服后看起来非常有活力，你可以说他/她“拉满”了，非常棒的状态。
### English Explanation
Refers to a significant improvement or enhancement in some aspects of someone's life, such as appearance, mood, or overall image. It's like when a character in a TV show has their status greatly upgraded, making them look amazing and refreshing. For example, if a friend gets a new haircut or outfit and looks very vibrant, you might say they are "glowed up," indicating they are in an exceptionally good state.
