## Glowed Up
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a phrase used to describe someone who has significantly improved or transformed themselves positively, often through changes in their appearance, attitude, or both. It's commonly used to express admiration for someone who has become much better or more appealing.
**中文:**  
"Glowed up" 是一个用来形容某人在外貌、态度或者两者上有了显著改进或积极转变的短语。这个短语通常用来表达对变得更好或更有吸引力的人的赞赏。
