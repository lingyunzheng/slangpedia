# Glowed Up 🌟
## English
**Glowed Up:**  
Got better, prettier, or more awesome in a noticeable way. Imagine lighting up like a neon sign after a makeover, or stepping out looking so amazing that you practically glow from the inside out. This phrase is often used to describe someone who has improved their appearance or vibe substantially.
Example:  
*After the makeover, she completely glowed up at the party!*
## 中文
**Glowed Up:**  
变得更好、更漂亮或更酷炫，在明显方面提升了魅力或外貌。想象一下，经过改造后像霓虹灯一样闪闪发光，或者在外出时看起来如此惊艳以致光彩夺目。这句话通常用来形容某人在外表或气质上有了显著的改善。
例子：  
在派对上，经过整容后的她完全变得光彩照人！
