### Glowed Up
**English Slang:** *Glowed Up*\n\n**Explanation:** When someone looks particularly good or beautiful, especially after some changes or improvements, this phrase is used. It suggests a noticeable and positive transformation in appearance. For example, if a friend has gotten a new haircut, improved their skincare routine, or simply dressed up for an event, you might say they "glowed up."
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
**英文俚语：** *Glowed Up*\n\n**解释：** 当某人的外表特别好或美丽，尤其是在经过一些改变或提升之后，就会使用这个短语。它表明了一种明显的、正面的外貌变化。例如，如果朋友换了一个新发型、改善了护肤习惯，或者仅仅是为某个活动打扮了一下，你可能会说他们“glowed up”。
