## Glowed Up
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a slang term mostly used on social media, particularly among teenagers and influencers. It describes someone who has undergone a major positive transformation—whether that's an overall improvement in appearance, lifestyle, or mood. It implies a noticeable and usually sudden change for the better.
**中文:**  
"Glowed up" 是一种主要在社交媒体上使用的俚语，尤其是在青少年和网红之间。这个术语描述的是某人在外貌、生活方式或情绪上经历了显著的积极变化。它暗示了一个明显的、通常是突然的好转。
