# "Glowed Up" (俚语解释)
## 英文
**Glowed Up**
- **Meaning**: This phrase is used to describe someone or something that has undergone a significant improvement or enhancement, often in appearance or overall quality.
- **Usage Example**: "She really glowed up at the wedding. She looked stunning in her dress!"
## 中文
**--------**
- **意思**: 这个短语用来形容某人或某物经历了显著的改进或提升，通常是指外貌或整体质量的改善。
- **使用示例**: "她在婚礼上真的是光彩照人。她穿那条裙子简直美呆了！"
