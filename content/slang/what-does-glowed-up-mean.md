# `glowed up`
## English Explanation:
To have "glowed up" means to experience a significant and positive transformation, often referring to someone who has improved their appearance, style, or overall presence. It can be used in the context of someone who has upgraded their makeup, fashion sense, or who has just experienced a major change that makes them look and feel better.
## Chinese Explanation:
“glowed up”的意思是指经历了一个重大的正面转变，通常指的是某人的外貌、风格或整体存在感有了显著提升。这个词可以用于描述那些通过改进妆容、时尚风格或经历了重大变化后，看上去和感觉更好的人。
**例句：**
- **English:** She got a new haircut and it completely glowed her up!
- **Chinese:** 她剪了一个新发型，完全焕发出光彩！
- **English:** He has truly glowed up since he started exercising regularly.
- **Chinese:** 自从他开始规律运动以来，他真的焕然一新了。
