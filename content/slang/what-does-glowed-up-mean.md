## Glowed Up
#### English
**Glowed Up:**  
This phrase is often used to describe a person who has changed in a positive way and looks better than expected. It can refer to physical appearance, mood, or even overall demeanor. It's akin to saying someone has "turned it up" or is looking fantastic.
#### Chinese
**Glowed Up:**  
这句话通常用来形容一个人在某个方面有了积极的变化，看起来比以前更好。它可以指外貌、心情，或是整体状态的提升。相当于说某人“变得更有光泽”或“看起来超级棒”。
