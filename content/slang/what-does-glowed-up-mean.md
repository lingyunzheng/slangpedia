## Glowed Up (原话: 状态升级 / 提升到了另一个level)
在中文口语中，"状态升级"或"提升到了另一个level"可以用来解释“glowed up”。这个词通常是指某人的外貌、气质或者整体表现有了显著的提升和改善，让人眼前一亮，仿佛有一种光圈罩在了他们身上。比如，一个人可能因为精心打扮、健康状况变好或者自信提高而显得更加迷人和光彩照人。
**英文解释:**
"Glowed up" is commonly used to describe a noticeable improvement in someone's appearance, demeanor, or overall presentation. It often refers to an individual looking or feeling much better than they did before, as if they have received a glow or uplift in their status or condition.
**Example Usage:**
- 在化妆之前和化完妆之后，她简直“glowed up”！
- Before and after applying makeup, she "glowed up"!
- 毕业典礼上她看起来“glowed up”，穿着学士服的她显得英姿飒爽。
- She "glowed up" at the graduation ceremony, looking elegant and radiant in her cap and gown.
