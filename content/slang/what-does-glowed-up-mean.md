# English: Glowed Up
**Explanation:** The term "glowed up" is used to describe someone who has significantly improved or transformed, often in a positive way. It can refer to physical appearance, overall demeanor, or overall style and confidence.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- 英文：Glowed Up
- 释义：当一个人在各个方面（如外貌、气质、风格和自信）都产生了大幅度的提升和改变时，人们会用 "glowed up" 来形容他。这个表达通常用来赞美一个人的整体形象变得更加出色。
