# Glowed Up 🌟
## English:
**Glowed Up**: This slang term is used to describe someone who has significantly improved or transformed themselves in a positive way, often through self-care, style upgrades, or personal achievements. It's similar to saying that someone looks or feels more polished and better than before.
Example: "Since starting her workout routine, Maria has totally glowed up! She looks healthier and more vibrant than ever."
## Chinese:
**Glowed Up**：这个俚语用来形容某人在自我提升或通过美容护理、风格上的升级或个人成就之后，变得更好、更强或焕然一新。它类似于说某人比以前看起来或感觉更精致、更出色。
示例：自从开始她的健身计划后，Maria 真的变得气质提升了！她的皮肤看起来更健康，更有活力。
