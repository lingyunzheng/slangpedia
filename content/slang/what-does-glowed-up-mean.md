# Glowed Up
## English
**Glowed Up**: This phrase is a fun, colloquial way to describe the improvement or dramatic enhancement of someone's appearance, mood, or overall vibe. When someone is said to have "glowed up," it means they have suddenly looked or seemed much more attractive or radiant.
**Example**: 
- "After hitting the gym and nailing her skincare routine, she completely glowed up at the party!"
## 中文
**Glowed Up**：这是一个充满趣味的俚语，用来形容某人的外貌、心情或整体感觉大幅提升或突飞猛进。当有人说某人“Glowed Up”时，意味着他们在某些方面突然变得更加引人注目或光彩照人。
**例子**：
- “她通过在健身房锻炼和护肤，派对上看起来完全不一样，真是太吸睛了！”
