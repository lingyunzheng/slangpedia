# Glowed Up
## English Explanation
"Glowed up" is a slang phrase primarily used in social media and online communities. It describes a significant improvement or transformation, often in how someone looks, dresses, or behaves. This term is often used to compliment someone who has made noticeable positive changes in their life or appearance. For example, if someone has upgraded their fashion sense, overcome challenges, or simply looks more confident, they might have "glowed up."
## Chinese Explanation
"Glowed up" 是一个流行于社交媒体和在线社区中的俚语，主要用来描述一个人在外观、穿着或行为上的显著改善或转变。当有人在时尚品味、克服挑战或看起来更自信等方面有了显著的进步时，人们可能会说他们“Glowed up”。举个例子，如果一个人的打扮变得更加时尚，或者变得更加积极自信，我们可以用“Glowed up”来描述这种变化。
