## Glowed Up
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a slang term that describes a noticeable improvement in someone's appearance, often after they've made changes to their skincare, style, or both. It can also apply to the improvement of someone's overall demeanor or vibe, making them look more attractive and appealing.
**Chinese:**  
"Glowed up" 是一种俚语表达，用来形容某人在外貌上出现显著改善，通常是通过改善护肤或提升风格的方式实现的。这个词也可以用于形容某人的气质和气场有了提升，使他们看起来更具有吸引力和魅力。
