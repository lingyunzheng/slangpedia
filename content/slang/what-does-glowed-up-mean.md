## "Glowed Up" (中文：upgraded)
**English Explanation:**
"Glowed up" is a slang expression used to describe someone who has significantly improved or transformed, usually in a positive way, leading to a more attractive or impressive appearance or character. 
**Example Usage:**
- She totally glowed up after her vacation; she looked so fit and happy.
- Him glowed up majorly last year—he got a new job and started working out.
**中文解释:**
"upgraded" 是一个网络用语，用来形容某人在某方面有了显著的改进或变化，通常用于正面的意义，并且这种变化会让某人的外观或性格变得更吸引人或更令人印象深刻。
