## Glowed Up
### English
Glowed up is a slang term used to describe someone who has improved, upgraded, or transformed in a positive way. This could be related to physical appearance, skills, personality, or overall quality of life. Essentially, it's like saying the person has "shone brighter" or become more impressive since the last time they were observed.
### Chinese
"Glowed up" 是一个俚语表达，用来描述某人在各个方面变得更棒、更出色或有所提升。这可能与外貌、技能、个性或生活整体质量有关。简单来说，就是说这个人在上次被观察到现在变得更吸引人或更出色了。
**Example Usage:**
- English: She has glowed up since high school, now she's a successful lawyer.
- Chinese: 她自从高中以来简直就是惊艳蜕变，现在成了一个成功的律师。
