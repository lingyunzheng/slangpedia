## Glowed Up
**English:**  
*Meaning:* To drastically improve one's appearance, usually through makeup, hair styling, or skincare, making someone look much better than they did previously.
**Chinese (Mandarin):**  
*意思:* 外观有了显著的提升，通常通过化妆、发型设计或护肤，让某人看起来比之前更出色。
---
Example Usage:  
*English:* She decided to go out and literally **glowed up** with a new haircut and makeup.
*Chinese (Mandarin):* 她决定出去，并通过新的发型和妆容让自己 **彻底变美了**。
