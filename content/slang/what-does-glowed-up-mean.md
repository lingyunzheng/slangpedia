# Glowed Up
**中文解释:**  
“glowed up”翻译成中文可以是“upgraded”，指的是某个人在某段时间内面貌焕然一新，变得更漂亮、更有魅力或者全面提升自己，无论是在外貌、气质还是内在品质上都有显著的提升。
**英语解释:**  
"Glowed up" literally means someone has become more attractive or more put-together, often after a period of time or with a significant life change. It can refer to someone's external appearance (like new clothing choices) or internal improvements (like a new attitude or confidence). The phrase implies a transformation that makes a person seem more polished and appealing.
