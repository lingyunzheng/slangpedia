# "Glowed Up"
## English
- **Meaning:** Improved or upgraded a lot in terms of appearance, personality, or overall vibe.
- **Example Usage:** "Since she got a new haircut and dressed in denim, she totally glowed up at the party!"
## 中文
- **意思:** 在外貌、个性或整体气场上有很大的提升或改进。
- **例句:** "自从染了新发型，还穿上了牛仔装扮，她在派对上简直焕然一新！"
