# "Glowed up"
## English
**Meaning:** To look much better or more attractive than before. Often used to describe someone who has improved their appearance through makeup, skincare, or simply through a more positive demeanor.
**Example Sentence:**
"I can't believe she got her hair done and put on makeup — she totally glowed up!"
## 中文
**含义:** 表示某人在外表上变得更好或更吸引人。通常用于描述通过化妆、护肤或更积极的态度而改善了外观的人。
**例句:**
"我真的不敢相信她剪了头发又化了妆，整个人都‘焕发了新的光彩’！"
