## 精灵劇場：glowed up
### 英文释义
"Glowed up" is a slang term that describes someone who has significantly improved or transformed themselves, often in a positive way. It can refer to someone who has gained confidence, adopted a more stylish or fashionable image, or achieved a goal that has made them appear more put-together or attractive.
### 中文释义
"glowed up" 指的是一个人在某些方面有了显著的变化或提升，通常是积极向上的转变。它可以描述某人变得更加自信、时尚或有魅力，或者达成了某个目标，使他们看起来更加整洁或吸引人。
