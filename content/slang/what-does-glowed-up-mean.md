## Glowed Up 🌟
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a slang term that originally referred to a person who looks especially good or stunning due to looking more attractive, feeling happy, or having a natural radiance. Now, it's used in many contexts, from a physical transformation in appearance to feeling extremely happy or successful.
**Chinese:**  
"Glowed up" 是一个俚语，最初用来形容一个人看起来特别好看、有魅力或者有自然的光彩。现在这个短语被广泛应用，在很多语境中，既可以指外貌有了显著变化，也可以用来形容感觉非常开心或成功。
