# Glowed Up
## English
**Definition:** When someone looks particularly beautiful or impressive, especially after a change in appearance or mood.
**Example:** "After getting her hair done, she totally glowed up for the party!"
## 中文
**释义：** 当某人在外貌或心情上发生改变，变得特别漂亮或引人注目时。
**例句：** "在晚会上做了头发后，她真的美翻了！"
