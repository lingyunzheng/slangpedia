# Glowed Up
**English:** 
"Glowed up" is a slang phrase used to describe someone who has significantly improved their appearance, mood, or general aura. It often implies that the person has changed for the better, usually through new relationships, personal style upgrades, or improvements in life or career.
**Chinglish (Chinese Slang):**
"焕发新颜" (Huàfānányán) 
意指某人的外貌、心情或整体气场有了显著的提升，通常是因为新恋情、着装风格的升级或生活和职业上的改善。
例子：
- 她刚换了一个新发型，整个人都 **glowed up**！
- 他最近升职了，整个人看起来都 **glowed up**。
Translation Examples:
- 她刚刚换了新发型，整个人都 **焕然一新**！
- 他最近升职了，整个人看起来都 **容光焕发**。
