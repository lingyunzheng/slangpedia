## Glowed Up
**English:** In recent years, "glowed up" has become a popular slang term, often used by young people to describe a positive transformation or improvement in someone's appearance, behavior, or overall vibe. It suggests that someone has become more attractive, put-together, or has improved themselves in some way, often resulting in a noticeable positive change that makes them seem more appealing or impressive.
**Chinese:** "Glowed up" 是近年来流行起来的一个网络用语，常用来描述某人在外貌、行为或整体气场方面的积极转变或提升。这个词意味着某人变得更加吸引人、更整洁有条理，或者在某些方面有所改进，从而导致一个显著的积极变化，让人看起来更迷人或给人留下更深刻的印象。
