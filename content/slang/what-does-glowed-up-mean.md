### "Glowed Up" (English)
*Literal Meaning:* Something or someone that has improved or gotten better.
*Bilingual Slang Explanation:* 
- **English:** Feel like I've "glowed up" today! Started the day a bit frazzled but ended up getting everything done with some extra energy!
- **Chinese:** 今天感觉状态好多了，之前有点手忙脚乱的，后来把所有的事情都处理完了，而且还多积累了一些能量！仿佛焕然一新！
### "Glowed Up" (Chinese)
*Literal Meaning:* 改善或变好了。
*Bilingual Slang Explanation:* 
- **Chinese:** 今天状态特别好，精神倍儿足，感觉整个人都“焕然一新”了。
- **English:** I woke up feeling not great, but after a couple of good cups of coffee and a nice talk with a friend, it feels like I've really "glowed up" today!
