## Glowed Up
**English Slang:** Glowed up
- **Explanation:** The phrase "glowed up" is used to describe someone or something that has significantly improved or enhanced their appearance, attitude, or overall quality. It often implies that the person or thing has become more attractive, animated, or impressive in a noticeable way. This could be due to a new hairstyle, better fashion sense, a more confident demeanour, or any other positive transformation.
- **Example Usage:** "Since joining the gym, she's really glowed up! Her confidence and physique have transformed."
**汉语俚语:** 翻身做了牡丹花
- **解释：** 汉语中的俚语“翻身做了牡丹花”用于形容某人或某事物在外貌、态度或整体品质上有了显著的提升或改善。这个表达通常意味着这个人或事物变得更加吸引人、更生动、或更令人印象深刻。这种改善可能是由于换了个新的发型、更好的时尚品味、更加自信的姿态或者其他任何积极的转变。
- **用法示例：** “自打开始上健身房以来，她的气色和体态真的焕然一新了！”
