# Glowed Up
## English
**Glowed Up**: This term is often used on social media, particularly TikTok, to describe someone who has drastically improved their appearance, style, or overall vibe. It suggests that the individual has undergone a transformation, possibly involving better grooming, outfits, makeup, or a whole new personality. The transformation makes the person "glow" with a newfound confidence and attractiveness.
Examples:
- "She totally glowed up after her holiday and started dressing all up!"
## 中文
**Glowed Up**: 这个词通常是在像TikTok这样的社交媒体上使用，用来形容某人在外貌、风格或整体感觉上发生了显著的变化。它意味着这个人的面貌焕然一新，可能涉及更好的打扮、穿着、妆容，或者全新的个性。这种转变让这个人看起来焕发光彩，充满了新的自信和吸引力。
示例：
- “她在假期后整个人都活力升级了，打扮也全然不同了！”
