## Glowed Up
**English:**  
"Glowed up" is a casually used term, often seen in social media contexts, to describe someone or something that has improved or undergone a significant positive change, making them more attractive, impressive, or desirable than before. It can be used to describe a person who has changed their appearance, improved their qualities, or simply become more confident, or it can refer to a setting or a theme that has been enhanced to look better.
