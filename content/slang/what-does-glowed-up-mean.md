# Glowed Up 🌟
## English
**Glowed Up**: This slang phrase, popular on social media, is used to describe someone who has drastically improved their appearance, style, or general demeanor. It's often accompanied by before-and-after photos or shared experiences of personal growth or transformation.
## Chinese
** glowed up**: 这个网络流行语经常用于描述某个人在外观、风格或整体气场方面有了显著的提升。它常伴随着前后对比的照片或个人成长或转变的经历分享。
### Example Usage
*English:* My friend really glowed up after that haircut and new makeup routine!
*Chinese:* 我的朋友在做了那个发型和新的化妆 routine 后真的变美了很多！
