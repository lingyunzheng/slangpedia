## Glowed Up (中文解释：焕然一新)
**In English:**  
Glowed up is a slang term that indicates someone has transformed or 'lit up' in a positive way, usually referring to improvement in appearance, mood, or overall energy. It emphasizes that this transformation is noticeable and impressive.
**In Chinese:**  
焕然一新（“焕然一新”通常指某人在外貌、情绪或整体能量上有明显的改善和提升，这种转变是显著且令人印象深刻的。）
Example Usage:
- English: "She completely glowed up after her holiday!"
- 中文：她度假回来后焕然一新！
