## English: Glowed Up
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:** The phrase "glowed up" typically means that someone or something has improved a significant amount, generally in a way that makes them more attractive, impressive, or appealing. It often implies a transformation from looking average or unremarkable to looking good or exceptionally good.
**Chinese:** “glowed up” 通常意味着某人或某物有了显著的提升，通常是以更吸引人、更引人注目或更令人愉悦的方式。它通常暗示从看起来平凡或普通变得特别好或非常好看。
### Example Usage
- **English:** After three months of intense training, she totally glowed up and blew everyone away at the competition.
- **Chinese:** 经过三个月的严格训练，她在比赛中完全蜕变成最美的自己，惊艳了所有人。
