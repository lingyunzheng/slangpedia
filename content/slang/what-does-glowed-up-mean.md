## "Glowed Up" (中文：焕然一新)
**English Explanation:**  
"Glowed up" is a slang term that describes someone who has significantly improved their appearance, behavior, or overall demeanor. It's often used to indicate that this improved state is noticeable and impressive.
**Chinese Explanation:**  
"焕然一新"（huàn rán yī xīn）用来形容某人在外貌、行为或整体状态上有了显著的改善，这种改变通常是显而易见且令人印象深刻的。
