```markdown
### English: Glowed Up
**Definition:** When someone looks or feels much better than they did before, often due to a change in appearance, mood, or overall attitude.
**Example:** After getting a new haircut and investing in some new makeup, she absolutely glowed up for her prom!
### 中文: 胶着了 / 挣扎了
**定义:** 表示一个人比之前看起来或感觉更好，通常是由于外观、情绪或整体态度的改变。
**例句:** 她在毕业舞会前剪了一个新发型，还化了妆，简直是脱胎换骨，简直美翻了！
```
Note: "胶着了" or "挣扎了" is a loose translation that conveys the meaning of "glowed up," as there isn't a direct one-to-one translation for this slang. The closest concept might be "脱胎换骨" (tuō tāi huàn gǔ), which means a complete change or transformation.
