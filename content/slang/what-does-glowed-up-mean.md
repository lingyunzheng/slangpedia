# "Glowed Up" (英语: Glowed Up)
## English Explanation
When someone has changed in a very positive way, often after a makeover or transformation. It can refer to physical appearance, personality, or lifestyle improvements.
## Chinese Explanation
当你经历了某种变化，无论是外表、个性还是生活方式，变得更好、更有吸引力时，就可以用 "glowed up" 来形容。这个短语通常用来描述经过改造或重生后带来的正面改变。
### Example Usage
- **English:** "Since starting the fitness program, she has totally glowed up."
- **Chinese:** “自从开始健身计划后，她整个人都亮了起来。”
