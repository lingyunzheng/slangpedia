# "Glowed Up" (English-Chinese Slang Explanation)
## English
### Meaning
- To look drastically more attractive or appealing, often due to new clothes, makeup, a fresh haircut, or a general aura of confidence.
### Example
- "She totally glowed up after getting a new haircut and wearing her favorite makeup. She looked fabulous!"
## 中文
### 含义
- 形容一个人看起来有很大的改变，变得更漂亮或更有吸引力，通常是因为换了新衣服、化了妆、理了新发型，或者整体上看起来充满自信。
### 示例
- "自从她做了新的发型，又化了妆，看起来完全不一样了，简直美呆了！"
