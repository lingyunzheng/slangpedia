# Glowed Up
## English
**Meaning**: This phrase is commonly used when someone looks better or has an improved appearance, sometimes after a period of being inactive or looking less put-together. It can also describe someone who has made a good impression or seems more successful or attractive.
**Example Usage**: "After getting her hair and makeup done, Taylor glowed up big time for her high school reunion!"
## 中文
**含义**：这个短语通常用来形容某人看起来更好看或有更好的仪表，有时候是在他们之前比较不活跃或看起来不太打理之后。它还可以用来描述某人给人留下了好印象，或者看起来更成功或更有魅力。
**用法示例**：在高中同学聚会那天，经过头发和化妆的打理， Taylor 的状态大为改观，真是一番新面貌！
