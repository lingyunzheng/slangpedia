```markdown
# Glowed Up 🌟
**English:** "Glowed up" is a slang phrase that originated in the beauty and fashion spheres but has expanded to various contexts. It is used to describe a person who has visibly improved their appearance, style, or overall makeup game. Essentially, they "glowed up" from a less appealing look to a much more attractive one.
**Chinese:** "Glowed up"（整容了）是一个流行于美容和时尚领域的俚语，但也被广泛应用于多种场合。它用来形容某人在外貌、风格或整体妆容上有了显著的提升，变得更好看。简单来说，就是从不太吸引人到变得更加迷人。
例句：
1. English: After a few viral TikTok makeup tutorials, she truly glowed up.
Chinese: 她在几个流行的TikTok美妆教程后，真的“整容”了。
2. English: He completely glowed up at the company party with that new haircut.
Chinese: 因为那次做了个新发型，在公司晚会上他整个人都“整容”了。
```
Note: The term "整容了" (glowed up) is loosely based on interpreting "glowed up" as a play on the concept of looking much better than before, akin to undergoing cosmetic surgery. This is a creative translation to convey the essence of the original phrase.
