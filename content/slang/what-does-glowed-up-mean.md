## Glowed Up 🌟
### English Explanation:
When someone is said to have "glowed up," it means they have improved in a noticeable way, often in terms of their appearance, personality, or overall demeanor. It's a positive term that suggests someone has reached a better, more appealing version of themselves.
### Chinese Explanation:
当有人被说成"glowed up"时，这意味着他们在某个显著方面有所提升，通常是在外貌、个性或整体气场方面。这是一个积极的评价，表示某人已经成为了一个更出色、更吸引人的自己。
