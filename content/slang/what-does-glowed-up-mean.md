## Glowed Up 🌟✨
### English
**Meaning:** When someone has gone through a transformation, either in appearance or personality, and has become more attractive, confident, or awesome. 
**Usage Example:** "Since she started exercising and eating healthier, she really glowed up!"
### Chinese
**意思:** 当有人经历了一次转变，无论是外貌还是性格，都变得更加吸引人、自信或棒极了。
**使用示例:** “自从开始锻炼身体，注意饮食之后，她简直就是焕然一新！”
