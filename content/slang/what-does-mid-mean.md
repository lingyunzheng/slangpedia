## 中文解释
**mid**: 在网络用语中，"mid" 有时被用作 "mido" 的缩写，"mido" 是“迷弟”（痴迷的粉丝）的非正式简写。因此，"mid" 通常用来形容对某人或某事物表现出极高热情和忠诚的追随者。
## 英文 Explanation
**mid**: In internet slang, "mid" is sometimes used as a shortened form of "mido," which is a non-formal abbreviation for "迷弟" (loyal fan). Thus, "mid" is often used to describe a follower who shows extreme enthusiasm and loyalty towards someone or something.
