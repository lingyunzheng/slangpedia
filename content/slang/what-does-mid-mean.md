## 中文 (Simplified)
,mid：在中文网络用语中，“mid”有时会用作“妹纸”的省略形式，“妹纸”是“妹妹纸条”的简称，指代女性粉丝或者网友。它通常用来称呼女性或表达对女性的喜爱。
## English
,mid: In Chinese internet slang, "mid" can be used as a short form of "妹纸," which is a shortened version of "妹妹纸条," referring to female fans or netizens. It is typically used to refer to or express fondness towards women.
