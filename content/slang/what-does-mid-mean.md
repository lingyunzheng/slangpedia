## Mid (俚语解释)
### 中文
**中**
1. **混子**: 通常用在网游或者手游中，指那些游戏技术一般，但也能勉强通关，中规中矩的玩家。这些人通常技术一般但不会特别差，能跟大部分队伍配合，但也不会特别突出。亦有“混混”的意思，但用于游戏语境中更多是指游戏中的普通水平玩家。
2. **二把交椅**: 比较不常用，指的是在某些组织、团队中不是绝对的领导者，但在实际操作中起到关键作用的人。在游戏或者其他团队活动中，这个角色通常是第二重要的人物。这个词可以说是一种讽刺或调侃，意指虽然没有正式的领导职责，但实际影响团队运作。
### English
**Mid**
1. **Mid**: Often used in online games or mobile games, it refers to players who have average gaming skills, can pass through games normally, but are not particularly outstanding. These players generally have okay skills, can join most teams to play together, but won't stick out. This term can also mean "villain" or "bad guy", but in the gaming context, it refers to ordinary-level players.
2. **Second-in-command**: Less commonly used, it refers to someone in a group or team who is not the absolute leader but plays a crucial role in practical operations. In games or other group activities, this role is usually the second most important. This term can be a form of irony or ridicule, implying that despite not having formal leadership responsibilities, they significantly influence the team's operation.
