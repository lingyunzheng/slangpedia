## mid (both English and Chinese slang)
### English
**mid**
- **Literal Meaning:** middle, intermediate
- **Slang Usage:** Often used in phrases like "midgame" in gaming contexts or to describe the middle part of something. In casual conversation, it can be used to refer to a mediocre or ordinary situation or person. For example, "This party is just mid; not bad, but not great."
### Chinese
**mid**
- **Literal Meaning:** 中间 (zhōngjiān) - middle, 中间人 (zhōngjiānrén) - referee, mediator
- **Slang Usage:** 可以用来形容普通、一般的状况或人物。比如，“这聚会也就 mid，不咋滴，但也不差。” 可以参照英文中的用法理解。
