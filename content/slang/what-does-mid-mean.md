# Mid (Both English and Chinese)
**English:**  
Mid can be used as an interjection to express disappointment, frustration, or to indicate that something is not great. It's often used when something falls short of expectations or is mediocre.
Example:
"Mid... the movie was okay, but I was expecting more."
**Chinese:**  
"Mid" 可以用作感叹词，表达失望、沮丧或表示某事物一般般、不令人满意。常用于表示某事物未达到预期或只是中等水平。
例子：
“Mid……电影还可以，但没有达到我的期望。”
