## 中文释义
**mid** - 中间人、调解者
在这个语境中，"mid" 被用来指代在双方之间起协调、调解作用的人或角色。比如，在人际交往中，如果你是中间人，就相当于“红娘”或“调停者”的角色，帮助化解矛盾或促成合作。
## 英文 Explanation
**mid** - mediator, intercessor
Here, "mid" is used to refer to someone who acts as a mediator or intercessor between two parties. For example, if you are a "mid" in a social or professional setting, you're essentially playing the role of a "matchmaker" or "conciliator," helping to resolve conflicts or facilitate agreements.
