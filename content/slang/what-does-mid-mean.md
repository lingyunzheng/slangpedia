## Mid
**English:** 
- **Definition:** A casual way to describe something that is moderately good or average.
- **Example Usage:** "This new app is mid, it's not the most exciting thing out there but it gets the job done."
**中文：**
- **定义：** 一种非正式的表达方式，用来描述某物只是普通水准，不算优秀，但也足以胜任。
- **例句：** “这个新应用就一般般啦，谈不上最棒，但也能用。”
