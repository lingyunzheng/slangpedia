## Mid (中文：mid)
### English Explanation:
**Mid:** This slang term is often used in online gaming contexts, particularly in first-person shooter (FPS) games, to describe a player's position or strategy that is neither aggressively pushing forward (front or frontside) nor defending the backline (back or backside). A mid player or position is generally centered, taking on both offensive and defensive roles.
### 中文解释:
**Mid:** 这个俚语词通常用于在线游戏的语境中，尤其是在第一人称射击游戏（FPS游戏）里，用来描述玩家的位置或策略既不是积极进攻（前排或前侧），也不是固守防守（后排或后侧）。Mid位置的玩家或位置通常是居中的，既要承担进攻的角色，也要承担防守的角色。
