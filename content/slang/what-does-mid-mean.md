## Mid (Chinese: 中)
### English
- **Meaning**: Short for "middle," it can refer to something average or mediocre. It's often used to describe a person's skill level, indicating that they are not particularly good but also not bad.
- **Example**: "John is mid when it comes to basketball." This means John has average basketball skills, neither exceptionally good nor bad.
### 中 (Chinese)
- **Meaning**: 在中文中，“中”通常用来表示某事或某人是普通或一般的。它可以用来描述一个人的技能水平，意味着他们既不特别好也不特别差，处于平均水平。
- **例句**： "约翰的篮球技能中等。" 这意味着约翰的篮球技能处于普通水平，既不是特别好也不是特别差。
