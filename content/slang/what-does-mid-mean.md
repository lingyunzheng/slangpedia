## 中文释义
**mid** - 中间的人；中间的
### 例句
- 我们队伍总共有五个人，他正好是**mid**。  
(His position in the team is the middle one, there are five people in total.)
## 英文 Explanations
**mid** - the person in the middle; middle
### Example Sentences
- We are a team of five, and he is the **mid**.  
(He is the one who occupies the middle position in the team of five.)
