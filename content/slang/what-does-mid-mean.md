## mid
**English:** 
- Short for "middle" or "middle ground, " it can refer to a situation of compromise or a person who is in the middle of a hierarchy or group.
**Chinese:**
- 简写“中间”或“中间立场”，可以指一种妥协的情况，也可以指在层级或群体中处于中间位置的人。
