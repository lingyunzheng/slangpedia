# Mid (English)
- **Definition**: Abbreviation of "middle" or "mid-level"
- **Usage**: Often used to describe someone or something that is average or mediocre.
# 中 (Chinese)
- **定义**: 缩写自“中间”或“中级”
- **用法**: 常用来形容某人或某事是普通的或一般的。
