# Slang Explanation: Mid
## English
**Definition:** "Mid" in slang can be used to describe a person or a situation that is average or mediocre, lacking in commitment or effort.
**Example Usage:**
- "I thought he would step up, but he's just mid about everything."
- "Her performance at the meeting was mid, there's nothing to impress anyone."
