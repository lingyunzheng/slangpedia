# Slang Explanation: "Mid"
## English
**Meaning:** In casual contexts, especially in online gaming or sports, "mid" can refer to a central or middle location or position, often on a map, field, or screen. It's frequently used to describe a strategic position that is not on the edges.
**Example Use:** "I need to stay in the mid lane to maintain视野 (vision) and control the lane against the enemy."
## 中文
**含义:** 在非正式语境中，特别是在在线游戏或体育运动中，“mid”可以指代中间或中心的位置，通常是地图、场地或屏幕上的中间位置。它经常用来描述一个非边缘的战略位置。
**例句:** "我需要待在中间路线来保持视野并控制与敌人的车道。"
