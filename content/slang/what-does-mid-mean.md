## 中文解释: mid
**意思:** 在比赛中处于中间位置的选手或队员。
**情境:** 通常用于团队比赛，指的是表现中等、既不是最突出也不是垫底的选手。
## English Explanation: mid
**Meaning:** A player or team member who is placed in the middle of the competition.
**Context:** Often used in team sports to describe a performer who is average, neither the standout nor the last in the lineup.
