# Slang Explanation: "mid"
## English
**Meaning:** To be unpredictable, flashy, or out of control in a situation, often because someone is trying too hard to stand out.
**Example:**
- "When he started miding the whole game, everyone just lost interest."
## 中文
**意思:** 在某个情景下变得难以预测、炫酷或失去控制，通常是由于试图过于引人注意。
**例句:**
- "当他整个游戏过程中都试图mid时，大家都失去了兴趣。"
