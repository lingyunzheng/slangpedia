## Mid (俚语解释)
### 中文
“中”一般用于描述某件事或者状态处于中间位置，或者某个人在某个情境中的表现一般般、不突出。有时也会用来形容人或事平平无奇。
### 英文
"Mid" is often used to describe something or a state being in a middle position, or someone's performance in a situation being average and not outstanding. It can also be used to describe a person or thing as boring or unremarkable.
