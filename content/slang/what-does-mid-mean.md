## 中文 (Chinese) - 中文解释 (Simplified)
**mid**: 在网络用语中，"mid" 主要用来指代“中途”或“中间”，但更多用于描述某种状态或行为发生在过程中的某个阶段。例如，“中途弃坑”就是指在尝试或者参与某事中途选择放弃。
### 英文 (English)
**mid**: In internet slang, "mid" is often used to refer to "middle" or "中途" in Chinese, but it is more commonly used to describe a state or action that occurs at a stage within a process. For example, "中途弃坑" can be translated as "mid-abandon" or "quitting in the middle," referring to someone leaving a project or attempt midway through.
