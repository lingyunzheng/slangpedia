## mid (mid)
### Chinese
**中间人 (Zhōngjiānrén)**
- **Explanation**: A mediator or go-between, often used in informal contexts to describe someone who is managing or facilitating a conversation or deal between two parties.
- **Example**: 在那个交易中，他扮演了中间人的角色，成功促成了双方合作。（In that deal, he played the role of a middleman, successfully facilitating the cooperation between both parties.）
### English
**Mediator / Intermediary / Go-between**
- **Explanation**: A person or thing that acts as a link between two or more other people or things; especially someone who is someone who induces a situation in which otherwise opposing parties can talk to or act towards each other.
- **Example**: She acted as a mediator in the family dispute, helping to find a common ground for all involved. (She acted as a mediator in the family dispute, helping to find a common ground for all involved.)
