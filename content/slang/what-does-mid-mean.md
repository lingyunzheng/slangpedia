```markdown
## mid
**English:**  
- *To be in the middle or middle-ground*. Often used to describe someone or something that's average or not extreme.
- *Between two parties or situations*. Common in contexts where a person or decision is trying to mediate or is a compromise.
**Chinese:**  
- *处于中间状态*：常常用来描述某人或某事处于一般水平，没有明显的特色或极端表现。
- *在两方之间*：常用在调解人或折中方案的场景中，表示某人或某决定站在中间立场。
```
```markdown
## mid
**English:**  
- *A casual reference to an average or typical person or thing*. Used informally, often with a hint of neutrality or mild indifference.
- *In the middle or halfway point*. Can also refer to an average value or standard.
**Chinese:**  
- *对普通人的轻松称呼*：通常用于非正式场合，带有一些中立或温和的冷漠意味。
- *中间或中途*：还可以指平均值或标准中间点。
```
