```markdown
## 中文 (Chinese)
**mid** - 中间人 / 谈判者 （中介）
用来指在两个或更多人之间传递信息、调解矛盾或促成协议的人。类似于“中间人”或“调解者”的概念。
例子：
1. 他们在这家公司的收购中起着 **mid** 的作用。
2. 他是那个项目成功的 **mid**。
## English
**mid** - Mediator / Intermediary
Used to describe someone who acts as a go-between, transferring information, resolving conflicts, or facilitating agreements between two or more parties. This term is akin to "mediator" or "intermediary."
Examples:
1. They played the role of **mid** in the company's acquisition.
2. He was the **mid** for the project's success.
```
