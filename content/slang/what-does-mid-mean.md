# "Mid" in Slang (Bilingual Explanation)
## English:
**Meaning:** Short for "middle" or "mid-level," often used to describe someone or something that is average, not exceptional, or in the middle of a range.
**Example:** "He's a mid-tier player in the league."
---
## 中文:
**意思:** "middle" 或 "中级" 的简称，通常用来形容人或事物平平无奇、一般般或处于某个范围的中间位置。
**例句:** "他是联赛中的中流砥柱球员。"
