# Brokie (Bro + Greek)
## English Explanation:
"Brokie" is a slang term often used humorously or derisively to describe someone who is overly confident or boastful, often in a way that is seen as exaggerated or insincere. It's a combination of "bro" (a informal term for a male friend) and "Greek" (used here to imply something excessive or pretentious). Essentially, if someone keeps boasting about how great they are or how many hot girls like them without having any real evidence to back it up, they might be called a brokie.
## Chinese Explanation:
"Brokie" 是一个网络流行语，通常用来形容那些过分自信或吹牛的人，语气上带有戏谑或讽刺的意味。这个词是“Bro”（一种对男性朋友的称呼）和“Greek”（这里用来形容某种过度或卖弄的行为）的组合。如果有人一直夸耀自己多么厉害或者有多少女生喜欢他们，却没有实际证据支持这些说法，那么这些人可能会被称为“Brokie”。
