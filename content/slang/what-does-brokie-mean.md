# Brokie
## English Explanation
"Brookie" is a play on the words "bro" (short for brother, a casual term of endearment or camaraderie) and "cookie." In this context, it's often used to describe someone who is "too good to be true" or seems overly friendly or gullible. It's similar to saying someone is "too sweet" or "naive" in a teasing way.
## Chinese Explanation
"Brokie" 是 "bro"（兄、兄弟，一种非正式的亲密称呼）和 "cookie"（饼干）的结合词。通常用来形容某人显得“太棒了以至于不真实”或者过于友善，容易上当。类似于说某人“太甜了”或者“太天真”时的调侃语气。
