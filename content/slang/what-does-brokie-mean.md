# Brokie (English)

**Definition:**  
A term often used to describe someone who is perceived as privileged, expects constant support or validation from others, or behaves in an entitled manner. It's a combination of "brother" and "princess," suggesting a mix of male privilege and excessive pampering.

**Example Usage:**  
"Man, that new intern is such a brokie. He expects us to do all his work and he's always complaining about the office environment."
