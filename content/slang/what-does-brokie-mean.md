## Brokie (Bro + Niece)
**English:**  
A term often used in casual or informal settings, "brokie" is a slang term that blends "bro" (a common casual term for a male friend) with "niece." It's used to describe a female who is perceived as overly or inappropriately friendly or flirtatious, especially in a workplace or between friends. This term is meant to be playful, but it can also carry a hint of disapproval or amusement.
**Chinese:**  
在非正式或休闲的场合中，“brokie”这个词是“bro”（一种常见的用来自男性朋友的称呼）和“niece”（外甥女）的结合体。它用来形容一个被感知为过于或者不恰当地亲切或挑逗的女性，尤其是在工作场所或朋友之间。这个词通常是玩闹的，但也可能带有轻微的不批准或玩味的意思。
