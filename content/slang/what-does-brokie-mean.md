# Brokie (Slang)
## English
**Definition:** A term often used to describe someone who behaves in an overly informed or overly aggressive manner, especially in tech or internet culture. Often associated with someone who makes a big deal out of minor issues, or who tries to outsmart others with their knowledge.
## 中文
**定义：** 一个用来形容行为过于精明或过于激进的人，特别是在科技或网络文化中。通常指那些对小事大惊小怪或试图用他们的知识出谋划策的人。
