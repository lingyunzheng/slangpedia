# Brokie (Bro + Fraud)
## English Explanation
"Brokie" is a slang term used primarily in online communities and social media, particularly among Millennials and Gen-Z. It's a combination of "bro" (an informal term of address for a man, often used to denote a friend or comrade) and "fraud," suggesting someone who is dishonest or unreliable. This term is often used to describe a person who makes false claims or promises, or does not hold up their end of a deal.
## Chinese Explanation
"Brokie" 是一个网络流行语，主要用于在线社区和社交媒体中，尤其是千禧一代和Z世代之间。这个词是由 "bro"（一个用来称呼男性朋友或同伴的非正式词汇）和 "fraud"（骗子）组合而成，用来描述那些撒谎或不可靠的人。经常用于形容那些做出虚假声明或承诺，或者没有履行协议的人。
