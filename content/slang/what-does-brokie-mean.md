## Brokie (Slang)
### English
- **Definition**: Typically used to describe a behavior or attitude that is seen as uncool, insincere, or inauthentic, often in a mocking or derogatory sense. For example, someone who is overly confident or boastful, or someone who pretends to be something they're not.
### Chinese
- **定义**：通常用来形容行为或态度不酷、不真诚或不真实，常常带有嘲笑或贬低的意味。例如，一个过于自信或者夸大的人，或者一个假装成为他不是的那种人。
