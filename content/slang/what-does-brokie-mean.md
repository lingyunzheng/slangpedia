# Brokie (知名品牌)
## English
**Brokie**: Slang for "big brand," often used to refer to well-known or popular brands that are highly recognizable. It can also imply that something is trendy or fashionable due to its association with a famous brand.
Example: "I just bought a new phone, it's a brokie one, very modern and sleek!"
## Chinese (简体)
**Brokie**：用来指代那些知名度高、广为人知的品牌，是“品牌”的一种俏皮叫法。也可以用来形容那些因为知名品牌的关联而显得时髦或流行的物品。
示例：刚买了一部新手机，是个 big brand 的，非常现代又精致！
