# Brokie (English)
## Meaning
- **Definition**: An often self-aggrandizing man, especially one who seeks praise or attention via social media and online platforms. Brokies compare themselves excessively to others and often use manipulative or dishonest tactics to portray themselves in a favorable light.
## Usage Example
- "Avoid being a brokie on social media; just post about what you genuinely enjoy."
# 哥米尔 (Pīnyīn: Gē Màir)
## 含义
- **定义**：一位常常在生活中寻求赞美和关注的男子，尤其是在社交媒体和在线平台上。哥米尔们常常自吹自擂，过分地拿自己与他人比较，并且经常使用欺骗性的或操纵性的手段来塑造自己在一个光鲜亮丽的形象。
## 使用示例
- “在社交媒体上尽量避免成为哥米尔，只发布一些你真正喜欢的内容。”
