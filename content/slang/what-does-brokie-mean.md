# Brokie (Bro + Chickie)
## English:
A term combining "bro" (short for brother) and "chickie" (a playful or casual term for a female). It's often used to refer to any person, usually male, but sometimes female, who displays a mix of stereotypical masculine and feminine behaviors or looks. The term is more commonly used in a teasing or derogatory manner, suggesting that the person is trying too hard to fit into gender stereotypes or is seen as effeminate by others.
Example:
- "Man, that new guy at the gym is such a brokie with his bright pink sneakers and long earrings."
## 中文解释：
“brokie”是“bro”（兄弟）和“chickie”（用来亲切称呼女性的一种俚语）的结合词。这个词通常用来形容那些表现上兼具性别刻板印象中的男性和女性特征的人，无论是男性还是女性。这个词多用于带戏谑或贬义的角度，暗示这个人试图过份迎合性别刻板印象，或者被认为在性别表现上过于女性化。
例句：
- “天哪，健身房里那个新来的人太逗了，穿著五颜六色的跑步鞋，还戴著长耳环。”
