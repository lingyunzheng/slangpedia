## Brokie (俚语解释)
### 中文
“坤哥”是Chinglish中的一个非常流行的称呼，通常用来调侃社交平台或游戏直播中的女性主播。它是由英文单词“Bro”（表示男性朋友）和“Kong”（意为“哥”，通常带有尊敬意味）结合而成。这个称呼常常带有一种轻松、调侃的语气，不带恶意，是一种友好的称谓方式。
### English
"Brokie" is a Chinglish term that has become very popular, often used to mock female streamers on social platforms or in gaming live streams. It is created by combining the English word "Bro" (meaning male friend) with the Mandarin Chinese word "Kong" (which means "big brother", usually with a respectful connotation). This term often carries a light, teasing tone but without any malice and is generally used in a friendly way.
