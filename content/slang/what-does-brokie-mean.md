## Brokie (Slang)
- **English Definition:** A person who is overly enthusiastic or friendly, often to the point of being annoying or sycophantic.
- **Chinese Explanation:** “Brokie”指的是那种过分热情或者友好的人，这种热情或友好到了让人感到烦躁或献媚的地步。通常用来形容追求过度、讨好型的性格。
