# Brokie (英语: Brokie)
## English Explanation:
A slang term often used to describe a young, self-centered male who is often obsessed with superficial things like appearance, tech gadgets, and may be seen as uppity or arrogant. It’s a mixture of "bro" and "tech" or sometimes just a playful insult pointing out someone's excessive interest in tech and fashion at the expense of substance.
Example Usage: "That brokie over there doesn't know how to have a real conversation; he's only interested in the latest phone specs."
## 中文解释:
一个俚语，通常用来形容那些自私自利、关注肤浅事物（如外表、科技产品）的年轻人。这个词汇结合了“bro”（兄弟）和“tech”（科技），有时也只是一个戏谑的侮辱，指的是某人的兴趣仅限于科技和时尚，而忽视了实质性内容。
使用示例：“那边那个brokie根本不会真正交流，他只会谈论最新的手机规格。”
