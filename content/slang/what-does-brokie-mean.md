# Brokie (Bro + Rake)
## English Explanation:
A term derived from combining "bro" (bros) and "rake," often used to describe someone who is a glass-half-empty type of friend or acquaintance. This person tends to always see the negative side of things and might make you feel like you're dragging them down with your problems. They can be a bit of a burden, given their pessimistic outlook on life and their knack for finding the worst in any situation.
## Chinese Explanation:
一个术语，来源于“bro”（兄弟）和“rake”（始作俑者）的组合，用来形容那种总是看到事情消极面的哥们儿或朋友。他们的玻璃杯永远是半空的，可能会让你觉得在他们身边似乎总是有负担。他们总是能从最坏的角度看待问题，并且擅长在任何情况下找到最糟糕的地方。
---
### Example Usage:
- In English: "You can't get a good conversation out of Brokie; he's always seeing the worst in everything."
- 在中文：和Brokie聊不到什么好话题，因为他总是看到事情最消极的一面。
