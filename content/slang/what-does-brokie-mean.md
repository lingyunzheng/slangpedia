# Brokie (普通话：屌丝)
## English Explanation:
A term often used for someone who is perceived as socially or financially disadvantaged, often seen as "low-interest" or "unremarkable" by others. It is a colloquial and somewhat derogatory term, akin to "square" or "nerd" in English. 
## Chinese Explanation:
一个常用来形容社会或经济地位相对较低，被认为“低司令”或“不显眼”的人的常用词汇。这是一个口语和略带贬义的词汇，类似于英语中的“square”或“nerd”。
