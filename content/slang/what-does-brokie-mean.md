# Brookie (英文：Brookie)
## English Meaning
The term "brookie" is primarily used in internet slang, particularly among younger generations. It is a portmanteau of "bro" (short for "brother," often used as a casual, informal term) and "cookie," usually referring to an attractive or appealing woman. Sometimes, it can also refer to men being particularly attractive or appealing.
### Example Usage
- "That brookie over there is flavoring the joint with her presence!"

# 靠包 （英文：Brookie）
## 中文解释
“靠包”一词主要来源于互联网 slang，尤其是在年轻人之间非常流行。这个词是“靠哥”（即“哥哥”，常被用作一种随意的称呼）和“饼干”（通常指迷人的女性）的合璧，主要用于形容一个特别吸引人或讨人喜欢的女性。有时候，这个词也可以用来形容男性非常有吸引力或讨人喜欢。
### 举例使用
- “那个靠包在那里，简直让整个地方都增色不少！”

Noting that "brookie" is an internet slang term, the explanations provided aim to cover the typical usage in an English-speaking context. The translation to "靠包" (kào bāo) is intended to mirror the casual and informal nature of the term.
