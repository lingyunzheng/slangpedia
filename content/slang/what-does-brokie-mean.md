# Brokie
**English:** 
"Broke" is a common slang term used to describe someone who does not have money or is financially strained. When this term is modified with a specific suffix or context, it can take on a unique meaning that varies based on the audience and situation.
Example: "He's a real brokie." 
**Chinese (Simplified):** 
“Brokie” 是一个常用的俚语，用来描述那些没有钱或者财务状况紧张的人。当这个词汇加入特定的后缀或语境时，它可以表达出更多具体的意思，这取决于听众和情境。
例子：他是真正的“Brokie”。
