# Brokie (Slang)
## English
**Brokie**: This term is a blend of "bro" (short for brother, often used informally to refer to male friends or as a general term for men) and "hick" (someone from a small town or rural area). It is used to describe someone who is perceived as uncool, unsophisticated, or lacking in urban or modern understanding. It can also imply someone who is naive or from a small, unhip community.
Example: "I can't stand this brokie talking about how great small-town life is; he has no idea what he's missing."
## 中文
**Brokie**：这个俚语是由“bro”（兄弟，通常用于指男性朋友或泛指男子）和“hick”（指来自小镇或农村地区的人）混合而成的一个词。用来形容那些被认为是不酷、不时尚或者缺乏城市或现代认知的人。这个词汇也可以暗示这个人缺乏见识或来自一个小众且不流行的社区。
例句：“我受不了这个brokie老是说小镇生活多好，他根本不知道他错过了什么。”
