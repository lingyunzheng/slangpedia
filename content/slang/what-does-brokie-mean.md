# Brokie (Bro + Rake)
## English Explanation
A term often used in online and social media contexts, "brokie" is a combination of "bro" (short for brother) and "rake" (an old slang term for a rake-his-harvest kind of scumbag). The term is generally used to describe a man who is seen as emotionally underdeveloped, immature, or who exhibits various forms of toxic masculinity. Often, the term is used to highlight behaviors like manipulation, selfishness, or a lack of empathy among men.
## Chinese Explanation
“Brokie” 是一个网络用语和社交媒体中常用的词，由“bro”（英语中的兄弟）和“rake”（一种老式的恶棍、收割者的俚语）组合而成。这个词通常用来形容一个情感上发展不成熟或表现出各种有毒 masculinity 的男性。这个术语经常用来强调男性中的操纵、自私或缺乏同理心等行为。
