## Brokie
- **English**: A term used to describe someone who heavily clings to or emulates internet culture, often displaying excessive behavior or opinions that are popular in online communities. It can also refer to someone who is very savvy about or frequently engages in internet activities, but can sometimes be seen as pretentious or not genuine in their enthusiasm.
- **Chinese**: 一个用来形容那些极度融入或模仿网络文化的人，经常表现出过度的行为或观点，这些观点在在线社群中很流行。这个词语也可以用来描述那些非常精通或频繁参与网络活动的人，但有时会被认为是夸张或不真诚地表现出热情。
