## Brokie
**English:**  
A term often used on the internet, particularly in social media and forums, to describe someone who is overly eager to adopt and display the latest fads, trends, or slang. Brokie individuals are typically characterized by their frequent references to pop culture, social media trends, and often come across as slightly awkward or inauthentic as they try to stay relevant.
**Chinese:**  
通常在网络、社交媒体和论坛上使用的俚语，用来描述那些过度急于采用并展示最新潮流、流行词或趋势的人。Brokie 通常会频繁引用流行文化、社交媒体趋势，有时会显得有些尴尬或不真实，因为他们试图保持相关性。
