## Brokie (English)
### Explanation
"Brokie" is a slang term used to describe someone who acts inauthentically or overly confident, often in a way that is perceived as pretentious or trying too hard. It can also describe behavior that is perceived as crossing the line from being engaging to being obnoxious. The term blends "bro" (short for brother, often used humorously or derogatorily to refer to a male) and "hick," suggesting a self-important or exaggerated country boy.
### Example
- *Using in a sentence:* "Stop being such a brokie at the party, no one likes you when you act like you're the center of the world."
## 哥吉 (Chinese)
### 解释
“哥吉”是一个网络俚语，用来形容那些行为不真实或过度自信的人，往往被看作是炫耀或太过自以为是。这个词结合了“哥”（对男性的一种戏谑或贬义称呼，类似于bro）和“乡巴佬”（hick），暗示一个人自视甚高或夸张的表现。 
### 例句
- *使用例句:* "别在派对上那么做作，这种时候你别当自己是世界的中心，大家都不喜欢像哥吉一样的人。"
