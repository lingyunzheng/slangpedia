# Brokie (俚语解释)
## 中文解释
"Brokie" 是从英文 "broke"（破产的）和 "fickin'"（一个俚语加强语气词）结合而成的一个网络用语，中文翻译可以是“破产同人”。这个词常用来形容那些看似阔气实则背后负债累累的人，或者是那些经常花光自己钱、甚至向别人借钱的人。在社交媒体上，这样的讽刺或挖苦表达尤为常见。
## 英文 Explanation
"Brokie" is a combination of the English words "broke" (which means financially ruined or bankrupt) and "fickin'" (a slang intensifier). It's often used to describe someone who might seem rich or successful on the surface but is actually deeply in debt. This term can be found in social media contexts, where it is used as a form of teasing or criticism.
