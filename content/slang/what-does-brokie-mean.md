## Brokie (英语: Brokie)
**English Explanation:**
- **Definition:** An informal term used primarily online to describe someone who has a particular set of behaviors or traits, often considered to be annoying or overbearing.
- **Characteristics:**
- Frequently interrupts others in conversations or meetings.
- Tends to talk excessively or boast about their achievements.
- May expect others to agree with them or follow their lead.
- **Context:** Commonly used in online forums, social media, or casual conversations among peers.
**中文解释:**
- **定义:** 在网络上常用来形容表现出特定行为或特质的人，这些特质往往被认为是烦人或者过分的。
- **特征:**
- 经常打断别人在对话或会议中的发言。
- 趋于过度谈论自己或吹嘘自己的成就。
- 可能要求别人同意他们的观点或遵循他们的领导。
- **语境:** 常见于在线论坛、社交媒体或同龄人之间的非正式对话中。
