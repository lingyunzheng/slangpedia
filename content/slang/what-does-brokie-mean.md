## Brokie
**English:** A term used to describe someone who engages in or promotes behavior that is generally considered indecent, inappropriate, or overly sexual. It's often used in a disapproving or sarcastic context.
**中文:** 用于形容那些表现出或者促进不恰当、过于性感或淫秽行为的人。这个词通常用于表示不赞成或讽刺的语境中。
---
Example Usage:
- **English:** "Brokie ran around the party flashing his underwear on purpose."
- **中文:** "Brokie 故意在聚会上展示他的内衣，行为确实让人觉得不雅。"
