# Brokie (俚语解释)
## 中文
**水军、键盘侠**
指在网络上从事发帖、灌水或对特定话题进行情绪化的评论以影响舆论的群体。类似于“键盘侠”，通常带有强烈的个人偏见或情绪，通过互联网影响公众舆论或攻击特定对象。
## English
**CyberMob, Internet Troll**
Refers to a group of people who post online, flood chat rooms, or emotionally react to specific topics in an attempt to influence public opinion. Similar to "keyboard warriors," they often have strong personal biases or emotions, using the internet to sway public perception or attack specific targets.
