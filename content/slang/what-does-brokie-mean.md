```markdown
## Brokie (US English)
**B rogue** (play on words with "bro") \ˈbrō-kē\ (noun)
- *Definition*: A term often used to describe a male who is very interested in fashion and grooming, possibly to the point of being seen as overly or inappropriately feminine. This term can carry a mix of teasing and admiration, often used among friends but can be derogatory in some contexts.
- *Example*: "That bro just stepped in with a Gucci suit; he’s such a brokie!"
## 弟riage (Chinese 芭比哥)
**B巴比哥 (bā bī gē)** (noun)
- *Definition*: 是指对时尚和外表有非常讲究的男性，有时可能被认为有些过于或不合适地女性化。这个词通常用于朋友之间，但有时也可能带有贬义。
- *例句*: “那个弟弟真是时尚达人，一身Gucci西装，真像个芭比哥！”
```
Note: "Brokie" and "弟riage" (巴比哥) are informal and region-specific terms, often used with a tone of familiarity and can be taken in multiple ways depending on the context and relationship between the speaker and the addressee.
