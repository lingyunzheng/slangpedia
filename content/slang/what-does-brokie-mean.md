## Brokie (English)
**Definition:** A term used to describe someone who dresses excessively in a preppy or fashion-forward style, often with a noticeable lack of coordination or sense of style.
**Example:** "That brokie over there is wearing a plaid shirt with a bright floral sweater – it's a fashion clash."
## Brokie (Chinese)
**定义：** 用来形容那些穿着过度追求时髦或英伦风，但往往缺乏搭配感和风格感的人。
**例句：** "那边的那个人穿着格子衫搭配鲜艳的花朵毛衣——简直就是时尚灾难。"
