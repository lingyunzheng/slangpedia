# Brokie (Bro + ukie)
## English Explanation
"Brokie" is a playful fusion of two terms: "bro," a common slang abbreviation for "bros" or "brothers," often used to refer to male friends or in a broader sense to indicate a casual, cool vibe; and "ukie" (sometimes "ukiee") which is derived from "UK" (United Kingdom) and is used to affectionately refer to someone or something from or associated with the UK. So, "brokie" can be used to describe a British man or a British male friend who embodies a laid-back, stylish, and cool demeanor. It often carries a tone of admiration or camaraderie.
## Simplified English
A cool, British guy, often used in a friendly way.
## Chinese Explanation
"Brokie" 是将“bro”（兄弟或朋友的一种非正式称呼）和“ukie”（来自英国的一种亲昵称呼）结合而成的词语。它用来形容一个充满随性、时尚且酷炫气质的英国男性朋友或与英国相关的事物。通常带有钦佩或亲密的语气。
## 简化中文
一个酷炫的英国小伙子，通常用在友好场合。
