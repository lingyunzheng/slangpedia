# Brokie (中文: 骗氪)
**Definition:**
- **English:** A term often used in the gaming community, particularly on platforms like Twitch or YouTube, where a viewer pays for in-game boosts or boosts to content creators, but it's suspected they might not provide as much as they claim. It's a blend of 'bro' (bros, friends) and 'kring' (from氪, which sounds similar to 'cripple' and means '氪金' in Chinese, referring to in-game spending).
- **中文:** 主要用来指在网络游戏直播平台（如Twitch或YouTube）上，观众花钱购买游戏内的增强道具，但有怀疑他们可能并没有按照承诺提供实际的服务。这个词是一个复合词，“Bro”(兄弟、朋友)和“氪金”（kòkjīn, 在游戏中花费金钱）结合在一起，用来调侃那些可能只是“骗氪”而不是真正花钱支持的内容创作者。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "That 'brokie' just bought a 500 bonus hour package but honestly, I doubt he even streamed 10 hours of real content."
- 中文: "那个‘骗氪'刚刚买了一个500小时包，但说实话，我怀疑他真的只播了10个小时的真实内容。"
**Context:**
- English: This term is commonly used among gamers, streamers, and viewers on platforms where streaming and content creation are monetized. It highlights skepticism and批评 towards perceived insincere supports from viewers.
- 中文: 这个词在游戏社区、直播平台以及内容创作者的观众中非常流行。它反映了人们对疑似隐蔽或虚假支持的怀疑和批评。
