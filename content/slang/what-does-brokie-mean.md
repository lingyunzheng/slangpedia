# Brokie
## English
**Brokie:** A portmanteau of "brother" and "bitch," this term is used to denigrate a male who is perceived as overly dramatic, whiny, or insincere. It is often used dismissively in informal settings, especially among youths. 
*Example:* "He's being so brokie about the situation. It's like he can't handle a little criticism."
## Chinese (简体中文)
**Brokie（brokie）:** 这个词是"哥哥"（哥哥，表示亲昵或友好的兄弟）和" bitch"（贬义，表示讨厌的女性）的混成词。用来贬低被认为过于戏剧化、抱怨或不真诚的男性。通常在非正式场合中使用，特别是在年轻人之间。
*示例:* "他对情况的态度好 brokie，就好像他不能接受一点批评似的。"
