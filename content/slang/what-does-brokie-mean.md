# Brokie (英语: Brookie)
## English Explanation
"Brookie" is a playful, modern variation of "bro" (bros), often used to describe someone who is sweet, harmless, and possibly a bit awkward. It's commonly used among younger generations to refer to a friend in a non-threatening, affectionate way. Imagine if your older brother tried to be cool but just ends up being endearing in a goofy way. That’s a "brookie."
## 中文解释
“Brookie”是“bro”（兄弟）一词的现代变体，常用来形容某个甜、无害且可能有点笨拙的人。在年轻人群体中，通常用来称呼朋友的一种非威胁性、亲密的方式。想象一下，你的大兄弟努力让自己看起来很酷，但结果只是以一种可笑的方式显得很可爱。这就是一个“brookie”。
