# "Sus" in English & Chinese
## English
### Meaning
"Sus" is a term that originated on internet forums to express doubt or suspicion about someone or something. It is often used to question the credibility or reliability of an action, statement, or person.
### Example
"I heard his story, but it just feels a little sus..."
## 中文
### 含义
“sus”是在网络论坛中出现的一个术语，用来表达对某人或某事的怀疑或怀疑其可信度或可靠性。通常用于质疑某人的言行或信息的真实性。
### 示例
“我听说他的故事，但是感觉有点sus……”
