## sus (Suspect / 疑惑)
### English Slang Explanation:
**sus** is a modern slang term often used to express doubt or suspicion about someone or something. It originated from Reddit users who used it as an abbreviation for "suspect," but it has since become a meme in popular culture. People might say "that's sus" when they feel something is suspicious or strange, but not necessarily harmful.
### Chinese Slang Explanation:
**疑惑 (yí huò)** 或 **可疑 (kě gāi)**  
这个术语“sus”最初是从 Reddit 用户口中出来的缩写，表示对某个人或某件事情的怀疑。它后来成为流行文化中的一个梗。当人们觉得某事可疑或奇怪时，但不一定是危险的，可能会这么说“这 sus”。
