## Slang Term: sus
### English
**Definition:** 
- Sus is often used on social media platforms, particularly on Reddit and Discord, to indicate that something or someone appears suspicious or questionable. It can be used to express doubt or uncertainty about the veracity of a post, comment, or behavior.
**Usage Example:** 
- "There's something sus about that username; I bet it's a bot."
### Chinese
**释义:**
- "sus" 在网络用语中通常被用来表示某事或某人看起来可疑或有问题。它常用来对帖子、评论或行为的真实性表示怀疑或不确定。
**使用示例:**
- "那个用户名看起来有点sus，我猜它可能是个机器人。"
