## English: "Sus"
### Definition
A term used to describe someone or something that is suspected of being dishonest, untrustworthy, or guilty of wrongdoing. It's often used in online environments to question the authenticity or reliability of a situation or person.
### Example Usage
- "I don't trust that guy, he's super sus."
- "That review is sus, it seems too good to be true."
## 中文: “sus”
### 解释
用来形容某人或某事被怀疑不诚实、不可信或有罪。它经常在网络环境中用来质疑情况或人的真实性或可靠性。
### 使用示例
- "我不信任那个家伙，他太sus了。"
- "那个评价是sus的，感觉太好了，不可能是真的。"
