## sus (adj.)

### English
**Meaning:** Sus is an internet slang abbreviation for "suspicious". It is commonly used to question the integrity, authenticity, or trustworthiness of someone or something.

**Example Usage:**
- "I'm feeling sus about this deal, let's do some background checks."
- "That social media profile looks sus, it may be a fake account."

### 中文
**含义:** "sus" 是一种网络俚语，来源于 "suspicious"，用来质疑某人的可信度、真实性或行为的可疑性。

**例句：**
- "我对这个交易感到有些疑虑，最好做些背景调查。"
- "那一个社交媒体账号看起来很可疑，可能是假账号。"
