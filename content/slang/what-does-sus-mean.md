## Suspicious (Sus)
**English:** 
"Sus" is a term often used in online communities, particularly in subreddits and Discord servers, to suggest that something or someone is suspicious, questionable, or potentially devious. It's a quick, modern way to express doubt or skepticism.
**中文:** 
"Sus" 是网络社区中常用的一个俚语，尤其是在 Reddit 和 Discord 服务器中，用来表示某事或某人可疑、存疑或可能带有不良动机。这是一个快速、现代的方式来表达质疑或怀疑。
