### "Sus" (Sus)
#### English
**Definition:** "Sus" is an internet slang term derived from "suspicious," used to express doubt or suspicion about the credibility or honesty of someone or something.
**Example Usage:**  
- "I feel sus about that guy—he offers $100 for a single coffee."
#### 汉语
**释义：** “Sus” 是网络用语，来源于“可疑”，用于表达对方的可信度或诚实性让人感到怀疑。
**例句：**  
- “我对那个人有疑虑——他说可以100块一份咖啡。”
