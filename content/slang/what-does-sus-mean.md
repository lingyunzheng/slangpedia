### Slang Term: "sus"
**English:** 
"Sus" is an acronym for "suspect" and is commonly used to express doubt or suspicion about something or someone. It often carries a negative connotation and is used casually in online or informal settings to indicate that something seems questionable or suspicious.
**Example Usage:**
- In a video game context, if a player suddenly makes a move that doesn't align with their usual playstyle, teammates might comment "That's so sus!"
---
**汉语简释:**
"Sus" 是 "suspect" 的缩写，常用来表达对某事或某人的怀疑。它通常带有负面含义，在网络或非正式场合中使用，用来表示某件事或某人的行径显得可疑或可疑。
**例句:**
- 在视频游戏中，如果一个玩家突然做出与其平时游戏方式不一致的动作，队友可能会评论说 "That's so sus！" （这事儿可疑！）
