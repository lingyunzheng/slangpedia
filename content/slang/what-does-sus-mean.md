# Slang Term: "Sus"
**English Explanation:**
"Sus" is a modern slang abbreviation of "suspicious." It's commonly used to express doubt or suspicion about someone's actions, intentions, or claims. If you think someone is acting weird or isn't being as transparent as they should, you might say, "That's sus."
**Chinese Explanation:**
"Sus" 是现代网络流行语，来源于 "suspicious"。它常用于表达对某人行为、意图或陈述的怀疑。如果你觉得有人表现得很奇怪或不够透明，可以这么说：“那挺 sus 的。”
