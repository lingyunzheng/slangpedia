# "Sus" Slang Explanation
## English
**Definition:** Sus can be used as a short form of "suspicious," indicating that someone or something is not quite right or trustworthy.
**Example:** "I saw sus activity on my account. I think someone might be hacking it."
## Chinese (简体中文)
**涵义:** Sus 可以作为"suspicious"的缩写，表示某人或某事似乎不对劲或不可信。
**例句:** "我看到我的账户上有 sus 行为。我觉得可能有人在搞黑客活动。"
