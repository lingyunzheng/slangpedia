## sus (English / Chinese)
**English Definition:** 
- **Slang Term:** Sus
- **Meaning:** Suspect, misleading, or suspicious. Often used to express doubt or suspicion about something or someone.
**Chinese Translation:**
- **中文释义:** Suspect, 可疑的, or 欺骗性的
- **用法说明:** 常用于表达对某人或某事的怀疑或不确定。
**Example Sentences:**
- "I think this person is sus; I don't trust them." (我觉得这个人可疑；我不信任他们。)
- "The salesperson's claims are so sus, we need to do more research." (这个销售人员的说法太可疑了，我们需要做更多的研究。)
