### sus (Suspect)
**English:**  
"Sus" is a slang term derived from the word "suspicious." It's used to express doubt or skepticism about someone or something. It could also denote that the speaker thinks something is fishy or wrong.
Example:  
"I don't know why he's been so quiet lately... he's totally sus."
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
"sus" 是从 "可疑" 这个词演变而来的俚语，用于表达对某人或某事的怀疑或不信任。也可能表示说话者觉得有什么不对劲或有问题。
例句：  
“他最近一直很安静……真是太 sus 了。”
