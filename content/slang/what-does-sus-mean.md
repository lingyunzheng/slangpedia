# "Sus" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
- **Definition:** "Sus" is an internet slang term, often used to describe someone or something that is suspicious or questionable. It can also imply a lack of trust or a feeling of unease about a person or situation.
- **Usage Examples:**
- "I got a feeling that John is sus, his behavior has become quite erratic lately."
- "The new app looks sus, I'm not sure if it's safe to use."
## Chinese
- **释义：** "Sus" 是网络用语，常用来形容某人或某事可疑或值得怀疑的。它也可以表达对某人或某种情况的不信任感或不安感。
- **使用例句：**
- "我觉得约翰很可疑，他的行为最近变得很不稳定。"
- "这个新应用看起来很可疑，我不是很确定用起来安不安全。"
