## Sus (短句)
### 中文解释
"短句"在中文网络用语中通常指的是某个行为或事物看起来可疑或者不正常，常常在讨论中带有微小的怀疑或不信任。就像你在想，"这到底合不合理？" 一样。
### 英文解释
"Sus" is a term used in online slang in Chinese, referring to something or someone that looks suspicious or abnormal. It is often used in discussions with a hint of doubt or mistrust. It's like you're saying, "Does this make sense?" or "Something doesn't seem right."
