## 汉语 (Sus)
### 源自英文
* **English**: 
- **SUS**: Short for "suspect" or "suspicious"
### 解释
* **Meaning**: 怀疑、怀疑。原意是指某人或某事可疑；在网络文化中，也可以用来表示一种愤怒或不满的情绪。
### 使用场景
- **情境1**: 玩家在游戏中被队友举报时，可能会说“sus”表示怀疑。
- **情境2**: 同事之间对某人的行为表示怀疑时，可能会用到这个词。
### 示例对话
1. A: 怎么老是丢东西？
B: 可能是sus吧。
2. A: 你怎么那么快就知道我做错了？
B: 没事啦，就是感觉你有点sus。
## 英语 (Sus)
### Origin 
* **汉语**: 疑惑、怀疑，或用来表示愤怒或不满的情绪。
### Meaning
* **Meaning**: Suspect or being suspicious; also used to express anger or dissatisfaction.
### Usage Context
- **Context 1**: In a gaming environment, when a player is reported by their teammates, they might use "sus" to express doubt.
- **Context 2**: In casual conversations, colleagues might use this term to question the motives or actions of others.
### Example Dialogue
1. A: Why are things always disappearing?
B: It might be 'sus' then.
2. A: How did you find out so quickly that I did something wrong?
B: It's nothing, just that you were a bit 'sus' in my opinion.
