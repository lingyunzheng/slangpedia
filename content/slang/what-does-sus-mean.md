## "Sus" (英) / " sus " (简体中文)
### 英语:
**Definition:** "Sus" is a portmanteau of "suspicious" and "suspect." It's often used to express suspicion, doubt, or suspicion about someone or something.
**Usage:** When you’re not sure about something or someone, you might say, "I think you’re sus," indicating that you find them questionable.
**例句:** "After seeing the suspicious behavior, everyone began to think the new colleague was sus."
### 简体中文:
**释义:** "Sus" 是"可疑"和"嫌疑犯"的混成词。通常用于表达对某人或某事的怀疑、怀疑或不信任。
**用法:** 当你觉得对某人或某事物有所怀疑时，可能会说 "我觉得你很 sus"，表示你认为他们有问题。
**例句:** "看到可疑的行为后，每个人都开始怀疑新同事很 sus。"
