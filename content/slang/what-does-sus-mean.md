## English - Chinese Slang: "sus"
### In English:
- **Definition:** Suspicious or questionable.
- **Example Usage:** "I don't think he's telling the truth, he looks really sus."
- **Context:** Often used to express doubt or suspicion about someone or something. It can be used in online contexts like gaming communities where players provide clues or warnings about suspicious behavior.
### 在中文中：
- **定义：** 疑似或可疑的。
- **使用示例：** "我觉得他没说实话，他的样子真有点儿sus。"
- **语境：** 常用于表达对某人或某事的怀疑或不信任。在网络游戏社区等在线环境中，玩家可以提供线索或警告，指出可疑的行为。
