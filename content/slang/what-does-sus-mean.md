## sus (Suspect)
### English
**Meaning:** Something or someone that seems suspicious or questionable.
**Example Usage:** "I think that's a sus comment on the post."
### 中文
**含义:** 用来形容某人或某事看起来可疑或有问题。
**用例示例:** "我觉得这条帖子的评论有点sus。"
