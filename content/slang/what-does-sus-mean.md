## Sus (English)
**Meaning:** Suspect, doubtful, or likely to be dishonest. 
- **Usage Example:** "That guy is sus about his story. I don’t believe him."
## 疑犯 (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 疑犯 (yí fàn) means suspect or one who is suspected of something, often used in a criminal context. It is also used colloquially to mean someone who is questionable or suspicious.
- **Usage Example:** "那个人很可疑，我怀疑他是疑犯。" (Nà ge rén hěn kāihuǐ, wǒ sīyí tā shì yí fàn.) — That person is very suspicious, I suspect he is a suspect.
