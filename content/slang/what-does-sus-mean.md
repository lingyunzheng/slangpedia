## 英文 slang: "sus"
### 中文解释: " sus "
**英文：** "sus" is often used as an abbreviation for "suspicious," but it's much more than just that. In internet culture, it has evolved into a meme and an emoji, often used to express a high level of suspicion or skepticism about something or someone.
**中文解释：** "sus" 是 "suspicious" 的缩写，但它的用法远不止于此。在网络文化中，"sus" 已经成为了一个 meme 和表情符号，常用来表达对某事或某人的高度怀疑或质疑。
**Example usage:**
- **英文：** This guy's behavior is so sus, I don't trust him.
- **中文解释：** 这家伙的行为这么 sus，我不信任他。
**Emoji/Icon:**
- ✴️ (This symbol is often used to indicate something is sus in memes and online posts.)
