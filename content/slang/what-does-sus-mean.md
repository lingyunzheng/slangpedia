```markdown
## sus (English)
### Explanation
- **Definition**: A term often used to express doubt, suspicion, or disbelief about the reliability or authenticity of something or someone.
- **Example**: "I can't believe she won the contest. She looks sus."
## sus (Chinese)
### 解释
- **定义**：一个常用来表达对某事或某人的可靠性和真实性感到怀疑或不信任的词。
- **例句**：我不敢相信她赢得了比赛，她看起来就挺sus的。
```
In both English and Chinese explanations, the term "sus" is used to convey a sense of suspicion or doubt.
