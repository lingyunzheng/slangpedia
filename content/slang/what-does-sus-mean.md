# "Sus" Slang Explanation
## English
**"Sus"** is a humorous and informal way to express doubt or suspicion about someone or something. It's often used to describe someone who seems shady or untrustworthy.
### Example Usage:
- "I don't think that new colleague is sus, but I keep seeing them looking around a lot."
## 中文
**"Sus"** 是一种欢快且非正式的方式来表达对某人或某事的怀疑或怀有戒心。它通常用来描述看起来可疑或不可信的人。
### 使用示例：
- "我觉得这位新同事可能不太可疑，但总觉得他们总是在四处张望。"
