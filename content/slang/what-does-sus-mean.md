## sus (Suspicious)
**English:**  
*sus* is a slang term used to express doubt or suspicion about something or someone. It often implies that the person finds something concerning, off, or misleading without having concrete evidence.
**Chinese:**  
*sus* (可疑) 是一种俚语表达，用来表示对某事或某人的怀疑或不信任。它通常意味着说话人认为有些事情或某人可疑，不合理，或者有误导成分，但不一定有确凿的证据。
