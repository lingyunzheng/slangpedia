# "sus" Slang Explanation
## English
##### Meaning: Suspicious
- **Explanation:** "Sus" is a slang term used to express suspicion or doubt about a situation or person. It's often used in online forums, tweets, or memes to indicate that something seems questionable or fishy.
- **Example:** "I wouldn't trust that person; they're so sus."
## Chinese
##### 意思: 可疑
- **解释：** "sus" 是一个网络用语，用来表示对某事或某人的怀疑或怀疑。它常用于社交媒体帖子或表情包中，表示某事显得可疑或有问题。
- **例句：** "我不会信任那个人，他们真的很可疑。"
