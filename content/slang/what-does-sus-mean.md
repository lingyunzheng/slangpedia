```markdown
## 英式 slang: Sus
**Meaning**: Doubtful or suspicious. Someone or something that seems fishy, not quite right, or possibly dishonest.
**Example**:  
- (If someone claims to have won a million dollars in a raffle) That guy is such sus. It sounds too good to be true.
## 网络用语: 赏金 VIRUS
**意思**: 怀疑或可疑。某人或某事看起来可疑，不完全正确，或可能不诚实。
**例句**：  
- 如果有人声称在抽奖中赢得了百万美元，那个人肯定有问题。这听起来太好了，让人怀疑。
```
Note: "Sus" as a slang term is more commonly used in British English, particularly in social media and among younger generations. "赏金 VIRUS" is a playful way to translate and explain the term for Chinese speakers, using a term that reflects a similar meaning in a distinctively Chinese expression.
