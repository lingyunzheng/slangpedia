## 英语 slang: Sus
### 中文 slang: 坑
**Explanation:**
- **English:** Sus is commonly used on the internet to express suspicion or doubt about someone's intentions or actions. It often implies that you believe they are manipulative, deceptive, or not telling the truth.
- **Chinese (Pinyin: Kēng):** 坑 (kēng) can mean "pit" or "trick," which relatedly conveys doubt or suspicion about someone's trustworthiness or intentions. It suggests that you think someone might be misleading you or setting up a trap.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I don't trust that person; they're sosus."
- **Chinese:** "我不太相信那个人，他是坑人的。"
