## "sus"
**English:**  
A term derived from the sound used when expressing suspicion or doubt, "sus" is used to indicate that something or someone is suspicious or suspect. It can also suggest that the person or thing in question is not what they seem.
**Chinese:**  
“sus”这个词来源于表示怀疑或猜测的声音“sus”。它用来表示某物或某人是可疑的或有嫌疑的，也可以暗示某人或某物并不是他们所表现出来的那样。
