# "Sus" Slang Explanations
## English
**Definition:** Suggesting someone or something is suspicious or questionable.
**Usage Example:** 
- When someone receives an unsolicited gift, a friend might say, "That's a sus gift. Are you sure they didn't have a ulterior motive?" 
## 中文
**定义:** 表示某人或某事可疑或有问题。
**用法示例：**
- 当有人收到一份莫名奇妙的礼物时，朋友可能会说，“这礼物好可疑，他们是不是有别的目的？”
