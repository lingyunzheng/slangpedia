## 英文 slang: sus
### 中文 slang: 疑疑sus
- **Definition:** 可疑的，怀疑；形容某人或某事让人觉得不太对劲。
- **Example of use (English):** "I got a sus feeling about this deal; something feels off."
- **Example of use (中文):** "我对这个交易有些怀疑，感觉有点儿疑疑sus。"
- **Origin:** Derived from a meme, often associated with the sound effects in video games, used to convey a sense of suspicion or unease.
This phrase is often used when someone or something triggers a sense of unease or suspicion, suggesting that there might be something amiss or dubious about the situation at hand.
