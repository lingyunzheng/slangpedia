# What does "sus" mean?（sus 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Sus" is a slang term used to express suspicion or doubt about someone or something. It often implies a feeling that something isn't right or that there might be a hidden agenda.

**中文解释：**  
“sus”是一个网络用语，用来表达对某人或某事的怀疑或质疑。通常意味着觉得事情不对劲，或者可能有隐藏的动机。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: I got a sus feeling about him.
   中文翻译：我对他的感觉很可疑。
2) English sentence: Sus vibes, something might be off.
   中文翻译：感觉不太对劲，可能出了什么问题。

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "sus" is derived from the text-topeech or spell-talk method where "suspicious" is abbreviated to "sus." It gained widespread popularity on platforms like Twitter and Reddit due to its concise and immediate way of conveying suspicion.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. Odd
2. Strange
3. Weird
4. Suspicious
5. Doubtful
