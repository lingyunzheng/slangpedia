## sus (Suspiciously)
**English**: "Sus" is a slang term often used to indicate that someone or something seems suspicious or questionable, without explicitly calling them out. It's a casual way to convey doubt or uncertainty about the authenticity or integrity of a person or situation.
**Chinese**: "Sus" 是一个俚语词汇，用来表示某人或某件事显得可疑或有问题，但并不直接指出来。这是一个 casual 的方式来表达对某人或某情况的真实性或诚信性的怀疑或不确定。
