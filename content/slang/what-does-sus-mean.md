# "Sus" Slang Explanations
## English
- **Meaning**: Suspicious or doubt. Used to describe something that seems fishy or not quite right.
- **Example**: "I thought about meeting up with them, but something just didn't feel sus."
## Chinese
- **含义**: 尴尬、可疑。用来描述看起来有点不对劲或不太对的事情。
- **例句**: "我本来想和他们见面，但总觉得有点sus。"
