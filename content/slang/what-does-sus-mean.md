## sus (形容词)
### 中文解释
"sus" 是一个源自网络的俚语，经常用来形容某人或某事显得可疑、不可靠、或者与实际情况不符。这个词汇在中文语境中主要用来强调一种怀疑和不安的情绪。
### 英文解释
"sus" is a slang term originating from the internet, often used to describe someone or something that seems suspicious, unreliable, or inconsistent with actual circumstances. In Chinese context, the term is mainly used to emphasize a feeling of doubt and unease.
