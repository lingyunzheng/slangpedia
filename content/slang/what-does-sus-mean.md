## "Sus" in English & Mandarin Chinese
### English
**Meaning:** 
- Sus is a slang term often used to express suspicion or doubt about someone or something. It's a casual way of saying "suspicious" or "skeptical."
**Example Usage:**
- "I heard she was sus when she offered to help clean the house for free."
- "Dude, you should be sus about that information; it sounds fishy."
### Mandarin Chinese
**Meaning:** 
- Sus 在中文中是一个网络用语，用来表达怀疑或怀疑某人或某事。它是“可疑”的一种非正式说法。
**Example Usage:**
- “我听说她给免费打扫卫生时有点 sus。” (Wǒ tiάо shuo tā gěi miάnmiάn dǎuān sānhé shí yǒu diǎnr su目.)
