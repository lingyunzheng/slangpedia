# "sus" in English and Chinese
## English: "sus"
### Explanation
**"sus"** is an internet slang term used in English-speaking communities to express suspicion, doubt, or to question the credibility or authenticity of someone or something. It's often used in situations where something seems fishy or off, and you're not entirely sure about its legitimacy or whether it aligns with common sense or expected behavior.
### Example
- **Native English Usage:** "I can't believe you won \$10,000 in one game of scratch-off lottery tickets. That's pretty sus."
### Transliteration
It's difficult to directly translate this expression into Chinese, as it's more of an emotive statement. However, in spoken Chinese, one might use:
- **"怀疑" (yí wèi)**: meaning doubt or suspicion
- **"不对劲" (bù duì jìng)**: meaning something is not right or suspicious
### Example in Chinese Context
- **现代汉语使用：** "你竟然在一个猜刮彩票游戏中赚了1万美元，这真的不对劲。"
- **Explanation**: "You managed to win $10,000 in one scratch-off lottery game. This really feels off."
---
This expression is widely used in online forums, social media, and other digital spaces where users communicate through text.
