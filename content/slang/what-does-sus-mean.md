## "sus" (英) / "sus" (中)
### English:
**Definition:** A term often used to express suspicion, doubt, or distrust in a situation or towards a person, often in a casual or internet context. It can also denote something that feels off or shady.
**Example:** 
- *Online Context:* "I'm starting to think this isn't sus, but I need more info to be sure."
- *General:* "I had a sus feeling about that guy, so I didn't trust his story."
### 中文:
**释义：** 一个常用于表达怀疑、怀疑或不信任的情况或某个人的词汇，通常在休闲或网路环境下使用。也可以表示某件事情感到不正常或可疑。
**例句：**
- *网络环境:* "我开始觉得这不是sus，但我需要更多信息才能确定。"
- *通用语境:* "我对这家伙有种sus的感觉，所以我没有信任他的故事。"
