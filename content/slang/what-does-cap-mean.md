# Cap (English-Spanish Slang)
## English
**Cap**: To realize something is not true or that someone is being sarcastic. It's an acknowledgement that something has been exaggerated or is fake news.
* Example: "He claimed to have hiked the entire Appalachian Trail in a day? Yeah, I totally **capped** that one."
## Spanish
**Cap**: Reconocer que algo no es cierto o que alguien está siendo sarcástico. Es una forma de decir que algo ha sido exagerado o es noticia falsa.
* Ejemplo: "Afirmó haber senderizado todo el Camino Appalachian en un día solito? Ya, acabas de **capar** eso."
