# Cap (Slang - Chinese & English)
## Chinese
**顶** (dǐng)
- **Explanation**: In Chinese slang, "顶" roughly translates to "cap" in English slang. It's often used to express agreement, support, or admiration. For example, if someone says something you agree with, you might respond with "顶", signifying you support or agree with their statement.
## English
**Cap**
- **Explanation**: In English, "cap" is often used in expressions like "capped off" or simply as "cap" to express support, agreement, or excitement. For example, in a discussion, if someone gives a point you strongly agree with, you might nod and say, "That's exactly what I mean—cap."
### Usage Examples
- **Chinese**: "我觉得这项政策是对的。" (Wǒ juédé zhège zhèngcè shì duì de.) -> "顶！" (Dǐng!)
- **English**: "I think this policy is right." -> "Cap!"
