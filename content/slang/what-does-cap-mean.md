## Cap
- **English:** Cap
- Definition: To surpass or outdo someone or something.
- Example: "He really wanted to cap his sister's achievements in singing."
- **中文:** Cap
- 定义：超越或超过某人或某事物。
- 例句："他很想在唱歌方面超过他姐姐的成就。"
