# Cap 🎲💰
## English Explanation
"Cap" is an informal slang term that can have a few different meanings depending on the context:
1. **Maximum Allowance**: It can refer to the maximum amount of something you are allowed to have. For example, "I can only spend $50, that's my cap for this week."
2. **To Understand**: In slang, "cap" can also mean "get it" or "understand." For example, "I didn't get the reference, do you think you can cap it for me?"
3. **To Block or Prevent**: Sometimes it's used to mean to limit something or prevent it from happening. For example, "The coach capped our success by making us practice every day."
## Chinese Explanation
"Cap" 是一种非正式的俚语，不同的上下文中含义有所不同：
1. **限额**: 指你允许拥有的某种东西的最大数量，例如，“我只能花费50美元，这是本周的限额。”
2. **理解**: 在俚语中，“cap” 还可以用来表示“懂了”或“理解”。例如，“我不明白这个典故，你能给我解释一下吗？”
3. **阻止或限制**: 有时用来表示限制某事或防止某事的发生。例如，“教练通过每天让我们练习来限制我们的成功。”
