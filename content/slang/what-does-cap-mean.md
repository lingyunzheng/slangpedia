# Cap (English)
**Meaning:** To put a limit on something or someone; to fully understand or agree with something.
**Example Use in a Sentence:**
- "Honestly, I cap you on this one. You're totally wrong."
**Translation into Simplified Chinese:**
# Cap (英语)
**含义:** 限制某人或某事；完全同意或理解某事。
**例句使用:**
- "老实说，我完全认同你的说法，你这次是对的。"
