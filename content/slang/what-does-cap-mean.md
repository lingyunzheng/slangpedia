# Cap (English)
## Definition
A slang term often used to contradict or disagree with someone's statement. It can also mean that something is the best or limit, depending on the context.
## Example
- "That's not how it works, cap?"
- "You really think so? That's the cap, bro!"
# 帽子 (Màozi, Chinese)
## 解释
一个俚语表达，用来反驳或不同意别人的说法。它也可以表示某事是最棒的或极限，具体取决于上下文。
## 示例
- "那可不怎么管用，帽子里的？"
- "你真的这么觉得？那真是一顶帽了，兄弟！"
