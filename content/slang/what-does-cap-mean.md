## Cap (英语文本)
### English
**Meaning:** The term "cap" is used in multiple contexts.
1. **To stop someone or something from doing something:** In this slang usage, "cap" means to silence, shut up, or stop. Example: "You cap that person when they keep talking nonsense."
2. **Gaming:** In the gaming community, "cap" can mean to reach the maximum value or limit for a statistic, like frames per second (FPS), or to describe the act of continuously pressing a button to the maximum extent.
3. **To prank or handbag someone:** In the UK, "cap" can mean to prank someone in a playful or less harmful way, often referred to as "handbagging," which is a term for a less violent, more friendly version of a physical altercation or a playful bout of wrestling.
