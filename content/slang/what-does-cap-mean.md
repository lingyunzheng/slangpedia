# Cap
## English
**To cap**: To stop something abruptly or put an end to it. Often used to indicate cutting off a conversation or an idea.
*Example*: "I was about to explain my full plan, but my friend capped me by mentioning we were running late."
## 中文
**Cap**：突然停止某件事情或将其终结。通常用来表示中断对话或想法。
*例句*："我正打算详细介绍我的计划，但我的朋友提到我们快要迟到了，于是打断了我的话。"
