# Slang Term: Cap
## English
### Definition
To acknowledge something as being unbelievable, usually in a sarcastic or mocking way. 
### Example
- *"She got a promotion to management in just one week. That's so cap!"*
## Chinese (简体中文)
### 释义
用来表示某件事是不可信的或夸张的，通常是带讽刺或调侃的语气。
### 示例
- *"她在一周内就升为管理层，这也太夸张了吧！"*
