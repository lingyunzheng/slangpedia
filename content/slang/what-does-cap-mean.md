# Cap (English/Chinese)
## English
**Cap**: Originating from the acronym "Costs And Policemen", it is often used to describe someone who believes they are better than others through their looks, achievements, or social status. They think they can "cap" (dominate) situations or others because they are superior.
Example:
- He's always cap, thinking he's the smartest person in the room.
## Chinese
**帽 (mào)**：源于“Costs And Policemen”这个首字母缩写，“帽”用来形容那些自认为自己无论在外观、成就还是社会地位上都高人一等的人。他们认为自己可以通过某种方式“帽”（控制或支配）他人或局势，因为他们觉得自己是 superior 的。
例子：
- 他总是满脑子的“帽子思维”，总觉得自已是房间里最聪明的人。
