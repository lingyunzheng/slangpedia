# What does "cap" mean?  
(cap 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
A slang term that can have multiple meanings depending on the context. Common uses include expressing disbelief, limiting or capping something (like a budget), or as a verb meaning to hit or defeat someone.

**中文解释：**  
一个俚语词汇，其含义根据上下文可能有所不同。常见的用法包括表示难以置信、限制或上限某件事（如预算），或者作为动词表示打击或击败某人。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: "Bro, you got capped that test!"  
   中文翻译: 哥们，你这次考试挂了！

2) English sentence: "The budget for the project has a cap at $500,000."  
   中文翻译: 该项目的预算上限为50万美元。

### Origin / 来源背景
The origin of the slang term "cap" is not definitively known, but it has been in use since the early 2000s. It’s thought to be derived from boxing, where a referee would "cap" an opponent, meaning to stop them or end the fight.

### Synonyms / 相似表达
1. Limit
2. Ceil
3. Censor
4. Hold back
5. Restrict
