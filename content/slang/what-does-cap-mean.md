## Cap
### English
"Cap" can be used in several ways. It often means to defeat or overpower someone, like "He totally capped that player in the last game." It can also mean to stop or interrupt someone's rant with a dismissive remark, like "She capped him off by saying they never agreed on anything." In casual contexts, it can refer to dressing up or making an impression by wearing stylish clothes, e.g., "She really capped it off with those glittery heels."
