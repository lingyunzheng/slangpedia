# Cap
## English
**Definition:** To make someone believe something is true when it's not; to trick.
**Example:** "He tried to cap me by saying the food was free, but they just wanted my credit card info."
## Chinese
**定义:** 用来指哄骗某人相信某事是真的，而实际上是假的。
**例句:** "他试图哄骗我，说食物是免费的，但实际上他们只是想获取我的信用卡信息。"
