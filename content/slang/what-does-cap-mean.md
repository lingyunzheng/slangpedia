## Cap
### English
**Meaning:** To surpass or outdo someone or something.
**Example Usage:** "He really capped that moment when he won the race."
### 中文
**意思:** 超过或胜过某人或某物。
**例句:** "他在比赛中获胜时真的把别人甩在了后面，真叫一个‘cap’。"
