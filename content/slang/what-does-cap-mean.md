### Cap (English)
**Definition:** To stop or hinder someone or something from achieving their goal or potential.
**Example Usage:** "Their old rules are capping our team's ability to innovate."
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** To stop or hinder someone or something from achieving their goal or potential.
- **Chinese:** 挡住或妨碍某人或某事实现其目标或潜力。
