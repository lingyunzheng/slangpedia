## Cap (英语)
### Meaning
"Cap" is a slang term with several meanings depending on the context. 
1. **To Outdo or Exceed**: 
- **English**: To cap someone or something means to outdo them or exceed their achievements, efforts, or results.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 超过某人或某个东西（意指在成就、努力或结果上超越他们）。
2. **To Sustain or Hold In**: 
- **English**: In some contexts, "cap" can refer to a theft technique where the perpetrator prevents their victim from crying out for help by putting something over their mouth or nose.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 指通过用东西堵住受害者的嘴或鼻子来防止他们呼救的一种盗窃手法。
### Example Usage
- **English**: "She totally capped the competition by winning the gold medal!"
- **Simplified Chinese**: "她通过赢得金牌彻底超越了竞争对手！"
- **English**: "He was so scared that the thief could cap him."
- **Simplified Chinese**: "他非常害怕小偷会用东西堵住他的嘴。"
