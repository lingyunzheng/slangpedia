### Cap (English)
**Definition:** 
- *To cancel out* or *to surpass* something.
- *Adblock* - refers to a filter that stops certain achievements or notifications from appearing, most commonly used on dating apps or social media.
**Example:**
- "After he got promoted, his achievements capped his old boss."
- "She used a cap to avoid seeing unfamiliar friend requests on the app."
### Cap (Chinese)
**定义：**
- *压过* 或 *超越* 某人或某事。
- *广告拦截* - 指的是阻止某种成就或通知出现在视窗中，最常用于约会应用或社交媒体上。
**例句：**
- "升职后，他的成绩超过了他之前的老板。"
- "她用了广告拦截功能，避免看到不熟悉的交友申请。"
