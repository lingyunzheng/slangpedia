# Cap
**English:**  
To cap something means to surpass or exceed it, often in a negative context. For example, if someone's cap on the number of tasks they can handle is reached, adding more tasks would be considered "capping" them.
**Chinese:**  
“帽子”一词在口语中常用来表示超过了某个上限，通常是负面的情境。例如，如果某人的任务上限已经达到了，再给他加任务就叫做“帽子”他了。
