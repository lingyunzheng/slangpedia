# Cap (English)
## Meaning
- To stop or limit something abruptly.
## Example
- "He tried to talk about politics, but his friend capped him right away."
# Cap (中文)
## 含义
- 突然中断或限制某事。
## 示例
- "他试图谈论政治，但他的朋友立刻就打断了他。"
