## Cap (English)
**Meaning:** Usually used as a slang term to describe someone or something that is the very best, top, or most impressive version in a particular category or context.
**Example:** "She's the cap on our team when it comes to public speaking."
## Cap (Chinese)
**意思:** 通常用来表示在某个类别或情境中最好的、最棒的或最令人印象深刻的版本。
**例句:** "她是我们团队中最出色的公众演讲者。"
