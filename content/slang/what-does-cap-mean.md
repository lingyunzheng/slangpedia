## Cap (English)
### Simplified Explanation
"Cap" is a slang term that can have multiple meanings depending on the context. It's often used to describe someone or something that is seen as being unable to handle a situation, or to describe a person who is overly impressed or happy in a way that seems forced or fake.
- **Example Usage:** "Man, you need to cap that deal! You're way over your head."
- **Meaning:** In this context, "cap" suggests that the person should stop pursuing the deal as they are not equipped to handle it.
- **Example Usage:** "Wow, that guy really capped it when he said he would run a marathon in under an hour."
- **Meaning:** Here, "capped it" means that the person overestimated their abilities, saying something impressive but impossible.
### Chinese Translation & Explanation
"帽" (mào) can be used in a few different ways to convey similar meanings, but it’s generally more formal or less commonly used in casual speech compared to its English counterpart.
- **含义1**：形容某人或某事被认为无法处理某个状况，或形容一个人非常钦佩或开心，但看起来是假的。
- **例句**："你需要停止追求那个交易，你根本不在这种水平。" —— 在这里，"帽"暗示这个人在做的事情超出了他的能力范围，无法胜任。
- **例句**："他真的说要在一小时内跑完马拉松，这太夸张了。" —— 这里，“帽”表示这个人对自己的能力估计过高，夸张地说了一些不可能的事情。
