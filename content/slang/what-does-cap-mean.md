```markdown
# Cap (English/Chinese)
## English
**Definition**: To consider someone or something as the best or most important. Often used to express agreement or admiration.
**Example**: "She's the cap when it comes to graphic design in our company."
## Chinese
**释义**: 将某人或某物视为最佳或最重要的。常用于表示赞同或钦佩。
**示例**: "她是公司里平面设计的顶儿尖儿。"
```
In Chinese slang, "顶儿尖儿" is used to convey the meaning of "cap" in terms of being the best.
