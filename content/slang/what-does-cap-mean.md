# Cap (Slang)
## English Explanation
"Cap" is a term used to indicate that someone has said or done something particularly stupid or foolish. It can also be used to show that someone has been tricked or fooled.
### Example
- **Monika:** "I just bought a new phone and it broke after two days!"
- **Alex:** "Wow, nice cap, Monika!"
### Conversation
- **Person A:** "I just ate all my vegetables at dinner."
- **Person B:** "Cap, that's gonna leave you with a belly ache!"
## 中文解释
“Cap”是一个俚语，用来表示某人说的话或做的事情特别愚蠢或不明智。它还可以用来表示某人被愚弄或骗了。
### 例句
- **Monika:** "我刚买了一部新手机，结果两天就坏了！"
- **Alex:** "哇塞，Monika，你真是cap了！"
### 对话
- **A:** "我晚餐把所有的蔬菜都吃光了。"
- **B:** "你真是cap了，这样肚子会痛的。"
