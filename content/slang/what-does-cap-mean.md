## Cap (English - Slang)
**Definition:** To cap someone or something means to defeat or defeat them decisively, often with ease.
**Example:** "He really cappen' that new streamer, barely put in any effort and got more views!"
**Translation in Chinese - 调侃用语:**
## Cap (中文 - 网络用语)
**释义:** “cap” 意味着彻底击败某人或某事，通常非常轻松。
**例句:** “他 really cappen’ 那个新主播，几乎不费劲就得到了更多的观看量！”
注意：这里的“cappen’”是中文网民创造的用来模拟英文发音的词语。
