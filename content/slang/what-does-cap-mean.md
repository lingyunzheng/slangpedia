# Cap (英/美俚语)
**English:** Cap  
**中文:** 超级厉害；酷毙了
## Explanation
**English:** "Cap" is an informal term often used to express strong approval or admiration for someone or something. It can also be used to indicate that something is at its maximum or limit. This term is commonly heard in casual conversations among friends or on social media.
**中文:** "帽儿" 或者 "帽儿飚" 是一种非正式用语，多用于表达对某人或某物的强烈赞同或钦佩。它也可以用来表示某事达到了最大或极限。这个词汇常见于朋友间的 casual 聊天或社交媒体中。
