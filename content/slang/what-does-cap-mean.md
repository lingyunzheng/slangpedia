# Cap 🧕✨
**English Slang:**
A cap can refer to a few different things depending on the context. It might mean to drastically reduce or eliminate something, such as a budget or a bonus. Alternatively, it can be used to denote a limit or a ceiling in numbers or value. In a more casual setting, it can also refer to a person or thing being at the top of their game or being the best in their category.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** To significantly cut off or eliminate something, or to describe a maximum limit.
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 减少或取消某物，或者描述最大限制。
- **Example Usage:**
- In the budget meeting: "We need to cap the marketing expenses to save money."
- Referring to performance: "She's truly the cap of our team, no one can compete with her."
- **Abbreviation:** CAPE (最高限额)
**English Slang:**
To put a stop to something, often used in a more abrupt or harsh sense.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** To abruptly stop something.
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 破坏或中断某物。
- **Example Usage:**
- In a conversation: "Bro, let's cap this chat, we should focus on work now."
**English Slang:**
In social contexts, sometimes "cap" is used to describe someone setting up a boundary with another person, especially in a romantic or social setting.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** To demarcate boundaries with someone, often in a social or romantic context.
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 设定社交或浪漫关系中的界限。
- **Example Usage:**
- In a relationship: "I think we should cap our dates to just once a week."
