# Cap (俚语解释)
## 中文
**帽**：在网络用语中，“帽”这个词有时用来表示一种很老套、没有新意的想法或者行为。常常用来形容那些已经过时或者被认为是无聊、不创意的行为或观点。
**用法举例**：别老想当年的事了，现在要跟上潮流，别太“帽”了。
## English
**Cap**：
- **Definition**: In internet slang, "cap" is often used to refer to something very outdated or lacking in originality. It's frequently used to describe actions or viewpoints that are considered boring or uncreative and out of touch with current trends.
- **Example**: Stop thinking about the past, you need to keep up with the times and don't be too "cap" (outdated).
