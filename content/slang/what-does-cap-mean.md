## Cap (English)
### Meaning
To understand or accept a situation fully, often used when someone is completely wrong or incorrect about something.
### Example
- "You think I don't know what happened? You're totally capped!"
## Cap (Chinese)
### 含义
完全理解或接受一个情况，通常用于形容某人完全错了或不正确的情况。
### 例句
- 你真以为我不知道发生了什么事吗？你完全错了！
