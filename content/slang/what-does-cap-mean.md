# Cap (俚语解释)
## 中文解释
“帽“，通常用于形容一个人某方面的水平或才能被高估了。例如，如果一个人的真实水平没那么高，但他却表现得好像非常厉害，你可以用“帽”来形容这种现象。
### 示例
- 他简直就是个篮球帽！（他明明篮球打得不好，却总表现得很厉害）
- 她唱歌简直就是个不折不扣的麦霸帽！（她可能歌唱得一般，但却非常喜欢在人前高声唱歌，表现出很强的“麦霸”风范）
## 英文解释
“Cap” generally refers to the act of overstating someone's level or talent in a particular area. When used, it suggests that the person's actual ability is actually lower than what they are pretending or perceived to be.
### Examples
- He’s literally a basketball cap! (He’s not as good at basketball as he pretends to be.)
- She’s a total karaoke cap when it comes to singing! (While she might not be a great singer, she loves to belt it out in front of others.)
