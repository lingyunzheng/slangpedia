## Cap (English)
### Meaning
- A slang term often used to express the idea of "maxing out" or reaching the highest possible level, degree, or quality of something.
### Example Usage
- "This is the cap on quality; you won't find anything better here!"
## Cap (Chinese)
### 含义
- 一个俚语，常用来表示达到某个事物的最大值、最高水平、最高等级或最佳状态。
### 使用示例
- 这是最高水准，你别找了，再也找不出更好的了！
