## Cap (US English)
**Definition**: An expression that means something is the ultimate or the best of something.
**Explanation**: When you say "it's the cap," you're saying that it's the top, the peak, or the absolute best. It's like saying, "it's the zenith or the pinnacle."
**Example**: "This pizza is the cap! I've never had one this delicious before."
## Cap (UK English)
**Definition**:俚语表达，意为"结束"或"打住"，多用于命令或建议停止某种行为或话题。
**Explanation**: 当你使用 "cap" 时，它通常用于命令或建议某人停止某种行为或结束某个话题。类似于 "cut it out" 或 "enough already"。
**Example**: "Hey, cap the noise! I'm trying to work."
