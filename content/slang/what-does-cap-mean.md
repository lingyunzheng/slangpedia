# Cap (英汉双解流行语解释)
**English:** Cap  
- **Definition:** To limit or restrict something.  
- **Example:** "The budget cap means we can't spend more than $500,000 this year."  
- **Popular Usage:** Often used in the phrase "That's the cap" to indicate a limit has been reached.  
- **Slang Note:** This usage has become more common with the rise of cryptocurrency and its associated limits and regulations.
**汉语:** Cap  
- **释义:** 限制或限制某事物。  
- **例句:** "预算上限意味着今年我们不能超过50万的支出。"  
- **流行用法:** 常常用于表达"那是上限"，以表明某事已经达到上限。  
- ** slang 备注:** 这种用法随着加密货币及其相关限制和监管的兴起而更加常见。
