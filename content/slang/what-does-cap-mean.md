# Cap (Vocabulary)
## English
**Cap**: When someone or something is the best, number one, or above all others in a particular category or situation. It can also mean to fully utilize the potential of something or someone to the highest degree.
*Example*: That new rapper is really **cap** at making viral songs.
## Chinese
**帽 (mào)**: 当某人或某事在某个特定的类别或情况下是最棒的、排名第一的，或者是在某个领域表现得最为突出时，就可以说它是“帽”。这个词也可以用来表示将某人或某事物的能力完全发挥到极致。
*例句*: 这位新rapper在制作病毒式歌曲方面真的非常“帽”。
