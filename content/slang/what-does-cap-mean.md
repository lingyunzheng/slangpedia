## Cap (to cap)
### In English
**Meaning:** To insult someone harshly, often in a public manner.
**Example:** "He got mad when someone called him lazy, so he decided to cap the person back on social media."
### In Chinese
**意思:** 用严厉的方式侮辱某人，通常是在公共平台上进行。
**例句:** "当他被人称呼懒惰后很生气，于是决定在社交媒体上回击那个人。"
