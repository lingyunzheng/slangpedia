# Cap
## English
**Meaning**: To put an end to something; to make something no longer exist or continue, often because it's the best or most appropriate thing to do.
**Example**: "We capped the meeting early because everyone agreed on the proposal."
## Chinese
**含义**: 停止或结束某事；使之不再存在或继续，通常是因为这是最好的或最合适的做法。
**例句**: "我们提前结束了会议，因为大家都同意该提案。"
