## En-Us Explanation: Gymcel
**Definition:**
A portmanteau of "gym" and "cell," often used to describe someone who is obsessed with going to the gym or maintaining a strict workout regimen. 
**Usage:**
- "OMG, this guy is a gymcel! He’s in the gym more than his own cell."
- "It’s like living with an actual gymcel now that John has joined the health club."
**Rough Translation:**
- **In Chinese:** 健身狂 (jiàn shēn kuáng) - refers to someone who is crazy about going to the gym.
