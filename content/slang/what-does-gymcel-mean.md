## Gymcel
**English:** A person who excessively uses the gym and defines their worth or social status through their physical appearance and fitness level.
- **Example Usage:** "John is really a gymcel; he's in the gym every morning before work and always posts about his workouts on social media."
**中文:** 表现为极度依赖健身，通过身体外形和健身水平来定义自我价值或社会地位的人。
- **使用示例:** "约翰真是个健身狂，每天早上在上班前都会去健身房，还总是把健身动态发到社交媒体上。"
