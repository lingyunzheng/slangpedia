## Gymcel
**English:** A gymcel is an individual who is obsessed with their physical fitness and spends an excessive amount of time at the gym, often at the expense of other important aspects of life such as relationships or work.
**Chinese:** gymcel（健身房成瘾者）是指那些对身体健身极度痴迷，花大量时间在健身房的人，往往会在其他重要的生活方面（比如人际关系或工作）上有所忽视。
Example Usage:
- English: She's such a gymcel, she works out at 6 AM and again at 6 PM every day.
- Chinese: 她真是个gymcel，每天早上6点和下午6点都去健身房锻炼。
