## Gymcel (健身强迫症)
**中文解释：**  
“健身强迫症”通常是用来形容那些极度痴迷于健身和保持身材的人。这些人往往会频繁地去健身房，沉迷于各种健身活动，对于自己的体重、肌肉线条甚至日常饮食都保持异常的关注。他们可能会表现出一些强迫性的行为，比如强烈注意每公斤体重的变化，或是每天必须进行一定的运动量才感到满足。
**English Explanation:**
"Gymcel" is a term often used to describe individuals who are incredibly obsessed with fitness and maintaining their physique. Such people frequently hit the gym, immersing themselves in various workout activities and showing an abnormal amount of concern for their weight, muscle tone, and even their daily diet. They may exhibit some compulsive behaviors, such as strongly focusing on every kilogram of weight change or feeling unsatisfied unless they meet a certain daily exercise quota.
