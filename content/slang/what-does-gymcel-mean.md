# Gymcel
**English:** A person who excessively focuses on their appearance and health, often spending a disproportionate amount of time at the gym or following extreme fitness routines.
**Chinese:** 健身狂热者 （jiàn shēn kuáng ruò zhě）—— 专注于外表和健康的人，经常花费大量的时间在健身房或进行极端的健身活动上。
Gymcel is commonly used to describe someone who is so obsessed with fitness and physical appearance that it becomes a common topic of conversation and a significant part of their lifestyle. This can sometimes be taken negatively, implying that the individual might be neglecting other important aspects of their life, such as relationships or personal well-being.
