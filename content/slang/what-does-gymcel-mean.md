# Gymcel
## English Explanation
**Gymcel:** A term used to describe someone who is overly obsessed with the gym and workout routines. This person might spend an inordinate amount of time in the gym, often to the exclusion of other activities or responsibilities. They might be extremely focused on their physical appearance, diet, and fitness goals.
## Spanish Explanation
