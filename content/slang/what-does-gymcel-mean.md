# Gymcel (健身控: jiàn shēn kòng)
## English Explanation
**Gymcel**: A term derived from the combination of "gym" (gymnasium) and "Addicted" or "Addict" (成瘾: chéng yǐn). It refers to someone who is obsessed with working out and fitness. A gymcel often spends a significant amount of time at the gym, is always looking for new workout routines, and is constantly seeking out ways to improve their physical fitness and overall health. They take pride in tracking their progress and may even use social media to share their fitness journey and challenges.
## Chinese Explanation
**健身控 (gōng):** 来源于“gym”（健身房）和“成瘾”（chéng yǐn，成瘾是指对某种事物有强烈的依赖或热衷）的结合，指那些对健身非常痴迷的人。健身控通常会在健身房花费大量时间，总是寻找新的锻炼计划，热衷于提升自己的身体健康和体能。他们以追踪进展为乐，并且可能会通过社交媒体分享自己的健身历程和挑战。
