```markdown
# gymcel
## English Slang
A portmanteau of "gym" and "cel" (short for cell phone or cellphone). It refers to someone who is so obsessed with using their phone that they even look at it or use it while working out at the gym. This phrase generally carries a negative connotation, suggesting that the person is not fully present or engaged in the exercise.
## Chinese Slang
一个由“健身房”和“cel”(手机或手机的缩写)组成的合成词。它指的是那些在健身房锻炼时还痴迷于使用手机的人。使用这个词时通常带有负面的含义，暗示这个人没有全情投入到锻炼中。
例句：
1. I saw a gymcel checking Instagram while on the treadmill.
我看到有人在跑步机上锻炼时查看Instagram，简直不可思议。
2. He's always a gymcel, completely addicted to his phone.
他总是个整天拿着手机的健身房狂热者。
```
