## gymcel
**English:** 
A portmanteau of "gym" and "cell." This slang term is used to describe someone with an obsessive enthusiasm for the gym, often to the point of behaving in a manner that could be perceived as borderline or excessively regimented. People labeled as gymcels might be seen excessively checking their workouts, counting calories meticulously, or showing extreme dedication to their fitness routines.
**中文:** 
“gymcel” 是由“gym”（健身房）和“cell”（监狱、笼子）这两个词合成的一个新词。这个词通常用来形容那些对自己的健身有着近乎痴迷的热情的人，有时候这种热情可能显得过于密集、规律，甚至有点矫枉过正。被称为“gymcel”的人可能极度关注自己的锻炼、精确计算卡路里，或者展现出异常的健身承诺。
Gymcels are often seen as taking their fitness routines to an extreme, much like an inmate would their daily routine in a jail.
Gymcels 可能会把他们的健身程序带到极端，就像狱中的囚犯对待他们日常程序一样。
