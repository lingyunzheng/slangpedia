## Gymcel: 肌肉细胞
**English**: Gymcel
**Definition**: A slang term combining "gym" (gymnasia) with "cell" (cell in biology) to refer to someone who frequently visits the gym to build and maintain muscular physique. Essentially, it describes a fitness enthusiast or a person focused on bodybuilding and physique.
**Etymology**: Originates from the prefix "gym," short for gymnasium, and "cell," metaphorically alluding to the individual cells in the body that get strengthened through regular workouts.
**Usage Example**: "I met a new gymcel at the gym yesterday and he was showing off his new arm gains."
**Synonyms**: Fitness fanatic, bodybuilder, weightlifter
**Antonyms**: Couch potato, food lover, recreational exerciser
