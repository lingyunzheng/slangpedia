## Gymcel
**English:** A person who frequently engages in excessive gym activities, often to the point of obsession. They might prioritize their workout routine over other responsibilities or social activities.
**Chinese:** 健身狂 (jiàn shēn kuáng) - 过度热衷于健身的人，往往沉迷于锻炼，对其他责任或社交活动不屑一顾。这些人会花费大量时间和精力在锻炼上，甚至达到痴迷的程度。
