## Gymcel
### English Explanation
"Gymcel" is a portmanteau of "gym" and "cell," often used humorously to describe someone who is so fixated on the idea of going to the gym that it feels like they're literally in a cell. This term can be used to tease someone who is overly obsessed with fitness, always talking about gym routines, or neglecting other aspects of their life in pursuit of their physical goals.
### Chinese Explanation
"Gymcel" 是“健身房”（gym）和“牢房”（cell）的混杂词，常用来戏谑那些如此专注于去健身房，以至于仿佛被困在牢房里的人。这个词通常用来调侃那些过度痴迷健身的人，总是谈论健身计划，或者忽略其他生活方面的追求。
