## Gymcel (健身操迷)
### 英文解释
**Gymcel** is a term that comes from combining the words "gym" (gym) and "cell" (细胞, cell). It refers to people who are deeply obsessed with fitness and spending a lot of time at the gym, often resembling someone who is feeding off the energy of their workout routine. Gymcels are those who consistently engage in intense workouts, always pushing themselves to the edge, and sometimes even going beyond necessary limits. If someone is a "gymcel," it’s likely they spend a significant portion of their day, week, or life at the gym, prioritizing their fitness goals over other activities.
### 中文解释
**Gymcel** 是一个将“ gym”（健身房）与“ cell”（细胞）结合在一起的新词。这个词用来形容那些极度热衷于健身并经常泡在健身房里的人，仿佛他们从中汲取了大量的能量。Gymcels 经常进行高强度的锻炼，总是努力突破自己的极限，有时甚至会超出必要限度。如果有人说某人是“gymcel”，大概意味着这位朋友将大部分时间、甚至是日常生活都用来在健身房里追求健身目标，而不太关注其他活动。
