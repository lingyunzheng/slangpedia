# Gymcel
## English Explanation
"Gymcel" is a portmanteau of "gym" and "cell," frequently used in casual and social contexts, especially among fitness enthusiasts and bodybuilders. It describes a scenario where someone is still thinking about the gym even when they should be at home or engaged in another activity. For example, "He keeps calling me to go to the gym, but it's 10 PM already—I'm actually in my cell (bed) right now, but his constant chatter about the gym is keeping me motivated!"
## Mandarin Explanation
"Gymcel" 是“健身房”（gym）和“牢房”（cell）的杂糅词，通常在日常和社交场合中使用，尤其是在健身爱好者和健美运动员中间。这个词用于描述一个人即使在应该在其他地方（如家中或参与其他活动）时，仍然脑海中满是健身房的情景。例如，“他一直催我一起去健身房，但现在都十点了呢—其实我现在在床上（cell），但我仍然被他对健身房的念叨所激励！”
