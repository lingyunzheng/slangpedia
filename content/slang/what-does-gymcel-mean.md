```markdown
## gymcel (英): gym addict (健身房成瘾者)
### Explanation:
gymcel 是 "gym" (健身房) 和 "cell" (细胞, cell 的音译) 的组合，用来形容那些痴迷于健身的人。这些人通常会花费大量时间在健身房锻炼，甚至像细胞分裂一样频繁地去健身，就像他们的生活中不可或缺的一部分。
In English, gymcel is a blend of "gym" (gymnasium) and "cell" (cell, which sounds like "cel"), referring to people who are obsessed with fitness. These individuals typically spend a lot of time working out in the gym, and might as well be splitting in half like cells, indicating how integral their gym routine is to their lives.
```
This explanation captures the essence of the term and provides a clear description in both Chinese and English.
