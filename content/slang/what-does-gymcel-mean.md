## Gymcel: 词源与解释
### 英文
**Gymcel** (slang term): A mock or playful term using "gym" (gymnasium, a fitness facility) and "cell" to describe a highly regimented or overly rigorous routine, especially in the context of fitness and workout regimens. It hints at the idea of being trapped in a repetitive, intense workout cycle.
