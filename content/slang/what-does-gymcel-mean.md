# gymcel

**English:** A term used to describe an excessively eager or obsessive gym-goer. This person is typically deeply committed to their fitness routine and spends an inordinate amount of time in the gym, often believing that constant exercise is the key to good health or success.

**中文:** 一个用来形容过于投入或痴迷于健身的人。这些人通常对健身计划有极强的执着，花大量时间在健身房，往往认为不断锻炼是保持健康或成功的关键。

---

Example Usage:
- He's such a gymcel, I swear he thinks his muscles grow overnight!
- 这个 gymcel 真是太沉迷健身了，他都以为肌肉是隔夜就长的吗？
