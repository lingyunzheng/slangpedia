## Gymcel
### English Explanation
A fusion of the words "gym" and "cell," "gymcel" refers to a person who dedicates a significant amount of their life to the gym, often to the point where they seem to spend more time there than in their own home or "cell." This term is often used in a lightheared or humorous manner to describe someone's enthusiastic and possibly obsessive commitment to fitness and working out.
### Chinese Explanation
"健身房中的监狱细胞" 或 "gymcel" 是由 "gym"（健身房）和 "cell"（细胞、监狱）这两个词混合而成的一个网络流行语。这个词用来形容那些非常投入健身、花费大量时间和精力在健身房里的人，他们可能会觉得健身房就像是他们的“家”一样。这个词语常常被用来善意地形容某人对健身的热情，有时也可能带有一些玩笑或戏谑的意味。
