# Valid
## English
**Definition:** Something or someone that is genuine, legitimate, or meets the required standards.
**Example:** "Is your ticket valid for today's game?" (Is your ticket real and acceptable for entry to today's game?)
## 中文
**释义:** 真实的、合法的或符合要求的。
**例句:** "你的票今天有效吗？" (你的票今天可以用来入场吗？)
