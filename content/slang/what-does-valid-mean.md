# English: Valid
- **Meaning:** Something that is real, true, or genuine.
- **Slang Explanation (Eng):** When you say something or someone is "valid," you're confirming it's legit and not fake.
- **Example Sentence (Eng):** "Her claim about the free concert is valid, she did win the raffle."
# 中文解释:
- **含义:** 真实、真实无误或真正的。
- **俚语解释 (英):** 当你说某事或某人是“valid”的时候，你是在确认它是真实的，而不是假的。
- **例句 (英):** "她的免费演唱会声明是valid的，因为她确实赢得了抽奖。"
