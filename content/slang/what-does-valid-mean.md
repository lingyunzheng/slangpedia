# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Valid"
## English
**Meaning:** 
- Something or someone that is genuine, real, or legally acceptable.
**Example Usage:**
- "His ticket is valid for travel on this route."
**Slang Context:**
- Often used in a humorous or sarcastic way to emphasize a point about authenticity or legality.
## Chinese
**意思:**
- 真实的，合法的，或者可信的。
**例句:**
- "他的票在这个路线是有效的。"
** slang用法:**
- 经常以讽刺或开玩笑的方式使用，强调真实性或合法性。
