# Slang Explanation for "valid"
## English
- **Definition**: Something that is valid is true, real, or has a proper purpose or justification.
- **Usage Example**: "That's a valid point; I hadn't thought about it that way."
## Chinese
- **释义**: 有效的或真实的，有正当理由或目的的。
- **使用示例**: “这是一个合理的观点，我没有从那个角度看问题。”
