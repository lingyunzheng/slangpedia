## Valid
### English
**Meaning:** Something that is true, real, or correct. Can also be used colloquially to confirm correctness or agreement.
**Example:** "Is that really how it happened?" "Yeah, it's valid."
### 中文
**意思:** 真实的、正确的或真实的。在口语中，也用来确认正确性或同意。
**例句:** "那 really 是真的那样发生的吗？" "是的，这是正确的。"
