# Valid
## English
- **Meaning**: Something that is genuine, true, or legitimate.
- **Example**: "His story about winning the lottery is valid if he has the ticket."
## Chinese
- **意义**：指真实、合法或正当的事物。
- **例句**：如果他有彩票，那么他赢得彩票的故事就是有效的。
