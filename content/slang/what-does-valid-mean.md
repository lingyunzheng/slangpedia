## Valid (有效)
- **English**: A term used to confirm or affirm something as being true, correct, or legitimate. Often used in casual conversation to give approval or to state that something is acceptable or correct.
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation**: 哦Yeah，这个是真的行（对的、好的）。例如，在讨论某人的观点时，如果你认为这个观点是正确的，你可能会说“valid”来表达你的认同。也可以用来表示某事物是有效的或符合标准的，比如“这个方法valid，有用！”
*(Translation: Oh Yeah, this is real! For example, when discussing someone's viewpoint and you believe it's correct, you might say "valid" to express your agreement. It can also be used to indicate that something is effective or meets standards, like "This method is valid, it works!" Bonds are often used in casual clicks or among friends to confirm something as true or correct.)*
