# Slang Explanation: "valid"
**English:**
- **Meaning:** This term generally refers to something that is real, authentic, or legally or logically correct.
- **Example Sentences:** 
- "Her arguments aren't valid, they lack any real evidence."
- "This ticket is valid for travel on Saturday."
**Chinese (简体中文):**
- **含义:** 这个词通常是表示真实的、真实的、合法的或逻辑上正确的。
- **例句:**
- "她的论点不成立，缺少任何实质性的证据。"
- "这张票周六有效。"
