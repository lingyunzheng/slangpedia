## Valid
### English
**Meaning:** Something that is considered true, correct, or appropriate.
**Slang Explanation:** "On point" or "got the goods." It's like saying something checks out or is legit.
### Chinese
**含义:** 指被认为真实、正确或适当的某件事。
**俚语解释:** "到位" 或 "有本事"。相当于说某件事是真的、正确的，或是有实质内容的。
