## English Slang Explanation of "Valid"
**Meaning**: Something that is real, genuine, or true.
**Example**: "This ticket is valid for tomorrow's show, but don't lose it!"
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
**英语俚语解释**: 有效的，真实的，真实的。
**例句**: "这张票明天的演出有效，但别弄丢了！"
