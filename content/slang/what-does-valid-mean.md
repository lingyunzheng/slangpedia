# Valid
### English
"Valid" in slang can mean something is real, genuine, or actually happening, as opposed to being fake or made-up. It can also indicate that something is logically consistent or supported by evidence.
### Chinese
"valid" 在网络用语中，常用来表示某事是真实的、实际发生的，而不是虚构或杜撰的。也可以用来形容某事是逻辑上站得住脚的，或有证据支持的。
