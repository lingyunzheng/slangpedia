# Valid
## English
"Valid" in slang, often means something is legitimate, correct, or meets the required standards. It can also be used to express agreement or approval, similar to saying "cool" or "good."
## 中文
"Valid" 在俚语中，通常表示某事是合法的、正确的，或者符合要求的标准。它还可以用来表达同意或认可，类似于说“酷”或“好”。
