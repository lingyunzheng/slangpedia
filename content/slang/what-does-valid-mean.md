# Valid
## English
**Meaning**: Something that is true, correct, or appropriate. It can also refer to being accepted or approved by a particular group or organization.
**Example**: "That's a valid point, I hadn't thought of that."
## 中文
**意思**: 表示某事是真实的、正确的或适当的。也可以指被某个团体或组织接受或批准。
**示例**: "这是一个有道理的观点，我没有想到这一点。"
