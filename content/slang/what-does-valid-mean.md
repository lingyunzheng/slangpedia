## Valid
### English Explanation
"Valid" is a term that can be used as both an adjective and a noun in English. As an adjective, it means something is legally or logically sound, or it has a recognized or accepted status. As a noun, it refers to something that is currently considered right or appropriate, or something that holds up under scrutiny.Sometimes, it can also be used in a casual sense to confirm or agree with something.
### Slang/Bilingual Explanation
- **English**: Get real! It's legit! ✔️
- **Literal Translation**: 真有道理！是正经事儿！✔️
"Valid" in a casual context might be translated using phrases like "真的有道理" (really makes sense) or "是正经事儿" (serious business). In English slang, someone might say "It's valid!" to confirm the legitimacy or correctness of something or someone's point of view, similar to saying "That's legit!" or "I see your point!"
