# Valid
## English
- **Definition**: Something that is considered to be correct or acceptable.
- **Slang Example**: "His claiming to know about jazz is valid if he's been to New Orleans."
## Chinese
- **定义**: 被认为是正确或可接受的。
- **俚语示例**: "如果他去过新奥尔良，那么他声称知道爵士乐就是有道理的。"
