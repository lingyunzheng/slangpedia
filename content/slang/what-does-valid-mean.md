## English Explanation
**Valid**: In casual slang, "valid" is often used to emphasize that something is legitimate, correct, or acceptable. It can also imply that something is reliable or has been proven to work. For example, if someone says their argument is "valid," they mean it's a strong and reasonable one.
## 中文解释
**有效**（Valid）：在俚语中，“有效”这个词常用来强调某事是合乎实际的、正确的或可接受的。它还可以表示某事是可靠的或已经被证明有效。比如，当一个人说自己提出的论点“有效”时，他们意思是这是一条强有力的、合理的论点。
