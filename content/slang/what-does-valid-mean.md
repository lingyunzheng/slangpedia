## Valid
### English Slang Explanation
"Valid" in slang can mean something is legally sound, reasonable, or makes sense. It can also imply that something is real or genuine.
### Chinese Slang Explanation
"有效" (Xiàotiǎo) 或 "管用" (Guǎn yòng) — 指某件事、某个论点或某个情况是合法的、有道理的或真实的。它还可以表示某件事是有效的或有用的。
Example Usage:
- English: "That's a valid point."
- Chinese: "这确实是一个有效的观点。" (Zhè shíqíshí shì yīgè xiàotiǎo de guānyìng)
- English: "His arguments are valid."
- Chinese: "他的论据是有效的。" (Tā de lùn jù shì xiàotiǎo de)
