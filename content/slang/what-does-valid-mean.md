# Valid (Valid)
## English
**Meaning:** 
- Something that is considered acceptable, correct, or proper.
**Example Sentence:**
- "Wearing sneakers to work is totally valid these days!"
## 中文
**含义：**
- 表示被认为是可接受、正确或妥当的事物。
**例句：**
- “穿运动鞋去上班现在完全没问题！”
