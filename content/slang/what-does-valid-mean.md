# Valid (有效)
## English
**Definition:** Something that is considered legitimate or acceptable, often used to affirm that something is true or rightful.
**Example:** "Your complaints are valid if you've been consistently late to meetings."
## Chinese
**定义:** 表示某事合法或可接受，常用于肯定某事的真实性或正当性。
**示例:** "如果你一直开会迟到，你的抱怨是有效的。"
