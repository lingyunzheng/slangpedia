## Valid (英/美)
### English Slang Explanation
"Valid" in slang can often refer to something being genuine, authentic, or real. It's used to affirm that something is true or legally acceptable. For example, if you say, "This ticket is valid for entry," it means the ticket is real and can be used as intended.
### Chinese Slang Explanation
"有效" (xiào hòu) 在网络用语中，可以用来指某样东西是真实的、可靠的或者合法的。比如，你可以说“这张票有效”，意味着这张票是真实的，可以用来进行门票检查。
---
例句:
- English: "This offer is valid for the next week only."
- Chinese: "这个优惠只在下周内有效。"
