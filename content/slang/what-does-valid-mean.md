## English Slang Explanation for "Valid"
**Meaning:** Something that is correct, real, or appropriate.
**Example:** "That was a valid point in the debate."
**Slang Usage:** "It's valid, man. We need to respect her opinion."
## Chinese 花样解释 for "valid"
**意思:** 正确的，真实的，或者恰当的。
**例句:** "这是辩论中一个合理的观点。”
**俚语用法:** “她的观点站得住脚，我们得尊重她。”
