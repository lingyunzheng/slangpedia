## Valid [English]
- **Definition:** Something that is legitimate, true, or correct.
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation:** 
- **有效** [Chinese] - Meaning something is real, genuine, or authentic. 
Example usage:
- *English:* "That discount code is valid for today only!"
- *Chinese:* "这个优惠码今天有效！"
## Valid [Chinese]
- **定义:** 合法的，真实的，或正确的。
- **双语俚语解释:**
- **有效** [English] - Refers to something being genuine or authentic.
例句：
- *Chinese:* "这个优惠码今天有效！"
- *English:* "That discount code is valid for today only!"
