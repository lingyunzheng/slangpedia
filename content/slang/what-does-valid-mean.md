#_VALID_
## English
### Meaning
"Valid" in slang can mean something is real, genuine, or actual. It can also be used to confirm or authenticate something.
### Example
- "That's valid, I've heard the same thing from a few people."
## 中文
### 含义
"Valid" 在俚语中可以表示某事物是真实的、真诚的或实际存在的。它也可以用来验证或确认某些事。
### 例句
- "确实有这种事，我听好几个朋友也这么说。"
