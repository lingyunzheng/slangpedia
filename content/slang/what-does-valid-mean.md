## English
**Valid**:
- *Meaning*: Something that is genuine, real, or legally acceptable.
- *Slang Explanation*: "Good to go" or "Legit." It's used to describe something that is true, right, or meets the required standards. For example, "That ID is totally valid; we can proceed with the transaction."
## 中文
**有效**:
- *含义*: 真实、合法或符合要求的。
- *俚语解释*: "没问题" 或 "靠谱的"。用来形容某事是真实的、正确的或符合标准的。例如，“这个身份证有效，我们可以进行交易了。”
