# Valid (有效的)
**English:**  
"Valid" is often used in slang contexts to express something that is legitimate, true, or correct. It can also mean something is useful, works as intended, or meets the required standards.
**Example:**
- "Is that passport valid?" - Are you legally allowed to travel internationally with that passport?
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **有效 (Xiào yào)**: 在日常口语中，"有效" 用来表示某物是合法的、真实的或正确的。它也可以表示某物有用、如预期工作，或者符合所需标准。
- "你知道这护照有效吗？" - 你知道这本护照是否合法你国际旅行用吗？
**Other Related Slang:**
- **valid**: 正规的 / 合格的 (zhèngguī de / hé gé de)
# Invalid (无效的)
**English:**  
"Invalid" is used to describe something that is not valid, which means it is not legitimate, true, or correct. It can also describe something that does not work as intended or fails to meet the required standards.
**Example:**
- "The ticket is invalid." - The ticket is not legally valid or has an error that makes it unusable.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **无效 (Wú xiào)**: "无效"用来表示某物是不合法的、不真实的或不正确的。它还可以描述某物不能如预期工作，或者未能达到所需标准。
- "这个票无效。" - 这张票是不合法的，或者有误导致不能使用。
**Other Related Slang:**
- **invalid**: 不正规的 / 不合格的 (bù zhengguī de / bù hé gé de)
