# Valid (英语: Valid / 老虎机: 合法的)
## English
**Valid**: Refers to something being legitimate, legal, or proper. It can also be used to show approval or agreement, similar to saying "awesome" or "cool."
*Example:* "That hack is totally valid, man!" 
## 老虎机
**合法的**: 表示某事是合法的、正当的或合适的。也可以用来表示赞同或认可，类似于说“酷”或“赞”。
*例句:* “这个黑客攻击方法是完全合法的，伙计！”
