# Valid
## English
- **Definition**: Something or someone that is considered genuine, correct, or acceptable.
- **Usage**: "His credentials are all valid, so there's no reason to doubt his abilities."
## Chinese
- **释义**：指被认为真实、正确或被接受的事物或人。
- **用法**："他的资历全是valid的，所以就没理由怀疑他的能力。"
