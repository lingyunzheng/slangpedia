# Valid [英/ˈvædɪd/]
## English Explanation
"Valid" can be a slang term used to mean something is correct, true, or logical. Alternatively, it can express agreement or approval, similar to saying "cool" or "good."
## Chinese Explanation
“valid” 可以作为俚语使用，表示某事是正确的、真实的或合乎逻辑的。此外，这个词还可以用来表达同意或批准，类似地说“酷”或“好”。
Example Usage:
- English: That plan seems valid to me.
- Chinese: 这个计划对我来说是有效的。
- English: This is totally valid!
- Chinese: 这完全有效/正确！
