## Valid
### English
- **Definition**: Something that is true, correct, or authentic.
- **Slang Explanation**: In casual or street slang, "valid" can sometimes be used to emphasize that something is correct, true, or makes sense. It can also be used to support or validate a point of view.
### 中文
- **定义**：是真实的，正确的，或者真实的。
- ** slang 解释**：在非正式或街头用语中，“valid”有时用来强调某事是真的、正确的，或有道理。它也可以用来支持某个观点。
