# "valid" in Slang
## English
**Valid** - Often used to mean "legit" or "real" in casual contexts. It can also imply something is correct or lawful.
## Chinese (普通话)
**valid** - 经常用于表示“靠谱的”或“真的”这类含义，在非正式场合使用。它也可以暗示某事是正确的或合法的。
### Example Usage
- English: "This discount code is valid until the end of the month."
- Chinese: "这个折扣码到月底之前是valid的。"
- English: "Your explanation is valid, but I have a counter-argument."
- Chinese: "你的解释是valid的，但我有一个反论点。"
- English: "That's a valid point, but I still disagree."
- Chinese: "那是一个valid的观点，但我仍然不同意。"
