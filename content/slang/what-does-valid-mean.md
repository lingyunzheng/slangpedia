# Valid
## English
**Meaning**: Something that is genuine, authentic, or correct.
**Example Usage**: "That's totally valid point! I never thought about it that way."
## 中文
**意思**: 真实的、确实的或正确的。
**用法示例**："这真是一个非常合理的观点！我以前没想到这一点。"
