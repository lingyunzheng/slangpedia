### 🇺🇸 English
**Valid**: (Short for "valid point" or "valid argument") - Refers to something that makes sense or is reasonable based on the facts or logic presented. 
*Example: "He raised a valid point about the project timeline."*
### 🇨🇳 中文
**valid**：（来源于“valid point”或“valid argument”）指基于事实或逻辑显得合理或有意义的观点。
*例句：他提出的关于项目时间线的观点是合理的。*
