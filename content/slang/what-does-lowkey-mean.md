## Lowkey
### English
- **Definition**: A casual way to say "low key" or "somewhat" to indicate that something is not exactly obvious or is being kept subtle.
- **Example**: "I lowkey hate this playlist, but I won't say anything."
### 中文
- **释义**：一种非正式的说法，类似于 "low key" 或 "有点儿"，用来表示某件事并不是非常明显或保持低调。
- **例句**：我不太喜欢这首播放列表，但我不准备说出来。
