## Lowkey
**English:** 
A slang term used to indicate that something is happening in a low-key or subtle manner. Often used to downplay the significance of an event or action.
**Chinese:**
一个用于表示某事以低调或不显眼的方式发生的小词汇。通常用于淡化事情的重要性或行动的规模。
Example Usage:
- English: He's lowkey crushing on her, but he won't admit it.
- Chinese: 他其实对她有点意思，但是一直低调着，不愿意承认。
