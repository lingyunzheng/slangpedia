## lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is a slang term used to describe something in a quiet or subtle way. It emphasizes that something is not particularly obvious or openly discussed; it's more on the low-key (low-key) side.
**Example:**  
"I'm lowkey in love with this song."  
This means the speaker is deeply fond of the song, but not in a way that's being loudly or boastfully.
### 中文
“Lowkey”是一个俚语，用来描述一种较为低调或细微的情况。它强调某事并不是特别明显或公开讨论的；这是一种较为低调（lowkey）的方式。
**例句：**  
"我低key深爱这首歌。"  
这意味着说话者非常喜欢这首歌，但这种喜欢并不是大声或夸耀的方式表达的。
