## lowkey
**English:**  
A casual way to say "low key," which means someone or something is not publicly known or not heavily emphasized. 
**Chinese:**  
"低声低调"（dīshēng dìzhào）或"低调地"（diàoɡào de）。用来形容某人或某事没有公开地引起注意，或者被轻描淡写地说的情况。
示例：  
English: I lowkey love this movie, but I never tell anyone.  
Chinese: 我低声低调地喜欢这部电影，但从不告诉别人。
