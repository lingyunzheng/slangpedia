## Lowkey
### English Explanation
"Lowkey" is a slang term used to describe something that is done or known to a limited extent, or is not openly or strongly emphasized. It often implies something is happening subtly or quietly, rather than overtly or loudly.
### Chinese Explanation
"Lowkey" 是一种俚语，用来描述一些仅限于小范围内做的事情，或者不是很公开或强烈强调的事情。这个词通常暗示某事是低调或默默进行的，而不是公开或大声进行的。
### Usage Examples
1. **English**: I lowkey can't believe she got the promotion.
**Chinese**: 我低key不敢相信她升职了。
2. **English**: He's lowkey pretty obsessed with that new game.
**Chinese**: 他低key对那款新游戏有点上瘾。
