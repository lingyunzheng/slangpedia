```markdown
## Lowkey
- **English**: A slang term used to indicate that something is known or considered to a limited extent, often in a hushed or casual manner. It can also be used to suggest something is happening less frequently or intensely than how it might be perceived otherwise.
- **Chinese**: "低音key" 或 "低调key"，用于表示某事知道或被认为是有限度的，通常以低语或随意的方式。也可以用来暗示某事发生得不如外界认为的那么频繁或强烈。
```
This markdown format provides a concise and clear explanation of the term "lowkey" in both English and Chinese.
