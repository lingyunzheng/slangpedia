# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Lowkey"
## English Explanation
"Lowkey" is an informal adverb used to indicate that something is happening or being said to a limited extent or in a subdued manner. Originally from the mid-2000s, this term is often used to describe something that is not a big deal or barely noticeable, but still true or important.
**Example:**
- "I'm lowkey excited for the concert tonight."
- Meaning: "I'm a bit excited for the concert tonight, but not overly enthusiastic."
## Chinese Explanation
"Lowkey"（低调地）是用来表示某事是轻描淡写地发生或被提及的一种非正式的副词。这个词汇原本起源于2000年代中期，通常用于描述某事虽然不大，但仍然真实或重要的情况。
**例如：**
- "我低key兴奋今晚的演唱会。"
- 含义："我对今晚的演唱会有点兴奋，但并不是非常热情。"
