# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "lowkey"
**English:**
- **Meaning:** Informally, "lowkey" is used to hint at something in a subtle or understated way. It suggests that something is noticeable but not extremely obvious.
- **Example:** "She's lowkey flipping out about the new cat, but nobody can tell."
- **Colloquial Usage:** Often used in casual conversations to share private or overlooked information.
**Chinese:**
- **含义:** "lowkey" 用于以一种微妙或低调的方式表示某事。它暗示某件事情是可察觉的，但并不显而易见。
- **例句:** "她对新来的猫有点低吼，但没人能看出来。"
- **口语用法:** 常常用于 casual 交谈中，分享一些私人或未被注意的信息。
