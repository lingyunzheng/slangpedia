## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Lowkey"
**English:**  
"Lowkey" is an informal adjective that indicates something is subtle, mild, or done in a subdued manner. It can also be used to convey that something happened infrequently or not widely known.
**Chinese:**  
"Lowkey" 是一个非正式的形容词，用于表示某事是隐秘的、轻微的或以低调方式进行的。它还可以用来表示某事发生得很少或不为人广泛知悉。
Example Sentence:
**English:**  
He's lowkey crushing on her, but he's too shy to admit it.
**Chinese:**  
他低 key 在暗恋她，但他太害羞了还没敢承认。
Example Sentence:
**English:**  
The new restaurant is lowkey爆火, but I only found out from social media.
**Chinese:**  
新餐馆低 key 爆火，但我只是从社交媒体上才得知。
