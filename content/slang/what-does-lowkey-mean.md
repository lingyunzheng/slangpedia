## lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is a slang term used to describe something that is secretly or quietly prominent or noticeable. It implies something that might not be overt or explicitly acknowledged, but is still important or impactful nonetheless.
### Chinese
"Lowkey" 是一个俚语，用来形容某事虽然不是公开或明显突出的，但却是秘密地或安静地重要或引人注意的。它暗示了一些事情可能是不明显的，但仍然在幕后起着重要作用。
例句:
- English: I saw him following me lowkey.
- Chinese: 我发现他在低声（低声地）悄悄跟着我。
- English: She's lowkey the most talented one in the group.
- Chinese: 她其实是这个团队中最 talented 的，不过并没有明显表现出来。（注：此处意译，因为“talented”最好用“有才华的”来对应）
