## Lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is a colloquial term used to describe something that is known by only a few people or is not widely publicized. It often conveys a sense of secrecy or a low-stakes, minor level of something.
### Chinese
"Lowkey" 是一个俚语，用于描述只有少数人知道的事情，或者不是广泛公开的。它通常传达的是一种秘密感或事情的重要性较低、规模较小的意思。
