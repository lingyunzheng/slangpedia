##低key (Lowkey)
### English Explanation
"Lowkey" is an informal term that is used to indicate something happening in a quiet or low-profile manner. It implies that something is important to the speaker or the person they are addressing, but not to a wider audience. It can also be used to denote something that is happening subtly or in secret.
### Example Usage
- "Lowkey feeling stressed about the tests, but won't admit it."
- "Friends lowkey planning a surprise party for him, I think."
### Chinese Explanation
"低key (lowkey)" 是一个非正式的词汇，用于表示某事物以低调或非公开的方式发生。它意味着你说的话或你想强调的事情对你说或对方很重要，但对更广泛的人群来说并不重要。此外，它也可以用来描述某种事情是以微妙或秘密的方式进行的。
### Example Usage
- "低key感觉考试压力很大，但不愿承认。"
- "朋友们低key在计划一个惊喜派对，我觉得是这样。"
