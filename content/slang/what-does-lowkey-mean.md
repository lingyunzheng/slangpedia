# Lowkey
**English:**  
"Lowkey" is an informal adjective used to indicate that something is known or important, but not to a great extent or not widely. It often implies that the action, person, or thing being discussed is subtle, small, or not the main focus. For example, if someone says, "Lowkey addicted to coffee," it means they are somewhat addicted to coffee but not completely to the extent that it defines their life.
**Chinese:**  
"Lowkey" 是一个非正式的形容词，用于表示某件事情虽然被知道或认为重要，但程度不大或者不广为人知。它通常暗示要讨论的人、事或物是微妙的、小规模的或不是主要焦点。例如，如果有人说“低配咖啡沉迷者”，意思是他们对咖啡的依赖确实存在，但程度不大，不足以完全定义他们的生活。
