# lowkey
## English
"Lowkey" is a casual slang term that suggests an action or feeling is somewhat or only mildly significant. It is often used to downplay the importance or extent of something.
## Chinese
"Lowkey" 是一种休闲俚语，用来表示某个动作或感觉是比较轻微或只是稍微有意义的。它通常用于淡化某事的重要性或程度。
Example Usage:
- "I lowkey want to try that new restaurant." 意为 "我其实想去试试那家新餐馆。"
- "She lowkey said she was fine, but I could tell she was upset." 意为 "她其实说她没事，但我能看出她是 upset 的。"
