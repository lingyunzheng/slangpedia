# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Lowkey"
## English
- **Definition**: Used to describe something that is known or done by only a few people, usually in a discreet way.
- **Example**: "I was lowkey surprised by the party they had at home last night."
## Chinese
- **定义**：用于描述只有少数人知晓或做的事情，通常是以一种不引人注目的方式。
- **例句**：昨晚他们在家里开 party，我有点低key地感到惊讶。
