## lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is a casual term used to emphasize that something is known by a few or is known (and often debated) in a subtle or low-profile manner. The term is generally used to suggest something is not widely publicized or is a bit of a secret.
**Example Usage**: "I lowkey hate when people eat with their phones out."
### 中文
"Lowkey" 是一个非正式的常用词，用来强调某事只有少数人知道，或者是在很低调和不显著的情况下被知晓。这个词通常用来暗示某事没有广泛公开，或者是一个小秘密。
**例句**："我低key讨厌别人吃饭时拿着手机。"
