## Lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is a slang term often used to modify a verb or adjective to indicate that something is done subtly, secretly, or to a lesser extent. It emphasizes that a particular action or condition is not overt or widely known.
### Chinese
"Lowkey" 是一种俚语，通常用来修饰动词或形容词，表示某件事是悄悄地、秘密地或较少地进行的。它强调某项动作或状态并不是公开或众所周知的。
