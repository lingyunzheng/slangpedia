# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Lowkey"
## English
**Definition:** 
- **Formal:** Somewhat, to a certain extent, mildly.
- **Informal:** Low-key, usually used to describe actions or statements that are not intended to draw much attention or seem very casual.
**Usage Example:**
- "I saw Jane at the coffee shop. Lowkey, she looked tired."
## 中文
**释义：**
- **正式:** 稍微，某种程度上，较轻微。
- **非正式:** 低(key)调，通常用来形容一些动作或言论不是有意引起注意的，显得非常随意。
**使用示例：**
- “我在咖啡馆遇到了简。低(key)调地说，她看起来挺累的。”
