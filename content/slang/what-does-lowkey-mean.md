## Lowkey
### English
*Definition*: A slang term used to indicate that someone or something is likely or probable, but without a strong or certain conviction. It conveys a sense of moderation or hesitation.
*Example*: "Lowkey feeling tired today, but I still might go to the gym if I'm not too tired."
### 中文
*定义*: 一个俚语，用于表示某人或某事很有可能或大概会怎样，但没有强烈的或确定的信念。它传达了一种中等程度的肯定或犹豫的意思。
*例句*: "低keyboard感觉今天有点累，但如果没有太累的话，我还是可能会去健身房的。"
