##低key (Lowkey)
**English Explanation:**  
"Lowkey" is a slang term used to imply something in a subtle or understated way. It suggests that something happened or is true, but with a level of moderation or without much fanfare. It's often used to emphasize that something is known or true by not many people or in a discreet manner.
**Example Usage:**
- "Lowkey I saw him at the mall yesterday." (I saw him at the mall yesterday, but not in a big deal way.)
- "Lowkey he's been into that for a while." (He's been into that for a while, but it didn't get much attention.)

##低key (Lowkey)
**汉语解释：**  
"低key" 是一个俚语，用来暗示某事以一种微妙或低调的方式。它意味着某事发生了或是真的，但没有太夸张或盛大庆祝。通常用来强调某事只被少数人知道或是在低调的情况下发生的。
**用法示例：**
- "低key 我昨天在商场见着他了。" (我昨天在商场见到他了，但并没有大肆宣扬。)
- "低key 他对此已经沉迷很久了。" (他对此已经很沉迷了，但这并没有引起太大注意。)
