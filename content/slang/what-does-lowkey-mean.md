## Lowkey
### English
"Lowkey" is an informal adjective used to convey that something is just moderately or barely noticeable or significant. It can also indicate that a speaker is speaking in a low voice or being secretive about something.
### Simplified Chinese
“Lowkey”是一个非正式的形容词，用来表示某件事情只是轻微或几乎不明显。也可以表示说话者低声细语或对某事保密。
### Example Usage
- **English:** I lowkey think you’re a genius.
- **Chinese:** 我其实觉得你挺有天分的。
