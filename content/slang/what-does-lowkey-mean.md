## Lowkey
**English:**  
"Lowkey" is a casual way to say that something is happening to a minor extent or that someone is only slightly involved. It suggests something happening in a subtle or low-profile manner.
**Chinese:**  
"低key"（dī kē）通常用来表示某件事情发生的程度不大，或者某人只是轻微地卷入。它暗示某事是以一种低调或不显眼的方式发生的。
