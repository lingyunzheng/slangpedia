# What does "lowkey" mean?（lowkey 是什么意思？）

**English Meaning:**  
"Lowkey" is a casual slang term often used to signify that something is subtle, quiet, or not overtly obvious.

**中文解释：**  
"低key" 是一种随意的俚语，通常用来表示某事是微妙的、安静的，或非常明显。
