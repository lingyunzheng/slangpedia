```markdown
##低key (Lowkey)
**English Explanation:** "Lowkey" is often used to describe something that is minor, not extremely important, or known to only a small group of people.
**Chinese Explanation:** "低key" 通常用来形容某件事情比较轻微、不怎么重要，或者是只有小范围的人知道。
*Example Usage:*
- English: I got a lowkey nervous before my speech.
- Chinese: 我在演讲前低key有点紧张。
```
This setup helps clarify the meaning of "lowkey" in both English and Chinese, providing a contextual example for better understanding.
