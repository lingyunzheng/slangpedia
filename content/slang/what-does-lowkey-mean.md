# Lowkey
## English
"Lowkey" is a casual slang term that implies something is happening to an extent that is somewhat noticeable but not overtly or loudly. It's often used to describe situations or feelings that are not at the forefront of a conversation or experience.
- **Example Usage:** "I'm lowkey excited for the concert tomorrow." (I'm somewhat excited, but I might not be shouting it from the rooftops.)
## 中文
"低key"（lowkey）是一种非正式的俚语，用来表示某件事发生的程度在一定程度上可以被察觉，但不是非常明显或大声的。它通常用于描述在对话或经历中并不占主导地位的情况或感受。
- **使用示例：** "我低key期待着明天的演唱会。"（我有点期待，但可能不会大声宣告这件事情。）
