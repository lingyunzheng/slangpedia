###低key (Lowkey)
- **Definition**: 低key可以翻译为"lowkey"，意指低调但也能让人感受到，有时候带有一丝无奈或羡慕的感情色彩。
- **Usage**: 常用来形容那些不张扬but实际很厉害的人或事物。例如，朋友小李学习特别努力，但从来不炫耀，这就是一个lowkey的例子。
- **Example**: 
- 他考试成绩很棒，但lowkey的只告诉了我和另外一个人。（He got a great score in the exam, but he lowkey only told me and one more person.）
### Lowkey
- **Definition**: Lowkey means being low in volume or not drawing attention, but still noticeable. It often carries a tone of understatement or a subtle hint of something being better or cooler than meets the eye.
- **Usage**: Typically used when talking about someone or something that is impressive but doesn't get a lot of attention or pride themselves on that. For instance, a friend might work hard on a project but doesn't show off about it.
- **Example**:
- She did really well on the test, but lowkey only told me and one other person. (She did really well on the test, but lowkey only told me and one other person.)
