# Slang Term: "Lowkey"
## English Explanation
**Lowkey**: This slang term is a hybrid that combines "low" and "key." It is used to indicate something is happening to a small or limited extent, or to a degree that might not be readily obvious. It often implies something that's happening in the background or behind the scenes, rather than being the main focus.
**Example**: "He's lowkey studying for his exams while hanging out with friends." This means he's studying, but not working on it exclusively; he might be doing it occasionally or in small bursts.
## Chinese Explanation
**低key (Dī Key)**: 这个俚语结合了“低”和“key”两个词，用来表示某事正在以较小的程度或范围发生，或者这种程度可能不容易被明显察觉。它可以暗示某事正在幕后或在后台进行，而未必是主要关注点。
**示例**：他在和朋友玩的同时低key在学习。这句话的意思是他确实有在学习，但可能不是全身心投入；他可能只是间歇性地学习或者时间并不长。
