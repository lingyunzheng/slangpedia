# Bilingual Slang Explanation: Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing:** Refers to engaging in activities or tasks outside the main objectives or plots of a game, often to gain rewards like items, experience points, or other benefits. This term is commonly used in video games but can also be applied to other contexts.
## Chinese
**边角料任务 (Biānjīnglèi Wèishì):** 指在游戏中完成主任务之外的额外任务，为了获得奖励（如物品、经验值等）。这个词通常用于视频游戏，但在其他情境中也可以适用。
