## Side Questing
### English
**Definition**: Side questing refers to the act of engaging in activities that are not the primary objective of a game or task, but are often available to the player. These activities can include exploration, minor quests, or other side missions that serve to enhance the gameplay experience.
### Chinese
**定义**: Side questing 指的是在一个游戏或任务中进行那些不是主要目标但经常可供玩家完成的活动。这些活动可能包括探索、小任务或其他次要任务，旨在丰富游戏体验。
