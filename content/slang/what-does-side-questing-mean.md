## Side Questing
**English:**  
Side questing refers to undertaking additional, non-essential tasks or activities in a game or project. While not necessary for the main objective, side quests often offer rewards such as experience points, money, or items that can contribute to improving the player's character or overall progress in the game. These quests can also provide alternative storylines, allowing for a fuller experience.
**Chinese:**  
“边角料任务”或“支线任务” （边角求）指的是在游戏或项目中，除了主要目标之外，你还可以接受的额外任务。这些任务不是必须完成的主要目标的一部分，但通常能提供经验点、金钱或道具等奖励，有助于提升角色的能力或促进整体进度。支线任务还能提供额外的故事线，使游戏体验更加丰富。
