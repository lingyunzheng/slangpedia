## Side Questing
### English Explanation
**Side Questing**: In the context of video games, a **side quest** refers to less central or less critical quests that players can undertake alongside the main storyline. These quests are usually optional but might reward players with additional loot, experience, or story elements. In a broader non-gaming context, **side questing** can mean pursuing an auxiliary or secondary activity that complements or distracts from the primary goal.
### Chinese Explanation
**支线任务 (Xiǎnzhì jiǎnyuán)**：在游戏的语境中，支线任务是指那些不那么核心或不那么重要的任务，玩家可以在主线剧情之外选择完成。这些任务通常是可选的，但可能会给予玩家更多的装备、经验和故事背景。在更宽泛的非游戏语境中，**支线任务**可以被理解为辅助的或次要的活动，这些活动可以补充或从侧面支持主要的活动目标。
