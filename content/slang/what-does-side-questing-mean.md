# Side Questing
## English Explanation
- **Definition**: Engaging in activities or tasks that are not the main focus or requirement but can add value or fun to your experience. These are often seen in video games as additional objectives that provide rewards but are not necessary to complete the primary quest.
- **Example**: In a fantasy RPG, a player might take on a quest to find a rare flower to help a village while the main mission is to defeat a dragon. The flower quest is a side quest.
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义**: 进行不是主要任务或必要条件，但可以增加体验的价值或乐趣的行为或任务。常出现在游戏中作为额外的目标，即使完成这些任务也不会影响主线任务的推进。
- **示例**: 在一部幻想角色扮演游戏中，一个玩家可能会接受任务去寻找一种稀有的花来帮助一个村庄，而主线任务是击败一条龙。寻找花的任务就是支线任务。
