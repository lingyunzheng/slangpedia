# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing:** Engaging in tasks or activities that are not the primary objective, but offer additional benefits such as rewards, experience points, or new knowledge. These are like the fun little detours in your main journey.
Example: "After finishing the main mission, I decided to side quest and clear the nearby dungeon. It was less challenging but gave me some cool gear and new skills!"
## 中文
**侧副本:** 从事主要目标之外的任务或活动，这些任务或活动会带来额外的好处，如奖励、经验和新的知识。它们就像是你在主线征程中的一些有趣小插曲。
例句：“完成了主线任务后，我决定侧副本，去附近的一个迷宫探险。虽然挑战性不大，但获得了些不错的装备和新技能！”
