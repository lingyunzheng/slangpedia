# Side Questing (English/Chinese)
## English
**Side Questing:** Engaging in extra, optional activities or missions that often provide additional rewards beyond the main storyline. It's like doing fun, little side adventures that can help you level up faster or get some cool loot you need.
## Chinese
**支线任务（Side Questing）:** 进行额外的、可选的任务或活动，通常提供除了主线剧情之外的额外奖励。它就像是做一些有趣的小冒险，能帮助你更快升级或者获得你所需要的稀有装备。
