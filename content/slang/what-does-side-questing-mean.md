```markdown
## Side Questing
**中文解释:** 
侧任务或支线任务，通常指的是游戏中除了主线任务之外的其他任务。玩家可以选择接受这些任务来获取额外的奖励或探索游戏世界。
**English Explanation:** 
Side questing refers to taking on tasks or quests that are not part of the main storyline in a game. These side quests allow players to explore the game world further and often provide additional rewards.
```
This markdown format clearly separates the English and Chinese explanations and makes it easy for readers to understand the concept of "side questing" in both languages.
