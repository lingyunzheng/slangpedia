### Side Questing
**English:**  
- **Definition:** Engaging in activities or tasks that are not the primary goal or main focus of your time, but are often rewarding and fun.
- **Example:** "I decided to take on a side quest by planning a surprise party for my friend, even though my main task for the day was to finish a project report."
**Chinese:**  
- **释义:** 指参与那些不是主要目标或主要任务的额外活动或任务，这些活动通常是为了增加乐趣或提供额外奖励。
- **示例:** “我决定通过计划一场惊喜派对来完成我的朋友的‘支线任务’，即使我的主要任务是完成一份项目报告。”
