## Side Questing (Side Questing)
### English:
**Side Questing:** When players in video games decide to take on extra, optional missions or challenges in addition to the main storyline. It's like doing the fun, extra tasks in your daily routine that aren't strictly necessary but add excitement and rewards.
### 中文:
**刷支线任务（Side Questing）：** 在玩电子游戏时，玩家选择完成额外的、非主线剧情的任务或挑战。就像是日常生活中做的那些不完全是必要的，但却增加了乐趣和奖励的小任务。
