## Side Questing
**English**: 
"Side questing" is a term borrowed from video games, where it refers to taking on additional, non-essential tasks that offer rewards or experience points. In everyday life, it can refer to engaging in activities or projects that are not the main focus but still bring value or enjoyment. For example, an office worker might take on a new hobby on the weekends, or a student might help a friend with their project as a form of side quest.
**Chinese (Simplified)**:
"Side questing" 这个词源自电子游戏，指的是完成一些额外的、非必要的任务，这些任务能够带来奖励或经验值。在日常生活中，它可以指从事一些不是主要目标但仍然带来价值或乐趣的活动或项目。例如，一个办公室工作人员可能在周末培养新的爱好，或者一个学生帮助朋友完成项目。
