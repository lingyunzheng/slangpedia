# Bilingual Slang Explanation: Side Questing
**English:**  
- **Definition:** Engaging in activities, typically in a video game, that are not part of the main storyline but offer additional rewards.
- **Example:** In the realm of gaming, players often take on side quests to gain experience points, collect items, or meet non-playable characters (NPCs) for story or lore.
**Chinese:**  
- **定义:** 在游戏或其他活动中参与非主线任务的活动，这些活动通常会提供额外的奖励。
- **例子:** 在游戏界，玩家常常会完成 SIDES 任务来获得经验值、收集物品，或者为了了解更多的故事和背景故事情节而与非玩家角色 (NPC) 交流。
