## Side Questing
### English
**Definition:** Side questing refers to undertaking additional, smaller tasks or adventures outside of the main story or plot in a game or narrative. These quests often provide extra rewards, experience points, or a deeper understanding of the world but aren't mandatory to progress.
### Chinese
**定义：** 边角任务（侧任务）是指在游戏中或叙事中除了主线剧情之外，进行的一些额外的小任务或探险。这些支线任务通常会提供额外的奖励、经验值或对世界的更深入理解，但不是强制性的，不进行这些任务也不会影响主线的进展。
