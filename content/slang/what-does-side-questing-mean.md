## Side Questing
### English
**Side Questing**: Engaging in tasks or activities that are not the main focus or objective of a game, project, or task but can still be fun and rewarding. In video games, for example, side quests are optional missions that often provide experience points, items, or storyline insights. It's like getting side orders at a restaurant - they're not strictly necessary, but they enhance the overall experience.
### 中文
**侧翼任务(side questing)**：完成那些不是主要任务或目标, 但仍然有趣且能带来奖励的任务或活动。在视频游戏中，侧翼任务通常是可选的额外任务，可以提供经验值、物品或剧情线索。就像在餐馆里点一些加餐——它们并非必须，但能使整体体验更丰富。
