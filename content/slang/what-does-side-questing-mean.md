## Side Questing
### English
**Definition**: Engaging in activities or tasks that are not the main objective but add depth or variety to a game or life experience.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation**:
* English: I just finished a side quest that gave me some extra gold and experience points!
* Chinese: 刚我才完成了一个支线任务，多赚了不少金币和经验值！(Gāng wǒ cái wánchéng le yī gè yánzhìwùzhī, duō zhǎo bù shǎo jīn biǎo hé xīn zhí diǎn！)
### Example Usage
* English: “I spent an hour on side questing, but it was totally worth it.” 
* Chinese: “我花了一个小时做支线任务，但是完全值得。” (Wǒ huā le yī gè xiǎoshī zuò yánzhìwùzhī, dànshì quán zuò zhídé.)
### Related Terms
* **Main Quest**: 主线任务 (Zhǔxiàn rènwù)
* **Grind**: 打怪/升级 (Dǎguài/ shèngjiǔ)
