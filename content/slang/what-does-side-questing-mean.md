# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing**: In video game jargon, "side questing" refers to undertaking optional missions or activities that aren't part of the main storyline. Players often embark on these quests to earn extra resources, experience points, or to explore the game world more fully. It's like getting a break from the main storyline to do some fun, smaller tasks.
## 中文
**边角任务 (Biānjiǎo Wénjùn)**：在电子游戏术语中，“边角任务”指的是那些不属于主线剧情的可选任务或活动。玩家通常会接受这些任务来赚取额外的资源、经验值，或者更全面地探索游戏世界。它就像是在主线剧情之外做一些有趣的小任务的休息时间。
