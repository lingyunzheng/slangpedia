## Side Questing (English/Side Questing (Chinese))
### English
"Side questing" is a playful term often used in the context of video games, but it can also apply to real life or other activities. It refers to engaging in smaller, often unrelated, objectives or tasks that are not the main goal. These might be additional missions or activities that can potentially lead to unique rewards, insights, or just added enjoyment.
### Chinese
“侧questing”（Side Questing）出自于电子游戏领域，但也适用于现实生活或其他活动中。它指的是参与一些较小的、通常是与主要目标不直接相关的任务或活动。这些任务或活动可能带来独特的奖励、见解，或者仅仅是增加了乐趣。
Example Usage:
- In games: "I've been side questing for a while, collecting various items and exploring random areas."
- In real life: "She enjoys side questing by volunteering at local charities and attending arts workshops to diversify her experience."
