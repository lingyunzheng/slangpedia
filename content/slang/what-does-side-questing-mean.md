## Side Questing
**English:**  
Side questing refers to engaging in tasks or activities that are not the main goals or objectives of a game or project but are often rewarding nonetheless. In video games, side quests are missions that players can undertake alongside the main storyline, providing extra experience points, items, or story development.
**Simplified Chinese:**  
边任务（Side Questing）指的是在游戏中参与那些不是主要目标或任务但通常也非常有奖励的内容。在视频游戏中，边任务是指除了主线剧情之外的任务，玩家可以选择完成这些任务以获得额外的经验值、物品或是故事上的发展。
**Example Usage:**  
- In the game, players can go on a side quest to hunt for a rare mythical beast, which is not required to complete the main quest but offers invaluable rewards.
- 举例来说，在游戏中，玩家可以去执行一个寻找稀有神话生物的边任务，这不是完成主线任务所必需的，但提供了极其有价值的奖励。
