## Side Questing
**English:**  
"Side questing" refers to engaging in activities that are not the main goal or primary objective of a task or game, but are typically smaller goals or tasks that can offer rewards or additional benefits. In the context of video games, side quests are often side adventures or missions that players can undertake outside the main storyline. In a broader sense, it can also refer to taking on additional work or smaller projects to supplement one’s main job or primary task.
**Chinese (汉语简体):**  
“侧线任务”指的是那些不是主要任务或目标的核心活动，但通常是一些较小的任务或目标，可以提供奖励或额外的好处。在视频游戏的背景下，逸出主线的任务通常是指那些玩家可以在主剧情之外进行的小冒险或任务。在更广泛的意义上，这也可能指除主要工作之外还承担一些额外的工作或小项目。
