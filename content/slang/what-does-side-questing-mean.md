## Side Questing
### English
**Definition:** Engaging in activities or tasks outside of the main storyline or objective, often for additional rewards or for character development.
**Example:** In a role-playing game, players might choose to take on side quests to gather rare materials or to earn extra gold and experience points.
### Chinese
**定义:** 指参与主故事情节之外的任务或活动，通常是为了获得额外奖励或提升角色实力。
**示例:** 在一款角色扮演游戏里，玩家可能会选择完成支线任务来收集稀有材料或额外赚取金币和经验值。
