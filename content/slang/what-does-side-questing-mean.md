# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing**: This is a term often used in the context of video games. It refers to taking on minor, optional missions that aren't part of the main storyline. Players do these to earn extra rewards or to explore more of the game world. Side quests can often be tackled in between the main tasks or for side missions that promise exciting rewards like rare items or hidden secrets.
## 中文
**side questing**: 在玩电子游戏中，这个词通常用来描述那些不属于主线剧情的次要任务。玩家会在完成主线任务的过程中，选择接取这些可选的支线任务来获取额外奖励或探索更多游戏世界的内容。支线任务可能在主任务之间完成，或者是为了获得稀有道具、隐藏秘密等精彩奖励。
