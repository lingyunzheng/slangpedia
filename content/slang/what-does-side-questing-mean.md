## Side Questing
- **English**: Side questing is a term often used in video games, but it can also be applied in various contexts. When you engage in side quests, you're taking on additional, non-essential tasks that usually offer rewards like experience points, items, or money, beyond the main story. It's like exploring the smaller side streets in a city while the main attraction is the central avenue.
- **Chinese**: "侧任务"（side questing）这个词常见于游戏中，但也适用于各种情境。当你投身于侧任务时，你就是在完成那些非主线任务，这些任务通常会提供额外奖励，比如经验值、物品或金钱，而不仅仅是遵循主要剧情。这就像在探索城市的次要街道，而主要的亮点是市中心的主线故事。
