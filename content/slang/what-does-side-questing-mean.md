## Side Questing
**English:** 
- *Literal Meaning:* Engaging in activities that are not the main goal but contribute to the overall story or experience.
- *Slang Explanation:* Doing "side jobs" or side tasks that aren't the main mission but are still important and can help you level up or gain resources. Think of it like doing errands or side missions in video games that offer rewards and make your adventure richer.
**中文:**
- *字面意思:* 不是主要目标但却贡献于整体故事或经历的活动。
- *俚语解释:* 做“副业”或次要任务，这些任务不是主要任务，但同样重要，还能帮助提升能力和获得资源。就好比在电子游戏中完成一些支线任务，它们能提供奖励并使你的冒险更丰富多彩。
