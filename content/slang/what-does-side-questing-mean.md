# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing**: In the context of video games, side quests refer to additional tasks or missions that are not part of the main storyline. Players usually undertake these quests to earn extra rewards, experience, or to uncover new areas. It's like doing the smaller, often optional tasks to satisfy your curiosity or to maximize your in-game benefits. In a broader sense, it can also refer to taking on smaller, supporting tasks or activities in any large project or activity.
## Chinese
**侧翼任务 (cè yì rèn wù)**: 在视频游戏的语境中，侧翼任务指的是不属于主线剧情的额外任务或使命。玩家通常会完成这些任务来获取额外的奖励、经验值，或者探索新区域。这就像是完成一些较小的、通常是可以选择完成的任务来满足自己的好奇心或最大化游戏内的收益。更广泛地说，它也可以指在任何大型项目或活动中承担较小的支持任务或活动。
