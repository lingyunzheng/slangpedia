## Side Questing (Simplified Chinese: 副本刷图; Traditional Chinese: 副本刷圖)
### English: 
Side Questing refers to the activity of engaging in minor or secondary objectives within a game, often to gain experience points (XP), items, or currency. These side quests are usually less important than main story quests but are necessary to enhance the player's character and level.
### 中文解释:
Side Questing 指在游戏里完成一些次要或附属任务的行为，通常是为了获得经验、物品或货币。这些支线任务一般比主线剧情轻，但对提升角色等级和能力是必需的。
