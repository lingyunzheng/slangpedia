## Side Questing
**English Slang Explanation:**  
"Side questing" is a term commonly used in the context of video games, but it has also become a popular phrase in everyday life. It refers to taking on minor or non-critical tasks or activities that are separate from the main goal or objective. In gaming, side quests are usually optional missions that offer additional rewards or storyline content, but are not necessary for completing the game. In other contexts, it can mean working on small projects or tasks that are interesting but not essential to the main project or goal.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**  
"Side questing" 在中文 context 中，主要是从游戏文化中借用的一个术语。它指的是那些与主要目标或任务无关的小任务或活动。在游戏中，side quests 是指那些可以完成也可以不完成的支线任务，它们提供了额外的游戏内容和奖励。而在日常生活中， side questing 可以类比为完成了额外的小项目或是对主要目标帮助不是很大的小任务。这些任务虽然不能直接帮助你达到最终目标，但可能让你的日常生活更加有趣或充实。
**Example Usage:**
- *English:* "I decided to side quest and do some minor house repairs just for fun."
- *Chinese:* "我决定在空闲时间做一些小维修，当作是我生活中的 side quests。"
