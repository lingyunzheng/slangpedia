# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing:** In the context of video games, "side questing" refers to the pursuit of the numerous smaller missions, tasks, or objectives that are often available outside of the main storyline. These quests typically offer rewards such as experience points, money, and sometimes unique items or storyline hints. Players often undertake these side quests to earn additional benefits or simply out of interest in exploring the game world. In a broader sense, "side questing" can also be used to describe any off-the-beaten-path activity that deviates from the primary goal.
## Chinese
**边角料任务（Side Questing）：** 在视频游戏中，“边角料任务”指的是那些不隶属于主线剧情的众多小任务或目标。这些支线任务通常提供经验值、金钱，有时还有独特的物品或剧情提示。玩家通常会去完成这些支线任务以获得额外的奖励，也可能仅仅是因为对探索游戏世界感兴趣。更广泛来说，“边角料任务”也可以指从主要目标上稍微偏离的任何活动。
