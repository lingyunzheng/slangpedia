## Side Questing
**English Explanation:**
"Sidequesting" refers to the practice of taking on additional, often optional, tasks or activities that are not part of the main goal or objective. In the context of video games, it involves completing side missions or quests that can provide players with rewards, such as experience points, items, or storyline depth. In a broader sense, it can also mean taking on extra projects or tasks in other areas of life to enhance one's overall experience or achieve personal satisfaction.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文解释:** "侧 questing"指的是除了主要目标或任务之外，接手一些附加的、常常是可选的任务或活动。在游戏的背景下，这涉及完成侧任务或支线任务，这些任务可以为玩家提供奖励，比如经验值、道具或故事情节的拓展。在更广泛的意义上，它也可能意味着在生活中承担额外的项目或任务，以提升整体体验或实现个人满足感。
