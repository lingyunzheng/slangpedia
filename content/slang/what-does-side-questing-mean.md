## Side Questing (侧翼任务)
**English:**  
"Side questing" in gaming context refers to undertaking additional, often optional tasks or missions in a game, separate from the main storyline. These quests often offer rewards like experience points, currency, or new abilities. Side quests are commonly available in role-playing and action-adventure games.
**Simplified Chinese:**  
"侧翼任务"主要用于指在游戏之外进行的附加任务或使命，这些任务通常与主线剧情无关。这些任务往往提供经验值、货币或新技能等奖励。这类任务常见于角色扮演游戏和动作冒险游戏中。
**Example:**
- English: "After completing the main quest, I spent a lot of time side questing to level up my character."
- 简体中文: "完成主线任务后，我花了很多时间进行侧翼任务来提升我的角色等级。"
