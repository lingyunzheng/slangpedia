# Side Questing
## English Explanation
"Side questing" in the context of video games refers to undertaking minor, optional objectives or tasks that are not part of the main storyline but can provide players with additional rewards, such as experience points, items, or gold. These tasks often lead to interesting or humorous content and are ideal for players who want to explore more of the game world or earn rewards without following the main narrative.
## Chinese Explanation
“ SIDE QUESTING ”是指在视频游戏中进行那些非主线任务的小任务。这些任务不是游戏的主要故事情节的一部分，但通常可以为玩家提供额外的奖励，例如经验值、道具或金币。完成了这些任务，玩家不仅能探索更多的游戏世界，还能获得额外的奖励，非常适合那些不想一直跟随主线剧情的玩家。
## Bilingual Example
> **English**: "I'm side questing to find a hidden treasure chest in the forest."
> 
> **Chinese**: "我在寻找森林中隐藏的宝箱，算是完成一个小任务。"
