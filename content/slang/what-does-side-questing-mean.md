# Side Questing
## English Explanation
**Side Questing:** In a gaming context, this refers to undertaking tasks or missions that are not part of the main storyline but offer additional rewards such as experience points, items, or gold. These quests often add depth to the game world and provide side benefits to players.
## Chinese Explanation
**扫侧任务 (sǎo cè rèn wù):** 在游戏中的“扫侧任务”是指那些不属于主线剧情，但可以为玩家提供经验和物品奖励的小任务或支线任务。这些任务通常会增加游戏世界的真实感，并给玩家带来额外的好处。
### Example Usage
- **English:** I spent most of the day side questing and ended up with a lot more gold than expected!
- **Chinese:** 我把大部分时间都用来完成扫侧任务，结果获得了比预期还要多的金币！
### Related Terms
- **Main Quest (主线任务)**: The primary story-related quests that drive the plot forward.
- **Level Up (升级):** Gaining experience to increase a character's level, unlocking new abilities.
