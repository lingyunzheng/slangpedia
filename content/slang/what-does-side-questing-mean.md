## Side Questing
### English
**Definition**: Engaging in activities or tasks that are not the main focus or goal but provide rewards or additional experience points. It often refers to taking on smaller tasks that can enhance your overall progress in a game or real-life context.
**Example**: In a video game, while your main quest is to defeat the dragon, side quests might include rescuing a village, finding a hidden treasure, or training with a new weapon. These activities generally aren't necessary to complete the main quest but can provide fun and additional benefits.
### Chinese (简体中文)
**定义**: 参与不是主要目标或重点任务，但能提供奖励或额外经验点的活动或任务。通常指的是在完成主要任务的同时，承担一些更小的间任务。这些任务不一定对完成主要任务是必要的，但可以增加乐趣并提供额外的好处。
**例句**: 在一款游戏中，你的主要任务是击败巨龙，而支线任务可能包括拯救村庄、寻找隐藏宝藏或与新武器训练。这些活动通常不是完成主要任务所必需的，但可以增加乐趣并提供额外的奖励。
