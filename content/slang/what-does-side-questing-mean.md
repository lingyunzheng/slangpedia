### Side Questing (中文：接额外任务)
**English Explanation:**
Side questing refers to taking on minor or optional tasks that are not part of the main storyline in a video game, but can often be just as engaging and rewarding. These tasks often provide access to new areas, items, or experience points. It can also be used more broadly to describe someone who is taking on small, additional tasks or projects outside of their primary job or responsibility.
**Chinese Explanation:**
“接额外任务”指的是在接受视频游戏中的主线任务之外, 承接一些较小的或可选的任务。这些任务通常不直接影响游戏的主线剧情,但它们可以提供新的地图区域、物品或是经验值。这个词还可以更广泛地用于描述某人在主要职责之外承担一些小的额外任务或项目的情况。
