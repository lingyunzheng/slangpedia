# Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing:** Engaging in smaller, supplementary tasks or adventures outside of the main storyline or objective. These side quests typically offer rewards, such as experience points, items, or money, and can provide a sense of exploration and achievement. Side quests are often optional, but can significantly enhance the overall experience.
Example: In many video games, side quests are important for acquiring rare materials or completing lore-based missions that don't directly impact the main quest but add depth to the game world.
## 中文
**刷支线任务(side questing):** 在主线任务之外进行一些较小的、补充的任务或探险。这些支线任务通常提供经验值、物品或金钱等奖励，并能提供探索和成就的感觉。支线任务通常是可选的，但可以显著提升整体体验。
举个例子，在许多游戏中完成支线任务可以让你获得稀有的材料或完成有关游戏背景的故事任务，但这些任务不会直接影响主线剧情，但却能增加游戏世界的深度。
