## Side Questing
### English
**Side Questing**: Engaging in activities or missions that aren't part of the main storyline but add flavor, variety, and sometimes additional rewards to a game. It's like taking little detours on your journey that can lead to hidden treasures or just a bit of extra fun.
### 中文
**支线任务 (Side Questing)**：指那些不隶属于主线剧情的任务或活动，它们能够为游戏增添特色、多样性和额外奖励。就像在旅程中做些小的绕路，也许能找到隐秘的宝藏，或者只是增添一些额外的乐趣。
