## Side Questing (侧任务探索)
**English Slang Explanation:**
"Side questing" is a term borrowed from video games, where players venture into smaller, optional missions that often offer additional rewards and can be done on the side while they progress through the main storyline. In non-gaming contexts, it can refer to taking on additional, smaller projects or tasks that are not part of the main goal but can enhance or diversify one's experience or achievements.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
"侧任务探索"（"侧任务"意指支线任务，"探索"意为探索、寻找）源自于电子游戏中的术语，指的是玩家在精彩主线任务之外，参与一些次要任务的行为。这些任务通常不会影响主线任务的进展，但却提供了额外的好处，如经验值提升或特殊奖励。在非游戏的语境中，这个词可以用来形容那些不是主要目标、但可以增加多样性和丰富性的额外项目或任务的探索和进行过程。
