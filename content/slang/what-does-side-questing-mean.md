# Side Questing (Both English and Slang)
## English
- **Definition**: Engaging in smaller, secondary objectives or missions within a larger project or narrative, often in the context of video games, but also applicable to real-life tasks or personal projects.
## Slang
- **Definition**: Doing minor or side tasks that aren't the main focus, but add value or enhance the experience. Often used in gaming contexts or for tasks that, while not critical, are still part of a bigger picture.
- **Examples**:
- "While trying to level up my character, I decided to do some side quests to gain extra experience points."
- "I side quested to complete that side mission, even though it wasn't required, it gave me valuable resources."
## Further Explanation
In gaming, side quests are often small challenges or storylines that players can undertake as they see fit, providing additional rewards or insights. In a broader sense, "side questing" can be used to describe any action that supports or enriches the main goals but isn't strictly necessary for completion.
