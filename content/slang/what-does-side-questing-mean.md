## Side Questing (中文: 副本任务)
**English:** Side questing refers to taking on additional, often lesser-known tasks or objectives that aren't part of the main storyline, but are commonly found in role-playing games, manga, anime, and other narratives. These side quests usually offer exploration, additional rewards, and opportunities to learn or master new skills.
**中文:** 副本任务指的是完成不在主线剧情中的额外任务或目标。这些任务通常可以在角色扮演游戏、漫画、动画和其他叙事形式中找到，它们通常旨在提供探索机会、额外奖励以及学习或掌握新技能的机会。这些任务可能不如主线任务那样广为人知，但却为角色的发展和完善提供了重要的补充。
In the context of **RPGs (角色扮演游戏)**, side quests often include finding hidden treasures, visiting abandoned locations, or assisting other characters, which go beyond the main storyline. In **manga and anime** (漫画与动画), side quests can be depicted as subplots that enhance the main story or characterize the characters more deeply.
In everyday language, people might use the term "side questing" to describe taking on smaller, less significant tasks that help build skills or acquit minor responsibilities to free up time or energy for their main goals.
