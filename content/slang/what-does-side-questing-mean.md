## Side Questing (Side Questing)
**Chinese:**
侧任务（Side Questing）在游戏或虚构世界中指的是那些不在主线剧情中的次要任务或活动。完成这些任务通常会提供额外的经验值、物品或其他奖励。就像在主任务之外寻找一些小任务一样，这些任务可以为玩家带来乐趣和成就感。
**English:**
Side Questing refers to smaller, secondary tasks or missions that exist outside the main storyline in games or fictional worlds. Completing these tasks typically provides players with additional experience points, items, or other rewards. It's like finding small side tasks in addition to the main quest, which can add fun and a sense of achievement for players.
