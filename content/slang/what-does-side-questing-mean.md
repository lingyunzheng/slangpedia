# Slang Explanation: Side Questing
## English
**Side Questing:** In the context of gaming, side quests are additional tasks or objectives beyond the main plot that players can undertake. These often provide rewards such as experience points, money, or rare items. Engaging in side quests is often referred to as "side questing." It's a way to explore the game world, learn more about the story, and accumulate various bonuses. In some cases, side quests can offer characters growth or insight that is not available through the main questline.
## 中文
**Side Questing:** 在游戏领域，“边角料任务”是指除了主线任务之外，玩家可以完成的额外任务或目标。这些任务通常提供经验值、金钱或稀有物品等奖励。参与完成这些任务被简称为“边角料任务”、“支线任务”或“支线任务群”。通过执行这些任务，玩家可以探索游戏世界、了解更深入的剧情，并累积各种奖励或增益效果。在某些情况下，支线任务还能为角色带来成长或额外的隐含故事背景，这是主线任务无法提供的。
