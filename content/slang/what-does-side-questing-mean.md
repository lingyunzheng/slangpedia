## Side Questing
**中文解释：** 本来是指角色扮演游戏（RPG）中的支线任务，后来拓展为日常生活中指完成一些额外或不那么重要的任务。例如，为了获得游戏中的经验值或物品而进行的临时任务。
**English Explanation:** Originally referring to side missions or secondary tasks in role-playing games (RPGs), the term has expanded to mean completing additional or less important tasks in everyday life. For example, doing some small tasks to earn some bonus points or rewards in a game.
### Examples
1. **中文示例：** 上下班的路上顺便买份报纸，和一个老朋友聊聊天，这些都是很好的“生活中的侧任务”。
2. **English Example:** Picking up a newspaper on your way to or from work, chatting with an old friend over coffee—these are great "side quests" in your daily life.
### Usage
- 她在地铁上完成了几个侧任务，给手机充了电，还顺带读了一篇文章。（She completed a few side quests on the subway, charged her phone, and read an article in the process.）
- 我在争取那个重大项目的同时，也完成了好几个侧任务。（I was pursuing that major project while also knocking off a few other side quests.）
---
The term "side questing" is often used in a playful or efficient manner, indicating the ability to multitask or complete small, additional tasks in a venue where they are not the primary focus.
