# Slang Explanation: "Side Questing"
## English
- **Definition:** Engaging in activities or tasks that are not your primary goal or duty, but are still interesting or useful.
- **Example Use:** "Even though completing the main mission was priority, he enjoyed side questing to explore new areas and collect rare items."
## Chinese (简体中文)
- **定义:** 完成不是主要目标或职责，但仍然有趣或有用的小任务或活动。
- **例句:** "即使完成主要任务是首要任务，他也乐于进行支线任务来探索新的区域并收集稀有物品。"
