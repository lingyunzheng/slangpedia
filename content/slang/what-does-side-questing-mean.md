### Side Questing (English)
**Definition**: Engaging in tasks, often unrelated to the main goal but offering rewards, in a game or activity. 
**Slang Explanation**: It's like going on a little adventure outside the main storyline, where you might stumble upon a fun or useful side mission that can earn you rewards, experience, or just keep you entertained. It's the playful stuff that can happen alongside the main quest.
**Example**: "I spent most of my time side questing in the last game, but I still managed to finish the main story on time."
### 边角任务 (Chinese)
**定义**：在游戏或活动中进行与主要目标无关但提供奖励的小任务。
**俚语解释**：就像在主要剧情之外进行的小冒险，可能会遇到一些有趣的或有用的支线任务，能获得奖励、经验值或者其他乐趣。这是在主要任务之外添加的趣味部分。
**例句**： "我花了大部分时间在做边角任务，但仍然按时完成了主线任务。"
