## Sigma Grindset (sigma 研石)
### English Explanation
**Sigma Grindset**: This term originates from the world of competitive gaming, specifically among players of the first-person shooter (FPS) games such as Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (CS:GO). A "sigma grindset" refers to the intense, meticulous, and nearly obsessive approach to the game, often involving the player performing numerous repetitive actions to improve their skills, such as practicing micro-movement, shot timing, and aiming techniques. It's also about maintaining a high level of discipline and dedication, often described as being in a "grind" state – putting in countless hours of practice to achieve mastery.
### Simplified Chinese Explanation
**sigma 研石**: 这个术语来自于电子竞技世界，具体来说是第一人称射击游戏（FPS）如《反恐精英：全球攻势》（CS:GO）玩家之间的用语。"sigma 研石"指的是对游戏进行极其细致、近乎痴迷的训练方式，通常包括进行大量重复的动作以提升技术水平，比如练习微移动、射击时机以及瞄准技巧。这还涉及到极高的自律和专注，经常被形容为“在打滚”——通过投入大量时间进行练习来达到精通。
