# Sigma Grindset
## English
**Sigma Grindset**: A term primarily associated with the gaming community, this slang refers to the consistent and often exhausting dedication to grinding (doing repetitive tasks to earn rewards or progress) to achieve a desirable outcome, such as leveling up, unlocking achievements, or obtaining rare in-game items. This can also apply to any context where one is intensely focused on achieving a goal through persistent and monotonous effort.
## 中文
**Sigma Grindset**: 这个术语主要来源于游戏社区，用来形容那些持续且常常令人精疲力竭地进行打磨（即重复执行任务以获得奖励或进展），以达到某种理想结果的行为，例如等级提升、解锁成就或获取稀有游戏道具。这个说法也可以应用于任何需要通过持续且单调的努力来达成目标的情境。
