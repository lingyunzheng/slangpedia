# Sigma Grindset
## English Explanation
A sigma grindset is a term used in the context of online communities, particularly on platforms like Reddit. It refers to a mindset or approach where one focuses on making small incremental improvements over a long period, as opposed to looking for quick results. The inspiration often comes from sigma notation in mathematics, implying a continuous, step-by-step process. People with a sigma grindset might engage in activities like consistently practicing quantifiable skills (e.g., typing speed, code-writing ability), networking, or smaller investments that collectively lead to significant long-term gains.
