# Sigma Grindset (Sigma 清溶解)
## English Explanation
**Sigma grindset** is a term that originated in the gaming and esports communities, particularly within the Dota 2 scene. It refers to a high-level, detailed form of analyzing and discussing gameplay. The "sigma" part of the term is a level of detail or precision, often seen in mathematical contexts, indicating that the analysis is thorough and in-depth. A grindset in this context means a mindset or approach, which in this case, is the rigorous and analytical attitude that players adopt when dissecting matches or strategies.
### Example Usage:
- "That player really has a strong sigma grindset; he can spot any tiny mistake in his team's strategy."
## Chinese Explanation
