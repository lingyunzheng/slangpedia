## Sigma Grindset (Sigma 磨砺心态)
**中文解释：**
Sigma 磨砺心态，通常指一种不畏困难，持续努力提升自我技能和能力的心理状态。这种心态像字母“σ”一样坚实稳定，象征着长期而不懈的努力。类似于“卷”的状态，但更加突出个人成长的过程和内在的韧性。
**英文解释：**
Sigma grindset refers to a mindset of perseverance and continuous effort to enhance one's skills and capabilities. This mindset is as strong and solid as the Greek letter "σ," symbolizing a long-term and unwavering dedication. Similar to a "study hard" or "self-improvement" attitude, but emphasizing the process of personal growth and inner resilience.
