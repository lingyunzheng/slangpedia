# Sigma Grindset
## English
**Sigma Grindset:** This term is often used in gaming communities, particularly among players of competitive games like League of Legends, Counter-Strike: Global Offensive, and others. It refers to a highly dedicated and disciplined approach to practicing and improving one's skills. "Sigma" is a reference to SigmaOmega, a popular Greek letter society known for its rigorous academic standards and dedication. A "Sigma Grindset" player engages in long, repetitive, and often tedious practices to become better, much like a student who dedicates themselves to their studies.
