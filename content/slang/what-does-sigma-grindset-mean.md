### Sigma Grindset (Sigma 倴dle 鐵心)
**English Explanation:**
(sigma grindset)
- **Literal Meaning:** A combination of the Greek letter "sigma" and "grindset," which refers to a certain attitude or mentality in a particular domain.
- **Slang Usage:** It describes someone who is dedicated and consistent in their efforts to improve or achieve something, often to the point of being obsessive. This could be about personal skill development, gaming, fitness, or any other area where consistent hard work is valued.
**中文解释:**
(sigma 研磨心态)
- **直译意义:** 希腊字母“sigma”与“grindset”的组合，表示在某一特定领域中持有一种特定的态度或心态。
- **俚语用法:** 用来描述那些在某项技能或目标上付出持续努力和作业的人，有时这种努力会显得有些痴迷。这可以是关于个人技能提升、游戏、健身或是任何其他领域，那里一致性努力受到高度重视的地方。
---
Sigma grindset 多用于描述那些在目标实现过程中持之以恒、不懈努力的人。
