# Sigma Grindset (Sigma 研磨组)
- **English**: "Sigma Grindset" is a term often used in the context of playing video games, particularly multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) games like League of Legends or Dota 2. It describes the mindset or belief that investing a significant amount of time and effort into a specific character or hero (often a high-priority, meta-relevant hero known as a "sigma" in both the game and grinding context) profoundly improves one's chances of winning matches. Essentially, it's the strategy of focusing on one character to become an expert.
- **Chinese**: "Sigma 研磨组"这个词常常用在玩电子竞技类游戏，特别是多人在线战斗竞技场（MOBA）游戏如英雄联盟或DOTA2的玩家中。"Sigma Grindset"描述了一种心态或信念，即投入大量时间和精力专注于特定的角色或英雄（往往是一个在游戏内外都备受重视、具有高优先级的英雄，被称为“sigma”），这样可以大大提高赢得比赛的机会。简单来说，这即是集中精力精通一个角色的战略。
