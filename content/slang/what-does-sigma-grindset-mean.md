**中文解释**：Sigma Grindset 是一种强调高效、有序地投入时间和精力来达成目标的心态或生活方式。它偏向于选择能带来最大价值的活动，避免无价值或低效的行为。
**英文解释**：A Sigma Grindset is an approach to life or work that emphasizes efficiently and methodically investing time and effort into achieving specific goals. Sigma grindset practitioners prioritize maximizing output and minimizing waste to optimize their efforts.
