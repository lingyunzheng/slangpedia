# Sigma Grindset
**English:**  
A **sigma grindset** is a slang term derived from the Greek letter σ (sigma) that represents the 19th percentile in a normal distribution. This term is often used to describe a person who engages in highly specific and often tedious tasks or habits, usually with the goal of improving or perfecting something. It can also imply a particular lifestyle or mindset focused on consistent effort and high standards.
