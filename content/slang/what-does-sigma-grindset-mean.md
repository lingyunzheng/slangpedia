# Sigma Grindset ( Sigma Grindset )
## 中文解释
(sigma grindset) 源自英文直译，指代一种专注于特定目标或领域的深入研究和实现坚定的努力态度。在现代语境下，它常用来形容那些有特定目标并且愿意付出巨大努力去实现它的人。
## 英文 Explanation
(Sigma grindset) comes directly from the English phrase, referring to a focused and determined effort towards a specific goal or field. In modern usage, it often describes individuals who have clear goals and are willing to put in tremendous effort to achieve them.
