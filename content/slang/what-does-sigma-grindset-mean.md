## Sigma Grindset (Sigma 忍者心态)
### 英文解释
- **Sigma Grindset**: This slang term is commonly used in online gaming and social media to describe a mindset or approach where a player or individual consistently and dedicatedly improves and works hard in pursuit of a goal. This can be seen in gaming, fitness, or any other area where continuous improvement and effort are key. The term sometimes refers to the feeling of being in a state of continuous improvement and dedication, akin to how a sigma (Σ) symbol might represent summing up small, consistent efforts over time.
### 中文解释
- **Sigma 忍者心态**: 这个俚语通常用于在线游戏和社交媒体中，指的是玩家或个人一种持续且专注地提高和努力以达成目标的心态。这可以体现在游戏、健身或任何需要持续改进和努力的领域中。有时这个说法指的是处于一种持续改进和专注的状态，就如同希腊字母 sigma (Σ) 表示累积小而稳定的努力一样。
