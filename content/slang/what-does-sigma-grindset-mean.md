## Sigma Grindset
#### English
**Sigma Grindset**: This term refers to a lifestyle or mentality where someone meticulously and consistently focuses on small, repetitive tasks to gradually achieve their goals. Think of it as a grindset with a clinical or precision-driven approach. It’s often seen in competitive gaming, fitness, and academic settings where consistent, incremental progress is the key to success.

**Sigma Grindset**：这个词指的是一个人有条不紊且持续地专注于小而重复的任务，以逐步实现目标的生活方式或心态。这通常表现为一种有临床或精密导向的“拧螺丝”精神。在竞技游戏、健身和学术领域，这种稳扎稳打、循序渐进的进展是成功的关键。
