# Sigma Grindset
## English Explanation
A "sigma grindset" is a term often used in online gaming communities, particularly in competitive and solo-focused games. It refers to the mindset or strategy a player adopts when they are determined to improve their skills and win, often characterized by prolonged and intense periods of self-improvement, often outside of standard play times. The term can also mean someone who consistently puts in extra effort and time to reach their goals, even if it means sacrificing other activities or social events.
## Chinese Explanation
