## Vibe Check (氛围检查)
### English
**Vibe Check**: A casual way to ask if someone is feeling or acting in a certain way, often used to confirm if a person is on board with a decision or plan. It's like asking, "Hey, how are you feeling about this?" but in a more relaxed, slangy tone.
### Chinese
**vibe check (氛围检查)**: 一种非正式的方式询问某人的感觉或行为是否符合预期，通常用来确认某人是否同意某个决定或计划。这就像问“嘿，你觉得这个怎么样？”但语气更加轻松随意。
