## Vibe Check
### English Explanation:
A slang term used to gauge the atmosphere or mood in a room, or to ask someone if they feel comfortable or trust a situation. Often used in casual conversations to ensure everyone is on the same page or to check if the group's energy is positive and appropriate.
### Chinese Explanation:
一个俚语表达，用来评估房间中的氛围或情绪，或者询问别人是否感觉舒服或信任当前的情况。通常在非正式对话中使用，以确保每个人都在同一个页面上，或者检查小组的能量是否积极且合适。
