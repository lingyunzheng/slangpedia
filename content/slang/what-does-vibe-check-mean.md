# Vibe Check (气息检测 / qì xì jiǎn cè)
## English Explanation:
**Vibe Check** is a term used in casual conversations, often online, to ask whether someone is feeling good or if the atmosphere around a situation is positive. It's a way of subtly assessing the mood or state of mind of those around you.
## Chinese Explanation:
**气息检测 (qì xì jiǎn cè)** 是一种在日常交流中常用的术语，尤其是在网上聊天时，用来询问对方是否感觉良好，或者周围的情况是否积极。它是一种微妙地评估周围氛围或者人们状态的方法。
### Example Usage:
**English:** 
"Hey, did you vibe check before the party? I want to make sure everyone is feeling cozy and relaxed."
**Chinese:** 
“嘿，聚会前你做了一下气息检测吗？我想确保每个人都感觉很舒适和放松。”
