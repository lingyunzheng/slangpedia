## Vibe Check (English/Chinese slang)
### English Explanation:
**Vibe Check**: This phrase is often used to ask if a situation or environment feels right or good. It can also be used to check if the tone or atmosphere aligns with what a person or a group intends. For example, before starting a conversation, someone might ask, "What's the vibe here?" to gauge the emotional atmosphere of the room.
### Chinese Explanation:
**频率检查 (pínghuàn jiǎnchá)**: 这个短语用于询问当前的氛围是否合适或舒服。也可以用来检查气氛是否符合说话人或一组人的预期。例如，在开始对话之前，有人可能会问“这里气氛如何？”来感受房间里的情感氛围。
### Example:
- English: "Is everything good? How's the vibe?"
- Chinese: "一切都好？这里气氛怎么样？"
