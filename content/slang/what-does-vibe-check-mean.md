# Vibe Check (English - Chinese)
**English:** A casual way to ask if the energy or atmosphere of a situation is positive or suitable.
**Chinese:** 一种随意询问当前氛围是否舒适、是否OK的方式。
- **Example Usage:**
- English: "Is everyone in the group up for a quick game of basketball? Vibe check!"
- Chinese: "如果大家节奏还行，咱们来场篮球怎么样？ vibe check！"
