### Vibe Check
#### English Explanation
A casual way to ask if everything is good or alright, often used to gauge the mood or atmosphere in a group. It can also be a polite way of asking for someone's impressions or thoughts on a situation.
#### Chinese Explanation
一种随意的方式，用来询问是否一切都好或气氛是否合适，常用于了解小组中的氛围或心情。也可以作为一种礼貌的方式，询问别人对某个情况的看法或印象。
