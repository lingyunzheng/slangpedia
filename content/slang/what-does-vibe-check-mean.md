## Vibe Check (Vibe Qiaochked)
**English:** A casual way to ask if someone is in the right mood for a gathering or activity, often used in social settings to gauge emotional and sociable preparedness.
**Chinese:** （問别人是否在適當時光Join）的一种随意表达方式，通常用于社交场合，用来评估人们的情绪和社交准备状态。例如，“你今天 vibe 吗？”可以理解为“你今天心情怎么样？适合一起出去玩吗？”
**Example Usage:**
- English: "Want to grab some coffee later? Want to do a vibe check first."
- Chinese: "后来想不想要点儿咖啡？要不先 vibe check 一下怎么样？"
