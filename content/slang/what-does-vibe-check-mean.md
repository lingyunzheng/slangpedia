## Vibe Check (英美 slang)
### English Explanation
A casual way to ask if everything is good or if there is any tension or discomfort in a situation. It's often used to ensure a positive atmosphere or to gauge the mood of a group.
### Chinese Explanation
一种随意的方式，用来询问情况是否一切良好，或者环境中是否有任何紧张或不适。它通常用于确保气氛积极，或者了解小组的情绪状态。
**Example Usage:**
- English: Hey, everything good? Do we need to do a vibe check?
- Chinese: 嘿，一切都好嘛？我们需要做一下 vibe check 吗？
