# Vibe Check (中文解释：氛围检查)
**English Slang Explanation:** 
A phrase used to quickly gauge if everyone present in a group feels okay with the environment, mood, or engagement level. Often used to ensure that the atmosphere is conducive to further actions or discussions, or to confirm that no one is feeling left out or uncomfortable.
**Chinese Slang Explanation:**
一个用来快速判断小组中每个人的氛围是否合适、心情是否良好的短语。通常用于确保现在的氛围适合继续进行活动或讨论，或确认没有人感到被忽视或不舒服。
