## Vibe Check (英语: Vibe Check)
### English Explanation
A slang term referring to the moment when someone asks if the energy or atmosphere in a social situation is good or appropriate. It can also be used as a general check-in to gauge the mood or feelings of the group.
### Chinese Explanation
一个俚语表达，指在社交场合中询问气氛是否合适或良好的时刻。它也可以作为一种通用检查，来了解小组的情绪或感受。
### Usage Examples
- **English:** "Hey, should we go for that late-night snack? Vibe check, anyone?"
- **Chinese:** "嘿，我们去吃个深夜小吃怎么样？ vibe check，大家感觉如何？"
