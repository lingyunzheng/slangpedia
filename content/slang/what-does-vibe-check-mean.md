# Vibe Check (中文： vibe check)
**Definition:**
- **English:** A slang term used to encourage or request someone to share their current mood or feelings.
- **Chinese:** 这个俚语用来鼓励或请求某人分享他们当前的情绪或感觉。
**Explanation:**
- **English:** If you're with a group of friends and you sense a bit of tension or if you feel like someone might be keeping something to themselves, you might say "Vibe check" to see if everyone is on the same page or to start a casual conversation.
- **Chinese:** 如果你在和一群朋友在一起时感觉到一丝紧张，或者你觉得有人可能隐瞒了某些事情，你可以问“你现在的 vibe 是怎样？”以此来检查大家的状态，或者开启一个轻松的对话。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "Hey, vibe check! Is everything good? I feel a little off."
- **Chinese:** "嘿，来个 vibe check！一切都好吧？我觉得有点不对劲。"
