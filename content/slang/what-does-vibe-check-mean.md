# Vibe Check ( vibe check )
## English
**Definition:** A casual way to ask if someone or something fits into a group or situation in terms of general energy or atmosphere.
**Usage Example:** "Before we start the party, let's do a vibe check to make sure everyone is in the right mood."
## 中文
**定义:** 一种随意的方式，用来询问某人或某事物是否适合某个群体或情境，从整体的氛围或气氛角度来看。
**使用示例:** "派对开始前，我们来个 vibe check，确保每个人的气氛都对了。"
