## Vibe Check (中文解释)
### English
**Vibe Check**: A casual way to ask someone if they feel comfortable with a situation or if they agree with the energy or atmosphere surrounding a group or conversation. It can also be used to subtly gauge another person's emotions or thoughts.
### 中文
**vibe check**: 一种随意的方式，用于询问某人是否觉得舒服，或者他们是否认同当前讨论或团体中的氛围。它还可以用来微妙地了解另一个人的情绪或想法。
