```markdown
## Vibe Check (汉语: 氛围检查)
**English**: A quick, casual way to gauge the mood or atmosphere of a group.
- **Usage in Sentences**:
- "Hey, can I do a quick vibe check? Everyone seems a bit quiet."
- "After the joke, I noticed a vibe check and realized not everyone was into it."
**Explanation**: This phrase is often used in social settings to see if the group's mood or reactions match what someone is hoping for or to confirm if everyone is on the same page. It’s a subtle way to ask, "Are we good?" or "Do you get it?"
```
```markdown
## Vibe Check (汉语: 氛围检测)
**English**: A casual inquiry to understand if the current mood or energy is as expected.
- **Usage in Sentences**:
- "Can we do a vibe check before we start the event?"
- "After the speech, I did a quick vibe check and saw that the audience was energized and engaged."
**Explanation**: Similar to "vibe check" in English, this term is used to assess the emotional or energetic state of a group of people. It can be helpful in determining whether the environment is appropriate for a particular activity or discussion.
```
