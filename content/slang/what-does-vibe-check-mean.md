## Vibe Check (普通话： vibe check)
- **English**: A quick check to see if the group or atmosphere is positive or if someone is feeling off. It's often used in online contexts to gauge the emotional atmosphere.
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation**: 
- **意思**: 一种快速检查以确定群体或氛围是否积极，或者有人是否感觉不对劲。它通常用于在线环境中，以评估情绪氛围。
- **用法示例**: 
- "Hey guys, vibe check! We need to make sure everyone's okay after that meeting." // 嘿，大家，氛围检查！我们需要确保会议之后每个人都还好。
- "She did a vibe check and noticed everyone was feeling a bit under the weather." // 她进行了氛围检查，注意到大家似乎都不太舒服。
