## Vibe Check (English-Chinese)
**English**: Vibe check is used to ask if the mood, energy, or atmosphere is as expected or desired.
**Chinese**: Vibe check 是一种询问气氛、能量或氛围是否如预期或所需的方式。
### Example Usage:
**English**: "Hey, anyone want to chill at the new rooftop bar? I just want to do a vibe check before we go."
**Chinese**: "嘿，想去新的屋顶酒吧放松吗？我们在去之前再做一下 vibe check 吧。"
