## Vibe Check
**English:** A casual way to ask if someone is okay or to check if there's any tension or discomfort in the atmosphere.
**中文:** 一种随意的方式询问某人是否一切正常，或者检查气氛中是否有任何紧张或不适。
Example sentences:
- "Hey, did you get that memo about the new policies? I just did a vibe check!"
- "嘿，你拿到那份关于新政策的备忘录了吗？我刚刚做了一个气氛查看！"
- "After she said that, I did a quick vibe check and could tell something was off."
- "她说完那句话后，我做了一个快速的气氛查看，可以看出有什么不对劲。"
