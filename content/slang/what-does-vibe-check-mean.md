## Vibe Check 🌟
**中文解释**：vibe check是指在对话或互动中，一个人以轻松或调侃的方式询问对方的情绪状态或整体氛围是否合适。这种表达通常用于确保大家在同一个频道上，或者在一种积极、合适的情绪状态下进行交流。
**英文解释**：A "vibe check" is a casual or teasing way of asking someone about their emotional state or the overall atmosphere. This expression is often used to ensure that everyone is on the same page or in a positive, appropriate mood for the conversation or interaction.
**例句**：
- 中文：大家都准备好了吗？来个vibe check。
- English: Is everyone ready? Vibe check.
