### Vibe Check
**English Explanation:**
A "vibe check" is a casual way of asking if something is alright or if someone is feeling okay. It's often used to ensure that the atmosphere or the energy in a situation is positive and aligned with expectations. For example, if a group of friends is about to embark on an activity together, one might say, "Hey, what's the vibe?" or "Let's do a vibe check."
**Simplified Explanation:**
It’s like asking, "Are we all good?" or "Is everything okay?"
**Chinese Explanation:**
“vibe check” 指的是一个随意询问是否一切都好的方式，用来确认气氛或环境是否符合预期。比如，一群朋友即将开始一项活动时，可能会说，“嘿，气氛怎么样？”或者“来做个 vibe check。”
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
它就像是在问，“我们都好吧？”或者“一切都好嘛？”
