### Vibe Check (English/Chinese)
**English:**  
A slang expression used to ask if someone is feeling good or if the atmosphere or dynamics are right. Essentially, it's a way to check the emotional or energetic state of a situation.
**Chinese:**  
一个网络用语，用来询问某人是否感觉良好，或者环境和氛围是否合适。简单来说，就是检查某种情感或能量状态的表达方式。
Example usage:
- "Hey, did the vibe check pass? How are y'all feeling?"  
- "大家感觉怎么样？ vibe check 是否通过了？"
