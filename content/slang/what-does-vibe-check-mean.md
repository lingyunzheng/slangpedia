## Vibe Check
**English Explanation:**
A "vibe check" is a casual way to ask someone if they are feeling good or if the atmosphere or mood is positive. It can also be used to see if everyone is on the same page or if there is any tension or disagreement in a group.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"vibe check" 是一种非正式的说法，用来询问某人是否感觉良好，或者氛围是否积极。它也可以用来了解大家是否意见一致，或者团体中是否存在紧张或分歧。
