```markdown
## Vibe Check (中文解释：调频/氛围检查)
### English Explanation
**Vibe Check:** A casual way to ask if the mood or atmosphere of a situation feels right. Typically used to ensure everyone in the group is on the same wavelength or to gauge the current mood before making a decision or taking further action.
### Chinese Explanation
**调频/氛围检查:** 一种随意的方式询问当前的气氛是否合适，通常用来确认小组中每个人都处于同一频道，或者在做出决定或采取进一步行动前，评估当前的气氛。
```
