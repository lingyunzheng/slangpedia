## Vibe Check (English / 中文)
**English:** 
A casual way to gauge the mood, atmosphere, or energy of a situation. It can also be used to directly ask someone to share how they are feeling.
**中文:**
一种随意的方式，用来评估一种情况下的气氛、情绪或能量。它也可以直接问某人他们现在感觉如何。
