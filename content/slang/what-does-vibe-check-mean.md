```markdown
## Vibe Check (简体中文: 氛围检查)
**English:** A casual way to ask if everyone is feeling good with the current atmosphere or situation. It can also be used to gauge if people are on the same page or if something is amiss.
**Example Usage in a Sentence:**
- English: "Hey, vibe check! Is everyone feeling okay with the plan?"
- 简体中文: "嘿，氛围检查！大家对这个计划没有意见吧？"
**Context:** This phrase is often used in casual or group settings, like meetings, social gatherings, or informal discussions, to ensure everyone is comfortable and aligned with the prevailing mood or direction.
```
```markdown
## Vibe Check (繁體中文: 氛圍檢查)
**English:** 一種非正式的方式來問詢現在的氛圍或情況是否都令人滿意，也可以用來估測大家是否在同一頁或有沒有哪裡不對勁。
**例句：**
- English: "Hey, vibe check! Is everyone feeling okay with the plan?"
- 繁體中文: "嘿，氛圍檢查！大家對這個計畫有異議嗎？"
**使用情境：** 這句話常在非正式或小組的場合使用，像是會議、社交聚會或非正式討論，來確保每一個人都感到舒適且與當前的氛圍和方向一致。
```
