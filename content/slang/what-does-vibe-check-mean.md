## Vibe Check (英汉俚语解释)
### English:
**Vibe Check**: This phrase is a casual way of asking if the energy or atmosphere is right for a particular situation, or if everyone is on the same page emotionally or energetically. It's often used in social settings to ensure that everyone feels comfortable and understands the mood.
### 中文:
**vibe check (气氛检查)**：这个短语是一种随意的方式，用于询问当前的氛围是否适合某种情况，或者每个人的情绪和能量是否一致。它常用于社交场合，以确保每个人感到舒适并且明白当前的氛围。
