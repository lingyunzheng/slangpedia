# Vibe Check (中文： vibe检查)
## English Explanation
**Vibe Check**: This is a term that originated in the early 2000s and has since become popular in the digital age. It is often used to gauge the atmosphere or the energy of a gathering or event. Essentially, someone might ask "How's the vibe?" or "Do we have good vibes?" to check whether everyone is on the same page, feeling good, and in a similar mindset. It's a way to ensure that the energy is positive and conducive to the activities at hand.
## 中文解释
**vibe检查**: 这个词起源于2000年初，随着数字时代的到来而流行起来。它通常用来衡量一个聚会或活动的氛围或能量。例如，有人可能会问“气氛如何？”或“我们有好氛围吗？”来检查每个人是否都在同一个频道上，感觉良好，并且心态一致。这是一种确保氛围积极且适合当前活动的方法。
