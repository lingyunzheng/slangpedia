### Vibe Check (English)
**Definition:** A casual way to ask if the atmosphere, mood, or situation is good or if it aligns with expectations.
**Example:** "Hey, you guys seemed a bit down. Did the vibe check go well after that joke?"
---
### 检查气氛 (Pinyin: Jiǎnchá Fènqì) (Chinese)
**Definition:** 一种轻松的方式来询问氛围、情绪或情况是否良好，或者是否符合期待。
**Example:** "嘿，你们看起来有点低落。那个笑话讲完之后，气氛检查怎么样？"
