```markdown
## Vibe Check (English-Chinese)
**Vibe Check**  
*English:* A casual way to ask if someone or a situation feels right or changes in the environment or emotions need to be acknowledged. It's often used in social situations to gauge the mood or atmosphere.
*Chinese:* 问氛围/检查氛围 (Wèn fēngwèi / jiǎnchá fēngwèi)
- 问氛围 (Wèn fēngwèi): 询问或感受氛围 (Ask or sense the atmosphere)
- 检查氛围 (jiǎnchá fēngwèi): 检查环境或情绪是否合适 (Check if the environment or emotions are suitable)
Example: "Can I give a quick vibe check? I think the energy in the room has changed."  
例子: "我能先感受一下气氛吗？我觉得房间里的氛围变了。"
```
This explanation provides a clear translation and usage context for the slang term "vibe check" in both English and Simplified Chinese.
