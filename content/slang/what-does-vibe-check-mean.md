## Vibe Check (中文解释)
### English:
**Vibe Check:** A casual check to see if the group or situation aligns with a certain energy or mood. It's often used to ensure everyone in the group is on the same page or feels comfortable with the direction things are going.
### 中文:
**vibe check (感觉检查):** 一种轻松的方式，用来了解大家的情绪或氛围是否一致。通常用来确认小组里的每个人是否对当前的情况感到满意或感到舒适，或者是否都认同某个发展方向。
