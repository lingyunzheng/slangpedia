## Vibe Check (中美双语俚语解释)
#### English Explanation
A "vibe check" is a way of asking someone if they're feeling good or if things are going well. It's also used to assess the atmosphere or mood of a situation or group of people. This phrase is often used in casual conversations to make sure everyone is on the same page or to gauge the current emotional or energetic state of the environment.
#### 中文解释
"vibe check" 是一种问对方是否感觉良好或事情进行得是否顺利的方式，也可以用来评估一个场合或一群人的气氛或情绪。这个词组常常被用来在非正式的对话中确保每个人都心意相通，或者评估当前的情绪或能量状态。
