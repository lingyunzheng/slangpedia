## Vibe Check (中文解释： vibe check)
- **English Slang Explanation:** A quick confirmation to gauge the current atmosphere or mood among a group of people, often asked as "what's the vibe?" or directly checking if everyone is feeling good or on the same page.
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:** 检查氛围，通常用于在一群人的对话或活动开始时，询问当前的大致感觉或情绪是否适合接下来的活动。这是一个快速确认大家是否相互理解或是否在同一频道上的举动。
