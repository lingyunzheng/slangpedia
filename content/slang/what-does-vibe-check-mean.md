### Vibe Check (英语: Vibe Check)
- **Definition:** A casual way to ask if the energy or atmosphere of a situation is good or if people are on the same page.
- **Explanation in English:** It's like asking if everyone is feeling the same "vibe" or if the mood is right. You might say, "Hey, what’s the vibe here?" if you want to ensure that everyone is agreeing with the idea before moving forward.
- **Explanation in Chinese:** 类似于询问当前的气氛是否合适，或者大家是否意见一致。你可能会问“嗨，现在的气氛怎么样？”来确认大家是否对某个提议达成一致意见。
