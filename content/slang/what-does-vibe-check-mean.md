# Vibe Check (中文: 氛围检查)
## English
**Vibe Check**: A casual way to ask if someone or something fits in well with the current environment or group dynamic. It can be used to assess the mood or to ask for a person's reaction to see if they are comfortable or agreeable with the situation.
* Example: "Is everything okay? Do you vibe with the energy here?" or "Can you give me a vibe check before I share that news?"
## 中文
**氛围检查 (Vibe Check)**: 一种随意的方式来询问某人或某事物是否适合当前的环境或群体氛围。它可以用来评估氛围或询问某人的反应，以确定他们是否感到舒适或同意当前的情况。
* 示例: "一切还好吗？你觉得这里氛围合适吗？" 或 "分享那条新闻之前，可以给我做一个氛围检查吗？"
