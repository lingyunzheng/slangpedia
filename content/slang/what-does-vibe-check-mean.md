### Vibe Check (中文： vibe check)
- **English Slang Explanation:**
A moment or conversation where someone checks if the overall mood, atmosphere, or energy level is aligned with their expectations or desires. It's often used in social media or casual conversations to gauge whether everyone is on the same page or in the right mindset for a particular activity or discussion.
- **Chinese Slang Explanation:**
一种在交流或活动前检查整体氛围、情绪或能量水平是否符合大家预期的方式。常用于社交媒体或口语交流中，用来判断大家是否在同一频道上或者是否适合进行某一活动或讨论。
