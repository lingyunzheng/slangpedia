# Vibe Check ( slang explanation )
## English
**Vibe Check**: This phrase is typically used to ask if someone is on the same wavelength or if there is a shared feeling or atmosphere. It’s often used in contexts where people want to ensure that their group is aligned or to gauge group energy. 
Example: "Hey, what's the vibe check? Are we all down for a surprise party?"
## Chinese
**vibe check**：这个短语通常被用来询问是否大家都处于相同的氛围中，或者是否有一种共同的感觉或气氛。在很多情况下，这句话用来确认小组是否一致，或者衡量群体的氛围。
例句："嘿，做一下 vibe check，我们都同意办一个惊喜派对吗？"
