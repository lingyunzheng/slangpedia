## Vibe Check (English)
**Definition:** A casual way to ask about the overall atmosphere or mood of a situation, often used as a binary question expecting a positive or negative answer.
**Example Usage:**  
- "Hey, are you down for a game this weekend?"  
- "Vibe check?"
## Vibe Check (Chinese)
**释义：** 一种非正式的方式询问当前氛围或情绪的整体状态，通常作为一个二选一的问题来问，期待肯定或否定的回答。
**例句：**  
- "这个周末你打算不打算玩游戏？"  
- "气场 check？"
