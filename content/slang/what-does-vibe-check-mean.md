## Vibe Check (English-Chinese)
**Vibe Check**:
- **English**: A casual way to ask if someone noticed a change or shift in the mood or atmosphere of a situation.
- **Chinese**: 一个轻松的方式来问对方是否注意到了气氛或情境的变化。
Usage Example:
- **English**: "Hey, does everyone feel good about the game plan? Vibe check!"
- **中文**: "嘿，大家对这个行动计划都感觉满意吗？气氛检查一下！"
