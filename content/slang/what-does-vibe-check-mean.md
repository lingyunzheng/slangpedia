# Vibe Check (Vibe Chek)
## English
**Vibe Check**: A casual way to ask if everything is good or if there's an issue with the atmosphere or energy of a situation. It's like saying, "Is everything alright?" or "Do you feel off about anything?"
Example: "Hey, what's up? Did you do a vibe check before we start the meeting?"
## 中文
**vibe check**: 一种非正式的方式来询问当前气氛或氛围是否良好，或者是否有任何问题。相当于问“你好吗？”或“你觉得有什么不对劲吗？”
示例：嘿，你好吗？我们开始会议前做过vibe check了吗？
