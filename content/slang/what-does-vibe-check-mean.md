## Vibe Check (英语：vibe check)
### English Explanation
A vibe check is a friendly way of asking if someone is feeling okay or if the energy in a particular environment is right. It can also mean checking if people are on the same page or if there's some underlying tension or discomfort that needs to be addressed.
### Chinese Explanation
“vibe check”是一种友好的方式，用于询问某人是否感觉良好，或者特定环境中的气氛是否适合。它也可以表示检查一下人们是否意见一致，或者是否存在一些潜在的紧张或不适感，需要进行处理。
Example Usage:
- **English**: "Hey, can you do a vibe check? I think someone might be feeling a bit off."
- **Chinese**: "嘿，做一下气氛感觉好吗？我觉得有人可能有点不舒服。"
