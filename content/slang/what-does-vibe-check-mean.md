## Vibe Check (vibe check)
**English Slang:** A casual gesture or comment used to gauge the overall mood or atmosphere in a group, or to confirm that everyone is on the same page or wavelength.
**Example Usage:**
- "Let's do a vibe check before we start the meeting to make sure everyone is feeling okay."
**Chinese Slang:** 试探气氛 (tiàn tàn fēn chì)
- 俚语解释：一种非正式的手势或评论，用于评估小组中的整体情绪或氛围，或者确认每个人是否在同一频道或想法一致。
- 示例用法：在会议开始前做一次气氛试探，确保每个人都感觉良好。
