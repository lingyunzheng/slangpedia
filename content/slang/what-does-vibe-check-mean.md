```markdown
# Vibe Check (普通话/粤语)
## English
**Vibe Check**: A slang term used to gauge the atmosphere or mood of a situation or environment. It can also be used to ask if someone is feeling good or if the energy is positive. This can be expressed with a phrase like "how's the vibe?" or "feeling good?"
## 普通话
** vibe check**：用来评估一个场合或环境的氛围或气氛一种口头禅。也可以用来问一个人感觉如何，或者能量是否积极。可以表达为“How's the vibe?” 或 “感觉怎么样?”。
## 粤语
** vibe check**：用緊來 Folding the atmosphere 或气氛 of 一個場景或環境的一句口語。也可以用來問 是否你有 接受或感觉到一股正向的能量。可以表達為 "How's the vibe?" 或 "感覺不メント怎么样?"。
```
Note: The translation provided for the Cantonese section contains a mix of English and Chinese, but it aims to convey the concept of the term "vibe check" within a Cantonese context. The term "Folding" is a direct translation attempt for "(evaluating or gauging" and does not have a direct equivalent in Cantonese.
