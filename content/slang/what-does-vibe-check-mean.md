# Vibe Check (中文： vibe 检查)
**English Explanation:**
A casual way to ask someone if the conversations, environment, or company is comfortable and likable. It's like asking, "Is everything good?" or "Do you feel good about this?" in a laid-back manner.
**Chinese Explanation:**
一种轻松的方式询问对方当前的交流氛围、环境或公司是否让人感到舒适和愉悦，相当于在问“一切顺利吗？”或“你对这个感觉怎么样？”
