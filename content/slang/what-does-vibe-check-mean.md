## Vibe Check (直觉检查)
**English Explanation:**
A slang term used to ask someone if they are feeling positive or optimistic about a situation. It's often used to gauge the emotional atmosphere or mood in a group setting. For example, if you're planning a get-together and want to ensure everyone is in a good mood, you might say, "Vibe check," to see if everyone is excited about the event.
**Literal Translation:**
直觉检查 (Zhíjué Jiance) - Intuition Check
- **直觉 (Zhíjué)** means "intuition" or "instinct."
- **检查 (Jiance)** means "check" or "inspection."
**Example Usage in a Sentence:**
"Hey, everyone, it’s a vibe check. Are you all ready for the party tomorrow?"
