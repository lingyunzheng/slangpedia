# Vibe Check (Noun)
## English Explanation
A casual way to ask if someone is feeling good, or if the atmosphere is positive and agreeable. Often used to check if everyone is on the same page or if there's any negativity that needs to be addressed.
## Chinese Explanation
一种随意询问某人是否感觉良好或氛围是否积极可接受的方式。通常用于检查 是否大家都心往一处想或者是否有需要解决的消极情绪。
**Usage Example:**
English: "Has anyone had a bad vibe about this project?"
Chinese: "有没有觉得这个项目的氛围不对劲？"
