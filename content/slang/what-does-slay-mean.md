## Slay (slai)
### English
To slay means to completely excel or succeed at something, often with confidence and style. It can also be used to describe someone who looks very good, especially in a fashionable or attractive way. The term has been popularized in social media and has a more casual, modern feel.
### Chinese
“宰” (zǎi) 或 “に乗る” (oto-naru, lit. "get on") 
宰意味着完全地、出色地完成某事，通常带有自信且时尚的态度。这个词在社交媒体中非常流行，带有一种更随意、现代的感觉。也可以形容某个事物或者是人看起来非常好，尤其是在时尚或吸引力方面。
例句：
- In the dance competition, she really slayed the stage. （在舞蹈比赛中，她真的主宰了舞台。）
- He looked absolutely slay in his new suit. （他穿新西装的样子真的非常帅。）
