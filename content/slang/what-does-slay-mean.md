## Slay 🎯
### English
**Meaning**: To perform with exceptional skill and style, often to impress or amaze.
- *Example*: "The dancer really slayed during her performance!"
### 中文
**含义**：表现出色、充满活力，通常令人印象深刻。
- *例句*：“舞者在她的表演中真的一如既往地slay！”
