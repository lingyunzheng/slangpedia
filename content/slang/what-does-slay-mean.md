## English: Slay
- **Definition:** To perform exceptionally well, to impress greatly, or to completely dominate a situation.
- **Example Usage:** "That dancer really slayed the performance!"
## 中文解释: Slay
- **定义:** 表示表现得非常出色，给人留下深刻的印象，或者彻底主宰某个情况。
- **例句使用:** "那个舞蹈演员真能把舞蹈表演得完美无瑕！"
