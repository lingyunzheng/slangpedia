# Slay (英/美俚语)
## English Explanation
**To slay** is an informal and increasingly popular slang term used to describe the act of doing something exceptionally well or impressively. It often conveys enthusiasm and approval. Imagine you are at a party and someone is dancing incredibly well - you might say, "They really slayed the dance!"
## Chinese Explanation
**开挂**（英/美俚语）用来形容做某事做得非常棒或给人留下深刻印象。它传达了很强的热情和认可。比如，如果你的朋友在派对上跳舞跳得非常出色，你会说：“他们跳舞可谓开了挂！”
## Example Sentences
- **English:** She totally slayed the interview; everyone was impressed by her knowledge.
- **Chinese:** 她面试简直开挂了，大家都被她的知识所折服。
