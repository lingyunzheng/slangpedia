## Slay
**English:**  
To slay means to impress or dominate with confidence and style. It can also be used in the present tense, as "slaying," which indicates someone is consistently performing well or making a strong impact. It’s often used to describe someone who is killing it in a particular activity or field.
**Chinese:**  
“slay” 表示令人印象深刻或占据主导地位，充满自信和风格。它既可以用于现在时“slaying”，表示某人在某个活动或领域中持续表现出色或制造强烈影响。常用来形容某人在特定方面表现出非凡的能力或魅力。
