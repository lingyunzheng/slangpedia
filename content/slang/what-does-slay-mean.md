## Slay (English)
**Meaning:** To impress, excel, or dominate in something.
**Example Sentence:** She really slayed at the dance recital!
---
## 硬核 (Yìngànhǎo, Chinese)
**Meaning:** To dominate, excel, or impress in something; often used in a context where one is demonstrating exceptional skill.
**Example Sentence:** 她在舞蹈比赛中硬核地表现了自己的实力。(Tā zài wǎnguǎn běncāngshì yìngánhǎo de biǎójìng zhòumìng de shìlì.) - She really slayed at the dance competition by showcasing her strength.
**Alternative Usage:** 他的演讲非常硬核，赢得了一片掌声。(Tā de jiǎnyǎng fēicháng yìngánhǎo, yíngdé le yì piàn zhēnshēng.) - His speech was very hard core, winning a round of applause.
