## Slay (Slay)
### English
**Meaning**: To excel, perform exceptionally well, or dominate in a particular activity or situation. It often implies a level of coolness, fierce competitiveness, or overall awesomeness.
**Example**: "She really slayed at the dance competition last night!"
### 中文
**含义**: 在特定活动或情况下表现出色、表现出优越的表现，或者彻底主导。这个词通常意味着超酷、极其竞争或者整体上很帅。
**例句**: "她昨晚在舞蹈比赛中真是表现得太棒了！"
