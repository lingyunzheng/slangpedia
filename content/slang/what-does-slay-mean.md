```markdown
# Slay (维持/保持)
## English Explanation
**Slay:**  
To perform extremely well or impressively, often in a captivating or even intimidating way. People use this term to express that someone or something is dominating their field or creating a highly impactful impression.
*Usage examples:*
- "She slayed her college entrance exams!"
- "He slayed in the new music video."
## Chinese Explanation
**维持/保持 (Slen/mian/BAI):**  
用来形容某人或某事在某个领域内表现出色，给人留下深刻印象，甚至有时使用这种表达会让人感到场面被完全统治或掌控。人们使用这个词来表达某人或某事物在某个领域中的统治地位或影响力。
*使用示例:*
- 她在高考中取得了非常出色的成绩，可以说：“她高考表现超群，基本可以说是碾压了！”
- 他在新发布的 MV 中表现得非常耀眼，可以说：“他在新 MV 中表现得太厉害了，可以说是征服了所有人！”
```
In this explanation, "slen/mian/BAI" is a representation of the pronunciation "维持/保持" in Pinyin, which is a way to transcribe Chinese characters into the Roman alphabet.
