## Slay (English)
**Meaning:** To completely excel or dominate in something, often to the point of impressing or overwhelming others.
**Example:** "She slayed her presentation in front of the board of directors."

**例句:** "她在董事会面前做的演讲简直精彩绝伦，真的是slay全场！"
