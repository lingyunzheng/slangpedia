# Slay (英 /slay/ 中 /slay/)
## English Explanation
**Slay** is a slang term often used to express that something or someone is extremely impressive, awesome, or effective. It conveys that the subject has performed exceptionally well or was highly successful in a particular endeavor.
Example: "That dance routine totally slayed the judges!"
## Chinese Explanation
**slay** 是一个俚语，用来表示某事物或某人非常精彩、酷炫或者非常成功。它传达出主题在特定努力中表现得极其出色或非常成功。
例句："那个舞蹈完全 slay 了评委！"
