```markdown
## Slay (verb) – 狠抽 (verb)
**English:**
- **Definition:** To perform exceptionally well, especially in a manner that impresses or excels over others.
- **Usage Example:** "She slayed her math test!" / "她这次数学考试表现得太出色了，简直就是吊炸天！"
**Chinese:**
- **Definition:** 指表现得特别好，特别是在某种表现或竞赛中大幅超过他人。
- **Usage Example:** "她这次数学考试表现得太出色了，简直就是吊炸天！"（She aced her math test and blew everyone away!）
```
Note: 狠抽 (hěn chōu) is another slang term that means "to impress" or "to do exceptionally well," derived from broken English where "to be awesome" is literally translated as "to抽" (to take or draw), hence the "抽" (chōu) in Chinese slang. It's often used in the context of impressive performance or skill.
