### Slay (简体中文: 斩扎)
**English:** Slay
- **Definition:** To perform exceptionally well or impressively, often in a competitive or challenging situation.
- **Bilingual Example Sentences:**
- "She slayed her presentation in front of the whole team!" (她在全队面前的演讲表现得太棒了！)
- "He slayed at the gaming competition last weekend." (上周末的游戏比赛中他表现出色！)
**Alternative Explanations:**
- To dominate, to crush, to excel, to rock, to dominate, or to ace something.
