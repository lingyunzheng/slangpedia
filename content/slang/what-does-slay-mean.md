# Slay 🦂
## English
**To slay:** To thoroughly dominate or excel at something, often to such an extent that it’s impressive or inspiring. It's like when you ace a test or totally nail a dance routine.
#### Example:
- "She slay in her new outfit, everyone was impressed!"
## 中文
**slay：** 完美地主导或在某事中表现出色，通常让别人印象深刻。就像是你在考试中表现出色，或是一场舞蹈表演做得非常棒。
#### 例句：
- “她穿新衣服非常好，每个人都感到印象深刻！”
