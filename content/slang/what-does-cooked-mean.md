## English: Cooked
### Meaning
- **Literal**: Food that has been fully prepared and cooked.
- **Slang**: Gone, done, or finished. Often used to describe a situation that is no longer salvageable or when plans have been fully executed.
### Usage Examples
- "Our chances in the competition are cooked; we're just not as prepared as the other teams."
- "She cooked her credibility after those rumors spread."
## 中文: 烧（煮）好了
### 含义
- **字面意义**: 食物被完全烹饪好了。
- **俚语**: 事情已经完了，或者计划已经执行完毕。常用来形容一个情况已经无法挽回，或者计划已经被彻底执行。
### 使用示例
- "我们在比赛中的机会已经烧好了；我们没有其他队伍准备得充分。"
- "谣言传开后，她的信誉就烧好了。"
