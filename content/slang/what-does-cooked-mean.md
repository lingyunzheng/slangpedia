## English
**Cooked**
- Meaning: Something or someone is in a bad situation, often due to previous actions leading to trouble.
- Example: "The company is cooked; they mishandled that contract."
## 中文
**煮熟了（Cooked）**
- 含义：某人或某事物处于一种糟糕的情况中，通常是由于之前的错误行为导致了麻烦。
- 例句：公司煮熟了，他们把那份合同弄砸了。
