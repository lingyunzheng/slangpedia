```markdown
## 煮熟了 (COOKED)
**中文解释**：
煮熟了：通常用来形容某人或某情况已经到了极点，没有办法再挽救或改善。类似于“完蛋了”或“没有希望了”的意思。
**英文解释**：
Cooked: This slang term is used to describe a situation or person that has reached a critical point, and there's no chance of rescue or improvement. It's equivalent to saying "it's all over" or "hopeless."
```
