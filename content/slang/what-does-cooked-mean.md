## English Explanation
**Cooked**: This slang term is commonly used to indicate that something is extraordinary or excellent. For example, if you say "That meal is cooked," it means the food is exceptionally delicious.
### Example Usage
- "Man, this new restaurant has cooked up a pretty good menu!"
## 中文解释
**煮熟了**: 这个网络用语通常用来表示某事很棒或出色。例如，如果你说"这顿饭煮熟了"，意思是指饭菜非常美味。
### 示例用法
- "这家新开的餐馆做饭做得真好啊！"
