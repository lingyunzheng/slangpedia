## English
Cooked: 
- **Meaning**: Already done or resolved, often used in a positive sense when something is complete or successful.
- **Example**: "The plan is cooked; we've secured all the funding and approvals."
## 中文
煮好了：
- **含义**：已完成或解决，常用于表示某事已完成或成功。
- **例句**：计划煮好了；我们已经拿到了所有的资金和批准。
