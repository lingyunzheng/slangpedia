## 英文 slang: cooked
### 中文 slang: 吃过了 (chī guo le)
**Meaning:**
- In English: "Burned" or "Cooked" (in a bad way), meaning something has gone wrong or isn't good.
- In Chinese: "吃过" (literally means "eaten" in Chinese), but it's used to describe something that's completely gone wrong, messed up, or in a bad state, similar to "cooked" in the sense of irreparably damaged.
**Usage Example:**
- English: "This computer is totally cooked - the hard drive crashed."
- Chinese: "这台电脑彻底吃过了 - 硬盘崩溃了。" (Zhè táierénlùn tèt Choose one - kěkè guo le, yìngkuǎn kē le.)
**Note:**
- "Cooked" can also mean "high" or "stoned" (for drugs), while "吃过" doesn't carry the same connotation.
