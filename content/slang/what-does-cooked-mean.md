```markdown
# "Cooked" 被煮了
**English:** "Cooked" in slang can mean you're in a tough situation or you've been put in a difficult spot, often by someone else. It can also imply that something is fake, fake news, or untrue.
**Chinese:** "被煮了" 在网络用语中通常表示你处在了一个难缠的局面或者被别人陷害了。也可以用来形容某事是假的、假新闻或者不真实的。
```
In both contexts, these terms suggest that something challenging or tricky has happened, indicating a negative situation that may or may not be directly caused by someone else.
