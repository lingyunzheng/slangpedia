```markdown
## English Explanation: Cooked
### Meaning
- **Literal**: Processed, cooked food.
- **Slang**: In casual or informal contexts, "cooked" often means something is "bad" or "not working," especially in terms of money or deals.
### Usage Examples
- *Literal*: I just heated up some cooked chicken for dinner.
- *Slang*: That deal is cooked; we lost a lot of money on it.
---
## Chinese Explanation: 烧 (qiú / chōu) / 炸 (zhà)
### 意思
- **字面意思**: 加工过的食物。
- **俚语**: 在非正式的语境中，"烧" 或 "炸" 常用来表示某个东西 "出了问题" 或 "坏掉了"，尤其在谈及金钱或交易时。
### 使用例句
- *字面意思*: 我用电饭煲加热了一盘煮好的米饭。
- *俚语*: 这笔交易搞砸了，我们在这上面赔了很多钱。
```
