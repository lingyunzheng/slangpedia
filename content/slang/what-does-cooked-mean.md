## English: Cooked
### Definition
- **Feeling**: Extremely good, excellent, or successful. This term is often used to describe a win or a situation that went well.
- **Example Usage**: "Our team totally cooked that game last night."
## 中文: 烧烤（cooked）
### 释义
- **感受**: 极其棒、出色或成功。这个词常用来形容胜利或者状况良好。
- **例句**: "我们昨晚的比赛简直是我们完全获胜了。"
