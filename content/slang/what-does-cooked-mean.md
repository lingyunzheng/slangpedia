## Cooked
### English
- **Meaning**: When something is **done for you** or **taken care of**. You don't need to worry about it.
- **Example**: "I cooked my homework; I didn't have to do anything."
### 中文
- **含义**：表示某事已经被处理好，不需要你自己操心。
- **例句**："我的作业被搞定啦，我不用管了。"
