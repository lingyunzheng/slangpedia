```markdown
## English: Cooked
### Meaning
- **Definition:** Slang used to describe a situation, plan, or person that is successful, complete, or well-played.
- **Usage:** "The poker player used an advanced strategy and was cooked when a future opponent used a similar tactic, but it doesn't matter since he was already ahead in the game."
## 中文: 炒了 / 烧烤
### 含义
- **定义：** 这个词用来描述一种成功、计划周密或技巧高超的情况。
- **用法：** "这个扑克玩家用了高级策略，但他仍然被那位后来的对手利用相似的技巧，搞定了，但这没关系，因为他已经处于领先位置。"
```
Note: The Chinese translation provided is a more idiomatic equivalent to the English slang rather than a direct translation. The term "cooked" often doesn't have a direct equivalent in Chinese slang, hence the example uses similar contexts to convey the meaning.
