## English: Cooked
### Chinese: 被搞定 (Bèi dǎgàn)
**Explanation:**
Cooked in English slang often means something is done, fixed, or taken care of. In Chinese, "被搞定" (bèi dǎgàn) is a colloquial expression that carries a similar meaning. It's used to describe a situation where a problem has been successfully resolved, or someone has been successfully taken care of or controlled. 
**Usage Example:**
- English: "I've cooked up a plan to surprise my girlfriend."
- Chinese: "我为给女朋友搞了个惊喜的计划。" (Wǒ wèi gěi nǚpéngyǒu gǎo le jīngxǐ de jìhuà.)
**Literal Translation:**
- English: "Cooked"
- Chinese: "搞定" (Dǎgàn)
This term is very casual and can be used in informal settings, like among friends or in entertainment contexts.
