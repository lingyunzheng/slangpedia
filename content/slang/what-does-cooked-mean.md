## English: Cooked
- **Definition**: Being tricked or cheated.
- **Example Sentence**: "Don't fall for his tricks; he's cooked the books to show a profit."
## 中文: 炒了
- **定义**: 被骗了或被蒙混过去。
- **例句**: "别被他骗了，他搞的那些账目都是炒的，只是为了显示盈利。"
