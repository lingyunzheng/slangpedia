## English Slang: Cooked
- **Meaning**: When something is done, solved, or decided.
- **Example**: "The problem was cooked by the team" means the team has successfully solved the problem.
- **Usage**: Commonly used in situations where an outcome or solution is already determined or finalized.
## Bilingual Slang: 烤焦 (Kào jiāo)
- **Meaning**: 类似 "煮熟" 或 "搞定"，表示事情已经解决或者达成一致。
- **Example**: "这个问题已经被团队烤焦了" 等同于 "The problem was cooked by the team."
- **Usage**: 用于表示某个问题已经被妥善处理，或者某个方案已经被确认。
