# Slang Meaning of "cooked" in Chinese and English
## English: "cooked"
### Chinese: **煮烂** (zhǔ làn) 
- **Literal Translation**: Boiled until soft
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation**: In English, "cooked" can refer to a person or situation where the plan or situation has been manipulated or fixed in a certain way to favor one side. In the Chinese slang term "煮烂** (zhǔ làn)", it conveys the idea of something being thoroughly done or rigged. For instance, "They say this game was cooked from the start" can be translated to "他们说从开始这游戏就煮烂了" (Tāmen shuō cóng kāishǐ zhè yóuxì jiù zhǔ làn le).
## English: "cooked up"
### Chinese: **悄悄地策划** (qiāo qiāo de kuàihuà) 
- **Literal Translation**: Quietly planned
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation**: "Cooked up" means to plan something secretly or in a tricky way. In Chinese, "悄悄地策划" (qiāo qiāo de kuàihuà) captures the essence of this, where "悄悄地" (qiāo qiāo de) means quietly and "策划" (kuàihuà) means to plan. For example, "They cooked up a scheme to cheat in the exam" can be roughly translated as "他们悄悄地策划了一个作弊的方案" (Tāmen qiāo qiāo de kuàihuà le yīgè chewearde fāng'àn).
## English: "cooked together"
### Chinese: **一起煮** (yīqǐ zhǔ) 
- **Literal Translation**: Cooked together
- **Bilingual Slang Explanation**: When used in this context, "cooked together" implies that something is done in collaboration or as a result of team effort. "一起煮" (yīqǐ zhǔ) means cooked together or done together. For example, "The team really cooked together to win the championship" could be translated as "这个队伍真正地一起煮, 一起赢得了冠军" (Zhège duìwèi zhēnzhēng de yīqǐ zhǔ, yīqǐ liúdé le jiànguó).
