## English: Cooked
### Chinese: 搞定 (gǎo dìng) / 煮熟 (zhǔ shú)
**Meaning:** 
When something is "cooked," it means it is handled or resolved perfectly, with no issues remaining. It can also literally mean something has been prepared or finished to the point of being just right.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "The assignment is cooked; I have everything done and handed in."
- Chinese: "作业搞定了，我一切都准备好了。" (zhuò yè gǎo dìng le, wǒ dōu yīgèng gòng bèi zuò hǎo le.)
---
In slang:
- English: "He has the project cooked, no need to worry about anything."
- Chinese: "他这个项目搞定了，什么都不用担心。" (tā zhège xiàn nèi gǎo dìng le, wèi shén me dōu bù yòng xiōng xīn.)
This phrase is used to indicate that a situation has been resolved, managed, or completed to the point where it's entirely satisfactory or trouble-free.
