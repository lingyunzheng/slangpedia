# Slang Term: "Cooked"
## English
**Meaning:** When something is "cooked," it can mean that it has been tampered with or rigged in a way that it is unfair or fraudulent. It can also refer to being sick or ill.
**Example Usage:**
- "The results of the contest were cooked, so the winner wasn't really fair to everyone."
- "I'm feeling a bit cooked today; I think I need to rest."
## 中文
**意思:** 当某个东西或比赛被认为是"cooked"时，它通常意味着被篡改或策划得不公平或欺诈。这个词也可以用来形容某人生病或不舒服。
**例句:**
- "比赛的结果被弄虚作假了，所以真正的赢家并没有体现公平。"
- "今天我感觉有点生病了，我需要休息一下。"
