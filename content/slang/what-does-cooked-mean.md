## English
**Cooked** - This slang term is often used to indicate that something is completely understandable or that the speaker is aware of all the details. For example, if someone says "That's cooked," they're saying that they understand the situation fully, and there are no hidden surprises. It can also mean that a plan or situation is finalized and ready to proceed without further complications.
## Chinese
**煮熟了** - 这个网络用语通常用来表示某件事情完全明了，或者说话者对所有细节都了如指掌。比如，如果有人说“这煮熟了”，他们就表示已经完全明白情况，没有未知的变数。这个词也可以用来表示一个计划或情况已经最终化，准备实施。
**例子：**
English: "I've got all the details now, the plan is cooked."
Chinese: "我现在都知道了，计划已经煮熟了。"
