## Slang Term: Cooked
### English
**Meaning**: When something is completely finished, often to the point of being overdone or ruined.
- Example: "The chicken was cooked when I tried to eat it, it was dry and tasteless."
### Chinese (简体)
**意思**: 当事情完全做完了，有时表示做得过头或者搞砸了。
- 示例：当我试着吃的时候，鸡肉已经煮过了，变得干瘪乏味。
### Bilingual Explanation
**English**: When something is completely finished, often to the point of being overdone or ruined.
**Chinese (简体)**: 当事情完全做完了，有时表示做得过头或者搞砸了。
