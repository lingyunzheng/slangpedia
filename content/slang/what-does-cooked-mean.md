```markdown
## Cooked (煮熟了)
**English:** Cooked - When something is completely analyzed, understood, or done to the point of being very obvious or common.
**Chinese:** 煮熟了 (zhǔ shú le) - 形容某件事被彻底分析或理解，或者被做得非常明显或普遍。常见于讨论某个问题已经被完全探讨清楚，没有太多新意的情况。
例子：  
- English: The theory is cooked. There's nothing new to discuss.
- Chinese: 这个理论煮熟了，没有什么新内容可以讨论了。
```
This explanation provides a clear definition and usage of the slang term "cooked" in both English and Chinese, along with a corresponding example to illustrate its application.
