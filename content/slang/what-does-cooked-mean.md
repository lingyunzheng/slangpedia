## Cooked (煮熟了) - 煮熟了
### English
- **Meaning**: Literally means “cooked” which in slang can refer to being “burned,” “caught,” or “got in trouble.”
- **Usage**: If you do something silly or get into some trouble and someone wants to let you know indirectly, they might say "You're cooked." This implies that you're in a difficult situation and probably won't be able to get out of it easily. 
### Chinese
- **意思**: 像字面上的“煮熟了”，在俚语中则常用来指“被抓住”、“被发现”或“陷入困境”。
- **用法**: 当你干了件蠢事或者进了麻烦，有人想要间接地提醒你的时候，可能会说“你煮熟了”。这表示你处于一个艰难的情况，很可能难以脱身。
