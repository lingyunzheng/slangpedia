# Slang Meaning of "Cooked"
## English
**Cooked:** 
- If something is **cooked**, it means it's excellent, top-notch, or perfect. It can also mean a situation or deal has been set up to go in favor of one side, often implying unfairness or manipulation.
## Chinese
**煮熟 (zu5 shuu4):**
- 如果某件事情或某种情况是**煮熟**的，它意味着非常出色、极好或完美。它也可以指某个情况或交易已经被安排有利某一方，通常意味着不公平或被操纵。
