# Slang Explanation: "cooked"
## English
**Meaning:** When something or someone is "cooked," it generally means it's excellent, great, or perfect.
**Example:** "This pizza is cooked! It's the best I've had in a long time."
## Chinese
**含义:** 当某物或某人被形容为“煮熟了”时，通常意味着它非常棒、超赞或完美。
**例句:** "这比萨煮熟了！这是我很久没吃过的最好吃的了。"
