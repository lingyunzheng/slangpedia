## English-Simplified Chinese Slang: "cooked"
### English
- **Literal Meaning**: Prepared, baked, or done
- **Slang Meaning**: Used to describe a situation that has been resolved in someone's favor or in a positive manner
#### Example:
- "The boss was mad about the missing report, but the intern cooked this one up really well. Now everything's fine." 
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 
- **字面意思**：准备好的，烤的或者做好了
- **流行用法**：形容某件事情以对自己有利、积极的方式被解决
- **例句**：老板对丢失的报告很生气，但实习生做的非常好。现在问题解决了。
