## English: Cooked
### Chinese: 烤（kǎo）了
**Explanation:**
* English: "Cooked" is slang used to indicate that someone has been caught or tricked in a situation. It can also mean that something is completely cooked or ruined.
* Chinese: "烤了" (kǎo le) literally means "roasted" or "cooked" but it has a slang meaning of being "got one," "tricked," or "caught out." It implies that a person has messed up or that a plan has failed.
**Usage Example:**
* English: "I think I’m cooked, they found out about the scheme."
* Chinese: "我觉得完了，他们发现了这个 scheme。" (Wǒ juéde wán le, tāmen fāxuéle zhège shénpǐ.)
**Context:**
This slang is commonly used in casual conversations or informal settings to express that someone is in trouble or that a plan has not worked out as intended.
