## 蒸熟 (zhēng shú) // Cooked (souped)
### Chinese Explanation:
“蒸熟” (zhēng shú) is a metaphorical expression often used in a teasing or lighthearted way. It mostly gets used in a negative context, meaning someone or something has become too familiar, obvious, or even plain and unappealing. It implies that something has been "cooked" or "overcooked" to the point where it no longer has its original flavor or appeal. 
### English Explanation:
"Cooked" (souped) is slang, mostly used in a teasing or dismissive manner. It's when something or someone has become overly familiar or unexciting, as if it has been "cooked" until it no longer has its original flavor or appeal. This term can be used to describe a situation or person that is no longer interesting or appealing due to overexposure or lack of creativity.
