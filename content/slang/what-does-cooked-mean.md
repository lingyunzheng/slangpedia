# Slang Explanation for "Cooked" (Cooked)
## English
**Cooked**: 
- *Meaning*: Slang term used to describe something that is fake, fake news, or a scam.
- *Example*: "I saw a job posting that was cooked - it was just a way to get my personal information."
## 中文
**煮过的 (zhuiguò de)**:
- *含义*: 表示某个东西是假的、谣言或者骗局。
- *例句*: "我看到有一个招聘信息是煮过的，其实就是为了获取我的个人信息。"
