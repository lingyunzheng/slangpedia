# Slang Explanation for "Cooked"
## English
**Cooked:** 
- Origin: This slang term comes from the idea that something is completely destroyed, messed up, or messed with so thoroughly that it is gone. It derives from the image of food being ruined by overcooking.
- Usage Example: "Man, I left my car unlocked with the keys inside, now the windshields, seats, and even dash are broken and all the electronics cooked!"
- Synonyms: Wrecked, F*cked up, Ruined
## 中文
**煮烂（cooked）:**
- 来源：这个词源自食物被煮烂的概念，比喻事情被搞砸或损坏得无法修复。
- 使用示例："哥们儿，我把车门忘了锁上，钥匙还放在里面，现在挡风玻璃、座椅和整个仪表板都破了，电子设备全都煮烂了！"
- 同义词：炸了（fucked up）、搞砸了（ruined）、毁了（wrecked）
