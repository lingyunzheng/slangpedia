## 中文解释
ate and left no crumbs: 吃得一干二净，连一点碎屑都没有留下。
## 英文解释
ate and left no crumbs: Cleaned the plate completely, leaving no bits or crumbs behind. This phrase is often used to describe someone who finishes every bit of food they have, showing they are very hungry or have a great appetite. Theres also a connotation of thriftiness or appreciation for the food served.
