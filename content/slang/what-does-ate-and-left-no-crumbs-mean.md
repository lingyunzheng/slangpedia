## Ate and Left No Crumbs
**English:** A phrase used to describe someone who eats all the food without leaving any leftovers.
**Chinese:** 形容一个人把所有的食物都吃光了，没有任何剩余。 
- English Slang Explanation: Literal translation would be "ate until no crumbs were left." This phrase is often used in a casual or humorous context to highlight a person's voracious appetite or emphasize that something (usually food or a resource) was completely consumed.
- Chinese Slang Explanation: 形容一个人吃东西非常彻底，直到连一丝碎屑都没有留下。这个短语通常用来描述一个人胃口很好，或者强调某种东西（通常是食物或资源）被完全消耗了。
Example Usage:
- English: "Steve ate and left no crumbs at the office potluck. We had to buy more food for the next one."
- Chinese: "斯蒂夫在办公室聚餐时把所有的食物都吃光了，所以我们不得不为下一次聚会再买些食物。"
