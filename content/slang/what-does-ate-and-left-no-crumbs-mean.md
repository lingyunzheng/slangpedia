## Ate and Left No Crumbs (English)
**Meaning**: Literally, it means eating all the food without leaving any residue. Figuratively, it can describe someone who consumes an opportunity, resource, or even someone's attention completely, leaving nothing for others.
**Example**: "After the party, John ate and left no crumbs on the snacks. By the end, there was nothing left for anyone else."
### Explanation in Chinese
**含义**: 字面上的意思是把所有食物都吃完，连渣都不剩下。比喻上，它可以描述某人完全利用了一个机会、资源，甚至是别人的注意力，不留任何余地给其他人。
**例句**：“聚会后，约翰把所有的零食都吃光了，连渣都不剩。到头来，别人什么也拿不到。”
