# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
- **Meaning**: Consumed every bit of something, leaving nothing behind.
- **Usage**: Generally used to describe someone who is very thorough and has consumed all available resources or food.
- **Example**: "She ate that sandwich and left no crumbs, I saw her finger scrape the plate clean!"
## 中文
- **含义**：将某物吃得一干二净，不留任何残渣。
- **用法**：通常用来形容某人非常彻底，吃掉了所有可获取的食物或者资源。
- **例句**："她把那个三明治吃得连渣都不剩，我看到她的手指还把盘子擦得干干净净！"
