### Ate and Left No Crumbs
**English:**  
"Ended up eating all of something and didn't leave any leftovers."  
Example: "He ate the whole pizza and left no crumbs. Talk about clean!"
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
“把某东西吃得一干二净，连一点 residue 都不留。”  
例句：他把整个比萨吃了个精光，连一点残渣都没有留下，真是吃得干净！
**Explanation:**  
This phrase is used to describe someone who eats everything they have, consuming it completely without leaving any leftovers. It's often used in a context where something is finished or consumed completely, leaving no trace or residue.
