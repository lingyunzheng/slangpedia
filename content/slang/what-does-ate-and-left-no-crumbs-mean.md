## Ate and Left No Crumbs
**English:**  
This phrase is used to describe someone who has eaten all of their food, leaving absolutely nothing behind. It implies that the person ate everything, nothing was wasted.
**汉语:**  
这个短语用来描述一个人把所有的食物都吃光了，连一点残渣都没有留下。这表示这个人吃得一干二净，没有任何浪费。
