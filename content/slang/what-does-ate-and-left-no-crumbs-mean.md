## Ate and Left No Crumbs
### English
**Meaning:** To eat everything and leave nothing behind, often implying that one ate all the food without leaving any leftovers.
**Usage:** I attended that party expecting some leftover snacks, but the host ate and left no crumbs!
### Chinese
**意思:** 指将所有食物都吃光了，不留任何残余，通常意味着一个人把所有食物都吃完了，没有留下一点剩饭。
**用法:** 我去参加那个聚会是希望能分到一些剩余的小吃，但主人把所有的食物都吃光了，连一点不剩的都没有留下。
