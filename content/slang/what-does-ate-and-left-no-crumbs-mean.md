## Ate and Left No Crumbs (English)
**Explanation:** This phrase means someone ate all of the food without letting any leftovers. It's often used to describe someone who is really hungry or does a thorough job of eating everything in sight.
**Example:** "John ate and left no crumbs at the party. He must have been really hungry!"
## 吃干抹净 (Chī gān mā jìng, Chinese)
**Explanation:** "吃干抹净" literally means "to eat dry and wipe clean." This phrase is used to describe someone who eats all the food, leaving no residue. It is a colloquial way of saying that someone is very thorough in their eating, similar to "ate and left no crumbs."
**Example:** "李华在聚会上吃干抹净，真是大快朵颐！" (Lǐ Huá zài jù huì shàn shàng chī gān mā jìng，zhēn shì dà kuài duǒ yí！) — Li Hua wiped clean at the party, truly enjoying his meal!
