## 肆意吃光 (Sìyì chīguāng)
**Ate and left no crumbs**
- **英文解释:** "Ate and left no crumbs" is an idiom used to describe someone who consumes all the food available, leaving none. This phrase is often used in a derisive way to criticize someone for being so greedy that they have taken everything, leaving nothing for others.
- **中文解释:** "肆意吃光"用来形容一个人把所有食物都吃得精光，没有留下任何残渣。这个词组通常用来批评某人因过于贪婪，以至于把一切据为己有，让别人一无所有。
