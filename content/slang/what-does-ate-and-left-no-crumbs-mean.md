# Ate and Left No Crumbs
**English Slang:** Ate and Left No Crumbs
This phrase is often used to describe a person who consumes a lot of food or resources, leaving nothing behind. It implies that the person was extremely greedy or didn't share, even with a small amount remaining.
**中文俚语:** 吃得干干净净，连渣都不剩
这个词组用来形容一个人吃掉了所有的食物或资源，不留一丝余地。它暗示这个人非常贪婪或自私，连剩下一丁点都不会留下。
Usage Example:
- **English:** She really did eat and left no crumbs at the party; the snacks were gone in no time.
- **中文:** 她在聚会上真的吃得干干净净，连渣都不剩；零食眨眼间就被吃光了。
