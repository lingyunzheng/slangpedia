# "Ate and Left No Crumbs" in English and Chinese
## English
**Meaning:** 
If someone "ate and left no crumbs," it means that they consumed all of something without leaving any leftovers. It can also be used to describe someone who is very thorough and takes full advantage of a resource or situation, ensuring that there is nothing left over.
**Example:**
- In a job interview, if someone asked for all the company's confidential data and used it without any hesitation, they would have "ate and left no crumbs."
## 中文
**含义:**
如果有人“吃了连一点碎屑也没留下”，这意指他们把什么东西都吃得一干二净，没有留下一点残渣。也可以用来形容某人非常彻底，充分利用资源或情况，确保没有剩余的东西。
**例句:**
- 在一次面试中，如果有人请求获取所有公司的机密信息，并且毫不犹豫地使用了这些信息，那么他们就可以说是“吃了连一点碎屑也没留下”了。
