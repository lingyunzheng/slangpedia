# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
### Explanation
This phrase is used to describe someone who eats a lot and finishes their meal completely, leaving absolutely no leftovers. It can be used in a positive or negative context, depending on the situation. For example, if friends are sharing a pizza, and one person eats it all up, leaving no toppings or pieces, they would have "ate and left no crumbs."
### Example Usage
- "John ate the entire pizza and left no crumbs, poor Linda has to eat the crust now."
## Chinese
### 解释
这句话用来描述一个人吃得很多，把食物完全吃完，一点都没有剩下。它可以在正向或负面的语境中使用，具体取决于情况。比如，如果几个朋友共同享用一个披萨，一个人把整个披萨都吃完了，连一点剩余的都没有，那么这个人“把食物吃得一丝不剩。”
### 例句
- "约翰把整个披萨都吃完了，连一点都没有剩下，现在可怜的琳达只能吃饼边了。"
