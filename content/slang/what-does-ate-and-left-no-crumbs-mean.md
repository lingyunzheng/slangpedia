# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
**Usage**: When someone has eaten all of something and there's absolutely nothing left.
**Example**: "She ate the entire pizza and left no crumbs."
## 中文
**用法**: 形容一个人把某样东西吃完了，不留任何剩余。
**示例**: “她把整个比萨都吃光了，连渣都不剩。”
> **Note**: This expression is often used in situations where someone is seen as wasteful or greedy, or to emphasize how much was consumed.
