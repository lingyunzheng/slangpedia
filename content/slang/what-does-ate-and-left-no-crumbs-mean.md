### Ate and Left No Crumbs
**English:** 
This phrase is used to describe someone who eats everything they can, leaving absolutely nothing behind. It's often used in a negative context to criticize someone for being greedy or wasteful.
**Chinese:** 
这个表达用来形容一个人把所有能吃的东西都吃完了，一点不剩。通常用于贬义，批评某人贪婪或浪费。
- **Example in English:** "Sally ate and left no crumbs at the reception, making everyone else hungry."
- **Example in Chinese:** "莎莉在招待会上把所有能吃的东西都吃完了，让其他人感觉又饿了。"
