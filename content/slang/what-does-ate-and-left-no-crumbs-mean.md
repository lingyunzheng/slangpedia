### 英文：E ate and left no crumbs
#### 中文：A吃了连渣都不剩
- **Explanation**: In both English and Chinese, this phrase is used to describe someone who eats so much that nothing is left behind. It's often used in a negative or joking context to imply that someone has been particularly greedy or has consumed all available resources, leaving nothing for others.
- **Context**: You might use this phrase when describing a friend who polished off an entire pizza or when complaining about someone who gathered all the snacks, leaving nothing for the others.
