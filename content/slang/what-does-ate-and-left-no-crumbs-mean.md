### 中文 slang: 吃干抹净 (chī gàn mǒ jìng)
**Explanation:** 
这个短语用来形容在吃完食物后，一点残渣都不剩，非常干净地吃完。类似于英文中的 "ate and left no crumbs"。通常用于夸赞某人吃东西非常干净，或者形容一个人非常节省，不浪费。
**Example Sentence:**
他每次吃饭都吃干抹净，一点儿也不浪费。
（He always eats up the food completely without leaving any scraps.）
### 英文 slang: Ate and left no crumbs
**Explanation:** 
This phrase is used to describe someone who has eaten so completely that not a single crumb of food remains. It implies that the person ate all the food available, leaving nothing behind. This expression can be used to praise someone's thoroughness and clean eating habits, or to describe someone who is very frugal and doesn't waste anything.
**Example Sentence:**
She ate and left no crumbs, finishing the entire plate of cookies.
(She ate and left no crumbs, finishing the entire plate of cookies.)
