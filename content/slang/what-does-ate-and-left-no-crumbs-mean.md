## "Ate and Left No Crumbs"
### English
- **Definition:** Refers to someone who consumes all of something, leaving nothing behind.
- **Example:** "John really went to town on the pizza, he ate and left no crumbs."
### 中文
- **定义:** 指某人将某物全部吃光，不留任何残渣。
- **例句:** "约翰对披萨可真是吃得很干净，连一点儿残渣都没留下。"
