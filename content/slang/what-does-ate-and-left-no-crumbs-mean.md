# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Ate and Left No Crumbs"
**English:**  
This phrase is often used to describe someone who eats a lot of food or uses a lot of something, leaving hardly anything behind. It can also describe someone who cleans their plate or uses up all the available resources. The phrase comes from the literal idea of eating until no crumbs are left on a plate.
**中文:**  
这个短语用来形容一个人吃掉了很多食物或用掉了大量的某样东西，几乎不留一点余地。也可以形容一个人把盘子里的食物吃干净或是用尽所有可用的资源。这个表达起源于吃完盘子上几乎不留任何面包屑的字面意思。
