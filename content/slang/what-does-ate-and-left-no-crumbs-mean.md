# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
**Meaning:** When someone finishes everything from a food item and there’s absolutely nothing left.
**Example Usage:** After the party, Joann came home to find her favorite cookies "ate and left no crumbs."
## Chinese
**意思:** 当某人把食物吃完，留不下一丝痕迹，一根头发丝都不剩。
**用例示例:** 在派对结束后，乔安回到家发现她最爱的饼干“吃了个精光，连一点饼干屑都不剩。”
