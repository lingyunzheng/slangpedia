## English
### Slang Term: *Ate and Left No Crumbs*
- **Meaning**: Describes someone who consumes every available resource or opportunity, leaving nothing for others. Used to refer to someone who is extremely greedy or selfish.
- **Example**: "John really ate and left no crumbs at that meeting. He took all the credit for the project and didn't give anyone else a chance."
## 中文
### 热门俚语：*一扫而光，不剩一丝*
- **含义**：形容某人把所有可用的资源或机会都毫不保留地利用了，结果什么也没留给别人。用来形容某人极其贪婪或自私。
- **例句**："在那次会议上，John 一扫而光，不剩一丝。他对项目所有的功劳都据为己有，没有给其他人任何机会。"
