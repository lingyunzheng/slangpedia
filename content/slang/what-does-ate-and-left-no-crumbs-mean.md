## Ate and Left No Crumbs
### English
- **Meaning**: To completely finish or consume something, leaving nothing behind.
- **Example**: After the party, Sarah ate all the cookies and left no crumbs.
### Chinese (简体中文)
- **意思**: 完全吃完或消耗掉某物，不留任何残余。
- **例句**：聚会上，莎拉把所有的饼干全都吃完了，连一点点碎屑也没留下。
