## Ate and Left No Crumbs
### Chinese (简体中文): “吃干净，不留渣”
**Explanation:** 这个表达意味着某人吃东西非常干净，连一丝渣子都不剩。就像用一块面包擦干净盘子一样，吃完后盘子上一片渣都不剩。
**Example Usage:** 你在聚会上吃零食怎么会这样？这桌子都被你吃干净，不留渣了！
### English: "Ate Clean and Left No Crumbs"
**Explanation:** This expression means someone ate something very thoroughly, leaving no trace behind. It's as if they used their food to clean up their plate so meticulously that not a single crumb remains.
**Example Usage:** How could you clean the snack table like this at the party? You ate so clean and left no crumb behind on that table!
