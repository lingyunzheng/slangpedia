### 英文 slang: ate and left no crumbs  
#### 中文 slang: 吃干抹净  
**Explanation:**  
- **English:** "Ate and left no crumbs" is a phrase used to describe someone who has completely finished their food and consumed every bit of it, leaving no leftover. It's often used in a positive or approving sense.
- **Chinese:** "吃干抹净" (chī gān mǒ jìng) literally translates to "eat completely, wipe clean" and refers to someone who eats all the food they have, leaving no remnants. This phrase is also used in a positive or approving context, especially when someone isn't wasteful.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** Sam always eats and leaves no crumbs, so there's never any waste.
- **Chinese:** 山姆总是吃干抹净，所以从来不会有剩饭。
(Shān Měi zǒng shì chī gān mǒ jìng, suǒ yǐ cóng lái huǒ nǐ huó bù yǒu shèn fàn.)
- **Chinese to English:** 明明吃了个精光，连一颗糖渣都没有留下。
(Míng míng chī le gè jīng guāng, lián yī kē táng zhā dōu méiyǒu diànlá)
= Ming Ming ate it completely, not leaving a single sugar crumb.
