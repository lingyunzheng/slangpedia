### 英文 slang: Ate and left no crumbs
#### Explanation:
- **Definition:** A phrase used to describe someone who eats all of their food without leaving any leftovers.
- **Usage Example:** "John is always so hungry; he can eat a whole pizza and leave no crumbs!"
### 中文 slang: 吃得干干净净，不见碎屑
#### 解释：
- **定义：** 用来形容一个人把食物吃得一干二净，连剩下的碎屑都没有留下。
- **用法示例：** "约翰总是特别饿，一个比萨饼他吃完后连碎屑都看不见！"
