# "Ate and Left No Crumbs"
## English
- **Meaning**: Describes someone who consumes all of something, leaving no remainder.
- **Example**: "After the party, John ate and left no crumbs. The entire pizza was gone!"
## Chinese
- **含义**: 描述一个人把所有东西都吃光了，没有留下任何残渣。
- **例句**："聚会后，约翰把所有的东西都吃光了。整个比萨饼都被他吃完了！"
