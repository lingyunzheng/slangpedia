## "Eaten and Left No Crumbs"
### English
- **Meaning**: Consumed something completely, leaving nothing behind.
- **Example**: "She ate the whole cake and left no crumbs at the party."
### Chinese
- **意思**: 完全吃光了某样东西，什么都没剩。
- **例句**: "她在聚会上把整个蛋糕都吃光了，连一点碎屑都没有留下。"
