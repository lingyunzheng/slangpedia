```markdown
## Ate and Left No Crumbs (English)

- **Meaning**: Completely ate everything, not leaving any leftovers.
  
- **Example**: "At the potluck, John ate and left no crumbs! He must have really loved those brownies."

## 吃得一干二净 (不吃就扫光了) (Chinese)

- **Meaning**: 完全吃光了，没有留下任何残渣。
  
- **Example**: " party 上，John 吃得一干二净 (不吃就扫光了)! 他对那些曲奇肯定非常喜欢！"
```

Note that "不吃就扫光了" is a colloquial expression that can be used as a translation of "ate and left no crumbs", which literally means "if not eaten, would be swept clean", emphasizing that nothing was left.
