# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
**Meaning:** To consume almost everything without leaving any leftovers.
**Example Sentence:** After the party, Tom ate and left no crumbs, leaving the snacks all gone.
## Chinese
**意思:** 吃得精光，不留一点残渣。
**例句:** 在聚会上，汤姆把所有的零食都吃光了，连一点渣都不剩。
