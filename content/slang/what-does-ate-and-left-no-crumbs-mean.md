### English: "ate and left no crumbs"
This phrase means that someone finished a meal or consumed an item completely, leaving nothing left behind. It can also imply someone was very greedy or selfish by not sharing and taking everything for themselves.
*Example:* "Jenny ate and left no crumbs from the whole pizza, she really had a big appetite today."
### 中文: "吃了干净，连渣都不剩"
这个短语指的是某人把食物或物品完全吃完，不留任何余物。它也可以用来形容某人非常自私，不与他人分享，把所有东西都独吞了。
*例句:* "珍妮把整个披萨都吃完了，连渣都不剩，她今天胃口真好。"
