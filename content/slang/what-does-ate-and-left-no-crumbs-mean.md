### Ate and Left No Crumbs (English)
**Meaning:** To eat an entire meal or something without stopping, ensuring you've consumed every single bite.
**Example:** "He was so hungry that he ate and left no crumbs from the pizza."
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **Ate and Left No Crumbs (English):** To eat an entire meal or something without stopping, ensuring you've consumed every single bite.
- **English in Chinese:** 吃得干干净净 (chī dé gān gān gèng jìng)
- **Explanation in Chinese:** 完整地把所有食物吃光，连一点渣都不剩。 (wán zhěng de bǎ suǒ yǒu shí wù chī guāng, lián yī diǎn jiā fēi bù shèng.)
### Ate and Left No Crumbs (Chinese)
- **吃了什么都没有剩 (chī le shén me dōu móu yǒu shèng):** 吃完了没有任何剩余的食物。
- **Explanation:** 完整地把某样食物或饭菜全部吃掉，不留任何残渣。 (wán zhěng de bǎ mǒ yàng shí wù huò mǎian dān tīng chī diào, bù liú ěr bù cán jiā.)
