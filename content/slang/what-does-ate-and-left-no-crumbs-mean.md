# "Eated and Left No Crumbs" 
## English Explanation
- **Meaning:** To have consumed all of something, leaving absolutely nothing behind.
- **Usage Example:** “John ate the entire pie and left no crumbs. Everyone was left disappointed.”
## Chinese Explanation
- **含义:** 完全吃光了某样东西，不留一点残渣。
- **使用示例:** “约翰把整个派都吃完了，连一点残渣都没剩下。大家都很失望。”
