### "Eat and Leave No Crumbs"
**English:**  
This phrase is often used to describe someone who eats everything they can, leaving nothing behind. The implication is that this person is extremely thorough in consuming everything available to them.
**Chinese (translated in slang):**  
这个词组用来形容一个人把所有能吃的东西都一扫而光，不留一丝痕迹。暗示这个人非常彻底地消耗掉了所有可利用的东西，通常带有讽刺或抱怨的语境。
**Bilingual Explanation:**  
- **English:** This can be used to describe a person who is very thorough in consuming everything available to them, leaving nothing behind.
- **Chinese:** 这个词组用来形容一个人把所有能吃的东西都一扫而光，不留一丝痕迹。暗示这个人非常彻底地消耗掉了所有可利用的东西。
