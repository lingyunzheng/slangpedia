```markdown
## Ate and Left No Crumbs (English)
Expression: "Ate and left no crumbs"
Bilingual Slang Explanation: 
- English: Refers to someone who eats greedily and completely, leaving nothing behind.
- 汉语: 形容一个人吃得很干净，一点也不剩，表示他们非常贪婪或毫无保留。
Usage Example:
- English: John really cleaned the plate; he ate and left no crumbs.
- 汉语: 约翰真的把盘子吃得干干净净，他吃得一干二净，连一丝食物都没剩下。
---
## 吃了个干净 (Chinese)
Expression: "吃了个干净"
Bilingual Slang Explanation: 
- English: Similar to "ate and left no crumbs," it means someone finished eating completely without leaving any food.
- 汉语: 形容一个人吃得非常干净，食物被完全吃掉，没有剩下任何东西。
Usage Example:
- Chinese: 妹妹把那份蛋糕吃得干干净净，吃了个干净。
- English: Sister ate the entire cake clean, ate it all up.
```
