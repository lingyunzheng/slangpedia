## Ate and Left No Crumbs (中文：吃干抹净)
### English Explanation:
"ate and left no crumbs" is an idiom used to describe someone who eats everything and leaves nothing behind. It is often used in a more positive sense to praise someone for being thorough and efficient in utilizing resources. However, in a negative context, it can mean that someone is extremely greedy or wastefully finishes everything they get their hands on.
### Chinese Explanation:
"吃干抹净" (chī gān mā jìng) 是一个比喻性表达，用来形容一个人吃得很干净，一点残渣都不留。这个成语通常用于表扬某人彻底且高效地利用资源。但如果在负面的语境中使用，它也可以意味着某人贪婪或浪费地把所有能得到的东西都吃光了。
