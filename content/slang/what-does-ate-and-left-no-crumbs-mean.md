## English: Ate and Left No Crumbs
### Meaning
When someone cleans themself out completely, leaving nothing behind. It's often used in a context where someone has taken every last bit of something, whether it's food, an opportunity, or anything else.
### Example
- "John ate the cake and left no crumbs. He must have been really hungry!"
---
## 中文：一扫而光，不留一丝痕迹
### 解释
形容某人把东西吃光或用尽，不留任何余下的部分。这个表达通常用来描述某人将某物全部消耗殆尽的情景，可以是食物、机会或其他任何东西。
### 示例
- "约翰把蛋糕一扫而光，连一丝痕迹都没有留下。他一定非常饿！"
