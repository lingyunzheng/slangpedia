### 英文俚语：ate and left no crumbs
**Explanation:** 
This phrase means that someone ate all of their food and did not leave any leftovers. It can also be used figuratively to describe a person who did something completely, leaving no room for doubt or any trace of what they did.
**中文俚语：吃干抹净（chī gān mǒ jìng）**
**解释：**
这个短语的意思是某人把食物全部吃完了，没有留下任何余下的部分。它还可以用来形容某人完全做了一件事情，以至于不留任何疑问或痕迹。
