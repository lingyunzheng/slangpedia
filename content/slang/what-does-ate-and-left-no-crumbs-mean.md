## 中文解释
**吃干抹净** (Chī gān mā jìn)
- **释义**: 这个短语用来形容一个人把东西吃得非常干净，不留一点儿残渣。就像把食物吃得太干净，连桌子上的面包屑都不剩一样。
- **用法**: 他把那瓶饮料喝得一滴不剩，真是吃干抹净。
## 英文解释
**Clean Plate Club**
- **Definition**: This phrase is used to describe someone who eats something so thoroughly that there isn't a single crumb left. It's like finishing every last bit of food as if it was cleaned off the plate.
- **Usage**: She drank the entire bottle of soda, not leaving a single drop, truly a member of the Clean Plate Club.
