## 英文解释：Ate and Left No Crumbs
**Definition:** This phrase is used to describe someone who eats absolutely everything available and leaves nothing behind. It's often used in a context to express surprise or admiration at how much one has consumed.
**Example:** "She ate and left no crumbs at the buffet, barely leaving room for dessert!"
## 中文解释：一扫而光
**释义:** 这个短语用来形容一个人吃东西时非常干净利落，连一点残留都不留下。它通常用来表达对某人摄入量大或吃得很干净的惊讶或赞叹。
**例句:** "她在自助餐上吃得很干净，几乎连一点空间都留不下给甜点！"
