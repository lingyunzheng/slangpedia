# Ate and Left No Crumbs
## English
- **Meaning:** To eat everything available to the last morsel, leaving absolutely nothing behind.
- **Usage Example:** "I went to the buffet and ate and left no crumbs, even cleaning up the spill someone dropped their plate on."
## Chinese
- **含义:** 将所有可获得的食物都吃得一干二净，连最后一丁点碎屑都不剩。
- **使用示例:** "我去自助餐，把所有东西都吃得干干净净，连别人洒在地上的汤汁都帮他们清理了。"
