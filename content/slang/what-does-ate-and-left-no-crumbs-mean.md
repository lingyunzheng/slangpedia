## Ate and Left No Crumbs
### English
**Meaning:** Completely ate or consumed everything available, leaving behind no remnants.
**Example Usage:** "She had an insatiable appetite and ate and left no crumbs at the potluck."
### Chinese
**意思:** 完全吃光了所有的东西，不留任何残渣。
**用法示例:** "她的食欲无法满足，在聚会上把所有食物都吃光了，连一点儿碎屑都不剩。"
