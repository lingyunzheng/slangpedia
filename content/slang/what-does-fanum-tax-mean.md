## Fanum Tax (fan-um tax)
### English Explanation
"Fanum Tax" is a playful, made-up term that combines two words, "fanum" (a Latin word meaning "temple" or "shrine") and "tax." In colloquial language, it's used humorously to refer to an unexpected or unnecessary fee or charge, often imposed on items or services. 
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
**fanum tax (范num 税)**
- **English**: A fun, imaginary term that mixes "fanum" (a Latin word for "temple" or "shrine") and "tax." It's used to describe an unexpected or unnecessary fee or charge on something. Essentially, it's a laughable way to complain about extra costs you didn't see coming.
- **Chinese**: 这是一个有趣的，由“fanum”（拉丁语中的“寺庙”或“神殿”）和“税”混合组成的一个虚构术语。它用来描述在某些事情上出现的意外或不必要的费用。总的来说，这在一种玩笑的方式中抱怨额外产生的费用。
