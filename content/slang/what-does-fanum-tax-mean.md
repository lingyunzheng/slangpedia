## Fanum Tax
**English:**  
A "fanum tax" is a playful, informal term often used to describe a non-existent or imaginary tax. It's typically used to mock or ridicule actual tax legislation by suggesting there's a tax on something absurd or trivial. For instance, if a community is debating a new, seemingly pointless tax, someone might joke, "Oh, they must be considering a fanum tax on invisibility or something similarly silly!"
**中文:**  
"fanum税" 是一种有趣的、非正式的术语，常用来形容一种不存在或想象中的税。它通常用于嘲讽或戏谑现实税收政策，暗示是在对某种荒谬或琐碎的事情征税。例如，如果一个社区正在讨论一种新的、看似毫无意义的税收，有人可能会开玩笑说：“看来他们正在考虑征收看不见的东西的fanum税了！”
