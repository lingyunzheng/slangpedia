Here's the bilingual slang explanation in Markdown format for "fanum tax":
```markdown
# Fanum Tax ( Fanshuit)
## 中文释义
粪税 (fènxì)：一种幽默或讽刺地称呼收费的戏谑方式，尤其用于对某些不合理收费或被认为是滥用权力收费的情况进行调侃。
## English Explanation
Fanum Tax (i.e., "Shit Tax"): A playful and mocking term used to refer to charges or fees, especially in contexts where these charges are seen as unreasonable or where there's a perception of using power to exploit others. It's often employed to satirize or criticize such practices.
```
This explanation aims to capture the essence of "fanum tax" as a slang term used both in Chinese and English contexts to describe a type of fee or charge in a lighthearted or critical manner.
