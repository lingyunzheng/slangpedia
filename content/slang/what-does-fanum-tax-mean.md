## fanum tax: 炫税 (Xiàn shuì)
### English Explanation:
"Fanum Tax" is a slang term in the cryptocurrency and blockchain communities, referring to the practice where owners of expensive, high-end items such as luxury cars, jewelry, or expensive electronics exaggerate the value of these items to meet the high tax threshold or to reduce the amount of taxes they owe. Essentially, it's about inflating the price tags to avoid higher taxes, often seen as a way to legally minimize tax obligations by splitting large, taxable items into smaller, less taxed components.
### Chinese Explanation:
“炫税” (Xiàn shuì) 是加密货币和区块链社区中的一种俚语，指的是拥有昂贵、高档物品（如豪车、珠宝或高端电子产品）的所有者，为了达到高的免税额度，或者减少他们应负担的税款，而夸大这些物品的价值。本质上，这是通过吹嘘价格标签来合法地最小化税收义务的一种方式，通常做法是将大宗、可征税物品划分成较小、征税较少的成分。
---
The term is often used humorously and to imply a certain degree of deception or cleverness in navigating this kind of tax strategy.
