```markdown
## Fanum Tax (Latin)
### English:
A **fake tax** or a **creative taxation** scheme, often used to evade actual taxation by posing fees or charges that don't have legal backing. It's like when someone tries to pass off a lemonade stand's cost of lemons as a "special handling fee" to avoid a bigger tax on the lemonade itself.
### 中文:
“假税”或“创意税收”方案，通常用于逃避实际税收，通过制造一些没有法律依据的费用或收费来掩盖真实的税收。就像是在柠檬水摊上把柠檬的成本打包成“特别处理费”来避免对柠檬水更大的税收。
```
This explanation uses casual and informal language to convey the concept of a "fanum tax" in both English and Chinese.
