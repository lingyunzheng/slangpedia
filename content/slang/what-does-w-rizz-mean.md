# W rizz
## English
"W rizz" is a playful and informal way to express that someone is cool, trendy, or has a certain level of swag. It's often used to describe a person's style, attitude, or overall vibe. For example, if someone is wearing the latest fashion trend or has a cool way of carrying themselves, you might comment, "W rizz!"
## 中文
"W rizz" 是一种轻松而又非正式的表达方式，用来表示某人很酷、时尚或者有一种独特的魅力。它常用于描述一个人的风格、态度或整体气质。比如，如果有人穿着最新的时尚潮流或者有很酷的举止，你可能会说，“W rizz!”
