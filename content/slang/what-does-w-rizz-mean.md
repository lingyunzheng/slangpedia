### W rizz
**中文解释:** 这个词源自网络用语，通常用来形容一个人具有很高的时尚品味或者对潮流有独到的见解。说某人“w rizz”其实是在夸奖他/她穿衣服很有型，打扮得很潮，或者在时尚圈有自己的理解和辨识度。
**English Explanation:** This slang term originates from internet culture and is used to describe someone who possesses a high level of fashion sense or unique insights into trends. When you say someone "w rizz," you're praising their ability to look stylish, dress fashionably, or have a distinct understanding and recognition in the fashion world.
