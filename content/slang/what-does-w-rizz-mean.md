# W rizz
**English Explanation:**  
W rizz is a modern slang term often used to describe someone, especially a man, who exudes a cool, laid-back, and fashionable aura. It is a combination of "who" and "rizzle," where "rizzle" is an extension of the term "rizzle," which means to have a style or attitude. Essentially, someone with "w rizz" is considered to be stylish, cool, and trend-aware.
**Chinese Explanation:**
