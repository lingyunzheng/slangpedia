```markdown
### W rizz
**English:** 
It's a laid-back, cool vibe or presence, often meaning someone is effortlessly stylish, laid-back, or carries a relaxed yet distinctively cool aura. It's about being effortlessly "on point" without trying too hard.
**中文：**
指一种随性和酷的氛围或存在感，通常意味着某人非常自如地展现出一种既轻松又独具魅力的风度。它代表了一种无需刻意表现就能做到“恰到好处”的感觉。
```
This concise explanation captures the essence of the term "W rizz" in both English and Chinese, highlighting its relaxed coolness and effortless style.
