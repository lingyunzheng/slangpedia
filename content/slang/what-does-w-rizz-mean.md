# W rizz
**English:**  
W rizz is a slang term used to describe someone who is cool, stylish, or has a lot of charisma. It can also imply that someone has a unique or distinctive sense of style or mannerisms. The term is often used to express admiration or acknowledgment of someone's presence, attitude, or overall vibe.
