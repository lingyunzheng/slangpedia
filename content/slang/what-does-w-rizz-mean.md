# W rizz
## English
**W rizz** is a modern slang term often used in internet culture, particularly on platforms like Twitter and TikTok. It means that someone is extremely cool, trendy, or stylish. The term is often used to describe someone who is on point or in the know about the latest trends.
Example: "That new sneaker release is w rizz—it’s got everyone talking!"
