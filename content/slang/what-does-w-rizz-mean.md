## W rizz
**English:**  
"W rizz" is a slang term often used to describe someone who has a lot of style, coolness, or hipness. It can also be used to indicate that something is trendy or fashionable. "Rizz" itself is slang for a specific kind of cool, often associated with a bit of edginess or sharpness.
**Chinese:**  
"W rizz” 是一个俚语，用来形容一个人很有型、很酷或者很潮流。有时也可以用来表示某样东西很时髦或者很流行。“Rizz” 是“酷”的一种特定表达，通常带着一点酷劲和精炼感。
