# W rizz
**English Explanation:**  
W rizz is a simplified version of the phrase "what rizz" and is commonly used in internet slang, particularly among younger generations. Rizz can be thought of as a quality or trait that reflects stylishness, coolness, or overall awesomeness. When someone asks "W rizz," they are essentially asking "What kind of style or coolness?" 
**Chinese Explanation:**
