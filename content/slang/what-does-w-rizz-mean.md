### W rizz
**English:** DIY (Do It Yourself) vibes! When someone is showing off their personal touches, unique style, or creative flair, they're "W rizz." It's all about making something special and unique with your own hands or by adding personal touches to whatever you're working on.
**中文:** DIY 感！当有人展示出个人风格、独特品味或者创意的时候，我们说他们“W rizz”。这表示你用自己的双手或是给某个事情加上个性化的元素，使其变得特别和独特。
