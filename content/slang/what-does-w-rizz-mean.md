# W rizz
**English Explanation:**
"W rizz" is a slang phrase that essentially means "what," used to ask for clarification or to indicate confusion about something. It's a playful and modern way to rephrase "what" in casual conversations.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"W rizz" 是一种网络俚语，相当于“what”，用于请求澄清或表示对某事的困惑。这是一种在轻松对话中重新表达“what”的现代且有趣的方式。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "W rizz, did you get the memo about the meeting time change?"
- Chinese: "W rizz, 你收到会议时间变更的通知了吗？"
