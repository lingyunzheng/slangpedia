### W rizz
**English Slang Explanations:**
- **W rizz**: This is a playful way to say "With class" or "With style". It's often used to describe someone or something that exudes a certain cool or fashionable vibe.
**Chinese Slang Explanations:**
- **W rizz**: 用来形容某人或某物很有型、有品位，或者说是一副极酷或时尚的样子。
