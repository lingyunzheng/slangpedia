## W rizz
### English
**Definition:** To perform or act in a way that is cool, stylish, or sophisticated, often in a hip-hop or street culture context.
**Example:** "He w rizzed through the party with his DJ skills."
### 中文
**定义：** 以一种酷、时尚或风骚的方式表现或行动，通常是在嘻哈或街头文化的情境中。
**例句：** "他在聚会上凭借他的DJ技巧 w rizzed。"
