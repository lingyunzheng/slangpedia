# Cope (English)
## Definition
When you're dealing with stress, pressure, or difficult situations in a calm and efficient manner, often involving problem-solving and grit.
## Slang Explanation
**Coping**: It's like you've got a superhero cape on and you're flying through life's obstacles, saving the day and keeping your cool.

# Cope (Chinese)
## 释义
在面对压力、困难局面时，保持冷静并高效解决问题，展现出坚韧不拔的精神。
## 花式表达
**应对**：就像是披着超级英雄的斗篷，穿梭在生活中的各种险滩激流，冷静地解决问题，化险为夷。
