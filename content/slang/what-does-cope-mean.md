# Slang Explanation for "cope"
## English: Cope
**Definition**: To handle or manage a difficult situation or stress.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation**:
- **English**: To deal with or manage a difficult situation or stress.
- **Chinese (Cantonese)**: 谐返架 (ge6 fan6 gaa3) - 不斷地嘗試解決困擾你的問題 (bun6 daan6 dei6 see6 saam1 sik6 gwo6 jih9 ge4 nom2 diz6) -的意思 (ge2 zi5) - 用各種方法應付困難 (yung6 jazz6 fou6 faang6 ying6 faau5 gun6 dam6)。
- This phrase roughly translates to "continuously trying to solve the troubles troubling you" and the meaning "using various methods to deal with difficulties."
- **Explanation**: In casual language, "cope" is often used to describe someone handling tough situations. The Cantonese phrase emphasizes the continuous effort and adaptive strategies involved in managing challenges.
## Examples:
- **English**: She's really good at coping with stress.
- **Bilingual**: 她貨運得妙 (taa6 go3 yun6 disiu3 maau6) - 與壓力作戰 (yu6 yih5 zaai6 zaa6)。
- She handles stress very well (lit. she fights well with stress).
- **English**: We need to learn how to cope with unexpected changes.
- **Bilingual**: 我哋要學懂應付突发事件 (ou5 dei6 yuk6 hong2 ying6 faau5 sutt6 man4 hing4)。
- We need to learn to deal with unexpected events.
