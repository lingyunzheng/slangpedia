```markdown
##cope##
### English ###
To deal with or manage a difficult situation; to handle or withstand a challenge.
### Chinese ###
解决或应对困难情况，或是处理挑战。可以理解为“应付”或“搞定”的意思。
例句：
- English: She really had to cope with a lot of work recently.
- Chinese: 她最近要应对的工作量真的很大。
- English: I need to find a way to cope with my anxiety.
- Chinese: 我需要找到一种方法来应付我的焦虑。
```
