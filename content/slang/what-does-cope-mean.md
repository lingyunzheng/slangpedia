```markdown
## Cope (English)
### Meaning:
To handle or deal with stressful or difficult situations.
### Bilingual Slang Explanation:
**English:** To handle or deal with stressful or difficult situations.
**Chinese (Slang):** “扛”（gāng） - 用来形容处理或应对压力大或困难的情况。
### Example:
He's really learning to **cope** with his new job.
他正在学习如何 **扛** 新工作的压力。
```
