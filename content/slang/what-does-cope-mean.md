## Cope (应对)
### English
**Cope**: To handle or deal with a difficult situation or stress. It's when you're managing problems or surviving tough times. 
Example: "I'm really trying to cope with all the changes at work."
### Mandarin
**cope**: 应对 (yìng duì) - 指的是处理或应对困难的情况或压力，当你在管理问题或度过艰难时期时用到这个词。
例句：我正在努力应对工作中的所有变化。
