## Cope (English)
### Explanation
- **Definition**: To handle or deal with a difficult situation, problem, or uncomfortable feeling.
- **Slang Variation**: "To cop" (UK slang) – To manage or handle a difficult situation.
- **Example**: "I need to cope with this stressful deadline. I'm going to take some deep breaths and stay focused."
## cope (中文)
### 解释
- **定义**：处理或应对困难的情况、问题或不舒服的感觉。
- **口语用法**："To cop" (英国俚语) – 承受或处理困难的情况。
- **例句**："我需要应对这个巨大的工作压力。我打算深呼吸，保持专注。"
