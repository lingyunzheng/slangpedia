## Cope (Chinese: 应对; pinyin: yìng duì)
### English
**Cope** is a verb that means to deal with a difficult or unpleasant situation. It's like when you're facing a big problem and you're trying your best to handle it, no matter how hard it is.
### 中文
**应对** (yìng duì) 通常用来形容人在面对困难或不愉快的情况时，努力想办法处理或解决问题。就像是面对很大的挑战时，尽自己最大的努力应对它。
