## Cope
### English
**Definition**: To deal with or manage a difficult situation.
**Slang Explanation**: When you "cope," you're basically bragging about how well you handle tough stuff. It's like saying, "No problem, I can handle this!"
### 中文
**定义**：处理或管理一个困难的情况。
**俚语解释**：当你"cope"时，你实际上是在吹嘘自己处理艰难局面的能力。就像在说，“没问题，我能搞定！”
