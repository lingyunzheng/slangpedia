## Cope (English)
**Meaning:** To deal with or manage a difficult situation; to adjust to or make the best of a challenging circumstance.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义：** 处理或应对困难的情况；适应或尽自己最大努力面对挑战。
- **Example Usage:** 
- She's really good at coping with stress.
- 她特别擅长应对压力。
## Cope (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 处理或应对困难的情况；适应或尽自己最大努力面对挑战。

- **English Explanation:** To deal with or manage a difficult situation; to adjust to or make the best of a challenging circumstance.
