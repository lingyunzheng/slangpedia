## Cope (English and Chinese slang)
### English
**To cope:** To deal with or manage a difficult situation, often involving stress or hardship.
**Example:** "I'm going to take some time to cope with the recent changes in my job."
### 中文 (Chinese)
** cope:** 用来形容处理或应对困难的情况，常伴随着压力或困难。
**例句:** "我需要一些时间来应对工作上的变化。"
