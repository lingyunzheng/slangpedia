## Cope
### English
**Cope:** To deal with a difficult or challenging situation; to handle or manage something stressful.
*Example:*
"I'm really stressed about the deadline, but I'll just have to cope somehow."
### 中文
**cope:** 应对或处理艰难或有挑战性的状况；管理或处理有压力的事情。
*例句:*
"我关于截止日期真的压力很大，但我得想方设法应对一下。"
