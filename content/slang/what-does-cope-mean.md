##cope
### English
**To cope**: To handle or deal with a difficult or stressful situation.
*Example*: "I'm really stressing out about the big project due next week, but I'll just need to cope with it somehow."
### Chinese
**扛过去 (káng guò qù)**: 出于应对或处理艰难或压力大的情况，这个词常用来形容在困境中努力坚持或解决困难。
*例句*: "下周的大项目让我感到非常紧张，但我得想办法扛过去。"
