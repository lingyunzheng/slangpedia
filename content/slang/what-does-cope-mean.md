# Cope (Bilingual Slang Explanation)
## English
**Cope** - To deal with or manage a difficult situation. Often used when someone is trying to handle stress, problems, or challenging circumstances.
- *Example:* "I've been really stressed with the project deadlines, but I'm learning to cope better by taking breaks and prioritizing tasks."
## Chinese
**Cope** - 用来形容处理或应对困难的情况。通常用于描述人们如何处理压力、问题或挑战性的情况。
- *Example:* "由于项目截止日期的压力，我一直在努力学着更好地应对，比如适时休息和优先处理任务。"
