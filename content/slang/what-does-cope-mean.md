##cope##
### English ###
**Cope**: To handle or manage a difficult or challenging situation. It often implies dealing with stress, problems, or adversity in a practical and effective way.
### Chinese ###
**熬过去 (áo guò qǐ)**: 指应对或处理困境、困难或压力。这个词经常用来描述以一种实际且有效的办法来面对和缓解问题或挑战。
