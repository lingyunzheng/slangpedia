## Cope (英) / 抗击 (中)
### English Explanation
**Cope** is to deal with or manage with difficulty, especially in a way that does not show stress or difficulty. It means handling a situation or problem, often with a sense of resilience and pragmatism.
### Chinese Explanation
**抗击 (Gǎnkòng)** 指的是面对并处理困难或问题时所展现出的不易察觉的压力下的应对能力。它表示以一种韧性和实际的态度去处理情况或问题。
### Bilingual Slang Usage
- **English**: "I had a lot on my plate last week, but I managed to cope pretty well."
- **Chinese**: "上周我有许多事情要处理，但我还是很有抗击精神地应对了。”
### Context Example
- **English**: "When dealing with family drama, it’s important to learn how to cope effectively."
- **Chinese**: "面对家庭中的纷争，有效地抗击非常重要。"
