## English: Cope
### Chinese: 应付 (Yìngfù)
- **Definition:** To deal with or manage a difficult situation.
- **Bilingual Explanation:** When life throws a curveball, you've got to **cope with it**, aka **应付一下 (yìngfù yíxià)**. Whether it's a heavy workload or unexpected challenges, if you're not giving up, you're **扛住 (kángzhù)** or **顶住 (dǐngzhù)**, managing and dealing with the issue at hand.
## Example Sentences:
- **English:** She really has to cope with her dad's illness.
- **Chinese:** 她真的得应付她爸的病。
- **Pronunciation:** tā zhēn de děi yìngfù tā bà de bìng.
