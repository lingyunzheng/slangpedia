## Cope (应对/应付)
### English
**Cope**: To handle or deal with a difficult situation. It can involve managing stress, adjusting to change, or solving problems. Often used when someone is trying to deal with an overwhelming or challenging circumstance.
*Example:*
She's been really coping with the sudden loss of her job.
### Mandarin
**应对**/**应付** (dishiàng/fùyìng): 指处理或应对困难的情况。可以涉及管理压力、适应变化或解决问题。通常用于描述某人在面对巨大或具挑战性的情况时所做的事情。
*例句：*
自从失去了工作，她一直在努力应对这种情况。
