## Cope (英语)
### Bilingual Explanation
- **Meaning**: To deal with or manage a difficult or challenging situation.
- **Slang Translation**: 咋整 (Zāzhěng) - A colloquial term often used to express dealing with or managing a situation, especially one that is tricky or difficult.
### Example Usage
- **English**: She had to cope with a sudden job loss.
- **Chinese**: 她不得不咋整一场突然的工作失业。
## Cope (中文)
### Bilingual Explanation
- **含义**: 面对或处理艰难或挑战性的情况。
- **俚语翻译**: 咋整 (Zāzhěng) - 一个口语化的表达，常用于表示处理或管理一个棘手或困难的情况。
### 用法示例
- **中文**: 她不得不咋整一场突然的工作失业。
- **English**: She had to cope with a sudden job loss.
