## Cope (应对)}
**English:** To cope means to deal with or manage difficult or challenging situations successfully.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **Chinese:** 背水一战 (bèishuǐ-yīzhàn) – This idiom literally means "fighting with the backs to the water", which describes a situation with no way to retreat, thus meaning to face and conquer a difficult situation with all your resources put on the line.
- **Simplified Explanation:** 当事情变得艰难时，你“背水一战”就是全力以赴地应对，没有退路可言。
## Example Usage:**
- **English:** I need to cope with this sudden project deadline.
- **Bilingual Example:** 我需要“背水一战”来应对这个突然的任务截止日期。
