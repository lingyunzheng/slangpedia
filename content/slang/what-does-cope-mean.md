“ cope ”在中文中通常用来指处理或应对困难、压力或挑战的能力。它表示一种应对机制或生存方式。
### 英文解释
- **Definition:** "cope" in English refers to dealing with or adapting to difficulties, stress, or challenges. It describes a method of handling or managing tough situations.
- **Example Sentences:**
- 中文例子：我有点 cope 不了这些工作压力。
- 英文例子：I'm having trouble coping with these work pressures.

- **"I'm trying to cope with this huge workload."**
- 试图应对巨大的工作量。
- **"She's amazed at how well he copes with the criticism."**
- 感到惊讶地是，他在面对批评时应对得很好。
