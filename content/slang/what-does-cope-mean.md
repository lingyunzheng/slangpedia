# Cope (English)
**Cope** - To deal with or adapt to difficult or challenging situations. It can mean managing or handling stress or problems effectively.
## Bilingual Explanation
- **English:** To deal with or adapt to difficult or challenging situations.
- **Simplified Chinese:** 处理或适应困难或具有挑战性的情况 (chǔlǐ huò shēntòng nánkòu huò jù yǒu zhànghǎo xìng de qiángkuàng)
- **Pinyin:** Chǔlǐ huò shēntòng nánkòu huò jù yǒu zhànghǎo xìng de qiángkuàng
- **Example Usage:**
- "I've been coping with high work stress lately." (最近我在处理高压的工作压力。zuì jìn wǒ zài chǔlǐ gāoyā de gōngzuò yālì。)
# 调适 (Coped - Chinese)
**调适 (tiáoshì)** - 与 "cope" 同义，表示适应或处理困难或具有挑战性的情况。
## Bilingual Explanation
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 调适 - 适应或处理困难或具有挑战性的情况 (zhōnghuà - shēntòng huò chǔlǐ nánkòu huò jù yǒu zhànghǎo xìng de qiángkuàng)
- **Pinyin:** Zhōnghuà - shēntòng huò chǔlǐ nánkòu huò jù yǒu zhànghǎo xìng de qiángkuàng
- **Example Usage:**
- "我正在调适新的工作环境。" (wǒ zhèngzài tiáoshì xīn de gōngzuò huánjìng。) - "I am adapting to the new work environment."
