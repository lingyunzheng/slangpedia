## English | 中文
### Cope 🙈
**Definition:** To deal with or handle a difficult situation or problem; to manage to do something.
- *Example:* I'm really not looking forward to coping with the backlog of emails, but I guess I have no choice.
- *中文解释：* 应对或处理困难的情况或问题；设法完成某事。
**Slang Usage:**
- **To Cope:** 通常用来形容面对挑战或困难时的应对能力，带有无奈或尽力而为的意思。
- *Example:* I just had to cope with the crazy day that was yesterday; I'm so exhausted.
- *中文例句：* 我不得不应付昨天那疯狂的一天；我累坏了。
**Creative Use:**
- "He's really good at coping with chaos."
- *中文转述：* 他真的很好应对混乱。
