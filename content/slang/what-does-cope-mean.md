## English: cope
### Chinese: 挣扎 (zhèngzhá) or 硬撑 (yìng chēng)
**Explanation:**
- **cope** (English): To deal with or manage a difficult situation.
- **挣扎 (zhèngzhá)** or **硬撑 (yìng chēng)** (Chinese): These phrases mean "to struggle" or "to force oneself to endure." They are used when someone is dealing with a tough situation, trying to maintain their composure or continue despite difficulties. 
Example sentences:
- **English:** She was coping with the loss of her job.
- **Chinese:** 她正在挣扎着应对失业的打击。 (Tā zhèngzài zhēngzhá zhe yìngduì shūyù de dedà.)
- **English:** He's been hard coping with his parents' divorce.
- **Chinese:** 他一直硬撑着面对父母离婚。 (Tā yīzhí yìng chēng zhe miàn duì fùmǔ líhūn.)
In casual contexts, these expressions are used to describe someone who is having a hard time but is trying to handle it.
