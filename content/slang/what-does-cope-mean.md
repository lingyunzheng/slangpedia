## Cope (English)
- **Meaning**: To deal with or handle a difficult situation.
- **Slang Explanation**: To be a boss and handle whatever life throws at you without breaking a sweat.
## Cope (Chinese)
- **含义**：应对或处理困难的情况。
- **俚语解释**：面对生活中的各种难题毫不费力地应对，像个老板一样强大和从容。
