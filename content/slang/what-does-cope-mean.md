```markdown
##cope##

### English Slang Explanation:
To cope means to deal with or manage difficult or unpleasant situations. It's like saying you're handling a tough situation as best you can.

Example:
"I'm really coping with the move to a new city, but it's tough sometimes."

### Chinese 花哨解释:
“cope” 意味着你是在艰难情况下尽量解决事情。就像是在艰难环境中努力撑下去。

示例：
“尽管搬到新城市很艰难，但我还是设法应对。”

### English Casual Explanation:
When you're coping, you're trying to deal with something that's really hard. It's like when you're trying to balance a really big, heavy object on your head—it's not easy, but you're keeping it up.

Example:
"I'm just coping with having to work late every day."

### Chinese 俚语解释:
当你 cope 的时候，就像是在应对一件非常难的事。就好比你要努力把一个又重又大的物体顶在头上，这没有那么简单，但你尽力支撑着。

示例：
“我每天都加班，真的挺难的，但我尽力应对。”
```
