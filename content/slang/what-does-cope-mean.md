##cope##
**English:** cope  
**中文解释:** 承受；应对
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **English:** To cope with something generally means to handle or deal with a difficult situation. It's often used when someone has to deal with stress, problems, or challenges.
- **中文解释:** "cope" 通常指的是处理或应对困难的情况。这个词经常用来描述某人面对压力、问题或者挑战时的过程。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** She's been really good at coping with the stress of her job.
- **中文解释:** 她在应对工作的压力方面做得非常好。
