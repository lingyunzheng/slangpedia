## Cope (English)
**Definition:**
To manage or deal with difficult or unpleasant situations. It involves handling stress or challenges effectively.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
**中文解释：** deal with 或者 拼命熬（表示面对困难时的坚韧）
- **Example Usage:** 
- "I had to cope with the surprise changes in the project schedule." -> 我不得不应对项目进度表突然的变动。
- "Coping with a break-up is hard, but she managed to cope well." -> 情侣分手很痛苦，但她处理得很好。
