# Cope (EN/-CN)
## EN
**To cope:** To handle or deal with a difficult or unpleasant situation.
Example: I need to cope with a lot of work while preparing for an exam.
## CN
** coping:** 处理或应对困难或不愉快的情况。
例句：考试准备的同时还要处理大量的工作，我需要好好 coping。
