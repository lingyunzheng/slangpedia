## Cope 🌟
### English
**Definition:** To deal with a difficult or stressful situation; to manage or handle something.
**Slang Explanation:** "Cope" is used when you're trying to handle or deal with a tough situation. It's like saying you're doing your best to manage through a hard time.
### 版本二 (Pin Yin: Bǎi ān)
### 中文
**释义：** 处理困难或压力大的情况；应对或解决某事。
**俚语解释：** "Cope" 是指你在尽力处理或应对艰难的情况。这就好比你在说你正在尽最大努力度过难关。
