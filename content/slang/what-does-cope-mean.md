## Cope (English)
### Explanation
- **Definition:** To deal with a difficult or unpleasant situation in a calm and sensible way.
- **Slang Translation:** 在困难或不愉快的情况下保持冷静和理智地处理问题。
- **Example Usage:**
- English: She really knows how to cope with her job stress.
- 中文: 她真的知道如何应对工作压力。
## Cope (Chinese)
### 解释
