# Slang Explanation for "lore"
## English
**lore** - *noun*
1. **In General Usage:**
- Things that are passed down from one generation to another as anecdotes, stories, or traditional beliefs.
- Examples: "Her family lore includes stories of their ancestors who were sea captains."
2. **In Gaming Context:**
- Detailed, often extensive background information and mythology specific to a game or universe.
- Examples: "Battle's lore includes detailed histories of its various factions and their relations."
## Chinese
**lore** - *（lore）*
1. **一般用途：**
- 代代相传的故事、传统信仰或民间传说。
- 例子：“她的家族传说中包含她的祖先曾是水手的故事。”
2. **游戏背景：**
- 游戏或宇宙中的详细背景信息和神话。
- 例子：“Battle的游戏背景包括对其各个阵营及其关系的详细历史。”
