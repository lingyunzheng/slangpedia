# Lore Slang Explanations
## English
**Lore**: In gaming and fiction contexts, lore refers to the detailed backstory and cultural context of a game, universe, or story. It includes mythology, history, geography, and any other data that helps players understand the world they are engaging with. Lore can be found in books, in-game narratives, cutscenes, dialogues, and sometimes even in official guides or interviews with creators.
## Chinese
**lore**: 在游戏和虚构的语境中，lore 指的是一个游戏、宇宙或故事背后的详细背景和文化背景。它包括神话、历史、地理和其他帮助玩家理解他们所参与的世界的数据。lore 可以在书籍、游戏中的叙述、预告片、对话，甚至有时在创作者的官方指南或访谈中找到。
