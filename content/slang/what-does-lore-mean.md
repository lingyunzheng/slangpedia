# Slang Explanation for "Lore"
## English
**Lore**: 
- *Definition*: General knowledge, traditions, or beliefs passed down through generations in a particular culture or community.
- *Example*: "The local lore says that the old bridge was built by fairies."
## 引用中文
**lore**:
- *释义*: 指某些特定文化或社群当中代代相传的知识、传统或信仰。
- *例句*: "当地人传说，这座老桥是仙女建造的。"
