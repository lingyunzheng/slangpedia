# Slang Explanation: Lore
## English: Lore
"Lore" refers to the traditional knowledge, beliefs, and customs passed down through generations within a culture or community. It's often used in fantasy or video game contexts to describe the history, mythology, and background information that enriches the world.
**Example in English:**
"In the lore of this game, the Forgotten City was said to be built by ancient wizards."
## 中文: 轶闻
"Lore" 指的是通过代代相传，在一个特定文化或社群中的传统知识、信仰和习俗。这个词常用于魔幻或电子游戏的语境中，用来形容丰富世界观的历史、神话和背景信息。
**中文例句：**
“在这个游戏的传说中，遗忘的城市是由古代法师建造的。”
