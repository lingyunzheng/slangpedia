## Lore (Slang)
### English
**Definition:** A piece of slang that refers to the detailed and often complex background or history of a game, character, world, or concept. It is the story, mythology, and cultural elements that enrich an experience beyond the surface-level events.
**Example:** "I can't wait to dive into the lore of the new fantasy game. It’s going to be so epic with all the dark magic and old histories!"
### 中文
**定义：** 表达的是一个游戏、角色、世界或概念背后详细的、通常很复杂的历史背景或是故事。这不仅仅是表面的事件，还包括丰富的故事、神话以及文化元素，使体验更加生动。
**例句：** “我迫不及待地想深入了解新奇幻游戏的世界观。里面有很多黑暗魔法和古老历史，听起来真的很棒！”
