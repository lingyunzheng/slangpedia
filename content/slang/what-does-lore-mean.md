# Slang Term: Lore
## English Explanation
**Lore**: This term often refers to stories, knowledge, or traditions passed down through generations. In a more modern context, it can also refer to the collective knowledge or history associated with a specific topic, character, setting, or community.
Example: In gaming, lore refers to the in-game history, mythology, and events that create a rich narrative around the game's world.
## Chinese Explanation
**lore**: 这个词通常指的是代代相传的故事、知识或传统。在更现代的语境中，它也可以指与某个特定主题、角色、设定或社群相关的集体知识或历史。
例句：在游戏领域，lore 指的是游戏世界中的历史、神话和事件，这些都会为游戏的背景故事创造一个丰富的叙事环境。
