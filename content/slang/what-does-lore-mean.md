# Slang Explanation: Lore
## English
**Lore:** This term usually refers to stories, legends, or detailed information about a specific topic or setting. In modern usage, it often describes the collective knowledge and history associated with a game, movie franchise, or fictional universe. For example, in the context of a video game, lore includes all the backstories, events, characters, and deeper meanings that flesh out the game world.
### Example Usage
- "To fully understand the game, you need to dive into its lore."
## 中文
**洛雷 (Lore):** 这个词通常指的是关于某个特定主题或设定的传说、故事或详细信息。在现代用法中，它通常指的是与一个游戏、电影系列或虚构宇宙相关的集体知识和历史。例如，在游戏的情境中，洛雷包括关于游戏世界的背景故事、事件、角色及其深层含义。
### 例句使用
- "要充分理解这款游戏，你需要深入了解它的洛雷。"
