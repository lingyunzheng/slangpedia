## Lore (English)
In gaming and fantasy circles, "lore" refers to the detailed background information about a game world, universe, or story. It includes the mythos, history, characters, and the cultures that exist within a setting. Essentially, it's the collective knowledge and narratives that make a created world feel real and immersive.
### 中文解释 (Lore in Chinese)
“lore”常用于游戏和幻想领域，指的是有关游戏世界的详细背景信息，涵盖宇宙、故事的神话、历史、角色以及存在的文化。简而言之，它是创造世界中所蕴含的集体知识和叙事，使这个世界显得真实且沉浸式。
---
**Example Usage:**
- **In English:** The lore of players' favorite game is just as fascinating as the gameplay itself.
- **中文例句:** 玩家们最喜爱的游戏背后的 lore 与游戏体验本身一样精彩。
