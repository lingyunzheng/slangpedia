# Lore (English)
## Explanation:
"Lore" refers to a collection of traditional stories, beliefs, and knowledge passed down through a culture or group. It can also refer to a specific set of tales or beliefs associated with a particular place, event, or group of people.
## Bilingual Slang Explanation:
- **English:** Traditional stories and knowledge passed down through generations.
- **Slang (English):** "Granny tales" or "elders' wisdom"
# Lore (Chinese)
## 解释：
“Lore”指的是通过世代流传下来的文化或群体的传统故事、信念和知识。它也可以特指与特定地点、事件或一群人相关的特定故事或信念集合。
## 双语俚语解释：
- **英语:** 代代相传的传统故事和智慧。
- **中文俚语:** “奶奶的话” 或 “老一辈的智慧”
