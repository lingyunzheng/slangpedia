## Lore
**English Explanation:**
Lore is a colloquial term that refers to a collection of stories, legends, and historical narratives that are passed down through generations, often within a certain community or culture. It encompasses both written and oral traditions.
**Simplified Slang Explanation:**
Lore is like a big, juicy collection of old tales and myths that people tell and retell over time. It's the wild stories and secrets that grandparents and great-grandparents might whisper about around a campfire.
**Chinese Explanation:**
“lore” 指的是通过代代相传的故事、传说和历史叙述组成的集合，往往存在于特定的社区或文化中。它既包括口头流传的故事，也包括书面的文字记载。
**简化俚语解释：**
“lore” 就是一大堆从祖辈口耳相传的老故事和传说。就像是在篝火旁，爷爷奶奶可能会悄悄告诉你的那些神奇的故事和秘密。
