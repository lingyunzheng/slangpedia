## Lore (英语: Lore)
**English:** A collection of stories, legends, and myths that relate to a particular place, person, or thing. It often includes interesting, sometimes exaggerated, tales passed down through generations. In the context of video games or fantasy settings, lore refers to the backstory and history, which players explore through quests, readouts, and characters' dialogue.
**汉语:** 一系列关于某个地点、人物或事物的故事、传说和神话。它通常包含了代代相传的有趣且有时被夸大的故事。在视频游戏或幻想设定的背景下，背景知识（lore）指的是玩家通过任务、告知信息和角色对话来探索的故事和历史。
---
示例用法:
- ** English:** "I heard there's a lot of lore about the old mansion in this town."
- **汉语:** "我听说有关这座古镇老宅的传说很多。"
- ** English:** "Exploring the game's lore was just as exciting as uncovering hidden treasure!"
- **汉语:** "探索游戏中的背景故事就像发掘宝藏一样令人兴奋！"
