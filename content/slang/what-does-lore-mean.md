# Slang Explanation: Lore
## English:
### Definition:
- **Lore:** Generally refers to the body of traditional beliefs and knowledge about a subject, typically passed down over generations. In gaming and fantasy contexts, it often means detailed backstory or information about the setting.
### Example:
- "I learned a lot of lore about the Dark Ages from reading historical books."
## Chinese:
### 释义:
- ** lore:** 普通意义上指的是关于某一主题的传统知识和信念，通常代代相传。在游戏和幻想的语境中，它通常指的是关于设定的详细背景信息。
### 例句：
- “我从读历史书籍中学到了很多关于中世纪的 lore。”
### 英文例子：
- "I studied the lore of the ancient world, and now I can tell you what kind of people lived there and what they believed in."
### 中文例子：
- “我研究了古代世界的 lore，现在我可以告诉你那里生活的人们以及他们相信的是什么。”
