# Lore Slang Explanations
## English
**Lore:** 
- *Definition:* Body of traditional or historical stories and beliefs associated with a particular culture, society, or habitat.
- *Usage Example:* "The island is rich with lore about haunted spots and lost treasure."
## Chinese
**lore:**
- *释义:* 某一文化、社会或栖息地的传统或历史故事和信念体系。
- *使用示例:* “这个岛屿充满了关于鬼魂出没地点和失落宝藏的传说。”
