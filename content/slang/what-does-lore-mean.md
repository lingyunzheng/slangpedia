## Slang Explanation: Lore
### English
**Lore**: In casual slang, "lore" generally refers to information, traditions, or stories that are widely known within a specific group or culture. It can also mean extensive knowledge or experience about something. This term is often used in digital contexts, especially in gaming, where it might refer to the overall background story and history of a game.
*Example:* "This lore includes details about the game's history and the myths surrounding its characters."
### 中文
**lore**: 在网络用语中，“lore”通常指的是某个特定群体或文化中广泛流传的信息、传统或故事。这个词也常用来指某人关于某事的丰富知识或经验。这个术语在数字娱乐领域尤其常见，特别是在游戏中，它可能指的是游戏的整体背景故事和围绕其角色的传说。
*例句:* “这段lore包括了关于这款游戏历史及围绕其中角色的传说的细节。”
