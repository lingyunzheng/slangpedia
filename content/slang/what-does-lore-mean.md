# English Slang Explanation
**Lore** - (n.)  
In modern slang, **lore** often refers to the collective knowledge, stories, or myths that are commonly held by a specific group or community. It can also describe a set of widely known or deeply believed tales or legends within a particular context, such as gaming lore for a specific video game.
# Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English Slang Explanation
**Lore** - (n.)  
In modern slang, **lore** often refers to the collective knowledge, stories, or myths that are commonly held by a specific group or community. It can also describe a set of widely known or deeply believed tales or legends within a particular context, such as gaming lore for a specific video game.
## Chinese Slang 解释
**lore** - (n.)  
在网络用语中，**lore** 经常指的是某个特定团体或社区中普遍持有的知识、故事或神话。它也可以描述在特定上下文中广为人知或深信不疑的传说或故事，比如对于特定电子游戏的“游戏背景知识”。
