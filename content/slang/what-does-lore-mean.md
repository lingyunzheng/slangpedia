## Lore
### English
Short for "narrative lore" or "mythology," lore refers to the body of traditional beliefs and legends of a society, group, or culture. It encompasses names, stories, and histories that are often passed down through generations by word of mouth or other cultural practices.
### 中文
"lore" 源自“叙述传说”或“神话”，指的是某个社会、群体或文化中流传的信仰和传说。它包括通过口口相传或其他文化方式代代相传的名字、故事和历史。
---
### Example Usage
- **English:** "The video game has its own rich lore that fans love to explore."
- **中文:** "这款游戏有着丰富的故事背景，深受粉丝们的喜爱去探索。"
