## Lore (English)
**Meaning:**  
*The collective history, stories, and knowledge of a group, usually associated with a specific culture, community, or genre like fantasy and gaming.*
**Lore Slang Explanation:**
- **B;base knowledge:** The foundational stories and myths that are widely recognized and accepted within a particular community or among a fandom.
## Lore (Chinese)
**意思:**  
*一个群体（通常与特定文化、社区或奇幻、游戏等领域相关）的集体历史、故事和知识的总称。*
**俚语解释:**
- **基文化:** 群体或粉丝圈中广泛认可和接受的基础故事和神话。
