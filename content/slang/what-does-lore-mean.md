# Slang Explanation: "lore"
## English
**Definition:** Lore typically refers to stories or legends that have been passed down through generations, often within a specific culture or community. In a less traditional sense, it can also refer to the collective knowledge or information about something.
**Example Usage:**
- "This town has its own lore about the haunted house on the hill."
- "Lore about the best spots to fish in the lake is passed down through the generations."
## 简体中文
**释义：** 贿言一般指代口口相传、通常在一个特定文化和社群中的故事或传说。在更广泛的意义上，它也可以指关于某种事物的集体知识或信息。
**例句：**
- “这个城镇有关山上鬼屋的传说闻名已久。”
- “关于湖里哪些地方钓鱼最好的信息代代相传。”
