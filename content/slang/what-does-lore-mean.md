# Lore (English/Slang)
## English Explanation
"Lore" generally refers to a collection of stories, legends, or knowledge passed down through generations. In gaming and fantasy contexts, it often refers to the underlying history and mythology of a fictional world.
## Slang Explanation
In trendy, informal contexts, "lore" can also be used to jokingly refer to outdated or incorrect stories or rumors that are widely believed, especially among a certain group of people. It often implies that the person is correcting misinformation with a dose of sarcasm.

# Lore (中文/俚语)
## 英文解释
"Lore" 通常指的是通过几代人口头传下来的故事、传说或知识的集合。在游戏和幻想的语境中，它通常指虚构世界背后的历史和神话。
## 俚语解释
在流行且非正式的语境中，“lore” 也有一个开玩笑的用法，指的是某一特定群体中广泛但可能是错误的传言或谣言。它往往暗示说话者在以某种讽刺方式纠正错误信息。
