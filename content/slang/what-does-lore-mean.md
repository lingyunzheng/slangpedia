# Slang Explanation: Lore
**English:** Lore
**Chinese:** 背景故事 (bēi jǐng gù shì)
## Explanation
In English gaming or pop culture slang, "lore" refers to the backstory or historical narrative that forms the setting of a game, story, or universe. It includes any information about characters, places, history, and plot details that exist unofficially or officially within a fictional world.
In Chinese, "背景故事" (bēi jǐng gù shì), which literally translates to "background story," captures the essence of the term. This term is often used in video gaming communities, discussions about fantasy literature or films, and in the broader context of storytelling. 
### Usage Examples
- **English:** "The lore around the ancient dragon jewels is fascinating, it talks about their origins and the mythical beings that used them."
- **Chinese:** "关于古代龙宝石的背景故事非常有趣，它讲述了这些宝石的起源以及它们是如何被传说中的生物使用的故事。"
This term helps convey the rich, layered stories that often accompany the worlds and characters in many video games, books, and other forms of media.
