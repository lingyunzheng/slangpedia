# Slang Explanation: Lore
## English
**Lore:** Informal term referring to detailed, often unofficial or extended information or backstory, typically about a fictional universe, game, or specific topic.
Example: 
- "The lore of the fantasy RPG is so rich, it feels like you're diving into a comprehensive history of another world."
## 中文
**lore:** 非正式用语，指关于虚构世界、游戏或特定主题的详细、通常是非官方或扩展的信息和背景故事。
例句：
- “这款游戏的lore非常丰富，让你感觉就像是在探索另一个世界的全面历史。”
