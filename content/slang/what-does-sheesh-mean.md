## Sheesh (English)
**Meaning:** An interjection expressing frustration, annoyance, disbelief, or mild disgust.
- **Example:** "Sheesh, it's already 10 PM? Time flies!"
- **Synonyms:** Gosh, oh man
## 哦（xiē）rouch (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 一个表达厌烦、失望或轻微厌恶的感叹词，类似于 "sheesh"。
- **Example:** "哦（xiē）rouch，都已经晚上十点了？时间过得真快！"
- **近义词:** 哦，天哪，天啊
