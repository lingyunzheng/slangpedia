# Sheesh (英/美 slang)
## English Explanation
**Usage:** This is a slang expression often used as an exclamation to express frustration, disappointment, or mild annoyance. 
**Example:** 
- "Sheesh, I can't believe I forgot my keys again!"
## Chinese Explanation
**用法:** 这是一个俚语表达，常用来作为感叹词，表示沮丧、失望或者轻微的不耐烦。
**例句:**
- “唉，我都忘了带钥匙了！”
