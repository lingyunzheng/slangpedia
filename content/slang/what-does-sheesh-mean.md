## Sheesh (英 / 奎奇)
**English Explanation:**
"Ah man" or "Oh my" used as a mild exclamation to express frustration, disbelief, or agreement. It's often used when something unexpected or annoys you.
**汉语解释:**
"啊呀" 或 "哦我的"，用作轻度的感叹词，表示不满、惊讶或赞同。通常当你遇到意想不到的情况或某个事情让你感到烦恼时使用。
Example Usage:
**English:**
"Did you see how she dressed today? Sheesh, that's ugly!"
**汉语:**
“你看到她今天是怎么打扮的了吗？啊呀，真难看！”
**English:**
"Sheesh, it's already 10 PM and my boss is still in the office. This is going to be a long night."
**汉语:**
“啊呀，都已经十点了，我老板还在公司。今晚估计要熬到很晚了。”
