## Sheesh (英 / 西 עסקית)
**English Explanation:**
- **Definition:** An interjection used to express a sigh of exasperation, disappointment, or mild annoyance.
- **Usage Example:** "Sheesh, why did you do that?"
- **Slang Alternative:** “Man” or “Geez”
**Xiangencentre (粤 / 随便讲):**
- **定义:** 一个感叹词，用来表达沮丧、失望或轻微的烦躁。
- **用法示例:** “啊呀，你干嘛要做那样的事？”
- **俚语替代:** “喂” 或 “靠”
