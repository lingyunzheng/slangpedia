## Sheesh (英) / 好吧 (中)
**Sheesh (英)**: An informal exclamation often used to express exasperation, disappointment, or disbelief. It's similar to saying "Oh gosh" or "Well, I'll be" in English.
**好吧 (中)**: 一个非正式的感叹词，常用来表达无奈、失望或不解。类似于中文中的“唉”或“原来如此”。
**Example:**
- **English:** "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible today!"
- **Chinese:** "好吧，今天这交通真是糟糕透了！"
