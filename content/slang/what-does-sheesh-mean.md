## Sheesh (英式/美式俚语)
**English:**
- **Definition:** An expression of disappointment, frustration, or mild surprise.
- **Usage Example:** "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible today!"
**中文:**
- **定义：** 表达失望、挫败或轻微惊讶的短语。
- **使用示例：** "她Marsh，今天这交通真糟糕！"
