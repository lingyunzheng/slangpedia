## Sheesh (英/美)
### English
**Sheesh**: An expression used to express disbelief, disgust, or annoyance. It's often followed by a phrase or filler like "I know," "right," or just left as a standalone exclamation.
**Example**: "Sheesh, that traffic is impossible to navigate right now, I know."
### Chinese
** sheesh**: 一个用来表达不相信、厌恶或烦躁的情绪短词。它通常后面跟着一个词或填充词，比如 "我知道"、"对吧" 或者干脆作为独立的感叹词使用。
**示例**: "sheesh，现在这个交通简直乱七八糟的，我知道。"
