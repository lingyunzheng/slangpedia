## Sheesh (English)
**Definition**: An exclamation expressing frustration, disbelief, disappointment, etc.
**Example**: "Sheesh, this traffic is crazy again today!" 
**Bilingual Slang Explanation**:
**她ốch (Tê-ốch) (Chinese)**
**释义**: 表达不满、惊讶或失望的一个感叹词。
**示例**: "她ốch，今天的交通又那么疯狂！"
