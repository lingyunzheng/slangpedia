# Sheesh (英/美式俚语)
## English Explanation
**Sheesh** is an interjection used to express exasperation, disappointment, or mild disgust. It's similar to saying "Oh man" or "What the heck."
**Example Usage:**
- "Sheesh, this traffic is moving so slowly!"
- "Sheesh, why did I have to eat all the popcorn?"
## 美式俚语解释
**Sheesh** 是一个表达挫败感、失望或轻微厌恶的感叹词。它类似于说“Oh man”或“What the heck”。
**用例示例：**
- “Sheesh, 这交通真慢！”
- “Sheesh, 为什么我把所有的爆米花都吃光了？”
