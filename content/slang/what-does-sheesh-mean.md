## "Sheesh" in English and Chinese
### English
**Sheesh**: An interjection often used to express frustration, disappointment, or disbelief. It's a casual way to say "Oh gosh" or "Pish posh."
### Chinese
**嘿哟 (Hēi yōu)**: 这个词通常用来表达失望、不满或轻蔑。它是非正式地说“哦天哪”或“别信他”的一种方式。
> **Usage Example**:
> - English: "Sheesh, I can't believe she canceled the plans at the last minute!"
> - Chinese: "嘿哟，竟然最后一刻取消计划了，这可真是让人意外！"
