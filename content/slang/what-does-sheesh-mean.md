## Sheesh (English)
**Meaning:** An exclamation often used to express frustration, disappointment, or exasperation.
**Example Usage:**
- "Sheesh, why are you late again?喳喳 (Zhāzhā) — 对不起，我迟到了又是因为啥呢？"
## 喳喳 (Mandarin)
**Meaning:** 用于表达沮丧、失望或厌烦的一个感叹词，类似于“哎呀”。
**Example Usage:**
- 喳喳，你又迟到了，到底是怎么回事？Sheesh, you're late again, what's the deal?
