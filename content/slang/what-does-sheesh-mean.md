## Sheesh
### English
**Meaning**: An exclamation of surprise, frustration, or disapproval. It can also be used to express that something is annoying or disappointing.
**Example**: "Sheesh, this traffic is never-ending!"
### Chinese
**意思**: 表达惊讶、烦躁或不赞成的情绪。也可以用来形容某事很烦人或令人失望。
**例句**: "喂，这交通简直是无止境！"
