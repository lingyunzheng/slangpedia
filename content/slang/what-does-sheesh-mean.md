## Sheesh (English to Chinese)
**English:** Sheesh  
**Chinese:** 哦我的天 (Oh my天)  
**Explanation:** A casual expression used to express frustration, disbelief, or annoyance. It's similar to saying "Oh my god" or "Really?" in a milder tone. 
**Usage Example:**
- **English:** Sheesh, it’s already 10 PM and I still have so much work to do!
- **Chinese:** 哦我的天，都快10点了，工作还有很多要做呀！
