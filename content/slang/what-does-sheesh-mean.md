## Sheesh (English)
### Meaning
- An interjection often used to express frustration, disappointment, or mild disbelief.
- Comparable to saying "Wow," "Oh man," or "That's pretty annoying."
### Example
- Sheesh, the traffic is terrible today!
## 喔! (Sheesh, 中文)
### 意思
- 一个感叹词，常用来表达沮丧、失望或轻微的不信感。
- 类似于说“哇”, “老天”, 或 “真是烦人”。
### 示例
- 喔! 今天的交通真是糟糕透了!
