## Sheesh (English)
**Meaning:** This colloquial term is used as an expression of disbelief, annoyance, or dismay.
**Example Usage:**  
- "Sheesh, it's already 10 PM?!"  
- "You took forever to make that! Sheesh!"
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- English: "Sheesh" 是一种表达惊讶、沮丧或烦扰的口语用法。
- 中文: "Sheesh" 是一种表达惊讶、沮丧或烦扰的口头用语。
## 嘻希 (中文)
**Meaning:** 这个口语表达用于表示惊讶、不满或失望。
**例句:**
- "嘻希，现在都10点了？！"
- "你做这个用了那么久！嘻希！"
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- 中文: "嘻希" 是一种口语表达，用于表示惊讶、不满或失望。
- English: "Sheesh" is a colloquial expression used to indicate surprise, dissatisfaction, or disappointment.
