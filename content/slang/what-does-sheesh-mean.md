# Sheesh (英/美): 
## English Explanation:
- **Meaning**: An exclamation used to express disbelief, disappointment, frustration, or disagreement. It's similar to saying "oh my God" or "really?".
## Chinese Explanation:
- **意思**: 这是一个感叹词，用于表达惊讶、失望、不满或不同意。类似于说“老天”或“真的吗？”。
