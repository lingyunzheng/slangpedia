## Sheesh (English/Chinese)
### English
**Sheesh:** An expression used to express disbelief, frustration, or exasperation, often followed by a shake of the head. It's a casual, informal way to say “Oh dear,” “Really?” or “Ugh.”
*Example:* "Sheesh, you're late again! The meeting started an hour ago."
### Chinese
**她 Sche (Tā She):** 一个用来表示惊讶、失望或厌烦的表达方式，通常会伴有摇头的动作。这是一种非正式的说法，类似于“哦，天哪”，“真的吗？” 或者 “不爽。”
*例句:* "她 Sche，你又迟到了！会议一个小时前就开始了。"
