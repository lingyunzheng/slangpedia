```markdown
## Sheesh (英式英语)
### English Explanation
"Sheesh" is an interjection used to express frustration, disappointment, or mild surprise. It's often used when something frustrating or disappointing happens. For example:
- "Sheesh, it's raining again. I was hoping for a nice day."
### 汉语解释
"她 NIGHTMARE"（非标准词汇，只是为了形象表达）是一个感叹词，用来表达烦躁、失望或者轻微的惊讶。通常在感到不满或惊讶的事情发生时使用。例如：
- “她 NIGHTMARE，今天竟然又是雨天。我还指望有个好天气呢。”
```
### Notes:
- "她 NIGHTMARE" 是为了形象化地表达 "sheesh" 的意思，并不是正式的中文译法。
- 在实际使用中，“她 NIGHTMARE” 可以替换为“真是够了”，“哎呀”等表达方式。
This provides a bridge between the English slang and a similar-sounding Chinese expression for better understanding and contextual use.
