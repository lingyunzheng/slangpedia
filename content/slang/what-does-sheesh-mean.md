# Sheesh (英/美俚)
**English:** 
- Exclaimed when expressing disagreement or disappointment.
- Often used to express frustration or exasperation.
**Example:**
- "Sheesh, did you really just do that?"
- "Sheesh, this traffic is ridiculous!"
**美式俚语:**
- 表达不同意或失望时使用的感叹词。
- 经常用来表达沮丧或无可奈何。
**例句:**
- “她_inputs脱_，你真的就这么做了？”
- “她_inputs脱_，这交通太糟糕了！”
