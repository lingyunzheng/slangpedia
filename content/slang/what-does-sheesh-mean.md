## Sheesh (英/中)
### English
- **Meaning**: An interjection often used to express disappointment, exasperation, or disbelief.
- **Usage**: "Sheesh, I can't believe I forgot my phone at home again!" 
### 中文
- **含义**：一个感叹词，通常用来表达失望、烦躁或不相信某事。
- **用法**："她esh，我都不能相信我竟然又把手机忘在家里了！"
