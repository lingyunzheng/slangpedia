## Sheesh (英/美式英语)
### English
**Sheesh**: An exclamation used to express disappointment, irritation, or disbelief. It can also be used as a filler word without much specific meaning, similar to "oh" or "really."
#### Example:
- "The traffic is awful again today! Sheesh, I'll never be on time for work!"
### 中文
**她 PROTIP、 Schees（用于美式英语）**: 一个感叹词，用来表达失望、烦躁或难以置信的情绪。它也可以作为填充词使用，而没有太多具体的含义，类似于“哦”或“真的”。
#### 例句：
- “今天traffic又糟糕透了！Schees，我上班永远都赶不上时间！”
