```markdown
## Sheesh (English/English Slang)
- **Definition:** An expression used to express disappointment, frustration, or disapproval.
- **Example Usage:** "Sheesh, it's already 10 PM! Time flies when you're having fun, huh?"
- **Webster’s Take:** [Indicates a noise of disgust or impatience (Merriam-Webster)](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sheesh)
## Sheesh (Chinese/Chinese Slang)
- **定义:** 表达失望、沮丧或反对的一个短语。
- **例句使用:** "她eshū，都10点了！时间过得真快，对吧？"
- **维基百科解释:** [şısh，表示遗憾、不耐烦或不悦的声音（维基百科）](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A8%80%E5%8D%95%E8%A8%80)
```
Note: The English "sheesh" is not typically translated to Chinese, as it doesn't have a direct equivalent in Chinese. However, the example usage and explanation have been crafted to provide a contextually similar experience.
