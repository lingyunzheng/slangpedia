# Sheesh (英/美式)
**English:** 
- *Informal Adjective*: Used to express surprise, frustration, or disappointment.
Example: "Sheesh, this traffic is crazy!"
**Chinese:**
- *非正式形容词*: 表达惊讶、失望或沮丧的情绪。
例句: “她噎ِ，这交通真是太疯狂了！”
