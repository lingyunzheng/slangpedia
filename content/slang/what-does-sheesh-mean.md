## Sheesh (英/美)
### English
- **Definition**: An interjection used to express exasperation, mild disgust, disappointment, or disagreement.
- **Example**: "Sheesh, this traffic is driving me crazy!"
### 翻译 (Translation)
- **定义**: 表达厌烦、轻微的厌恶、失望或不同意的感叹词。
- **例句**: “她什，这 traffic 真的是让人抓狂！”
