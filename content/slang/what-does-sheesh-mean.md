# Sheesh (英/美俚语)
**English:** *Sheesh*  
**Chinese (Simplified):** 哦我的天 / 哇塞  
**Chinese (Traditional):** 哦我的天 / 哇塞  
**Explanation:**
- **English:** Sheesh is a casual interjection expressing various emotions, like disbelief, exasperation, mild disgust, or annoyance. It's often used to show that something is ridiculous or disappointing.
- **Chinese (Simplified):** 哦我的天 is a common exclamatory phrase to express surprise or disbelief. Similarly, 哇塞 (wā sāi) is used to convey excitement or a reaction to something impressive or strange. Both are similar in that they're used to show an emotional reaction to a situation.
