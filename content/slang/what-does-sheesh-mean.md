## Sheesh (英 / 简 / 全)
### English
**Sheesh**
- **Meaning:** An expression indicating mild disbelief, exasperation, or disgust. It's akin to saying "Oh my, really?" or "That's ridiculous."
- **Usage Example:** "Sheesh, what a messy room! Did you even try to clean it?"
### Simplified Chinese
**她 seize / tā shī / tā shī**
- **意思:** 一个表示轻微的不相信、烦躁或厌恶的表达。类似于说“哦我的，真的吗?” 或 "那真是荒谬。"
- **使用例句:** “她 seize / tā shī，多乱的房间！你真的一点都没整理吗？”
### Traditional Chinese
**她 çev / tā shī / tā shī**
