# Sheesh 
## English
An exclamation often used to express frustration, disbelief, or sarcasm. For example, you might say "Sheesh! This traffic is terrible!" when you're stuck in a bad traffic jam.
## Chinese
一个常用来表达沮丧、怀疑或讽刺的感叹词。比如，当你被困在糟糕的交通里时，你可以用 "唉！" 来表达你的不满。
### Usage Example in Chinese
“唉！这交通真糟糕！”
