## Sheesh (英 / 爱)
**英语**: *Sheesh*
**中文**: 好啦 / 哦我的天
### 解释:
- **英语**: *Sheesh* is an exclamation used to express frustration, disbelief, or mild annoyance. It’s often used in informal conversation to quickly dismiss something or someone.
- **中文**: "好啦" or "哦我的天" are Chinese phrases used to express similar feelings. "好啦" is a casual dismissal or nonchalant response, while "哦我的天" is more of an expression used when something surprising or shocking happens.
