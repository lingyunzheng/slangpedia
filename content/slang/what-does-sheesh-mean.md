## Sheesh (英 / 简体中文)
**她ssi（她茜 / Tā shī）**
1. **Definition**: A dismissive expression.
2. **Usage**: Used to express disappointment, disbelief, or exasperation.
3. **Example in English**: "Sheesh, this traffic is moving slower than snails!"
4. **Example in Chinese**: "她ssi，这交通怎么这么慢！"
