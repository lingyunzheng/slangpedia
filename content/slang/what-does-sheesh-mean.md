# Sheesh
## English
"Wow" or "Oh my goodness" - An expression used to express surprise, disbelief, or exasperation. Often accompanied by a groan or a sigh.
Example:
- "Sheesh, did you see the traffic today?"
- "Sheesh, what a mess!"
## Chinese
"哎呀" (Aiyaye) - 表示惊讶、失望或烦躁的情绪。常常伴随着一声叹气或呻吟。
例句：
- “哎呀，今天堵车堵得这么厉害？”
- “哎呀，这太乱了！”
