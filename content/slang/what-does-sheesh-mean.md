```markdown
## Sheesh (英/中)
### English
**Sheesh**: A casual interjection used to express disappointment, frustration, disbelief, or mild disgust. It's often equivalent to saying "Oh come on," "Really?" or "Eh, whatever."
### Chinese
**她什（shéesh）**: 一个口语化的插话词，用来表达失望、沮丧、不可置信或轻微的厌恶。它相当于说“哦来嘛”，“真的吗？”或“行吧，随它去吧”。
Example Usage:
- English: Sheesh, I can't believe it took them this long to decide!
- 中文: 她什，我简直不能相信他们决定了都用这么长时间！
- English: Sheesh, does he really think I'll believe that?
- 中文: 她什，他真的以为我会相信那句话吗？
```
