## Sheesh
### English
**Definition**: An expression used to show disbelief, frustration, or disappointment.
**Example**:  
- "Sheesh! That traffic is unbelievable today."
- "Sheesh, I can't believe she did that!"
### Chinese
**释义**: 一个用来表达不可置信、失望或烦恼的情绪词汇。
**例句**：
- “她靠！今天这堵车真是没边了。”
- “她靠，我真不敢相信她做了这种事情。”
