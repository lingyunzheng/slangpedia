## Sheesh (英/美)
### English Explanation:
**Sheesh:** An informal interjection commonly used to express disappointment, disgust, or mild frustration. It's often used in a similar context to "oh man" or "seriously."
### Chinese Explanation:
**sheesh (示例):** 一个非正式的感叹词，常用来表达失望、厌恶或轻微的挫败感。和 "哦天啊" 或 "真andle " 意思相近。
Example Usage:
- **English:** Sheesh, what a long day!
- **Chinese:** 唉，真是漫长的一天！
- **English:** Sheesh, why did you do that?
- **Chinese:** 唉，你干嘛要做那种事？
