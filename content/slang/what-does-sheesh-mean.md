## Sheesh (英 / 翻译)
### English
**Sheesh:**
- **Definition:** A casual interjection expressing disbelief, disappointment, or exasperation.
- **Usage:** "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible today!"
- **Example Sentence:** "Sheesh, I can't believe we missed the game again."
### 翻译
**sheesh:**
- **定义:** 一个口语化插入语，用于表达惊异、失望或厌烦。
- **用法:** "Sheesh, 这交通今天糟糕透了！"
- **例句:** "Sheesh, 我们又错过比赛了，这怎么说都不奇怪。"
