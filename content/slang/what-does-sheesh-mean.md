## Sheesh (英式英语)
### English Explanation
used as an exclamation to express disgust, annoyance, or disbelief. For example, "Sheesh, did you really just say that?"
### 别斯 (中文解释)
用于表达厌恶、烦躁或不可置信的情绪，相当于“天啊”或“喂”。例如，“别斯，你真的说了那样的话？”
