# Sheesh (Sheesha, Shesh)
## English
- **Meaning**: An exclamation of mild surprise, frustration, or dismay.
- **Usage**: Often used when something strikes one as particularly odd, annoying, or disappointing.
- **Example**: "Sheesh, why did it have to rain on such a perfect day?"
## 中文
- **含义**：表示轻微的惊讶、挫败或难以置信的一个感叹词。
- **用法**：常在某事显得特别奇怪、烦人或令人失望时使用。
- **例句**：" Sheesh, 为什么偏偏在完美的日子里下雨？"
