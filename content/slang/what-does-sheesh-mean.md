## Sheesh (她shit / 他shit)
**English:**  
Sheesh is an exclamation often used to express disappointment, frustration, or disgust. It can be seen as a milder version of swear words, used when something is not to your liking or when you're facing a tricky situation.
**Chinese:**  
她shit（对于女性）或他shit（对于男性）是一个常用的感叹词，用来表达失望、烦躁或厌恶的情绪。在英语中，她shit可以看作是一种比较温和的脏话，用于表示某事不符合你的期望，或者你在面对棘手的情况时。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible!"
- **Chinese:** “她shit，这 traffic 真 恶劣！”（这里的 “traffic” 需要用中文具体的形容交通堵塞的情形来替换，例如“她shit，这交通简直就是一场噩梦！”）
