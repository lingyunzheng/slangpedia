## Sheesh (英式英语)
**Definition**: An expression used to express exasperation, disbelief, or mild disappointment.
**Usage**: When something happens that surprises or frustrates you, you might say "Sheesh!".
### Example in Context
- **English**: "Sheesh, it's raining again! I was hoping for a sunny day."
- **Chinese**: 好槽，又下雨了！我还指望有个晴天呢。
## 好槽 (中式英语)
**Definition**: A slang term used to express frustration, disbelief, or mild disappointment, similar to "Sheesh".
**Usage**: When something unexpected or unpleasant occurs, "好槽" can be used as an exclamation.
### Example in Context
- **Chinese**: "好槽，他又迟到了！我都等了他半个小时了。"
- **English**: "Good grief, he's late again! I've been waiting for him for half an hour."
