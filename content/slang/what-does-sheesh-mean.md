# Sheesh (English / 中文)
**English**: *Sheesh* is an interjection used to express frustration, disbelief, or exasperation. It's similar to saying "Oh gosh" or "Ugh."
**中文**: *Sheesh* 是一个感叹词，用来表达失望、不可置信或烦躁的情绪。类似于说“哦天哪”或“唉”。
Examples:
- English: "Sheesh, I can't believe she did that again!" 
- 中文: “唉，我真不敢相信她又干了！”
