## Sheesh (英/美俚语)
### English
**Sheesh**: It's a casual interjection that can be used to express disappointment, frustration, or disbelief. It is often equivalent to saying "Oh man," "Good grief," or "Wow."
**Example**: 
- "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible!" 
- "Sheesh, I can't believe you did that!"
### 美式俚语
**Sheesh**: 这是一个随便使用的感叹词，可以用来说表达失望、沮丧或难以置信的情绪。相当于说“哦天啊”、“真够呛”或“天啊”。
**例句**:
- “Sheesh, 这交通太糟糕了！”
- “Sheesh, 我真不敢相信你竟然这样做！”
