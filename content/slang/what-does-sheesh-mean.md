## Sheesh (sheesh)
### English Explanation:
"Sheesh" is an interjection used to express frustration, disbelief, or exasperation. It is often used when someone is tired of a situation or reacting to something considered annoying or troublesome.
### Chinese Explanation:
"她什"（她什）是一个表示沮丧、不可置信或烦躁的感叹词。当某人对某个情况感到厌烦或对被认为很烦人或麻烦的事情作出反应时，常常用到这个词。
