# Sheesh 
## English
An exclamation used to express disappointment, disapproval, or frustration. It's often used when something unexpected or annoying has occurred.
**Example:**
"Sheesh, you're late again! We waited forever."
## Chinese
一个表示失望、不批准或挫败的感叹词。当发生了一些令人意外或烦人的事情时，经常使用。
**例子:**
"她呀，你又迟到了！我们都等了好久。"
