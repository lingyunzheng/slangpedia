## Sheesh
### English
A casual, exasperated exclamation often used to express annoyance, disappointment, or dismay. 
*Example:* "Sheesh, this traffic is so bad today!"
### Chinese
一个常用的排遣不满的感叹词，表达厌烦、失望或沮丧的情绪。
*例句:* "哎呀，今天这 traffic 真是堵得要命！"
