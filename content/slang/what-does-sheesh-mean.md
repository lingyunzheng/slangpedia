## Sheesh (English)
**Meaning:** An expression used to convey disappointment, exasperation, or mild frustration. It's often used as a breathy sound when feeling annoyed or letting out an opinion.
**Example:** "Sheesh, this traffic is never going to move!"
## 喂 (Chinese)
**Meaning:** A colloquial interjection used to express frustration, exasperation, or disappointment, similar to "sheesh". It can also be used as a breather or sigh when something is bothering you.
**Example:** "喂，这交通永远都走不开了！"
**Translation:** "喂, 这交通永远都走不开了!" (Sheesh, this traffic is never going to move!)
