### Sheesh
**English:** 
A colloquial exclamation expressing exasperation, annoyance, or disbelief. 
**Chinese (Pinyin):** Shēishē
**中文解释：**
一个口头感叹词，用来表达烦躁、不满或难以置信的情绪。
