## Sheesh (English)
### English Slang
**Sheesh** is an interjection used to express frustration, disbelief, or mild annoyance.
### Chinese Slang
**哎呀** (Āi ya) 或 **尼玛 (ní mǎ, colloquial for "my God")**
- **哎呀** (Āi ya) is used similarly to sheesh, conveying disappointment or a slight shock. It's a mild exclamation often used when something unexpected or slightly negative happens.
- **尼玛 (ní mǎ)** is a more colloquial and informal way to express frustration, often used when someone is fed up with a situation or when something went wrong.
### Example
- **English**: Sheesh, this traffic is so bad!
- **Chinese**: 哎呀，这交通真糟糕！ (Āi ya, zhè jiāotōng zhēn zāo nào!)
- **Chinese (Colloquial)**: 尼玛的交通真是太糟糕了！ (Ní mǎ de jiāotōng zěn me tài zāo nào le!)
This interjection is used to add emphasis and convey the speaker's emotional state without making a strong or heated statement.
