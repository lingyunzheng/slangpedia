## Sheesh
### English
**Meaning**: An expression of frustration or exasperation. It's similar to saying "Oh man!" or "Come on!"
**Example**:  
- *English*: "Sheesh, this traffic is terrible today!"
- *Explanation*: The speaker is expressing frustration over the heavy traffic.
### Chinese
**俚语解释**：表示烦躁或无奈的情绪，类似于说“哎呀！”或“真是的！”
**例句**：  
- *中文*: “哎呀，今天这交通真是糟糕透了！”  
- *解释*: 说话者因为交通状况不佳而感到烦躁。
