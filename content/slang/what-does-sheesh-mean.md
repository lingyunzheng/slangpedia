# Sheesh (英): 
**Meaning:** Used to express disappointment, frustration, or exasperation.
- **Example:** "Sheesh, why did you do that again?"
# 哦我的天 (中): 
**Meaning:** 用于表达失望、沮丧或烦躁的情绪，类似于“哎呀”、“我的天”。
- **Example:** "哦我的天，你又那样做了！"
