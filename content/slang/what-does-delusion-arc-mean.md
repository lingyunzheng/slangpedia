# Delusion Arc
## English
**Delusion Arc**: This term is a playful twist on "Delusion," often used in informal settings like social media, online games, or gaming communities. It refers to when a player's belief in the efficacy of a specific item or strategy in a game is so strong that they continue to use it despite strong evidence against its effectiveness. Think of it as when a player is so deeply convinced that their chosen method is the best, they persist even when it clearly isn't working. It indicates a state of optimism or refusal to accept the truth, which might be humorous or frustrating depending on the context.
## 中文
**Delusion Arc**：这个词是"Delusion"的一个风趣的变体，在社交媒体、在线游戏或游戏社区中常被使用。它指的是在游戏过程中，当一个玩家对某个特定道具或策略的效能怀抱极强的信念时，尽管有足够的证据表明其无效，他们仍然继续使用。这可以用在描述玩家因为坚信自己的选择是最优方案，即使这种方法明显不奏效，依然坚持使用的情境。这种状态类似于一种乐观或不接受现实的态度，可能在不同的上下文中既有趣又好笑，也可能令人沮丧。
