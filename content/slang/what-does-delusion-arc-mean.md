## Delusion Arc (幻觉弧线 / Huǎnyuè Fúxián)
### English Explanation:
**Delusion Arc**: Often used in online gaming communities, especially in MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) games. It refers to the curved path that a player imagines their character is taking, which is different from the actual trajectory. This term is used humorously when a player believes their character is dodging or moving in a more complex pattern than what is actually observed. It can also be used to describe a situation where a player thinks they have found a hidden strategy or pattern in the game that doesn't exist in reality.
### Chinese Explanation:
**幻觉弧线 (Huǎnyuè Fúxián)**: 这个词经常在在线游戏的社区中被用来形容MOBA（多人在线战术竞技）游戏中的一种幽默用法。指的是玩家所想象的角色所走的曲线路径，这与实际的轨迹不同。这个词用于描述一种情况：玩家认为他们的角色在躲避或移动时采取了一个比实际观察到的更复杂的路径。也可以用来形容一种玩家找到了游戏中并不存在的隐藏策略或模式的心态，但实际上是幻觉。
---
通过这两种语言的解释，可以看出"Delusion Arc"是一个在电竞尤其是在 MOBA 游戏社区里流行的幽默用语，用来描述一种明显的认知偏差或幻觉。
