## Delusion Arc (delu cháng 畅)
### English Explanation
**Delusion Arc**: This term is a playful blend of "delusion" and "arc," often used to describe a situation where a character or someone is caught in a long and winding story of illusions or delusional thinking. It might be used in a narrative to highlight how someone is stuck in a false reality or in a complex, seemingly endless cycle of misunderstandings or self-deception.
**Example**: "Ever since he got that mysterious phone call, John's been living in a delusion arc, never able to tell what's real and what's not."
### Chinese Explanation
**delu cháng（畅）**: 这个词是“妄想”与“弧线”的结合体，常用来描述一个人陷入想入非非或妄想的世界的情况。它可能用于描绘一个人一直处于虚假现实的困局，或是复杂而看似永无止境的误解或自我欺骗循环中。这个表达也常常用来形容故事中的情节发展，让人觉得故事像一个复杂的图形，没有明确的方向。
**例句**: “自从接到那个神秘电话后，约翰就一直生活在妄想弧线中，无法分辨哪些是真的，哪些是假的。”
