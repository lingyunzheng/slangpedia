## Delusion Arc (Delusion 弧线)
**中文解释：**  
“Delusion Arc”这个词组虽然是一个虚构的英语表达，但可以被理解为一个比喻性说法，指的是一个人在精神错乱或妄想状态下的主观经历，类似于精神上的“弧线”或轨迹。有时这可能指的是一个人沉浸在自己的幻想中，不愿意面对现实中的问题。在某些背景下，也可能用来描述一个人误入歧途或沉浸在错误的认知中的状态。
**英文解释：**  
“Delusion Arc” is a figurative term, though not an actual English expression, that can be interpreted as a metaphor for a person's subjective experiences when in a state of delusion or psychosis. This can be seen as a metaphorical "arc" or path in one's mental state, where the individual is immersed in their own fantasies and unwilling to confront real-world problems. In certain contexts, it might also describe someone who has gone off the wrong path or is stuck in a mistaken belief system.
