# Delusion Arc (Delusion弧度)
## 中文解释
" Delusion Arc "是网络用语的一种形式，用来形容一个人的理想、幻想与现实之间的巨大差距。通常用于描述那些过于理想化和脱离实际的想法和计划。这个表达强调了一种“弧度”，寓意理想从一开始到最终实现之间的曲线变化，是一种揭示理想化过程崩溃的幽默或讽刺表达。
## 英文 Explanation
"Delusion Arc" is a slang term in internet language that describes the significant gap between an individual's fantasies and reality. It is often used to describe overly idealized and unrealistic thoughts or plans. This expression highlights an "arc" to signify the curve of change from the initial ideal to its eventual failure, often used in a humorous or sarcastic manner to point out the breakdown of idealization.
