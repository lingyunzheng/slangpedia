**English:** "Delusion Arc" is a term that might not be widely recognized in everyday slang, but it can be understood as a phrase used to describe a situation or scenario where someone is so delusional or lacking in reality that they are fully believing in an imaginary or fabricated set of circumstances. It can be used humorously to describe a person's overly optimistic or unrealistic view of a situation.
**汉语:** "Delusion Arc" 这个词可能在日常俚语中并不常见，但它可以用来描述一种情况或场景，即某个人如此梦幻或脱离现实，以至于完全相信了一些虚构或编造的情况。它可以幽默地用来形容一个人对情况的过分乐观或不切实际的看法。
