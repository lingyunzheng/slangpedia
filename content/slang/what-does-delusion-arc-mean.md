# Delusion Arc
## English
**Delusion Arc:** In casual slang, particularly within certain online communities and social media platforms, **Delusion Arc** can refer to a fabricated or exaggerated storyline, often centered around a person's delusions or fantastical scenarios. It’s used to describe situations or stories that are far from reality but are widely believed or spread by a group of people, often due to misinformation or a desire for attention.
## 中文
**Delusion Arc:** 在某些在线论坛和社交媒体平台上，**Delusion Arc** 通常指一种虚构或夸大化的剧情，往往围绕着一个人的妄想或幻想场景展开。这个词用来描述那些远离现实但被一群人广泛相信或传播的故事，通常是因为误导信息或者是博取关注的需要。
