# Delusion Arc (幻象弧线)
## English Explanation
**"Delusion Arc"** is a slang term often used in online gaming communities, particularly in role-playing games (RPGs) or battle royale games, to describe a player's or team's path or decisions that seem to be part of an elaborate and misguided plan. It often implies a level of grandiose thinking or hyperbolic strategy that may not lead to a practical outcome.
## Chinese Explanation
**“幻象弧线”** 是一个常用的网络流行语，特别是在角色扮演类游戏（RPGs）或多人生存游戏（如吃鸡游戏）中，用来形容玩家或团队所走的路线或所采取的决策，给人一种精心策划但实则基于错觉或者误导性的印象。它通常表示一种宏大的思考方式或夸张的策略，实际可能并不能导向实际的效果。
