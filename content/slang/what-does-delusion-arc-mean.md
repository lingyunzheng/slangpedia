## Delusion Arc
**中文解释：**
“幻觉弧”通常用来形容一个人陷入了极度的自我幻想或理想化的世界观中，这种状态让人无法客观地看待外界的真实情况。就像一个人走在一条不断放大偏见的弧线上，越走越深，最终迷失在了自己的幻想世界里。
**English Explanation:**
"Delusion Arc" is used to describe someone who is deeply immersed in a self-indulgent or idealized worldview, leading them to fail to objectively perceive the true nature of external realities. It's like someone walking on a curve that magnifies their prejudices; the deeper they go, the more they lose sight of reality, trapped in their own fantasies.
