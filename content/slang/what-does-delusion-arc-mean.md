# Delusion Arc (Delusion Arc)
## English Explanation
**Delusion Arc:** A term often used in online gaming communities or tech circles to describe a situation where someone is so deeply immersed in a specific concept or idea, such as a theory, narrative, or gameplay strategy, that they have fundamentally altered their perception of reality. It's akin to being in a self-created or prolonged fantasy world that feels more real than the outside world. This can also refer to a mental perspective that is so skewed by emotions or misinformation that it leads to irrational and damaging decision-making. 
## Chinese Explanation
** delusion arc (幻觉弧线 huànjué héxuán):** 在网络游戏社区或科技圈中，用来描述某人因过于沉迷于某一特定概念（如理论、叙事或游戏策略）而严重扭曲了现实感知的情况。它就像是在建构或长时间沉浸于一个自创的奇幻世界中，认为这个世界比外部现实更加真实。这也可以指情绪或错误信息导致的偏颇观点，使其做出不理智且有害的决定。
