# Delusion Arc (妄想弧 Wàng xiǎng hú)
## English
**Delusion Arc**: This term is a casual way to describe a period or series of events that are overwhelmingly dominated by someone's delusions or false beliefs. It's often used when someone's actions or thoughts are heavily influenced by their own false perceptions, leading to a series of irrational or unrealistic outcomes. It's similar to saying someone is "living in a fantasy" or is "lost in their own delusion."
### Example Usage
- "Since she started watching that conspiracy theory show, she's been living in a delusion arc, completely convinced that the government is controlling weather patterns."
- "His belief that he's a world-famous actor is leading him into a delusion arc, causing him to reject any real acting offers."
## 中文
**妄想弧 (Wàng xiǎng hú)**: 这个术语是用来描述一段时间或一系列事件，这些事件主要由某人的妄想或错误信念所主导。当某人的行为或想法受到其自身错误感知的影响，导致了一系列非理性和不切实际的结果时，就会用到这个词。这类似于说某人“生活在幻想中”或“沉浸在自己的妄想中”。
### 使用示例
- "自从她开始看那个阴谋论节目后，她陷入了妄想弧，完全相信政府在控制天气模式。"
- "他坚信自己是著名的演员，这让他陷入了妄想弧，拒绝了所有的真正角色邀请。"
