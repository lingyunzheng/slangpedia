# Delusion Arc

## 中文解释
**迷途之弧**：这个词通常用来形容一个人陷入了极端的幻想或错误的信念中，就好比走在了一条通向错误信念的虚假路径上。这类似于比喻一个人走的是迷途，通往的却是一场梦的泡影。

## English Explanation
**Delusion Arc**：This slang term is often used to describe someone who has fallen into extreme fantasies or erroneous beliefs, as if they are on a path leading to mistaken ideas. It's akin to saying someone is on a wrong path, but instead of leading to a destination, it leads to an illusion or a false dream.
