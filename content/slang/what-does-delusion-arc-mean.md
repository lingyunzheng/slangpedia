## Delusion Arc
### 中文解释
“-delusion arc-”在某些中文网络社区或亚文化圈中，被用来形容一个人过度理想化或自我欺骗的行为轨迹。它指的是某人在一段时间内沉浸在错误的自我想象中，不能认清现实的情况。这个词语通常带有批评或讽刺的味道。
### 英文解释
"-delusion arc-" is a term used in some English-speaking online communities or subcultures to describe a pattern of behavior or trajectory where someone excessively idealizes or self-deceives. It refers to a situation where a person is stuck in a misguided or imaginary reality and fails to recognize the truth. This term often carries a critical or mocking tone.
