# Delusion Arc (delusion arc)
## English Explanation
In many video game contexts, a "delusion arc" can refer to a meta-slang term used sarcastically to describe a plot twist or narrative turn that is perceived as absurd or overly convoluted, often experienced by new players who might not fully understand the game's lore. It is a way of expressing frustration or disbelief at what seems to be a disconnected or implausible storyline.
