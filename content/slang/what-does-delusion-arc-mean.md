# Delusion Arc
## English
**Delusion Arc**: In some online communities, especially those centered around controversial or conspiracy theories, the "Delusion Arc" is a playful term used to describe a series of consecutive posts that are absurdly off-topic or completely unrelated to the original discussion. It is often used to mock or highlight the tangential nature of these posts. For example, if a community is discussing a specific conspiracy theory, someone might post about baking a cake or the benefits of plant-based diets, causing a sort of indirect or "arc-like" departure from the original topic.
## 中文
**幻觉弧线**：在一些以争议性或阴谋论为核心话题的在线社区中，“幻觉弧线”是一个幽默的说法，用来描述连续出现的与最初讨论完全无关或离题甚远的帖子。人们常用这个词来调侃或指出这些帖子的偏离程度。例如，如果一个社区在讨论某个特定的阴谋论，有人可能会突然发布关于做蛋糕或植物性饮食的好处等内容，导致一种间接或“弧线形”的话题偏移。
