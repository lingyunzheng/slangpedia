# Delusion Arc (Delusion 弧)
## English Explanation
**Delusion Arc**: This term is often used in online gaming communities, particularly in arenas where competitive play is prevalent, such as competitive video games. It refers to a specific strategy, tactic, or loop of behavior that players or teams follow repeatedly, believing it to be the best approach regardless of changing circumstances. The term "arc" suggests a trajectory or path that is perceived as the optimal route, even if it becomes outdated or ineffective. 
Example usage: "That team was stuck in their Delusion Arc, their standard playstyle didn't adapt to the new meta, and they lost."
## Simplified Chinese Explanation
**Delusion 弧**: 这个术语常被用在网络游戏社区中，特别是在电竞领域。它指的是玩家或队伍重复使用的一种策略、战术或行为模式，坚信这是最好的方法，即使是在不断变化的情况下。这里的“弧”意味着一条被视为最佳路线的轨迹，即便这条路线已经过时或无效。
举例使用：“那支队伍被困在了他们的Delusion Arc中，他们的固定玩法没有随着新游戏模式的出现而改变，结果输掉了比赛。”
