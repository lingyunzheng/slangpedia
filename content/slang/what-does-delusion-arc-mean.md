## Delusion Arc (Delusion 弧线)
**Chinese Explanation:**
"Delusion 弧线 (Delusion Arc)" 这个词语来源于网络用语，通常用于形容人或事情进入一种自以为是的、不切实际的状况中。这里的“弧线”是指一条曲线，形象地比喻为一个思维过程中的幻想或错觉阶段，就如同一条偏离正常轨迹的曲线。用“弧线”来形容一个人或事物进入了一个自己幻想出来的、但与现实背离的状态，表达了对情况的误解或误判。
**English Explanation:**
"Delusion Arc" is a term used in internet slang to describe someone or something that has entered a self-delusional state, where they are living in a reality that isn't based in actual facts or circumstances. The term “arc” refers to a curve or a path, symbolizing the progression of a fantastical or illusory phase. It vividly portrays a situation where someone is operating under an incorrect or misguided understanding, straying from the real path. The "arc" metaphorically represents deviating from the factual reality into a personal, often false, illusion.
