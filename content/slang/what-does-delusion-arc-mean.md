## Delusion Arc (幻觉弧线 / Huànjué Húxiàn)
**English Explanation:**
"Delusion Arc" is a slang term often used in discussions about anime and manga, particularly in the anime community. It refers to a moment in a story where a character's delusions or misconceptions lead them to believe a situation to be true, even when there is evidence to the contrary. This can involve seeing or interpreting events in a way that is not actually happening, creating a curve or arc that deviates from the reality.
**Chinese Explanation:**
"幻觉弧线" (Huànjué Húxiàn) 是动漫讨论中，尤其是在动漫社区里常用的网络用语。它特指故事中某个角色的妄想或误解导致他们认为情况是真实的，即使有相反的证据。这种情况下，角色的感知可能会出现偏离实际情况的“曲线”或“弧线”。
---
Here are a couple of examples to illustrate:
1. **Example 1:**
- English: "In the series, the main character sees his deceased friend as if he is still alive, even when everyone around him can prove the opposite. This is a classic example of a delusion arc."
- Chinese: "在系列中，主角常常将已故的朋友视若还生，即使周围的人都能证明相反的事实。这就是典型的‘妄想弧线’的例子。"
2. **Example 2:**
- English: "When a character confuses a nightmare for reality and acts on it, leading to misinterpretations and incorrect conclusions, this scene is often described using 'delusion arc'."
- Chinese: "当某个角色将噩梦误认为是现实，进而采取行动并导致错误的解读和结论时，这个场景通常被描述为‘妄想弧线’。"
