## Delusion Arc (Delusion 弧线)
### 中文解释
“幻觉弧线”这个词组虽然是一个直译，但在网络语言中，它被用来表示一个人因为长期沉浸在不切实际或完全错误的想法中，逐渐偏离了正确的认知路径。可以用在描述一个人的思维在一种错误但又是自我感觉良好的状态下不断扩展，就像一个逐渐变大的弧线一样。
### 英文 Explanation
"Delusion Arc" is a play on words, as it’s a direct translation. In internet slang, it refers to someone who becomes increasingly detached from reality and logic, gradually straying away from accurate cognition, while feeling good about their own delusional beliefs. It can be used to describe a person's thoughts expanding into a larger arc based on false but satisfyingly self-validating ideas.
