# Delusion Arc
## 中文解释 🏴‍☠️
“Delusion Arc”这个词汇可能不是直接源自中文的，但我们可以给它一个有意图的中文翻译。它指的是在某个特定的情境下，个体完全沉浸在一种非现实或不切实际的想法或幻想中，这种状态类似于日文中的“妄想弧”（まばゆい妄想 arc，Mabayui mangasho arc），这个词组用来形容一个人沉浸在强烈的幻觉或错误的假设中，完全无法看清现实。
## 英文解释 🏴‍☠️
"Delusion Arc" refers to a period or state where an individual is deeply and completely immersed in unrealistic or false thoughts and fantasies, essentially living in a fabricated world of their own making. Similar to the Japanese term "妄想弧" (Mabayui mangasho arc), which describes someone being so entangled in strong delusions or incorrect assumptions they can't distinguish between truth and illusion. It often implies a loss of touch with reality, where the person's mental state is so twisted that they cannot see the truth any longer.
