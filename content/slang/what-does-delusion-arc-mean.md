## Delusion Arc (梦幻弧光 / Huànmiàn Húguāng)
**English: Delusion Arc**
- **Meaning:** A term tossed around in internet subcultures, often used to describe an unrealistic or overly optimistic outlook on a situation, usually in the context of online interactions or fantasy scenarios. It can also refer to a phase or period where someone is under the influence of a false idea or set of ideas.
**Chinese: 幻想弧光**
- **Meaning:** 来源于网络亚文化，常用来形容一种不切实际或过于乐观的看待事物的态度，特别是在网络互动或幻想情节中的情境下。它也可以指一个人在受到虚假想法或者一组错误想法的影响下的阶段或时期。
