## Delusion Arc
### English:
**Delusion Arc**: A term often used in informal or internet slang, particularly in online gaming communities or fictional scenarios. It generally refers to a situation where a person or character is in a state or experiences events that are entirely imaginary or irrational, akin to being in a fantasy world unaffected by reality. Characters in a "Delusion Arc" might act based on false beliefs, leading to potentially humorous or chaotic outcomes.
### Chinese:
**幻觉弧线 (Huànjué huósùan)**：这是一个常见于非正式或网络俚语中的词，尤其是在线游戏社区或虚构场景中。通常用来形容一个人或角色处于完全想象或不理性的情况，就像生活在一个人间仙境，不受现实影响。处于“幻觉弧线”中的角色可能基于虚假的信念行动，导致可能的有趣或混乱的局面。
