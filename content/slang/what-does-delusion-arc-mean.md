## Delusion Arc (妄想弧)
### Chinese Explanation
**妄想弧 (Wàng xiǎng hú)**: 在中文网络用语中，“妄想弧”通常用来形容一种处于幻想或不切实际的想象中的状态或经历。类似于英文中的“delusion arc”，它指的是一个人进入了一个完全脱离现实的幻想世界，或者是在某些虚构的、不可实现的情境中迷失了自己。
### English Explanation
**Delusion Arc**: This term is used in English slang to describe a period or state where someone is deeply immersed in illogical or unrealistic thoughts or experiences, as if they have entered a fantasy world separate from reality. This state of mind can be seen as an arc or journey through irrational beliefs or wishful thinking that may not align with the real world.
---
Both terms are used metaphorically to describe a mental or emotional journey through fictitious or highly unrealistic circumstances or states of mind.
