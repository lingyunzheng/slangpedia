## Delusion Arc
### English Explanation
A **delusion arc** in gaming or social media context is a phase or period where a player or a group of players is stuck in a loop of irrational or wishful thinking, believing that a certain strategy or idea is infallible, even when faced with clear evidence to the contrary. This term is often used to describe a situation where players become overly confident and refuse to adapt to changing circumstances.
### 中文解释
在游戏或社交媒体的语境中，“幻象弧”指的是当一名玩家或一群玩家陷入一种不理性的或是幻想性的思考循环，坚信某种策略或想法是无懈可击的，即便面对明显相反的证据也不愿意改变。这个术语通常用来描述一种玩家过于自信以至于不愿意根据情况变化进行调整的状态。
