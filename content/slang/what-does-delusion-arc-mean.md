## Delusion Arc (中文：幻觉弧线)
### English Explanation:
**Delusion Arc** is a term that can refer to a situation where someone's actions are based on a false belief or a figment of their imagination. It can also describe a narrative arc where a character experiences or exhibits delusions, leading them to make decisions based on these false beliefs. This term might be used in fiction, psychology, or everyday conversations to describe a path or journey taken under the influence of a delusion.
### 中文解释:
**幻觉弧线（Delusion Arc）**，是指一个人的行为基于一种虚假的信念或纯粹的幻想。这个词也可能用来描述一个故事情节中，角色经历或表现出幻觉，导致他们根据这些虚假的信念做出决定。这种术语可以在小说、心理学或日常生活对话中用来描述在幻觉影响下所走的道路或旅程。
