# Delusion Arc (幻觉弧线)
**中文解释**：德兜孪（délōu tuán）弧线，是指一个人陷在自己编织的幻想中，不愿意承认现实的情况。这种状况就像是被一个虚假的光环笼罩，使得他们看不到周围的实际情况。通常用来形容沉浸在个人想象或错误信念中的人。
**English Explanation**：
**Delusion Arc** (Phantasm Arc): Refers to a person who is so deeply entrenched in their own imagined scenarios that they are unwilling to face reality. This can be likened to being under the spell of an illusory arc that prevents them from recognizing the true circumstances around them. It's commonly used to describe individuals who are deeply entrenched in their fantasies or false beliefs.
