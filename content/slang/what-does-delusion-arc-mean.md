## Delusion Arc
### English
**Definition:** A delusion arc is a phase in various contexts, often in fiction or mock discussions, where a character or the narrator falls into a state of false or mistaken belief, typically involving grandiose or paranoid ideas.
**Example Usage:** "After几个月的疯狂追查，主角发现了一个涉及整个国家的宏伟阴谋，这简直就是一个典型的delusion arc，让观众无法理解他的行为。"  
(Translation: "After months of intense investigation, the protagonist discovers a grand conspiracy involving the entire nation. This is a classic delusion arc that makes it difficult for viewers to understand his actions.")
### Chinese
**释义:** Delusion arc是一种情节发展，通常在虚构故事或模拟讨论中发生，主角或叙述者陷入一种虚假或错误的信念中，通常包含极度膨胀或偏执的想法。
**例句使用:** "主角开始对他人的行动产生极度偏执的看法，并误认为自己是拯救世界的唯一希望，这正是一个典型的delusion arc。观众不禁因为主角的奇怪行为而困惑。"  
(Translation: "The protagonist begins to form extremely paranoid views about others' actions, mistakenly believing himself to be the sole savior of the world. This is a classic delusion arc, leaving the audience baffled by the protagonist's strange behavior.")
