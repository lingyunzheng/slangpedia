## Delusion Arc
### English
**Delusion Arc**: This term is a playful or ironic way of referring to a period or event where someone is knowingly or unknowingly living in a fantasy or false reality, often caught in a self-created or induced bubble of illusion. It's like when someone is so deep into a dream world that they believe they're living a perfect life, even if it's not based in truth.
### Chinese
** delusion arc **（幻想弧线）: 这个术语是一种轻松或讽刺的方式来描述一个人有意识或无意识地生活在一个幻想或虚假现实中的时期或事件。它指的是某人沉浸在一个自己创造或陷入的幻想泡泡中，相信自己过着理想的或完美的人生，尽管这可能并不符合现实。想象一个人完全沉浸在自己的理想世界里，认为自己处于梦幻般的生活中。
