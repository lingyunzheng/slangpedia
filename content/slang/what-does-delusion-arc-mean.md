# Delusion Arc (双重幻想弧形)
**英语版:** 
"Delusion Arc" is a slang term that describes a situation where two people are caught in a web of misunderstandings and false beliefs, often leading to a confusing or frustrating scenario. It's like when two people have very different perceptions of the same situation, and neither of them can see the other's point of view.
**中文版:** 
"Delusion Arc" 是一个俚语，用来形容两个人陷入了误解和虚假信念的重重迷雾中，常常导致混乱或令人沮丧的局面。就好比两个人对于同一情况有着完全不同的看法，而他们却都看不见对方的视角。
