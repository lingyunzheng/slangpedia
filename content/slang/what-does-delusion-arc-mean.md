# Delusion Arc
## English
**Delusion Arc**: A humorous or satirical term referring to a situation that seems out of touch with reality, often used lightheartedly to describe a person or group's stubborn belief in something improbable or absurd. It's often employed in a playful manner, like in memes or discussions about extreme conspiracy theories or overly optimistic plans.
Example Usage: "That new business model he's pushing seems like a Delusion Arc – no one believes in it."
## 中文
**Delusion Arc**: 一个充满幽默或讽刺的表述，用来形容一种看似脱离现实的情况，通常用来描述一个人或一群人的顽固信念，这种信念常常是不可行或荒谬的。通常这种说法是以一种玩笑的方式来使用的，比如在网络上的表情包、关于极端阴谋论或过于乐观的计划的讨论中。
