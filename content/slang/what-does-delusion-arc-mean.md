# Delusion Arc (Delusion 弧线)
## English Explanation
**Delusion Arc**: This term is often used in online gaming or social media communities to describe a situation where players or members of a community become overly optimistic and believe that a certain strategy or plan will lead to success. It's likened to a "delusion arc" where there's an initial upward curve of hope, followed by a steep decline when reality sets in.
## 中文解释
**Delusion 弧线**: 这个术语常用于网络游戏或社交媒体社区中，用来形容一种情况：玩家或社区成员变得过于乐观，坚信某种策略或计划能够带来成功。这种现象被视为一种“幻觉弧线”，起初会有一个希望上升的曲线，随后当现实来临，会迎来一个急剧下降的过程。
