# Delusion Arc
## 中文释义
_delusion arc_ 网络用语，意指一个人陷入某种虚幻或不切实际的想法、情感或者观念之中，就像在一个扭曲的、不现实的弧度里一样。这个词通常用来形容一个人被某种幻想所蒙蔽，无法清醒地看待现实。
## 英文 Explanation
_**Delusion Arc**_ is a slang term on the internet that refers to someone who is deeply immersed in a fabricated or unrealistic set of thoughts, feelings, or beliefs, as if they are trapped in a distorted, unrealistic arc. This phrase is often used to describe a situation where someone is heavily influenced by an illusion and fails to see reality clearly.
This term is often used in contexts where individuals are mislead by fantasy or an idealized version of reality rather than the harsher, more truthful aspects of life.
