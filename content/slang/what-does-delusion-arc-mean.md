## Delusion Arc (梦境弧线)
**中文解释**：这是一个涉及到不切实际的想法或者对未来的一种乌托邦式的期待的语言表达。通常这种说法用来描述某个人处于一种自欺欺人的状态，执着于一种不合实际的情景或者情节。
**English Explanation**：This phrase refers to unrealistic ideas or an utopian expectation of the future. It is often used to describe someone who is in a state of self-deception, clinging to an unrealistic scenario or plot.
**Example Usage**：
中文示例**：他在玩一款游戏，一直沉浸在“ delusion arc”的世界里，认为自己能够破解所有困难。
**English Example**：He is playing a game and is deeply immersed in the "Delusion Arc" world, believing he can overcome all difficulties.
