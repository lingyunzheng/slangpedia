## Delusion Arc
**中文解释:** 
“幻觉弧”是一个网络用语，用来形容一个人在其乌托邦式幻想中的生活，并且深陷其中而不愿面对现实的情况。它形象地描述了一种逃避现实的心理状态，仿佛进入了一个只有自己感受到的虚幻世界。
**英文解释:** 
"Delusion Arc" is a netspeak term that describes a person living in a utopian fantasy and unwilling to face reality. It vividly portrays a state of mind where one is escaping from reality, as if they have entered a quasi-fictional world that only they can perceive.
