# Delusion Arc
## English Explanation
A "delusion arc" is often used in anime and manga contexts to describe a storyline or plot turn where a character's delusions or false beliefs significantly impact the narrative. It typically refers to moments where a character's perception is skewed, leading to a series of misunderstandings or misfortunes that can advance the story in unexpected ways.
## Chinese Explanation
"幻觉弧线"（huàn jué huábxiàn）常用于动漫中，指角色的幻觉或错误信念对剧情产生重大影响的故事转折点。它通常指的是角色的感知扭曲，导致一系列误解或不幸事件，从而以意想不到的方式推动故事情节的发展。
### Example Usage:
- In the anime series **Present Arc**, the protagonist experiences a delusion arc where they believe they are constantly being watched, leading to a series of misadventures and misunderstandings.
- 在动漫系列《现在弧线》中，主角经历了一个幻觉弧线，他们误以为自己一直被监视，从而引发了一系列的困境和误会。
