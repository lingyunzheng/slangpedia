## English Slang: Pop Off
### Definition
"Pop off" is a colloquial phrase often used to mean to start doing something with enthusiasm, usually involving excitement, action, or energy. It can also refer to making a sound (like a loud noise or a sharp click), or in a heated situation, it can be used to describe someone who is acting aggressively or overreacting.
### Example Usage
- **English**: "When the bar opened, everyone started popping off with drinks and dancing!"
- **Chinese**: "当酒吧开门的时候，每个人都开始尽情地喝酒和跳舞！"
### Context
This term is often used in casual conversations, especially among young people or in social media contexts. It can convey a sense of revelry, excitement, or intense engagement in an activity.
### Related Terms
- **英语近义词**：Start doing something with energy, Go wild
- **中文近义词**：尽情参与, 发疯般地做某事
