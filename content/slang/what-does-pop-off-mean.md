## English Slang: Pop Off
### 中文俚语解释：爆改 / 爆发
#### Definition:
- **English:** To behave in an abrupt or unexpected manner, often with an excess of energy or enthusiasm.
- **中文:** 指突然或意外地表现出激烈或过分的热情或能量的行为。
#### Example:
- **English:** He started pop off and got into a big argument with his coworker.
- **中文:** 他突然爆改，和同事吵起了一架。
#### Context:
- **English:** This slang is used to describe someone who is suddenly intense, boisterous, or aggressive in a situation.
- **中文:** 这个俚语常用于形容某人在特定情况下突然变得激烈、狂野或具有攻击性。
