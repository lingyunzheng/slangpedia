## pop off
**English:**  
To suddenly become very angry and start talking or acting in an unpleasant or insulting way. It can also mean to start doing something in an enthusiastic or intense way.
**Example:**  
- "When he didn't get the promotion, he really popped off at the boss."
**中文:**  
突然变得非常生气，开始以不愉快或冒犯的方式说话或行动。也可以指以激动或奋力的方式开始做某事。
**示例：**  
- "当他没有得到晋升时，他真的对老板大发雷霆。"
