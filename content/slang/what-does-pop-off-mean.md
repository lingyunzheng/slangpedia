## Pop Off (also "Poppa Off")
### English:
When someone gets so excited, angry, or riled up that they start acting impulsively or aggressively. Can also mean to express shock or disbelief in a dramatic way, often followed by crude or witty remarks.  
**Examples:**
- "He just popped off when he found out he didn't get the job."
- "When she heard the news, she popped off with a series of expletives."
### 中文解释:
指一个人因为兴奋、愤怒或被激怒而变得冲动或攻击性地行动。也可以用来形容以戏剧化的方式表达震惊或惊讶，并常伴有粗鲁或俏皮的评论。
**例句:**
- "他发现没有拿到那份工作后就开始胡言乱语起来。"
- "听完那个消息后，她用了一系列脏话来表达自己的惊讶。"
