## "Pop Off" 反转
### English Explanation
"Pop off" is a slang phrase that can be used in a few different ways. Primarily, it's used to describe a quick, sharp movement or action, like popping the cap off a soda bottle. In a more colloquial context, it can also mean to start acting crazy or violently, or to engage in a high-energy, loud, or disruptive activity. Sometimes, it can be used to express excitement or to initiate something intense or reckless.
### Chinese Explanation
“Pop off” 这个俚语用法比较多样。首先，它可以用来形容一种快速而干脆的动作，比如拉开可乐瓶盖。在更为口语化的语境中，这个词也可以指变得疯狂或暴力，或者是参与高强度、大声或具有破坏性活动。有时，这个词还用于表达激动情绪或是启动某种激烈或鲁莽的行为。
