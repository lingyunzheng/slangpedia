## "Pop Off"
**English:**  
To act aggressively or confrontationally; to start making noise or suddenly become very enthusiastic.  
*Usage:* "When the game was tied, he just started pop off and scored three times in a row."
**Chinese (Mandarin):**  
猛烈开干；开始大声吵闹或突然变得极其兴奋。  
*用法:* “当比分为平时，他开始猛烈开干，连续得分三次。”
