```markdown
## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Pop Off"
**English:** To engage in a sudden or impulsive action, often impetuously or violently.
- **Example in Use:** "When he didn't get the promotion, he just decided to pop off and quit his job."
- **Synonyms:** Blow up, explode, go off, rant
**中文:** 指突然或冲动地采取行动，往往带有突然性和激烈的意味。
- **例句使用:** "当他没有得到晋升时，他决定突然辞职了。"
- **近义词:** 爆发, 爆炸, 激烈行动, 大发雷霆
```
