## "Pop off" (English to Chinese)
### English Definition
"Pop off" is an informal phrase that can have a few different meanings depending on the context. It's most commonly used to mean to become very excited, start talking loudly, or to begin something aggressively or competitively.
- Derived from: The verb "pop" can mean "to explode" or "to start suddenly," and "off" is often used to indicate the start of an action or reaction.
### Chinese Explanation
1. **变兴奋/开始激烈讨论 (Biàn Xìnggùn / Kěng Jié Tàolùn)**
- 意思是指某人突然变得非常兴奋，或是开始激烈地讨论某个话题。
- 例如：他一听到这个消息就pop off了，不停地兴奋地说个不停。
2. **开始激烈竞争 (Kěng Jié Jìngzhēng)**
- 意思是指某人开始激烈地竞争或参与某种竞争性活动。
- 例如：比赛刚开始，所有人都pop off，全力以赴地竞争每一个环节。
### Example Usage
- **English:** He popped off the moment he heard about the concert.
- **Chinese:** 他就听到演唱会的消息时立刻pop off了。
---
### Context and Usage Tips
Use "pop off" in casual settings to describe an excited outburst or an enthusiastic action. It's often associated with youthful or energetic behavior, and can sometimes imply a more abrupt or intense reaction than other terms might convey.
