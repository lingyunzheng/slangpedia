# "Pop Off" (also "popoff")
## English
### Meaning
To start or engage in an argument, confrontation, or fight, often with a sudden and agitated manner.
### Example
- "Yo, don't pop off with me like that! Calm down!"
## 中文
### 含义
突然开始争论、对抗或打架，通常带有激昂的情绪。
### 例句
- "喂，别对我这么激动！冷静点！"
