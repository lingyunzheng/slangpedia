## 翻译解释：Pop Off
### 中文解释：
“pop off” 翻译成中文时可以表达为“开撕”或“开干”。这个词组通常用来形容在聊天或社交媒体上激烈争论、争吵，或是指在某种情况下采取激烈的行动。
### 英文解释：
- "pop off": This phrase is often used to describe a situation where someone starts arguing or engaging in a heated discussion, usually on the internet or in a social media setting. It can also mean taking a more direct and aggressive action in response to something.
Example Usage:
- 中文：他在Twitter上Pop Off了，和其他网友激烈争论。
- 英文：He popped off on Twitter, getting into a heated argument with other users.
