## "Pop off"
### English Explanation:
To "pop off" is an informal and often colloquial phrase that typically means to start doing something with a lot of energy, enthusiasm, or sometimes to break out into conflict or go for it. It can also imply unplugging from technology or abruptly ending something, like a relationship.
### Chinese Explanation:
"Pop off" 是一个非正式且常用于口语的短语，通常意味着以极大的热情、活力或有时是冲突的方式去做某事。它也可以表示脱离技术或突然结束某事，比如一段关系。
