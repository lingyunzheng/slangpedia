# Pop Off (俚语解释)
## English
**Pop Off**: To start yelling or ranting, often in a loud and expressive way, or to begin arguing or fighting. It can also refer to the act of abruptly moving to a new place or situation.
**Example**: "When he found out he didn't get the promotion, he really popped off and started yelling at everyone in the office."
## 中文
**Pop Off**: 指大声喊叫或发泄，通常是夸张地表达情绪，也可以指突然决定搬家或改变处境。
**例句**: “当他发现没有晋升后，他真的大喊大叫，开始向办公室里所有人都发泄。”
