# "Pop Off" - Different Meanings in English and Chinese
## English Slang: "Pop Off"
- **General Meaning**: To start doing something abruptly, often with enthusiasm or aggression.
- **Context**: This can be used to describe sudden, intense actions or reactions.
- **Example**: "When he heard the news, he pop off and got into his car to drive to the venue."
## Chinese Slang: "pop off" (暴走, "bào zǒu")
- **General Meaning**: 表示一种不受控制，突然爆发的行为，可以是激动、愤怒或者是兴奋的状态。
- **Context**: 描述一种突然的冲动行为，可能带有情绪化的色彩。
- **Example**: “听到这个消息，他暴走起来，直接跳上车往目的地开去。”

This translation captures the essence of "pop off" in both English and Chinese, adapting it to fit the different cultural contexts.
