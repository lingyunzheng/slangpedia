## Pop Off (英语: Pop Off)
### English Explanation
To pop off is an informal and colloquial expression often conveying that someone is acting in a very aggressive, disruptive, or dramatic manner, often in a situation where they are not expected to. It’s also used to describe someone venting or getting into something (like rap battles, arguments, etc.) in a forceful and often confrontational way. Essentially, it means someone is being hyperactive or extremely loud and assertive in a given moment.
### Chinese Explanation
“Pop Off” 是一个非正式的俚语表达，通常用来形容某人以非常激进、破坏性或戏剧化的方式行动，尤其是在不被期望如此做的情况下。此外，它还可以描述某人发泄情绪或进入某种情况（比如说唱battle、争论等），以一种强制性和常常是冲突的形式进行。总的来说，这意味着某人在特定时刻表现得非常活跃或特别大声且具有强烈的表达欲。
