# "Pop Off"
## English
To act aggressively or boastfully. Often implies a show of force or confrontation.
**Example:**
- "He decided to pop off when he saw his rival winning the game."
## Chinese
炫耀或者挑衅地行动。通常暗示展示力量或引发争端。
**例句:**
- "当他看到对手赢了比赛时，决定炫耀自己(或挑衅对手)。"
