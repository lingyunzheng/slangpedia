## "Pop Off"
### English Explanation
To "pop off" in slang is to suddenly and enthusiastically start acting or doing something. It can also indicate spontaneous or intense activity, often with a feeling of excitement or aggression.
### Chinese Explanation
"Pop off" 作为一种俚语，在英语中的意思是突然且热情地开始做某事。这个词也可以表示自发或激烈的行动，通常带有兴奋或激烈的意味。
