# "Pop Off" in English & Chinese Slang
## English
**Meaning:** To make a fast, energetic, or sudden movement, often in an exciting or thrilling manner.
**Example:** "He popped off down the street as soon as the race began."
**Chinese (Simplified):** 跳起来 (tiào qǐ lái) — 比喻采取快速或突然的动作，充满活力或兴奋。
**Example:** “他一听到枪声就跳了起来。”
## Chinese
**Meaning:** 像突然跳起来一样行动，通常带有激动或兴奋的感觉。
**Example:** 他一听到枪声就跳了起来。
**English Equivalent:** To pop off.
