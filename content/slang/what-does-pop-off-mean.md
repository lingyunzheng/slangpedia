## "Pop Off" - English & Chinese Slang Explanation
### English
**Pop Off**:
- **Meaning**: To start a fight or conflict; to start expressing intense emotions, often aggressive or angry.
- **Example**: "He got so mad that he started to pop off at his boss."
### Chinese
**开炮 / kāi pào**:
- **Meaning**: 指开始大声争吵或打架，通常是激烈的、带有攻击性的；也指情感激动地表达自己的情绪。
- **例句**：他非常生气，于是开始开炮和老板争吵。
