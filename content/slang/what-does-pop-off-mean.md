## Pop Off
### English
To pop off means to start speaking or acting in a way that's aggressive, boastful, or competitive, often in the heat of the moment. It can also refer to abruptly leaving a situation, usually due to feeling frustrated or annoyed.
**Example:**
- "Once he realized he lost the game, he started to pop off about how unfair it all was."
### 中文
"Pop off" 表示开始表现为挑衅的、夸耀的或竞争性的行为，通常是在情绪激动的时候。这个词也可以用来形容突然离开某个情境，通常是出于失望或生气的原因。
**例句：**
- "一旦他发现输掉了比赛，就开始抱怨比赛是多么的不公平。"
