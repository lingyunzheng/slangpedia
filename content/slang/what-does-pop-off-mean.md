# "Pop Off"
## English Explanation
To "pop off" is a slang term that originated in urban American English. It can be used in a variety of contexts:
- **To Start Something Aggressively or Violently:** "He got a text saying his ex was checking him out, and he literally popped off."
- **To Begin Doing Something with Enthusiasm or Zeal:** "When she started painting, she really popped off."
- **To Begin Behaving in a Nasty or Mean Manner:** "He was nice at first, but then his friends showed up, and he really popped off."
## Chinese Explanation

- **形容充满热情或激情地开始做事:** 比如：“她一拿起画笔，简直是 pop off 了。”
