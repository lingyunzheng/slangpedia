## Pop Off

### English
To start something suddenly or impulsively, often in a energetic or confrontational manner. Can also be used to describe weird or inappropriate behavior.

**Example:** "He popped off when he found out he didn't get the promotion."

### 中文
突然或冲动地开始做某事，通常是一种充满能量或具有对抗性的方式。也可以用来描述怪异或不适当的行为。

**例句:** "当他发现没有得到晋升时，他突然发火了。"
