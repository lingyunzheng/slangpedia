```markdown
# "Pop off"
**English:** To start doing something with energy, enthusiasm, or aggression.
**Chinese:** 开启激烈前行或快速行动，通常带有精力充沛、充满激情或带有攻击性的情绪。
## Example:
- **English:** "He was so excited about the game, he just had to pop off and start playing immediately."
- **Chinese:** 他对于比赛非常兴奋，简直迫不及待地想要开始玩了。
- **Literal Translation:** 他太激动了，直接开启激烈前行。
```
```markdown
# "Pop off"
**English:** (Disとも interpret) To stop talking or communicating, especially to abruptly end an ongoing conversation, usually out of irritation.
**Chinese:** 停止说话或交流，尤其是在对话进行中突然并且带有烦躁的情绪结束对话。
## Example:
- **English:** "She got annoyed with his constant questions and just popped off without answering."
- **Chinese:** 她被他不停地提问惹怒了，结果直接结束对话没有回答。
- **Literal Translation:** 她忍无可忍，直接停止说话。
```
