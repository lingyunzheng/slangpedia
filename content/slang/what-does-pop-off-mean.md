# Slang Explanation: Pop Off
**English:**
- **Definition:** To start or escalate an argument, conflict, or confrontation.
- **Example Use in Sentence:** "When he didn't pay his share of the bill, his friend just decided to pop off and start yelling."
**Chinese (简体中文):**
- **解释:** 开始或升级争端、冲突或对抗。
- **例句使用:** "当他不付他那份账单时，他的朋友就决定开骂，开始大声喊叫。"
