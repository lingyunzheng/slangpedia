## Pop Off
**English:**  
To start doing something aggressively, energetically, or to express excitement and enthusiasm in a forceful way.
**Chinese (简体):**  
疯狂行动起来，用一种具有攻击性、充满 vigor 的方式开始做某事，或者表达激动和热情时使用。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** He really popped off when he started playing basketball, giving it all he had.
- **Chinese:** 他一打起篮球来就疯狂行动起来，全身心地投入比赛。
