# "Pop off"
## English Explanation
To "pop off" is a slang phrase that generally means to start engaging in intense or aggressive action. It can also refer to making a sudden, surprising move or to continuing an activity with great energy and enthusiasm.
### Example:
- "He decided to pop off and give a speech at the rally."
## Chinese Explanation
“pop off” 的俚语含义通常是开始进行激烈或挑衅的行为。它也可以指突然做出动作或以极大的热情和活力继续某种活动。
### 示例：
- "他决定 pop off 并在集会上发表演讲。"
