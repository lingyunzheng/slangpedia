## "Pop Off" in English
### Definition
- **Literal Meaning:** To pop off can also mean to shoot or fire a gun.
- **Colloquial Meaning:** In a broader, less violent context, it often means to get excited, start doing something abruptly or energetically, or to have a breakdown or mental breakdown.
### Usage Examples
- *Literally:* "He popped off and shot the intruder."
- *Colloquially:* "Yo, when the game started, he just popped off and took the lead!"
- *Colloquially:* "She was so frustrated that she popped off and said some pretty harsh words."
## "爆肝" (Bào Gān) in Chinese
### Definition
- **Literal Meaning:** "爆肝" literally translates to " exploding liver," which is a hyperbolic way to describe extreme effort or working/exercising excessively.
- **Colloquial Meaning:** It is used to describe intense study or working to the point where one feels as though their liver is about to explode with the effort.
### Usage Examples
- *Colloquially:* "他熬夜爆肝准备期末考试。" (Tā yānhuó bào gān zhǔnbèi qūmò kǎoshì.) — He pulled all-nighters and worked super hard to prepare for the final exam.
- *Colloquially:* "训练要爆肝才能提高体能。" (.Xùnliàn yào bào gān cái néng tígāo tǐnéng.) — You have to work hard and intensely to improve your fitness.
