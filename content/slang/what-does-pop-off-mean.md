### English: Pop Off
**Definition**: To abruptly start a behavior, usually competitive, intense, or expressive in nature. It can also mean to suddenly engage in an activity or start talking excitedly.
**Example**: "When he heard the news, he just popped off and started fencing about what he was going to do next."
### 中文：Pop Off
**释义**：突然开始某种行为，通常是竞争性的、激烈的或表达式的。它也可以指突然参与某个活动或开始兴奋地说话。
**例句**："当他听到消息时，他just popped off，就开始兴奋地讨论接下来要做什么。"
