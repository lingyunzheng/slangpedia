# "Pop Off" (俚语)
**English Explanation:** 
- To "pop off" means to act aggressively, violently, or impulsively, often in reaction to a provocation. It can also indicate the beginning of something, suggesting an exciting or explosive start.
**中文解释：**
- "pop off" 表示行动激烈、暴力或冲动，通常是对挑衅的反应。这个词也可以暗示某物的开始，意味着一种激动人心或爆炸性的启动。
