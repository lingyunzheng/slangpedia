### English: Pop Off
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
| **English** | **Simplified Chinese** | **Explanation** |
|------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Pop Off    | 抠脚、爆肝             | **Meaning**: To do something with a lot of energy, often in a way that's surprising or intense. It can also refer to when someone gets into an argument or starts fighting, usually in a sudden and passionate way. <br> **Example**: "He popped off when he heard that someone was spreading rumors about him." - 他听到有人在背后说他坏话的时候，突然开始跟人吵架。 |
