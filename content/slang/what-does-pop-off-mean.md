# Pop Off (俚语解释)
## 中文解释
“pop off” 多用于英文俚语中，可以翻译成中文的类似表达有“发起攻击”、“突然行动”、“大搞特搞”或“发飙”。具体含义根据语境有所不同。
## 英文解释
- ** initialValues**
- To start doing something suddenly or aggressively, often referring to criticism, complaints, or behavior that is seen as disruptive or aggressive. For example, if someone starts complaining about a situation, they might be "popping off."
- ** Example Usage:**
- "He just couldn’t take it anymore and started to pop off at his boss for no reason."
- "When the game wasn’t going their way, the team popped off by attacking their opponents."
- ** Related Terms:**
- Blab: To talk or talk about something in a gossipy or indiscreet way.
- Dump: To throw something away or stop using it, often used to talk about relationships.
- ** Cultural Note:**
- The term is common in American and some other English-speaking cultures, often used in casual or informal settings.
