## "Pop Off"
**English:** To act in an aggressive or disruptive manner, often coinciding with excitement or anger.
**中文:** 原本指以非常激进或破坏性的行为应对某事，如今多用于表达兴奋或愤怒的情绪。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "The fans started to pop off after the team lost the game."
- 中文: "球迷在球队输掉比赛后开始表现出非常激进的行为。"
