## "Pop Off" (俚语解释)
### 中文
“pop off” 的中文俚语解释是 **开玩笑了** 或 **搞起来**。
- **开玩笑了**：表示某人话中的讽刺或嘲讽意味，通常带有开玩笑的语气。
- **搞起来**：带有更直接的行动感，可以用来形容开始行动或兴奋地做某事。
### 英文
- When someone **"pop off"**, it can mean they are **sounding off** (getting in touch with or voicing one's real feelings or beliefs in a bold manner), or they are **getting into something** (starting to do something more actively or intensely).
- The phrase can also imply a shift in tone or behavior, similar to "ranting" but often with a bit more energy and less seriousness, used in casual or informal conversation.
