### pop off
**English:** 
To start talking or acting in a very enthusiastic or excited way, often in a loud or aggressive manner. Can also be used to describe breaking away or leaving quickly.
**Literal Meaning and Usage in Chinese:**
"发出声音"或者"突然开始激烈行动"，通常用来形容说话或行动变得非常热情或激动，有时带有大声或激进的意思。也可以用来描述迅速离开或行动。
**Example in a Sentence:**
- **English:** He really got into debating today and it was like he popped off!
- **Chinese:** 今天他真的被辩论吸引进去了，就像是突然‘发了声音’一样！
**Elevated Version:**
- **English:** He went off on a tangent about the climate crisis, truly popping off with enthusiasm!
- **Chinese:** 他关于气候危机侃侃而谈，真的是充满了热情地'开始了'！
**Fun Fact:**
The phrase likely originates from the sound of a small gun, which is often described as "popping off." Over time, it evolved to mean sudden, forceful actions or speech.
