## "Pop Off" - 爆粗
**English:** Pop off  
**Chinese (爆粗):** 爆粗
**Explanation:**
- **Pop off** in English is a slang term that generally means to go crazy, to rage, or to start acting in an aggressive, loud, or intense manner. It can also imply that someone is totally getting into something, like a situation or an activity, sometimes with a bit of exaggeration or even reckless behavior.
- **爆粗（bào cū）** is a Chinese internet slang term that carries a similar meaning. It literally translates to "exploding coarsely" or "blasting coarsely." It is used to describe someone or something that is going wild, crazy, or extremely intense. It often conveys a sense of excitement, anger, or frustration that is expressed in an extremely loud or blunt manner.
**Usage Example:**
- **English:** "He really popped off when he found out about the prank."
- **Chinese:** "他知道了这个恶作剧后真的爆粗了。" (Tā zhī cáozhe zhège èzuòjù hòu zhēn de 爆粗 了。)
This phrase can also be used in contexts where someone is getting into something enthusiastically:
- **English:** "She popped off the moment she heard the news."
- **Chinese:** "她一听那个消息就爆粗了，立刻行动起来。" (Tā yī tīng nà gē ge xi īnxīnɡ jiù 爆粗 了，立刻行动起来。)
