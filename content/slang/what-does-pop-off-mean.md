# "Pop Off" in English and Chinese
## English
**Pop Off**: To start doing something abruptly, often in a dramatic or enthusiastic way. It can also mean to indulge in excessive, often disruptive behavior.
### Example
- *English*: He decided to pop off and start performing his dance routine right in the middle of the lecture.
- *Chinese*: 他决定突然开始在讲座中即兴跳舞。
## 中文
**pop off**：突然做一些事情，往往是戏剧性或充满激情的方式。它也可以指放纵行为，通常带有破坏性。
### 例句
- *中文*: 他决定在讲座中间突然开始跳起舞来。
