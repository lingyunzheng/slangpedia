# "Pop Off"
## English
**Meaning:** To act aggressively, impulsively, or in an excited and disruptive manner.
## Chinese
**含义:** 指行为上变得激进、冲动或非常兴奋和破坏性。
### 示例
- **English:** He really popped off when the game score changed.
- **Chinese:** 当比赛比分变化时，他真的变得激进了。
