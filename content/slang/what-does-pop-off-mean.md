```markdown
## Pop Off
**English:** 
To start doing something abruptly or enthusiastically, often in a way that could be disruptive or offensive.
**中文解释：**
突然开始做某事，通常是激动地、猛然地，并且可能有些冒犯或破坏性。
```
```markdown
## Pop Off
**English:** 
Short for "getting popped off," it means to start acting aggressively, rebellious, or getting into conflict.
**中文解释：**
“getting popped off”的简称，指开始表现得具有攻击性、叛逆或者陷入冲突。
```
