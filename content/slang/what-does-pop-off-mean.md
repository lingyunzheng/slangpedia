# Slang Term: Pop Off
## English Explanation
To "pop off" means to start an intense or aggressive action, often in a sudden or unexpected way. It can also refer to talking or reacting in an enthusiastic, energetic, or argumentative manner.
## Example Usage
- *He was about to pop off when he saw the approach of the police.*
## Bilingual Slang Explanation
**中文解释**：当某人突然采取激烈的行动或行为，或者以一种激动、有活力或争论的方式讲话或反应时，就可以说是“pop off”。这里既可以指具体的行动，也可以指言语上的激烈反应。
**例句**：
- 他一看到警察过来就要“pop off”（他一看到警察过来就准备采取激烈行动）。
