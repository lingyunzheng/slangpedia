# "Pop Off" in English and Chinese
## English
- **Meaning**: To start behaving in an aggressive or boastful way, often in a showy or exaggerated manner.
- **Example**: "As soon as the music started, they decided to pop off and show off their dance moves."
## 中文
- **含义**：指的是开始以具攻击性或夸耀的方式行为，通常表现得很夸耀或夸张。
- **例句**："一听到音乐，他们就开始飚技，pop off 来展示他们的舞技。"
