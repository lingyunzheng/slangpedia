## Drip (English)
**Drip:** A slang term often used to describe someone or something that is considered to be second-rate, mediocre, or unimpressive. It can also refer to situations or individuals who are perceived as not putting in full effort or not being genuine. 
**Example:** "That performance was a real drip; I expected more from him."
## 滴 （中文）
**滴:** 相当于形容某人或某事非常普通、平庸或不令人印象深刻的一种俚语表达。此外，它也可能用来描述那些看起来不够尽力或不够真诚的人或情况。
**例句:** "他的表演真是没劲，我没指望他能做得多好。"
