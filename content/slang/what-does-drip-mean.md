# Drip (汉语：滴答)
## English Explanation
**Drip**: In slang usage, "drip" usually refers to someone who is perceived as ostentatious or showy, often to an annoying or fake degree. They might be flaunting their wealth, fashion sense, or other possessions in a way that borders on being obnoxious. For example, someone buying the latest expensive tech gadgets just to show off or wearing overly excessive jewelry might be called a "drip".
## 汉语解释
**滴答 (dī dā)**：在俚语中，“滴答”通常指的是那些被人们认为炫耀或夸张的人，这种炫耀往往近乎哗众取宠。他们可能在炫耀财富、时尚品味或其他财产时，显得过于张扬或假模假式。比如，购买最新的昂贵科技产品只是为了炫耀，或者佩戴过度奢华的首饰，这类人可能被称为“滴答”（意指炫耀过度之人）。
