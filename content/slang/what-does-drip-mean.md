## Drip (English)
**Definition:** 
A slang term often used to describe someone or something that is perceived as "lit" or in very good spirits, mood, or condition. It can also be used to express admiration or envy towards someone who is doing something exceptionally well or enjoying themselves immensely.
**Example:**
"Wow, that party was a total drip! Everyone was having so much fun and the music was amazing."

“那个聚会真的超级 Drip，每个人都玩得特别开心，音乐也超级棒。”
