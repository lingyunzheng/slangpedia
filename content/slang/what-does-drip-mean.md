# Drip (Chinese: 滴水)
## English
### Meaning
- **To flow slowly and steadily**: Often used to describe someone who consistently performs well or contributes resources over a long period.
### Example
- "He's been a drip, always showing up for meetings and keeping the project on track."
### Usage
- Commonly used in contexts where someone is showing consistent effort or maintaining a steady contribution.
## 中文
### 含义
- **缓慢而稳定地流出**：常用来形容某人在长时期内持续表现出色或稳步贡献资源。
### 例句
- "他一直给项目添砖加瓦，是个可靠的滴水，确保了项目的顺利进行。"
### 使用场景
- 经常用于描述某人在某一领域或任务中持续稳定地付出努力的情况。
