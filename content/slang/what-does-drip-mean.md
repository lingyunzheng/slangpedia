## Drip (English)
**Explanation:** In English slang, "drip" is often used to describe someone who is showy or flashy with their possessions, often in an attempt to display wealth or status. It can also be used to negatively describe a style or behavior that is seen as too obvious or exaggerated.
**Example Use:**
- "He thought getting that brand new sports car was going to make him look cool, but it just made him seem like a drip."
- "Wow, her outfit is dripping with glitter, she really wants to stand out at the party."
## 滴漏 (Dǐlòu, Chinese 赤字)
**Explanation:** In Chinese, "滴漏" (Dǐlòu) is a metaphorical term that translates to "drip" but is primarily used in the financial or economic context to describe a situation where expenses exceed income, leading to a deficit or debt. It can refer to the excess spending or the accumulated debt.
**Example Use:**
- "公司一直在滴漏，最近财务报告显示，他们已经连续亏损了好几个季度了。" (The company has been dripping, recent financial reports show that they have been continuously losing money for several quarters.)
- "我们要控制开支，避免国家滴漏越来越严重。" (We need to control our expenses to avoid the nation’s deficit getting worse.)
