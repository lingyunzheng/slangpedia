## Drip
### English
**Drip**: This term is used to describe someone or something that is excessive, over the top, or overkill. It often refers to a style or behavior that is so intense or lavish that it can be seen as a bit ridiculous. For example, if someone puts on a whole bunch of makeup and accessorizes with lots of shiny jewelry, they might be described as a "bling drip."
### Chinese
**滴 (Dī)**: 这个词用来形容某人或某事是过度的、夸张的或过分的。它通常指某种风格或行为过于强烈或奢华，以至于显得有点荒谬。例如，如果有人化了很多妆并且挂满了闪闪发光的首饰，他们可能被形容为“充满光芒的滴”。
