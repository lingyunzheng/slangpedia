## Drip (English)
### Explanation
"Drip" is a slang term often used in online communities, particularly in gaming and social media contexts. It refers to someone who is perceived to be unrealistically good at something or who is viewed as pretentious due to their excessive display of skill or status. Essentially, a "drip" is someone who is considered snobby or excessive in their display of superiority or talent.
### Example Usage
Example 1: "That guy in the game is such a drip, showing off his high-end gear and bragging about it non-stop."
Example 2: "Don't invite him to the party, he's a total drip obsessed with his achievements."
