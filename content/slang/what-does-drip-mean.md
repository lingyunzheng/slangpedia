## Drip (英: Drip)
### English
**Drip**: A slang term that originated in the hip-hop and internet cultures, traditionally refers to someone who is considered arrogant, attention-seeking, or overall a pain to deal with. It has evolved into a general term used to snipe at someone's behavior or possessions, implying they are showing off or behaving in a way that is obnoxious.
### 中文
**滴ppy (Drip)**：源于说唱文化及互联网中的俚语，最初用来形容那些自视甚高、渴望获得注意或者总体上让人厌烦的人。这一词汇随着时间的发展，变成了一种普遍使用的术语，用来指责某人的行为或拥有物，暗示他们是在炫耀或以一种令人厌烦的方式行事。
