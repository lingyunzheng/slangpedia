## Drip (Chinese: 滴血 / 刺儿头; Pinyin: dī xuè / cì'ér tóu)
**English:** Drip
- **Meaning:** A term often used in hip-hop or street culture to describe someone or something that is perceived as desperate, cheap, or trying too hard to attract attention. Essentially, a "drip" is someone or something that is seen as overly flashy or attention-seeking, often in a negative light.
- **Chinese:** 
- **滴血 (dī xuè)**: Originally refers to dripping blood, but has evolved in popular culture to describe a person or thing that is seen as overly aggressive, attention-seeking, or desperate. This term can be used in a context where someone is showing excessive emotion or trying to create an image of toughness or coolness, but ends up appearing weak or ridiculous.
- **刺儿头 (cì'ér tóu)**: Literally translates to "thornhead," which is used to describe someone who is always getting into trouble, is very brash, or constantly demanding attention. This term often implies a person who is difficult to handle or has a tendency to be confrontational or overly assertive.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "He really is a drip with his overpriced sneakers and fancy watch."
- Chinese: "他浓妆艳抹穿的那些高价运动鞋和名表真是刺儿头，太醒目了。" (Tā nóng zhuāng yàn mǒ chuān de nàxiē gāojīng yùndòng xiān hé míng biǎo zhēn shì cì'ér tóu, tài yǐng bù le.)
