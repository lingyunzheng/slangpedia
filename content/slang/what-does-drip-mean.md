## Drip
### English Slang Explanation:
In English slang, **"drip"** is often used to describe someone or something in a way that carries a negative connotation, usually indicating someone who is pretentious, overconfident, or trying too hard to appear cool or stylish in ways that come off as artificial or fake. 
For example, someone might say, "He's such a drip, always trying to wear the most expensive clothes and pretending they fit his personality." In this context, "drip" suggests someone who is trying too hard and failing to be genuine.
### Chinese Slang Explanation:
在中文俚语中，“drip”这个外来词被用来形容某人或某事带有负面含义，通常指的是那种假装高端时髦或者自认为很酷但实际上显得做作或不真实的一个人。
例如，有人说：“他真是个drip，总是穿着最贵的衣服还硬要显得很符合他的个性。”在这种语境下，“drip”意味着一个人过度尝试呈现某种形象而显得不真诚。
