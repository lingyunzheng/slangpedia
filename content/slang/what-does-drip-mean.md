## Drip - English
**Meaning:** To talk or behave in a way that is showing off or boasting.
**Example:** "Stop dripping about your new car, nobody cares."
## Drip - Chinese
**Meaning:** 自夸，故作炫耀。
**Example:** “别跟我们吹嘘你的新车了，我们才不稀罕听呢。”
