## Drip (英汉俚语)
### English
**Drip**: Originating from the verb "to drip," this slang is used to describe something or someone in a gradual, constant, and often annoying way. It is often used when someone or something is doing something repetitively, and the behavior is seen as mildly irritating or incompetent.
**Example**: She's always *drip* with questions, but no matter how many times you explain, she never really gets it.
### 汉语
**滴（drip）**: 这个俚语来源于动词“滴”。它用来形容某人或某事以一种逐渐的、持续的，有时令人生厌的方式进行。通常用于描述某人或某事重复某个行为时，这种行为让人觉得有些烦人或无能。
**例句**：她总是不停地问问题，不管你怎么解释，她总是没有真正理解，真是太“滴”了。
