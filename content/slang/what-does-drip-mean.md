## Drip (英语)
- **Definition**: Originally comes from the word "drip," meaning something that falls in drops or is delivered slowly. In slang, it often refers to someone or something that is continually problematic, annoying, or exhausting.
- **Example**: "He's such a drip, always causing trouble and not caring about the consequences."
## 滴 （中文）
- **Definition**: “滴”原本是指液体一滴滴落下来，意指缓慢滴落。在网络用语中，它通常用来形容经常带来麻烦、令人厌烦或让人感到疲惫的人或事物。
- **Example**: "他真是个‘滴’，总是制造麻烦还不放在心上。"
