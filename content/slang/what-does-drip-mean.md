# Drip (英汉双解俚语)
## English
**Drip**: This term is used primarily in online communities and gaming contexts. A "drip" often refers to someone or something that is excessive, over the top, or not to be taken seriously. It can also imply that someone is fake or insincere, often with a humorous or mocking connotation.
Example:
- "That guy's dance moves are such a drip."
## 汉语
**滴水（Drip）**: 这个词主要出现在网络社群和游戏环境中。“滴水”通常用来形容某人或某事过度夸张、不值得一提或者不真实。它也可以用来指某人是假的或不真诚的，带有幽默或嘲讽的意味。
示例：
- “那个 guy 的舞蹈动作真是假滴水。”
