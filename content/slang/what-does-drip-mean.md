## Drip
### English
**Drip**: When used as slang, "drip" often refers to someone who is showing off their wealth or possessions in an exaggerated and boastful manner. This could include flashy clothing, expensive cars, or other luxury items. Essentially, it's a way of saying someone is trying too hard to appear wealthy or desirable.
### 中文
**滴血（Dī Xiě）**: 当作俚语使用时，“drip”通常指的是一个人在炫耀他们的财富或财产时表现得过度和夸耀。这可能包括花哨的服装、昂贵的汽车或其他奢侈品。简单来说，就是指某人过于努力地试图显得富有或迷人。
