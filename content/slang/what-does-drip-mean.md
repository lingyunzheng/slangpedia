## Drip (英语: Drip)
### English Explanation
"Drip" is commonly used in internet slang to refer to someone who is overly confident, ghetto, or streetwise. Someone who is seen as a drip is often thought to be exhibiting behavior that is considered gauche or embarrassing by more sophisticated standards. For instance, a drip might wear ill-fitting clothes or make crass jokes that are out of place in formal settings. 
This term is often used in a derogatory manner to mock someone's style, behavior, or mannerisms that are deemed inappropriate or lacking in taste.
### 中文解释
"Drip" 是互联网俚语中用来形容过分自信、粗俗或街头风格的人。被认为是 drip 的人通常被认为表现出的行为在更正式或优雅的场合显得不合时宜或尴尬。例如，一个 drip 可能会穿着不合身的衣服，或者在正式环境中说粗俗的笑话。这个词语通常带有贬义，用来嘲讽某人的风格、行为或举止被认为缺乏品味或不恰当。
