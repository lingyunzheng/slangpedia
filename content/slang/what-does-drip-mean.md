## Drip
### English
**Drip**: Describes someone or something that is excellent or extremely capable. It's often used to compliment someone's style, skills, or achievements.
Example: 
- *He's a total drip when it comes to fashion, but his dance moves are dripping with talent.*
### 中文
**滴血（Dī Xuè）**: 用来形容某人或某事非常出色或极其有能力。通常用于赞美某人的风格、技能或成就。
例句：
- *他在时尚方面是个滴血的人，但他的舞技简直可以说是滴血般的优秀。*
