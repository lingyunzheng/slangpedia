# Drip ( inglés-Chino )
## English
**Drip** - originally a term used to describe a smooth, constant, and confident flow of substances, often in a slang context related to music or hip-hop culture. It has evolved to mean something that is smooth, stylish, and elite. For example, "He's got a drip game with that new car." 
## 中文
**Drip** - 一开始是用来形容一种平滑、连续且自信的流体流动，尤其是在说唱文化或音乐背景下使用。它现在已经引申为表示某人或某事物非常酷、时尚和精英。例如，“他开着新车，显示出了自己的滴流风格。”
### Example Usage
- English: "She's got a drip with that outfit, looks amazing!"
- 中文: “她穿这身衣服真有‘滴流’的范，看起来真漂亮！”
