## Drip (英语)
**Explanation:** In slang, "drip" is often used to describe someone or something that is exceptionally cool, trendy, or has a high level of style. It can also be used to describe someone as being extremely confident or self-assured.
### Example:
- English: "That guy has the latest sneakers, his style is really dripping."
- 中文: "这家伙穿的最新款运动鞋，他的风格真的是超级酷炫。"
## Drip (中文)
**Explanation:** 在网络用语中，“drip”常用来形容某人或某事物非常 cool（酷），很时髦，或者具有很高的品位。也可以用来形容某人非常自信或自以为是。
### 示例：
- 中文：她的穿搭真的超级滴（drip），每次都能吸引大家的目光。
- English: Her outfits are truly dripping, always capturing everyone's attention.
