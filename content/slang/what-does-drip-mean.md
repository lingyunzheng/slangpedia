# Drip (英汉双解俚语)
**English:** Drip  
>- **Definition:** Originally used to describe the sound of water slowly dripping, it has evolved into a slang term used to describe someone or something that is extremely confident, overbearing, or boasting.  
>- **Example Usage:** "That new rapper is such a drip, always showing off his expensive clothes on social media."
**中文:** 滴水（Drip）
>- **释义：** 最初用来形容水滴落的声音，现在演变成了一个俚语，用来描述某人或某物极其自信、强势或自吹自擂。  
>- **用法示例：** “那个新说唱歌手真是个十足的‘滴水’，总是在社交媒体上炫耀自己的名牌衣服。”
