## Drip (English)
**Definition:** A term used to describe an impactful or highly stylish performance in a particular area, often related to fashion, music, or general coolness.
**Example:** "That new pair of sneakers he just got are a total drip, but so stylish!"

**定义:** 用于描述在特定领域（如时尚、音乐或酷感）中表现极具影响力或潮流的一种说法。
**例子:** “他最近刚买的那双运动鞋简直是一个Drip，但是真的很潮！”
