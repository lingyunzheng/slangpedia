```markdown
## Drip (英汉双解俚语)
- **English Explanation:**
- **Meaning:** To leak or dribble, often used to describe money. It can mean spending money very slowly or, more colloquially, spending it in a snobbish or pretentious manner.
- **Example:** "He's a real drip, always using his credit card to buy a $5 cup of coffee."
- **Chinese Explanation:**
- **意思:** 漏或滴，通常用来形容钱。这个词既可以指慢慢地花钱，也可以指以一种好炫耀或装腔作势的方式花钱。
- **例句:** "他真是个银样镴枪头，总是用信用卡买5块钱一杯的咖啡。"
```
Note: This slang term can be rather informal and is best used in casual settings. The example provided aims to illustrate the sense of extravagance in spending.
