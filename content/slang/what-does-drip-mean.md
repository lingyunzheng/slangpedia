## Drip (英语解释)
### 在嘻哈文化中的意思
- **英语解释:** In hip-hop culture, "drip" refers to a style or fashion that emphasizes conspicuous consumption and excess. It can also describe the way someone acts or behaves in a flashy and showy manner, often to impress others.
### 双语解释
- **英语:** In hip-hop culture, "drip" means a style or fashion that highlights excessive spending and grandeur. It can also describe the way someone acts or behaves in a flashy and showy manner, often to impress others.
- **中文:** 在嘻哈文化中，"drip"指的是通过过度消费和奢华来展现的风格或时尚。它也可以用来形容一个人行为上极为华丽、炫耀，通常是为了给别人留下深刻印象。
