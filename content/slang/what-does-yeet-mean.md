## Yeet 🛸🚀
### English
**Yeet**: An enthusiastic, often dismissive or celebratory gesture or action, typically associated with throwing or ejecting something (or someone) with force. It can also be used as a verb to express excitement, approval, or to show that something is undesirable.
*Usage Example:*
"I finally uploaded my project to the server, and it was a total yeet!"
### 中文
**Yeet**: 这个词通常用来表示一种充满激情的、有时是轻蔑或庆祝的动作或行为，常与用力扔或把某人（或某物）抛出联系在一起。它也可以用作动词，表示兴奋、赞同，或者表明某事物是不想要的。
*使用示例:*
“终于把我的项目上传到服务器了，简直是太yeet了！”
