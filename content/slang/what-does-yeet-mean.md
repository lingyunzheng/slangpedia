*Usage:* "I yeeted that dress because it was too small and old."

*例句:* "我直接yeet了这件裙子，因为它又小又旧。"
## Yeet (Noun)
- **English:** Can also be used as a noun, often in online gaming contexts, where it refers to a precise or powerful throw or toss.
*Usage:* "I got an awesome yeet during the last match!"
