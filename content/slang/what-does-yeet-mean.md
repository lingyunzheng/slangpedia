# Yeet
## English
**Definition**: A playful and dismissive toss of an object, used metaphorically to express frustration, rejection, or enthusiasm.
**Usage Example**: "I don't like this movie at all; it's just a big yeet!"
**Context**: Common in internet slang, often used with a GIF of someone throwing an object upside down or a character's displeasure with a situation.
## 中文
**含义**: 一个好玩的、带有玩耍性质的扔东西的动作，常常用来表达了挫败感、拒绝或者兴奋的情绪。
**使用示例**: "我对这部电影一点兴趣也没有，简直是yeet!"
**语境**: 常见于网络用语中，经常伴随着模拟扔东西或表达不满表情包的使用。
