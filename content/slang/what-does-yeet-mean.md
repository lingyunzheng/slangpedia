# Yeet
## English
**Yeet**: A slang term often used to express throwing something, whether literally or metaphorically. It can also be used to express excitement, rejection, or to throw something off, depending on the context.
Example:
- *Literal*: "I yeeted the ball over the fence!"
- *Metaphorical*: "They yeeted the idea out of the meeting."
## 中文
**Yeet**：这是一个俚语，通常用来表达扔东西，无论是字面上的还是比喻意义上的。也可以用来表达激动、拒绝或扔掉某事，具体取决于上下文。
例句：
- *字面意思*： "我把球yeeted过了篱笆！"
- *比喻意思*： "他们在会议中yeeted出那个想法。"
