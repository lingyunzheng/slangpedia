# Yeet (英/entifier/)
#### English:
**Yeet** (verb)  
- **Definition**: A slang term that originated from the video game Realm of the Mad God, commonly used to express a stunning throw or cast of an item or spell. It has since been adopted by various online communities, including social media platforms, to describe an action that is surprisingly effective, impressive, or with a flair of disregard for caution.  
- **Usage Example**: "I finally yeeted that throwable over the wall and got the treasure!"
#### 中文:
**Yeet** (动词)  
- **定义**: 这个词来源于视频游戏《疯狂上帝的领域》(Realm of the Mad God)，通常用来形容一种令人印象深刻的投掷或施法动作。它已广泛被各种在线社区采用，包括社交媒体平台，用来形容一个惊人有效、引人注目或带有不加谨慎意味的动作。
- **用法示例**： "我终于将那个可扔物品yeet过了围墙，拿到了宝藏！"
