## Yeet (English)
### Explanation
"Yeet" originally comes from the video game scene, but it has now become a popular internet slang term. It's commonly used as an exclamation to express excitement, rejection, or to throw something. 
- **Ex. I didn't want that job, yeet!**: I rejected the job offer.
- **Ex. Yeet that banana!**: Throw that banana!
### Origin
The term was popularized on gaming platforms and spread through memes and social media.

## 抛弃 (Pābì) (Chinese)
### 解释
"Yeet" 这个词最初来自电子游戏圈，但现在已经成了一个流行的网络俚语。通常用于表示兴奋、拒绝或扔掉某个东西。
- **例句 不想要这个工作，抛掉它！**: 我拒绝了这份工作。
- **例句 抛掉那个香蕉！**: 扔掉那个香蕉！
### 起源
这个词在游戏平台上传播开来，并通过各种网络流行内容进一步扩散。
