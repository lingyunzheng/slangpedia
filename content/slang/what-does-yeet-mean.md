## Yeet
### English
**Meaning:** 
`Yeet` is often used as an enthusiastic or dismissive response, similar to saying "throw it out," "discard," or "reject." It can also be used in a more positive context, like exclaiming approval or excitement.
**Example:**
- "I don't want this dress, yeet!"
### Chinese
**意思:** 
`yeet` 形容一种热情或轻蔑的反应，类似于说“扔掉”、“扔出去”或“拒绝”。它也可以用于更积极的语境中，比如表示赞同或兴奋。
**例句:**
- 我不想要这条连衣裙，yeet！
