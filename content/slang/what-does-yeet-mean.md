## Yeet
**English:** 
- *Verb:* Derived from video gaming and popularized by social media, "yeet" is used to express a wide range of emotions, from excitement to pride. It's often used when throwing something, making a strong pass, or just to say goodbye in a breezy, dismissive manner.
- *Example Usage:* "I just got that job! Yeet!"
**中文:**
- *动词:* "yeet" 这个词源自于电子游戏，并且在社交媒体上广泛流行。它用来表达各种情绪，从兴奋到自豪。通常在扔东西、做出强力传球或者只要在轻松或轻蔑的语气中说再见时使用。
- *使用示例:* “我刚刚得到了那份工作！Yeet！”
