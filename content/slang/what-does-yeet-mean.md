## Yeet 🏀💨
### English
**Meaning:**  
1. To throw something away or reject something with force.  
2. In gaming, it can be used as an animated gesture to get an opponent out of the game.
**Example Sentence:**  
I just got("."). I'm yeeting this project and moving on to the next one.
### 中文
**含义：**
1. 用力扔掉或拒绝某物。  
2. 在游戏中，可以用来表示将对手击退或淘汰。
**例句：**  
我决定了，把这个项目甩一边，开始新的计划。
