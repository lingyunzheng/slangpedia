# Yeet
## English
**Definition:** A slang term often used to express rejection, dismissal, or throwing something away. It can also be used to express excitement or enthusiasm.
**Example:** "I've been dating her for months, but now I think it's time to yeet her out of my life."
## 中文
**释义:** “yeet” 是一种俚语，常用来表示拒绝、否定或丢弃某物。它也可以用来表达兴奋或热忱。
**例句:** "我跟她交往了几个月，但现在我觉得是时候把她从我的生活里‘yeet’出去了。"
