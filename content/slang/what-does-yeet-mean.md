### Yeet (英语/Chinese)
**English:**
- **Definition:** "Yeet" is an internet slang term that originated from video game communities but has spread to casual internet usage. The term is used to express excitement, approval, or to throw something with force.
- **Example Usage:** "I just yeeted that ball so far!"
**中文:**
- **释义："Yeet"** 是一个起源于电子游戏社区的网络用语，后来被广泛用于日常网络交流中。这个词语用来表达兴奋、赞同，或是用力将某物扔出。
- **使用示例：** "我刚刚用力扔出了那个球！"
