## Yeet
### English
**Definition:** A casual term often used to express dismissal, rejection, or to throw something away. It can also be used as an enthusiastic endorsement or to express extreme dislike, depending on the context.
**Example:** "I totally yeeted that idea. It didn't work at all!"
### 中文
**释义：** 这个词通常用于表达拒绝、抛弃，或者扔掉某物。根据语境不同，它也可以表示强烈的赞同或者极端的不喜欢。
**例句：** “我完全yeeted那个想法。完全没用！”
