```markdown
# Yeet (noun, verb)
- **English:** A slang term that can be used to express rejection, forceful throwing, or dismissing something. It’s often used in a playful or energetic way.
- Example Usage: "I just yeeted that bad idea into the trash!"
- **Chinese (简体中文):** 一个网络用语，可以用来表达拒绝、用力扔掉或抛弃某物。通常使用时带有轻松或充满活力的态度。
- 示例用法： "我刚把那个糟糕的想法给yeet了，直接扔进了垃圾桶！"
```
