## Yeet (名词或动词)
**English Explanation:**
"Yeet" is an internet slang term that originated from online gaming communities, particularly on platforms like Twitch and Discord. It can be used as a noun or a verb. When used as a verb, it is often associated with a sudden, forceful, and decisive action, such as throwing something with power and force. More broadly, it can convey a feeling of satisfaction or triumphant dismissal, similar to "smash" or "boot."
**Chinese Explanation:**
"Yeet" 是一个起源于在线游戏社区（特别是在 Twitch 和 Discord 上）的网络用语。它既可以作为名词也可以作为动词使用。当用作动词时，它通常与一种有力而突然的动作相关联，类似于用力扔东西。更广泛地说，它可以传达一种满意或胜利驳斥的感觉，类似于“粉碎”或“踢出”。
