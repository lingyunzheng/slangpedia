## Yeet: 抡掉 (Lūn Diào)
### English Explanation:
**Yeet** is a slang term that originated from online gaming culture, particularly in Rocket League, where players would often say "Yeet" when they made a successful pass or hit the ball. Over time, it has been adopted more broadly to express enthusiasm, approval, or effectively "pitch" something away. Usage examples: "I just got a new phone, yeet this old one!" or "That's some serious talent, yeet!"
### Chinese Explanation:
**YEET** (抡掉) 是源自于网络游戏文化的一个俚语，最初出现在《火箭联盟》中，玩家在成功传球或击中球时经常说“YEET”。随着时间的推移，它被更广泛地用来表达热情、赞成，或者轻蔑地“扔掉”某物。使用示例：“我刚买了一个新手机，YEET掉这个旧的！”或“你的天分真是太厉害了，YEET一个！”
