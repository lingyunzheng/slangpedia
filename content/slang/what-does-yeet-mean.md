# Yeet
## English
**Meaning:** 
- To throw something away or discard it.
- To reject someone or something in a forceful, dismissive manner.
- In modern context, often used as a playful or enthusiastic way to indicate rejecting or getting rid of something.
**Example:**
- "I yeeeted that old phone because it was outdated."
## 中文
**意思:** 
- 扔掉或抛弃某物。
- 拒绝某人或某事物，带有强烈的否定和排斥意味。
- 在现代语境中，常常用于表达一种玩趣的、积极的方式拒绝或放弃某物。
**例句:**
- "我yeeeted那部旧手机因为它已经过时了。"
