# Yeet (中英双语 slang explanation)
**English:**  
- *Cliché:* To throw something.  
- *Casual:* Used to express rejection or to get rid of something or someone. For example, "Yeet that old phone; it's time for a new one!"
**Chinese:**  
- *直译:* 扔掉某物。  
- *口语:* 用来表达拒绝或抛弃某物或某人。例如，“把那部老手机yeet掉吧，是时候换新的了！”
---
You might hear someone use "yeet" in a sentence like:
- "I totally yeeted that bad idea!"
or
- "She yeeted him when he started flirting with her sister."
In modern internet culture, "yeet" has evolved into an expression of rejection or dismissal, often used in a digital context.
