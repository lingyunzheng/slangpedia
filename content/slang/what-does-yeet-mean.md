## Yeet (俚语解释)
### 中文
“Yeet” 最初来源于网络和电子游戏社区，现在广泛用于表达一种强烈的抛掷动作或是彻底丢弃某事物的动作。还可以用来表示极度不喜欢某人或某事物，并迅速摆脱或放弃。
### 英文
"Yeet" originated from online and gaming communities, and it's now commonly used to express a strong throwing action or a complete disposal of something. It can also be used to indicate an extreme dislike for someone or something and a swift act of getting rid of them.
