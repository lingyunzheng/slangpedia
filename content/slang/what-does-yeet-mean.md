## Yeet
**English:** 
A slang term often used to express a forceful action, typically a throw, but can be applied to any action done with vigor and enthusiasm. It can also be used to convey rejection or to express extreme disagreement.
- Example: "I just yeeted that pizza out of the window because I was so full."
- Example: "She yeeted out of the room when she heard the news."
**汉语:**
“yeet”是一个网络俚语，常用来表示一种强有力的行动，通常是投掷，但也可以应用于任何充满活力和热情的动作。它还可以用来表示拒绝或表达极端的不认同。
- 例子：“我吃了太撑了，就把披萨yeeted出窗外了。”
- 例子：“听到那个消息，她yeeted出了房间。”
