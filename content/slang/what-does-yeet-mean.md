# Yeet (英语: Yeet)
## English Explanation
**Yeet** is a slang term often used to express a sense of throwing or rejection. It can also be used as an energetic exclamation to signify a choice, an action, or to express excitement or dismissal. The term has gained significant popularity, particularly on social media platforms.
### Example Usage
- "I'm done with this job, yeet!"
- "She's not my type, yeet me out of here!"
## 中文解释
**Yeet** 是一个网络俚语，用来表达一种抛开或拒绝的感觉。它也可以作为一种充满活力的惊叹表达选择、动作，或者表达激动或拒绝的意思。这个词在社交媒体上特别流行。
### 使用示例
- "我已经不干这份工作了，yeet！"
- "她不是我的类型，yeet我走吧！"
