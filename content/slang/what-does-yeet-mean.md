# Yeet
## English
**Meaning**: To throw something, often metaphorically, away or out. It can also be used to express excitement, rejection, or disapproval. 
**Example**: "I just yeeted that failed test and never looked back!"
## Chinese
**意思**: 用来扔东西，有时字面意义上，也可以用来表达兴奋、拒绝或不赞同。 
**例句**: "我刚把那只失败的考试给yeet了，绝口不提它！"
