## Yeet (英语) / 凶车 (中文)
### English Explanation
**Yeet**: Originating from the online gaming community, "yeet" is used to denote a sudden, forceful, or energetic action, often in the context of throwing or launching something. It has evolved to be a casual term used to express strong emotion, approval, or excitement.
### 中文解释
**凶车 (Xiong Che)**: 起源于网络游戏圈，“yeet”用于表示一个突然的、有力量的或充满能量的动作，通常是在扔或发射某物的情境下使用。这个词进一步被用来表达强烈的情感、赞同或兴奋。
