### looksmaxxing
**English:**  
"Looksmaxxing" is a slang term commonly used to describe something that is extremely appealing or beautiful, often to the point of being overly attractive. It can be used both literally (referring to physical appearance) and figuratively (referring to other forms of attractiveness such as objects, ideas, or experiences). It's a playful way of highlighting maxed-out attractiveness or appeal.
