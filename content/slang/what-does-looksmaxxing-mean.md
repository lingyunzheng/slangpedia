# "Looksmaxxing" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English:
**Definition:** The behavior or practice of constantly displaying one's appearance or physical features in a way that emphasizes how attractive or fashionable one is.
**Context:** Often used in online or social media contexts, especially among teenagers and young adults who aim to gain attention or approval through visuals.
**Example:**
- "I spent the whole day looksmaxxing for the school photo day."
## 中文:
**定义:** 在展示自己的外貌或体态时，不断突出自己有多么迷人或时尚的行为或做法。
**应用场景:** 经常在在线或社交媒体环境中使用，特别是在青少年和年轻人中，他们希望通过视觉效果吸引关注或获得认可。
**例句:**
- “为了班级的毕业照，我特地打扮了一整天进行looksmaxxing。”
