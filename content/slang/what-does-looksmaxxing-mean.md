## looksmaxxing
### Chinese
**含义**: "看起来是最大值"  
**解释**: 用来形容一个人外貌非常出色，美得让人感觉仿佛是屏幕上的最大值、巅峰之作。通常用于赞美那些长相出众的人。  
**使用场景**: 好友聚会时，当有人问起某人的外貌，你可以用这个词来赞美那个人。
### English
**Meaning**: "looksmaxxing" implies that someone is extremely handsome or beautiful, so stunning that it seems like the peak display or pinnacle. It's commonly used to praise individuals who have outstanding appearances.  
**Usage Examples**: When you're hanging out with friends and someone asks about another person's looks, you can use this term to express how stunning they are.
