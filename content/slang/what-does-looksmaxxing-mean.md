**looksmaxxing**: A term often used in social media and internet culture to describe someone or something that is extremely attractive, especially in a way that makes others feel envious or appreciative of their appearance. It's derived from the concept of making something (or in this case, looking) extremely maxed out—meaning it's at the peak or maximum level of attractiveness.
