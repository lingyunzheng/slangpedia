# looksmaxxing
## English
**looksmaxxing:** A term used to describe someone who excessively makes an effort to look attractive or to purposely display their aesthetic appeal in an over-the-top manner. It might imply that the person is trying too hard to attract attention due to insecurities or an attempt to compensate for other shortcomings.
Example: "She's been looksmaxxing all night, trying to outshine everyone at the party."
