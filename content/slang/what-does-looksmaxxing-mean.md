## looksmaxxing (中文：看起来很max)
**English Explanation:** 
- **Definition:** Using extensive makeup and styling to look as beautiful or attractive as possible.
- **Usage Example:** "She went to the party looking maxxing, with every detail perfect."
- **Etymology:** A portmanteau of "max" (maximal) and "xing" (look in Cantonese).
**中文解释:**
- **定义:** 通过使用大量的化妆和造型来让自己看起来尽可能美丽或有吸引力。
- **使用示例:** "她去参加派对时看起来很maxxing，每一个细节都完美无瑕。"
- **词源:** “maxxing” 是从“max”（最大化）和粤语的“xing”（在“look”中发音）结合而来。
