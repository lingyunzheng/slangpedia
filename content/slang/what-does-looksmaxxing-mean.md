# looksmaxxing
## English Explanation
**looksmaxxing**: This term is used in social media and online communities to describe someone who is excessively cute or attractive, often to the point of being eye-catching or even distracting. It's a playful way to highlight someone's good looks, sometimes bordering on the humorous or campy.
