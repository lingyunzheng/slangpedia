## "looksmaxxing" (英汉双解俚语)
#### English Explanation:
"Looksmaxxing" is a playful or humorous term used to describe someone who is extremely vain about their appearance, especially when they are trying to attract attention through their looks. It often implies a focus on physical attractiveness to the point of being overbearing or obsessive.
#### 汉语解释:
"looksmaxxing" 表示一个人对自己的外表极其自恋，尤其是在通过外貌吸引注意力时。这个词通常暗示了对外表的高度关注，以至于到了令人感到烦腻或痴迷的程度。
