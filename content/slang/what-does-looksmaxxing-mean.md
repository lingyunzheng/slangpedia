## 英文解释：Looksmaxxing
- **Definition**: The verb "looksmaxxing" is derived from the combination of "looks" and the suffix "-maxxing" (a portmanteau of "maxing"), meaning a behavior or action aimed at enhancing one's appearance or attractiveness. 
- **Example**: "She's been looksmaxxing all week, trying on multiple outfits and applying the most expensive makeup to appear perfect."
## 中文解释：looksmaxxing

- **例句**："她这一周都在looksmaxxing，试了好几种不同的妆容和服装，力图让自己看起来完美无缺。"
