## looksmaxxing (英语: looksmaxxing)
### Chinese Explanation
“looksmaxxing” 是一种新兴的网络词汇，用于形容一个人过度注重外表，并且总是努力让自己看起来尽可能的好看。这个词源自“looks”（外观，长相）和“maxxing”（最大化，使达到极限）。用来形容在社交媒体或现实生活中，为了保持外表的完美，不惜花费大量时间和金钱的人。
### English Explanation
"looksmaxxing" is a newly emerging internet slang term used to describe someone who excessively focuses on their appearance and constantly strives to look as good as possible. It's derived from "looks" (appearance, looks) and "maxxing" (maximizing, pushing to the limit). This term is often used to characterize individuals who spend a lot of time and money to maintain their perfect appearance, whether on social media or in real life.
