### looksmaxxing
**English:**  
A term often used in online communities and social media circles to describe someone or something that is exceptionally aesthetically pleasing or has extremely attractive qualities, to the point where it can make others feel inadequate or "maxxed out" in terms of beauty.
**中文:**  
一个常用于线上社区和社交媒体中用来形容外观极其出众或拥有极其吸引人的特质的人或事物的词。这种描述可能会让 others 感到自愧弗如，有种被“最大极限”级的美貌碾压的感觉。
