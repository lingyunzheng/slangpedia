# looksmaxxing
## English
**looksmaxxing**: This is a slang term often used to describe someone who is trying extremely hard to appear attractive or puts an excessive amount of effort into their physical appearance. It can sometimes have a negative connotation, implying that the person might be overly focused on their looks to the exclusion of other important aspects of themselves or their personality.
## 中文
**looksmaxxing**: 这个网络俚语通常用来形容某人非常努力地试图显得有吸引力，或者极尽所能地打理自己的外貌。这个词有时带有负面含义，暗示这个人在关注自己的外表上花费了过多的努力，而忽视了其他重要的方面或是自己的个性。
