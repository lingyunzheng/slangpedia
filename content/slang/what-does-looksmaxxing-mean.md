# Slang Explanation: looksmaxxing
## English
**"Looksmaxxing"** is a blend of "looks" and "maxxing," derived from the term "maxxing out," which means to reach the highest or maximum level in something. In the context of social media and dating apps, looksmaxxing refers to someone (typically a man) putting an excessive or high amount of effort into making themselves appear as physically attractive as possible. This can involve spending a lot of time on grooming, strategically posting photos, or using image-enhancing techniques to present an idealized image of themselves.
### Example
- She’s always looksmaxxing for her photo upload; even the slightest imperfection is digitally excised.
## Chinese
**"looksmaxxing"** 这个词融合了“looks”（外貌）和“maxxing”（最大化），后者通常指的是达到最大或最高水平。在社交媒体和约会应用中，“looksmaxxing”指的是某人（通常是男性）花大量的时间努力让自己看起来尽可能地帅气和有吸引力。这可能包括精心打理外表、精心挑选照片，或使用图像美化技术来呈现出理想化的自画像。
### 示例
- 他总是精心打扮以上传照片；照片里几乎没有任何瑕疵被保留。
