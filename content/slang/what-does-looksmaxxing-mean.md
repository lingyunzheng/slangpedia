## English: Looksmaxxing
**Definition**: A playful way to describe something or someone that is incredibly visually appealing, often to the point of being overly attractive. It's like saying something is visually perfect or top-notch.
**Example**: "Her outfit is looksmaxxing today, really makes her stand out."
## 中文: Looksmaxxing
**定义**: 一种玩味的方式来描述某事物或某人极其吸引眼球, 有时甚至到了令人觉得过分吸引的程度。就像是在说某事物或某人视觉上非常完美或者顶级。
**例句**: “她今天的打扮真是looksmaxxing，真的让她特别显眼。”
