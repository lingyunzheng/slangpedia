# Looksmaxxing
## English
**Looksmaxxing**: This term is a mix of "look" and "maxxing," which means something or someone is extremely attractive or eye-catching. It's often used to describe appearances, usually in a way that emphasizes a high level of attractiveness. For example, "Her outfit is totally looksmaxxing!"
## 中文
**Looksmaxxing**：这个词是由“look”（看）和“maxxing”（最大化）拼接而成，指的是某人或某物非常吸引人或引人注目。通常用来形容外观，尤其是在强调极高吸引力的情况下。例如，“她的 outfit 真的超级 looksmaxxing！”
