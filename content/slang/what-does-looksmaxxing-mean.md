# looksmaxxing
## English
**looksmaxxing** - (From "lookкамень" + "maxxing") This term is a playful way to describe someone or something that is extremely attractive, often to the point of being brutally perfect. It's like saying someone or something is so stunning, they are "lookmaxed" out.
## Chinese
**looksmaxxing** - (从 "look石头" + "maxxing" 来创造) 这个词是一个有趣的方式来描述某人或某物极其吸引人，通常是完美到极致的程度。就像说某人或某物非常迷人，以至于他们已经被“lookmax”了。
---
In both English and Chinese, "looksmaxxing" is meant to exaggerate the level of attractiveness in a humorous or exuberant manner.
