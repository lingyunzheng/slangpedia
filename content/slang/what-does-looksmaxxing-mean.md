# looksmaxxing (外观优化至极)
### English Explanation
"Looksmaxxing" is a combination of "look" and "maxxing," derived from the verb "maxxing" which is used to describe the act of making something as good or perfect as possible. "Looksmaxxing" specifically refers to the excessive or hyperbolized emphasis on making something extremely attractive or beautiful, often to an exaggerated or unrealistic degree.
### Chinese Explanation
"looksmaxxing" 是将 "look"（外观）和 "maxxing"（使达到最大或最好）两个词结合而成的新兴网络用语。它特指对某物极度美化或使其非常吸引人的行为，经常达到夸张或不现实的程度，强调视觉上的极致效果。
Examples:
- She spends hours on looksmaxxing her Instagram profile.
- That new phone design is clearly looksmaxxing the competition.
