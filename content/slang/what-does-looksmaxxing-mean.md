# Looksmaxxing
## Chinese (简体中文): 
“ Looksmaxxing ”是一种新兴的网络用语，源于“look maxxing”(看最大化)，用来调侃或形容某人或某事物在视觉上极为吸引人，达到极致的程度。这个词通常用于社交媒体或聊天时，当某个人的照片、外貌或其他视觉呈现非常出众，超越了平均水平，达到让人惊叹的水平时，就会使用这个词。
**例句：**
- 哇塞，她的妆容真是Looksmaxxing，太美了！
## English:
“Looksmaxxing” is a newly emerging internet slang, derived from "look maxxing" (look maximized), used to ridicule or describe someone or something as visually stunning to an extreme degree. This term is commonly used on social media or in chats when someone's appearance, makeup, or other visual presentation is exceptionally impressive, surpassing the average level and leaving people in awe.
**Example sentence:**
- Wow, her makeup is really Looksmaxxing, so beautiful!
