```markdown
# looksmaxxing
## Chinese (简体中文)
**译义：** 好看极了，强调某人的外貌极其出众。
**例句：**
- 她今天穿的裙子简直了，真的是 looksmaxxing！
## English
**Translation:** Super cute! Emphasizes that someone's appearance is exceptionally attractive.
**Example Sentences:**
- She looks absolutely stunning in that dress, it's looksmaxxing!
```
In both Chinese and English, "looksmaxxing" is used to describe someone or something that is extremely attractive or appealing, with a slight emphasis on the visual appearance.
