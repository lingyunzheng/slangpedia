# looksmaxxing
## English
**looksmaxxing**: This term originated from the combination of "looks" and "maxxing." It refers to something or someone that is exceptionally attractive or beautiful to the point where it may be considered excessive or over the top. It's often used to describe visuals that are so stunning they might max out one's ability to appreciate their beauty further.
### Example
- "The sunset at the beach was looksmaxxing, I didn't know color could be used like that in nature!"
## 中文
**looksmaxxing**：这个词来自于“looks”（颜值）和“maxxing”（最大化的）的组合。它用来形容极其吸引人或美丽的事物，到一定程度就显得过于夸张或不切实际。通常用来形容视觉效果极其惊人的场景，美到让人难以继续欣赏其美丽。
### 示例
- "日落时分的海滩景色简直looksmaxxing，我都不知道自然界的色彩能美到这种程度！"
