# Slang Term: looksmaxxing
## English Explanation
- **Definition:** This term is used, primarily among young online communities, to describe someone who puts an extreme amount of effort into looking their best, often to impress others. This can include everything from grooming and fashion to makeup and self-presentation.
- **Usage Example:** "She's been looksmaxxing all week for the big party, trying on like a dozen dresses!"
## Chinese Explanation
- **定义:** 这个术语通常在年轻人的网络社群中使用，用来形容一个人非常努力地显得自己最漂亮，通常是想给他人留下深刻印象。这包括但不限于从打扮到化妆再到自我展示的各种细节。
- **使用示例:** "为了那个大派对，她这几天一直looksmaxxing，试了好几个礼服！"
