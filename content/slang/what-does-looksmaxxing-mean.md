# "looksmaxxing" 英文与中文释义
**English Explanation:**
- **looksmaxxing:** When something or someone is so incredibly beautiful or attractive that it almost reaches a level of ideal perfection. It's often used to describe people, places, or things that surpass ordinary standards of beauty or appeal.
