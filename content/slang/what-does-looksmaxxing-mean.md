## "Looksmaxxing" in English
**Definition:**
"Looksmaxxing" is a combination of "looks" and "maxxing," meaning to make one's appearance or attractiveness extraordinarily appealing. It's often used in contexts where someone is trying exceptionally hard or going to extreme lengths to look good, typically to impress others.
**Example:**
She was looksmaxxing for the big party, wearing her most expensive outfit and doing her hair and makeup meticulously.
