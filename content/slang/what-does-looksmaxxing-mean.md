## looksmaxxing (英语: looksmaxxing)
**含义**：
- **中文**: 看上去非常有魅力、非常帅气或漂亮的。这个词通常用于形容一个人的外貌非常吸引人，尤其是在社交媒体上展示自己的外貌时。
- **English**: Refers to someone who looks extremely attractive or handsome. It is often used to describe someone who has a very attractive appearance, especially when showcasing it on social media.
**示例**：
- **中文**: 小张最近出镜率很高，每次晒照片都能 looksmaxxing 到朋友。
- **English**: Zhang Xiaozhao has been very visible lately, and every time he posts pictures, he looksmaxxes his friends.
