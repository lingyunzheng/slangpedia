**looksmaxxing**: When someone is intentionally or excessively beautifying or enhancing their appearance to an extreme degree, often to attract attention or attention from others. Example: "She's completely looksmaxxing tonight with her new hair color and hues of makeup—that's one way to grab everyone's attention!"
