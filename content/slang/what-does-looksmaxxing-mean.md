## looksmaxxing (英语: looksmaxxing)
### 中文解释
looksmaxxing，意指某人的外貌到了极致的程度，超出了常人的想象，简直美得不真实，通常用于赞美一个颜值非常高的人。
### 英文解释
looksmaxxing refers to someone's appearance being exceptionally gorgeous or beautiful, to the point where it seems almost unreal. It is often used to express admiration for someone with extremely high attractiveness.
```
### 使用示例
- **中文示例：**
- 她实在是太好看啦，完全就是个looksmaxxing的存在！
- **英文示例：**
- She's so gorgeous, she's basically a looksmaxxing phenomenon!
