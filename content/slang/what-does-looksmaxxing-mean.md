# "Looksmaxxing" (外观最大化)
## Chinese (中文解释)
- **定义**: "Looksmaxxing" 是“追求极致颜值”的一种表现形式。即将一个人的外表、造型打扮得极致美丽或帅气。
- **情境使用**: 在社交媒体和娱乐讨论中，当粉丝或观众对某人的外貌称赞或讨论其美容技巧时，就可能用到这个词。
- **例子**:  
- 在微博上，谈到一位明星的装扮，粉丝可能会说：“这位明星今天真是 looksmaxxing，美得不像话！”
- 观众点评网络红人的视频造型时，可能会评论：“她今天的 looksmaxxing 拿手好戏，真是每个角度都好看！”
## English (English Explanation)
- **Definition**: "Looksmaxxing" refers to the practice of making someone's appearance as gorgeous or handsome as possible. It involves styling and dressing to achieve the maximum level of attractiveness.
- **Usage Context**: This term is commonly used in social media and entertainment discussions to describe someone who is giving their best in terms of appearance, often with an emphasis on beauty or attractiveness.
- **Examples**:
- On Weibo, fans might comment, "This celebrity looks looksmaxxing today, it's amazing!"
- When viewers discuss a beauty influencer's video, they might say, "Her looksmaxxing skills today are on point, every angle is stunning!"
