# "Looksmaxxing" 
## English
- **Definition**: Describing someone who puts an extremely high emphasis on physical appearance or looks to the point of being overly concerned or showcasing their appearance in every possible way.
- **Usage**: Used to describe individuals who are preoccupied with enhancing or displaying their attractiveness to others, often for social media or personal attention.
