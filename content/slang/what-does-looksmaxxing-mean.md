# Looksmaxxing
## English Explanation
"Looksmaxxing" is a slang term derived from "looksmashing," which describes someone who is so attractive that they could literally make anyone look bad in comparison. It's used to describe an exceptionally good-looking person who could potentially "max out" the attractiveness scale, meaning no one would be able to surpass their level of cuteness, handsomeness, or overall aesthetic appeal.
## Chinese Explanation
"Looksmaxxing" 是一个从“looksmashing”演变而来的网络流行词，用来形容极其帅气或漂亮的人，以至于他们让周围的人都相形见绌。用通俗的话来说，就是这个人的颜值超高，以至于别人看起来都黯然失色，仿佛达到了“颜值极限”，简直无人能敌。
