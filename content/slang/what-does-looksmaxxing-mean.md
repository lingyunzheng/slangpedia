## looksmaxxing
### English
**"looksmaxxing"** is a slang term that combines "look" and "maxxing out." It refers to someone who tries to look their absolute best, often with a heavy emphasis on fashion, makeup, or grooming, or posting impeccably styled photos. It's commonly used in online contexts, especially on social media, to describe someone who is overly concerned with their appearance or who puts extreme effort into it.
### Chinese
**"looksmaxxing"** 这个网络用语将“look”和“maxxing out”结合在一起，指的是试图把自己打扮得尽可能漂亮，通常强调时尚、化妆或整容，或是发布看起来非常得体的照片。这个词汇常常用于在线场合，尤其是社交媒体中，用来形容那些过于关注外表或是极度注重打扮的人。
