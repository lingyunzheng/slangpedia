## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Maxxing
**English:**  
- To push yourself to the absolute limit, often in terms of physical or mental exertion. It can also refer to achieving the highest level or maximum capacity of something.
**Chinese:**  
- 磨xing (mó xìng) - 意味着将自己的身体或精神推向极限，通常指的是在体能或精神上进行最大强度的锻炼或挑战。它也可以指达到某事的最大限度或最高容量。
例句：
- English: She's been maxxing herself out at the gym every day.
- Chinese: 她每天都在健身房把自己练得筋疲力尽。
- English: We maxxed out the server processing speed by running the most intensive operations.
- Chinese: 我们通过运行最密集的操作将服务器的处理速度推到了极限。
