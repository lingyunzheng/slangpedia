## Maxxing
### English
**Meaning**: To push something or someone to their absolute limits, often in a way that is intense or extreme. It can refer to physical, emotional, or psychological pushes beyond their usual capacity.
**Example**: "He maxxed himself out at the gym by doing three extra sets of his usual routine."
### 中文（中文解释）
**含义**：这个词用来表示将某事或某人推到极限，通常是极端或强烈的程度。它可以指物理上、情感上或心理上超出了他们的通常能力。
**例句**："他通过多做三组平时的训练，在健身房把自己逼到了极限。"
