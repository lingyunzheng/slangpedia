# Maxxing (英语-中文)
## English Explanation
"Maxxing" is a slang term often used to describe the performance or efficiency of a machine or system being pushed to its absolute极限, or maximum极限. It's similar to saying it's running at optimal performance.
## Chinese Explanation
"Maxxing" 是一个俚语，用来形容机器或系统被推到极限状态，或说是运行在最优性能上。这相当于说它运行在最佳状态。
例句:
- English: Maxxing the server means running it at its absolute peak performance.
- Chinese:Maxxing服务器意味着让它运行在其最佳性能状态。
