```markdown
## Maxxing
**English:**  
Maxxing out, often used in the context of credit cards or personal finances, means spending or using all of one's available credit. It can also be used more generally to describe pushing something to its absolute limit.
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
"maxxing" 通常用来形容透支信用卡或者个人财务，意味着使用了所有的可用信用额度。也可以更广泛地使用来表示把某事推到极限。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "He maxxed out his credit card after buying a new car."
- Chinese (Simplified): 他买了一辆新车之后把信用卡透支了。
```
```markdown
## Maxxing
**English:**  
To get/max everything out of something means to use or exploit it to its fullest potential. It can describe someone who is trying to get the maximum benefit or enjoyment from an experience or resource.
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
“maxxing” 指的是将某样东西发挥到极限，或者尽可能多地利用它。这可以用来描述一个人试图从经历或资源中获得最大的利益或乐趣。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "She's always trying to max everything in her life out for the best possible outcomes."
- Chinese (Simplified): 她总是在生活中试图充分利用一切，以获得最好的结果。
```
