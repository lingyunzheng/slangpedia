## Maxxing (English)
**Definition:** To push something or someone to the absolute maximum capacity or limit, often in a forceful or intense way.
**Example:** "He really maxxed out the car's performance by tuning up the engine and adding an upgraded chip."
## 赛车加速到极限 (Chinese)
**定义:** 把某样东西或某人推到绝对的最大容量或极限，通常是以一种有力或强烈的方式进行。
**示例:** "他通过调校引擎和增加升级芯片，把车的动力发挥到了极致。"
