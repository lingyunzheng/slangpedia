# Maxxing
## English
**Maxxing:** Pushing something or someone to the absolute limit, often in a stressful or extreme way. When someone is maxxing something, they are going all out to achieve the maximum possible result or experience.
*Example:* "They maxxed the party with crazy decorations and music all night long."
## Pinyin
**Maxxing:** Chūnghóng (折腾)
*Chūnghóng* literally means 'messing around' or 'wasting time' but is used similarly to "maxxing" to describe going to extremes, especially in causing trouble or provoking a reaction.
## Chinese
**maxxing**：将某事或某人推到极限，通常是在压力极大的情况下或者极端方式下。当有人在最大化某事物或某件事时，他们用尽全力以获得最大程度的结果或体验。
*例句*：“他们maxxing派对，用疯狂的装饰和音乐狂欢一整夜。”
