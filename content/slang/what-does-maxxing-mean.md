# Maxxing
## English
**Meaning:** To push something or someone to their absolute极限.
**Example:** "She maxxed out her credit card during the sale."
## 简体中文
**意思:** 尽力做到极限，通常指推进某事或某人达到极致。
**例子:** "她在促销活动期间透支了信用卡。"
