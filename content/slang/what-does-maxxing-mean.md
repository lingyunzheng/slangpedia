# Maxxing
## English
**maxxing**: To perform at the peak or best level possible, often in a context that involves pushing oneself to the utmost limits or boundaries. It can be used in various scenarios like fitness, gaming, or any situation where one is striving to make the most out of their abilities or resources.
**Example**: "She maxxed out her strength by lifting 100 pounds in the world's heaviest banana lifting competition."
## 中文
**maxxing**：指尽全力或发挥出最佳水平，通常用于表达在某个领域或情境中将个人的极限推向极致的情况。这个词可以应用于健身、游戏或任何试图充分利用自身能力或资源的场合。
**例句**：“她通过举起100磅的最重香蕉，将力量发挥到了极致，赢得了世界最重香蕉举重比赛。”
