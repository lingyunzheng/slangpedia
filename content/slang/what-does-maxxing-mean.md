# Slang Explanation: maxxing
## English
**maxxing**  
- **Meaning**: To push something or someone to the absolute limit; to achieve the highest level of performance or to consume something to excess.
- **Example**: "He maxxed out his energy on that Friday night, going to so many parties and staying up all night!"
## Chinese
**maxxing**  
- **意思**: 尽力把某事或某人推到极限；达到最高的性能水平或过度消费某物。
- **例句**: “他那个周五晚上 maxxed out 了能量，去了很多聚会，熬夜到很晚。”
---
这个词常用于描述某人竭尽全力做事或过度享受某种活动。与汉语中的“拼尽全力”或“玩到尽兴”类似。
