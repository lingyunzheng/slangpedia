# Maxxing (中文俚語解释)
**English:** Maxxing out  
**中文:** 吃老本（吃老本）
**Meaning:**
- **English:** When someone spends beyond their means, typically using credit cards and maxing out their limits.  
- 例如：He maxxed out his credit card because he didn't save enough for the big purchase.
- **Chinese (俚语):** 用尽家底，也就是用完了家里所有的积蓄。也可以比喻耗尽了某个资源或优势。  
- 例如：她这次比赛表现得这么好，真是吃老本吃出了真本事。
**Usage:**
- **English:** If you're maxxing out your credit cards, you're spending more than you can realistically pay back, which can lead to debt and financial troubles.  
- 英文例句：If you're maxxing out your credit cards, you're basically using up all your available credit and could be headed for financial trouble.
- **Chinese (俚语):** 他每次考试都这么优秀，根本不需要再费脑筋复习，就是吃老本。  
- 中文例句：今年公司的业绩下滑了，大家都得努力工作，不能再吃老本了。
