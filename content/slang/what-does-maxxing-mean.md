# Maxxing (English - Chinese)
## English
1. **"Maxxing Out"**
- *Definition:* Running and maintaining a credit card to the fullest credit limit.
- *Example:* "I finally maxxed out my credit card last month after all those online shopping deals."
2. **"Maxxing It Up"**
- *Definition:* Doing something to the absolute maximum, pushing oneself to the limit.
- *Example:* "We decided to max it up last night and hit up every bar in the city, from 6 PM to 6 AM."
3. **"Maxxing Your Skills"**
- *Definition:* Pushing one's abilities to the highest level.
- *Example:* "She really maxxed her skills in the hackathon, coming up with the most innovative solution."
4. **"Maxxing It All Out"**
- *Definition:* Giving one's all in something, often in a way that is chaotic or uncontrolled.
- *Example:* "They maxxed it all out at the party last night, causing quite a disturbance."
## Chinese
1. **“透支”**
- *定义:* 将信用卡使用到最大的信用额度。
- *例句:* “上个月终于把信用卡全部透支了，买了一系列网购折扣商品。”
2. **“玩命（拼）”**
- *定义:* 做某事到最大化，极力推动自己。
- *例句:* “我们决定昨晚好好狂欢，在城市里的每家酒吧逐个玩命逛了个通宵。”
3. **“把能力发挥到极致”**
- *定义:* 推动自己的才能达到最高水平。
- *例句:* “她在这次黑客马拉松中把能力发挥到了极致，提出了最具有创新性的解决方案。”
4. **“玩儿疯了”**
- *定义:* 在某件事上下够全力，有时候可能是混乱或不加控制的。
- *例句:* “昨晚他们在聚会上玩儿疯了，引发了一系列混乱。”
