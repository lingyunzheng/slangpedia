## Maxxing
### 中文解释
“maxxing”是网络流行语，“榨干”的意思，通常用于形容一个人已经尽了最大努力，到达了极限状态。
### 英文 Explanation
“Maxxing” is a colloquial term from the internet, which means "extorting / squeezing to the max," typically used to describe someone who has done everything they can or reached their absolute limit.
