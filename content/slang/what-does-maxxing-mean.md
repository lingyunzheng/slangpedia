# Maxxing (正最大化)
**中文解释:**  
“maxxing”是一个英文俚语，用来表达一种让自己感觉最好、最棒的状态或行为。无论是物质上还是精神上的极致，追求让自己感到最大的满足感或者表现得最好。
**英文解释:**  
"Maxxing" is a slang term used to describe the act of achieving the highest level of performance or satisfaction in a particular activity or state. It can be applied to both physical and mental enhancements, often referring to doing something to the absolute best or fullest extent to feel or perform at one's peak.
