# Maxxing
## English
**Meaning**: To achieve the highest level of something, typically in terms of enjoyment, excitement, or performance. It emphasizes pushing oneself to the absolute best or limit.
**Example**: "I'm maxxing out on this video game, it's so much fun!"
## 中文
**含义**：达到某事的最高峰或极致，通常是指在享受、兴奋或表现方面达到最佳状态。它强调把自己推向极致或极限。
**例句**：这款游戏实在是太好玩了，我都达到极限了！
