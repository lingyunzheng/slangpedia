## maxxing
### English
**Maxxing out**: This term is often used to describe spending until one's credit limit is reached, or essentially spending as much as one can afford (or borrow). It can also be used more generally to describe pushing something to the absolute limit in any context.
### 中文
**Maxxing 出**: 这个词通常用于描述透支信用额度，即把钱花到极限，或者尽可能多地支出。它也可以更广泛地用于描述在任何情况下把某事推到极致。
