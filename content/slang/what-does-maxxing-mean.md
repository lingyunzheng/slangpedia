# Maxxing: 吃老本
## English
**Maxxing**: To rely on one's past successes or established reputation to coast through a situation without putting in much effort. It's like sitting back and living off the efforts and achievements of the past, rather than actively contributing or innovating.
## 中文
**吃老本 (Chī Lǎoběn)**: 利用自己的过去成功或建立的声誉来敷衍了事，不需要付出太多努力。就像依靠过去的努力和成就来维持现状，而不是积极地贡献或创新。
