## Maxxing
**English:**
- **Meaning:** To do something to the absolute limit or maximum extent, often in a way that is intense or excessive.
- **Example:** "He was maxxing out on the video games last night and didn't sleep at all."
**Chinese (简体中文):**
- **意思:** 做某事做到极致或者最大范围，通常带有夸张或过度的意思。
- **示例:** "昨晚他在玩电子游戏玩得可嗨了，整夜都没睡。"
