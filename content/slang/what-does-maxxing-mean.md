## Maxxing (汉语: 抓瞎)
**English**: "Maxxing" generally refers to a situation or behavior where someone is operating at or near their maximum capacity, usually in a context where they are being pushed to their limits. This could be physically, mentally, or emotionally.
**汉语**: "抓瞎" 这个词通常用来形容一个人被逼到了极限，无论是身体上、精神上还是情感上。这个词语更侧重于描述一种“抓狂”的状态，意思是完全失去控制或者完全没有头绪，手足无措。
Example Usage:
- **English**: She's been maxxing all week with her tight schedule, and now she's totally exhausted.
- **汉语**: 她这一周被紧凑的日程安排得抓瞎，现在完全筋疲力尽了。
- **English**: When he maxxing, he'll push himself to the point of burnout.
- **汉语**: 当他抓瞎的时候，会把自己逼到崩溃的边缘。
