## Maxxing (English)
**Meaning:** To engage in an extreme or intense activity to the point of exhaustion or maximum capacity. Often used in contexts like workouts, games, or creative projects.
**Example:** "I maxxed out my energy playing video games all night long."
## Maxxing (Chinese)
**意思:** 努力进行到极致或最大限度的活动，通常用于描述锻炼、游戏或创意项目等场景。
**例句:** "我整晚都在玩电子游戏，把精力都用尽了。"
