## Maxxing
### English
**Meaning**: Intense effort or performance, often to achieve the highest level of something.
**Example**: "She maxxed out her potential to get that promotion."
### Chinese
**意思**: 一门心思地投入所有精力或努力，达到最高水平或尽最大努力。
**例句**: "她尽全力发挥了自己所有的潜力，以获得晋升的机会。"
