## Maxxing
### Chinese (中文): 打卡
- **Explanation**: 这个词来源于“打卡”，原意是指在工作或学习中按时记录自己的出勤情况。在社交网络和短视频平台上，“打卡”被赋予了更多的意义，它指的是分享自己生活中经常性、有规律的行为或习惯，比如每天早晨跑步、固定时间做瑜伽、每天阅读等。这种行为通常是为了鼓励自己坚持某种生活方式，或者是为展示自己的个人习惯和生活品质。
### English: Maxxing
- **Explanation**: "Maxxing" hails from a similar concept of "打卡" (marking attendance) in Chinese. Originally, it referred to recording one's attendance at work or studying. In today's social media and video-sharing platforms, "maxxing" takes on a more expanded meaning. It involves sharing on a regular basis, something you do habitually, like morning runs, set yoga routines, or daily reading. These posts are often aimed at motivating oneself to persist with certain lifestyles or to show off personal habits and quality of life.
