## Maxxing
### English
**Maxxing**: To push something or someone to the absolute limit. It can be used to describe achieving peak performance, effort, or satisfaction. It often implies going all out or achieving a maximum level of something.
*Example*: "I'm tired of maxxing myself every day; I need a break."
### Chinese
**maxxing**：将某事或某人推到极限。它可以用来描述达到最佳表现、努力或满足感。通常意味着全力以赴或达到某事的最大水平。
*例句*：我每天把自己推到极限也累了，我需要休息一下。
