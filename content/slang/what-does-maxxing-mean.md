## English Explanation
**Maxxing** is a slang term that refers to the act of pushing something or someone to their absolute limit. It can also mean extracting the maximum potential or effort from something or someone.
## Chinese Explanation
**Maxxing** 是一个俚语，指的是将某事或某人推至极限的行为。它也可以指从某事或某人中榨取最大的潜力或努力。
