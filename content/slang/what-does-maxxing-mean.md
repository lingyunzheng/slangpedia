## Maxxing
### English
**Meaning:** To push something or someone to the absolute limit; to do something with maximum effort or intensity.
**Example:** "Dude, we're maxxing this party tonight! Get ready to dance like no one's watching!"
### 中文
**释义:** 尽力做到极致；用最大努力或强度去做某事。
**例句:** "兄弟，今晚我们把派对 maxxing 了！准备好尽情跳舞吧，仿佛全世界只有你们两个人。"
