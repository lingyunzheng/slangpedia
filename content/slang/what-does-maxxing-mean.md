# Maxxing
## 在中文（中国大陆）中的解释
"maxxing" 一般源自英文，是指让人感到极度兴奋、充满能量和战斗力的状态。这种感觉通常伴随着高度的兴奋和渴望展示自己的欲望。可能因为做某件事达到了极致，或者是体验到了某种强烈的情感，让人感觉非常亢奋。
## 在英文中的解释
"Maxxing" is typically derived from English and refers to a state of extreme excitement, energy, and a sense of combat readiness. This feeling usually comes from doing something to the extreme or experiencing a strong emotion, which makes one feel extremely pumped up and eager to show off.
