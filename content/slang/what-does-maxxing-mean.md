## Maxxing
### English
**Maxxing**: When you're having the absolute best time, experiencing peak levels of enjoyment or excitement. It's like hitting the max on a pleasure meter.
### Chinese
**Maxxing**: 当你获得了极致的快乐或兴奋体验，享受到了最大限度的乐趣。就像是快乐或兴奋的最高值。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "She was maxxing out on the roller coaster, screaming with joy."
- Chinese: "她在过山车上面最大限度地享受着快乐，尖叫着兴奋不已。"
