# Maxxing
## English
*Definition:* To push something or someone to the absolute limit, often to an extreme or excessive degree.
*Example:* "He really maxxed out his credit card after the shopping spree."
## 中文
*定义:* 将某事或某人推至极限，通常是推向极端或过度的程度。
*例句:* "他在购物狂欢后真的把信用卡额度用到了极限。"
