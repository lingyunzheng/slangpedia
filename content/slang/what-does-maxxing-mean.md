# Maxxing
**English:**  
"Maxxing" is a slang term often used to express that someone is doing something to the absolute limit or extreme. It can also refer to pushing yourself to the maximum capacity, whether physically, emotionally, or in terms of effort. For example, "I maxxed out my energy during the concert and am crashed now."
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
"Maxxing" 是一个 slang 词，常用来表示某人正在将某事做到极限或极致。它也可以指推动自己达到最大容量，无论是身体上、情感上还是在努力上。例如，“我在演唱会中 maxxing 自己的能量，现在累垮了。”
**Usage Examples:**  
- English: I maxxed out my physical limits doing those workouts.
- Chinese: 我做那些锻炼时把自己逼到了物理极限。
- English: She maxxed it up at the party last night.
- Chinese: 她昨晚在聚会上玩了个痛快。
