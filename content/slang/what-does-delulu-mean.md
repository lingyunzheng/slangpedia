### Delulu - 芜湖 （Wuhu）
#### English Slang Explanation
**"Delulu"** is a term borrowed from Chinese internet slang, where **"芜湖" (Wuhu)"** is often used as an exclamatory phrase to express excitement, joy, or agreement. Users typically type "Wuhu" in place of "Wow," "Cool," or "Agreed."
#### Chinese Slang Explanation
**芜湖 (Wuhu)** 是一种借用自中国的网络用语，通常作为感叹词来表达兴奋、喜悦或赞同。用户通常在表达“哇塞”、“酷”或“同意”时会使用“Wuhu”。
Example Usage:
- **English:** "That's amazing! You're so talented!"
- **Chinesed:** "这是Delulu！你真的太有才了！"
