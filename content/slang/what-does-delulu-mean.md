## Delulu (English)
**Meaning**: Delulu is an informal slang term occasionally used to refer to someone who is believed to have no intention of improving or changing their behavior, often used in a dismissive manner.
**Example**: "He's just a lazy Delulu, nothing will ever get done with him."

## Delulu (中文)
**含义**: Delulu 是一个非正式的俚语，用来指代那些被认为毫无改进或改变行为意愿的人，通常带有贬义和轻视的语气。
**例句**: "他就是一个懒惰的Delulu，他什么都做不了。"
