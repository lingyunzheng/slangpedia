# Delulu (Slang)
**中文释义**  
Delulu是指一个非常可爱或者是令人疼爱的人物形象，通常用来形容某个人或某种事物非常可爱或者需要被照顾的样子。类似于“萌萌哒”或“软萌”。
**English Explanation**  
Delulu refers to a very cute or adorable character or object, typically used to describe someone or something as incredibly cute or in need of care. It's similar to saying "cute" or "soft and cute" in English.
