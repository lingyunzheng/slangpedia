# Delulu
## English
**Delulu**: A playful and informal term often used to describe a person who is very enthusiastic and energetic, but can also come off as a bit naive or inexperienced. This term can be seen as a friendly and mild way to tease someone who is overly optimistic or not fully aware of the situation they're in. 
**Example**: "Delulu got so excited about the new project that he didn't even notice the potential obstacles."
