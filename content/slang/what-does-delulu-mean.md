# Delulu Slang Explanation
## English
**Delulu**: A playful way to refer to someone in a friendly or affectionate manner. It's often used to address a person in a casual and warm context, akin to calling someone "buddy" or "pal." It can also be used as a nickname or to express fondness, similar to "sweetie" or "cute one."
## Chinese
**Delulu**: 用来亲昵地称呼某人的一种playful方式。它通常用于一种随意且温暖的语境中，类似于称呼某人为“朋友”或“伙伴”。还可以作为昵称使用，表达喜爱之情，类似于“甜心”或“可爱的人”。
