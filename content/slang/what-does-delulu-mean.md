## Delulu (屌露露)
**Chinese Definition:**
- 尼玛设计师 (Nima Shejiyianshi): A clumsy or inefficient designer.
- 屁兔子 (Pi Tuizi): A term often used to mock someone who claims to be advanced or special but actually has little substance.
**English Explanation:**
- Delulu is a term often used to mock a person or something that is perceived as incompetent or overhyped. Just like "delulu" suggests, it often refers to someone who claims to have superior skills or knowledge but ends up looking foolish or ineffective. It's often used to sarcastically describe someone or something that's been oversold or overhyped without delivering the expected results.
