## Delulu (Simplified Chinese: 德蓝лу; Traditional Chinese: 德蓝盧)
### English Explanation
**Delulu** is an internet slang term often used humorously or sarcastically to refer to someone who appears particularly awkward or uncomfortable, often in social situations. It's derived from a character in a popular online series or meme, where the character is portrayed as being very clumsy or囧 (jiǒng, meaning 'awkward' or 'embarrassed').
