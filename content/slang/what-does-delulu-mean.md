# Delulu Slang Explanation
## English
### Meaning:
Delulu is a slang term often used in online communities, particularly on social media platforms. It is a playful and casual way to refer to someone who repeatedly makes the same argument or point, seemingly without considering other viewpoints. It can also imply that the person is not engaging in a meaningful discussion and is instead just repeating themselves.
### Example Usage:
- "Stop being a delulu, have you heard anything I said about the new product?"
- "They're just a delulu, saying the same thing over and over without listening."
