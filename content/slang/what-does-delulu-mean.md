## Delulu (Both English and Chinese)
**English:**  
Delulu is a popular internet slang term that originated from a comedic internet character. It's often used to describe someone or something that is extremely unlucky or experiencing a run of bad luck. When people use "Delulu," they are jokingly commenting on someone's or something's series of unfortunate events, almost as if it's a humorous destiny or fate.
**Chinese:**  
Delulu（德鲁鲁）是一个流行的网络 slang 词汇，源自一个喜剧性的网络角色。通常用来形容某人或某物正经历一系列极不走运的情况。当人们说“Delulu”时，是在开玩笑地评论某人或某物的倒霉命运或一系列不幸事件，仿佛是一种幽默的宿命论。
Usage Example:
- **English:** "Look at those storms! You named your cat Delulu? I guess she's one of the Delulus."
- **Chinese:** "看看这些暴风雨！你给猫起名叫Delulu？看来Delulu本Delulu了。"
