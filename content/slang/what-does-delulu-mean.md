## Delulu (普通话)
**释义**：Delulu 是一个源自中国大陆的网络流行词汇，常用于表达对某个品牌或产品的极大不满或愤怒。它通常带有强烈的负面情绪和批评意味。
**例子**：这个手机质量太差了，我真的被 Delulu 了！
## Delulu (English)
**Definition**: Delulu is a Chinese internet slang term originating from mainland China, often used to express extreme dissatisfaction or anger towards a brand or product. It typically carries a strong negative emotion and criticism.
**Example**: This phone quality is terrible; I'm genuinely Delulu-ed!
