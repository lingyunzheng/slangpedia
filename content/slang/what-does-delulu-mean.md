## Delulu (Simplified Chinese: 呱唧; Pinyin: gua4 ji4) & (English: Delu lu)
* **Simplified Chinese: 呱唧 (guā jì)**
- **Pinyin**: gua4 ji4
- **Meaning**: This is a phonetic representation of a sound, often used to describe something that is endless or in a state of inertia. It can also be used to represent a filler sound in conversations, similar to "uh" or "um" in English.
* **English: Delu lu**
- **Origin**: This is typically a phonetic translation from Chinese "呱唧" and it's often used to describe a situation or state that seems endless or unchanging.
- **Usage**: In casual conversations, "delu lu" can be used to express boredom or a sense of things being repetitive and uneventful. It's a fun, onomatopoeic way to depict something that's stuck in a rut or continuously repeating in a monotonous manner.
**Example Usage:**
- **Chinese**: 有时候感觉时间就像在呱唧！ (Sometimes feeling like time is just gua4 ji4!)
- **English**: Sometimes it feels like time is just dragging on and on and on...
