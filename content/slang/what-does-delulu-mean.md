# Delulu (English)
**Meaning**: A playful and affectionate term often used to address a close friend or loved one in a family. It's a term filled with warmth and intimacy.
**Example**: "Delulu, have you finished your homework today?"

# 德路露 (Délùlù) (Chinese)
**Meaning**: 德路露 (Délùlù) is a Chinese transliteration of the English term "Delulu," often used as a playful nickname for a close friend or family member. It conveys a sense of affection and closeness.
**Example**: "德路露，你今天作业完成了吗？" (Délùlù, nǐ jīntiān zuòyè wánchéng le ma?) - "Delulu, have you finished your homework today?"
