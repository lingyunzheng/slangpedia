# Delulu Slang Explanation
## English
**Delulu**: This term is often used to refer to someone who is naive or easily fooled. It can be likened to the character Delu Lu (Delulu) from the Webtoon and animated series *Future Diary*, known for their simplicity and unwavering naivety. In casual conversation, calling someone "delulu" might be used affectionately or in a joking manner, implying the person is innocent and easy to manipulate.
## Chinese
**Delulu**: 这个词通常用来形容一个很单纯或者很容易被骗的人。类似于漫画和动画系列《未来日记》中的人物Delu Lu（Delulu），这个角色以单纯以及不变的天真著称。在非正式的对话中，称呼某人为“delulu”可能是开玩笑或者亲昵的方式，暗示这个人天真无邪，容易受骗。
