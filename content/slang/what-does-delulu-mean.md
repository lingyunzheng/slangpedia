## Delulu (English)
**Meaning:** 
Delulu is an internet slang term that started from online forums and social media platforms. It's often used to refer to someone who is overly sensitive, high-maintenance, or difficult to please. Essentially, it describes a person who requires a lot of pampering or excessive attention.
**Example Sentence:** 
"That new intern is such a delulu; she can't do anything without constant reassurance."

**意思:**

**例句:** 
"那个新来的实习生真是个delulu，连个任务都得不停地鼓励她。"
