## Delulu
### English Explanation
**Yo Dudes and Dames**: A casual, informal greeting used to address a group of friends or acquaintances, often带着一种轻松的态度, 面向一群朋友或熟人使用的随意招呼。It's like saying "hey guys" or "what's up" but with a bit more zest.
### Chinese Explanation
**delulu**：一种轻松随意的招呼方式，类似于说“嘿，哥们儿”或“啥事”，面对一群朋友或相识的人使用时会显得更加热情和活泼。
---
This slang term "delulu" is used to express a friendly and relaxed atmosphere while greeting and addressing a group of people. It's commonly used in informal settings among friends and can vary in intensity based on the tone and context.
