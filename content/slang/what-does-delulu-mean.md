## Delulu (English)
**Meaning and Usage:**  
Delulu is an Internet slang term that has gained popularity in recent years, often used in casual conversations to express disbelief, frustration, or disappointment. It can be seen as a softer version of saying "What the hell?" or "That's ridiculous!"
**Example Usage:**  
- User: "Delulu, did you see the price of gas today?"  
- Response: "Delulu, yeah, it's crazy!"
## 咔咔LuLu (Chinese)  
**含义和用法:**  
咔咔LuLu 是一个网络流行语，近年来在网络社交中广泛使用，用来表达惊讶、失望或者沮丧的复杂情绪。它类似于说“卧槽”或“这是什么鬼”时的轻描淡写。
**示例用法:**  
- 用户: "咔咔LuLu，你们信么，今天加油站汽油的价格涨了好多!"  
- 回复: "咔咔LuLu，确实涨得厉害。"
