## Delulu (English)
**Delulu**: This term is a playful and affectionate nickname that is often used in casual conversations, usually to refer to a cycloptic character from the *Puzzle & Dragon* game series. In casual use, it can also refer to someone or something that is losely orb-shaped, with one or no clearly defined eyes, hence the reference to the character's appearance. It's often used in a light-hearted or teasing manner.
## Delulu (中文)
