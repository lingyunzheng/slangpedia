## Delulu (正式词汇: 希拉里·克林顿 Hillary Clinton)
**English:**
- **Meaning:** A nickname for U.S. politician Hillary Clinton, often used informally, mainly by people who are fans or supporters of Hillary. It can have a variety of connotations depending on context, from a show of affection to a casual reference.
- **Slang Usage:** " DELUDELOU " is a playful and affectionate way of addressing Hillary Clinton, often seen in social media and fan communities. It can also be used to express admiration or to reference her prominence in U.S. politics.
**Chinese:**
- **意思:** 希拉里·克林顿（Hillary Clinton）的昵称，通常在非正式场合被粉丝或支持者使用。根据语境的不同，可能带有各种含义，从表达喜爱到简单的称呼不等。
- **俚语用法:** "DELULU" 在社交媒体和粉丝社群中是一种轻松且表示爱意的方式称呼希拉里·克林顿。也可以用来表达对她的敬意，或引用她在美国政治中的地位。
