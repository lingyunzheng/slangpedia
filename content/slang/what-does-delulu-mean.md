## Delulu (英语: Delulu)
### Chinese (中文)
Delulu：这是一个在网络聊天、社交媒体和论坛等地方流行的网络用语，源自Anime《一拳超人》（One Punch Man）中角色“Netero”的中文翻译“德勒”。Delulu常用来指那些行为或言论看似非主流、但本质上主流的群体。也可以用于形容某些人的做派、风格，是一种开玩笑或者调侃的方式。
### English
Delulu: This is a popular internet slang term widely used in online chats, social media, and forums, originating from the anime series One Punch Man, where the character "Netero" is translated as "Delulu" in Chinese. Delulu is often used to describe groups or individuals whose actions or words may appear unconventional or non-mainstream but are actually aligned with mainstream values. It can also be used to jestingly describe someone's behavior or style.
