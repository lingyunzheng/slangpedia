## Delulu (汉语/英语)
### 汉语解释：
** Deadpool 的变形词**  
Delulu 是来自于知名漫改电影《死侍》（Deadpool）中主角的名字。Deadpool 的名字是一句对白“Everyone I ever killed, I killed in the shower”之转写，加上个人魅力和幽默感，红遍全球。而 Delulu 则是 Deadpool 的变形词，常用来形容一些满嘴脏话、自我解嘲、酷炫又带点反叛的人。在社交媒体上，Delulu 成为一种对特定个性角色的戏谑称呼。
### 英语解释：
**Deadpool's Variant Term**  
Delulu is a variant term derived from the name of the protagonist in the popular film "Deadpool." Deadpool's name, a palindromic play on the English phrase "Deadpool," referring humorously to his statement "Everyone I ever killed, I killed in the shower," is widely known for his charm, humor, and rebellious nature. In Chinese social media, Delulu has evolved into a term used to describe individuals who are witty, self-deprecating, and have a bit of a rebellious attitude, often accompanied by a penchant for foul language. It's a fun way to label someone with a similar personality or style to the famous character.
