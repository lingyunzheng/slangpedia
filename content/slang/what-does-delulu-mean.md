## Delulu (英语: Delulu)
**中文解释:**  
Delulu 是近年来在年轻人中流行的网络用语，原意是一种 ủy婉的拒绝，带有俏皮和幽默的语气。在中文网络文化中，它通常被用来表示温柔地拒绝别人，或者是以一种更像是撒娇的方式拒绝他人。
**English Explanation:**  
Delulu is a popular internet slang term among young people in recent years, originally meaning a mild refusal with a playful and humorous tone. In Chinese internet culture, it typically refers to a gentle refusal or a way of deferring to someone, often done in a playful or cutesy manner.
