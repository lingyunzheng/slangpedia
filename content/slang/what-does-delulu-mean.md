# Delulu (English)
**Meaning:** Delulu is a term often used to describe someone who behaves in a childish or whiny manner, demanding attention or expressing overly strong emotions.
**Example:** "Don't be such a delulu; grow some teeth!"
# Delulu (Chinese)
**意思:** Delulu 是用来形容一个人行为像个孩子或者撒娇，过度表达感情或寻求关注。
**例句:** "别闹了，像个 delulu 似的！"
