## Locked In
### English
- **Definition**: Confined to a particular commitment or situation from which escape is difficult or impossible.
- **Usage Example**: "We're locked into this contract for another year, so let's make the most of it."
### 中文
- **释义**：被某种承诺或情况困住，难以逃脱或改变。
- **用法示例**： "我们已经签订了一份一年的合约，所以让我们好好利用这段时间吧。"
