## English: Locked In
**Meaning:** When someone is "locked in" in a situation, it means they are unable to escape or change their current circumstances because of various constraints such as financial, emotional, or physical limitations.
**Example Sentence:** "After taking on this job, she felt locked in because she had to pay off her student loans."
## 中文: 锁定
**意思:** 当某人被“锁定”在某种情况下时，意味着他们因各种限制（如财务、情感或身体上的）而无法逃脱或改变当前状态。
**示例句子:** "在签下这份工作合同后，她感觉自己被锁定了，因为要还清学生贷款。"
