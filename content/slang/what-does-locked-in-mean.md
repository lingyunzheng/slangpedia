```markdown
## Locked In
**English:** 
"Locked in" is a slang term used to describe a situation where someone is committed to a decision, plan, or relationship and cannot easily change their mind or exit the situation. It can also refer to a state where someone is fully engaged and unable to provide a break or shift focus.
**Example:**
- You're really into that dating app? Are you locked in for another month?
**Chinese:**
"锁定了"（yuè dìng le）是指一个人对某个决定、计划或关系已经作出了不可轻易更改的选择，无法轻易改变或退出。它也可以用来形容一个人完全投入，无法暂时中断或转移注意力的情况。
**例句:**
- 你还在那个交友软件里粘着不走，锁定在里面了吗？
```

```markdown
## Locked In
**English:** 
This term is often used in a technology or gaming context to indicate that a device, software, or platform is bound to a specific network, service, or system, making it difficult or costly to switch to a different one. It can also metaphorically describe a situation where a consumer is tied to a product or service due to investments in usage or setup.
**Example:**
- If you go with this cell phone, it's locked into a specific carrier, and you can't change it for two years.
**Chinese:**
这个俚语常被用于科技或游戏的语境中，表示一个设备、软件或平台被绑定在某个特定的网络、服务或系统中，使之难以或成本高昂地切换到不同的系统。它也可以用来比喻性地描述消费者因为使用和设置方面的投入而被某个产品或服务束缚的情况。
**例句:**
- 如果你选择这个手机，会被特定的运营商锁定，两年内都无法更换。
```
