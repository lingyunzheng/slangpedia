## English
**Locked In**: 
- *Meaning*: When someone is fully committed to a decision, plan, or relationship, and there's no turning back.
- *Example*: "She's locked in for the job interview; she can’t back out now."
## 中文
**锁定/锁定了**:
- *含义*: 当有人完全决定了某个决定、计划或关系，且无法回头时。
- *例句*: "她已经锁定这份工作面试了；现在不能退出了。"
