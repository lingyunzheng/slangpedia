## English: Locked In
### Definition
When someone is "locked in" to a situation, it means they are stuck or unable to get out of it easily. The phrase can be used in various contexts, from relationships to job situations.
### Example
*She feels locked in at her current job due to the high cost of relocating and long tenure in the company.*
### Chinese (简体中文): 锁定
### 解释
当某人“锁定”在一个情况下时，意味着他们无法轻易地摆脱这个情况。这个短语可以应用于各种情境，无论是人际关系还是工作环境。
### 示例
*由于搬家成本高以及在公司工作很长时间，她感觉自己现在的工作陷入了“锁定”状态。*
