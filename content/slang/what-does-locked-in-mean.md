## English: Locked In
**Definition:** Refers to a situation where someone is committed to a decision, plan, or situation to such a degree that they can no longer easily change or leave it.
**Example Sentence:** I feel locked in with this job because I already invested so much time and money into training.
## 中文: 锁定 (suǒdèng)
**释义:** 指某人已经对某个决定、计划或情况做出了如此巨大的投入，以至于难以轻易改变或离开。
**例句:** 因为我已经在培训上投入了很多时间和金钱，所以我觉得和这份工作已经陷入了“锁定”。
