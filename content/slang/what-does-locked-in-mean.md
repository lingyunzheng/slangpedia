## English: Locked In
**Definition:** Refers to being committed to a situation, deal, or decision with no easy way out, often used in contexts where one has agreed to something they might later regret.
**Example Usage:** "She's locked in with that job offer, so she'll have to start there next week even if she finds something better."
## 中文：被锁定了
**定义：** 表示陷入某种情况、协议或决定之中，无法轻易退出，常用于描述已经做出决定，即便改变也很难实现的情境。
**例句：** "她已经接收了那份工作提议，所以下周就得开始那边的工作，即便找到更好的机会也很难改变这个决定。"
