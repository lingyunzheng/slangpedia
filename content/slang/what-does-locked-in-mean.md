### English: Locked In
This phrase is often used to describe a situation where someone is committed to a course of action, decision, or consequence with no way out. They have made a decision that they can’t easily reverse.
**Example:** "I'm locked in on this vacation plan, so you can book the flights now."
### 中文: 锁定 (Luò Dìng)
这个俚语用来描述一个人已经做出了无法轻易逆转的选择、决定或后果。他们已经投入了，退不出来。
**例句:** "我对这个度假计划已经锁定，所以你可以订机票了。"
