## English: "Locked In"
### Definition:
- **Meaning**: When a situation, deal, or commitment is finalized and cannot be changed or exited easily.
### Example Usage:
- "We're locked in with this deal, so we need to make it work."
### Bilingual Slang Explanation:
- **English**: When a situation is "locked in," it means that once you commit to it, it's difficult to leave or change it. It's like securing a deal or a decision that has no turning back.
- **Chinese**: 当一个情况、协议或承诺被确定下来，无法轻易更改或退出时，我们说它是“锁定”的。就像是确保了一个协议或决定，你无法轻易改变它。
## English: "Locked In"
### Definition:
- **Meaning**: Also used to describe a person having a condition where the body is unable to move, especially when referring to a spinal cord injury.
### Example Usage:
- "He was locked in after the accident, unable to move from the neck down."
### Bilingual Slang Explanation:
- **English**: When someone is "locked in," it can also refer to a condition where the person is unable to move from the neck down, often due to a spinal cord injury. It signifies severe physical immobility.
- **Chinese**: 当某人由于脊髓损伤而“锁定”，无法从颈部以下移动时，我们说他是“锁定”的。它表示严重的身体无法移动。
