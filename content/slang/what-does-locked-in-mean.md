### locked in
**English:**  
- *Slang:* If something is "locked in," it means that a decision or arrangement has been finalized and cannot be changed. It's typically used in contexts where a plan or commitment is set in stone.
**Example:**  
"After we signed the contract, the deal was locked in, and we can't back out now."
**中文：**
- *俚语:* 如果某事“锁定”了，这就意味着一个决定或安排已经最终确定并且不能更改。通常用在计划或承诺已经固定的场景中。
**例句:**  
"签订合同之后，协议已经锁定，我们现在不能退出了。"
