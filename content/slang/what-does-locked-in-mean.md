```markdown
# English Slang: Locked In
**Meaning:** Confirmed or set in a situation or decision, often implying that it is no longer changeable.
**Example:** "We're locked in for the ski trip next weekend. No going back now!"
**Chinese Slang: 定局 (Dìng jú)**
**Meaning:** 指某件事已经确定或无法改变，通常用于描述一个已经决定下来且无法再改变的情况。
**Example:** "我们去滑雪的周末已经定了，无法更改了。" （We're locked in for the ski trip next weekend. No going back now!）
```
