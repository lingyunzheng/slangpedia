## English
**Locked In**: Refers to a situation where someone or something is permanently committed to a specific choice, agreement, or decision, often with no way to back out. 
Example: "He's locked in with his girlfriend and won't consider anyone else."
## 中文
**锁定**: 指某人或某事已经永久性地做出了某种选择、协议或决定，通常无法逆转。
例句：他和女朋友已经确定下来了，不会考虑其他人了。
