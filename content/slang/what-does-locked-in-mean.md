## English: Locked In
### Definition
When someone is "locked in" in a conversation or situation, it means they are fully committed to it and there's no going back. It could refer to a moment where they are stuck in a discussion or scenario they cannot escape from.
### Example
*He was locked in the debate and couldn't back down even when he knew he was wrong.*
## 中文: 锁定
### 释义
当某人被“锁定”在一个对话或情境中时，意味着他们全身心投入其中，无法退出。这可能指的是一个无法逃避的讨论或情况。
### 示例
*他陷入了一场辩论中，即使知道自己错了也无法中途退出。*
