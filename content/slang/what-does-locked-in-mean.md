```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Meaning
- **Definition**: Officially committed or agreed to something from which there is no easy escape.
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
- **English**: Officially committed or agreed to something from which there is no easy escape.
- **Chinese**: 正式承诺或同意了某件事，而无法轻易退出。
### Example Usage
- **English**: She's locked in to attend the conference next month.
- **Chinese**: 她已经正式承诺下个月参加这个会议了。
```

```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Meaning
- **Definition**: Used to describe a situation where one is too invested in something to leave, often due to financial or emotional commitments.
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
- **English**: Used to describe a situation where one is too invested in something to leave, often due to financial or emotional commitments.
- **Chinese**: 描述一个人已经深深投入某件事，以至于无法轻易离开，通常是出于经济或情感方面的承诺。
### Example Usage
- **English**: He's locked in on his investment in the company and can't get his money out.
- **Chinese**: 他在公司里的投资太多了，资金已经投入，拿不回来了。
```

```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Meaning
- **Definition**: Figuratively used to describe a feeling of being trapped in a relationship or situation with no obvious way out.
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
- **English**: Figuratively used to describe a feeling of being trapped in a relationship or situation with no obvious way out.
- **Chinese**: 形容人在一段感情或状态下感觉被困住，而没有明显的出路。
### Example Usage
- **English**: I feel locked in with my current job; I'd love to switch to something more fulfilling.
- **Chinese**: 我现在的工作让我感觉被困住了，我非常想换一份更有意义的工作。
```
