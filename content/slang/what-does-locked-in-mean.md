## "Locked In" in English and Chinese
### English
**Locked In**: This phrase is often used to describe a situation where a deal, agreement, or plan is finalized and cannot be changed. It can also mean someone is committed or stuck in a particular role, situation, or decision.
### 中文
** Locked In**: 这个短语通常用来描述一种情况，即交易、协议或计划已经最终确定，无法更改。它也可以指某人已经被困或固定在特定的角色、状况或决定中。
