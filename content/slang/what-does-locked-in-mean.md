## English: Locked In
### Bilingual Explanation
**English:** "Locked In" is used to describe a situation where someone or something is trapped or unable to escape or be changed. It can also refer to the final stage of a deal or agreement where all details are settled and the decision is final.
**中文:** "Locked In" 用来形容某人或某事被困住或无法逃脱或改变的情况。它也可以指一个协议或交易的最终阶段，所有细节都已经确定，决定也是最终的。
