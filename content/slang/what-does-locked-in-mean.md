## English: Locked In
**Definition:** Refers to a situation where one is committed to a decision, deal, or plan, often with no easy way out.
**Example:** "After signing the contract, you're locked in, so make sure you review everything carefully!"
## 中文: 锁定
**释义：** 指某人对某个决定、交易或计划已经做出了承诺，通常无法轻易退出。
**例句：** "签订合同之后，你就锁定在其中了，所以一定要仔细检查一切！"
