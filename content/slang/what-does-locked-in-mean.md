```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Chinese: 被锁定了 ( bèi suǒdìng le )
- **Definition**: Used to describe a situation where a party is forced to continue a commitment or deal due to restrictive conditions. It implies a lack of options or freedom to exit the agreement.
- **Example Usage**:
- In English: "The baseball player was locked in to a five-year contract, even if his performance declines."
- 在中文：这位棒球运动员被锁定了五年合约，即使他的表现下滑也能继续履行。
```
