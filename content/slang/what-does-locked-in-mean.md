## English: Locked In
### Definition
You are "locked in" when you are stuck with a decision, situation, or choice and cannot get out of it easily. This phrase is often used in contexts where you have committed to something without an easy way to back out.
### Example
- If you sign a long-term lease for an apartment, you might say, "I'm locked in for the next two years."
## 中文: 锁定（锁定在某事中）
### 定义
当你“锁定在某事中”时，意味着你已经做出了一个决定、陷入了某种状况或者做出了选择，而无法轻易地退出或改变这种状况。这个短语常用于描述那种没有容易的撤销选择的情境。
### 示例
- 如果你签订了两年的租房合同，你可能会说：“我接下来两年都锁定在那里了。”
