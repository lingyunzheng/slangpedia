## English: Locked In
**Slang Explanation**: This term is commonly used to describe a situation where someone feels they have no choice but to continue a commitment, deal, or relationship because of circumstances that make it difficult or impossible to back out.
### Example Usage:
- "I signed a two-year contract with them, so I'm locked in for now."
- "Once you start that new business with these terms, you're pretty much locked in."
---
## 中文: 锁定（锁定在里面）
**俚语解释**：通常用来描述一个人处于一种不得不继续某种承诺、交易或关系的情境中，因为环境因素使得退出变得非常困难或不可能。
### 例句：
- "我已经签了两年的合同，所以现在基本上是被‘锁定’了。"
- "一旦你同意了这些条款开始与他们合作，你就差不多‘被锁定’了。"
