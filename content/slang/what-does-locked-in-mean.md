## English: Locked In
### Chinese: 被锁定了 (Bèi suǒdìng le)
**Explanation**:
- **English**: If you're "locked in" to something, it means you are stuck with it and can't easily change your situation. This can apply to a contract, a decision, or any other commitment you can't reverse.
- **Chinese**: 当你说“被锁定了”时，意味着你被某种情况限制住，无法轻易改变。这可以是合同、决定或其他你无法取消的承诺。
**Usage Example**:
- **English**: I feel locked in with this apartment lease until next year.
- **Chinese**: 我对这个公寓租约感到被锁定了，至少要到明年底才能改变。
## English: Locked In
### Chinese: 被关在里面 (Bèi guān zài nǐtālǐ)
**Explanation**:
- **English**: "Locked in" can also mean physically being trapped inside a place or a situation. This often describes a scenario where someone is unable to leave due to external barriers like a locked door or figuratively due to emotional entanglements or constraints.
- **Chinese**: “被关在里面”不仅指的是物理上被关在某个地方，还描述了一种由于外部障碍（如门锁）或情感纠葛等而无法离开的状况。
**Usage Example**:
- **English**: She felt locked in by the high walls of her garden.
- **Chinese**: 她被花园的高墙所困，感到像是被关在里面了。
