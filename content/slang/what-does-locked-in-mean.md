## English: Locked In
### Explanation:
"Locked in" is a slang term often used to indicate a situation where someone is committed to a decision, outcome, or agreement with no easy way to back out. It can also refer to a state where someone feels stuck, unable to break free or change their current path, often due to external factors such as contractual obligations, financial constraints, or emotional entanglements.
### Bilingual Example:
- **English:** I regret getting into this "locked in" contract with no clauses for early termination.
- **中文:** 我后悔签了这个没有提前终止条款的“无法脱身”的合同。
## 中文: 锁定了
### 解释:
“锁定”一词在俚语中通常用来表示一个人已经做出了决定、达成了协议或陷入了某种情况，没有轻易退出的方式。它还可以指一个人感到被束缚住，无法改变当前路径，通常是因为外部因素如合同义务、财务限制或情感纠葛。
### 双语示例:
- **中文:** 我后悔签了这个没有提前终止条款的“无法脱身”的合同。
- **English:** I regret getting into this "locked in" contract with no clauses for early termination.
