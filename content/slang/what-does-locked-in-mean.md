## English: Locked in
### Explanation
When you're "locked in" about something, it means you are committed or stuck with a particular decision or situation. You can't easily change your mind or get out of it.
**Example:**
- I'm locked in on this job offer; it's a great opportunity.
## 中文: 锁定
### 解释
当你"锁定"某事时，意味着你已经做出了决定或陷入了某种情况，无法轻易改变或退出。
**例句:**
- 我已经锁定了一份工作机会，这是一次很好的机会。
