```markdown
## English: Locked In
**Definition:** When a deal, situation, or decision is finalized and cannot be changed or exited from easily.
### Example Usage:
- "The company signed the big contract with no escape clause, they are totally locked in."
---
## 中文: 锁定
**释义：** 当一项交易、状况或决定已经达成且不能轻易更改或退出时。
### 示例用法：
- "公司签署了包含无退出条款的大合同，他们已完全锁定。"
```
