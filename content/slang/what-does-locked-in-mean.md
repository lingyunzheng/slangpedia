# English: Locked In
**Meaning:** To be committed to something, often unable to change or exit due to external factors.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **英语释义：** 指某人被某种情况或承诺所束缚，通常难以改变或退出，受到外部因素的影响。
- **例句：** After signing the long-term contract, he felt locked in to the company for the next five years.
- **例句翻译：** 签署了长期合同后，他觉得自己接下来的五年都“被锁定了”在这家公司。
