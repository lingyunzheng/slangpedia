# "Locked In" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## English
**"Locked In"** - This phrase is commonly used when someone or something is sealed or confined in a certain state or situation, often without the possibility of escaping or changing things. It can also be used figuratively to describe being committed to a decision or agreement that cannot be easily reversed.
**Example:**  
- *I wish I hadn't signed that contract – I'm now completely locked in!*
## 中文
**"锁定在"** - 这个词组通常用来描述某人或某事物被固定在一个状态或情况下，无法逃脱或改变。它也可以形象地用来形容处于一个不能轻易反悔的决定或协议中。
**例句：**  
- *我本不应该签那个合同，现在我已经完全锁定在其中了！*
