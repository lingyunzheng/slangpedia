## Locked In (锁定)
### English
**Meaning:** Being "locked in" can have several meanings, but commonly it refers to being committed to a decision or situation to such a degree that it can no longer be easily changed. For example, you might be locked into a contract, or feel locked into a relationship.
**Example Sentence:**  
"I'm locked into this job contract for another two years."
### 中文
**含义:** "锁定" 这个俚语通常表示已经被某个决定或情况困住了，改变起来相当困难。例如，你可能被一个合同“锁定了”，或者在一段关系中“锁定”了。
**例句：**
“我这个工作合同还要签两年，算是被锁定了。”
