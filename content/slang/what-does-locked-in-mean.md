## English Slang: Locked In
- **Meaning**: This phrase is used to describe a situation where someone is committed to a decision or plan and cannot or will not change their mind about it.
- **Example**: "I'm locked in on the date for the wedding. No backing out now!"
## 中文俚语: 锁定 (Luò Dìng)
- **含义**: 这个短语用来形容一个人已经作出了决定或计划，并且不能或不会改变主意的情况。
- **例句**: "我已经锁定婚礼的日期了，现在没退路了！"
