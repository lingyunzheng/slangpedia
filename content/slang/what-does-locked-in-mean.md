## English
**Locked In**: This slang is often used to describe a situation where a decision has been made and cannot be changed or reversed. It can also refer to being committed to a plan, deal, or situation without any escape route.
## 中文
** locked in**：这个俚语常用来形容一个决定已经做出且无法改变或撤销的情况。它也可以指已经承诺某个计划、交易或状况，无法退出。
