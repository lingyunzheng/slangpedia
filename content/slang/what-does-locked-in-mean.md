# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Locked In"
## English Explanation
"Locked in" can be used in a few different contexts:
1. **Financial Terms**: When invested in a market or product to the point that there is a significant commitment, typically to a company’s stock, a business venture, or a specific investment that is not easily reversible.
- *Example*: "He's locked in with the tech stocks after his retirement, so his financial situation is secure."
2. **Personal Relationships**: Describing a situation where two individuals are mutually committed or entangled in a relationship that is hard to separate from, often because of emotional or financial ties.
- *Example*: "They say they are not ready to lock in with each other, as they still want to see where their careers take them."
## Chinese Explanation
"锁定" 通常有两种不同的用法：
1. **财经用语**：在某种市场或产品中投入巨大，通常是对公司股票、商业项目或特定投资的深度承诺，且难以逆转。
- *例句*：在退休后，他投入到了科技股之中，因此他的财务状况是安全的。
2. **人际关系**：描述一种双方已经深度承诺或纠缠在一起的关系，通常由于情感或财务纽带，难以分离开来。
- *例句*：他们说还无法锁定彼此，因为他们还想看看自己的事业会走向何方。
