### English: Locked In
**Definition:** Being committed or stuck in a particular situation, relationship, or agreement from which it is difficult to escape.
**Example:** "After signing the contract, he realized he was locked in for at least another year."
### 中文: 锁定（锁定在里面）
**定义：** 指被某种情况、关系或协议束缚，难以摆脱。
**例句：** "签署合同时，他意识到自己至少要被困在这个承诺里一年。"
