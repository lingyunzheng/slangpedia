## English: Locked In
### Chinese: 被锁定了 ( bèi suǒdìng le )
**Explanation:**
This phrase is used when someone is "locked in" a situation, meaning they are stuck or trapped in a certain state or position and cannot easily get out of it. It can also be used to describe when someone is "stuck" in a relationship, a decision, or a predicament from which they have difficulty escaping.
**Example Usage:**
- English: "I feel like I'm locked in this job with no chance for promotion."
- Chinese: 我感觉我在这家公司没有晋升的机会，感觉自己被锁定了。（Wǒ gǎnjué wǒ zài quègè gōngsī mǎiyǐ bù jīngshēn de kěyǐ, gǎnjù wǒ bèi suǒdìng le.）
- English: "She's locked in a toxic relationship."
- Chinese: 她陷入了毒 relationships，感觉被锁定了。（Tā xiàxiànle dú relationship, gǎnjù bèi suǒdìng le.）
