# English: Locked In
**Explanation:** When someone is "locked in" to a situation or arrangement, it means they are trapped or stuck in it without an easy way out. This can refer to a bad relationship, a contract, or any scenario where escaping seems difficult.
**Example Sentence:** "After they bought the house, they felt locked in because the market was too slow for a quick resale."
# 中文：锁定
**解释:** 当某人处于"锁定"中，意味着他们被某种情况或安排困住了，很难摆脱。这可以指一段糟糕的恋情、一份合同，或者任何让人难以逃脱的情境。
**例句:** "买了房子之后，他们觉得自己被锁定在了这种情况中，因为房地产市场的销售情况很慢，难以迅速转售。"
