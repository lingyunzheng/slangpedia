## English Slang: "Locked in"
**Meaning:** Definitely; for sure; without a doubt.
**Example:** "You're locked in for the interview next week."
### 中文俚语解释：“锁定了”
**意思：** 绝对的；肯定的；毫无疑问的。
**例句：** “你下周面试锁定了。”
