# 英汉双解俚语释义：Locked In
**英 文:** Locked in
**汉 语:** 锁定的
**解 释:** When something is described as "locked in," it means that a decision, agreement, or plan is final and there is no turning back. It implies that the outcome or situation is sealed and that further changes are unlikely.
例如:
- 英文例句: The contract is now locked in.
- 汉语释义: 合同现在已经确定无法更改了。
- 英文例句: She's locked in for a spot on the team.
- 汉语释义: 她已经被选入团队，无法更改。
