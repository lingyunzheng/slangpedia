```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Meaning
When something is "locked in," it means that a decision has been made and cannot be changed or reversed easily.
### Example
"I locked in my flight reservations for next month's trip." 
### Bilingual Explanation
**English:** When something is "locked in," it means that a decision has been made and cannot be changed or reversed easily.
**Spanish:** Cuando algo está "bloqueado," significa que una decisión ya ha sido tomada y no se puede cambiar o revertir fácilmente.
```

```markdown
## English: Locked In
### Meaning
The phrase "locked in" can also refer to a situation where multiple changes or conditions have been put into effect, leaving little room for future modifications or flexibility.
### Example
"The company is locked in to its new strategy with no room for changes."
### Bilingual Explanation
**English:** The phrase "locked in" can also refer to a situation where multiple changes or conditions have been put into effect, leaving little room for future modifications or flexibility.
**Spanish:** La frase "bloqueado" también puede referirse a una situación en la que se han aplicado varias modificaciones o condiciones, dejando poquísima posibilidad de cambios o flexibilidad futuras.
```
