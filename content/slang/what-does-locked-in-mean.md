## English: Locked In
### Definition
- **Meaning**: Officially committed to a particular plan, decision, or situation, often with no easily seen option to get out.
- **Usage Example**: "We're locked in for the ski trip next week, no changing my mind!"
## 中文: 锁定（锁定了）
### 释义
- **含义**：正式承诺某个计划、决定或情况，通常没有明显的退出选项。
- **使用示例**：下周的滑雪之旅我们已经确定了，我不再改变主意了。
