```markdown
## English: Locked In
**Meaning:** Once something is "locked in," it can no longer be changed, canceled, or reversed.
**Example Usage:**  
- "We have decided to move into the new office, and the lease is locked in for two years."
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义:** "锁定"之后，表示某事已经确定，不能再更改、取消或逆转。
- **例句:** "我们决定搬到新办公室，租约已经锁定两年。"
```
```markdown
## English: Locked In
**Meaning:** In the context of a relationship or date plan, it means that someone is unable to back out once they have committed, often because they've already made other plans or arrangements that depend on their presence.
**Example Usage:**  
- "I wish I could go to that concert tonight, but I'm locked in with a work conference."
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义:** 指在一段关系或约会计划中，一旦承诺了就无法退出，通常是已经安排了其他事情，取决于自己的出席。
- **例句:** "我本来想找今晚去听演唱会，但我已经被一个工作研讨会'锁定'了。"
```
