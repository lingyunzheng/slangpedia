## English: Locked in
### Definition: 
When someone or something is "locked in," it means they are stuck or confined in a particular situation and cannot easily escape from it. This phrase often implies a situation that is difficult to change or overcome.
### Example:
- *He's locked into his current job because of the massive student loan debt he has.*
## 中文: 锁定
### 释义: 
当某人或某物处于“锁定”状态时，表示他们被某种情况所困，难以轻易摆脱。这个短语经常指的是难以改变或克服的情况。
### 例子:
- *因为他巨大的学生贷款债务，他被困在了目前的工作中。*
