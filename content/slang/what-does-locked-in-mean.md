## English
**Locked In**: 
When something is "locked in," it means it is set in stone, finalized, and unlikely to change. It's often used when discussing decisions or deals that are finalized.
Example: "After we signed the contract, the deal was locked in."
## 中文
**锁定（Lock In）**:
当某件事“锁定”时，意味着它已经固定下来，难以改变。通常用于讨论已经确定的决策或交易。
例子: "签约后，交易就被锁定下来了。"
