## Locked In (English/锁定中 - Chinese)
### English Explanation
"Locked in" is a slang term that can have multiple meanings depending on the context. It often refers to being committed to a particular course of action, decision, or situation from which it is difficult to escape. For example, being "locked in" on a deal means that the terms are finalized, and backing out would be challenging.
**Usage Example:**
- "I'm locked in on this vacation package, it's too expensive to cancel now."
### Chinese Explanation
"锁定中"（锁定中状态）这个网络流行语在不同的上下文中也有多种含义，主要指的是无法轻易退出某种预先确定的行动、决定或状况。比如说，你“锁定”了一份交易，意味着条款已经最终确定，退出可能会非常困难。
**使用示例：**
- “我已经‘锁定’了这个度假套餐，取消的话太费钱了。”
### Cognitive Connection
In both English and Chinese, the term reflects a state of being unable to change or reverse a previously decided or committed situation, emphasizing a level of commitment or finality.
