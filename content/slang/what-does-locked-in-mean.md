## Locked In (锁定中)
### English:
**Meaning:** Typically used to describe a situation where someone or something is committed to a particular plan, deal, or arrangement, and can't or won't back out.
**Example:** "I think we're locked in with the contract now that we've signed the agreement."
### Chinglish:
**Meaning:** 常用来形容某人或某事物被某个计划、协议或安排所束缚，无法或不愿意退出。
**Example:** "我们现在签了协议，好像就被这个合同困住了，无法退出了。"
