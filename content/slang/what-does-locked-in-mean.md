```markdown
## English: Locked In
**Definition:** Refers to a situation where a person is fully committed or made to do something, usually in a way that cannot be easily changed or avoided.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** Refers to a situation where a person is fully committed or made to do something, usually in a way that cannot be easily changed or avoided.
- **中文:** 指一个人已经完全陷入某种状况或被逼去做某事，通常是无法轻易改变或避免的。
## Example Sentences:
- **English:** She feels locked in her current job because she has a mortgage and kids to support.
- **中文:** 她觉得自己在当前的工作中已经陷入了困境，因为她有房贷和孩子需要抚养。
```
