## English - Shook
### Definition:
"Shook" is an onomatopoeic slang term often used to express that someone or something has had a significant emotional impact, usually in a negative way, or that an event or situation was intense, surprising, or scary.
### Example of Use:
- "When I heard the news about the cheating scandal, I was completely shook!"
---
## 中文 - Shook
### 解释：
"Shook" 是一个拟声词俚语，通常用来表示某人或某事对某人产生了极大的情感冲击，通常是负面的，或者是某事或情况非常强烈、令人惊讶或恐怖。
### 使用示例：
- “当我听说那个出轨丑闻时，我完全被震撼了！”
