# English: Shook
**Definition:** This slang term means someone is utterly impressed, shocked, or emotionally affected by something, often to the point of being unable to process the situation fully.
**Example Use in Sentence:** "When she found out she won the lottery, she was totally shook."
# 中文: Shook
**定义：** 这个俚语用来形容某人被某事深深打动、震惊或情感上受到了强烈影响，以至于无法充分处理这种情况。
**例句使用：** "当她得知自己中了彩票时，她完全shook了。"
