## Shook
### English
**Definition:** Extremely impressed or shocked.
**Example Usage:** "I just heard that Taylor Swift is coming to our school, I'm totally shook!"
### Chinese
**定义:** 非常震惊或印象深刻。
**例句:** "我刚听说泰勒·斯威夫特要来我们学校了，我都被震到了！"
