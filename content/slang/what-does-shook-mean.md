## Shook (英语) / 碎了 (中文)
### English Explanation:
**Shook:** This slang term is commonly used to express that someone or something has shaken you to your core, whether in a positive or negative way. It can convey a strong emotional response, often indicating that something has profoundly affected the speaker. For example:
- "I was totally shook by her performance; it was just on point."
- "He's gonna be shook after the news; he'll have to regroup."
### Chinese Explanation:
**碎了 (Shuì le):** 这个网络用语用来表示某人或某事给了你极大的冲击，可能带来强烈的正面或负面情绪反应。它可以传达一种深刻的情感反应。例如：
- "她的表演彻底震碎了我，简直是太精彩了。”
- “听到这消息后，他会被震碎，需要好好调整一下。”
In both contexts, "shook" and "碎了" convey a sense of being deeply affected or emotionally stirred.
