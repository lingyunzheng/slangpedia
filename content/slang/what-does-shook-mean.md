### Shook
**English:** 
- **Definition:** Feels like someone has shaken your world or beliefs to its core, often in a negative or alarming way.
- **Example Use Case:** "After the scandal, his entire career was shook."
**Chinese:**
- **定义:** 指仿佛有人把你的世界观或信念摇晃得彻底颠覆，通常是一种令人不安或惊吓的感觉。
- **使用案例:** "在那个丑闻之后，他的整个职业生涯都被动摇了。"
