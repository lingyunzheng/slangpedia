## English: Shook
### Definition
"Shook" is a slang term commonly used to describe someone or something that is extremely impressive, shocking, or has had a strong emotional impact on someone.
### Example
- "She got engaged to her high school crush? That's totally shook!"
## 中文: Shook
### 释义
“Shook”是一个俚语，通常用来表示某人或某事给人的印象极其深刻、令人惊讶，或者对某人产生了强烈的情感冲击。
### 示例
- "她和高中的暗恋对象订婚了？这太震撼了！"
