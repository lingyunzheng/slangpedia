# Shook

## English
**Meaning:** When someone or something surprises you so much that it feels like they are physically shaking or shaking you up. It can also indicate deep emotion, shock, or a reevaluation of your thoughts and feelings about something.

**Example:** "When I heard about the fire at the studio, I was really shook."

## 中文
**意思:** 当某人或某事给你带来巨大的惊讶或冲击，以至于感觉像是你在摇晃或是被对方摇晃的情景。这个词也可以用来表示强烈的情感、震惊或是对你对某件事的看法的重新评估。

**例句:** "当我听到工作室发生火灾的消息时，我深受震动。"
