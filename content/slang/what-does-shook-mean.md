## Shook (US English / Slang)
**Meaning:** When something or someone has dramatically affected you emotionally or surprised you in a powerful way.
**Explanation:** It's like when a song hits you so hard you feel the beats physically, or you hear a story that emotionally shakes you to your core. You feel deeply affected by it, like the ground is literally moving under your feet.
**Example:**  
- **US English:** "I was shook when I found out my best friend was moving to another city. I can barely breathe right now."
- **Chinese (Simplified):** "当我发现我最好的朋友要搬到另一个城市时，我感觉心都碎了。我现在几乎喘不过气来。"
**Usage tip:** Use it when you want to emphasize a strong emotional reaction or a surprising experience.
```
## Shook (Hong Kong Chinese / Slang)
**Meaning:** When something or someone has dramatically affected you emotionally or surprised you in a powerful way.
**Explanation:** 在香港潮语中，“shook”意味着某件事或某人给你带来了强烈的情绪冲击或惊讶，让你感到非常震撼。
**Example:**  
- **US English:** "I was shook when I found out my best friend was moving to another city. I can barely breathe right now."
- **Chinese (Traditional/Hong Kong):** "我發現我最好的朋友搬到另一個城市，我感到非常震驚，現在幾乎喘不過氣來。"
**Usage tip:** 使用它來強調您對某種強烈的情緒反應或令人驚奇的經歷的感受。
