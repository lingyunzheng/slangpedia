## Shook
### English
**Meaning**: Deeply affected emotionally, typically in a negative way, feeling shocked or destabilized.
**Example**: "I just found out my favorite band broke up, I'm totally shook."
### 吵了
### Chinese
**Meaning**: 深深受到影响，通常是负面的方式，感到震惊或动摇。
**Example**: "我刚得知我最喜欢的乐队解散了，我真是受到打击了。"
