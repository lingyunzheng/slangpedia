# "Shook" Bilingual Slang Explanation
## In English
**Meaning:** To be emotionally shaken or deeply affected by something; to be very impressed or shocked.
**Example Usage:**  
- "When I heard about the earthquake, I was totally shook."
- "Seeing her perform live really shook me."
## In Chinese
**意思:** 被某事强烈触动或深深震惊；非常印象深刻。
**例句：**  
- "当我听到地震的消息时，我感到非常颠覆。" 
- "看到她的现场表演真是让人印象深刻。"
