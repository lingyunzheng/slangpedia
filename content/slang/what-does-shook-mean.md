## English: Shook
### Definition
- **Meaning**: An informal term used to express that someone or something has caused a strong emotional reaction or major disturbance.
- **Example**: "I just heard about the concert being cancelled, I am so shook."
## 中文解释
### 含义
- **意义**：一个非正式用语，用来表示某人或某事引起了强烈的情绪反应或重大震动。
- **示例**："我刚听说演唱会取消了，我真是吓坏了/震惊了。"
