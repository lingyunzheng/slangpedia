## English: Shook
### Chinese: 心碎 (xīn suì) / 被震到了 (bèi zhèn dào le)
**Explanation:**
- **English:** Feels like your insides are rattling. It's used to describe feeling shocked, scared, or overwhelmed by something, often to the point where you can't believe what you've just experienced.
- **Chinese:** 形容一种震惊或极度惊吓的感觉，仿佛心都碎了，或者被某种事情深深震撼。
**Example Sentences:**
- **English:** I was really shook when I found out my favorite band was breaking up!
- **Chinese:** 当我得知我最喜欢的乐队要解散的时候，我简直心如刀绞。
- **English:** I was totally shook when she told me she didn’t want to be friends anymore.
- **Chinese:** 她告诉我她不再想做朋友的时候，我简直被震撼到了。
