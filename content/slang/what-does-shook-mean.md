## Shook
### English
"Shook" is a slang term that indicates a strong emotional state, often one of shock, surprise, or being substantially affected by something. It can be used to express that something has significantly impacted the speaker.
### Chinese
“Shook” 是一种 slang 词汇，用来表示强烈的情感状态，通常是震惊、惊讶或被某事大幅影响。它可以用来表达某件事对说话者产生了显著的影响。
