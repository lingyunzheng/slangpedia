```markdown
## English: Shook
### Definition
Feeling or expressing extreme shock, surprise, or fear. This term is often used when someone or something has had a massive impact on the speaker's emotions.
### Example
- **English**: I was totally shook when I found out my best friend was cheating on me.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 我得知最好的朋友在跟我背后搞外遇的时候，简直被震惊到了。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 我得知最好的朋友在跟我背後搞外燚的时候，簡直被震驚到了。
### Usage in a Sentence
- **English**: You really shook me when you quit your job and moved to another city.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 你真的把我的心都震動了，辭職然後搬到另一個城市。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 你真的把我的心都震動了，辭職然後搬到另一個城市。
```

```markdown
## English: Shook
### Definition
This term is commonly used to express that someone or something has made you feel extremely surprised or shocked. It's often said with a raised voice and added emphasis for maximum effect.
### Example
- **English**: I was totally shook when I heard the results of the contest.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 听到比赛的结果时，我简直被吓坏了。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 听到比賽的結果時，我簡直被嚇壞了。
### Usage in a Sentence
- **English**: She was so shook by the news that she couldn't even speak.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 这个消息把她吓得说不出话来。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 这個消息把她嚇得說不出話來。
```

```markdown
## English: Shook
### Definition
Sometimes used to emphasize that something has completely changed someone's perspective or feelings, often dramatically so.
### Example
- **English**: That movie really shook my view of what is possible in Hollywood.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 那部电影彻底改变了我对好莱坞可能性的看法。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 那部電影徹底改變了我對荷里活可能性的觀點。
### Usage in a Sentence
- **English**: The new policy has really shaken up the industry.
- **Simplified Chinese**: 新政策彻底改变了这个行业的运作方式。
- **Traditional Chinese**: 新政策徹底改變了這個行業的運作方式。
```
