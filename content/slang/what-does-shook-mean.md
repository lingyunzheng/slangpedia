## Shook (English)
**Meaning:** Extremely shaken up, amazed, or disturbed. It can be used to describe both positive and negative strong emotional reactions.
**Example:** "The concert was so intense, I was totally shook."
## Shook (Chinese)
**意思:** 形容非常震惊、惊讶或受打击。它可以用来描述强烈的情绪反应，无论是积极的还是消极的。
**例句:** "演唱会太震撼了，我都快被shake了。"
