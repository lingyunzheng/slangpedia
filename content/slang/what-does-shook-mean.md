## Shook
### English
**Meaning:** To be deeply shaken or surprised, often to the point of feeling unstable or upset.
**Example:** "I just found out my ex got married, I'm totally shook."
### 中文
**含义:** 深深震动或惊讶，通常到了感觉不稳定或极度不安的程度。
**例句:** "我刚知道我前女友结婚了，我完全shook了。"
