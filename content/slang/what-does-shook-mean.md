## English Slang: "Shook"
### Meaning
Feeling extremely shaken, disturbed, or impressed to the core.
### Example
- _"I was shook after watching that concert! The energy was unreal!"_
### Chinese Slang: "热血沸腾" / "shè yuè fèi téng"
### Meaning
极度震撼，热血沸腾意味着被某种强烈的情感或经历深深触动，好像血液都烧沸了一样。
### Example
- _"我去看那个演唱会了，热血沸腾！感觉太燃了！"_
- Translation: "我去看了那个演唱会，热血沸腾！感觉太燃了！" (I went to that concert and I was totally shaken! The energy was unreal!)
