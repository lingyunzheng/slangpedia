## English: Shook
### Definition: 
A term used to convey that someone or something has had a strong emotional or surprising effect, often resulting in a state of shock or disbelief.
### Example Usage:
- "I just found out my favorite band broke up last year, I was totally shook."
- "When she ran in the race and finished first, I got shook by her speed."
## 中文: Shook
### 解释:
一个用来表达某人或某事产生了强烈的情感或惊讶效果，常导致震惊或不可置信的状态的俚语。
### 使用示例:
- "我刚刚得知我最喜欢的乐队去年解散了，我简直无法接受，超shake！"
- "她参加比赛并以第一名的成绩冲线，这让我超shake她的速度！"
