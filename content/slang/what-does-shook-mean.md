```markdown
## Shook (English)
**Meaning:** Extremely surprised or upset, often to the point of being shaken.
**Example Usage:** "When I heard she quit her job, I was totally shook!"
---
## 摇晃 (Yáoguǎng) (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 极度惊讶或受惊，常达到惊抖的程度。
**Example Usage:** "当我听说她辞职时，我简直摇晃了。"
```
In both English and Chinese, "shook" refers to a state of being extremely surprised or upset, often to the point where it feels like one is literally shaken or disturbed.
