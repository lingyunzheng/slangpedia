## Shook
- **English:** If something has "shook" you, it means it has made you feel very emotionally upset or scared. You might say "I was shook" after hearing some unbelievable news.
- **Chinese (简体中文):** 如果某件事让你"shook"，意味着这件事让你感到非常情绪激动或害怕。你可能会在听到一些难以置信的消息后说“I was shook”。 
**Example Usage:**
- English: "I was shook when I heard about the accident."
- Chinese: "我听事故的消息时，真的被吓坏了。"
