## English Slang: Shook
**Definition:** 
- **Meaning:** Feeling extremely shaken, scared, or upset. It can also denote that someone has been profoundly affected by an event or experience.
**Use Case:**
- "I went to that party and saw my ex with someone else, I was totally shook!"
**Markdown Explanation:**
Shook is commonly used to describe a state of extreme emotional disturbance or being deeply affected by something. It can be used in situations where a person has experienced a shock, surprise, or trauma, leading to a strong emotional reaction.
```
## Chinese Slang: 震动 (zhèndòng)
**定义:**
- **含义:** 强烈的情感冲击或被某事深深震撼。可以用于描述某人经历了震惊、惊讶或创伤，导致强烈的情感反应。
- **使用场景:**
- "我去那个聚会的时候看到了我前任和别人在一起，我简直震惊了！"
**Markdown 解释:**
震动（zhèndòng）常用来描述强烈的情感冲击或被某事深深震撼。可以用在某人经历了震惊、惊讶或创伤，导致强烈的情感反应的场合。
```
