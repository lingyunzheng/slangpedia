## English: "Shook"
### Definition: Slang term expressing intense disturbance, shock, or heightened emotion.
#### Example Usage:
- "She heard the news about her ex and she was **shook**."
- "The performance was so intense, the crowd was all **shook**."
## 中文: "Shook"
### 定义: 表达强烈的震惊、冲击或情绪激动的俚语。
#### 使用示例：
- "她听说了关于她前男友的消息，整个人都**震了**。"
- "表演太精彩了，观众们都**震了**。"
