### Shook (Shook)
**English Definition:**
When someone is "shook" in slang, it means they are extremely shocked, surprised, or emotionally disturbed by something they have experienced or seen.
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **中文解释:** "Shook" 表示某人被某些事情震惊、大吃一惊，或者是经历了某种事情后深受情感上的冲击。
- **Example:** "I was totally shook when I heard the news about her quitting her job."
- **中文例句:** 当我听说她辞职的消息时，我完全被震惊了。
