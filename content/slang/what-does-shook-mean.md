```markdown
## English: Shook
**Definition:** Someone or something that is severely impacted; shaken to the core.
**Usage Example:** "Man, when she found out he was married, she was totally SHOOK."
## 中文：Shook
**释义：** 指某人或某事物受到了极为强烈的影响；被彻底震撼。
**使用示例：** "当他告诉她他已经结婚了，她简直被打得七荤八素了。"
```
This slang term is used to describe a situation where someone is deeply affected, often in a negative or surprising way.
