## English
### Shook
- **Definition**: Feeling extremely surprised, shocked, or emotionally affected.
- **Example**: "She saw the news about the earthquake and shook at how many lives were lost."
## 中文
### Shook
- **释义**：极度感到惊讶、震惊或情绪受到影响。
- **例句**："她看到关于地震的消息，感到非常震惊，为那些失去生命的人而心痛。"
