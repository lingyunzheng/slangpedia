## English: Shook
### Chinese: 振撼
**Explanation:** 
In English, "shook" is often used to describe a powerful emotional reaction or to convey that someone is extremely affected by something that happened. It's generally used in an excited or negative context. For example, if someone tells a very shocking or intense story, another person might say, "I was totally shook by that!"
In Chinese, 振撼 (zhènhàn) is similar in meaning. It is often used to describe a profound disturbance or a strong emotional impact. For instance, 振撼人心 (zhènhàn rénxīn) means "touching and stirring," describing something that deeply affects people emotionally.
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "When I heard about the scandal, I was totally shook!"
- **Chinese:** “当我听说那个丑闻时，我被震撼了！" (Dāng wǒ tīngshuō Nàgè chǒunwén shí, wǒ bèi zhènhàn le!)
