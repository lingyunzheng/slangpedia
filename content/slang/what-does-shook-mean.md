# Shook
## English
**Definition:** Extremely surprised or shocked.
**Example Sentence:** "When I heard the news, I literally got shook!"
## 中文
**定义：** 极其震惊或受惊。
**例句：** "当我听到这个消息时，我简直被打动到‘shook’！"
