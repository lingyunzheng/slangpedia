# Slang Term : shook
## English
- **Definition:** Feeling or expressing shock, disbelief, or an overwhelming emotional response.
- **Example:** "Dude, I just lost in a video game to a friend I thought was worse than me, I was totally shook!"
## Pinyin & Tone Marking (for Chinese approximation)
- **Pronunciation:** 敢 (gǎn)
- **Definition:** 感到震惊、难以置信或强烈的情绪反应。
- **Example:** “哥们儿，我刚在一个游戏中被一个我以为比我差的朋友打败了，我真是震惊了！”
