## Shook (English)
- **Meaning**: Extremely shocked, surprised, or disturbed.
- **Example Usage**: "I just got the news that my favorite band is breaking up, I'm totally shook."
## Shook (Chinese)
- **含义**: 非常震惊、惊讶或受惊。
- **用例**: “我刚得知我最喜欢的乐队要解散了，我都被吓蒙了。”
