## Shook
**English:**  
- Definition: Completely shaken, often in a negative way. Can describe emotions ranging from fear to anger, or even disbelief and astonishment.
- Example: "I was shook when I heard the news about the scandal."
**Chinese:**  
- 含义：完全被震动了，通常带有负面的情感，可以形容从恐惧到愤怒，甚至是震惊和难以置信的情绪状态。
- 例句：当听说那个丑闻时，我完全被震动了。
