## English Slang: Shook
**Definition:** Absolutely stunned or shocked by something intense or unexpected.
**Example Usage:** "I was so shook after hearing the news that my favorite band is going on tour!"
## 中文 slang: Shook
**定义:** 在听到或看到一些突如其来的强烈信息后感到极度震撼或震惊。
**例句:** "得知我最喜爱的乐队要巡演了，我简直不敢相信，真是太 shook 了！"
