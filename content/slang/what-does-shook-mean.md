```markdown
## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Shook
**English:**
- **Meaning:** Feeling extremely shaken or upset by an event or piece of news.
- **Example Usage:** "I heard about the crash; I'm so shook."
**Chinese:**
- **意思:** 一个被某个事件或消息深深震动或不安的状态。
- **例句:** “我听说那个车祸的事儿了，我太难受了。”
```
This markdown format clearly delineates the meaning and usage in both English and Chinese, providing an easy reference for users.
