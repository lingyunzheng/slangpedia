## Shook (英 / ʃaʊk / 中 "被搞乱了")
### English Explanation
"Shook" is a slang term that means someone or something is deeply impressed, shocked, or affected by an event or situation. It's commonly used to express that something has had a big impact on someone, whether it's positive or negative.
**Examples:**
- "I was totally shook when I saw her perform on stage."
- "The news shook the entire community to its core."
### 中文解释
"Shook" 是一个口头禅，用于表示某人或某事被深深地打动、震惊或受到某个事件或情况的影响。它常用来表达某件事对某人产生了重要的影响，无论是积极的还是消极的。
**例句：**
- "当我看见她在舞台上表演时，我完全被弄乱了。"
- "这个消息把整个社区都搞乱了。"
