## Shook (英式/美式俚语)
### English
**Shook**
- **Meaning:** Extremely shocked or affected.
- **Explanation:** When someone is "shook," they are deeply impacted by something, often to the point where it has noticeably altered their mood or view.
### Chinese
**shook**
- **意思:** 非常震惊或受到强烈影响。
- **解释:** 当某人感到“shook”时，他们受到了极大的冲击，这种影响往往足以显著改变他们的情绪或观点。
