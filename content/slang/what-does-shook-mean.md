# Shook (English/Chinese)
## English
**Shook:** An expression used to indicate that something has had a major impact on someone, often resulting in a state of shock or surprise. It can also describe a situation that is so impressive or shocking, it changes one's perception of a person or a scenario.
*Example:* "Wow, I just found out my favorite actor is getting divorced! I'm so shook."
## 中文
**Shook:** 这个短语用来表示某事给某人带来了巨大的冲击，通常会导致震惊或惊讶的状态。它也可以用来描述一种令人印象深刻或震惊的情况，以至于改变了对某人或某种情况的看法。
*示例:* "哇，我刚刚得知我最喜欢的演员要离婚了！我好震惊。"
