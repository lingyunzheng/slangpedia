# Shook (英语: Shook)
**英语解释:** 
Shook is a slang term that originates from African American Vernacular English (AAVE) and is commonly used to describe a strong emotional or physical impact, often negative. It can indicate feeling emotionally shocked, disturbed, or upset. For example, saying "I was shook after hearing the news" means you experienced significant shocking emotions.
**中文解释:** 
"shook" 是一种来源于美式英语非主流黑人方言（African American Vernacular English, AAVE）的俚语，常用来形容强烈的情感或身体上的影响，通常为负面的。它可以表示感到强烈震惊、惊吓或不安。比如，“我在听到这个消息后感到非常震惊”就相当于“I was shook after hearing the news”。
