# English: Shook
## Definition
When someone or something is "shook," it means they are really affected by something, often in a negative or surprising way. It can also suggest the person is completely overwhelmed, shocked, or shaken up.
## Example Usage
- "She was so shook after finding out about her friend's diagnosis."
- "The news shook the entire community to its core."
# 中文: 抖 (Shook)
## 释义
当某人或某事被描述为“shook”时，意味着他们受到了某种强烈的影响，通常是负面的或令人惊讶的。也可以表示这个人被彻底震惊或强烈动摇。
## 使用示例
- "她得知朋友的诊断后非常震惊。"
- "这个消息把整个社区都震动了。"
