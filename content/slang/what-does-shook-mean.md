# "Shook" (English to Chinese)
## English: **Shook**
## Chinese: **颤抖 (chàn dǒu)**
**Explanation:**
- **English:** "Shook" is an informal slang term used to express surprise, shock, or intense emotion. It's often used in situations that are so surprising or dramatic that they cause someone to shake or feel physically affected by the intensity of the event or situation.
- **中文:** "颤抖 (chàn dǒu)" 这个非正式俚语用来表达惊讶、震惊或强烈的情感。通常用于描述一些令人极为惊讶或戏剧性的情况，以至于让人感到震动或因为事件的强烈程度而产生生理上的反应。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I was shook when I saw her with another man. It was so unexpected!"
- **中文:** "当我看到她和另一个男人在一起时，我简直震惊了。这完全是我意料之外的事！"
This slang is often used in texts, social media, or casual conversations among friends.
