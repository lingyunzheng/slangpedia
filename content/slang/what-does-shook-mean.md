## English Slang: Shook
**Definition:**
- **Meaning:** When someone is so shocked, surprised, or emotionally affected by something that it changes their mindset or behavior.
- **Example Usage:** "I was totally shook when I saw the bill, it was way more expensive than I thought!"
## Bilingual Slang: 打晃 (Dǎhuǎng)
**Definition:**
- **Meaning:** 指某人被某种消息或事件震惊到，以至于改变了原有的想法或行为。通常带有强烈的情感色彩。
- **Example Usage:** "我看到账单的时候简直打晃了，比我想象的贵多了！" (Wǒ kàndào zhàngdiàn de huìshí jíběn dǎhuǎng le, bǐ wǒ xiǎngxiàng de guì le duō le! — I was totally shook when I saw the bill, it was way more expensive than I thought!)
