# Shook
## English
**Meaning:** This slang term is often used to describe a state of shock, surprise, or being deeply affected by something to the point where one feels unsteady or upset.
**Example Sentence:** "I was totally shook when I found out the concert was cancelled."
## 中文
**含义:** 这个 slang 词汇通常用来表示震惊、惊讶，或者是被某样东西深深影响到，感到不安或者失衡的状态。
**例句:** "当我得知演唱会取消时，我完全 shook 了。"
