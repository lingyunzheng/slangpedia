# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Shook"
**English:**  
- **Definition:** Being or making someone deeply shocked or changed.  
- **Usage Example:** "When I saw the score, I was totally shook!"
**Chinese (Simplified):**  
- **释义:** 深深的震惊或改变，使人大吃一惊。  
- **使用示例:** "当我看到比分时，我简直被震住了！"
