## Situationship (中文: 小情况关系)
### English Explanation:
"Situationship" is a playful term that refers to a kind of ambiguous or undefined relationship. It's often used when two people are in a complex or unclear romantic or social situation, but are not officially dating or committed. It's a situation where the boundaries of the relationship are not clearly defined.
### Chinese Explanation:
"小情况关系"是一种轻松的说辞，指的是那种模糊不清或不确定的恋爱关系。当两个人处在一种复杂的或不明确的恋爱或社交状态下，但还未正式成为情侣或承诺时，可以用这个词来形容。这种关系中的边界并不清晰定义。
