## Situationship (English - Simplified Chinese)
**Simplified Chinese:** 状态恋爱（Zhàntàì Àilàng）
**Explanation:** This is a playful or informal term that translates to "status quo dating." It describes a situation in a relationship where the partners maintain a romantic or semi-romantic connection but avoid making any long-term commitments. They might date in a casual way but keep things flexible, often using it as a way to explore attraction without fully entering into a commitment. 
**Example Usage:**
- "They're in a situationship, so they enjoy the fun without the pressure of a real commitment."
- "It's a good way to test out if you're in a serious relationship or just having a situationship."
