# Situationship
**Chinese (简体中文):** 现实中的那段时间，双方可能既不算正式伴侣，又无法确定是否最终能成为正式伴侣，处于一种朦胧暧昧却又不得不共处的关系。通常可翻译为“一种介于朋友和情侣之间的复杂关系”。
**English:** A "situationship" is a relationship that exists partly or mostly in the real world where two people are neither officially dating nor have sorted out their relationship status. It's often described as being between friendship and a romantic partnership, where both parties are unclear about the future but still obligated to spend time together. Commonly, it's like when two friends start experimenting with being more than friends but haven't decided if they want it to be more serious.
