## Situationship (英语: Situationship)
### What is it?
"Situationship" is a portmanteau of the words "situation" and "relationship." It is a casual term used to describe a situation where two people are acting like they are in a romantic relationship, but they aren't officially dating. This can include activities like going out together, texting, or caring about each other's day-to-day lives, but without the commitment or exclusivity of an actual relationship.
### English Example:
"Joey and Emily started hanging out a lot after their mutual friend set them up on a date, and now they're in a situationship. They talk to each other every day, but neither of them has said anything about being exclusive."
### 中文解释：
“Situationship”这个词是“situation”和“relationship”的合成词，用来描述一种状态，即两个人的行为表现为情侣，但实际上并非正式的恋爱关系。这种关系可能包括一起去外面活动、发信息交流、关心对方的生活，但没有正式的承诺或排他性。
### 中文例句：
"Joey 和 Emily 在他们共同朋友安排的约会后就开始了很多次相聚，现在他们处于一种 situationship 中。他们每天都会彼此联系，但谁也没有说过变得排他的话。"
