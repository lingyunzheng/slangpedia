# Situationship (Situa-tion-ship)
## 中文解释
"situationship" 是一个结合了 "situational" 和 "relationship" 的新词汇，用来描述那种因为某种特定情境而建立的、不稳定的或暂时的恋爱关系。这种关系可能没有明确的长期承诺，通常由于工作、朋友介绍或是共处的环境而形成。
## 英文解释
A "situationship" combines "situational" and "relationship" into one word, used to describe a type of unstable or temporary romantic relationship that is formed due to a specific situation. This kind of relationship often lacks clear long-term commitments and can occur due to work, friend introductions, or shared environments.
