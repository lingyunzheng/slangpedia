## Situationship
**English:**  
"Situationship" is a playful blend of "situation" and "relationship," often used to describe a romantic or sexual relationship that lacks mutual commitment or clear boundaries. It's like when you're in a confusing lover's position that doesn't quite fit any standard relationship description.
**中文解释：**  
"Situationship" 是 "situation" 和 "relationship" 两个词的巧妙结合，通常用来描述一种既不太符合传统定义的浪漫关系，也不完全是单纯的性关系。它指的是那种缺乏明确承诺或边界的关系，处于一种模棱两可的状态。
