# Situashion (situation + relationship)
## English Explanation
A colloquial term used to describe a semi-relationship or a potential relationship that is evolving but isn't quite established or formal. It often implies a complex, ambiguous, and possibly uncomfortable interpersonal dynamic between two people where there's chemistry and interest but no clear definition of their status.

A menudo implica una dinámica interpersonala compleja, ambigua y posiblemente incómoda entre dos personas que tienen química e interés, pero sin una definición clara de su estado.
