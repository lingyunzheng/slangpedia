```markdown
# Situationship (关系)
## Chinese (中文)
**定义**: 在新型恋爱关系中，当双方明确知道自己处于一种不确定、非正式的状态，但又不愿或无法结束这种关系时，这种状态被称为“关系”。这类关系通常没有明确的时间线或目标，意在保持某种亲密感和接近感，但又避免了正式恋爱关系带来的责任和束缚。
**解释**: 两人可能在一起一段时间，但双方都不愿意或还没有准备好表白或者分手。这种状态用“关系”来描述，是一种介于朋友和情人之间的暧昧状态，通常会通过频繁的沟通来维持。
## English
**Definition**: In a modern dating context, if two people are in an unclear, informal relationship where they are aware that they are in this situation but are not ready or willing to end it, they may describe this state as a "relationship." Such a relationship lacks a clear timeline or goals, aimed at maintaining a certain level of intimacy and closeness, but avoiding the responsibilities and constraints of a formal romantic relationship.
**Explanation**: Two people might be together for a while, but neither of them is ready or willing to make things official or break up. Describing the situation as a "relationship" helps to keep a level of closeness without the serious commitment that comes with a formal relationship. This often involves frequent communication to maintain the understanding and comfort level between the two parties.
```
Note: The term "situationship" is a playful combination of "situation" and "relationship," and it's used to describe this ambiguous and sometimes confusing state of being.
