# Situationship (Situation + Relationship)
## English
**Definition:** A casual or undefined romantic or sexual dynamic that is still in a developing stage, but lacks clear commitment or boundaries. Often used when the situation is complex or unclear.
**Example:** "Tom and Lisa are in a situationship because they enjoy going on dates, but neither wants to take it to the next level."
## Chinese (简体中文)
**定义:** 一种不明确的浪漫或性关系，正处于发展阶段，但缺乏明确的承诺或界限。通常用于描述情况复杂或不清楚时的关系状态。
**例句:** "汤姆和丽莎处于一种‘situation relationship’中，因为他们享受约会，但两人都不想将其进一步发展。"
