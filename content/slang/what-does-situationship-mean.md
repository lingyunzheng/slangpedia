## Situationship (中文: 情况关系)
**Definition:**
Situationship is a blend of the words "situation" and "relationship." It typically refers to a complex and often confusing relationship or dynamic between two people, where there's a mutual understanding that something is going on, but it's not quite a typical dating or romantic relationship. It may involve feelings of affection or attraction without clear boundaries or commitments.
**Example:**
- In English: "John and Jane have been in a situationship for months. They enjoy spending time together, but neither of them wants to label their relationship as anything official."
- In Chinese: "约翰和简有一种情况关系，已经持续了几个月了。他们很享受彼此相处的时光，但都没有打算给这段关系一个确切的定义。"
