# Situationship (Situation + Relationship)
## English
A situation where a person has feelings for someone but the relationship is not moving forward or is unlikely to be romantically compatible. Often used to describe a relationship that remains informal and friend-like despite mutual feelings of affection.
### Example
- "I wish it could be more than just a situationship with him, but it seems like we're both just getting along as friends."
## 中文
一种状态指的是一个人对另一个人有感情，但双方的关系并没有进展，或者从浪漫角度来看并非兼容的。常用来形容一种保持在非正式、朋友式关系而双方都有好感的情况。
### 例子
- "希望能够从友情上升为恋爱关系，但看起来我们之间只能维持朋友的状态。"
