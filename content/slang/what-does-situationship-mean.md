## Situationship (Situation + Relationship)
### English
"Situationship" is a blend of "situation" and "relationship" and is used to describe a type of romantic or sexual relationship that is not clearly defined. It often includes vague boundaries, uncertain roles, or situations where people are involved in some level of intimacy without a clear commitment level.
Example: "John and Sarah have been in a situationship for a while now, which means they're occasionally involved but don't really know where they stand in terms of future plans together."
### Chinese
“Situationship”是指“情境”和“关系”的结合词，用来描述一段既不是明确的恋爱关系又不是单纯的性关系的暧昧状态。这种关系通常涉及到模糊的界限、不确定的角色定义或双方在某种程度上亲密但未来计划未明确的情况。
示例：约翰和莎拉已经处在一段烂糊关系里一段时间了，这意味着他们偶尔会有亲密行为，但两个人并不清楚自己在未来的计划里处于什么位置。
