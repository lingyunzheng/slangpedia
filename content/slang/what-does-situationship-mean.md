# Situationship
## English
A playful way to describe a situationally defined relationship. It's often used when the nature of a relationship is not clearly romantic, friendly, or_enemy relationship_. This term encompasses a broad range of ambiguous dynamics between people, like when you're not exactly dating but also not just friends with benefits.
## Chinese
一种用来描述关系模棱两可状态的轻松说法，通常是指关系不够明确，不是纯粹的爱情关系，也不是简单的友谊或敌对关系。这句话涵盖了人与人之间多种模糊关系类型，比如你们之间不是纯粹的恋爱关系，也不是单纯的情侣关系，而是介于两者之间的一种状态。
