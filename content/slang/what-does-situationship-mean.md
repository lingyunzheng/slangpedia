## Situationship
**English:**  
A term often used when two people are in a complex romantic or sexual situation, but their relationship status is not clear or defined. It can also refer to situations where someone is pretending to be in a relationship to avoid dating other people.
**Chinese:**  
一个用来形容两个人在一种复杂的情感或性关系中，但他们的关系状态既不明确也不固定的情况。这个词也可能指代某人为了躲避约会而假装自己已经有伴侣的情形。
