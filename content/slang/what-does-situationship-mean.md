# Situationship
## English
A slang term for a type of relationship that is not quite a full-fledged relationship but also doesn't resemble a friendship. It often involves two people seeing each other frequently or doing things together, but there's no clear commitment or romantic expectation. It's like being in a relationship but not in one.
Example: "It's a bit of a situationship with Sarah - we go out for drinks a lot, but we haven't said 'it's a relationship'."
## 中文
一个用来形容一种既不是真正的恋爱关系，又不像普通朋友的关系。通常指两个人很频繁地见面或一起做事情，但双方都没有明确的承诺或浪漫期待。就像是处于一种恋爱关系，但又不是那种关系。
例句：“和莎拉之间有点不太确定的‘关系’—我们经常一起去酒吧，但从来没有说过这是我们的关系。”
