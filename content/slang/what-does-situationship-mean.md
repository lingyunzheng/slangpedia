# Situationship ( Situationship )
## English
- **Definition:** A vague, undefined romantic relationship where the boundaries and expectations are unclear.
- **Example Usage:** "I'm not sure if we're dating or just friends, but it's become a situationship."
## Chinese
- **定义:** 一种模糊的、不确定的恋爱关系，其中的边界和预期都不明确。
- **例句:** "我也不清楚我们是恋爱还是仅限朋友，但这已经演变成了一个situationship。"
