## Situationship (English)
**Definition:** A vague, undefined, or complicated romantic or sexual relationship that is not officially a "date" or "relationship," but isn't quite a friendship either.
**Example:** "I guess I'm in a situationship with him, since we hang out sometimes but neither of us knows how to make it official."
## 情况关系 (Pinyin: Qíngkuàng Gànxì) (Simplified Chinese)
**Definition:** 模棱两可或复杂的浪漫或性关系，既不是正式的“约会”或“关系”，也不是纯粹的朋友关系。
**Example:** “我大概和他有一种情况关系，因为我们有时会一起玩，但双方都不知道自己是什么关系。”
