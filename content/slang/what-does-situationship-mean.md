# Situationship (中美双语俚语解释)
## 中文解释：
**情境恋爱**
两个人因为某种特定的情境或者暂时的需要而产生的恋爱关系，类似于试婚或试爱的关系。他们可能并没有深厚的感情基础，更多是在一段特定的情况或环境中互相吸引或者相互利用。
## English Explanation:
**Situationship**
A relationship that occurs between two people due to a specific situation or temporary need, rather than a deep emotional connection. It can be akin to a trial marriage or trial relationship. They might not have a strong emotional foundation, but are attracted to or benefit each other in a particular situation or setting.
