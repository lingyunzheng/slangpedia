## Situationship (友谊的一种特殊形式)
### 中文解释
“友谊的一种特殊形式”指的是超越普通朋友关系，但又不如情侣或伴侣关系的那种朦胧、不太确定的情感状态。常用来形容两个人之间复杂的情感纠葛，或者双方心里有特殊的情感，但又不愿或不知道如何明确表达或行动。
### 英文解释
"Situationship" is a special form of friendship that goes beyond the normal friendship but is not quite romantic or dating. It's often used to describe a complex emotional state between two people, where both have some special feelings for each other but are uncertain or unwilling to define or act on those feelings.
