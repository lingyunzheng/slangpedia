# Situationship (Situation + Relationship)
## English
**Definition:** A complex, often confusing, and undefined relationship. It's a situation where two or more people are involved in a relationship that lacks clear parameters, boundaries, and expectations. 
**Example:** "John and Sarah started out as friends but are now in a situationship. They go out together, but they're not sure if they're dating or just buddies."
## 中文
**定义:** 一种复杂、常带困惑且关系不够明确的关系。这是一种两者或多人之间存在的关系，缺乏清晰的界限和预期。
**例句:** "约翰和莎拉开始只是朋友，但现在他们处于一种‘情敌关系’。他们一起出去玩，但不清楚这是在谈恋爱还是只是朋友。"
