## Situationship: 现实关系
**English:**  
"Situationship" is a portmanteau of "situation" and "relationship." It refers to a situation where two people are in a casual, irregular, or undefined relationship, without clear boundaries or expectations. It can often be used to describe a situation in which there is a mix of friendship and romance, but without the commitment or labels that come with a traditional relationship.
**中文:**  
"situationship" 是“situation”和“relationship”的混成词，指的是两个人之间存在一种非正式、不明确或无定形的关系，没有明确的界限或期望。常常用来描述一种既有友情又有恋爱感觉，却没有传统关系中承诺和标签的情况。
