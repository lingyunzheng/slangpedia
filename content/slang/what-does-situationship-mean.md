## Situationship (英语: Situationship)
### English Explanation
A messy, undefined romantic or sexual relationship that lacks clear labels or defined boundaries. It often describes a type of relationship marked by ambiguity, where things are not clearly defined as a girlfriend/boyfriend setup or a one-time hook-up, but something in between.
### Chinese Explanation
一种模糊不清、定义不明的浪漫或性关系。它经常描述一种关系，这种关系带有模糊性，没有明确的女朋友/男友设定或一次性约会，而是一种介于两者之间的状态。情侣之间的情况往往不明确，可能存在情感依赖但又缺乏承诺感。
### Usage Example
- **English:** "Is this a thing or just a casual meetup? We've been in this situationship for months!"
- **Chinese:** "这算是正式的恋情还是随便见个面？我们俩这种‘情况’已经好几个月了！"
