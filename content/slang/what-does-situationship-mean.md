# Situationship
## English
A term often used for a vague or unclear romantic or sexual relationship. It implies that the people involved are in a situation that's not quite a real relationship but also not just friends. It's like being "kinda-sorta dating" but without the clear commitment.
## Chinese
一个用来形容一种含糊不清的浪漫或性伴侣关系的术语。它暗示双方的关系不算是一个真正的恋爱关系，但也绝对不是单纯的朋友关系。有点像“kinda-sorta dating”(就是那种模糊的恋爱关系)，但缺乏明确的承诺。
