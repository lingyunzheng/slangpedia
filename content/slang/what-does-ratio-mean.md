# What does "ratio" mean?（ratio 是什么意思？）

## **English Meaning:**  
Ratio is a slang term that originated from social media and forums, commonly used to express a comparison or contrast between people or things, often in a teasing or mockingly friendly manner.

**中文解释：**  
Ratio 是一个起源于社交媒体和论坛的俚语，通常用来表示人们对人或事物之间的比较或对比，经常带有一种戏谑或友好的讽刺意味。

### Examples / 例句
1) English sentence: "You just got six inches on him, don't choke the ratio, dude."  
   中文翻译: "你才比他多出了六英寸，别占太大的便宜，哥们。”

2) English sentence: "It’s just about showing that you are in that league with others, that you have no issues or are comfortable with it."  
   中文翻译: “这只是关于展示你与他人在同一个层次上，你对此没有意见或者感到自在。”

### Origin / 来源背景
The term "ratio" in this context is believed to have started as a playful way to emphasize one's superiority or to compare two individuals or achievements. It often appears in contexts where people are discussing their skills, possessions, or experiences online.

1. Tease
2. Jeer
3. Mock
4. Outdo
