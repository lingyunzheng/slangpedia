### Ratio (English)
**Definition:** In slang, "ratio" often refers to the comparison of the positive or negative attributes of two things, typically used to criticize or compliment someone's appearance, behavior, or possessions.
**Example:** "That guy has a bad ratio, you should probably stay away from him."
### 比率 (Bǐlǜ) (Chinese)
**Definition:** 在俚语中，“比率”通常用来指两个人或事物在某些方面的对比，往往用于批评或赞美某人的外貌、行为或财产。
**例句:** "那个家伙的比率不太好，你最好还是离他远点。"
