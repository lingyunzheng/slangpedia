## Ratio (English)
**Definition:** A comparison of two things, often used to show that one is much better or worse than the other. It can also imply that something is overrated or not worth the attention, especially on social media platforms.
**Example:** "This video has a higher ratio of likes to views than mine, it's really overhyped!"
## 比例 (Pinyin: bǐ lì, Chinese)
**Definition:** 比例通常指的是两者的对比关系，特别是用以表明一方远比另一方更好或更差。它还可能暗示某事不值得过多关注，特别是在社交媒体平台上。
**例句:** “这条视频的点赞与观看比例远高于我的，真的被过分吹捧了！”
