```markdown
### Ratio 🇺🇸 : 关系 🇨🇳
**English: Ratio**
- **Definition:** A comparison of two quantities, often used to describe the fairness or imbalance in a situation.
- **Slang Use:** Typically used in social media contexts or among friends to indicate that one person should apologize for a perceived slight or injustice. Often followed by a demand for that person to "do the ratio."
- **Example:** "John always cuts in line. He needs to do the ratio."
**汉语: 关系**
- **释义:** 比率或比例，通常用来描述某种情况下的公平性或失衡状况。
- **俚语用法:** 常常用于社交媒体或朋友间的对话中，来暗示一方应该为一次感知到的轻慢或不公进行道歉。经常以“do the ratio”（还个公道）的形式出现。
- **例句:** "每次队列时，约翰总是插队。他需要还个公道。"
```
