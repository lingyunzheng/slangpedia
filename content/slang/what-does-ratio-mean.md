## Ratio
**English:**  
"Ratio" in slang contexts often refers to a way of comparing or disadvantaged competition. It can also be used to describe a situation where someone is easily angered or likes to engage in online confrontations, often seen in social media or gaming communities. 
**Chinese:**  
“ratio” 在网络流行语中通常指的一种比较方式，或者不公平的竞争。它也可以用来形容某人很容易生气，或者喜欢参与线上争执，常常见于社交媒体或游戏社区中。
