# Ratio (Chinese: 比较 / 比拼)
## English Usage
- **Definition**: Ratio is often used as slang to describe a situation where people are comparing themselves to others, especially in terms of achievements, assets, or lifestyle. It can also be used to describe a confrontational situation where one party challenges another to a duel or competition.
- **Example**: "John won the game last night, and now everyone is trying to calculate ratio with him to see how much better he performs."
## Chinese Explanation
- **含义**: 比较/比拼通常作为非正式的俚语，用来描述人们相互比较自己的成就、财产或生活方式的情况。它还可以用来描述一种挑战性质的情况，其中一方向另一方发出决斗或竞赛的挑战。
- **例句**: “约翰昨晚赢得了比赛，现在每个人都试图与他比较，看看他在各方面如何表现得更好。”
