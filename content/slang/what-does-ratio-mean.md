# Ratio (English-Chinese Slang)
## English
**Ratio**: A way to express the comparison between two things, often in a negative context. It describes how one thing is significantly better or worse than another.
For example: "That brand's customer service is way out of ratio with their product quality." Here, the speaker is saying the customer service quality is much worse than the product quality.
## Chinese
**比例（ratio）**：用来比较两件事情的一种方式，通常是在一个负面的语境中。它描述了一件事与另一件事之间悬殊的差距。
例如："这家品牌的客户服务的质量与他们的产品质量相比差出不是一点半点比例。" 这里说话的人的意思是客户服务的质量远远不如他们的产品质量。
