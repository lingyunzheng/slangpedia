# Ratio (ratio)
**English:** Ratio is a slang term often used to compare the perceived value or quality of something or someone. It can also be a way to express feeling dissatisfied with a situation.
**Chinese:** Ratio 是一个网络俚语，常用来比较某事物或某人的感知价值或质量。也可以用来表达对某种情况感到不满。
Example Usage:
- English: "I can’t believe you went to that restaurant; the food was just soRatio."
- Chinese: "我简直不敢相信你去了那个餐厅；食物真的Ratio得不行。"
Translation Explanation:
- The word "Ratio" here carries the sense of "not worth it" or "deserves little to no consideration," implying that the food or experience was subpar.
