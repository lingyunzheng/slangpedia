## Ratio (俚语解释)
**中文 (中文解释):** 比率；输赢；面对面的较量或竞赛。
**英文 (英文解释):** 
1. **Ratio (in a general sense):** The comparison of two amounts showing how much bigger one quantity is compared to another.
2. **Win/Loss or Comparison:** A term often used in online contexts, particularly on social media, to refer to a disagreement or challenge where one person tries to prove that they are superior to another. It can also refer to the result of such an exchange, where the "better" person is considered to have a "good ratio" and the "worse" person has a "bad ratio."
**示例 (Example):** 
中文：他在那个帖子下面收到了很多的ratio，因为他输了那场辩论。
英文：He received a lot of ratio under that post because he lost the debate.
