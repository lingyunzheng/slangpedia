# ratio (ratio)
**English:** A ratio is often used to describe the comparison of two things, typically in the context of something being better than another. For example, if someone has better gaming skills than another, they might say "The ratio is 10:1" to mean they are vastly superior.
**中文:** 在中文中，“ratio”通常用来形容两者之间的比较，在某些情况下特指某事物明显优于另一事物。比如当有人说自己打游戏的技术比别人好时，可能会说“比例是10:1”，意思就是自己要比对方强得多。
例如：
- English: His reaction time is so fast, the ratio of his skill to mine is 10:1.
- 中文: 他的反应速度特别快，技能跟我比简直是10:1的比例。
