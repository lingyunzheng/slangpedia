## Ratio (English)
### Explanation:
**Ratio**: A term often used in social media and online communities to express the desire for a positive outcome compared to a negative one, usually in situations involving conflict or comparison. When someone says they want a "good ratio," it means they want more positive comments, interactions, or support compared to negative ones.
### Example:
- *In a heated online debate*: "I really want a good ratio on this post, especially after those trolling me earlier."
---
## 比率 (Bilingual)
### 解释：
**Ratio**: 一个常用于社交媒体和网上社区的词汇，用来表达希望正面结果多于负面结果的愿望，特别是在冲突或比较的情况下。当有人希望“有好比率”时，就意味着他们希望正面的评论、互动或支持多于负面的。
### 示例：
- *在一个激烈的网上辩论中*: "我真的希望这篇帖子有好比率，尤其是那些早些时候围攻我的人之后。"
