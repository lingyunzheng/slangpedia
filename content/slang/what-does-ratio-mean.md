### Ratio (Chinese: 比例; Pinyin: Bǐlì)
#### English Slang Explanation:
"Ratio" is often used in internet culture, especially in social media and forums, to describe a conflict or argument between two people or groups. When someone is said to be "in a ratio," it implies they are engaged in a heated debate, especially on social media platforms, that has escalated to a point where it might draw considerable attention or is being viewed as a spectacle. The term can also refer to a less severe disagreement.
#### Chinese Slang Explanation:
“比例”这个词在网络文化中,特别是在社交媒体和论坛中,用于描述两个或多个群体之间发生的冲突或争吵。当有人说某人“处于比率状态”时，这意味着他们在社交媒体平台上进行的辩论已经激化到可能引起广泛注意或被认为是一场热闹的情况。这个词也可能指一个较为轻微的争论或争执。
