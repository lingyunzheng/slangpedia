# Ratio
## English
**Ratio**: This term can be used in a few different contexts, mainly as a playful way to describe a confrontation or comparison between two people or things. It originated from betting terminology, referring to the potential financial gain from a bet. In slang, it's often used to describe a situation where someone is trying to one-up or antagonize another, possibly leading to an argument or feud. Phrase: "He's brought the ratio in the chat."
## 中文
**比例**：这个词在俚语中主要有两种用法。一种是对抗或比较两个事物或人的说法，通常表示在聊天或社交媒体上有人故意激怒他人，引发争执或矛盾；另一种用法来源于下注术语，指的是从一场比赛中可能获得的收益。俚语中经常会说“他在聊天中带来了比例”，表示某人试图通过挑衅升级冲突。
