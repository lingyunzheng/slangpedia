## Ratio (English)
**Meaning:** A comparison of two things, often used to highlight the superiority of one thing over another. It can be used to describe anything from physical attributes to skill levels.
**Bilingual Example:** 
- *English:* "The ratio of his abs to the rest of his body is insane!"
- *中文:* "他的马甲线和他的其他部分的比例简直太夸张了！"
## 比率 (中文)
**Meaning:** 一种比较的表达方式，经常用来凸显一方在另一方之上的优势。它可以用来描述从体形特征到技能水平的各种情况。
**Bilingual Example:** 
- *English:* "Her dance skills are way above the ratio of her peers."
- *中文:* "她的舞蹈技巧比她的同龄人高得多。"
