## Ratio (English)
### Meaning
"Ratio" in colloquial English is often used to describe a situation where someone is publicly comparing something, usually in a very negative or competitive way, to provoke an emotional response or to offend someone. It's a term derived from the idea of comparing values but commonly used for the purpose of confrontation or criticism.
### Example
- English: "Dude, don't even think about it. The ratio is real on this one."
- Chinese: "兄弟，别这么干，这可是真·比烂啊！"
## 比烂 (Chinese)
### Meaning
"比烂" (bǐ làn) is a colloquial term in Chinese that can be translated as "competing in being worse" or "ratio." It is often used when someone is engaging in negative, confrontational behavior, or when they are publicly comparing things in a way that implies they are both bad.
### Example
- Chinese: "你看他俩说话多难听，这是在比烂吗？"
- English: "Look how they talk to each other; are they just competing in being the most unpleasant?"
Note: Both "ratio" and "比烂" are informal terms often used in online conversations and social media.
