# Ratio (English-Chinese Slang)
## English
**Ratio**: Originated from social media and street culture, "ratio" often refers to a way ppl express satisfaction or dissatisfaction through making fun of others' emotions, lifestyles, or appearances. It's a kind of competitive conduct in social interactions. 
- Example: "Did you see how much deeper that guy's car is compared to yours? This is what we call a 'deep ratio'!"
## 中文
**比例/碾压**：这个词起源于社交媒体和街头文化，常常用来形容人们通过取笑他人的感情、生活方式或外貌来表达满意或不满意的姿态。这是一种社交互动中的竞争行为。
- 例句： "你有没有发现，人家的车足足比你深了一个级别？这就是所谓的‘碾压’！"
