### Ratio
**English:** 
A slang term that often refers to the comparison or contrast between two things, particularly in a way that is about quality, value, or effectiveness. More colloquially, it can imply a challenge or competition, especially on social media where people try to outdo each other in terms of achievements or possessions.
**Chinese (简体):**  
一个俚语表达，通常用于对比或比较两件事物，特别是在质量、价值或效果上的对比。更通俗地讲，它可以指挑战或竞争，尤其是在社交媒体上，人们试图通过展示成就或拥有物来互相对比和超越对方。
