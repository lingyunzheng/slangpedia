# Ratio
## English
**Meaning:** A term often used to compare the quality or value of something. If someone says something or someone has a good "ratio," it means it is better or more valuable in comparison.
**Example:** "His new car has a great ratio compared to his old one; it's much faster and more expensive."
## 中文
**含义:** 一个用来比较某物的质量或价值的术语。当有人说某物或某人有好的“比例”时，意味着它在比较中更加优秀或更有价值。
**例句:** "他新买的那辆车和旧车比起来比例非常好，开起来更快也更贵一些。"
