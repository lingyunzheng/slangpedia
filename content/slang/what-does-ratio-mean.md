## Ratio (英语 / 粤语)
**English:**
- **Ratio:** This term is often used in contexts related to comparing one thing to another, often in a negative or competitive way, especially in social media or online gaming communities. It refers to the act of comparing one's own situation or possessions to someone else's in a way that highlights someone else's perceived inadequacy. It's seen as a form of online taunting or boasting.
**粤语:**
- **比例:** 这个词通常用于社交媒体或在线游戏社区中，形容一种负面的竞争心态。它指的是将自己的情况或拥有的东西与他人比较，从而凸显别人的不足或不够好。这是一种在线夸耀或羞辱的行为。
