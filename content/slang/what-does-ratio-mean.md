# Ratio
**English:**  
Ratio is a term commonly used in social media and internet conversations to refer to the act of publicly criticizing or mocking someone, especially when their actions or statements are perceived as embarrassing or offending. It's often associated with the ones who don't take the criticism personally, but rather, openly mock the one being criticized. This term has rooted deeply in online culture, particularly on platforms like Twitter.
