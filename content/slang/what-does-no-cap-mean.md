## No Cap
### English
"No cap" is a slang phrase that means "for real" or "literally." It's used to emphasize that what is being said is true or genuine. 
**Example:**  
- "I'm going to the beach tomorrow."  
- "No cap, I'm going to the beach tomorrow."
### Chinese
"不带 caps" 是网络用语，意思为“真的”或“确实如此”。它用于强调所说的内容是真实的或真诚的。
**例子：**
- "我明天要去海滩。"  
- "不带 caps，我明天要去海滩。"
