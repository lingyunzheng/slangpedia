## "No Cap"
### English
"No cap" is a slang expression used to denote that what has been stated is true and without exaggeration. It indicates honesty or emphasizes that a claim is genuine and straightforward. 
### Bilingual
- **English**: "No cap" means that something is true and there's no exaggeration.
- **Chinese**: "No cap" 表示所说的话是真的，没有夸张。
