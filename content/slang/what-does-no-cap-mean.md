# No Cap (原意: 不戴帽子 / 没有夸张的意味)
**English Explanation:**  
No cap is a slang term that means "sincerely" or "I'm not exaggerating." It’s often used to emphasize that what you are saying is true and not an overstatement.
**中文解释:**  
“no cap” 是网上的俚语，用来表示“诚实地”或“我没有夸张”。通常用于强调你说的话是真实的，不是在夸张。
Example Usage:
1. **English:** "I really like your new haircut. No cap, it looks great on you."
- **中文:** "我真的很喜欢你的新发型。诚实地讲，你看起来很帅。"
2. **English:** "No cap, I ran a 5-minute mile today."
- **中文:** "我没有夸张，今天我跑了一个5分钟一英里的成绩。"
