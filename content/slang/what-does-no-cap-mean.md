## English: No Cap
### Definition
"No cap" is an informal phrase used to indicate honesty or to emphasize that something is true or real. It means that what is being said is not exaggerated or falsified. The exact origin of the phrase is uncertain, but it's widely used in casual conversations.
### Example
- **English:** "I ran 10 miles today, no cap."
- **Chinese:** 我今天跑了10英里，不吹牛。
## 中文：no cap
