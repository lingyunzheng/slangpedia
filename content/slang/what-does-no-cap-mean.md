# "No Cap" Slang Explanation
## English
**No Cap:**  
This phrase is often used to indicate that what is being said is 100% true or genuine, and there's no exaggeration involved. It can also be used to assert that one is not bluffing or exaggerating their claims.
**Example Usage:**  
"I got a job offer from Google this morning - no cap!"
## 中文
**没有腠理 (No Cap):**  
这个短语通常用来表明所言之事完全真实，没有夸大其词。它也可以用来表明某人没有在吹牛或夸大其词的陈述。
**例句:**  
“今天早上我收到了谷歌的入职邀请—没有腠理！”
