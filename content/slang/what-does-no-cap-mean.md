## English: No Cap
### Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:** No cap means to say something seriously or truthfully, often to indicate that something is true or accurate. It's used to emphasize the sincerity of a statement.
**Chinese:** "No cap" 指的是认真或真诚地说某件事，常用于表明某事是真实的或准确的。它用来强调陈述的真实性。
Example:
- **English:** "No cap, I really scored a free concert ticket!"
- **Chinese:** "No cap, 我真的中了一张免费的演唱会门票！"
