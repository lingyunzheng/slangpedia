## No Cap
### English
**No cap**: Used to emphasize that what you are saying is completely true and without exaggeration. It's like saying "I'm not lying" or "I'm telling the truth".
Example:
- "I ran 10 miles yesterday no cap 😎"
### Chinese
**No cap**: 指的是强调你所说的绝对是真实且没有夸大的话。类似于说“我没有在说谎”或“我正在说实话”。
例句：
- "我昨天跑了10英里 no cap 😎"
