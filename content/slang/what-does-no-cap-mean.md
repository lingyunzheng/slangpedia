### no cap
**English:**  
- **Definition:** A phrase used to emphasize that what is being said is genuine, truthful, or not exaggerated. It's a way to show one is being sincere or straightforward.
- **Example:** "I finished the project in just one day? No cap, bro!"
**Chinese:**  
- **释义:** 用来强调所说的话是真实的、未经夸大的，展示说话人是诚恳或诚实的。
- **例句:** “项目我一天就搞定了？No cap，哥们儿！”
