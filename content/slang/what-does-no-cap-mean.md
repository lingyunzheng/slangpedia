# No Cap
## English
**No Cap**: Used to say "I'm not lying" or "That's the truth." It emphasizes honesty and sincerity. For example, "I really ate the last cookie, no cap."
## 中文
**No Cap**: 表示“我没有撒谎”或“这是事实”。用来强调诚实和真诚。例如，“我真的吃了最后那块饼干，no cap。”
