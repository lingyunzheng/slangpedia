## "No Cap"
### English
**Meaning:** 
"No cap" is a slang phrase used to emphasize that something is true, real, or legitimate. It's often used to express authenticity or to clarify that one is not exaggerating or joking. It's similar to saying "for real," "for sure," or "you bet."
**Example:**
- English: "I'm gonna take that offer, no cap."
- Translation: "我接下那个报价，绝对是真的，无帽。"
### Chinese
