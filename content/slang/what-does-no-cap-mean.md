```markdown
## English: No Cap
**Meaning:** Saying "no cap" can have two primary meanings, depending on the context:
1. **Literal Meaning:** It literally means "no cap" or that something is true and not an exaggeration. For example, "I got a promotion at work. No cap."
2. **Figurative Meaning:** In slang, it indicates something is true, real, or genuine. It suggests that there is no hiding the truth. For example, "I saw your ex at the party. No cap."
**Origin:** The phrase originates from African-American Vernacular English (AAVE) where expressing the sincerity of a statement is important.
**Example in Sentence:**
"I finally finished that novel in one sitting. No cap."
```

```markdown
## 中文: No Cap
**含义:** "No cap" 这个俚语有两种主要含义，具体取决于上下文:
1. **字面意义:** 通常等同于“真的没有帽子”或表示某事是真的，没有夸张。例如，“我在工作中得到了晋升。No cap。”
2. **比喻意义:** 在俚语中，“no cap” 意味着某事是真实的、真实的或诚恳的。它表明没有隐藏真相的可能。例如，“我看见你前任在聚会上了。No cap。”
**来源:** 这个短语源自非洲裔美国人英语 (AAVE)，在 AAVE 中，表达真实性的重要程度是很关键的。
**例句:**
“我终于一口气读完了那本小说。No cap。”
```
