## No Cap
**English:**  
Not lying; being truthful. It's a way to assert that what you are saying is genuine and not an exaggeration or joke.  
**Chinese:**  
不带帽的 / 不带夸张的  
意指说真话，没有夸大或者开玩笑。用于强调所说的内容是真实的，没有在吹牛或开玩笑。
Example Usage:  
- English: "Really scored 100 on the test! No cap."
- Chinese: "考试真的得了100分！不带帽子的。"
