# "No Cap" (无滤镜)
**English**: "No cap" is a slang term used to indicate that something is true or genuine, that it's not an exaggeration or a lie. It suggests honesty and authenticity.
**Chinese**: "无滤镜" (Wú lǜjìng) 是一个俚语表达，用来表明某事是真实的或诚恳的，它不是夸张或谎言。这表达了诚实和真实性。
