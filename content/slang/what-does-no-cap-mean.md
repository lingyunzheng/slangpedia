# No Cap
## English
"No cap" is a casual expression used to assure someone that what you are saying is truthful and genuine. It's a way of saying, "I'm not lying" or "I'm serious."
## Chinese
“no cap” 是一个非正式的表达，用于向别人保证你说的话是真实和真诚的。它相当于说，“我没有撒谎” 或 “我是认真的。”
