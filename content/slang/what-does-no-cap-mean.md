## No Cap
### English
**Meaning**: A term often used to convey "for real" or "literally" to emphasize sincerity or confirm authenticity. It can also be used to express disbelief or as a cop-out statement when someone makes a bold claim.
**Example**: "I just got a promotion!" "No cap, that's legit."
### Chinese
**含义**: 一个常用来表示“真的”或“确实”的词语，用来强调真诚或确认真实性。它也可以用来表达惊讶或在某人做出豪言壮语时作为扯淡的一种说法。
**例子**: “我刚加了薪！”“没问题哥，这是真的。”
