## no cap
- **English:** Used to emphasize that something is true and not an exaggeration or joke.
- **Chinese:** 用来强调某事是真的，并非夸张或开玩笑。
- Example Usage: "I ran 10 miles today. no cap." -> "今天跑了10英里。真的，可不是吹牛。"
