```markdown
## "No Cap"
- **English:** There's no exaggeration or truth-shaping involved. It means something is said or claimed in a straightforward and genuine manner.
- **Chinese (简体中文):** 这句话是直接的、真实的，没有夸大或修饰的成分。它表示某件事情是以一种坦诚、不虚假的方式说出来的。
```
