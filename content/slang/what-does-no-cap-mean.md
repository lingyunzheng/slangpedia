## No Cap (无 cap)
### English
**Meaning:** Don't take it literally or believe everything. It's often used to downplay something or to indicate that what is being said is not to be taken seriously, literally, or too seriously.
**Example:** "I can run faster than Cheetahs!" - "No cap, it's just a figure of speech."
### 中文
**含义:** 不要当真或全信。通常用来表示什么话不需要当真或者不要过于认真理解，有时候用来轻描淡写某件事。
**例句:** "我能跑得比猎豹还快！" - "无 cap，这只是一种夸张的说法。"
