## "No Cap"
### English
- **Definition**: To express that one is not exaggerating or is telling the truth.
- **Example**: "I really ran 10 miles today. No cap."
### Chinese
- **释义**：表示自己没有夸张，说的话是真实的。
- **例句**：“我今天真的跑了10英里。No cap。”
