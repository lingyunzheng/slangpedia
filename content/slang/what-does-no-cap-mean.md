## "No Cap"
**English:**  
No cap is a phrase used to emphasize that something is true or genuine. It indicates that what is being said is not an exaggeration or a joke. 
**Chinese:**  
“No cap” 用来强调某件事情的真实性或真实性，表明所说的话并没有夸张或开玩笑。
Example Usage:
- **English:** She said she ran a mile in under five minutes. No cap.
- **Chinese:** 她说她在五分鐘內跑了一英里。No cap.
