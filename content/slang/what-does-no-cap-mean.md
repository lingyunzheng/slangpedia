## No Cap
### English
**Meaning:** 
- To state something truthfully, without exaggeration.
- Often used to indicate approval or to agree with someone's point of view.
**Example:**
- "He really did run a mile in under four minutes? No cap."
### 中文
**意思：**
- 诚实地说某件事，没有夸大其词。
- 常用来表示同意或赞同某人的观点。
**例句：**
- "他真的在一分钟内跑了四百米吗？No cap。"
