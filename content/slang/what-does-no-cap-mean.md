## English: No Cap
### Definition
"**No cap**" is an informal phrase used to emphasize that what is being said is true, real, or literal. It's often used to downplay any appearance of boasting or exaggeration.
### Example
- "I ran five miles today after work. No cap!"
---
## 中文: 没带
### 解释
“**没带**”是一个非正式用语，用于强调所讲的内容是真实的、认真的或字面意思的。它经常用来降低任何炫耀或夸张的印象。
### 示例
- “今天下班后我跑了五英里。没带。”
