## "No Cap"
**English:**  
"No cap" is an informal expression used to emphasize that something is true or accurate without exaggeration. It's often added to the end of a statement to assure the listener that the speaker is not exaggerating or lying about what they are saying. It's commonly used in casual conversation to reassure someone that what is being said is real and genuine.
**中文:**  
"No cap" 是一个非正式用语，用于强调某事是真的或准确的，没有夸张。它常被添加在陈述句的末尾，以确保听者知道说话人没有夸大或撒谎。在轻松的对话中，它常用来保证所说的话是真实的和认真的。
