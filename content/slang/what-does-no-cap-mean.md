## "No Cap"
### English Slang Meaning
"No cap" is a phrase often used to express sincerity, insisting that something is true or realistic. It means "don't exaggerate" or "it's for real."
### Chinese Slang Meaning
“no cap” 表示诚意，强调某事是真的或现实的。相当于说“别夸张”或“不是吹牛”。
