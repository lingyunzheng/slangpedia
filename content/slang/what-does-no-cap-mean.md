# No Cap
## English
**Meaning:** 
"No cap" is a phrase used to assert that what is being said is true or sincere, indicating that one is not exaggerating or lying. It's often used to affirm something without a doubt.
**Example:**
" Yeah, I got tickets to the concert, no cap."
- Translation: I really do have tickets to the concert, I'm not kidding.
## Chinese
**含义:**
"no cap" 是一个用来表示所说的内容是真实的、真诚的短语，表明自己没有夸大或撒谎。通常用于坚定地肯定某事，确信无疑。
**例句:**
"是的，我有演唱会的门票，no cap。"
- 释义：我真的有演唱会的门票，我没有在撒谎。
