## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "No Cap"
**English:**  
"No cap" is an internet slang term used to denote authenticity, seriousness, or to indicate that one is not exaggerating. It originated from the phrase "no cap," which was first popularized by American rapper Snoop Dogg. When someone says "no cap," they are asserting that what they are saying is true or accurate, or they are simply indicating a serious statement.
**Example Usage:**  
- "I really ran a 10-minute mile last night. No cap."
- "I can teach you how to code from scratch. No cap."
