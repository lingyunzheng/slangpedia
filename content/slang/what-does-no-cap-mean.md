## No Cap
### English
"No cap" is a slang phrase commonly used to indicate truthfulness or honesty. It is employed to assure the listener that what is being said is genuine and not exaggerated or false. The phrase originates from the idea of not having a "cap" (lid) on the truth—meaning there's nothing concealing or limiting the accuracy of the information.
Example: "I ran a mile in under five minutes? No cap."
### 中文
“No cap” 是一种常用的俚语，用来表明真实性或诚实性。这句话用于向对方保证所说的内容是真实的，没有夸大或虚假成分。这个说法起源于没有“帽子”（或盖子）限制真相——也就是说，没有隐瞒或限制信息的准确性。
例如：“我在五分钟内跑了英里？No cap。”
