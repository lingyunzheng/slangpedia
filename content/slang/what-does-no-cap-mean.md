## "No Cap"
### English
"Big deal; it's not a big deal." Often used to downplay something. For example, if someone says they ran a marathon, a response could be "No cap, that's nothing."
### 中文
"大 deal；那不算什么大事。" 经常用来轻描淡写地回应某些事情。比如，如果有人说到他们跑了全程马拉松，可以答道 "No cap, 那不算什么。"
