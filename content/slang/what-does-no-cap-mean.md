## "No Cap"
### English
"No cap" is a slang phrase that originated in urban and digital communities. It is used to denote sincerity or truthfulness. When someone says "no cap," they are affirming that what they are saying is not exaggerated or fabricated. 
For example:
- "I really ran 10 miles today." — "No cap, I'm just fit!"
This phrase is often used to stress that a statement is factual and not an overstatement.
### Chinese
“no cap” 是源自于城市和网络社区的一种俚语。它用来表示诚意或真实性。当有人使用 "no cap" 时，他们是在强调他们所说的是真实的，没有夸大或者虚构。 
例如：
- "我今天真的跑了10英里。" —— "no cap，我只是比较健壮！"
这个词组经常用来强调陈述的真实性，避免被认为是在夸张。
