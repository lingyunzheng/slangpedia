## English: No Cap
**Definition:** A phrase used to signify that someone is being genuine or truthful, or to assert that what they are saying is not exaggerated or a lie.
**Example:** "I literally just won the lottery! No cap."
## Chinese: 不带帽
**定义:** 用于表明某人是真诚的或在讲真话，或断言他们所说的内容没有夸大或撒谎。
**例句:** "我真的中彩票了！不带帽。"
