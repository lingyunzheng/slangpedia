## "No Cap"
### English
"No cap" is a slang phrase used to indicate that someone is not exaggerating or lying about what they are saying. It is often used to emphasize the truthfulness of a statement.
#### Example
- *English*: "I just won a million dollars in the lottery! No cap."
- *Translation*: 我刚刚中了百万大奖！没有夸张，真的。
### Chinese
"不带帽" (bù dài mào) 是一个口头禅，用来表明某人没有夸张或撒谎，正在说真话。它常用于强调所说内容的真实性。
#### 示例
- *中文*: "我刚刚中了百万大奖！不带帽。"
- *Translation*: 我刚刚中了百万大奖！没有夸张，真的。
