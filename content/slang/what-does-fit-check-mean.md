# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Fit Check"
**English:**
- **Definition:** A fit check is generally used in the context of fashion or style to see how well clothes or accessories fit. It can also be used more informally to check if something is appropriate or suitable.
**Example:** "I need to get a fit check for this new suit before the wedding."
**Definition in Chinese:**
- **定义:** "Fit Check" 通常用于时尚或风格上下文，指检查衣物或配饰是否合身。它也可以用于非正式的语境中，检查某事是否合适或适合。
**例句:** "在婚礼前，我需要做一次试穿检查（fit check），确保新的西装合身。"
