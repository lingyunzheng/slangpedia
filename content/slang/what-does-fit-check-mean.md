# fit check
## 中文解释
fit check：试穿检查。通常用于购物时，特别是购买服装、鞋子等需要试穿的商品时，试穿过后进行的检查，确认尺码是否合适、是否有瑕疵等问题。
## 英文解释
fit check: a trial fitting. Used when shopping for items like clothes or shoes that require trying on, to verify that the item fits properly and to check for any issues like flaws or misfit.
