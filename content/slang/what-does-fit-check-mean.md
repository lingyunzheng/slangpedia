# "Fit Check" (俚语解释)
## 中文
**fit check**: 用于形容某样东西（通常是衣服、鞋子或其他物品）是否合适或看起来很搭。类似于“试穿”或“试戴”的意思，但更多带有一种时尚或潮流的感觉。
## 英文
**fit check**: refers to checking whether something (usually clothing, shoes, or other items) fits well or looks good. It's similar to "try on" or "try out," but often carries a sense of fashion or trend.
