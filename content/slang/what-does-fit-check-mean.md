# fit check
- **English**: A casual term used to describe a situation where someone is showing off or checking to see if their clothes look good on them.
- **Chinese**: 一个 casual 词汇，用来形容有人在秀穿搭或者查看衣服穿在自己身上的效果。通常带有炫耀或自检的意味。
