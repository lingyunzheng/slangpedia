## fit check (适合中文解释)
**English:** fit check  
**Chinese:** 穿搭测试 / 试装  
**Explanation:**  
In the context of fashion or social media, a "fit check" is when someone takes a picture or video of themselves to show how their outfit or makeup looks. It's like a personal show-and-tell for their appearance, often done to get feedback or simply to share their style. It's commonly used in posts to show off new outfits or makeup looks, or to ask friends if the clothes fit well or if the make-up looks good.
Example Usage:
- "I just did a fit check for my birthday outfit. @Fashionista, what do you think?!"
- "Posting a fit check of my new jacket. Feels just right!"
