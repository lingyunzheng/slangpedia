## fit check
### 在中文中的解释
“fit check” 这个词通常用来描述一种超现实或理想化的情况，即某人对自己的身材或某种理想状态感到很满意，但实际上这种状态非常不现实，甚至可能不存在。类似于中文里的“画饼充饥”或“自欺欺人”。
### 在英文中的解释
"Fit check" is often used colloquially to describe a scenario where someone is extremely pleased with their physique or a certain idealized state, but this condition is highly unrealistic or simply not attainable. It's akin to the English phrase "pie in the sky" or "self-deception."
