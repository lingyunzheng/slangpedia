# Fit Check
## English
**Fit Check:** A term commonly used in the fashion and body-positive communities. It refers to the act of checking how something fits on your body, often to ensure it meets your expectations in terms of size, shape, and comfort. This can also be a light-hearted way to refer to someone wanting to see if they've changed their body shape due to diet, exercise, or other factors.
## 中文
**Fit Check:** 这个词常用于时尚和身体积极的社区中，指检查某件衣服或配饰在自己身上的合身程度，通常用来确保其大小、形状和舒适度符合预期。这也可以作为一种轻松的方式，指的是想看看自己的身材是否因饮食、运动或其他因素而发生变化。
