## fit check (中文：fit check)
### English
**fit check:**  
A casual term often used in fashion or social media contexts, referring to the process of checking if an item of clothing fits well. It can also metaphorically refer to verifying that something meets expectations or standards.
### 中文
**fit check:**  
一个常用在时尚或社交媒体上下文中的随意术语，指的是查看一件衣服是否合身的过程。此外，它还可以比喻成验证某样东西是否符合预期或标准。
