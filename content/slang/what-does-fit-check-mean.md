## English: Fit Check
A casual term used to describe a situation where someone is verifying the appropriateness or conformity of something. Often used in the context of clothing, it can also extend to any scenario needing evaluation or approval.
### Chinese: 模特试穿
一种口头表达方式，用来描述某人正在验证某物的合适与否或是否符合标准。这个词组大多用于服饰场景，也可以泛指各种需要评估或确认的场合。
```
## English: Fit Check
When someone wants to make sure that something (usually clothing) fits properly or looks good on them.
### Chinese: 试穿检查
当有人想要确保某物（通常是衣物）穿在身上合适或看起来好看时使用的说法。
