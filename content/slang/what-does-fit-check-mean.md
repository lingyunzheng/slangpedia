## fit check
**English:** 
A casual way to describe when someone is trying on clothes to see how they look. This can be used when someone asks for your opinion or to simply refer to the act of trying on clothes.
_example:_ 
- "I just got a new outfit, can you do a fit check for me?"
**中文:** 
一种口语表达，指的是人在试穿衣服看看合不合身。这个短语可以用来描述某人寻求意见，或是单纯指试穿衣服的行为。
_example:_ 
- "我刚买了一套新衣服，能帮我做一次 fit check 吗？"
