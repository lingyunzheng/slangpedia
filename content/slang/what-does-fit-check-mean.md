# fit check
## English
**fit check**: A casual term used to describe the act of verifying or checking if something, usually clothing or accessories, fits properly. Often used in social media posts or among friends when someone is about to post a picture of themselves wearing new clothes to see if others approve of how it looks on them.
Example: "I'm doing a fit check before posting my new outfit on Instagram!"
## 中文
**fit check**: 一个常用的非正式词汇，用于描述检查衣物或其他配饰是否合适的行为。通常用于社交媒体发帖前，当一个人打算展示他们新买的衣服的照片，看看别人是否认为穿起来好看时使用。
例如： "我在发Instagram之前先做了一次fit check！"
