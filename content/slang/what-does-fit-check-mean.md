## Fit Check (中文解释)
### English
**Fit Check**: A casual term often used in fashion or bodybuilding communities to refer to checking one's appearance in a mirror or a photograph, usually after trying on new clothes or working out. It can also be used to show off a new outfit or physical achievements.
### 中文
**fit check**: 一个常用在时尚或健身社群中的轻松术语，指的是试穿新衣服或健身后照镜子或查看照片检视自己的外观。这个词也可以用来炫耀新的穿搭或身体上的成就。
