# Slang Term: "Fit Check"
## English Explanation
A "fit check" is a phrase often used in the context of shopping for clothes, particularly in social media posts or fashion circles. It refers to the act of posting a photo of a piece of clothing you've bought, especially a new out-of-the-box item, to see what people think about the style, fit, or the overall look. The term is a blend of "fit" (how the clothes look on your body) and "check" (to see/examine). It's a way to get feedback and validation from your online community.
Example Usage: 
"I just got these new sneakers and need a fit check before I hit the streets!"
## Pinyin & Character Explanation
裁剪检查 ("cái jiǎn jiǎn chá")
- **裁剪** ("cái jiǎn") - Fitting
- **检查** ("jiǎn chá") - Check
Chinese Explanation: 
"裁剪检查" 是指在购买服装后，尤其是在网上分享穿搭照片时，为了获得对自己新衣服的外观、合身程度和整体搭配的看法，而去获取反馈的一种行为。
Example Usage in Chinese:
“我刚买了新运动鞋，需要来做一次裁剪检查，看看大家觉得怎么样。”
