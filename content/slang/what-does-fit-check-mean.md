## fit check (英语)
### 解释
- **中文**: 拍照秀身材
- **英文**: Showing off one's body shape in photos, often while wearing the clothes one is trying on.
### 例句
- **中文**: 她每次买新衣服都会做一次fit check，发到朋友圈里炫耀一下。
- **英文**: She always does a fit check every time she buys new clothes, showing off in her feed.
## fit check (中文)
### 解释
- **英文**: Checking how well one's body fits into clothing by taking and sharing photos. Often used among friends or online to share and receive feedback on one's appearance in clothes.
### 例句
- **中文**: 在fit check的时候一定要找到最好的角度，这样才能完美展示身材。
- **英文**: When doing a fit check, you need to find the best angles to showcase your body shape perfectly.
