## Fit Check
**English:** A casual term used to describe the act of checking if an outfit or piece of clothing fits properly. People might say "Let's do a fit check" before going to a party or an event to ensure their clothes are the right size and look good.
**Chinese:** （名词或动词）指检查衣物是否合身的行为。在参加聚会或重要场合前，人们可能会说“做一下fit check”，确保自己的衣服既合适又好看。
**Example Usage:**
- English: "Can I come to the party? I need to do a fit check on my new suit."
- Chinese: "我可以去派对吗？我需要先试一下我新买的西装合不合身。"
