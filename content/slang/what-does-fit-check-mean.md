# "fit check" (noun)
## English
A casual, informal term referring to the act of checking if clothes or an outfit looks good on a person. Often used when someone is trying on new clothes at a store or in front of friends.
Example: "I'm doing a fit check on these new jeans before I buy them."
## Chinese（中文）
一个非正式的词语，用来描述试穿衣服或检查某件衣服是否合身是否好看的行为。常用来描述在商店试衣服或是朋友面前试穿新衣服时的情景。
示例："我在买之前先做了一个试穿（fit check），看看这条牛仔裤合不合身。"
