### fit check
#### English
- **Meaning**: A casual observation to see how well something fits, often used in the context of clothing. It can also be used generally to assess if something looks or functions well.
- **Example**: "I’m going to do a fit check on these new jeans to make sure they’re not too tight."
#### Chinese
- **意思**: 一种随意检查某物是否合身的方式，通常用于衣物。也可以泛指查看某物是否合适或工作得当。
- **示例**: “我要做一下试穿，看看这些新买的牛仔裤是否合适。”
