# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "fit check"
## English Explanation
A "fit check" is an informal phrase often used to imply that someone is checking if a piece of clothing fits well before deciding whether to buy it. It can also be used more generally to describe the act of seeing if something is suitable or appropriate for a particular situation.
## Chinese Explanation
“fit check” 表示一个人在决定买衣服之前检查衣服是否合适的行为。它也可以被更广泛地用来描述看看某样东西是否适合特定情况的行为。
Usage Examples:
- I need a fit check on this jacket to see if it’s my size. (我需要试一下这件夹克看看是否合身。)
- She insisted on a fit check before making a purchase. (她在购买之前坚持要做一次试穿。)
