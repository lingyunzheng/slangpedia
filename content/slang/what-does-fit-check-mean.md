# Fit Check
**English:**  
A informal term used in fashion and social media to refer to the act of someone wearing clothes and either showing the outfit to others or posting a picture to ask for feedback on the look, especially on how well the clothes fit. It's common to use this term when trying out new outfits or when sharing style tips with friends or followers.
**Chinese (简体中文):**  
一个非正式用语，用于描述有人试穿衣服并将这套搭配展示给他人看，或者在社交媒体上发照片寻求别人关于衣服合身程度的反馈，尤其是在尝试新衣服或者分享穿搭心得时常用的表达。通常在展示新搭配或者与朋友/粉丝分享时尚建议时使用。
