## Bilingual Slang Explanation: Fit Check
### English
**Meaning**: A fit check usually refers to the act of checking if something (like clothes or images) looks good on someone. In a more general sense, it can also refer to a verification to ensure something meets a certain standard or requirement.
**Example Sentence**: "I did a fit check in the mirror to make sure the suit was tailored perfectly."
### Chinese
**含义**: fit check 通常指的是检查某物（如衣服或图片）是否适合某人。在更广泛的意义上，它也可以指验证某物是否符合一定标准或要求。
**例句**: "我在镜子前做了个fit check，确保西装裁剪得刚刚好。"
