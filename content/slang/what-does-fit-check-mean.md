## fit check (适合检查, 正确检测)
**English:**  
A term commonly used in fashion contexts, often on social media, where a person is showing off how well their newly purchased clothes fit them. It can also be used when someone is looking to see if an outfit or style suits them.
Example: "Just did a fit check for my new jacket, it's looking killer!"
**Chinese:**  
一个常用于时尚场合的术语，特别是在社交媒体上，指一个人展示自己新买的衣服是否合身。也可以用来形容有人在检查自己的造型是否合适。
示例： “刚做了新夹克的 fit check，效果真棒！”
