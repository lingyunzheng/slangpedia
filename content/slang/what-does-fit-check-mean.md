## fit check
**English:** A trendy fashion term used to describe a moment or occasion where someone tries on or examines their outfit to ensure it looks good before going out.
**Chinese:** 一个流行的时尚术语，用来形容一个人在出门前试穿或检查自己的服装，以确保看起来很得体的时刻或场合。
