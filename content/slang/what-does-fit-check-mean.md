# "fit check" Explanation
## English
A "fit check" is a casual term, primarily used in fashion and style circles, to refer to the process of ensuring that an outfit or piece of clothing fits well before it is worn, made, or purchased. It often involves trying on clothes to check how they look and feel, usually at the beginning of the design and manufacturing stages.
**Example Usage:**
> "Before we go to the event, we need to do a quick fit check to make sure everything matches perfectly."
## 中文
"fit check" 是一个俚语，在时尚和潮流圈广泛使用，特指在穿着、制作或购买衣物之前，确保衣物合身的过程。通常涉及试穿衣物以检查其外观和穿着感受，常在设计和生产初期进行。
**例句：**
> "在参加派对前，我们需要快速做一次 fit check 确保所有东西都能完美搭配。"
