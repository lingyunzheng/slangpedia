# fit check 🛍️💨
## 中文解释
“fit check”是指在购买衣物或鞋类产品时，试穿以确认尺寸是否合适，就像在购物时快速检查商品的合身程度一样。
## 英文解释
“fit check” refers to trying on clothes or shoes to ensure they fit properly, similar to when you quickly test the fit of an item while shopping.
