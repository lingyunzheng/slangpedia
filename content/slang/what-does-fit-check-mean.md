# Slang Explanation: "fit check"
## English
**Meaning:** A close inspection to see if something (usually clothing) matches well on a person, is in style, or fits properly.
**Example Usage:**
- "Before you buy that shirt, you should do a fit check to make sure it looks good on you."
## Chinese
**意思:** 一个近距离的检查，看看某样东西（通常是衣物）穿在人身上是否合适，是否时尚，或者是否贴身。
**例句:**
- "在你买那件衬衫之前，你应该做一个 fit check，确保它穿在你身上很好看。"
