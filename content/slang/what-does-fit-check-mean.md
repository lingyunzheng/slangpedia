# fit check
## English
**fit check**: A casual phrase used to describe the act of verifying if new clothes or an outfit suits you. It can also be used more generally to check if something is correct or appropriate. 
Example: "I just did a fit check and I look awesome in this new suit!"
## 中文
**fit check**: 一个常用的 slang 词，指的是检查新买的衣服或 outfit 是否合身。它也可以用作更广泛的检查，看某事物是否合适或正确。
例句: "我刚刚做了一个 fit check，穿上这套新西装 really 服气！"
