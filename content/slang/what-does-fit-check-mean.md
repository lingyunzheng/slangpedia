## fit check
**English:**  
A term typically used in the context of trying on clothes or checking if an item of clothing looks good on the wearer. It can also be used more generally to check if something fits or is suitable.
**Chinese:**  
用于试穿衣服或检查衣物是否适合穿戴的术语，通常在时尚和购物的语境中使用。也可以泛指检查某事物是否合适或合适。
