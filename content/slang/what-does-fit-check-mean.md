# fit check
## English
**fit check**: A casual term used to describe a scenario where someone is checking how well something, usually clothing, fits them. 
Example: "I'm doing a fit check on this new suit to make sure it fits perfectly before the big meeting."
## 中文
**fit check**: 一个常用的 informal 术语，用来描述某人检查某物（通常是衣服）是否合身的情景。
例子: "我正在做 fit check，确认这件新西装是否合身，以便在重要会议前确保一切妥当。"
