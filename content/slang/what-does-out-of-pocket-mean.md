# "Out of Pocket" in English and Chinese
## English Explanation:
"Out of pocket" is an English idiom that literally means a person has paid for something themselves out of their own funds, rather than it being covered by a company, insurance, or another party. It can be used to describe monetary expenditures that are not reimbursable or included in a budget.
- **Example:** "The taxi ride cost fifty dollars, and since it wasn't covered by the company, it's out of pocket for me."
## Chinese Explanation:
"出自己腰包"（Chū jìzi yāobāo）是中文中的俚语，意思是指某项开销是自己个人支付的，而不是由公司、保险或其他人负责。它可以用来描述那些没有报销或不包含在预算中的费用。
- **例句：** "出租车费五十元，因为我们公司没有报销，所以这笔费用是自己出的腰包。"
