# Out of Pocket (English)
**Meaning:** When something costs more money than you initially thought, or when you have to pay extra money for something beyond what you planned.
**Example:** "When I went to the doctor, the consultation fee was out of pocket because my insurance didn't cover it."
# 出Pocket (Chinese)
**Meaning:** 当某件事情花费超出你原先预想，或者你不得不为某事支付额外的费用时，这种情况可以用“出Pocket”来描述。
**Example:** "当我去看医生时，咨询费用是出Pocket的，因为我保险没有涵盖这个费用。"
