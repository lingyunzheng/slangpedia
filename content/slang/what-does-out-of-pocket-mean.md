# "Out of Pocket" Bilingual Slang Explanation
**English:** Out of pocket
**Explanation:** This phrase is commonly used to describe a situation where you have to pay for something yourself, rather than being reimbursed or the cost being covered by another party.
**Example Usage in Sentences:**
- "I borrowed $50 from my friend, but I'd prefer if you could pay for the drinks myself since the last time it was **out of pocket** for me."
- "Even though the meal was supposed to be free, it turned out to be **out of pocket** because the waiter mistook our order."
**Chinese:**
**意思:** 这个短语通常用来表示你不得不自己花钱，而不是由另一方报销或承担费用的情况。
**例句:**
- "我朋友借了我五十美元，但最好这次我们自己掏腰包付酒水钱吧，上次是 **自己掏腰包** 呀。"
- "尽管本来说饭是免费的，但最后还是 **自己掏腰包** 了，因为服务员搞错了我们的点餐信息。"
