## Out of Pocket
**English:**  
- **Definition:** When you have to pay for something yourself, even though it is supposed to be covered by another source, such as insurance or an employer.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- 直接买单 (Zhíójī dāimiàn): 直接 (zhíjié) means directly, and 点单 (di diàn) means to check out or pay. This slang captures the essence of when you have to pay for something yourself, highlighting a direct financial responsibility that shifts away from the usual payer.
