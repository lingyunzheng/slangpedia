# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Out ofPocket"
## English
**"Out of Pocket"** - This term is often used to describe personal financial expenditure. It means that someone has to pay for something with their own money, rather than it being covered by an insurance policy, employer, or other third-party payer. It can also refer to additional costs that aren't part of the initial budget or estimate.
Example: "I was out of pocket for $50 because the insurance didn't cover the costs of the new phone."
## 中文
**"出 of Pocket"** - 这个短语通常用来描述个人财务支出。它意味着某人需要用自己的钱支付某事，而不是由保险政策、雇主或其他第三方支付。它也可能指的是超出初始预算或估算的额外费用。
例句：因为我提供的保险没有覆盖新手机的成本，所以额外支付了50美元，算是我自己出的。
