# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Out of Pocket"
**English:** Out of Pocket  
- **Definition:** This expression is used to describe an expense that must be paid by the individual, rather than being reimbursed or covered by another party.
- **Example:** If you go out to eat and your friend says, "I'll treat you," and you pay for the meal anyway, you might say, "Well, technically I was out of pocket." 
**中文:** 出自己的腰包 (Chū zhuāng jīng de yāo bāo)  
- **定义:** 这个表达用来描述不得不自己支付的费用，而不是由另一方补贴或承担。
- **例句:** 如果你和朋友去吃饭，朋友说“我请客”，但你还是付了钱，你可以笑说，“严格来说，我是出自己的腰包。”
