## Out of Pocket
### English
"Out of pocket" is a phrase most commonly used when talking about expenses or money. It refers to money that someone must pay themselves, rather than being reimbursed or covered by another entity. It can be used in formal or casual contexts.
Example:
- "I got a new phone, but the screen protector was out of pocket."
### 中文
"出 of pocket" 是一个常用于谈论开支或费用的短语。它指的是个人必须自行支付的费用，而不是由其他实体报销或承担。这个词既可用于正式场合，也可用于非正式场合。
例子:
- "我新买了个手机，但屏幕保护膜的钱得我自己出 of pocket。"
