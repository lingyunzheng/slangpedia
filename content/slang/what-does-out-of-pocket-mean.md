## Out of Pocket
**English**: Something that you have to pay for yourself, often beyond your regular budget.
**中文**: 自掏腰包，指的是你自己需要支付的费用，通常超出你的常规预算。
**Example Usage**: 
- *English*: "I had to buy a new laptop, which was out of pocket since it wasn't in my monthly budget."
- *中文*: "我不得不买了一台新笔记本电脑，这超出了我的月度预算，所以是自掏腰包。"
