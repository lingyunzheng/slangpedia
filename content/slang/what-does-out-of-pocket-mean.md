## Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Out of Pocket"
**English:** Out of pocket
- **Definition:** This phrase is used to describe the amount of money that someone spends directly, without help from any other sources such as insurance, a job, or borrowed money.
- **Example:** "I had to pay out of pocket for the emergency dental appointment, which cost me $500."
**中文：出-of口袋**
- **释义：** 这个短语用来描述一个人直接支付的金额，不包括其他任何来源的资助，比如保险、工作收入或借款。
- **例句：** "我不得不自掏腰包支付紧急牙科就诊费用，花费了500美元。"
