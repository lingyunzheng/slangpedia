# Bilingual Slang Explanation: "Out of Pocket"
**English Explanation:**
"Out of pocket" is a phrase commonly used to describe expenses that you pay yourself, rather than having someone else cover the cost. It can be used in different contexts, such as expenses that you directly pay from your own money or costs that you personally covered for a friend or colleague.
**Chinese Explanation:**
“出 of pocket” 是一个常用的俚语，指的是你自己支付的费用，而不是由别人支付。这个词可以用于多种情境，比如自己支付的费用，或者是你个人替朋友或同事垫付的费用。
**Example Sentences:**
- *English:* I had to pay for the taxi ride out of pocket.
- *Chinese:* 我不得不自己支付出租车费。
