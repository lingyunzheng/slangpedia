## Out of Pocket

### English
**Definition:** When you pay for something directly from your own money, rather than being reimbursed or covered by another source, such as an insurance plan, company, or other payer.

**Example:** "I went out of pocket for the Uber because my company's travel policy doesn't cover ride-sharing services."

### 中文
**定义：** 当你用自己手中的钱直接支付某项费用，而不是由其他方（如保险公司、公司或其他支付方）代为支付时，就说是“自掏腰包”。

**示例：** “我自掏腰包叫了Uber，因为公司的差旅政策不包括打网约车。”
