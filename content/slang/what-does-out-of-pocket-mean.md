## Out of Pocket
### English
**Meaning:** When you directly pay for something, usually referring to expenses not covered by insurance, a budget, or someone else.
**Example:** "I had to pay out of pocket for the doctor's visit because my insurance didn't cover the medication."
### Chinese
**意思:** 指你自己直接支付某项费用，通常指的是没有被保险、预算是某人支付的费用。  
**例句:** "因为保险没有覆盖药物费用，所以我不得不自己掏腰包去看医生。"
