## English: Out of Pocket
In casual conversations, "out of pocket" is used in a broader sense to mean "personally." It refers to something that one has to pay or do themselves, rather than being provided for or reimbursed by someone else.
### Example:
- **English:** "I'm out of pocket for lunch today because my company didn't have a budget for it."
- **Simplified Chinese:** "我得自己出钱请客吃饭，因为公司这周的预算里没有这部分费用。"
### Translation:
- **English to Chinese:** You have to pay personally.
- **Chinese to English:** 你得自己出钱。
## 相关用法
- **英语:** "It cost me $50 out of pocket for the taxi."
- **中文:** "这出租车我是自己出了50块钱。"
