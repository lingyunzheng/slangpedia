# Out of Pocket
**English:**  
- **Meaning:** When you pay for something yourself, rather than it being covered by another party.  
- **Example:** "I had to pay for the cab out of pocket since my budget was full for the month."
**汉语解释:**  
- **意思:** 自己支付某项费用，而不是由其他方承担。  
- **示例:** “因为我的预算这个月已经满了，所以出租车费用得我自己出。”
