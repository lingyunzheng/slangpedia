```markdown
## English: Out of Pocket
**Definition:** When a person has to pay for something themselves, rather than being reimbursed or having the cost covered by someone else.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **英语释义:** 当一个人不得不自己付费，而不是由别人报销或承担费用时。
- **直译:** 自己掏腰包
- **实际用法:** 如果朋友让你请吃饭，你觉得不划算，你可以说：“这次我自己掏腰包吧，下回你请我吧。”
```
This explanation provides a direct translation and a contextual example to help understand the phrase "out of pocket" in both English and Chinese.
