## Out of Pocket
### English
"Out of pocket" is a phrase that can mean a few different things:
1. **Finances**: When you have to use your own money for something, rather than it being covered by a company, a health insurance, or another organization. For example, if you buy a coffee with your own money, it's "out of pocket" expenses.
2. **Physically**: Sometimes used to refer to something that is no longer within a person's physical possession or control. For example, after a tough game, a player might say, "I'm out of pocket," meaning they are mentally or emotionally distant or preoccupied.
### 中文
"out of pocket" 这个短语有几种不同的含义：
1. **财务方面**：当你自己掏腰包支付某项费用，而不是由公司、医疗保险或其他组织覆盖时。例如，如果你自己付了咖啡的钱，这就是“自掏腰包”（out of pocket）的花费。
2. **身体上**：有时候用来形容某物不在某人的物理掌控之内。例如，在一场激烈比赛后，一个选手可能会说，“我 out of pocket”，意味着他们身心疲惫或情绪上已经远离比赛的状态。
