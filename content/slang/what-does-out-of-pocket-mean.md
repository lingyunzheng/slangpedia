## Out of Pocket
### English
**Out of Pocket**: This phrase is used to describe when someone pays for something themselves, rather than relying on a company, organization, or others to cover the cost. It can also be used to denote the direct personal expense, often in contexts where there's a discussion of costs or expenses.
Example: 
- "He bought the tickets out of pocket, which cost him $50."
### 中文
**出-of-pocket**: 这个短语用来描述当某人自己支付某项费用，而不是依赖公司、组织或他人来承担这个成本。它可以用来表示直接的个人开销，常用于讨论费用或支出的场合。
例子：
- "他用自己的钱买了票，花了50美元。"
