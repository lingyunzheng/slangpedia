## Out of Pocket (英 / 美)
**English Explanation:**
- *Literal Meaning:* Literally means spending money from your own pocket.
- *General Usage:* Used to describe a situation where you personally have to pay for something. This could be expenses, costs, or even a debt.
**Chinese Explanation:**
- *直白意思:* 从自己的口袋里花钱。
- *一般用法:* 用来描述你需要自行支付某项费用、成本或债务的情况。
**Example Usage:**
- *English:* I had to buy the ticket out of pocket.
- *Chinese:* 我得自己掏腰包买票。
