```markdown
## Out of Pocket
#### English:
"Out of pocket" refers to money that you personally pay out of your own funds, as opposed to money that is covered by insurance, a company, or another source.
#### Chinese:
"自掏腰包" （zì tāo yāo bāo）指的是你用自己的钱支付，而不是由保险、公司或其他来源支付。这个词通常用来形容你自己承担费用的情况。
**Example Sentence:**
- **English:** I have to pay the bill out of pocket because the insurance didn't cover it.
- **Chinese:** 我不得不自己掏腰包付账单，因为保险没有覆盖。
```
This markdown format includes both the English and Chinese explanations for "out of pocket," providing clarity and context for both audiences.
