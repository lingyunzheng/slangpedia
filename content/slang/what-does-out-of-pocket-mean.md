## Out of pocket
### English
"Out of pocket" is an informal phrase used to describe the situation where someone has to pay for something themselves, often because it exceeded the amount covered by a previously agreed-upon budget or insurance plan.
### 汉语
"出自己的腰包" 是一个非正式的表达，用来描述某人自己支付某项费用的情况，通常是因为超出了一个预先约定的预算或保险范围。这句话强调了个人需要承担额外的费用。
