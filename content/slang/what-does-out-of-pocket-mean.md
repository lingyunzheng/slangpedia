# Out of Pocket (English-Chinese Slang Explanation)
**Out of Pocket**:
- **English**: When expenses are not covered by a budget, insurance, or other sources, and are instead paid directly by the individual.
- **Chinese (Simplified)**: 自掏腰包 (zì tāo yāo bao) - 指个人直接支付原本应该由他人或保险等承担的费用。
- **Literal Translation**: 自掏腰包字面意思是“自己掏钱包”，形象地表达了个人承担开支的情景。
Example Usage:
- English: "I had to pay out of pocket for the medical expenses this time."
- Chinese: "这次我不得不自掏腰包支付医疗费用。"
