# Out of Pocket
## English
**Out of Pocket:** This phrase typically means that the costs are coming from your own finances, rather than being covered by someone else. It can be used in various contexts, such as paying for a bill yourself or covering expenses that weren't included in an initial agreement.
Example:
"Since the meeting, I've been out of pocket for multiple business trips, and my travel expenses are not reimbursable."
## 中文
**出口袋 (Chū kāitate):** 这个词组通常表示花费是从自己的腰包里出的，而不是由他人承担。它可以用于各种情境中，例如自己支付账单或承担一些不在最初协议中的费用。
示例：
“自从那次会议之后，我每次公差都得自己出钱，而且相关的交通费用也无法报销。”
