## Out of pocket
**English:** A slang term used to refer to expenses that need to be paid personally, often exceeding the amount one has budgeted for or is able to pay from their usual account.
**Chinese (简体中文):** 一个俚语表达，用来指超出预算或预付款需要个人支付的费用。常常用于描述超出预期的支出或需要自掏腰包的情况。
的例子:
- English: I had to pay out of pocket for the medical check-up because my insurance didn't cover it.
- Chinese (简体中文): 我不得不个人支付体检费用，因为我保险不包括这部分。
