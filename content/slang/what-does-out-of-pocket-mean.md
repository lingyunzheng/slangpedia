# "Out of Pocket"
## English
- **Definition**: When you have to pay for something yourself, rather than it being covered by a company, insurance, or anyone else.
- **Example**: "I spent $50 out of pocket for the new shoes after my insurance didn't cover the cost."
## Chinese (中文解释)
- **定义**：当你需要自己支付某样东西的费用，而不是由公司、保险或者其他任何人支付的情况。
- **例子**："我自己花了50美元买新鞋，因为保险不报销这笔费用。"
