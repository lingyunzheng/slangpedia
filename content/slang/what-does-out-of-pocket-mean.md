## Out of Pocket
**English:**  
- **Literal Meaning:** When expenses are not covered by insurance, a health plan, or another party, and you must pay for them yourself.
- **Slang Explanation:** "Outta pocket" or "OOTP" means you're responsible for paying the cost yourself. It's like if you get a ticket for a concert without a group and have to buy your own ticket instead of splitting the cost.
**Chinese:**  
- **字面意思:** 当费用没有被保险、健康计划或其他方覆盖时，你需要自己支付这些费用。
- **俚语解释:** "自掏腰包" 或者 "自费" 意味着你需要自己承担支付成本。就像你一个人买音乐会门票，而不是和别人分担费用一样。
