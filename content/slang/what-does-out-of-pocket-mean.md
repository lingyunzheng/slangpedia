## Out of Pocket
### English
**Out of pocket**: This phrase is used to describe any expenses that you have to pay yourself, rather than being reimbursed or covered by another party. This could include direct payments for goods or services, as well as incidental costs like transportation or meals.
### Chinese
**出了口袋 (Chūle kāndlàng)**: 这个短语用来形容你需要自己支付的费用，而不是由另一方报销或承担的费用。这包括直接支付的商品和服务费用，以及诸如交通和餐饮等零星开支。
