### Bilingual Slang Explanation: Out of Pocket
**English:**
- **Definition:** When something costs you personally, usually referring to the money that you have to use to cover expenses, rather than a company or another entity.
- **Example:** If you pay for lunch and the company does not reimburse you, it's considered "out of pocket."
**中文:**
- **释义:** 指某项费用需要你自己个人承担，通常指的是你需要用自己的钱来支付开销，而不是由公司或其他实体来承担。
- **例句:** 如果你支付了午餐费用而公司没有报销，这就算是“自掏腰包”。
::: tip 注意
“Out of pocket” 和 “自掏腰包” 都是用来形容一个你需要自己承担费用的情况。
:::
