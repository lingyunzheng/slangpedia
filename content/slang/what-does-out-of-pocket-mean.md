# Out of Pocket 💸
## English
- **Meaning:** When you pay for something yourself, rather than being reimbursed or covered by someone else, such as an organization, insurance, or another person.
- **Example Sentence:** "I had to go out of pocket for the taxi fare since my insurance doesn't cover transportation expenses."
## Chinese
- **意思:** 当你自己支付某项费用，而不是由其他人（如组织、保险公司或其他人）报销或承担时，就称之为“自掏腰包”。
- **例句:** "因为我保险不包括交通费用，所以我不得不自掏腰包支付出租车费用。"
