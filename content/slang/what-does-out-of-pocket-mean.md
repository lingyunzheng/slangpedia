## Out of Pocket
**English:**  
"Out of pocket" is an expression used to refer to expenses that one has to pay personally. It often implies that the cost is not covered by another party or a financial mechanism like insurance.
**Chinese:**  
"Out of pocket" 指的是个人需要自行承担的费用。通常意味着这笔费用并没有由其他人或保险公司等支付。
例如：
- English: The cost of groceries was out of pocket.
- Chinese: 食物杂费全部自己掏腰包。
