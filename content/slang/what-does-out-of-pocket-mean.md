## Out of Pocket (English)
**Definition:** When you pay for something yourself, often out of your own pocket or cash on hand. It refers to personal financial responsibility for costs that are not covered by a third-party, such as insurance or another party.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **英语释义：** 当你自己掏腰包付账时，指的是对于那些没有被第三方（如保险或其他人）覆盖的成本，你需要自己来支付。
- **中文俚语解释：** 自掏腰包。比如，朋友之间请客吃饭，你没有带钱，最后你只能自己买单，这就是“自掏腰包”。
