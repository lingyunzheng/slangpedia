# Out of Pocket
## English
"Out of pocket" is a phrase that refers to expenses paid by an individual, rather than being covered by a business, insurance, or any other third party.
## 中文
"出 own腰"（出于口语，也可以说是"out of pocket"的中文表达）是指个人自行承担的费用，而不是由公司、保险或其他第三方承担的费用。
