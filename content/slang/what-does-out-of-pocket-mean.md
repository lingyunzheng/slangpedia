## Out of Pocket
### English
- **Definition**: When you personally pay for something that wasn't initially covered by a budget or another source of funds.
- **Example**: If you buy a coffee and the cost is not reimbursable by your company's budget, you are "out of pocket" for the purchase.
### 中文
- **释义**: 当个人支付了一些本不在预算或其它资金来源范围内的费用时。
- **例句**：如果你买了杯咖啡并且这个费用不能由公司预算报销，那么这笔开销就是你“自掏腰包”承担的。
