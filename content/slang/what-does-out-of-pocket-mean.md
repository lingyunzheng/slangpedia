```markdown
### 在口袋外 (Wèi Kuài Duì Wài)
**English:** Out of pocket
- **Meaning:** When something costs more than the money you have on you, or the money you initially planned to spend.
- **Usage Example:** "I wanted to buy that coffee, but it was 5 yuan, which was out of my pocket, so I just drank water instead."
```
