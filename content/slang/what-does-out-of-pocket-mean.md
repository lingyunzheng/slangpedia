## Out of Pocket (英) / 出Pocket (中)
**英:** Out of pocket  
**中:** 出Pocket
### Explanation:
- **英:** "Out of pocket" literally means spending money from your own pocket. It refers to spending money that you have directly rather than using something provided for free or by someone else.
- **中:** "出Pocket" 形容自己花钱的情况，通常指的是自掏腰包买某些东西，而不是使用免费提供的或者别人的提供的东西。
### Example Usage:
- **英:** If the bus fare is too expensive, we might find it cheaper to ride our bikes — that would be out of pocket, though.
- **中:** 如果公交费用太高，我们可以选择骑自行车，但这显然是自掏腰包的费用。
### Related Phrases:
- **英:** "I paid out of pocket for the groceries."
- **中:** "我自掏腰包买了杂货。"
