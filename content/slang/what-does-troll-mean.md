# Troll (中文解释: 搞笑大王)
## English Explanation
**Troll:** The term originally comes from internet parlance and refers to someone who posts comments or information on the internet with the intention of causing disruption, anger, or amusement. These individuals often engage in provoking or misleading others in online forums, social media, or gaming communities. Trolls enjoy seeing how far they can push the boundaries and rile up others for amusement.
## Chinese Explanation
**搞搞笑星人 (Gǎo gǎo xiǎo xīng rén):** 该词来源于网络用语，指那些在网上故意发布评论或信息，目的是造成混乱、愤怒或娱乐的人。这些人经常在论坛、社交媒体或游戏社区中挑起事端或误导他人。搞搞笑星人喜欢看看自己能走得有多远，从其他人那里挑起情绪以获得乐趣。
