# Troll (Slang)
## English Explanation
A troll is someone who posts spiteful or inflammatory comments online, often in order to upset people or provoke a reaction. They're usually seen as negative and annoying, taking joy in causing chaos or argument.
**Example Usage:**
- "Stop being such a troll and quit posting those mean comments!"
