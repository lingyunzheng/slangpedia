# Slang Term: Troll
## English Explanation
A troll is someone who posts disruptive, provocative, or insulting comments online with the intention of causing arguments or upsetting people. Internet trolls often seek to challenge or anger others by posting inflammatory messages.
## Chinese Explanation
troll (网络水军) 是指在网络上发布具有破坏性、激进或侮辱性评论的人，目的是引起争端或让他人不满。网络水军往往通过发布引人争议的信息来激怒他人或挑起争端。
### Example Use in Sentences
- **English:** He was just a troll, not really trying to help.
- **Chinese:** 他就是一个网 Troll，没真正想帮忙。
