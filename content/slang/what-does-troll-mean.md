## Troll
### Simplified Chinese
**解释**: 恶作剧者或网络捣乱分子。这类人喜欢在网络环境中散布不良信息，引发争论或扰乱秩序，以此来寻求关注或娱乐自己。
### Traditional Chinese
**解釋**: 搕ｆ／ protester 或網絡捣亂分子。這些人喜歡在網絡環境中散佈不良資訊，引發爭論或擾亂秩序，以此來博取關注或娛樂自己。
### Explanation
"Troll" is commonly used to describe someone who seeks to provoke or upset others by posting controversial or disruptive comments online. This term captures individuals who enjoy causing chaos or debate and often get a sense of satisfaction from doing so.
