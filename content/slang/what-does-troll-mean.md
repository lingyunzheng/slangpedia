# Troll (名词/动词)
## 中文解释
- **名词**：网上的捣乱分子，喜欢在论坛、社交媒体等平台上制造混乱、嘲讽或误导他人。
- **动词**：发布具有误导性信息或故意制造麻烦的言论。
## 英文解释
- **Noun**: A troublemaker on the internet who likes to disrupt forums, social media platforms, and other online spaces by causing chaos, mocking, or misleading others.
- **Verb**: To post misleading information or intentionally cause trouble.
