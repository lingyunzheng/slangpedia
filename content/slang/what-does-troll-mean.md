```markdown
## Troll (English-Chinese)
### English
**Troll**: Someone who posts comments or shares content with the intent to provoke, offend, or disrupt a community or conversation. Often used to refer to people who deliberately post controversial or inflammatory messages to create conflict.
### Chinese
** Troll**: （潜水者/挑拨者）故意发布引起争议或使他人愤怒的言论以激化矛盾或引发讨论的人。通常用来形容那些在群里或论坛上故意发帖挑事的人。括号中的词组是对 "troll" 的形象化解释。
### Example Usage
- In English: He's just a troll; don't bother replying.
- In Chinese: 他就是一个水军/挑拨者，别理他。
```
