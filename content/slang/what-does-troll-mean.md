# Troll (单双语随性解释)
## 中文解释
**中文**: 恶作剧者或泼皮，常常在论坛、社交媒体上发布带有挑衅性、讽刺性的信息，以获得关注或引起争论。
### 示例
- 他在评论区发了一些奇怪的评论，逗得大家哈哈大笑，可能就是为了招摇过市，算是一种网络“ Troll”行为。
## 英文解释
**English**: A troublemaker or prankster who often posts provocative or sarcastic content on forums or social media to gain attention or start debates.
### Example
- He posted some weird comments in the comment section, which got everyone laughing. It’s probably just his way of trying to draw attention, which is a form of "trolling" online.
