# Troll (中文： Troll)
## English Slang Explanation
A troll is a person who posts inflammatory, extraneous, or off-topic messages in an online discussion group with the intent of provoking Conflict, flame wars, or increased activity. The term is derived from Internet slang and folklore. Trolls often enjoy the chaos and reaction they create.
## Chinese Slang Explanation
"Troll" 意指在在线讨论组中发布挑衅、无关或偏离主题的帖子以激化矛盾、引发论战或增加活动的人。这个词源自互联网俚语和民间传说。Trolls 通常喜欢他们造成的混乱和引起的反应。
