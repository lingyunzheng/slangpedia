# Slang Explanation: Troll
## English
**Definition**: A troll is someone who posts inflammatory, dishonest, or disruptive messages online with the intent to upset or provoke others.
**Example**: "When you see a photo posted on a funeral page, and someone leaves an offended and overly critical comment, they're being a troll."
## Chinese
**含义**: 一个 troll 是指在网上发布激进、不诚实或破坏性的言论的人，目的是激怒或挑逗他人。
**示例**: “当有人在追悼会的相册下留言，批评过度而且冒犯时，他们就是 troll。"
