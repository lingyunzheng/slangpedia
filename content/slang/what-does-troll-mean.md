## Troll (English)
A troll is an internet user who posts inflammatory, offensive, or irrelevant comments with the intention of upsetting others or disrupting online discussions. They thrive on creating chaos and drawing attention to themselves through provocative behavior.
### Bilingual Explanation in Chinese and English
**中文释义**  
troll：在互联网上故意发布引战、冒犯或无关紧要的评论的人。他们乐于制造混乱并吸引关注，通过具有挑逗性的行为引起纷争。
**Example:**
- **English:** That commenter really is a troll.
- **Chinese:** 那个评论者真是个恶意挑事者。
