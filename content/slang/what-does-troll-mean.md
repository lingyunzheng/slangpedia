```markdown
### Troll
- **English:** A person who posts inflammatory, offensive, or irrelevant comments with the intention of provoking readers or disrupting a discussion.
- **Chinese:** 恶作剧者或煽动者，故意发布挑衅、侮辱性的评论或帖子以激怒读者或扰乱讨论。
**例句：**
- English: She's a real troll; everything she says is pure annoyance.
- Chinese: 她就是一个真正的恶作剧者，她的每一句话都纯粹是添乱。
**注释：**
- 在网络社区中，troll常被视为破坏整体氛围的行为者。
```
