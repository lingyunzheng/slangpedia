## Troll
### English
*Troll*: A cowardly competitor or internet user who intentionally posts inflammatory, insulting, or irrelevant messages to provoke a reaction or disrupt conversations.
### 中文
*Troll*: 在网络上故意发布具有煽动性、侮辱性或无关紧要的信息，以引起争端或扰乱对话的人；也指在竞争中故意进行挑衅或破坏的懦夫。
### Example Usage
- **English**: "Don't be a troll and start a fight in the comment section!"
- **中文**: "别做喷子，在评论区引发争斗！"
