## Troll
### English
A troll is someone who purposely posts inflammatory, irrelevant, or offensive messages online with the intention of provoking an emotional response, often stirring up conflict or disrupting discussions. Think of them as online vandals,最爱捣蛋的网虫。
### 中文
“ troll ”是指那些故意在线上发布引发情绪反应的、无直接关联或具有冒犯性的信息的人，他们常常扰乱讨论或激化冲突。就像是网络捣蛋鬼，最爱惹麻烦的网虫。
例子：
- In the comments section, she was a true troll, posting comments just to upset people.
- 评论区里，她就是一个真正的“ trolls ”，只是为了惹麻烦而发评论。
- The website got flooded with trolls, causing a massive disruption in the community discussion.
- 该网站被大量的“ trolls ”涌入，导致了社区讨论的严重中断。
