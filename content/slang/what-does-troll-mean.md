## Troll (英) / 畏灵 (中)
### English Explanation:
1. **Definition**: A troll is someone who posts inflammatory, extraneous, or off-topic messages in an online discussion forum with the intention of provoking other users into an emotional response or of otherwise disrupting normal fuerban participation.
2. **Usage Example**: "I couldn't believe he just asked such a dumb question in that serious thread, it was so typical of a troll to try and derail the discussion."
3. **Cultural Nuance**: The term "troll" is often employed in internet communities, forums, and social media platforms. It reflects a real-world phenomenon where people pretend to have malicious intent or disrupt community discourse for the sake of causing chaos.
### Chinese Explanation:
1. **定义**：发帖者故意在在线讨论论坛中发布具有煽动性、无关或偏离主题的消息，以激发出其他用户的情绪反应或以其他方式扰乱正常的讨论参与。这种行为的人被称为“畏灵”。
2. **使用示例**：“我简直不敢相信他在那个严肃的讨论中居然提了一个那么愚蠢的问题，这太典型了，那些‘畏灵’总是试图把讨论偏离主题。”
3. **文化背景**：术语“畏灵”通常用于互联网社区、论坛和社交媒体平台。这个词反映了现实生活中的一种现象，即人们假装有恶意意图或扰乱社群讨论以造成混乱。
