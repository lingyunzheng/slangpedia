# Troll
**English Meaning:** A person who posts comments or engages in behavior to provoke or upset others.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **English:** A troublemaker on the internet who loves to stir the pot. They might post funny but misleading comments or start harmless arguments just for the chaos.
- **Chinese (简体中文):** 在网络上故意制造麻烦、挑起争端的人。他们可能发布一些有趣但具有误导性的评论，或者引发无伤大雅的争论，只为制造一些混乱和话题。
