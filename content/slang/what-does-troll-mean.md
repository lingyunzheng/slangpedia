```markdown
# Troll (中英文俚语解释)
**中文:** 恶作剧者 / 故意制造混乱的人
**英文:** Someone who deliberately causes trouble or creates chaos, often on the internet. Trolls enjoy causing upset or trying to disrupt discussions.
Example Usage:
- 他是个真正的网络恶医，总是发帖撩拨其他网友。（He's a genuine online troll, always posting to stir up other netizens.)
- 不要把她的话当回事，她就是个无赖恶少。（Don't take what she says seriously, she's just a troll.)
```
