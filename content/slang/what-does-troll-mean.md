## Troll
### English
**Definition:** A troll is a person who posts artificial or provocative messages online in order to upset, distract, or otherwise provoke other participants.
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
A troll is like a pesky fly in a cafeteria. You can't catch them, they keep buzzing around, and they just want to ruin your lunch and make everyone angry. In the digital world, trollers post nonsense or offensive stuff just to see the reactions and arguments it creates.
### Chinese
**定义：** 恶搞者或者破坏者，通常在网络上发布故意引起争议或让其他参与者生气的消息或评论。
**双语俚语解释：**
恶搞者就像是网络上的刺头，他们发布一些无意义或者刺激性言论只是为了博人眼球，引发争论和愤怒。在网络世界里，他们就像是飞来飞去的苍蝇，扰乱了平静和谐的环境，让所有人感到烦躁和不满。
