# Troll ( troll )
## English Explanation
A troll in internet slang refers to a person who posts inflammatory, offensive, or irrelevant comments with the intent of provoking readers into an excessive reaction, or who posts off-topic comments on a blog or forum to disrupt discussions and create arguments.
## Chinese Explanation
网络用语中的“ Troll”指的是在网络上发布挑逗性、侮辱性或与主题无关的评论的人，他们故意引发读者的过度反应，或是发布不相关的内容来干扰讨论和制造争端。
### Example Usage
- English: "That guy is such a troll, just trying to start an argument."
- 中文： "那个人真是个 troll，就是想挑起争端。"
