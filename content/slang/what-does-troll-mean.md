## Troll (网络用语)

### 中文解释
**网虫** / **作弄人的人**
在互联网环境中，**troll** 通常指的是那些以发布引战、扰乱言论、制造冲突为目的的用户。这类用户往往喜欢在论坛、社交媒体等平台上故意发表一些负面或挑衅性言论，以引发其他用户的反应。他们的目标往往不是为了真正讨论问题，而是为了破坏对话的氛围，消耗其他参与者的时间和精力。

### 英文 Explanation
**Internet Troll** / **Mischief-Maker**
In internet environments, a **troll** is usually someone who posts inflammatory, disruptive, or off-topic comments with the intent to provoke an emotional response from other users or simply to sow discord. Trolls often enjoy creating conflicts or debates purely for the sake of disrupting the flow of conversation. Their primary goal is not to genuinely address or contribute to the discussion but to provoke and divert it.

---

### Example Usage
- **中文示例**
  - 他只是一个普通的用户，但自从开始发帖后，就成了一个真·troll。
  - He was just a regular user, but once he started posting, he became a true internet troll.

- **英文示例**
  - She was just a regular user, but once she started posting, she turned into a real internet troll.
