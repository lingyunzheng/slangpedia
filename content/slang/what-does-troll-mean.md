## Troll
### English
A troll is someone who deliberately posts inflammatory, offensive, or irrelevant messages online to provoke a reaction. They often enjoy the chaos or emotional responses they create in discussions.
### 中文
Troll 是指那些故意发布刺激、冒犯或无关信息在网络论坛上的人。他们通常喜欢自己制造的混乱或激起的情绪反应。
