```markdown
## Troll
**English Explanation:** A troll is someone who intentionally behaves in a disruptive, annoying, or provocatively ways online to upset or provoke others. They are often found in forums, social media, and gaming communities.
**Cantonese Explanation:** 滅吞 (meih6 taam1) - 係指個 الشخص故意都做啲攛DDL或者搵人搗亂嘅行為在网上，旨在氣同搗を与え嘅人。常见嘅地方有論壇、社會媒體同遊戲社区。
**Pinyin:** Meih6 Taam1
**英語解釋：** 滅吞是指一個人故意做出打搅、搗亂或者挑釁他人的行為在網上，目的是激怒或搗亂他人。它們常常出現在論壇、社交媒體和遊戲社區。
**粵語解釋：** 滅吞係指個個人故意都做啲攛DDL或者搵人搗亂嘅行為在網上，旨在氣同搗を与え嘅人。常见嘅地方有論壇、社會媒體同遊戲社区。
```
Note: The term "滅吞" is a translation used here to provide a common slang term in Cantonese that can be used to explain the concept of a "troll." However, it's often more common to use the term "搵人搗亂嘅人" (moy6 jan4 tau6 laan5 ye5 ren5) directly, which literally translates to "someone finding trouble."
