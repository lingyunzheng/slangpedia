```markdown
# Bruh (英: Bruh)
**Definition:** A versatile interjection used to express a wide variety of emotions such as agreement, disbelief, confusion, or sharing in another person's sentiment. Often used with a drawl or stretched pronunciation like "Brrruuh."
**Usage Examples:**
- "Bruh, that was sick! I still can't believe they won the game like that."
- "Brrruuh, the traffic is outta control today, I'm gonna be late for work."
**Context:** Commonly used in informal, relaxed conversations among friends or in online contexts like social media and forums.
```
```markdown
# Bruh (中: 布鲁 / bù lǜ)
**定义:** 一个多功能的感叹词，用来表达各种情绪，如认同、怀疑、困惑或分享他人的感受。常带有拖长的发音，类似于 "Brrruuh"。
**使用示例:**
- “布鲁，这真是了不得！我还真的不敢相信他们竟然能在比赛中赢得如此之顺利。”
- “布鲁，今天交通状况真是一塌糊涂，我大概要迟到了。”
**语境:** 经常在非正式、放松的对话中使用，尤其是在朋友之间或在线平台上如社交媒体和论坛中。
```
