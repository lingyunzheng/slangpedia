## Bruh (English/美国英语)
**Definition:** A versatile interjection used to express a wide range of emotions or reactions, often conveying agreement, confusion, disbelief, or exasperation. It's a term that has found its way into everyday conversations, memes, and internet culture.
**Usage Example:** "Bruh, that test was hard as hell!"
**Bilingual Explanation:**
- **English:** Oh my gosh, that test was extremely difficult!
- **美国英语:** Bruh, that test was hard as hell!
**Context:** Often used in casual or non-traditional settings, particularly among younger generations.
---
## 哇 subdued (汉语)
**定义:** 一个有用的感叹词，用于表达广泛的情绪或反应，经常传达同意、困惑、不信或挫败感。这个词已经渗透到日常对话、表情包和互联网文化中。
**用例示例:** "哇，这测试好难！"
**双语解释:**
- **汉语:** 哇，这测试好难！
- **美国英语:** Bruh, that test was hard as hell!
**场景:** 经常用于非正式或非传统环境中，特别是在年轻人之间。
