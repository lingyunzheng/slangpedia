## Bruh (英语)
### Definition
"Bruh" is a casual, often exasperated interjection used to express disbelief, frustration, or exasperation. It originated from African American Vernacular English (AAVE) and has since become a widely used term in internet slang and among younger generations.
### Usage Example
- **English**: "Why did you leave all the trash in the living room?!"
- **Bilingual**: "Why did you leave all the trash in the living room?!" *bruh*
## 哈喽 (Bruh, 简体中文)
### 解释
