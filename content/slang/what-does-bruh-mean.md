## Bruh (Brutha)
### In English:
> **Definition:** A versatile interjection typically used to express surprise, disbelief, embarrassment, or mild annoyance. It's often used when something absurd or unexpected happens.
### In Simplified Chinese:
> **释义：** 一个多功能的感叹词，通常用于表达惊讶、难以置信、尴尬或轻微的不满。当发生荒诞或意想不到的事情时，人们经常会用到这个词。
### English Example:
- "Bro, did you see the price of that? Bruh, that's way too much!"
### Simplified Chinese Example:
- "Bro，你看那个价格？Bruh，那个太贵了！"
```
### Chinese Translation of Example:
> "Bro, 你看见那个价格了没？Bruh，那个太贵了！"
### English Example:
- "Bruh, you actually canceled our plans last minute?!"
### Simplified Chinese Example:
- "Bruh，你居然最后取消我们的计划？！"
