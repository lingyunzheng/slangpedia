## Bruh (俚语解释)
### 中文
布鲁·亨利（BroHHenry）曾说过：“布鲁”本是20世纪90年代常用来称呼撒旦的歌词，如今它在北美和南美等地区很流行，用于表达惊讶、困惑或迷惘的情绪。
### English
"Bruh" is a popular slang term originating from the 1990s, where it was used as a term for Satan in lyrics. Today, it is widely used in North and South America and other regions to express surprise, confusion, or bewilderment.
