## Bruh (English/Chinese)
**English**: *Bruh* is an interjection used to express various emotions like confusion, surprise, disbelief, or exasperation. It can also be used to lighten the mood in a humorous way, often when something is so absurd that it can’t be taken seriously.
**Chinese**: *Bruh* 是一个感叹词，用于表达多种情绪，如困惑、惊讶、难以置信或烦躁。它也可以用来在幽默的语境中缓和气氛，通常当某事荒谬到无法认真对待时。
Example Usage:
- **English**: That exam was so hard, bruh!
- **Chinese**: 那次考试好难啊，备货行！（“备货行”是网络流行语，意思是“真是的/太难了”）
