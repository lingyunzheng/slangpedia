# Bruh: 哥们儿 / 哥们哥
A versatile interjection used to express a range of emotions or reactions, such as surprise, disbelief, disappointment, or frustration. It's often used by younger generations, particularly in online and informal settings.
- **English:** Bro, dude, like, wow!
- **中文:** 哥们儿，哥们哥，我的天！
Example:
- **English:** Bruh, did you see the score in that game last night?
- **中文:** 哥们儿，你看到昨晚那场比赛的比分了吗？
