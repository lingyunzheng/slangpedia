## Bruh (American English / Urban Dictionary)
**Definition:**
- Used as an exclamation to express disbelief, confusion, or agreement.
**Example in a sentence:**
- **English:** "Bruh, have you seen the traffic today? It's insane!"
- **Chinese:** “Bruh，今天你看见交通有多堵了吗？简直无法想象！”
**Explanation:**
- "Bruh" is an informal interjection commonly used in American English, especially in urban and online contexts. It's a versatile word that can be used to indicate surprise, exasperation, or when you want to acknowledge something absurd or clear. The term "Bruh" is often associated with a relaxed or laid-back attitude.
### Related Phrases:
- **Bro:** Shortened version, often used in a similar context.
- **Broh:** Another variation of "bro" with a softer "o" sound.

## 哥们儿 (Guo Men Er - Chinese)
**Definition:**
- Used as an informal term for a close male friend, similar to "bro" or "man" in English.
**Example in a sentence:**
- **Chinese:** “哥们儿，今天我们去吃烤串怎么样？”
- **English:** "Bro, what do you think about going to eat skewers today?"
**Explanation:**
- "哥们儿" (Guó mén ér) is a casual term used among friends in Chinese. It's often used in a way that expresses a sense of camaraderie and close friendship, similar to saying "man" or "bro" in English. This term can be used to refer to a close male friend or in conversation with a male acquaintance in a friendly context.
