```markdown
## Bruh (English)
### Explanation:
"Bruh" is a slang term used to express confusion, surprise, skepticism, or disbelief. It often conveys a sense of exasperation or disbelief. It can be used in various contexts, much like "dude" or "man," but is more casual and informal.
### Example:
- **English:** "I can't believe you just ate that whole pizza by yourself!"
- **Bruh:** "BRUH, how?"
## 布鲁 (Chinese)
### 解释：
“布鲁（Bruh）”是一个用来表达困惑、惊讶、质疑或怀疑的俚语。它传达了一种无奈或怀疑的感觉。这个词语在各种语境中都可以使用，类似于“伙计”或“家伙”，但更加随意和非正式。
### 示例：
- **英语：** "I can't believe you just ate that whole pizza by yourself!"
- **布鲁：** "布鲁，怎么了？"
```
Note: "布鲁" is a transliteration of "bruh" in Chinese, but it may not be commonly understood. In practice, Chinese speakers might use something closer to "伙计" or "哥们儿" to convey a similar meaning in casual conversation.
