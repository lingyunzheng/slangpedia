## Bruh 🤷‍♂️
**English Slang:**
*Bruh* is a versatile word that originated in African American Vernacular English (AAVE) and has become widespread in American English. It's often used to express disbelief, sarcasm, or to interject in conversation, similar to saying "heck" or "gosh". 
**Bilingual Slang Explanation:**
- **中文释义：** "Bruh" 是一个具有广泛用法的词汇，起源于美国非裔美国人英语（AAVE），并逐渐在美式英语中流行起来。这个词经常用来表达惊讶、讽刺，或者在对话中插话，类似于说 "heck" 或 "gosh"。
- **Usage Examples:**
- **English:** "This isn't possible, bruh! There has to be a glitch in the system."
- **中文示例：** "这不可能，Bruh！系统里肯定有bug！"
--- 
Note the casual and informal tone required when using *bruh* in both English and Chinese translations.
