## bruh
**English Explanation:**
A versatile interjection used to express a range of emotions, typically frustration, surprise, exasperation, or mild amusement. It originated from online communities but has become widespread.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
一个多功能的插话词，用来表达多种情绪，包括失望、惊讶、烦躁或轻微的滑稽。它起源于网络社区，但现在已经很普遍了。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I waited in line for over an hour, and they told me they're closing early."
- **Translation with "bruh":** 我等了超过一个小时，他们就说要提前关门了，这真是个破事。
- **Chinese with "bruh":** 我等了超过一个小时，他们就说要提前关门了，啊，bruh。
