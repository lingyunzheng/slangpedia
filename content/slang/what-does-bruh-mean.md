## Bruh: A Universal Expression of Innocence and Absurdity
### English Explanation
**Bruh** is a versatile slang term used to express a wide range of emotions, often involving a sense of bewilderment, exasperation, or laying back. It's often used in contexts where the speaker lacks context or is unphased by a situation. The term can be placed before a noun (e.g., "bruh move"), after a noun (e.g., "that's a bruh moment"), or as a standalone interjection (e.g., "bruh?").
### Chinese Explanation
**Bruh** 是一种普遍用来表达困惑、无奈或随性情绪的俚语。它使用十分灵活，可以在名词前（如 "bruh move"），名词后（如 "那是我见过的 bruh 时刻"），或作为独立的感叹词（如 "bruh?"）。它通常用于缺乏上下文或对某一情况无动于衷的情境。
