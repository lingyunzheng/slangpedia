## Bruh: Brotherhood Slang
### English Explanation
**B魯h**: This term is often used to express exasperation, bewilderment, or acknowledgment. It can also be used to express surprise or disbelief. It's a versatile interjection that can be added to the beginning or the end of a sentence to convey a range of emotions, from mild annoyance to intense surprise. It has become popular in online communities and text messaging.
### Bilingual Example
- English: "That's so weird, bruh!"
- 中文 (翻译): "这真的太奇怪了，老兄！"
### Origin and Usage
The term "bro" (short for brother) combined with "uh" to create "bruh" was first used in 2009. Since then, it has evolved into a widely used term in casual conversation and has become an integral part of internet slang across various platforms.
