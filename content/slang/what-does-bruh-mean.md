## Bruh: English & Chinese Slang Explanation
### English
**Bruh** is an informal interjection often used to express a range of emotions, from confusion and frustration to surprise and amusement. It's commonly used in casual conversational settings and can be adapted to fit multiple contexts. 
### Chinese
**Bruh**（布瑞，布鲁）是一种非正式的语气词，常用来表达多种情绪，包括困惑、沮丧、惊讶和娱乐等。它广泛用于非正式的对话中，可以根据不同情境进行调整使用。
