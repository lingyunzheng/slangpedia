# "Bruh" in English and Chinese
## English
**B鲁 (Brush)**
"Bruh" is a versatile interjection that functions as a way to express disbelief, agreement, or frustration. It's commonly used among friends or in casual settings to add emphasis or laughter to a conversation.
*Example:*  
"Hey, did you see that trick the skateboarder did? *B鲁!* That was insane!"
## Chinese (普通话)
**Bro 哥 (Bro Ge)**
"Bro 哥" (Pronounced "B鲁", Bruh) is a way to express surprise, amusement, or excitement in a casual or humorous context. It's similar to how "Bruh" is used in English and is often used between friends or in relaxed settings to emphasize a point or show approval.
*Example:*  
"你看过那个滑板小子的表演吗？*B鲁*！太酷了！"  
("Have you seen that skateboarder's performance? *B鲁*! That's awesome!")
*Example:*  
"这问题太难了，*B鲁*！我都快放弃了！"  
("This question is too difficult, *B鲁*! I'm about to give up!")
