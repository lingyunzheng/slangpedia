# Bruh: 布鲁
**English:**  
"Bruh" is a slang term used in informal, laid-back conversations. It's a casual way of addressing someone or used as a filler word to express disbelief, agreement, or to emphasize a point. Essentially, it's a versatile interjection that can be used in place of "bro" (short for brother) or other words depending on the context.
**Chinese (繁體字):**  
"布魯" (Bùlǔ) 是一種非正式、放鬆的口頭表達方式中使用的网络俚語。這是一個隨便ér 的方式去稱呼某人，或者作爲填充詞來表達不驚訝、同意的情緒，或者強調某一點。簡單來說，它是一個多用途的畢竟詞，可以在需要使用“Bro”（Brother的縮寫）或其他詞語的情況下替代使用。
**Note:** While "布魯" attempts to phonetically represent "Bruh," it's important to note that this is a transliteration and doesn't perfectly capture the actual pronunciation in standard Mandarin. The meaning and usage remain the same in both languages.
