## Bruh
### English Explanation
"Brüh" is a slang term often used as a filler word to express disbelief, confusion, or agreement. It can also be used to convey a "meh" or indifferent attitude.
### Chinese Explanation
“布瑞” 是一种俚语，常用来作为填充词，表达惊讶、困惑或认同。它也可以用来表达一种“无所谓的”态度。
