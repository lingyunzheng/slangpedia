### "Bruh"
#### English
A casual interjection or term of address used to express surprise, exasperation, or agreement. It is often used in place of "brother" or to emphasize a point.
Example: 
- **"Bruh, that party was lit!"** - Expressing excitement about how great the party was.
#### Chinese
一个非正式的插句或者用来表达惊讶、挫败或同意的称呼。它经常用来替代“兄弟”或强调某个观点。
例子：
- **"Bruh，那个聚会超级棒！"** - 表达对聚会非常好的认同。
