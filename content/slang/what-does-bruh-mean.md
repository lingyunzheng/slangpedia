# "Bruh" Slang Explanation
## English
**Definition:** "Bruh" is a casual, conversational term that can be used to address a male friend or to express surprise, confusion, or reaction.
**Usage:** 
- Ex. "Hey bruh, did you see the game last night?"
- Ex. "Bruh, that's wild, I can't believe you did that!"
## Chinese
**释义：** "Bruh" 是一种非正式的用语，常用于称呼男性朋友或用来表达惊讶、困惑或反应。
**用法：**
- 例句：嘿，Bruh，你昨晚看了比赛吗？
- 例句：Bruh，那真新鲜，我简直不敢相信你居然做了那件事！
