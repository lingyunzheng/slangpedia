## Bet
**English:**  
A bet is a wager, often made with a friend or acquaintance, where you agree to stake something of value (money, items, or services) on the outcome of a particular event or prediction. It's a form of risk-taking and can range from friendly and lighthearted to competitive and serious.
**Simplified Chinese & Pinyin:**  
赌局（Dǔjú）  
赌局是一种约定，通常与朋友或熟人之间进行，双方同意将某种有价值的东西（金钱、物品或服务）作为赌注，押在某一事件的结果或预测上。这是一种风险行为，可以是友好的和轻松的，也可以是竞争性和认真的。
**Explanation in Slang:**  
In casual English, people might use slang terms like "spill the beans" to talk about something that was kept secret being disclosed, or "ballin'" to describe someone who is living a fancy lifestyle. Similarly, in Chinese slang, "透底" (tòu dǐ) can be used to describe someone who gives away secrets, while "炫富" (xuàn fù) can describe someone showing off their wealth.
