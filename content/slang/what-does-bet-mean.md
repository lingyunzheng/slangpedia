# Bet
## 中文解释
**押注** - 在某些情境下，可以指赌注或打赌。有时用于形容对某事非常有信心并愿意冒险。
## 英文解释
**Bet** - Often refers to a wager or a bet. Can also imply a high level of confidence in something, with a willingness to take a risk.
