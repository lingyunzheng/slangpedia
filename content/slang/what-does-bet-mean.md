## bet (英语/汉语)
### English
**bet** - *noun* or *verb*  
1. **To Stake**: When you make an uncertain prediction or declaration, often by risking something of value, like money.  
Example: *I bet that the team will win the game*.
2. **High RISK**: Undertaking a risky action or engaging in a challenging situation without guaranteeing success.  
Example: *She bet on a new investment without doing thorough research*.
### 汉语
**bet** - *名词*或*动词*  
1. **下注**：做出不确定的预测或声明，通常通过押注某些有价值的东西（如金钱）来进行。  
例句：*我打赌那队会赢比赛*。
2. **高风险行为**：进行一种有风险的行动或参与一项具有挑战性的情况，而不能保证成功。  
例句：*她为了一个新投资而冒险，却没有做彻底的研究*。
