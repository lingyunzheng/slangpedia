## 中文解释
**bet**  
含义：赌博，打赌。  
用法示例：他非要打个赌，看明天会不会下雨。
## 英文解释
**bet**  
Meaning: gambling or making a wager.  
Usage Example: He insisted on betting that it would rain tomorrow.
