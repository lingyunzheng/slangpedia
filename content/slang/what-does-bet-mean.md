## Bet
**English:**
A bet is a wager where one person or more agree to exchange money or other things based on the outcome of an event, often a game or a contest. It can also be used as a casual term for a situation where two people are trying to outdo each other.
**Chinese:**
bet (bài)：赌注，打赌。表示人们基于某个事件的结果而进行的一种金钱或物品的交换。在日常对话中，"bet" 也常被用作一种轻松的说法，指两个或多个人在尝试互相超越的情境。
例如：
- English: "I bet you won't finish the game in under an hour."
- Chinese: "我打赌你在一小时内不会完成这个游戏。"
