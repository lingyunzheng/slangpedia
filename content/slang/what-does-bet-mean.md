## English Slang: "Bet"
### Definition
An informal challenge or wager, especially on the outcome of an event or situation, where the person offrering it is very confident about their prediction.
### Example Usage
- "I bet you can't finish that entire pizza by yourself!" 
### Bilingual Explanation
- English: "I bet (someone) something about (an event or situation)."
- 中文: "我打赌（某人）关于（某个事件或情况）的事。"
```
## English Slang: "Bet I Can"
### Definition
A common exclamation or expression of confidence, often followed by the person expressing their ability to achieve something.
### Example Usage
- "Bet I can clean the whole house in 30 minutes!"
### Bilingual Explanation
- English: "Bet I can (do something)"
- 中文: "我打赌我能（做某事）"
