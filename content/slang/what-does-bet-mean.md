## Bet
### English
A bet is an agreement between two or more parties where each stakes something of value (usually money) on the outcome of a specified event. If the event goes against the stakeholder, they forfeit what was bet. If it goes their way, they keep what they bet and may also win additional money based on the odds set by the other party.
### Chinese
赌 (Dǔ) 是两个或多个当事人之间的一种协议，每个人都押注某种有价值的物品（通常是钱）在某个指定事件的结果上。如果事件的结果对押注者不利，他们就得放弃所押的赌注。如果事件的结果对他们有利，他们不仅能保住自己的赌注，还可能根据双方约定的赔率赢取更多金钱。
