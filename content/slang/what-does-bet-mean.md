# Slang Term: Bet
## English
**Meaning:** An amount of money that is wagered on an outcome of an event or a guess. It can also be used as a challenge or a way to illustrate belief in the outcome of something.
**Example Usage:** "I bet I can run faster than you!"
## 中文
**含义:** 在某个事件的结果或猜测上押注的金钱数量。它也可以用来作为一个挑战或表明对某件事情结果的信心的方式。
**用例:** "我打赌我能跑得比你快！"
