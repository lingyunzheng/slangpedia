## English: Bet
**Definition**: A wager or stake on the outcome of an event, often for entertainment or to test others.
- **Bilingual Explanation**: 
- English: A bet is a gamble made with someone else, often depending on the outcome of a game, competition, or even an opinion. It can be for fun or with money.
- 中文: 赌注是指与他人打赌一个事件的结果，通常是出于娱乐或者是测试对方的意见。这可以是无赌注的打赌，也可以是有赌注的打赌。
```
## English: Bet
**Definition**: Also used informally to mean a strong belief in something or someone.
- **Bilingual Explanation**: 
- English: Informally, "bet" can mean 'I bet you', which is an expression of strong belief or confidence in a statement, contrasting with uncertainty.
- 中文: 在非正式场合，“bet”也可以用来表示“我敢说”，这是一种强烈相信某句话或某人的表达方式，与不确定性相对。
```
