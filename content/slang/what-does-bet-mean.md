## Bet
### English
A bet is a wager where one or more parties agree to exchange money or other valuables based on the outcome of an uncertain event, such as a sporting event, a game, or a personal prediction. It often implies a high level of confidence in a particular outcome.
### 中文
“Bet”是指参与方同意基于某个不确定事件的结果而交换金钱或其他有价值的物品的一种赌注，它可以是体育比赛、游戏或者个人预测。往往意味着对某一个结果的极高信心。
