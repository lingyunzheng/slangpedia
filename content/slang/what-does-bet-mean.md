## Bet (Gambling Slang)
- **中文解释**: 下注
- **英文解释**: Placing a bet, typically involves putting money on the outcome of an uncertain event, such as a sporting event or a race.
## Bet (Friendship Slang)
- **中文解释**: 打赌，用来表达某种强烈的情绪或信念
- **英文解释**: Used to express a strong belief or emotion, often in a non-gambling context. For example, "I bet you can't finish this project by tomorrow!" can be used to challenge someone or express high confidence in a result.
