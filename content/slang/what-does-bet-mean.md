## Bet (赌博)
**中文解释**
- **赌博**：指非法的以财物作注约定输赢的游戏。通常涉及金钱或贵重物品，参与者希望通过运气赢得更多赌资，但也可能输光所有财产。在中国大陆是非法的。
**英文解释**
- **Gambling**：Refers to an illegal game where bets are placed with the intention of making a profit based on chance. Participants risk their money or valuable items, hoping to win more gambling money, but they might lose everything. Gambling is illegal in mainland China.
```
## Bet (打赌)
**中文解释**
- **打赌**：指双方就某个问题或事物达成一种契约，决一胜负，输的一方按预先约定接受某种惩罚或奖赏。一种不涉及非法活动的娱乐或赌注行为。
**英文解释**
- **Wager**：Refers to a bet where two parties agree on a contest regarding a particular issue or thing, to determine the winner. The losing party has to accept a predetermined penalty or reward. A form of entertainment or gambling without illegal activities involved.
```
