# Slang Explanation for "Bet"
## English
**Bet**:  
- **Meaning**: To make a wager or bet on the outcome of an event, usually involving money.
- **Example**: "I bet you're wrong about that!"
## Chinese
**赌**（Dǔ）：  
- **含义**: 下注或赌注在某个事件的结果上，通常涉及金钱。
- **例句**: "你肯定搞错了，我打赌你错了！"
