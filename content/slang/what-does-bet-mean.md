## Bet (英语/Bet (中文))
A term used to describe a wager or a gamble, usually involving money or something of value. It can also be used to express strong certainty about the outcome of an event.
### English:
To bet means to make a wager or gamble, often involving money or something of value. It can also be used to express strong certainty about the outcome of an event, meaning "to take a bet" on something.
**Example:**
- If you're sure about something, you might say, "I'll bet it rains tomorrow!" (我打赌明天会下雨！)
### Chinese:
"赌" (dǔ) 一词用于描述一种下注或赌博，通常涉及金钱或其他有价值的东西。它也可以用来表达对某一事件结果的强烈确定性，意思是“打赌”某个结果。
**例句：**
- 如果你很确定某件事情，可能会说，“我打赌明天会下雨！” (I'll bet it rains tomorrow!)
