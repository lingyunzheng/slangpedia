# Slang Explanation for "Bet"
## English
**Bet**: This term is generally used as a non-comparative statement, often implying the speaker is taking a strong stance on something. It can be used to express confidence in the accuracy of a statement or belief.
Example: "It's going to rain today, I bet!"
## 中文
**Bet**: 通常这个词汇作为表达强烈观点的一种非比较性陈述，说话人往往是在表达对其所说的话或信念的高度自信。
例句：今天肯定会下雨，我打赌！
