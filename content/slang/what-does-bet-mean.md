### Bet
**English:**
A bet is a wager or gamble, usually involving money or stakes, where the outcome is determined by a chance event or a future event. It is often placed on the outcome of a sports game, a lottery, or any uncertain event.
**Slang:**
- "I'm down for a bet" – I agree to make a wager.
- "That's a bet!" – Agreed, or expressing strong approval.
- "That's a no-contest bet" – A very certain bet; equivalent to "It's a sure thing."
- "You bought the farm" – If you've lost a big bet, you’ve gone broke.
**Chinese (简体中文):**
赌是在某个事件（通常是体育比赛、抽奖或任何不确定的事件）的结果未知的情况下，人们用钱或物品进行的一种押注或赌博。赌注通常会在某一方同意的情况下进行。
**俚语:**
- “我干，来一注” – 我同意进行一次赌注。
- “那是赌！” – 同意或表达强烈的赞同。
- “那是无异议的赌注” – 一个非常有把握的赌注；相当于“那是铁板的事”。
- “你倾家荡产了” – 如果你输了大赌注，你已经破产了。

```markdown
### Bet
**English:**
A bet is an agreed-upon wager on the outcome of an uncertain event, often used in gambling contexts. It can range from a small, casual bet to a larger, more serious one.
**Slang:**
- "To bet the farm" – To risk everything on a single outcome.
- "Even money" – A bet where the amount won and lost is the same.
- "To come up trumps" (British slang) – To win a bet or to succeed.
- "To back out of a bet" – To refuse to take part in the bet after agreeing to it.
**Chinese (简体中文):**
赌是对不确定事件结果的押注，通常用于赌博情景中。赌注可以从小打小闹的随意赌注到大额、严肃的赌注。
**俚语:**
- “把家底押上” – 把所有的东西押在一个结果上。
- “平局” – 赌注中赢输的金额相同。
- “抽到王牌”（英式俚语） – 赢了赌注或成功。
- “反悔不赌” – 在同意后拒绝参与赌注。
```
