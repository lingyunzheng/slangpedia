### bet
**English:** A bet is a wager or a stake involving a sum of money or something of value, where the outcome of an event determines the winner and the loser. In slang, it can also refer to a challenge or a risk taken to prove something.
**普通话:** 赌注，是指用一定的金钱或有价值的东西作为赌注，根据某个事件的结果来决定输赢。在俚语中，它也可以指一种挑战或冒险，用来证明某件事。
Example in English: "I'm gonna bet that it rains tomorrow."
例句：我敢打赌明天会下雨。
Example in Chinese: "他赌注他能跑赢所有人。"
例句：He bet that he could outrun everyone.
