```markdown
## English: Bet
Chinese: 外借
- **Meaning**: To lend or borrow, but often used in a casual or slang context to mean more than just lending a physical object. It can imply that you're minding or looking after something or someone for a period of time. For example, if someone says they will "bet" your pet, it might mean they will take care of it while you're away.
## Example Usage:
- **In English**: I'll bet your dog while you're on vacation.
- **In Simplified Chinese**: 我帮你养你的狗吧，你出去度假的时候。
- **In Traditional Chinese**: 我幫你養你的狗吧，你出去度假的时候。
```

```markdown
## English: Bet
Chinese: 赌注 / 打赌
- **Meaning**: When used in its traditional sense, it means a bet or a wager, like in gambling or making a bet that something will happen. In a more casual context, it might be used to express a strong belief or prediction in something happening or not happening. 
- **Usage**: In phrasal terms, it can also be used to express a strong preference or opinion about something, similar to "for sure" or "definitely." For example, if someone says, "I bet you my job that you won't finish this project by tomorrow," it could mean they are highly confident about their prediction.
## Example Usage:
- **In English**: I bet you can't finish this project by tomorrow.
- **In Simplified Chinese**: 我打赌你明天完不成这个项目。
- **In Traditional Chinese**: 我打賭你明天完不成這個項目。
```

```markdown
Chinese: 吃老本
```
