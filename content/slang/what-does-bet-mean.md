## bet (英/美)
### English
- **Bet** - An informal way to strongly agree with something or to assert that something will happen. Often used when you are very confident about the outcome.
- Example: "Bet it's going to rain tomorrow."
- Explanation: This means the speaker is very confident that it will rain tomorrow.
### 翻译
- **Bet** - 一种非正式表达方式，用来强烈同意某件事，或断言某件事情会发生。通常用于当你对结果非常确定时。
- 例句： "Bet it's going to rain tomorrow."
- 解释：这意味着说话者非常确信明天会下雨。
