# Bet
## English
**Meaning**: A bet is a wager or a gamble where two or more parties put money, effort, or other resources on the outcome of an event, often for entertainment or recreation.
**Example**: "I bet you $5 that the Lakers will win the game tonight."
## 中文
**含义**：赌是一个打赌或下注的行为，双方或多方便将自己的金钱、努力或者其他资源押在某个事件的结果上，通常是为了娱乐或消遣。
**例句**："我打赌今晚湖人队会赢，赌5美元怎么样？"
