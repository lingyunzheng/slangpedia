### bet (赌博)
**English Explanation:**
A bet is a wager or a gamble, typically involving money or other stakes, where two or more people agree to give something of value based on the outcome of an event or a guess of future results, such as a sports game, a coin flip, or any other situation with uncertainty.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
赌注，指人们基于某个事件的结果或猜测未来结果而下注的打赌行为，涉及金钱或其他赌注。通常由两人或多人约定，在事件结束后根据结果给予一定价值的东西作为赌注。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I'll bet you $10 that my team wins the game!"
- **Chinese:** "我打赌我的队伍赢了比赛，我给你10块钱！"
```
### bet (发誓，赌咒)
**English Explanation:**
In a more colloquial context, "bet" can mean to swear or make a solemn promise, often to prove one's seriousness or honesty.
**Simplified Chinese Explanation:**
在俚语中，“赌”意味着发誓或做誓言，通常是为证明自己的真诚或诚实。
**Example Usage:**
- **English:** "I'd bet the farm on this being true!"
- **Chinese:** "如果这不是真的，我宁愿把家产都输掉！"
```
