# Bet
## English
**Meaning**: A wager or dare, often implying that one is very confident about something.
**Example Usage**: "Are you betting that you can finish that project in half the time?"
## Chinese
**含义**: 博注或挑战，通常意味着某人对自己所说或所做之事非常自信。
**使用示例**: "你敢打赌你能把那个项目缩减到一半时间完成吗？"
